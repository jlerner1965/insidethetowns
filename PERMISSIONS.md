# Photo permissions

Permission granted to publish someone else's photographs, recorded verbatim.

`IMAGE_LICENSES.csv` carries one row per image file. Where an image is covered
by a grant below, its `license` column cites the section here rather than
restating the terms, so the wording lives in exactly one place.

A grant is only worth what its wording says. Record the sentence, who sent it,
their role and the date — not a paraphrase, and not a licence name the sender
did not actually use.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | The images on the three named gallery pages |
| **Credit** | **Required.** "Please attribute Boulder County." We credit "Boulder County Parks & Open Space" |
| **Standing** | Not stated. Treat it as covering these three pages only |

Read the second paragraph before reusing anything else of theirs. Their site
carries images the county does not own and republishes under someone else's
attribution — the Carnegie Library for Local History historic photographs are
the example they give. "Most of the images on our website can be used with
attribution" is not the same as all of them, and the grant above names three
pages, not the site. Anything from a fourth page needs its own ask.

These photographs carried EXIF, which Erie's did not. It is dropped on import,
as for every image on the network: camera metadata can carry GPS and a
photographer's name, and neither belongs in a file we republish.

Boulder County's galleries are largely accessibility documentation — restrooms,
ADA parking, trailhead surfaces — which is useful to the county and not to a
guide. The three chosen are the scenic frames among them.

## Stock licences — bought photographs

`docs/HERO-PHOTOS.md` is the list of heroes worth buying.

One entry per photograph, written the day it is bought: the agency, the
asset number and its page, the photographer, the licence bought (standard
or editorial, and the size), the order or invoice number, the date and the
plan or pack it came from. Its row in `IMAGE_LICENSES.csv` starts with the
agency and the kind of licence, then the photographer and the year, and
cites this section: `iStock editorial licence (John M. Chase; 2021);
bought 2026-11-02 — see PERMISSIONS.md, section: Stock licences; resized to
2400px`. The validator reads that into the credit on `/credits/` and fails
a row that does not start that way.

An editorial licence covers the guide's pages, the share image included,
and nothing promotional: not a sponsor's post, an ad or a printed card.
Cropping and the hero's overlay are fine; retouching is not.

### What the iStock licence asks of us

From iStock's Content License Agreement, June 2026 version, which the owner
sent on 28 October 2026. Adobe's terms are not recorded here yet.

- **Editorial use only (3b).** Content marked so may not be used "for any
  commercial, promotional, advertorial, endorsement, advertising, gambling
  … or merchandising purpose"; it is for "descriptive purposes such as …
  human interest topics (for example, in a blog …)". The Berthoud hero
  describes the town on the guide's own pages. It never goes in an ad, a
  sponsor's post, a promotion of the guide itself or printed matter.
- **No standalone file (3c).** No use "that allows others to download,
  extract, or redistribute content as a standalone file".
- **One user; not on a shared server (5).** Bought with credits, the file
  "may only be available to one person at any one time", and without an
  extended licence it may not be stored "on a server (giving more than
  one person simultaneous access to the content)".
- **Credit beside an editorial photograph (7):** "iStock.com/Artist's
  Member Name". The hero prints it.
- **Keep the embedded record (11d):** "retain the copyright symbol, the
  name of iStock, the content's identification number and any other
  information that may be embedded in the electronic file".

**Made private, 28 October 2026.** The repository was public on GitHub
when the three files were uploaded, so for a few hours anyone could
download them, which 3(c) and 5 do not allow. The owner made it private
the same day. Keep it private while it holds bought files.
