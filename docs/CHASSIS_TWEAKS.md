# Chassis tweaks

Where the shared town template did not fit a town, logged as it was found:
the town, what happened, and what a fix would be. Nothing here is fixed
unless its row says so; the owner picks which to do. Started 6 October 2026,
when the seven towns after Longmont, Loveland and Carbon Valley were
scaffolded (Severance, Fort Lupton, Castle Rock, Estes Park, Golden,
Evergreen, Nederland).

| # | Town | What happened | A fix would be | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Castle Rock, Evergreen | `npm run new-town` writes the domain as `inside<slug>.com` and the README template says `inside__SLUG__.com`; the owned domains are insidecastlerockco.com and insideevergreenco.com (the plain names belong to someone else). Windsor, Longmont and Loveland had the same. | Take the domain as a third argument, or prompt for it; have the README read the domain from the config. | Logged. Configs set by hand; the two READMEs still say the wrong domain. |
| 2 | Evergreen | The template assumes a municipality: `officialLinks.townSiteLabel` defaults to "the Town of X", the holding page and Moving Here copy speak of a town, `policeNonEmergency` expects a police department. Evergreen is unincorporated: Jefferson County, the Evergreen Park & Recreation District and the fire district share what a town hall does, and the sheriff polices it. Niwot has the same shape and was handled line by line. | A `governance` note on the config ("unincorporated; Jefferson County") that the About, Moving Here and holding pages read, instead of per-town copy. | Logged. Config points `townSite` at Jefferson County with a label saying so. |
| 3 | Golden, Fort Lupton | The scaffold's `townSiteLabel` is "the Town of X"; Golden and Fort Lupton are cities. Longmont, Loveland and Fort Collins were corrected by hand too. | Scaffold could ask for the municipal form, or derive it from `government_type`. | Logged. Set by hand in each config. |
| 4 | All seven | `new-town` writes a sample event, place and article that have no `source` or `verified`, so the accuracy gate excludes them and the validator warns on every run; the sample Moving Here page carries placeholder copy. | Scaffold should write the README and an empty `staging/` instead of invented samples, or write the samples into `staging/` with a `review` block. | Logged. The three sample files were deleted in each new town; `pages/moving-here.md` keeps its labelled sample copy until launch. |
| 5 | All seven | `new-town` appends an `IMAGE_LICENSES.csv` row reading "CC0 placeholder — replace before launch", which `publicCredit` refuses by design, so `npm run validate` and `test/credits.test.ts` went red on a freshly scaffolded town. | Either the scaffold writes no row until a photograph exists, or the check tolerates a placeholder on a town that is not live. | **Fixed, the one fix in this log**, because it blocked CI: the validator and the credits test now skip a placeholder row only while the town's status is not `live`, and `/credits/` on a holding-page town lists nothing (it ships no photograph of the guide's; the same row made the whole holding-page build fail at `/credits/`). The day a town is flipped with the placeholder still in place, it is an error again (`scripts/validate-content.ts`, `test/credits.test.ts`, `src/lib/photo-credits.ts`). |
| 6 | Castle Rock | `TownMap.astro` draws Castle Rock as a hard-coded context city ("Cities with no guide of their own"). The day Castle Rock goes live it will appear twice on the hub map, once as a pin and once as context. | Drop it from `CONTEXT` when the guide is flipped, or have the map leave out any context city that has a live guide. | Logged. Nothing shows until the flip, because the map now receives only live towns. |
| 7 | Hub | The hub home listed towns that were not live under a dashed "Coming next" box, and passed every town, live or not, to the map. The owner's rule is that a town stays off the hub until flipped. | Done as part of the regions work: the box is gone and the map, the picker, the cards and the counts all read the live list. | Done (6 October 2026). |
| 8 | Estes Park, Golden, Evergreen, Nederland | `/things-to-do/` is headed "Trails, parks and venues" and its description says the same; on a mountain guide the trailheads are the main entry and the heading does not name them. The directory groups by type, so a "Trailheads" group does appear under it. | Heading and description read from the types present. | Logged. |
| 9 | Estes Park, Golden, Evergreen, Nederland | "Eat & Drink / Things to Do / Moving Here" is the suburb's nav. A mountain guide's readers are as often visitors as residents: lodging sits under Things to Do as "Stay the night", and there is no "Plan a visit" entry. | A `visit` nav item or a lodging page for the mountain variant. | Logged. |
| 10 | Nederland, Estes Park | The holding page and the hub's tagline say "the towns of Colorado's Front Range". Nederland (8,235 ft) and Estes Park (7,743 ft) are in the Front Range as a mountain range, but a reader there would not call their town a Front Range town. | Leave, or have the holding page use the region: "the Mountains & Foothills guide…". | Logged. |
| 11 | Nederland, Estes Park, Severance | `new-town` sets `region: 'Northern Colorado'` as a placeholder; three of seven needed changing, and a wrong region would have grouped a town under the wrong heading on the hub with nothing failing. The towns test now pins every town's region, which is what caught it. | Scaffold asks for the region, or refuses to run without `--region`. | Logged. |
