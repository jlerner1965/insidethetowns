# Chassis tweaks

Where the shared town template did not fit a town, logged as it was found:
the town, what happened, and what a fix would be. Nothing here is fixed
unless its row says so; the owner picks which to do. Started 6 October 2026,
when the seven towns after Longmont, Loveland and Carbon Valley were
scaffolded (Severance, Fort Lupton, Castle Rock, Estes Park, Golden,
Evergreen, Nederland).
