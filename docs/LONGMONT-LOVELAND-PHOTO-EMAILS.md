# Longmont and Loveland photo requests, ready to send

The first photo pass for the two newest guides used Wikimedia Commons only, and
Commons is thin for both: five listings have a photograph. This file holds the
requests that fill the gaps, in the shape of `VENUE-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`. Send from
`hello@insidelovelandco.com` or `hello@insidelongmontco.com`, which both
forward to the editor's inbox.

When permission comes back, add the image with its row in `IMAGE_LICENSES.csv`:

```
content/<town>/images/<file>.jpg,<their page or the email>,Used with permission of <body> (<date>),y,<town>
```

Keep the reply itself. Permission you cannot produce later is not permission.

## 1. Loveland High Plains Arts Council (Sculpture in the Park) — Benson Sculpture Garden

**To:** lhpac@sculptureinthepark.org
**Phone:** (970) 663-2940 · P.O. Box 7006, Loveland, CO 80537-0006
(contact details from sculptureinthepark.org/contact, read 5 October 2026)

**Why an email and not a walk:** the garden's works are modern sculpture by
living artists, and the US has no freedom-of-panorama exception for sculpture,
so a photograph of the garden carries the sculptors' copyright as well as the
photographer's. Two Commons views were left out for that reason (DECISIONS.md,
"Completed, 5 October 2026"). The arts council runs the garden's show and
speaks for many of the artists; it is the one body that can say yes.

**Subject:** A photograph of the Benson Sculpture Garden for Inside Loveland

> Hello,
>
> I publish Inside Loveland (insidelovelandco.com), a free local guide to the
> city. The Benson Sculpture Garden has a listing on it, and our families guide
> sends readers there, with the facts taken from your own pages: the 188 pieces,
> the free admission, the August show and your group tours. Nobody pays to be
> listed.
>
> The listing has no photograph, on purpose: the sculptures are the artists'
> work, and we would rather ask than assume. Two questions:
>
> 1. Does the arts council hold a photograph of the garden, a wide view of the
>    park with works in it, that we could use on the listing and the guide? We
>    would credit the council (and the photographer, if you name one) and take it
>    down the day you ask.
> 2. If not, would the council be content for us to photograph the park
>    ourselves for those two pages, crediting the council and naming any work
>    that is clearly the subject?
>
> Either way the listing stays, and we keep its facts in line with your pages.
>
> Thank you,
> <name>
> Inside Loveland · hello@insidelovelandco.com
