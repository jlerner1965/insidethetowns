# Park photo requests, ready to send

Ten emails covering the 41 parks, trails and open spaces that have no
photograph. Each names the exact sites, so the recipient can answer in one
reply rather than asking what you mean.

Send them from a real address on the town's own domain where you have one
(`hello@insideberthoud.com` and so on). A request from the site being asked
about is answered far more often than one from a generic address.

**Read this before sending number 8.** Boulder County's own permits page says
still photography needs no permit when it happens during open hours with fewer
than 25 people. Heil Valley Ranch, Rabbit Mountain and the Niwot Loop Trail can
simply be photographed on a walk. Send that email only if you want the county's
own library as well.

**And before photographing any Fort Collins natural area.** Commercial
photography there needs a Commercial Use Permit, $50 and up to 15 business days.
Arapaho Bend and Fossil Creek already have photographs, so nothing is blocked
today, but do not shoot there for the guide without the permit.

Two things every email asks, deliberately:

1. **May we use photographs you already hold?** Parks departments usually have
   a library from programme brochures and social media, and sending you three
   files is less work for them than anything else you could ask.
2. **Do we need a permit to take our own?** Several Colorado jurisdictions
   require one for commercial photography on public land. Asking now costs
   nothing and avoids a problem later.

When permission comes back, record it. Add the image with a row in
`IMAGE_LICENSES.csv`:

```
content/<town>/images/<file>.jpg,<their page or the email>,Used with permission of <body> (<date>),y,<town>
```

Keep the reply itself. Permission you cannot produce later is not permission.
