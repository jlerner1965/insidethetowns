# Archive

out, email drafts that were sent, and the call list and verification queue
that `TODO.md` replaced. Nothing here is a rule. The rules in force are in
`HOW-IT-WORKS.md` at the root; the reasons are in `DECISIONS.md`. Files
here keep their original paths in links from the log, prefixed with
`docs/archive/`.
