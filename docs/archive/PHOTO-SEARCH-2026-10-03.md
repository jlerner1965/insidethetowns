# The photo search of 3 October 2026

Every listing without a photograph, across the nine guides, was searched
against the two places openly licensed photographs live: Wikimedia Commons
and the Creative Commons corner of Flickr (through Openverse, which indexes
it). This is what was searched, what came back, and what it means for the
rest.

## What was searched

For each town: every file in the Commons category tree for the town (two
levels down), every geotagged Commons file within ten kilometres of the town
centre, a Commons full-text search on each listing's name, and an Openverse
search on each park, trail, venue, lodging and bar by name plus town-level
Openverse queries ("Old Town Fort Collins", "Horsetooth Reservoir", "Lyons
Colorado"). Only CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and public-domain files were kept, at
900 pixels wide or more, maps, logos and scans excluded.

Each town's pool was then matched to its listings by a reader who looked at
every candidate before choosing, under the rule that a photograph must show
the listed place itself: a street scene, a neighbouring building, a previous
tenant's sign, a different park or an unplaced stretch of river is a wrong
picture and worse than none. People in the foreground, licence-plate
close-ups and pre-renovation views were rejected too. Every pick was then
looked at a second time before it went in.

## What was added (16)

- **Elizabeth:** Casey Jones Park, the Stampede arena (Elizabeth Stampede,
  2012, CC BY 3.0; 1089 px, the only photograph of the park). *Withdrawn 4
  October: it is the same Commons file the Stampede grounds listing already
  carried, so the two cards sat side by side with one picture. See
  DECISIONS.md, "One photograph, one listing".*
- **Fort Collins (15):** the Avery House, Canvas Stadium, the Museum of
  Discovery, Horsetooth Rock for Horsetooth Mountain Open Space, Arthur's
  Rock for Lory State Park, Moby Arena, the old high school for the
  University Center for the Arts, the post office entrance for the Museum of
  Art, New Belgium's taproom, the Opera House Block with the Jax neon for
  Jax Fish House, the C&S Freight Depot that is the Downtown Transit Center,
  and from Flickr the Cathy Fromme Prairie trail, the Maxwell hogback and a
  Riverbend pond.
- **Windsor:** the 1909 City Hall that houses the Art and Heritage Center.

Rejected after viewing, with the reason: the Armstrong Hotel (a former
ground-floor tenant's sign dominates), Odell's patio (faces in the
foreground), City Park (a child fishing in the foreground, then a murky
2008 phone frame), the Lory Student Center (2011, before the renovation),
Pineridge (prairie dogs, no landscape), the Poudre River Trail (the river at
Laporte, not the trail), the Allicar museum (would duplicate the UCA
photograph), the Gardens on Spring Creek (its gateway sculpture, with
visitors under it), Lee Martinez Park (the barn is a speck), the Erie 1889
building for Sweets (a neighbour's building), the Scheels photograph for

Timnath's Feed & Grain building (2005, as a biker bar), Windsor's Boardwalk
Park (the lake aerial the hero already shows) and Chimney Park (storm-dark).

## What this settles

Nothing openly licensed exists for any of the 257 that remain. Commons holds
each town's landmarks, parks and historic buildings, photographed mostly by
a handful of contributors around 2010 to 2017, and almost nothing of a
working storefront with its present sign; Flickr's open corner holds the
same subjects plus beer. The remaining list is therefore not a search
problem. It is the walk and the asks already set out in `PHOTOS.md` and
`ALL-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`:

- **Storefronts (about 190, every town):** photographed on the walk from the
  public sidewalk, or supplied by the business under the one-to-one ask.
- **Parks in Johnstown (7), Timnath (3), Elizabeth (3), Windsor (5), Lyons
  (2), Niwot (2), Berthoud (4):** the walk, with the Town's own answer where
  one came (Johnstown: no permit, no park users; Erie: done).
- **Fort Collins parks, trails and natural areas (14 of 23 remain):** the
  City of Fort Collins holds a library of its natural areas and parks; it
  has not been asked. That ask is the single largest remaining yield.
- **Campus (Avenir, the Flower Trial Garden, the Lory Student Center):** CSU
  Photography's library, not yet asked.
- **Lyons' river, parks and trails:** Boulder County's grant covers Hall
  Ranch, Rabbit Mountain and the Niwot trails only; the Town of Lyons has
  been written to (A5, B7) and has not answered.

A re-run of this search is cheap (the scripts are in the session scratchpad,
not the repository) and worth doing yearly, since Commons grows; it will not
change the picture for storefronts.
