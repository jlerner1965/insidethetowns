## Wanted but could not source (left out)

- 2020 census count 5,904 and 2010 count 5,858: only on Wikipedia; the page uses DOLA's estimate series (5,877 for 2020, 5,865 for 2010, 5,946 for 2024) instead.
- Elevation 7,743 ft (GNIS via Wikipedia): not on any readable official page; the page gives the school district's 7,522 ft, attributed. The config's figure and the district's differ.
- "Statutory town" and "incorporated 1917": DOLA's LGIS is behind a reCAPTCHA and the State Archives incorporation-records page answered 403. The page says "Town" only.
- Any home price, median value or rent: Town (403), Larimer County assessor (403), Estes Park Housing Authority (unreachable). Workforce housing programs: same.
- The Town's own electric and water utility (Estes Park Power & Communications / Utilities): only inferred from Platte River ("their utility customers") and the sanitation district ("water usage recorded by Town of Estes Park"); written that way, attributed.
- Upper Thompson Sanitation District (the other sewer district): utsd.colorado.gov answered 403, so it is not named; the page tells movers to ask which district a house is in.
- Estes Valley Recreation and Park District facilities, fees, residency: evrpd.com returned a compressed/unreadable body.
- Trash provider: no readable official source.
- Police non-emergency number and department details: Town page 403; linked only.
- Larimer County zoning, septic, building and short-term-rental rules for the unincorporated valley: larimer.gov 403.
- Bustang to Estes 2026 end date: not stated on the route page (only "starts May 23rd"); NPS directions page's Bustang text is dated 2023 and was not used for dates.
- Fire district sales-tax election details: page carries only PDFs (survey media releases); not read.
- Elk in town (as opposed to the park): NPS only says herds winter at lower elevations and into the Front Range; written that way.
