# Navigation and polish audit, 22 October 2026: what was done

For James. This is the reply to "Inside the Towns: Navigation & Polish
Audit" (22 October 2026), item by item against its prioritised list. Every
item has been handled. Two of them had a detail I left for you, and both
are under "Left for you" at the end.

## 1. Clear the stale Berthoud home

Nothing needed purging. On 22 October, insideberthoud.com/ and
insidelyons.com/ served the flagship layout, and the Spartans and Lions
links were in the menu. The responses carried `server: Vercel` and
`x-vercel-cache` headers and no Cloudflare headers, so there was no proxy
holding an old copy. The stale page the audit saw had cleared by the next
deploy.

## 2. One menu on every guide, with "sports" in the label

- **The menu is a rule** in `src/lib/nav.ts`; the town configs no longer
  have their own lists.
  - The items are: This week · Events · *Mascot* sports · Eat & drink ·
    Families or Things to do · Living here or Moving here.
  - A guide gets the sports, Families and Living here items when it has
    those pages. The towns' `nav` arrays and `DEFAULT_TOWN_NAV` are gone.
- **Lyons** has "Lions sports" in the menu.
- **Loveland** says "High school sports".
- **Carbon Valley** says "Frederick sports" (`SchoolSports.navLabel`).
- **The masthead is the same everywhere.** Each guide renders one list:
  - the flagships show it as pills;
  - the others show it in small capitals;
  - below 80rem every guide has the same menu button.
- **The footer** lists the sections that are not in the menu.
- **Sentence case** is used for every section name.

## 3. One home recipe

- **Both homes open the same way:**
  - the county;
  - the town as the page's one h1;
  - what the guide is for (`home.purpose` on the flagships);
  - the week's games beside that (`SportsStrip`).
- **Then the shared This week block.** A quiet week leads with "Next on
  the calendar", and the empty-week line shows only when nothing later is
  listed.
- **The classic home:**
  - drops the "What's on" grid that repeated the week;
  - moves "Start here" and the email below the content;
  - leaves "independent" to the footer.

## 4. Sports page: upcoming first, season collapsed, one source line

- **Next up** is the soonest game of any team (`bigGame`). Niwot's
  Thursday soccer now leads, not Friday's football.
- **Each team** shows its next games first, or its last three results
  once the season is over. The whole season sits behind "Show all N
  games" (`SeasonTable`).
- **One source line at the top**, e.g. "Scores from MaxPreps, read
  Oct 21, 2026; dates checked against the school calendar".
- **Removed:**
  - the per-team "updated" lines;
  - the record-count notes;
  - the empty cross country line;
  - the closing method paragraph.
- **Kept:**
  - the held-games line;
  - a team's own read date, shown only when it is more than three days
    older than the rest.
- **The foot of the page** is one sentence and a link to /editorial/.
- **On a phone:**
  - three score cards instead of six;
  - the h1 is 2.25rem, so "Resurrection Christian School sports" takes
    two lines instead of three.
- **Duplicate softball results** (the same day, opponent and score under
  two MaxPreps ids) are shown once (`dedupeResults`). The reader will stop
  writing them on its next run. I did not hand-edit the data files.

    rather than the check date.
