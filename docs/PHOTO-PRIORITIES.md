# Photography priorities for the three flagship guides

15 October 2026. The pages below need an original photograph most, on the
three guides getting concentrated effort. None of these photographs exists
yet; this is the shot list, not a record. No stock or generated image is to
be used (docs/PHOTOS.md, and the owner's standing rule). Every photograph
that is made needs a row in `IMAGE_LICENSES.csv` and, where someone else
took it, their wording in `PERMISSIONS.md`.

Ranking, in this order: a page that already draws search visits (Vercel
Web Analytics, 19 September to 15 October 2026; Search Console is not yet
connected, so impressions are unknown), a page the home page features
(`featured: true`), a downtown district or a major park or trail, the Moving
Here guide, a business that could plausibly buy a placement, and the rest of
the home page's links. Alt text is written for the reader who cannot see the
picture: what is in the frame, where, and when it was taken.
