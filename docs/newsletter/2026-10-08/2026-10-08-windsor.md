**Inside Windsor** · October 8, 2026

The Community Recreation Center turns ten on Friday, with free admission all day, donuts at 7:30 and cookies at 3:30; the Mother Son Dance is there that evening. Saturday has the craft fair at Eagle's Peak, the Treasure Island clean-up with lunch provided, and the History Museum after dark with Paranormal Windsor. This is the first issue: Inside Windsor went up this week, and everything on it is new.
