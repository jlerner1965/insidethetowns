**Inside Fort Collins** · October 8, 2026

FoCo Fall Fest takes Old Town Square from Friday. On Saturday the Giant Pumpkin Weigh-Off is at Fort Collins Nursery from 10 and Community Cue, the barbecue competition, at New Belgium from noon. Soapstone has two outings: black-footed ferrets on Friday night and the Smithsonian's Lindenmeier dig on Saturday morning, both free with registration. The Legend of Sleepy Hollow opens at the Lincoln Center's Magnolia Theatre on Friday; opening night is sold out, with eight performances to follow. This is the first issue: Inside Fort Collins went up this week, and everything on it is new.
