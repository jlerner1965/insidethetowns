**Inside Erie** · October 8, 2026

The Westerly Fall Festival on Saturday afternoon is free and open to anyone, bluegrass band, hayrides and food trucks included. The library has paella with Chef Steve and a T-shirt alteration class the same afternoon. Before the weekend, on Thursday evening, the Town takes residents' questions on its license plate reader cameras at Town Hall, 6 to 8, in person or over Zoom.
