**Inside Lyons** · October 8, 2026

A full weekend. Second Gear Bluegrass plays the Rock Garden on Friday evening and the piano benefit is at the Community Church at 7. Saturday has Old DogAPalooza at the Rock Garden from noon and the library's seed swap at the same hour. The library's ukulele group has moved to the library's own dates; the next session is Tuesday the 13th at 6:30.
