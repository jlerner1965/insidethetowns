## Worth reading

- [Explore Grand Lake: seven places in walking order](https://insidegrandlake.com/articles/explore-grand-lake-in-walking-order/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Coffee before the 2:30 kitchen cutoff, the boardwalk shops, Town Square Park, two history museums in their summer season, the lakefront beach, and dinner before a show, all on foot from the Lakefront Park lot. Adams Falls and the park's visitor center are a short drive.
- [Grand Lake history: from a log hotel to a motor court](https://insidegrandlake.com/articles/grand-lake-history/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Kauffman House and Cottage Court put two eras of travel beside the lake: a log hotel and an early motor court. Their museums are seasonal; the downtown walk between them is not.
- [Grand Lake with kids: the waterfront, a library and one planned outing](https://insidegrandlake.com/articles/grand-lake-with-kids/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — A family day by the lake and downtown, with Juniper Library as the indoor option and one larger outing: the Adams Falls walk or a show at Rocky Mountain Repertory Theatre, each checked ahead.
