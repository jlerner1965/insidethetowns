## New on the guides

- **Windsor** [Blue Spruce Park](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/blue-spruce-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — A quiet park in the Timber Ridge subdivision built around a stocked pond with a fountain, where licensed anglers may fish; picnic tables, benches, shade trees and lawn.
- **Windsor** [Karen Suman Park](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/karen-suman-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Neighborhood park in the Timber Ridge subdivision, at Timber Ridge Parkway and Pinon Pine Drive, named for Karen Suman, mayor from 1998 to 2000; picnic shelters and tables and a Play-On! playground.
- **Windsor** [Vecinos Mexican Grill & Cantina](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/vecinos-mexican-grill-and-cantina/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Mexican grill and cantina on West 4th Avenue whose menu centers on green-chili-smothered burritos, chimichangas and enchilada platters, with separate breakfast and drink menus; closed Mondays, with delivery and pickup.
- **Windsor** [Windsong Estate Event Center](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/windsong-estate-event-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Family-run wedding and event venue on Saddler Boulevard, in business since 2013, with indoor and outdoor spaces for up to 300 guests and views of the Rocky Mountains; weekend tours by appointment.

## Closed
