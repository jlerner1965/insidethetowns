## New on the guides

- **Idaho Springs** [Indian Hot Springs](https://insideidahosprings.com/places/indian-hot-springs/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — The town's hot springs resort since the 1860s: a mineral-water swimming pool under a plant-filled dome, private baths, outdoor tubs, mud baths and the vapor caves, with a hotel and a spa, on Soda Creek Road at the edge of downtown.
- **Idaho Springs** [Starbucks — Colorado Boulevard](https://insideidahosprings.com/places/starbucks-colorado-boulevard/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — A café and drive-through at the east end of Idaho Springs, with indoor and outdoor seating, Wi-Fi, warmed food and nitro cold brew; it opens earlier Friday through Sunday.
- **Idaho Springs** [Uplift Inn](https://insideidahosprings.com/places/uplift-inn/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Suites on Colorado Boulevard with keyless self check-in. Check-in is after 4 pm and check-out by 10 am; parking is first come, first served, with one car per room.
- **Georgetown** [Cooper's on the Creek](https://insidegeorgetownco.com/places/coopers-on-the-creek/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Locally owned restaurant on Argentine Street with small and large plates centered on wild game and elevated comfort food, Colorado craft beer, and a deck over Clear Creek. Closed Wednesday.
- **Georgetown** [Kneisel & Anderson](https://insidegeorgetownco.com/places/kneisel-and-anderson/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Specialty and imported food shop on 6th Street, which the Town's directory calls the oldest store in Georgetown, with Scandinavian, German, English, Swiss and Swedish foods.
- **Georgetown** [Microtel Inn & Suites by Wyndham Georgetown Lake](https://insidegeorgetownco.com/places/microtel-georgetown-lake/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — A hotel beside Georgetown Lake with an indoor pool and hot tub, breakfast, Wi-Fi, parking and round-the-clock service. Dogs are allowed for a fee, but not in suites.
