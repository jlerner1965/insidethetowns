## New on the guide

- [Discovery Lodge](https://insideestespark.com/places/discovery-lodge/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — Lodging on Big Thompson Avenue with a hot tub, breakfast and outdoor gathering areas. Check-in is after 4 pm and check-out at 11 am; the front desk closes earlier outside peak summer.
- [Estes Valley Community Center](https://insideestespark.com/places/estes-valley-community-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — The recreation district’s community center: lap and leisure pools, a gymnasium, weight and cardio equipment, fitness classes, sports for all ages and rooms to rent. Pool schedules differ from the building’s hours.
- [Silver Moon Inn](https://insideestespark.com/places/silver-moon-inn/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-15) — A family-run inn on Fall River close to downtown, with continental breakfast, a hot tub and evening s’mores. Check-in is 4–9 pm; upper-floor rooms are reached by stairs.
