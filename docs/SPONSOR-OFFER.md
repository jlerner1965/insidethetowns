# The founding sponsor offer

Drafted 20 October 2026 for the 90-day test (docs/90-DAY-TEST.md). The
price is the owner's to set; the figure below is the assistant's proposal
and is marked as such until the owner replaces it. Nothing here is on a
site: `hub.rates` stays unset, and `/advertise/` keeps its founding-offer
copy, until a sponsor has paid.

## What is sold

One labeled sponsor line per town, in two places:

- The Thursday email, under the weekend listings: one line, the sponsor's
  name, one sentence, one link. Labeled "Sponsor".
- The town site, the same line on the events page and the home page.
  Labeled the same way.

One sponsor per town at a time. Three-month minimum. The listings stay
editorial; a sponsor's own listing is checked like any other and says
nothing it would not say unsponsored.

## What the sponsor gets each month

A one-page report: editions sent, subscribers at the end of the month,
opens, clicks on the sponsor line, and any enquiry that came through the
site's contact form naming them. Numbers from Buttondown and Vercel; no
estimates, no promised lead volume.

## Price

**Proposed, not set: $200 a month, $600 for the three months, paid up
front.** Founding rate: it holds for the sponsor as long as they renew
without a gap, whatever the rate card says later. The owner sets the number.

## The phone pitch, three sentences

"I run Inside [Town], the events guide and the Thursday email for the town.
I'm taking one sponsor per town, one labeled line in the email and on the
site, three months at a founding rate, with a plain monthly report of what
it did. I'm asking you first because [specific reason: you're on the
listings people search for most / you told me you wanted to reach new
residents / you asked about the site]."

Then stop talking. The objection is the data.

## Who to ask first, from the search data

The businesses people already search for on each guide, from Search
Console, 20 September to 17 October 2026 (docs/archive/SEARCH-CONSOLE-2026-10-20.md):

| Town | Searched by name | Why they would care |
|---|---|---|
| Niwot | Cimmini's, Inkberry Books, Niwot Market, Old Oak Coffeehouse, Sachi Sushi | Their names bring readers to the site already |
| Lyons | Mainstage Brewing, Oskar Blues, A Lodge, Wee Casa | Mainstage is the most-searched Lyons business on the guide |
| Berthoud | Arcana House, the poke place, the businesses around Oktoberfest | Berthoud has the network's largest audience |

A realtor is the other candidate in every town: the Moving Here page is the
highest-intent page a town guide has, and a realtor sponsorship of that
page is a second product once the first one sells.
