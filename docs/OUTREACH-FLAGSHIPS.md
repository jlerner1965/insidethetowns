# Outreach checklist: Lyons, Niwot and Golden

**Human partnership tasks.** Everything on this page is work for a person:
an email, a phone call, a meeting. None of it has been done. No contact
below has been written to, nothing has been agreed, and no organisation has
linked to a guide as far as anyone has checked. The organisations and URLs
are the ones the guides already cite as sources (docs/BACKLINKS.md, verified
20 September 2026; each URL below answered a request on 15 October 2026
unless marked). Named people are not listed because none has been spoken to.

What to ask for, in order of worth: a link from an "about town" or
"community links" page (the library and the municipality are worth most, the
oldest and least commercial domains); permission to list their calendar as a
confirmed source (`npm run sources -- confirm <town> <id>`); distribution of
the Thursday roundup to their members or readers; and, where they publish a
calendar, an embeddable one of ours on their site. docs/BACKLINKS.md has the
draft email and the two domains never to write to.
