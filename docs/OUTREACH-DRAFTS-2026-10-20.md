# Outreach drafts, 20 October 2026: the first link asks

**Drafts only. Nothing here has been sent.** James sends from his own
account, one at a time, after reading each. The relationships skill
(`.claude/skills/relationships/`) would keep these in its memory store; that
store is not mounted in the session that wrote them, so they sit here until
they are sent and logged.

Why these seven first: docs/OUTREACH-FLAGSHIPS.md, "Order of work", step 2.
Each organisation below has already said yes to something (PERMISSIONS.md),
so the email picks up a thread rather than opening one, and the ask is small:
a link from a page they already keep. Nobody is asked for anything in
exchange for a listing (docs/BACKLINKS.md, "What not to do"), and no two of
these go to organisations that share a board.

Before sending any of them: open the organisation's site and confirm the
page named in the email still exists, re-count the citations
(`grep -rl '<domain>' content/<town>/ | wc -l`), and check that the
hello@ address signs the email from a mailbox that receives (mail routing
was set up for every domain on 10 October 2026; DECISIONS.md).

The standing links page the sites can offer in return is `/credits/`, which
already names every one of these organisations as a rights holder, linked.
That is a fact to mention, not a trade.
