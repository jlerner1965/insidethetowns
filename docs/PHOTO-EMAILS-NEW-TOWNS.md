# Photo emails for the towns that had none

10 November 2026. 72 messages for the eleven guides that had no photo
requests written: Castle Rock, Estes Park, Fort Collins, Frederick, Golden,
Lafayette, Leadville, Longmont, Loveland, Nederland and Windsor (with
Severance). The other towns' messages are in
`docs/archive/emails/ALL-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`. Fort Collins' parks, natural areas
and CSU (E1 to E3) and Loveland's Benson Sculpture Garden request are there or
in `LONGMONT-LOVELAND-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`, and are not repeated here.

Drafts only: nothing has been sent and no form submitted. Organizations with no
email to be found are in `docs/PHOTO-NO-CONTACT.md`.

## How these were written

- Every address was read on 10 November 2026 on the organization's own site or
  an official government page, and each message says where (**Found on**). A
  few were read from a page's protected-email code or a site's public feed;
  those messages say so. Where an address could not be confirmed, the message
  says that instead of giving one.
- Each message names the listings it is about, from the guide's own files.
  Counts of business listings leave out town halls, police, chambers and
  similar rows filed as "service"; each file's note says which.
- The money line is the one in `docs/PHOTOS.md`: nobody pays to be listed or
  placed higher, and any sponsorship is labeled and kept apart.
- Individual businesses get no message here. The trade-body message asks the
  chamber or downtown group to pass the ask on; the one-to-one wording is in
  `docs/PHOTOS.md`, section 2.

## Before you send

1. Send from your own address. The text names `hello@<the town's domain>`,
   which forwards to you.
2. Sign each one: every message ends with `<name>`.
3. Where a town has two trade bodies, or one inbox gets two messages
   (Boulder County gets Longmont-4, Lafayette-6 and the optional Nederland
   one), send them a week apart or merge them. Each message's note says so.
4. When a photograph comes back, add its row to `IMAGE_LICENSES.csv` and the
   permission to `PERMISSIONS.md` before it goes on a page, and keep the
   reply (`ALL-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`, "When a photograph comes back").
