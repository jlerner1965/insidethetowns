# Berthoud: getting the photographs

Written 9 November 2026 after the audit of that day found 51 of 58 places,
84 of 85 events and 5 of 7 articles without a photograph. The free sources
are exhausted (`docs/PHOTOS.md` lists every search already made; do not
repeat them). The long-term answer is a library the guide owns: one paid
shoot, a re-shoot each year, and one photograph whenever a place opens.

Nothing here has been sent. The emails are drafts.

## What changed on the site the same day

- The Carter Lake article and Berthoud with kids carry photographs that
  were already in the repository.
- An event with no photograph of its own shows its venue's, captioned
  "The venue, <name>", where the venue is a building and its photograph may
  be used beyond its own listing. A place can opt out with
  `imageListingOnly: true`; the library's photograph does, because the
  library's permission covers its listing only (`PERMISSIONS.md`).
- Today that gives the Recreation Center's events a photograph. One
  photograph of City Star Brewing would cover 35 event pages.

least 1,600 px on the long edge, mid-morning or late afternoon, no
recognisable faces. Interiors only with the owner's yes.

## The photographer: a short ad

> **Paid half-day shoot in Berthoud.** Inside Berthoud, the independent
> local guide at insideberthoud.com, needs storefront photographs of about
> 45 downtown businesses and parks, from a written list in walking order.
> A phone or any camera is fine; daylight, landscape, the sign in the
> frame. One afternoon, paid on delivery, with a photo credit on the site
> if you want one. A good fit for a Berthoud High photography student or a
> local hobbyist. Reply to hello@insideberthoud.com with two or three
> photographs you have taken.

## The agreement, in plain words

A draft for the two of you to sign, not legal advice; have a lawyer read it
if the amount is large.

> **Photography agreement.** [Photographer] will photograph the places on
> the attached list in Berthoud, Colorado, and deliver the files by [date].
> Lerner Works will pay [amount] on delivery.
>
> [Photographer] grants Lerner Works a permanent, worldwide, exclusive
> licence to use, crop and publish the photographs in any Inside the Towns
> guide, email, social post or printed piece, and to let the pictured
> businesses use the photograph of their own premises. [Photographer]
> keeps the copyright and may show the photographs in a portfolio.
>
> Lerner Works will credit "[name as they want it]" where a credit is
> shown, unless [Photographer] prefers none.
>
> [Photographer] took the photographs personally, from public places or
> with the owner's permission, and no one else holds rights in them.
>
> Signed and dated by both.

If the photographer is under 18, a parent or guardian signs as well.

## The five emails

Each from hello@insideberthoud.com. Record every yes in `PERMISSIONS.md`
and keep the email; a permission that cannot be produced is not one.

**1. City Star Brewing** (the one that matters most)

> Subject: A photograph for your Inside Berthoud listing
>
> Hello. I run Inside Berthoud, the free local guide at insideberthoud.com.
> City Star has a listing on it, and your trivia, cribbage, social run,
> releases and music nights are on our calendar every week, each checked
> against your own events page. Nobody pays to be listed.
>
> None of those pages has a photograph. Could you send one of the taproom
> or the beer garden that you hold the rights to, and say that we may
> publish it on your listing and on the pages for events held at City
> Star? Tell us how to credit it. We will take it down the moment you ask.
>
> The listings stay either way.

**2. Berthoud Community Library**

> Subject: Using your photograph on the library's event pages
>
> Thank you again for the photograph of the library sign, which is on the
> library's listing with your credit. We also list your storytimes, book
> club and other programs, about fifteen pages, each checked against your
> Events & Classes page. May we show the same photograph on those pages,
> captioned as the venue, with the same credit? If you would rather we did
> not, nothing changes.

**3. Wildfire Community Arts Center** and **4. Berthoud Historical Society**

> Subject: A photograph for your Inside Berthoud listing

## Keeping it going

- A new place gets its photograph the week it is listed.
- One walk each autumn re-shoots anything that changed its sign.
- Three or four event shoots a year (the Parade of Lights, Oktoberfest,
  the Saturday market, the Blues Bash) give the annual listings a
  photograph that is reused every year.
