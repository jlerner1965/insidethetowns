# Photo requests with no email to send to

10 November 2026. The companion to `docs/PHOTO-EMAILS-NEW-TOWNS.md` and
`docs/archive/emails/ALL-PHOTO-EMAILS.md`: everyone we would ask for a
photograph but could not find an email address for. Each needs a phone call, a
contact form, a visit, or a browser check of a site that refused automated
reading. Nothing here has been sent.

When one of these turns up an address, add it to the town's message in
`PHOTO-EMAILS-NEW-TOWNS.md` and take the row out of here.
