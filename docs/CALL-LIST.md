# The call list

Listings the weekly rotation could not check online. The rule is that
hours, phone and address come from the business's own site; for these,
that was not possible. What to ask is in the last column. Remove a row
once its listing carries a `verified` date after the one it was added on
(3 October 2026 for every row here); tell Claude what you heard, in any
form, and the listing is updated with that date.
