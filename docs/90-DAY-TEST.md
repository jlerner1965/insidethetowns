# The 90-day test: Niwot, Lyons, Berthoud

Decided 20 October 2026 (DECISIONS.md, "The 90-day test"). Three guides,
one product (the Thursday email), one proof (a sponsor who renews). The
clock starts with the first automated Thursday send, 22 October 2026 if that
pull request is merged; day 90 is 20 January 2027.

Every cell is filled from the named source or left `unknown`; nothing is
estimated. The assistant fills the search columns each Monday from Search
Console (through GSC Wizard). The owner supplies subscribers, opens, sends
and sponsor status; Buttondown has the first three.

## Targets at day 90

| Evidence | Niwot | Lyons | Berthoud |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consecutive weekly editions | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Confirmed subscribers | 200 | 150 | 150 |
| An institution sharing the email | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Paid sponsor pilots (network) | 2 | | |
| Renewals (network) | 1 | | |

Workload cap: ten hours a week across everything. Over the cap two weeks
running is itself a finding.

## The decision, 20 January 2027

- A renewal and two of three subscriber targets: repeat the model in Golden
  and Erie.
- Readers but no sponsor: fix the offer, with a budget and a deadline.
- Neither: the network stays live on automation as a portfolio and
  community project; it stops being run as a business.
