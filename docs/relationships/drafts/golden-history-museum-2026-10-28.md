I run Inside Golden (https://insidegolden.com), an independent local guide to Golden: an events calendar, a directory of places and things to do, and articles about how the town works.

The museum runs all through the guide. The guide's short history of Golden (https://insidegolden.com/articles/golden-history/) opens on your Indigenous Connections ethnographic study, the Clear Creek corridor and the 12,000 years. The museum and the History Park have their own listings, and your events page is where I read the Donut Dollies talk on November 4. All told, the guide links to goldenhistory.org in 43 places. I wanted to introduce myself rather than keep citing you anonymously.

Two things, and you're welcome to ignore either one:

Your Resources we trust page links to the sites you use for your own research. If you think the guide's history page earns a place there, I'd be glad to be considered. And if it gets anything wrong, about Golden's history or about the museum, please tell me and I'll correct it the same day.

The site is new, so I have no audience figures to offer you. What I can offer is that your events will be listed accurately whether or not you link back. Anyone can submit one at https://insidegolden.com/submit-event/.

Thank you,
