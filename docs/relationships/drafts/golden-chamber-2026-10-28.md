Subject: Inside Golden — introducing myself to the Chamber

Hello,

I run Inside Golden (https://insidegolden.com), an independent local guide to Golden: an events calendar, a directory of places and things to do, and articles about how the town works.

The Chamber is already on it. The guide's Get Involved page (https://insidegolden.com/get-involved/) lists Golden Young Professionals and your volunteer sign-up for the Farmers Market, the Fine Arts Festival and Olde Golden Holidays, and your events calendar is one of the sources I check when I update the guide's calendar. All told, the guide links to goldenchamber.org in 14 places. I wanted to introduce myself rather than keep citing you anonymously.

One ask, which you're welcome to ignore: if there's a place on goldenchamber.org for community resources, next to the Visiting Golden link in your menu or on the Business Resources page, I'd be glad to be considered for it.

And one offer: if anything on the guide about the Chamber or a member business is wrong, tell me and I'll correct it the same day. Chamber events go on the calendar whether or not you link back, and anyone can submit one at https://insidegolden.com/submit-event/.

The site is new, so I have no audience figures to offer you.

Thank you,
James Lerner
Inside Golden · hello@insidegolden.com
