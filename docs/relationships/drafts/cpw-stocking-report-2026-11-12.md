# Draft: courtesy email to Colorado Parks and Wildlife, stocking report

Not sent. For the owner to send from his own address. CPW's site lists no
contact for web or data questions on the stocking report page; the general
contact page is https://cpw.state.co.us/contact-us , and the media kit page
(https://cpw.state.co.us/news-and-media/media-kits) names the public
information office. Pick the one you prefer; the address is not filled in
here because none was confirmed for this purpose.

**To:** (CPW public information office, or the contact form)
**Subject:** Linking to the trout stocking report from a Colorado town guide

Hello,

I publish Inside the Towns (insidethetowns.com), an independent set of
community guides to Colorado towns. On a small number of town pages we now
show a short block titled "Recently stocked near <Town>", drawn from your
public trout stocking report at
https://cpw.state.co.us/fishing/stocking-report.

What we show, for each water near that town:

- the water's name;
- the date it was added to your report, worded "Added to the report", never
  "stocked on", with your note that the stocking may have been earlier in
  that week;
- a link to the water in the Fishing Atlas and a link to your report, named
  as the source, with the date we last read it.

We copy no text, photographs or other media, and we read the page twice a
week with a single request. The block comes down by itself when the report
has had nothing new for three weeks.

I am writing to let you know, and to ask whether you are content for us to
carry on this way. If you would rather we credit the report differently,
read it less often, use another source for it, or not show it at all, tell
me and I will change it.

An example page: https://insidethetowns.com/grand-lake/

Thank you for publishing the report.

James Lerner
Inside the Towns · Lerner Works
hello@insidethetowns.com
