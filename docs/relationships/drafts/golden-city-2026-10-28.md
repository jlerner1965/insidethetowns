# Draft: first email to the City of Golden

Drafted 28 October 2026. Not sent. James sends it from his own account. Nobody
from the guide has written to the City before.

**Check before sending**

- **Where it goes.** The address is `communications@cityofgolden.gov`
  (Communications, 303-384-8000). It is on the department list at
  https://www.cityofgolden.gov/services/contact_us.php. Confirm it there.
- **One relationship.** Visit Golden (the City's marketing arm), the Golden
  History Museum (City-run) and the DDA (a City board with no site of its own)
  are all the City. This email covers Visit Golden and the DDA. The museum gets
  its own email (golden-history-museum-2026-10-28.md), a week or more after this
  one.
- **The figure.** 184 is the Cited count in docs/BACKLINKS.md: the links to
  `cityofgolden.gov` (and its old `cityofgolden.net`) on pages the guide serves on 28 October (the editor's source
  list, staged files and ended events left out). The number moves as
  listings come and go; recount before sending.
- **The items named.** The wildfire-plan open house is on 9 November and
  Cookies with Councilors on 5 December. If the email goes out after 9
  November, drop the open house. The scam alert is the City's of 9 October; the
  guide published it on 28 October.
- **The scam alert.** The City has just warned residents about emails that
  impersonate its staff and ask for payments. This email asks for nothing of
  the kind. Keep it to the one link to the site, with no attachments.
- **A question you may get.** The City's own pages disagree on their counts.
  The New Resident Guide says 21 parks and over 24 miles of City-maintained
  trails, while the guide's Living here page gives 19 parks and 42 miles of
  paved trails "as the City counts them" (from the City's Parks & Trails page,
  read 28 October). Leave it out of the email, but expect the question.
- No audience, traffic or subscriber numbers are in it. Keep it that way.

Subject: Inside Golden — a question about your New Resident Guide

Hello,

I run Inside Golden (https://insidegolden.com), an independent local guide to Golden: an events calendar, a directory of places and things to do, and articles about how the town works.

The City's pages are the guide's backbone. The guide's Living here page (https://insidegolden.com/living-here/) is built from them, from Pay-As-You-Throw and the Ore Cart to how to speak at Council. Your calendar is where I read the Council meetings, the wildfire-plan open house and Cookies with Councilors. And the first local update the guide published was your October 9 scam alert. All told, the guide links to the City's site in 184 places. I wanted to introduce myself rather than keep citing you anonymously.

Two things, and you're welcome to ignore either one:

The New Resident Guide on cityofgolden.gov points newcomers to VisitGolden.com and the Golden Library. If there's room there, or on another page where the City lists local resources, I'd be glad to be considered. And if anything the guide says about City services is wrong, please tell me and I'll correct it the same day.

The site is new, so I have no audience figures to offer you. What I can offer is that City events will be listed accurately whether or not you link back. Anyone can submit one at https://insidegolden.com/submit-event/.

Thank you,
James Lerner
Inside Golden · hello@insidegolden.com
