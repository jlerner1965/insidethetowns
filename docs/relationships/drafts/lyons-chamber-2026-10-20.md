Subject: Inside Lyons, the Thursday email, and a concrete idea

Hi [name],

Thanks again for the conversation [when]. One concrete thing came out of it
for me, and I'd rather put it in writing than let it drift.

Every Thursday I send a short Inside Lyons email: what's on this weekend,
what's on next week, and anything that opened, closed or changed in town.
It's built from the same listings as insidelyons.com, every one checked
against the organizer's own page. This week's is here: [link to the
/newsletter/ archive page]. No ads in the listings, ever.

The ask: would the Chamber send it to its members, or link it from the
members' newsletter, each week? In return, three things from me:

1. Every Chamber member business gets its listing on the site checked and
   kept current, with its own photo if you can get me one, at no cost.
2. Chamber events go in the email and on the events page the week you
   confirm them. You can send them to me directly or I read them off the
   Chamber's page, whichever is less work for you.
3. If a Lyons business wants to sponsor the email, the Chamber takes a
   share of that. One sponsor line a week, clearly labeled, three-month
   minimum. I'd rather the Chamber bring the sponsor and keep part of it
   than cold-call businesses myself.

If that's worth a short call, I can do [two options]. If it's not the right
fit, tell me what would be; the site is here either way and I'd like it to
be useful to the Chamber.

James
Inside Lyons · insidelyons.com
