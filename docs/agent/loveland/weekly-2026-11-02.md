### Pick: how it was checked
- **Builder's Ball, Sat Nov 7** is the pick because it is the only listing in the window where date, time, venue and cost are all sourced.
  - Date and time: Saturday, Nov. 7, at Ellis Ranch, 5 to 9 p.m., with dinner, beer and wine, live and silent auctions, and a paddle raise.
  - Music: Nashville artist KC Johns plays live, and guests are asked to wear boots and Western attire.
  - Cost: the NOCO Style and North Forty News stories both give tickets at $90 (secure.qgiv.com/event/buildersball/). North Forty News credits the information to Loveland Habitat for Humanity.
  - Date only, from the Chamber: the Chamber events calendar lists the 10th Annual Builder's Ball on Saturday Nov 7, 2026.
  - Sources are local news and the Chamber calendar. I did not read Habitat's own site or ticket page. The Habitat Facebook page only showed in search results, with a sponsor thank-you for the 10th Builder's Ball.

Every source I read puts the event at Ellis Ranch, so the pick body says Ellis Ranch. If the listing says the Ranch complex, it needs fixing.
