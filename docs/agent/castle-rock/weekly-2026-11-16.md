## Editor's notes

*For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.*

**Pick.** Starlighting is the only listing in the Nov. 16–22 window that the Town, the Chamber and the county listing all carry. The Town calendar and the Chamber page (castlerock.org, updated Nov. 9) both give Sat. Nov. 21, 2–6 p.m., Wilcox Square and Festival Park, ceremony at 5 p.m. on the Main Stage at 4th and Wilcox, Star at about 5:30 with fireworks. Visit Castle Rock and Live Well Douglas County agree. Free admission and "cashless" are from the Chamber page.

**Headline facts and where they come from**
- Starlighting: the Chamber and Town pages above.
- Carriage rides from Sun. Nov. 22 and the free winter trolley from Mon. Nov. 23: both are existing guide listings. downtowncastlerock.com/2026events (Downtown Alliance) shows carriage rides Wed–Sun, 11/22/26–1/3/27, ticket required. It also shows free winter trolley rides Mondays and Tuesdays, 5:30–7:30 p.m.
- Town Council Tuesday, Nov. 17, 6 p.m., Town Hall Council Chambers, 100 N. Wilcox St.: Town calendar.

**Starlighting details if you want them on the event page** (Chamber page)
- Santa is in Festival Park 2:00–4:15 p.m.
- The Biff Gore Band plays the Main Stage 2:00–4:45 p.m.
- The Kids Zone is on 4th Street just west of Wilcox.
- The Firefighters' 90th Annual Chili Supper is at Fire Station 151.
- Free shuttles run 1:00–8:00 p.m. from three lots: AdventHealth Castle Rock (Alexander lot, 2352 Meadows Blvd.), Douglas County High School south building (2842 Front St.), and Douglas County Fairgrounds Lot 5 (500 Fairgrounds Dr.). Pickup is at 5th and Wilcox, or between South and Phelps streets.
- Garages: Encore (Perry and South) and the Douglas County garage (3rd and Jerry).

**Changes.** Dunkin' and Peak Pizza are not in `changes`. See the unverified items below.

**Town news worth a listing or update file** (all from crgov.com calendar and news pages)
- Town Council meets Tue. Nov. 17, 6 p.m. There is also a notice for a rescheduled Council public hearing on the Crystal Valley Ranch Single Family Neighborhood proposed Site Development Plan, on the same date at Town Hall. The notice text itself was not readable.
- Neighborhood Meeting No. 3, Dawson Trails Apartments, Planning Area D, proposed Site Development Plan: Mon. Nov. 16, 6–7 p.m., Coff33, 20 Wilcox St., Suite 111C.
- Neighborhood Meeting No. 3, Fire Station 152 wireless cellular facility, proposed Use by Special Review: Thu. Nov. 19, 6–7 p.m., Town Hall.
- Design Review Board special meeting Wed. Nov. 18, 6 p.m. Parks and Recreation Commission Wed. Nov. 18; I found no time.
- Election: the Town's Nov. 4 news flash (crgov.com/m/newsflash/home/detail/2670) says unofficial results show Laura Cavey for Mayor, Matt Smith for District 3 and Mitchell Sawin for District 5. Results are certified by Nov. 25. Council then has 30 days to fill the District 2 vacancy. It is a candidate for an `updates` file (kind `decision` or `notice`); I did not draft one.
- Festival Park Starlight Market is Dec. 4 and 5 (Town calendar, featured events). The Town calendar entry gave no hours.

**Events found, not yet on the guide**
- Coast2Coast Culinary Thanksgiving meal kit. Order by Thu. Nov. 19; pickup Wed. Nov. 25, 7:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m. at 1605 Park St., Suite C, or delivery for $30 within 15 miles; $285. Source: visitcastlerock.org/events/. It is a business offer, not an event; check Coast2Coast's own page before using it.

- Castle Rock Dance Academy's *The Nutcracker of Castle Rock*, Castle View High School: Dec. 17 and 18 at 6 p.m.; Dec. 19 and 20 at 1 and 6 p.m. Tickets go on sale about 6 weeks before. Source: castlerockdanceacademy.com. Outside the two-week window.
