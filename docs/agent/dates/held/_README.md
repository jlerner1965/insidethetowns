# Read on the source, left off the town pages

These records were read on the organizer's own page on 12 November 2026 and
are sound, but each is a weekly market or a weekly series written as one
season. A hub town page is dates only, with no weekly events (rule 2 of the
restructure brief), so they wait here. To publish one, move it to
`content/hub/traditions/`.

The eight `nps-fee-free-*` records are one per fee-free day of 2026, all now
past. The pages carry one record in their place, `nps-fee-free-days`, which
shows the most recent day (Veterans Day, 11 November 2026) and that the 2027
list is not yet announced; eight rows saying the same thing would crowd a
town's own dates. Split it again once the National Park Service publishes
the 2027 days.
