# Notes: Rocky Mountain National Park shared records (checked 2026-11-12)

All nps.gov pages read with `curl -A "Mozilla/5.0"`. News releases were found through the park's news RSS (https://www.nps.gov/feeds/getNewsRSS.htm?id=romo).

## trail-ridge-road-opens
- 2026: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/trail-ridge-road-opens-for-the-season-timed-entry-reservations-required-for-trail-ridge-road-9-am-to-2-pm.htm — "News Release Date: May 29, 2026 … Trail Ridge Road in Rocky Mountain National Park is open for the season." CAVEAT: the release does not say "opened today"; 29 May 2026 is the release date (a Friday). Confirm before printing "opened May 29".
- 2025: same release: "Trail Ridge Road historically opens in late May; last year the road opened on May 30." The 2025/2026 closure release repeats it: "In 2025, Trail Ridge Road opened on Friday, May 30."
- Also there: "The earliest the road has opened was on May 7, 2002; the latest June 26, 1943."
- 2027: nothing published. Typical window in NPS's words: "historically opens in late May" (also "opens to thru travel during the last week in May, weather permitting").
- Town lines: https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/trail_ridge_road.htm — "Covering the 48 miles between Estes Park on the park's east side and Grand Lake on the west". NPS pages say nothing specific to Lyons, Berthoud, Loveland or Longmont, so those carry no line.

## trail-ridge-road-closes
- 2025: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/trail-ridge-road-is-closed-to-through-travel-for-the-winter-2025-2026-season.htm — "Today, Friday, November 14, 2025, Trail Ridge Road (U.S. Highway 34) inside Rocky Mountain National Park (RMNP) officially closed to through travel for the winter season. Trail Ridge Road is closed on the east side of the park at Many Parks Curve and on the west side of the park at Colorado River Trailhead." "Trail Ridge Road normally closes in mid-October".
- 2026: no season-closure release in the feed as of today (latest release is 26 Oct 2026). The road status page (https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm, "Last updated: November 5, 2026") says "Trail Ridge Road is currently closed to through travel at Many Parks Curve … and at Colorado River Trailhead" but does not say it is closed for the season or since when. Written as 2026 not-announced; recheck the news feed.

## rmnp-timed-entry
- https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm ("Last updated: May 14, 2026"): "Timed Entry reservations are needed to enter Rocky Mountain National Park between the hours of 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. daily beginning on Friday, May 22 through Monday, October 12, 2026." "Timed Entry+ Bear Lake Road Reservations are needed to enter the Bear Lake Road Corridor between the hours of 5 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily beginning on Friday, May 22 through Sunday, October 18, 2026."
- The record's endDate is 18 October (last day any reservation was required); the park-wide 9 am to 2 pm reservation ended 12 October. Editor's choice which to show.
- Monthly release rule: "Beginning on May 1 at 8 a.m. MDT reservations will be available for the dates of May 22 through June 30. On June 1 at 8 a.m. MDT … July 1 - July 31 … On July 1 … August 1 - August 31 … On August 1 … September 1 - September 30 … On September 1 at 8 a.m. MDT reservations will be available for the dates of October 1 - October 18" (each release also includes unreserved days left in the current month). "Additional timed entry reservations will be released on Recreation.gov beginning at 7 p.m. on the night before a desired arrival date"; "40 percent of each day's available reservations are released via Recreation.gov at 7 p.m. MDT on the evening prior".
- Cost, from the release https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/rocky-mountain-national-park-announces-2026-timed-entry-reservation-system.htm (February 18, 2026): "The only cost associated with booking a reservation is a $2 recreation.gov processing fee." The same release words the end as "will end on Monday, Oct. 19 … the rest of the park reservation system will end on Tuesday, Oct. 13", i.e. the day after the last required day.
- 2027: nothing published (the 2026 system was announced on 18 February 2026). recreation.gov was not read.

## estes-park-old-fall-river-road-opens / -closes
- 2025 opening: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/old-fall-river-road-closed-to-all-uses-july-2-through-july-3-will-open-to-vehicles-for-the-season-on-friday-july-4.htm (June 30, 2025): "Old Fall River Road will open for the season to vehicles on Friday morning, July 4. This historic road normally opens to vehicles in early July."
- 2026 opening: no release in the feed; not recorded.
- Closing: https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm (updated November 5, 2026): "Old Fall River Road is closed for the season to vehicles." "Old Fall River Road is a seasonal road open to vehicles each year from early July to early October." "On Saturday, October 17, the road will reopen to bicycles, leashed pets and pedestrians for the winter." No dated vehicle closing is given, so the closing record is a typical window only. (Another NPS page, nps.gov/thingstodo/enjoy-a-scenic-drive-on-old-fall-river-road.htm, says "early July to mid-October".)
