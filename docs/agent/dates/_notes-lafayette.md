(5 Dec 2026 is a Saturday.) The Chamber's annual-events page calls it "Annual Lafayette Holiday Market".
- **lafayette-art-night-out** — https://www.lafayetteco.gov/565/Art-Night-Out : "Every second Friday, May-September, 5-9pm / S. Public Road between Emma and Cannon Streets"; "Thank you for a great 2026 season!"; "Even though the 2027 season is a way off…". 2026 dates from the City's volunteer portal, engage.lafayetteco.gov/PublicActivityVolunteerRegistration/…: "Art Night Out - May 8 / Festival Plaza / Friday, 08 May 2026 @ 05:00 PM to 09:00 PM", likewise "Friday, 12 June 2026", "Friday, 10 July 2026", "Friday, 11 September 2026" (the 14 Aug listing was not opened; it is implied by the rule only). Recorded as first night 8 May, last night 11 Sept.
- **lafayette-peach-festival** — https://www.lafayettecolorado.com/annual-events : listed as "Annual Lafayette Peach Festival", no date for any year on the Chamber's site (page © 2026). 2027 not-announced; no past date recorded because none is on an organizer page (search snippets said Sat 8 Aug 2026; not used).
- **lafayette-independence-day-fireworks** — same page: "Independence Day Fun & Fireworks … the breathtaking fireworks show at dusk!" No date, time or place on the page. lafayetteco.gov/IndependenceDay is a 404.
- **lafayette-waneka-lake-boathouse-closes / -opens** — https://www.lafayetteco.gov/2647/Waneka-Lake-Boathouse : "Every summer (depending on water levels) - rent watercraft for use on Waneka Lake"; "2026 Season Update: Due to low water levels, we have ended rental operations effective Aug. 11 for the remainder of the season. We look forward to getting you back out on the water in summer 2027!" 11 Aug 2026 was an early, low-water closure, not a normal season end. No opening date for any year.
