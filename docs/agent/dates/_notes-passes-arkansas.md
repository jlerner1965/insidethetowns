# Notes: Independence Pass and Cottonwood Pass (read 2026-11-12 by `curl -A "Mozilla/5.0"`)

## Independence Pass (CO 82) — CDOT
- Closes 2026: https://www.codot.gov/news/2026/november2026news/co82-independencepass-close-for-winter (Nov. 3, 2026): "As of Nov. 3, the Colorado Department of Transportation has closed Colorado Highway 82 over Independence Pass between Aspen and Twin Lakes for the 2026-2027 winter season. CDOT originally closed the pass temporarily on Saturday, Oct. 31 ... but have now extended the closure into the seasonal closure". Recorded as 2026-11-03; the road has in fact been shut since Oct. 31.
- Typical windows, same release: "closure gates near Aspen and Twin Lakes will remain closed until CDOT reopens the pass next spring, typically around the Memorial Day holiday weekend, weather permitting." and "Independence Pass typically closes in late Oct. or early Nov., depending on weather conditions." Closure history listed: 2020 Nov. 13, 2021 Oct. 28, 2022 Nov. 4, 2023 Oct. 30, 2024 Oct. 31, 2025 Nov. 17.
- Closes 2025: https://www.codot.gov/news/2025/november/co82-independence-pass-closed-nov17 : "closed ... for the 2025/2026 winter season as of Nov. 17."
- Opens 2026: https://www.codot.gov/news/2026/may2026/independence-pass-open-may21 (May 19, 2026): "will open Independence Pass on Colorado Highway 82 for the season on May 21 at noon". This is an advance release; no after-the-fact confirmation read. Vehicles 35 feet or longer are prohibited.
- Opens 2025: https://www.codot.gov/news/2025/may/independence-pass-open-for-2025-season : "Workers unlocked seasonal closure gates on Thursday, May 22 at 4 p.m." (Thursday checks.)
- 2027 opening and closing: not announced.

## Cottonwood Pass (Chaffee CR 306 / Gunnison CR 209)
- https://www.chaffeecounty.org/Road-and-Bridge ("Cottonwood Pass Closure/Opening Policy"): "Cottonwood Pass will be closed for the Winter Season starting Monday November 2nd, 2026 at 9:00am. Both gates on the Chaffee County and Gunnison County sides will be locked at 9:00am." / "From November 1st until June 15th this road is not maintained beyond Denny Creek Trailhead." / "In the spring signs state that June 15th is the day the pass will open. However, the Road and Bridge Department will try and have Cottonwood Pass open before Memorial Day, depending on snowfall." (Monday checks.)
- https://gunnisoncounty.org/AlertCenter.aspx?CID=7 : alert of May 19, 2026: "Cottonwood Pass WILL BE OPENING Friday, May 22, 2026 at beginning of shift (approximately 7:00 a.m.)."; alert of October 20, 2026: "Cottonwood Pass WILL BE CLOSED Monday, 11/2/26 at 9am." (Friday checks.)
- ODD: a search snippet of a Chaffee County social post said the pass would reopen "May 21st ~5pm"; not read on a county page, so the Gunnison County alert (May 22) is used. The Chaffee page read has no 2026 opening date.
- The `closes` typical window is the county's unmaintained period, the nearest thing the page gives to a usual closing date.
- Town lines: only Buena Vista (CR 306, Denny Creek Trailhead) and Leadville on Independence (gates near Aspen and Twin Lakes) are supported by the pages. Not to be confused with the Eagle County Cottonwood Pass Road near Gypsum.
