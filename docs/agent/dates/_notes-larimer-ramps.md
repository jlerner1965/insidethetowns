**The tables give no year.** They are written as 2026 because the holiday anchors fit only 2026: "May 23 – September 7 (Labor Day weekend)" (Labor Day is 7 Sept in 2026, 6 Sept in 2027) and "April 1 – May 22 (Memorial Day weekend)".
