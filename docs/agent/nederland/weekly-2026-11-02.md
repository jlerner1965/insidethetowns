### Changes
- Included: Nederland Sort Yard closing date. The source is the Boulder County Parks & Open Space release dated Oct. 16, 2026. The county's sort yard page shows the same 2026 schedule (May 7 – Nov. 14, closed Nov. 11): https://bouldercounty.gov/property-and-land/forest-health/community-forestry-sort-yards/
- `expires` is 2026-11-14, the dated closing day, which is sooner than the default 2026-11-16.
- Not included, no owner or official announcement of a change:
  - **Nederland Mining Museum.** The county page lists hours of June 1 through Oct. 31 (Fri–Sun, 11 a.m.–5 p.m.). It does not say the museum is closed for winter, so I did not tag it `closed`. It is probably worth a check with Boulder County Parks & Open Space.
  - **Eldora opening day.** Eldora's own page says only that the 26/27 season is "less than 30 days away", with no date. Ikon Pass lists a tentative Nov. 13 (https://www.ikonpass.com/en/opening-closing-dates). Freeskier says Nov. 12 and Uncover Colorado says an estimated Nov. 27. These conflict and none is from Eldora, so I left it out.
  - **Carousel of Happiness.** The home page says "Stay tuned for special Holiday hours." No dates are given yet.
