## Editor's notes

*For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.*

**Pick.** Small Business Saturday (Nov. 28) is the only pick-eligible listing that is an all-town event. The Main Street events page gives only the date and a general description, with no times and no cost, so the body gives none for the day as a whole.
- The two times in the body are from other pages. City Star's calendar lists the Handmade Market 12:00 to 4:00 pm. The Historical Society lists "Small Business Saturday – Museum Open" 10:00 am to 4:00 pm.
- Fallback picks, all in the Nov. 23–29 window and all confirmed: Town Board meeting, Tue Nov. 24, 6:30 pm, Town Hall (Town calendar). Chai Watchman release, Fri Nov. 27, 3 to 10 pm, City Star (City Star calendar).

**Headline facts and where they come from**
- Thanksgiving break, schools closed Monday through Friday, Nov. 23–27: the BHS calendar (bhs.tsd.org). It is Berthoud High School's calendar, so the headline says only "Berthoud High School."
- Town Board: Tue Nov. 24, 6:30 pm to 9:30 pm, in person at Town Hall, streamed on the Town's YouTube channel (berthoud.org/Calendar.aspx).
- Library closure: the library's 2026 Closures page lists Thursday and Friday, Nov. 26 and 27, and an early close at 6:00 PM on Wednesday, Nov. 25. The library hours page agrees on the Thursday and Friday closure. The events page says it is closed Friday, Nov. 27.
- I took "instead of 8 pm" for Wednesday from the events page's browsing hours (Wed and Thu 10 am to 8 pm).

**[UNVERIFIED] and not used**
- Town Hall closure for Thanksgiving: the only source found is the November 2025 InFocus newsletter, which is last year's. No 2026 notice found. Left out.
- Town's calendar lists a Planning Commission meeting on Thu Nov. 26, which is Thanksgiving Day. It is not on the guide and I could not open the event page. Probably a calendar error; check before listing it.
- Berthoud Main Street's Small Business Saturday page: the search result showed last year's (Nov. 29, 2025) specials. The page was not fetched directly. I did not use any business specials.
- Boys and girls basketball vs Mountain View on Monday, Nov. 30 (MaxPreps data) falls outside this week, so it is not mentioned. The BHS calendar lists only a girls C-team game that Monday, and varsity games vs Timnath on Tuesday, Dec. 1. Worth a look before next week's notes.
- The BHS calendar also lists a girls varsity basketball game at Silver Creek on Tue Nov. 24 (11:30 AM) and wrestling at Broomfield on Fri Nov. 27. Neither is in the sports data, so neither is used.
- Mountain Vista Park bond measure (3G) on the Nov. 3 ballot: Keating Research, an aggregator, shows it failing 40.5% yes to 59.5% no, unofficial. I have no official result and it is older than 10 days, so I did not draft it.
- KDVR (Nov. 18) reported a police-activity shelter-in-place near W. County Road 14 and S. County Road 15H. I could not open the story and found no official follow-up. Not used.
