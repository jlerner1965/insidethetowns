### Why this pick
- The Recreation Center's fifth anniversary (Fri Nov 20, 4–8 p.m.) is on the Town's own News Flash page (posted Oct 22) and on the Town calendar at 4:00–8:00 PM. It is free. The listing's 16:00 start matches.
- Runner-up: the Historical Society talk, Tue Nov 17, 7–9 p.m., free, at the Recreation Center, 1000 Berthoud Parkway. The Society's own event page says it is free and open to everyone. The speakers are the Town's Historic Preservation Advisory Committee and Anne Best Johnson.

### Check before publishing
- **[UNVERIFIED] Town Board study session, Tue Nov 17, 6:30 p.m. (listing `town-board-study-session-2026-11-17`).** The Town calendar (berthoud.org/Calendar.aspx) shows nothing on Nov 17. It shows Town Board meetings on Nov 10 and Nov 24 (6:30 p.m., Town Hall, in person, streamed on the Town's YouTube channel). I did not use the study session in the headline or anywhere else. Please check the listing's source.
- **[UNVERIFIED] Rec Center Thanksgiving hours.** The two Town calendar entries ("Facility Closed," Nov 26 all day; "Facility Hours: 12pm - 6pm," Nov 27) sit in the "Facility Special Hours" category. That category also carries the Recreation Center's lap swim, family swim and spa hours, and the Nov 11 entry shows the regular 5 a.m.–9 p.m. weekday hours. The entries do not name the building. If you want a name on the page, confirm it with the Recreation Center (970-532-1600) or drop this item.
- **Library closure dates.** The library's Hours page says closed Thursday and Friday, Nov 26 and 27, and closing at 6 p.m. on Wed Nov 25. Its Events & Classes page says closed Friday, Nov 27, which agrees. The library's separate "2026 Closures" page showed a Thanksgiving closure on "Nov 27 and 28" in the text I saw. That looks like a leftover from last year, because Nov 28, 2026 is a Saturday and the senior yoga class is listed that day. Please check the library's closures page.
- **Planning Commission, Thu Nov 26, 6 p.m.** This is on the Town calendar, but Nov 26 is Thanksgiving Day. It is not on the guide. It is probably an error on the Town's side. I did not use it.
- **High-school sports.** The Spartans' volleyball game at the State Championships (Denver Coliseum, Thu Nov 12, 8 a.m.) is before the week begins. I put no sports in the notes, since the result will be known by Nov 16. The BHS calendar shows no Spartans games on the calendar between Nov 16 and Nov 22.
