The Town of Berthoud's fifth annual Holiday Lights Contest is open for entries, and it is free to enter. Entries must be received by Monday, December 7 to be eligible for voting, and the home must be inside Town limits. Only the owner or occupant may enter, and entrants must be at least 18. A link to the map and voting page goes live at 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, December 10, and residents can vote through Sunday, December 20 for People's Choice and Brightest Berthoud Business. One random voter wins a $50 gift card to a Berthoud business. Paper lists of entries go to the front desks of Town Hall and the Recreation Center on December 11. Town employee judges choose the other categories, and winners are announced Monday, December 21, with $100 gift cards available for pickup during regular business hours.
