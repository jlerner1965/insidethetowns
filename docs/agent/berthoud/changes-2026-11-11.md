The Town's fifth annual Holiday Lights Contest is open to homes within Town limits with exterior holiday lighting, and it is free to enter. Only the owner or occupant may enter, and entrants must be at least 18. Entries opened October 29 and must be received by Monday, December 7 to be eligible for voting. A map and voting page go live December 10 at 5:00 PM, and residents can vote through December 20 for the People's Choice and Brightest Berthoud Business winners. Anyone who votes is entered to win a $50 gift card to a Berthoud business. Paper lists of entries, with addresses in a driving order, will be at the front desks of Town Hall and the Recreation Center on December 11. Town employee judges choose the other winners on December 21. That day the Town announces winners, and $100 gift cards can be picked up during regular business hours. A home can win only once every three years.

Thompson School District's choice enrollment window for the 2027-28 school year opened November 3 and runs through January 29, 2027. Every student keeps a guaranteed seat at the neighborhood boundary school. Families who live inside the district can apply for a different school, and eligible applications are entered in a lottery. Families who live outside the district can apply for open enrollment from November 3 through August 18, 2027, and those applications are processed after the choice lottery ends. Both start with an online application, and a family with several children files one application. Families offered a seat must accept it within five business days. Applicants not offered a seat are waitlisted, and a student not accepted attends the boundary school. The district says it will not provide transportation to choice or open-enrolled students in almost all circumstances. Choice and open enrollment do not apply to preschool. Questions go to the district's central registration office at 970-613-6877.
