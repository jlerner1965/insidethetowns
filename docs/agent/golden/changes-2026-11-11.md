The Town's legal notice, published Oct. 8, says a proposed annual budget and capital improvements program for fiscal year 2027 has been submitted to City Council. Copies of both are filed with the City Clerk and open for public inspection. The notice lists consideration at regular meetings on Oct. 13 at 7:00 p.m. and Nov. 10 at 6:45 p.m., and adoption at the regular business meeting on Dec. 8 at 6:30 p.m. at Golden City Hall, 911 10th Street. Anyone may file comments at any time before final adoption, at the meetings or in writing to City Council at 911 10th Street, or by email to PublicComments@cityofgolden.gov.
