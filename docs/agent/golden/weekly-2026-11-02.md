**Pick.** Only two existing listings fall between Mon Nov 2 and Sun Nov 8: The Polar Express (Fri Nov 6) and Mineral ID Day (Sat Nov 7). I picked The Polar Express.
- Date, show times, prices and the lap-rider and pre-Thanksgiving discount come from https://coloradorailroadmuseum.org/polar-express/. The museum's events listing also shows evening performances on Nov 7 and Nov 8. I saw that in a search snippet, not on a page I opened.
