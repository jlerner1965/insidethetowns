## Editor's notes

*For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.*

### Why this pick
Trick-or-Treat Street is free, needs no registration, and the chamber (JDDA) page confirms Saturday, Oct. 31, 10 a.m. to noon in Downtown Johnstown. It matches the guide listing at 10:00. The Cemetery Crawl starts at 10 a.m. the same morning, so the two overlap. I did not pick the Crawl because its page contradicts itself on registration (see Unverified).

### The one change, and what I left out
- **Northern Colorado Long Term Acute Hospital:** the source is the hospital's own notice, which I read in a search result. I did not open the page directly because my fetch limit ran out. The notice says the hospital and its pharmacy at 4401A Union St. cease operations as a long-term acute care hospital effective October 1, 2026. It also says the 20 non-ICU beds will be added as inpatient rehabilitation facility beds to Northern Colorado Rehabilitation Hospital at 4401 Union Street, with the date shown as "TBD". The effective date is three weeks old. Drop the item if an earlier week already covered it or the guide has no page for this hospital.
- **Smalls Sliders, 4781 Thompson Pkwy: left out, [UNVERIFIED].** The company's press release says the grand opening was set for Oct. 2 at 10 a.m. The company's own Johnstown location page lists 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily and a (970) 578-4288 phone number. The company's Colorado locations page still shows Johnstown as "Opening Soon" with a different phone number. That conflict could be a stale cache. I found no first-hand confirmation that it is open, and the opening is outside the 10-day window. A DoorDash page showed it "temporarily closed". I did not treat that as a source.
- **Ehrlich Lake Trail partially closed: left out, [UNVERIFIED].** The only source is a Johnstown Breeze headline dated Oct. 15, with no article text. I found no Town notice. Missing: which section, why, and until when.
- **Johnstown Center Drive partial closure: left out, [UNVERIFIED].** This is another Breeze headline ("closed through the week"). The index I saw gave no reliable date, and I found no Town notice.

### Town news worth a listing
All from local news, not official minutes. Check the Town's Oct. 19 agenda and minutes before publishing.
- **Oct. 19 Town Council meeting** (Johnstown Republic, Oct. 20):
  - A proposal to cut the Town's property tax mill levy by eight mills over four years failed on a 3–3 vote at first reading.
  - The Council approved a $500,000 agreement to buy one share of water rights from Gillam Development Corporation. It is subject to verification and closing.
  - The Council approved two agreements for U.S. Engineering to expand at the Johnstown Commercial Center near I-25 and Highway 60. It deferred Highway 60 improvements.
- **Mayor recall petition:** the Town Clerk found it fell eight signatures short after the cure review (Republic, Oct. 9). The Breeze ran "JT Mayoral recall effort fails" on Oct. 15.
- **Nov. 3 ballot:** Weld RE-5J (Johnstown-Milliken) voters decide whether to extend a $4 million annual mill levy override (Republic headline, Aug. 29). The Weld County sample ballot lists a RE-5J measure and says ballots are due Nov. 3 at 7:00 p.m. I did not read the RE-5J ballot text in full.
- **Watering:** the Town's Oct. 9 alert says the summer watering season ended Oct. 15. Hand watering of trees and shrubs is Saturdays and Sundays only until April 15, 2027. The Town homepage banner reads "Level 3 Drought Watering Schedule in Effect". I did not open the page behind that banner.
- **Trash:** the Town calendar lists pickup Monday, Oct. 26 for homes west of Colorado Blvd and Wednesday, Oct. 28 for homes east.
