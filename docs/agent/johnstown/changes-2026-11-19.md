The Town's Cone Zone page for the week of November 16 says the intersection of Larimer County Roads 3 and 18 is closed for intersection widening tied to the Thompson River Ranch development, starting November 12. The Town gives December 4, 2026 as the anticipated completion date and says all information is weather pending and all completion dates are tentative. The Larimer County road-closure list shows a full closure of County Road 3 for about a quarter mile from County Road 18, from November 12 to December 4, last updated November 9.

The Weld RE-5J School District's website carries a November 13 announcement that preliminary results show its mill levy override extension passing. The question on the November 3 ballot, Issue 5A, asked voters to extend, without imposing a new tax, the tax the district's voters approved in 2020, which brings in $4 million a year and is set to expire on December 31, 2030. The ballot language, posted by the Larimer County Clerk, lists the uses as keeping and attracting teachers and staff, adding safety, security and mental health personnel, expanding career exploration and preparation programs, and maintaining early childhood programming. The results stay unofficial until the county certifies them.
