## Loveland

### loveland-festival-of-lights: tree-lighting, 2026 confirmed (2026-11-20)
- https://www.downtownloveland.org/festivaloflights (the Downtown District's own page). It says: "Festival of Lights / FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 2026 | 5:00PM - 8:00PM" and "4th Street between Garfield Avenue and Washington Avenue". It also says: "Holiday Tree Lighting at 7:30pm. Gather with the community as we light up Downtown Loveland's Christmas tree at 7:30 p.m. Located on 4th Street in front of the Rialto Theater. The tree lighting is the highlight of the evening…"
- **Judgement call for the owner:** the tree lighting is one part of the Festival of Lights, not a separate event. The record uses the organizer's name for the whole event, "Festival of Lights", with its start time (5 pm) and place. The tree is lit at 7:30 pm on 4th Street in front of the Rialto Theater. If you would rather the record describe only the lighting, change the name to the page's heading "Holiday Tree Lighting", the startTime to "7:30 pm" and the place to "4th Street in front of the Rialto Theater".
- Not a parade of lights. "Floats on Fourth" is a "stationary float parade" ("floats parked along the sides of the street"), not a lighted moving parade, so no parade-of-lights record was written.
- Organizer: the page and its footer name it "Loveland Downtown District" ("© 2026 Loveland Downtown District"). The repo's event listing uses "Loveland Downtown Partnership", and the event manager's email is at lovelandpartnership.org.
- cost: not stated. The page lists free hot chocolate, s'mores, carriage rides and art activities, but no admission line.
- 2025: no organizer page stating the 2025 date was found. The Downtown District page says only "In 2025 there was approximately 8-10k people in attendance". News items (nocostyle.com, northfortynews.com, ground.news, reported via search) say Friday, Nov. 21, 2025, but those are not organizer pages, so there is no 2025 entry. The Downtown District calendar (https://www.downtownloveland.org/calendar?format=json) is drawn by a widget and returned no event items.
- firstYear: not stated. The page says only "A hometown tradition".

### Loveland: no parade of lights
- No lighted holiday parade in Loveland was found on any organizer page. Searches for "Loveland CO parade of lights" turned up only the Festival of Lights' stationary floats.
- City of Loveland "Community Tree Lighting Ceremony" at the Civic Center: the only source is a 2014 radio item (https://k99.com/community-tree-lighting-ceremony-at-lovelands-civic-center-park-on-wednesday-december-3/, published December 3, 2014). - Centerra and Chapungu (Winter Wonderlights, "Flip the Switch at Chapungu Illuminated", "All is Bright Tour") are light displays, not tree lightings or parades. None was found to be a tree lighting.

It says: "Windsor Wonderland / Saturday, December 5, 2026", "Date: December 5, 2026", "Time: 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM", "Location: Boardwalk Community Park, 100 N 5th Street".
