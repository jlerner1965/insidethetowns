**Pick.** Construction and Cocoa is a Town event. I read the Town's event page (EID=12274). It gives Thu Nov. 19, 5:30–6:30 pm, Service Center, 1700 Avalon Ave. It also says the meeting is indoors without central heat, and that site work is expected to begin April 2027, with construction running through early 2028. The page states no cost and no registration requirement. I left cost out of the pick body. Add "free" only if you can confirm it.
