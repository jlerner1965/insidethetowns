The Colorado Department of Transportation and contractor Zak Dirt of Mead are widening and repaving CO 7 at Willow Creek Drive, Park Lake Drive and CO 42, between mile points 58 and 60. Willow Creek and Park Lake get dedicated left-turn lanes. CO 7 and CO 42, also called Arapahoe Road and 95th Street, gets more east-west lanes, longer turn lanes, transit-priority lanes for RTD, 12-foot shared-use paths and signalized pedestrian crossings. First work is at Willow Creek in early November: removing curbs and ramps and updating drainage at the Dry Creek Davidson Ditch crossing. CO 7 traffic moves onto temporary paved lanes to the south, with barriers, and flaggers may be on site. CDOT says similar shifts come near Park Lake as work progresses in late 2026 and at CO 42 in early 2027. Normal hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., with some night and weekend work. CDOT expects the project to finish in late 2027.

The on-time Choice Enrollment window for the 2027-28 school year opened November 1 and closes December 18, 2026. Applicants rank the schools they want and upload proof of age and address if they do not already attend a BVSD school. Selection is by lottery, and BVSD says families who are not selected can still pursue enrollment at their neighborhood school. Families are notified of seat offers on or about December 23 and have 13 days to accept the seat and register the student. A family that accepts a school of choice gives up its guaranteed seat at the neighborhood school and must apply through Choice Enrollment to return. Families are also responsible for transportation. BVSD accepts applications until August 31, 2027. After the lottery, applicants go on wait lists and seats are offered as space is available.
