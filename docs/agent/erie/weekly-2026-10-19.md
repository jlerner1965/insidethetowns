### Town news worth a listing
- **Mayor's resignation.** 
Mayor Andrew Moore said he will resign effective immediately after the Town adopts its 2027 Budget.
 
Adoption is scheduled for Nov. 10, 2026.
 
Under the Home Rule Charter, Mayor Pro Tem Brandon Bell then becomes Mayor.
 
His District 2 seat stays vacant until the newly elected member takes office in January 2027.
 **Sources differ on the date.** The Town says "immediately following" budget adoption (Nov. 10). 
Yellow Scene's October roundup says the resignation is effective Nov. 10.
 
A Sept. 28 Yellow Scene article says Nov. 12.
 Use the Town's wording ("after adoption of the 2027 Budget, scheduled for Nov. 10"), not a fixed effective date.

The election is Nov. 3.
 
Seats up in 2026 are District 1 (John Mortellaro), District 2 (Brandon Bell) and District 3 (Brian O'Connor).
 
The Town's election page lists "Ballot Creation/Mailing: October 16."
 The snippet doesn't say who mails ballots (Erie spans Boulder and Weld counties). I'd confirm with the county clerks before printing.
