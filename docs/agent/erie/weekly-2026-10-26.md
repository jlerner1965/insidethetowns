## Editor's notes
*(For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.)*

**Pick.** I chose the Tree Diversity Walk because every detail is confirmed on the Town's own event page. Date, time, place, "free, no registration" and the "final tree walk of the year" wording are all there (https://www.erieco.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=5482). The Eerie Erie 5k and 10k (Sat., Oct. 31) was the runner-up. I left it out of the pick because I could not confirm the base registration fee. The Optimists' page only says race-day sign-up costs $5 more, and the Active.com registration page was not read. Swap it in if you prefer.

**Town news worth a listing**
- **2027 budget, first reading.** The Town says the 2027 Recommended Budget goes to First Reading on Tuesday, Oct. 27, with adoption scheduled for Tuesday, Nov. 10. The Town says Council may change it before adoption. The budget is about $190 million across 16 funds and includes three proposed new full-time positions (two police officers and a wastewater manager). Source: https://www.erieco.gov/m/newsflash. The Agenda Center lists the Oct. 27 Town Council meeting at 6:30 p.m. in Council Chambers, 645 Holbrook St. (https://erieco.portal.civicclerk.com/?category_id=26). This matches the existing guide listing. The Nov. 10 Town Council listing is also already on the guide.
- **Town Manager.** The Town announced on Oct. 16 that Council negotiated a contract with Kelli Johnson, now Deputy City Manager in Greeley. Council was to consider it at a special meeting on Oct. 20. If approved, she starts Jan. 4, 2027. Source: https://www.erieco.gov/m/newsflash. See `[UNVERIFIED]` below for the vote.
- **Paving.** The Town says Street Maintenance Paving Project Phase 2 (Vista Ridge and Old Town) continues through Saturday, Oct. 31. No full closures are planned. Partial lane closures with one-lane access run 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on affected streets. Source: https://www.erieco.gov/2663/Old-Town-Paving-Project---SummerFall-202. Separately, the Town's Oct. 5 weekly update says citywide crack sealing runs Oct. 5 to Nov. 30, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. daily, with no full closures planned (https://www.erieco.gov/CivicSend/ViewMessage/message/302603).
- **County road closure.** The Town's Cone Zones page says Weld County will fully close WCR 1.5 between Erie and WCR 3, and WCR 3 between WCR 10 and 10.5, from Oct. 13 to Nov. 26, with detours (https://erieco.gov/ConeZone). I saw this only as a search-result excerpt. Check the page before using it.

**Events found that are not yet on the guide**
- **Goblin Run for kids, Sat., Oct. 31, 11 a.m.**, Coal Creek Park (575 Kattell St.). It is for ages 10 and under, and costumes are encouraged. Each child needs a grown-up with them. It is a clockwise loop around the grass area, finishing by the playground. Source: https://erieoptimists.org/eerie-erie/. The Optimists say they are still finalizing details.
- **Terror in the Corn, Anderson Farms, final nights:** Thu., Oct. 29, 7–10 p.m.; Fri., Oct. 30, 7–11 p.m.; Sat., Oct. 31, 7–11 p.m. It is closed Mon.–Wed. The ticket includes Fall Festival entry. I read the dates and times from a calendar table on the ticket page. Source: https://tickets.andersonfarms.com/p/terror-in-the-corn. I did not find ticket prices there, so none are quoted.
- **Library:** the 2026 Sundance Film Festival Short Film Tour is Sat., Dec. 12, 1–3 p.m. at Erie Community Library. Registration opens at 1 p.m. on Sat., Oct. 31. Source: https://highplains.libcal.com/event/16923053. The event is outside the 14-day window.

**Conflicts**
- **Eerie Erie times.**
  - The Town calendar lists Eerie Erie as 9–11 a.m. at Coal Creek Park (https://www.erieco.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=5449).
  - The organizer lists the 10K start at 9:30 a.m., the 5K at 9:50 a.m., on-site registration and packet pickup from 7:30 a.m., and the Goblin Run at 11 a.m. It also says start times may change (https://erieoptimists.org/eerie-erie/).
  - The existing guide listing uses 9:30 a.m. I would go by the organizer.
- **Anderson Farms home page.** It still carries an August sunflower weather alert. It also says the farm is "currently not open to the public," even though the Fall Festival runs Sept. 23–Oct. 31. I treated the banner as stale and used the pricing page, which was modified Oct. 9.
