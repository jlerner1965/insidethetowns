On Nov. 10, the Town Council approved Ordinance 037-2026, which rezones the Coal Creek property from Agricultural Holding to Agricultural/Open Space and Reserved Open Space. The ordinance describes two parcels totaling 46.508 acres, along Weld County Road 1.5 and the east bank of Coal Creek. The Town filed the application on Aug. 3, and the Planning Commission recommended approval on Oct. 7. The Council found that the approval criteria in the Unified Development Code are met. The ordinance takes effect 10 days after publication following adoption.

The Town lists Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, and the day after, Friday, Nov. 27, as observed holidays, when its administrative offices are closed. The Erie Community Center, at 450 Powers Street, is open with amended hours of 6 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 26. It is open with regular operating hours on Friday, Nov. 27. The Town's holiday page says other Town facilities follow a different holiday schedule from the Community Center.
