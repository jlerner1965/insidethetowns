The Town's e-newsletter says fall street sweeping for all Town streets is scheduled for Thursday and Friday, November 19 and 20, starting between 7 and 8 a.m. each day. On Thursday the contractor starts near High Street and works the north side of town from west to east: Eagle Canyon Subdivision, Vasquez Ct., Vasquez Rd., Horizon Dr., Steamboat Valley Road, 5th Ave., 4th Ave., 3rd Ave., 2nd Ave., Mountain View Drive, 1st Ave and Kelling Drive. On Friday the work begins on Main Street and Railroad Ave., then moves east to west on the south side: Stone Canyon, Lyons Valley Park, 3rd Ave., 4th Ave., Confluence, Meily St., Prospect St. and Ewald St. The Town asks residents to park off the street or in driveways and to clear debris from the curb, and says the contractor will not sweep where items obstruct the roadway. The Town calls the schedule and route tentative, depending on the number of trucks and the weather, and says it sweeps twice a year, in spring and fall.
