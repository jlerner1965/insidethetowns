---

The Town has changed how electric usage appears on utility bills. Usage now shows as three lines with your meter number, ending in T1, T2 and T3. T1 is off-peak, 9 p.m. to 4 p.m. the following day. T2 is mid-peak, 4 to 5 p.m. and 8 to 9 p.m. T3 is peak, 5 to 8 p.m. Customers with solar panels see two sets of lines: DT1, DT2 and DT3 for electricity delivered to the property, and RT1, RT2 and RT3 for electricity the Town received from their system. The Town says a large share of its wholesale electricity cost comes from peak demand, and that peak use in Lyons generally falls between 5 and 8 p.m. The rate has not changed. Customers pay the same rate per kilowatt-hour in all three periods. Any change to rates that vary by time of day would be reviewed, discussed and voted on by the Board of Trustees before it took effect.
</file>
