In a Nov. 13 project update, the Town of Windsor said it would host a ribbon-cutting on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 1:15 p.m. to celebrate the substantial completion of the "backlots" parking lot between Main St. and Boardwalk Park, and invited residents and business owners to get a first look. The Town closed the lot on Monday, June 15, for the 5th St. Backlots Project, which includes waterline replacement, sewer line installation and parking lot reconstruction. An Oct. 27 update said roadway striping would begin Oct. 28, with lane shifts on Main St. between 3rd and 4th streets.
