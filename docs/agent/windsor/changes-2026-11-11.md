The Town says the new water main along 15th St. is installed and active, and crews have moved to the last phase of the project, patching and repaving the roads the work touched. The intersection of 15th St. and Walnut St. is fully closed on Wednesday, Nov. 11, from about 8:30 to 10:30 a.m.; the Town's detour is 14th St., Lakewood Dr., Southwood Ln. and Fernwood Dr. Starting Monday, Nov. 16, crews will mill and overlay the road at the north end near Main St. (CO 392) and work south through the week. Work locations and traffic impacts will change with each day's production, and flaggers will be on Walnut Dr. to Lakewood Dr. to help residents reach their homes. The Town's Oct. 22 notice listed southbound 15th St. from CO 392 to Westwood Dr. as closed Oct. 21 through Nov. 19, with residential and business access kept and through traffic detoured. The Town expects the work to finish before Thanksgiving, weather permitting.

The Town's Colorado Blvd. Realignment Project is underway. It shifts the Colorado Blvd. intersection at Jacoby Rd. west and adds a dedicated northbound turn lane, as the first phase of a larger multi-phase project. Per the Town's Oct. 16 update, Colorado Blvd. was closed Monday, Oct. 19 and Tuesday, Oct. 20 from CO 392 to the Kyger Reservoir parking lot for the roadway tie-in, and all through traffic had to detour. Access to Kyger stayed open from the north. The update then says the closure moves to the stretch from the Kyger Reservoir parking lot to Jacoby Rd. and runs through late November. Through traffic must still detour, and Kyger Reservoir stays open from the south. The Town lists the project budget as $365,000.
