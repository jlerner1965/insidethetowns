## Editor's notes

**Pick.** Floral Alchemy (Fri Nov 6) is the pick. The Town's exhibitions page gives Friday, Nov. 6 through Sunday, Nov. 15, with an opening reception Friday, Nov. 6, from 5 to 8 p.m. The Town's Art & Heritage Center page (https://www.recreationliveshere.com/185/Upcoming-Exhibitions) lists gallery hours as Friday to Sunday, noon to 4 p.m. The facility page (https://www.recreationliveshere.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Art-Heritage-Center-37) lists the admission fee as free. Those pages don't say whether the reception itself has a separate cost.

**Teens Cook (Mon Nov 2)** is confirmed on the Clearview event page (https://clearview.libnet.info/event/17449973).
- It runs 4:30 to 6 p.m. at Windsor Library, in the Pikes Peak room.
- Registration is required, and it is for ages 12 to 18 only.
- The library says it cannot accommodate severe gluten, dairy or nut allergies.
- The page showed 6 seats remaining when I read it on Oct 28.
- I left it out of the pick because the floral show is open to everyone.

**Town news worth a listing**
- **Leaf & Branch Drop.** North Forty News says Windsor residents can recycle yard waste on weekends at the Public Services Campus, with fees starting at $10 per load. I read the full article: it runs through Nov 15, Fridays to Sundays, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., with final loads by 2:30 p.m. Payment is by card only, and Windsor residents need proof of residency. This is a secondary source. The Town's own yard waste page was not fetched (see URLs below).
- **Election Day, Tuesday, Nov 3.** Windsor sits in both Weld and Larimer counties, so a Weld-only line could mislead Larimer voters. I kept it out of the headline. The Weld sample ballot gives Election Day hours of 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. The Windsor Recreation Center, 250 11th St, is on the Weld voting-location list. The ballot PDF appears to list it among sites opening Monday, Nov 2, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. That reading is not certain. The 24-hour drop box there closes at 7 p.m. on Election Day. I did not find the Larimer County side.
- **Town budget.** The Town's budget timeline shows a Town Board work session on follow-up budget items on Nov 9, and a public hearing and 2027 budget adoption on Nov 23. The guide's Nov 9 Town Board listing is the regular meeting. I have not confirmed the work session time.
