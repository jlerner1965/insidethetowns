### Town news worth a listing
- Evergreen is unincorporated, so there is no Town government to report on. These items come from Jeffco and the fire district.
- **Jeffco open house, Mon., Oct. 26, 5–7 p.m., Wulf Recreation Center, Birch Room.** Jeffco Parks & Open Space wants input on community gathering space, including the Fairgrounds and Fehringer Ranch. It is already on the guide. Source: https://www.jeffco.us/m/newsflash?cat=25
- **Evergreen Fire Protection District Ballot Issue 7B, Election Day Nov. 3.** The measure is a 0.89 percent sales tax. The ballot language says it would raise $5,440,000 a year starting Jan. 1, 2027. Source: https://www.jeffco.us/4652/On-the-Ballot. The district's homepage also lists it: https://www.evergreenfirerescue.com/. Report the facts only; I did not read the district's detail page.
