### Town news worth a listing (not yet on the guide, as far as I was given)
All from the City of Longmont:

- Hover Street bridge over Oligarchy Ditch: construction began Oct. 5, with one lane each direction over the bridge (https://longmontcolorado.gov/news/construction-begins-hover-bridge-oligarchy/).
- 9th Avenue pedestrian bridge over Spring Gulch #1: closed to pedestrians in October 2026. A long-term pedestrian detour is due in early November (https://longmontcolorado.gov/projects/pedestrian-bridge-replacement-9th-spring-gulch-1/).
