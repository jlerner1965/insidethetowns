### Pick
- I chose the Saturday, Nov. 7 listing of the Handweavers Guild show. It is the one day that also has the Longmont Farmers Market (8 a.m.–1 p.m.) and the EBCA Autumn Artist Market (Barn A, 10 a.m.–5 p.m.) at the same fairgrounds.
- The Saturday hours (10–6) agree across the guild's own pages (hgbsale.org, handweaversofboulder.org) and the Boulder County Arts Alliance listing.
- Sources conflict on other days:
  - Thursday is 10–8 on the guild's pages and 10–6 on bouldercountyarts.org.
  - The Eventbrite page's headline says "10 AM–3 PM" for the whole run. That matches only Sunday, so I ignored it.
  - The pick body avoids the disputed days. The guide's Thursday listing may need a look.
- EBCA market hours: Saturday 10–5 and Sunday 10–4 (ebcacolorado.org). The county fairgrounds listing shows Nov. 7 only, 10–5.

### Town news worth a listing
- **Election, Tuesday, Nov. 3.** The Council referred four items to the ballot. Ballots are due to the County Clerk & Recorder by 7 p.m. (https://longmontcolorado.gov/city-clerk/election-information/november-3-2026-election-ballot-measures/)
  - 3A: public safety sales and property tax increase.
  - 3B: charter amendment on open space lands.
  - 3C: charter amendment allowing ranked choice voting.
  - 3D: charter amendment on Council election cycles.
  - The City's plain-language page is https://longmontcolorado.gov/information/ballot-information-november-3-2026-election/
- **City Council, Tuesday, Nov. 3.** Pre-Session is at 6 p.m. in the Study Session Room. The regular meeting is at 7 p.m. at the Civic Center, 350 Kimbark St. The next meeting is Nov. 17, and Nov. 24 is cancelled for Thanksgiving. (https://longmontcolorado.gov/city-council/, https://longmontcolorado.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026CityCouncilMeetingDates.pdf)
- **Longmont Museum.** Galleries reopened Oct. 17 after the expansion. This is not new this week. (https://longmontcolorado.gov/news/longmont-museum-celebrates-grand-reopening-with-free-celebration-oct-17/)
