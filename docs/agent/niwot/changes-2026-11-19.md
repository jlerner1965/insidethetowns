The Boulder County Commissioners unanimously approved the changes after a temporary moratorium, a comment period and two public hearings, according to the county's Nov. 13 news release. The changes went into immediate effect on Nov. 10, 2026, and they define data centers and detention centers as land uses for the first time. Detention centers are prohibited in all zoning districts in the unincorporated county. A data center is limited to an information technology energy load of 50 kilowatts or less, is allowed only in the Light Industrial and General Industrial zoning districts, and needs a Limited Impact Special Review. Larger data centers are not permitted. Server rooms that support an allowed main use are still allowed. The moratorium on applications for both uses, which the commissioners began in June, ended on Nov. 10. The rules apply to the unincorporated areas of the county, not to municipalities such as Boulder or Longmont.

Boulder County's hours-of-operation page lists these holiday closures for county government offices: Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, and the day after, Friday, Nov. 27; Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, closed at noon; Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25; New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31, closed at noon; and New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027.
