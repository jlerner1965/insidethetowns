## Brief

- **Reader's question:** What is the history of Niwot, and why does the town look the way it does?
- **Primary topic:** Niwot, Colorado history. **Supporting questions:** who Chief Niwot was; when and why the town was platted; why the grid runs at an angle; what happened to the west side; why Niwot has no town government; what's left to see.
- **Format:** History or profile (house template): one sentence of scene, point by the end of paragraph one, chronology, where to see it today, Before you go.
- **Angle:** The 1875 plat explains the town's shape, and three later events explain the rest: trains stopped calling (1932), the Diagonal cleared the west side (1958–59), and the town never incorporated (still true after the November 2026 vote).
- **Evidence for the angle:** LHVC (plat date, never incorporated, Hinman Street removed for the Diagonal), NHS depot history and 2025 walking tour (trains stopped 1932; west side razed or moved by 1959), Dyni (section house origin, nothing left west of the tracks), Boulder County results (2026 vote).
- **Page:** update the existing guide article at the same URL rather than a new one. The existing page is a short outline that already ranks for nothing (see below); a second Niwot history page, on the guide or the hub, would compete with it.
