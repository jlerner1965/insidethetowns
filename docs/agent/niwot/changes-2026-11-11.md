Boulder County voters approved Ballot Issue 1A, the Early Childhood Care and Education Mill Levy Increase and Voter-Approved Revenue Change, according to the unofficial results from the Boulder County Clerk & Recorder. The county's unofficial count has 95,445 votes for and 67,341 against. The measure raises property taxes by 2.579 mills. The county says the money will lower the cost of child care and preschool for families with young children, raise pay for child care and preschool teachers and staff, and add capacity to reduce waitlists. The Board of County Commissioners voted unanimously to send the measure to voters after a public hearing in July.
