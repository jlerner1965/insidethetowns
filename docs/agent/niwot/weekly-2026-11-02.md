## Editor's notes

**Pick.** Election Day (Tue Nov 3) is the strongest listing inside Mon Nov 2 to Sun Nov 8. The NLIDAC meeting (Tue Nov 3, 7 p.m.) and the Rotary breakfast (Thu Nov 5, 7:30 a.m.) are also in the window. The Oct 29 and Oct 31 listings are before Nov 2. The Nov 10, 11 and 12 listings are after Nov 8.

**What the pick body rests on**
- Ballots must be received by 7 p.m. on Nov 3, with no postmark rule. Sources: https://bouldercounty.gov/elections/information/ and the county TABOR notice at https://assets.bouldercounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-TABOR-Notice.pdf.
- Vote centers run 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Election Day. Gunbarrel Library, 6500 Lookout Road, is on the county's Phase 3 list, open Oct 30 through Election Day. Source: https://bouldercounty.gov/elections/information/vote-centers/.
- Incorporation questions go to electors inside the proposed boundary. Source: https://niwotelection.org/ballot.
- The Left Hand Valley Courier says the closest vote centers to Niwot are the Boulder County Clerk and Recorder's Office and the St. Vrain Community Hub. The county list also has Gunbarrel Library, so I did not use "closest".
- I did not find a Niwot-area ballot drop box on the county list. I left drop boxes out of the pick body. Check https://bouldercounty.gov/elections/information/ballot-drop-boxes/ before adding any.
- I left out any cost statement. No primary source I read says what it costs to vote.

**Headline and knowTitle.** I filled both. Delete them if Niwot is not a flagship guide.

**Other items in the window**
- NLIDAC, Tue Nov 3, 7 to 9 p.m., Niwot Inn Conference Room, 342 Second Avenue. Sources: https://bouldercounty.gov/event/niwot-local-improvement-district-advisory-committee-nlidac-meeting-3/2026-11-03/ and https://bouldercounty.gov/government/boards-and-commissions/niwot-local-improvement-district/. The county says to check the Commissioners' advance agenda to confirm each meeting. The Sept 1 meeting was canceled for lack of business, so confirm this one before it goes out.
