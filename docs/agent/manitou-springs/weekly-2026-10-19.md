### Pick check: Coffin Races
- Confirmed on the Chamber page (https://manitousprings.org/emma-crawford-coffin-races/, page modified 2026-10-15): Saturday Oct. 24, 900 block of Manitou Ave, Fan Zone opens 10 a.m. (700 block), parade at noon, races follow (about 1–2 p.m.), free.
- Chamber lists the after-party at Soda Springs Park, 2–5 p.m., with Mighty Holler.
- **Sources conflict on the after-party.**
  - Visit Colorado Springs (https://www.visitcos.com/events/annual/emma-crawford-coffin-races/) lists "RADO" at 2 p.m.
  - Uncover Colorado (https://www.uncovercolorado.com/events/emma-crawford-coffin-races-parade/) says 5 p.m.
  - I went with the Chamber, who run the event.
- The Chamber's event-parking page (https://manitousprings.org/events/event-parking/), read as a search excerpt only, says:
  - Road closure 9 a.m.–3 p.m.
  - Shuttles 9 a.m.–6 p.m., last one leaves Memorial Park at 6 p.m.
  - Shuttle fare is $5 round trip, cash only. Ages 12 and under ride free.
- **Shuttle stop conflict.** Visit Colorado Springs names Hiawatha Gardens (10 Old Mans Trail) as the shuttle stop; the Chamber page names Memorial Park. Check the Chamber's current parking page before using either.
- Chamber's Eventeny page (https://www.eventeny.com/events/32nd-annual-emma-crawford-coffin-races-2026-28385/) gives event hours of 10 a.m.–5 p.m. and the 32nd-annual count.
