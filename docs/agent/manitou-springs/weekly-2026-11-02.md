- Manitou voters have one city item on the ballot, Ballot Issue 2A.

**Town news worth a listing**
- **Ballot Issue 2A.**
  - It would add 4.03% to the city's current 5% excise tax on tickets and admission fees, for a total of 9.03% (CPR, Oct. 28: https://www.cpr.org/2026/10/28/manitou-springs-2a-excise-tax-explained/).
  - A similar measure failed last year, 56 to 44 percent (same CPR story).
  - Council voted unanimously on Oct. 6 for a resolution supporting 2A (Gazette: https://gazette.com/2026/10/06/manitou-city-council-votes-to-support-amusement-tax-increase-on-ballot-3/).
  - The ballot notice estimates $1,128,000 in new revenue for 2027 (County TABOR notice: https://epc-assets.elpasoco.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2026-General-Election-TABOR-Accessible.pdf).
  - I kept this out of the headline and the changes list. If you list it, report the dates and the measure's text only.
- **Coffin races.** The 32nd Emma Crawford Coffin Races were Sat Oct. 24. KOAA reports more than 50 teams (https://www.koaa.com/news/local-news/spooky-season-rolls-into-manitou-springs-with-annual-coffin-races).
