- **Larimer County Farmers' Market.**
  - The source is CSU Extension's market page on larimer.gov, which says "May 16–Oct 31, Saturdays, 9am–1pm."
  - I tagged it `closed` because it is the season end. Drop it if you don't want that tag used for a seasonal close.

  - Source: Wildcraft's own site. It calls the market "Colorado's first year-round farmers market, located inside Foothills," and gives Nov. 7, 10 a.m.–2 p.m.
  - The page says the Fort Collins Winter Farmers Market "is now" 20 years of Saturdays. It does not state a rename outright. The guide listing uses the old name, so check whether the listing and this item need updating.
  - Wildcraft's page gives no street address, so I wrote only "inside Foothills."
