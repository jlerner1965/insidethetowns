## Editor's notes
*(For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.)*

**Pick.** I chose the Studio Tour because it is free, it is in the week, and the City's own pages confirm the hours. Source: 
Saturday, Oct. 24, 10 a.m.–5 p.m., and Sunday, Oct. 25, 12–5 p.m.
 The "more than 70" figure comes from 
the same City news release, which describes it as a self-guided tour of more than 70 local artist spaces
. The City's Studio Tour page (fortcollins.gov/Activities/Arts-and-Culture/Studio-Tour) has a typo in the Sunday hours ("12:00 AM – 05:00 AM"). I used the news release and event pages, which say noon–5 p.m.

### Town news worth a listing
- **Municipal Court closure, Oct. 19–23.** This is the `closed` item above. The City's article lists contact details: 
970-221-6800 or court@FortCollins.gov
. I did not check whether the guide has a page for the court. If it doesn't, the item may need a place page or a plain news line.
- **2027–28 City budget.** The City's October newsletter says 
there are public budget hearings Oct. 6 and 20 at 6 p.m. at City Hall, 300 Laporte Ave.
 It also says 
Council will spend the fall reviewing and modifying the budget before adoption in November
. The Oct. 20 Council agenda includes 
a public hearing on the 2027-28 budget
. This fits the existing Oct. 20 Council listing.
- **Council Oct. 27 work session (agenda planning calendar).** 
Topics are the Housing Action Plan, homelessness updates and regional strategy, and an age-restricted tobacco and psychoactive products program.
 I have not confirmed time or location.
- **Pumpkins on Parade.** The guide has only the Oct. 22 listing. The City says 
it runs Oct. 22–25
, with 
timed-entry tickets 5–8 p.m. nightly and grounds closing to the public at 9 p.m.
 Prices are 
$15 adults, $10 ages 5–17, free for 4 and under
. Gardens page: gardens.fortcollins.gov/Events/Public-Events/Pumpkins. This was the other pick candidate. I went with the Studio Tour because it is free.
- **Downtown Holiday Lighting Ceremony.** 
Friday, Nov. 6, Old Town Square, sponsored by Fort Collins Connexion.
 This is outside the 14-day window, but it could be a listing.
- **Larimer County Farmers' Market.** The larimer.gov page says 
Saturdays 9 a.m.–1 p.m., May 16–Oct. 31, 200 W. Oak St.
 I mean the page I fetched, not the newsletter. The last market is Saturday, Oct. 31. It is not in the guide's list, so it may be worth a line.
- **Leads only, not read in full.** Denver7's Fort Collins page lists headlines about 
a Poudre School District recommendation to close nine schools and the City shutting down Flock Safety cameras
. I have not read either story or found a primary source, so I have not used them. Separately, 
CSU Police reported an officer-involved shooting near Newton Plaza at about 5:35 p.m. Oct. 12, and the suspect was taken to a hospital
. Fort Collins Police are the lead investigating agency. Not suitable for weekly notes.
