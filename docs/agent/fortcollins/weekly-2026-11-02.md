## Editor's notes

*(For the reviewer. Stripped before publishing.)*

**Pick.** I chose the Downtown Holiday Lighting Ceremony. It falls inside the window (Fri 2026-11-06), and the City's own event page and Oct. 13 news release confirm 5:30–7 p.m. in Old Town Square, with the ceremony at 6:30 and a cost of "Free". The City's release says street closures near the square start at 3:30 p.m.

Other listings in the window that I checked:
- First Friday Art Walk, Nov. 6, 6–9 p.m., free. Confirmed on downtownfortcollins.com.
- Alice by Heart, Nov. 6, 7:30 p.m., University Theatre, 1400 Remington St. Confirmed on theatre.colostate.edu. The page also lists Nov. 7, 12, 13 and 14 at 7:30 p.m. and Nov. 8 and 15 at 2 p.m.
- City Council regular meeting, Tue Nov. 3, 6 p.m., City Hall, 300 Laporte Ave. Confirmed on the City's event page.

**Voice note.** The style guide says to call the municipal government "the Town". I wrote "City Council" and "the City" because Fort Collins is a city.

**Check before publishing**
- **Winter Farmers Market listing** (/events/fort-collins-winter-farmers-market/). The market is now Wildcraft Farmers Market, at Foothills (215 E. Foothills Pkwy), Saturdays 10 a.m.–2 p.m., and year-round (wildcraftmarkets.com; BizWest, Oct. 27). BizWest says the market moved to Foothills in 2020. If the listing still names the Opera Galleria or the old name, it needs fixing. Opening day also has a 10:15 a.m. ribbon-cutting with Mayor Emily Francis at the Foothills south entrance and live music from Luke Eddington (BizWest; the ribbon-cutting is also on wildcraftmarkets.com).
- **CSU football vs. Boise State, Sat Nov. 7.** Sources conflict on kickoff, so I did not use it for the pick.
  - 4 p.m. MT: Boise State alumni page, Wikipedia's 2026 CSU and Boise State season pages, and the Gazette's May schedule story.
  - 6:00 p.m.: CBS Sports, ESPN and Sports-Reference.
  - I could not read csurams.com (not accessible). The guide listing says 16:00. Confirm against the CSU athletics site before relying on it.

**Town news worth a listing**
- Council's Nov. 3 meeting. The City's planning calendar lists first readings of the 2027/28 budget appropriation, the annual DDA budget and the annual GID #1 budget. The calendar says it is "subject to change", and I did not find the posted Nov. 3 agenda.
- Data center moratorium. News reports say Council voted unanimously on first reading Oct. 20. They say second reading is Nov. 3, and that if adopted the six-month moratorium runs to April 6, 2027. I did not confirm this against the City's agenda, so I left it out of the headline. Sources: Coloradoan via Yahoo (Oct. 21), BizWest (Oct. 23), Longmont Leader.
