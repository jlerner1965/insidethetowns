The Town of Firestone says staff will bring the proposed 2027 budget forward for review and adoption in early 2027, after the newly elected Board of Trustees is sworn in. The Town gives three reasons for the later schedule: a vacant Finance Director position, an Interim Finance Director coming on, and an extended period without a Board quorum. Colorado law lets a town that has not adopted appropriations by December 31 continue on a temporary reappropriation of 90 percent of the prior year's operations and maintenance appropriations, and the Town says it will operate that way from January 1, 2027 until the budget is adopted. The Town says there will be no disruption to services, no change to service levels and no effect on Town staff. It adds that the temporary measure does not give the same flexibility for new capital projects, major equipment purchases, land acquisitions or new debt. The Town says it will publish the 2027 budget timeline at firestoneco.gov/budget once it is set.

The Town of Firestone is offering free Leaf Drop-Off Events, new this season, on Fridays from November 6 through December 4, from 12 to 3 p.m. at the Firestone Maintenance Facility, 7500 WCR 20. The events are for leaves only; the Town says it will not accept tree limbs or other woody yard waste, and staff on site direct traffic and help unload. The Town also says it is unlawful in Firestone to blow or place yard waste, including leaves and grass clippings, into the street, and that the street sweeper will not pick it up. Other options the Town lists are setting bagged leaves at the curb on your trash pickup day, mowing and mulching them, or composting them. For questions about code violations, call the non-emergency number, 720-652-4222, and ask for a Firestone Community Service Officer.
