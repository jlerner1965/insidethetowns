From now until late May, every Georgetown Loop train leaves from the Devil's Gate Depot, 646 Loop Drive in Georgetown, off I-70 at exit 228. The Silver Plume depot opens only from late May to mid-October, and the railroad says the ride is [exactly the same from either end](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/faqs-directions/). Book online or by phone, 888-456-6777, because trains sell out often, and collect your printed tickets at the depot 30 minutes before departure. The winter trains run to January 3, 2027; the 2027 regular season is not yet announced.

This is for anyone planning a ride this winter, or next summer with a choice of station. Tickets are not mailed or emailed, and you cannot board without the printed ones.

## Which station

| | Devil's Gate, Georgetown | Silver Plume |
|---|---|---|
| Address | 646 Loop Drive; I-70 exit 228 | 825 Railroad Avenue; I-70 exit 226 |
| Open | All season, winter trains included | Late May to mid-October only |
| At the depot | Ticketing, gift store, restrooms, parking | Restrooms, gift store, a car building with rolling stock |
| Extra | Gold panning on summer weekends | — |

The stations are two miles apart on I-70. Wherever you board, the train runs to the other depot and back. From Georgetown the ride to Silver Plume takes about 30 minutes, then a 15-minute layover. There are no restrooms on the train. The railroad says parking is available at the depot and asks you to [arrive 30 minutes early](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/planning-your-visit/) to park and collect tickets; trains leave promptly.

| [Encore Train](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/glrr-events/lighted-forest/) | January 2 and 3, 2027 | Daytime and evening; the evening trains keep the lights |

Winter trains run with diesel engines; the railroad says steam generally runs from Memorial Day weekend to early October and is never guaranteed. Prices and departure times are on each train's booking calendar.

## What to book

In winter there are four classes, from the railroad's [classes of service](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/classes-of-service/):

- **Coach:** bench seats in enclosed, heated cars; no snacks or drinks.
- **Parlor:** enclosed booths, with snacks and a soft drink; beer, wine and holiday cocktails for sale.
- **Tahoe Executive:** the club car with the bigger windows, which the railroad suggests for families.
- **Waldorf Presidential:** the top class, with snacks, a soft drink and a Golden Ticket to take home.

Parlor and Presidential tables seat three or four. Babies up to 12 months ride on an infant ticket; older children need a seat. In summer, coach seats are open-air, unassigned and first come.

- **Wheelchairs:** coach cars are accessible; Parlor has limited wheelchair seating, and the Waldorf and Tahoe cars have none. Tell the reservation agent when you book.
- **Dogs:** only service animals, not emotional-support animals.

## Changes, refunds and weather

- **Up to 48 hours before:** cancel or move your tickets by calling 888-456-6777.
- **Inside 48 hours:** no refund unless you bought Trip Protection, 10 percent of the booking, which allows refunds up to an hour before departure. Without it, the 10 percent is charged on each change.
- **Never refunded:** changes inside the last hour, no-shows, and weather once you have boarded. The train runs rain or shine.
- **Dress:** in winter, an insulated coat, gloves, hat and warm boots.

## The mine tours are for spring and summer

The Lebanon Silver Mine tours, reached only by the train, run [from April into September](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/mine-tours/) and add about an hour and a quarter. Children under 5 cannot go into the mine, and strollers and wheelchairs are not allowed; it is 44 degrees inside all year.

## If it's sold out

- **Walk the town:** Georgetown is a National Historic Landmark District. Pick up the guide to its historic sites, a self-guided walking tour, at the [visitor center](https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/visitor.php), 1491 Argentine Street at the I-70 exit, where the restrooms are open from 6 am to 10 pm.
- **Hamill House:** tours [by reservation](https://historicgeorgetown.org/) only, 303-569-2840.
- **The Christmas Market:** December 5–6 and 12–13, 2026, downtown, with [free admission, parking and a shuttle](https://historicgeorgetown.org/georgetown-christmas-market-2/).
- **Bighorn sheep:** Colorado Parks and Wildlife calls the Georgetown herd, 250 to 350 sheep, one of the [most popular sheep viewing sites](https://cpw.state.co.us/species/rocky-mountain-bighorn-sheep) in the nation; breeding peaks in early December. The Town lists a [warming hut and wildlife viewing park](https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/town_government/parks_and_trails.php).
- **The lake loop:** the flat trail round Georgetown Lake, about 2 miles.

## Before you go

- **Tickets and times:** [georgetownlooprr.com](https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/events/) or 888-456-6777.
- **The road:** I-70 to exit 228, then Argentine Street to the end, where it meets the depot.
- **2027:** the 2026 season was announced on February 28 for a March 20 opening; watch the railroad's site for 2027's.
