The 66th Georgetown Christmas Market fills downtown on two December weekends: 5 and 6 December and again on 12 and 13 December 2026. Three outdoor European-style marketplaces with more than 30 artisans, an Alpine Biergarten at 6th and Taos with Cabin Creek Brewing's beer, wine and wassail, food vendors, Victorian street carolers, hot chestnuts at Strousse Park, horse-and-wagon rides through the Victorian neighborhoods and the museums decorated for the holidays. Stage entertainment at Rose and 6th runs 11 am to 4 pm, the Santa Lucia Children's Procession led by Saint Nicholas sets off at noon each day, and the Book Nook of Clear Creek County and Colorado authors is open in the Community Center on Saturdays 10 am to 4 pm. The Christmas Afternoon Tea at the Snetzer Building needs a reservation. Admission, parking and the shuttle are free. Read from Historic Georgetown's Christmas Market page.
