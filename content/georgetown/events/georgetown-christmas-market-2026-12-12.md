The second weekend of the 66th Georgetown Christmas Market, 12 and 13 December 2026, after the first on 5 and 6 December. The same three artisan marketplaces, the Alpine Biergarten at 6th and Taos, food vendors, carolers, hot chestnuts at Strousse Park, horse-and-wagon rides and decorated museums; stage entertainment at Rose and 6th 11 am to 4 pm, the Santa Lucia Children's Procession at noon each day, and the Book Nook in the Community Center on Saturday 10 am to 4 pm. Admission, parking and the shuttle are free. Read from Historic Georgetown's Christmas Market page.
