Two ticketed evenings in the 1867 Hamill House, Saturday 12 and Saturday 19 December 2026, 6 to 9 pm: a Yule log procession, a reading of 'Twas the Night Before Christmas, the lighting of the tree, and Saint Nicholas and the Silver Plume Singers as guests, with appetizers, cocktails and desserts included. Evening, cocktail or late-1800s dress is suggested; children 12 and under cannot be accommodated. $85 a person, every guest needs a ticket; reserve on 303-569-2840 or preservation@historicgeorgetown.org.
