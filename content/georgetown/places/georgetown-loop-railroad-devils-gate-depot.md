The Georgetown Loop Railroad runs between its Devil's Gate Depot in Georgetown and its Silver Plume depot at 825 Railroad Avenue. From I-70 exit 228, turn into Georgetown, take the first right at the roundabout onto Argentine Street, stay right at the fork, and the road ends at the depot. The railroad asks passengers to arrive 30 minutes before departure to pick up printed tickets; group reservations are on 303-569-0133.

The railroad's FAQ says the ride is about an hour and a quarter in the main season and about 55 minutes from mid-October through early January, that steam generally runs from Memorial Day weekend through early October but is not guaranteed, and that the Silver Plume station is open late May through mid-October and closed in spring and winter. Mine tours run April through September. Its events page (https://www.georgetownlooprr.com/events/) lists the 2026 themed trains: Pumpkinfest, Autumnfest, the Trick or Treat Train and the holiday trains from 6 November to 24 December. Tickets are sold through the railroad's online calendar; no prices or departure times were on the pages read. Facts from the railroad's FAQs & Directions and events pages, read 10 October 2026.
