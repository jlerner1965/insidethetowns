Cabin Creek Brewing is on 22nd Street at the lake end of town. Its site has sections for its beers, liquor and cocktails, food, live music and private events, and lists a Halloween bash and a 7 November wax party without a year.

The brewery's site carries no dated post (its footer says 2021), so the guide looked for a second source: Historic Georgetown's page for the December 2026 Georgetown Christmas Market (https://historicgeorgetown.org/georgetown-christmas-market-2/) names Cabin Creek Brewing as sponsor of the market's Alpine Biergarten. Hours and phone from the brewery's home page, read 10 October 2026.
