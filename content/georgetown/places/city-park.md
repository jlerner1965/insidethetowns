City Park fills the block between Rose and Taos streets south of 10th Street. The Town of Georgetown's parks and recreation master plan (a draft of March 2021, https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/Georgetown%20MP%20Report_FINAL_20210401.pdf) gives its address as 1001 Taos Street and describes it as one of the most popular and heavily used parks in town, a traditional family park used for local events and the Fourth of July: a gazebo in the center, historic arched gateways at each entrance, mature trees, benches, picnic tables, grills, a BBQ pit, a fountain, a flagstone path along its west and east sides, about 50 parking spaces, and Foster's Place, the community-built playground. The plan proposed improvements, including a restroom, that this guide has not confirmed were made.

The Town's Lake, Parks & Trails page, read 10 October 2026, lists City Park under Town Parks; neither it nor the plan gives hours or rules, and the Town's number is 303-569-2555. The location and features are from the 2021 plan, read the same day.
