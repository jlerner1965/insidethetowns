Historic Georgetown, Inc., the nonprofit that preserves and interprets the town's historic buildings, runs the Hamill House Museum at its offices on Argentine Street. The house dates from about 1867. There is no admission charge; donations are welcome, and group or private tours can be arranged by phone. The same organization runs the Alpine Hose No. 2 firefighting museum on 5th Street and is restoring the Centennial Mill and the Kneisel House.

The season: when read on 18 November 2026, Historic Georgetown's site said Hamill House tours are available by reservation only, on 303-569-2840, and that both of its museums will be open for the 66th Annual Georgetown Christmas Market, December 5–6 and 12–13, 11 am to 3 pm. On 10 October 2026 the site said its museums closed for the season on Sunday, September 27th; the in-season hours it listed then are no longer on the page. The house also opens for the ticketed Christmas at the Hamill House evenings on 12 and 19 December 2026. Season from Historic Georgetown's home page and Hamill House page.
