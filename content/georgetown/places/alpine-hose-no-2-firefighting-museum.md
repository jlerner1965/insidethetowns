Historic Georgetown, Inc. runs the Alpine Hose No. 2 Georgetown Firefighting Museum in the old hose company's building on 5th Street, alongside its Hamill House Museum on Argentine Street. The phone is the organization's office number.

Historic Georgetown's site lists the museum's hours as Thursday through Sunday, noon to 4 pm, and says its museums close for the season on Sunday, September 27th, with no reopening date given; the closing date above assumes the 2026 calendar, in which that day is a Sunday. Facts from Historic Georgetown's Hamill House Museum page, read 10 October 2026.
