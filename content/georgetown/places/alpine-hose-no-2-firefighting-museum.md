Historic Georgetown, Inc. runs the Alpine Hose No. 2 Georgetown Firefighting Museum in the old hose company's building on 5th Street, alongside its Hamill House Museum on Argentine Street. The phone is the organization's office number.

When read on 18 November 2026, Historic Georgetown's site said the Alpine Hose Fire Museum is closed for the season, with no reopening date, and that both of its museums will be open for the 66th Annual Georgetown Christmas Market, December 5–6 and 12–13, 11 am to 3 pm. On 10 October 2026 the same site listed in-season hours of Thursday through Sunday, noon to 4 pm, and said its museums close for the season on Sunday, September 27th; the closing date above assumes the 2026 calendar, in which that day is a Sunday, and those hours are no longer on the page. Facts from Historic Georgetown's Hamill House Museum page.
