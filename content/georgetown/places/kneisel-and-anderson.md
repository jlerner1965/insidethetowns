Kneisel & Anderson is a specialty food market on Sixth Street, established in 1883 by its own account. The selection draws on Northern European traditions and local Colorado producers: salty and sweet licorice, old-fashioned candy, artisanal cheese, and locally made jams, jellies and honey. A stop for edible gifts and specialty groceries among the downtown museums and shops.

The store is at **509 6th Street**, phone **303-569-2650**, open **Wednesday through Sunday, 11 am to 5 pm** and closed Monday and Tuesday.
