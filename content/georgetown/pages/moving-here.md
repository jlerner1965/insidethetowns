## Know which address and which authority

The [Town of Georgetown](https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/) site links town departments, planning documents, meetings and services. Confirm that a home is inside the town boundary and which agencies and providers serve it; a Georgetown postal address alone does not settle that.

Municipal utilities start at the Town's [Water and Wastewater page](https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/town_government/departments/water_and_wastewater.php). Ask about account setup and service for the specific property: bills, connection status and the condition of the lines vary from one home to the next.

## Historic character and building plans

The downtown is a National Historic Landmark District, and many people come to Georgetown for its older buildings. Before buying with plans to change a building, fence, exterior or use, ask town planning what applies to that property, and get the records and an inspection of the building itself.

## Schools and the working week

[Clear Creek School District RE-1](https://ccsdre1.org/) runs a four-day week plus optional Enrichment Fridays, which shapes work and childcare. Its site has enrollment and school information and the current calendar; confirm the assigned school and transport with the district at 303-567-3850.

The [John Tomay Memorial Library](/places/john-tomay-memorial-library/) is the local community stop. Its calendar, on the county library district's site, lists book groups and children's programs.

## Winter travel and errands

[COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) has current road conditions; drive the route to work and regular services before deciding. Guanella Pass is a recreation route with seasonal limits, not a year-round commute. The [October-to-May guide](/articles/georgetown-october-to-may/) covers how the seasons change the visitor town.

An errands visit tells more than a sightseeing one: groceries, school travel, parking and the services a household relies on. Ask an internet provider about the exact address if remote work is part of the move, and find out who clears snow and what the lease or ownership documents assign to the occupant.

## Before deciding

Compare a full year of housing and work, not a holiday weekend: utility service, school assignment, planned building use, internet and the winter drive. The [history](/articles/georgetown-history/) and [family](/articles/georgetown-with-kids/) guides show the town's daily pace.
