The Town of Georgetown's History and Tours page (last updated 16 July 2025) lists the Hotel de Paris Museum at 409 6th Street and calls it a Site of the National Trust for Historic Preservation; its Shopping & Galleries page lists the museum shop, "reminiscent of a Parisian flea market", at 409 Sixth Street, 303-569-2311. Both were read on 10 October 2026.

The handoff packet says, unconfirmed, that the museum is the former French inn built by Louis Dupuy in the nineteenth century, shown with its furnished rooms, and that its How to Visit page gives an accessible entry to the first story with the second story reached only by stairs. None of that could be read on the museum's own pages today.
