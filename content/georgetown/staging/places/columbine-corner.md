Columbine Corner describes itself as curated with modern, locally sourced gifts for Colorado locals and visitors. Its online store lists bags, T-shirts, hats, stickers, glassware, women's and men's clothing, hair and home accessories, candles, jewelry, hoodies and crewnecks, kids' and winter accessories, holiday items and art, and features the Colorado artist Caroline Clark.

The shop's Customer support page, read 10 October 2026, gives 970-368-3279 and the address 614 Sixth St., which the Town of Georgetown's Shopping & Galleries page (https://georgetown-colorado.org/shopping-galleries/) assigns to the Georgetown Gallery of Fine Art; the Town's page lists Columbine Corner at 507 Sixth Street with the same phone. The address above is the Town's and the packet's until the shop confirms it.
