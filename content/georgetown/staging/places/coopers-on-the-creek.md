Cooper's on the Creek is owned by Ryan and Amanda Cooper, whose site describes mining-inspired vintage decor, a menu of small and large plates with an emphasis on wild game and elevated comfort food, Colorado craft beers on tap, wines by the glass and cocktails, a deck overlooking Clear Creek and a fireplace. The favorites on its home page, read 10 October 2026, include a green chile dip ($10), duck poutine ($13), the Zen Burger ($20), the Cooper Burger ($19) and a grilled Angus ribeye ($44); happy hour is 3 to 5 pm daily, and the site notes that hours are subject to change. Its events page describes a main dining room seating up to 70 and an enclosed, heated back patio over the creek for up to 50, but still says it is not booking events because of COVID-19.

Address, phone and hours were read on the restaurant's Contact & Hours page on 10 October 2026. The Town of Georgetown's Where to Eat page (https://georgetown-colorado.org/where-to-eat/, last updated 9 September 2026) lists Cooper's on the Creek at 1500 Argentine Street with the same phone, which is the evidence that it is operating; only the Sunday question holds it back.
