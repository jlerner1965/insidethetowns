The Town of Georgetown's Shopping & Galleries page (last updated 16 July 2025) lists Kneisel & Anderson at 509 Sixth Street, 303-569-2650, as "the oldest store in Georgetown", selling specialty and imported Scandinavian, German, English, Swiss and Swedish foods.

The handoff packet says, unconfirmed, that the market dates to 1883 and stocks licorice, candy, artisanal cheeses, jams, jellies and honey alongside local Colorado goods, and that some older visitor guides give a different street number. The Town's page, read 10 October 2026, agrees with the packet's 509 6th Street.
