The home page of shoppeinternationale.com, read 10 October 2026, says that for over 50 years Shoppe Internationale has welcomed visitors to Georgetown with gifts from around the world, including Scandinavia and Northern Europe, and gives the shop's address as 608 Sixth St. Its catalog lists gnomes and ornaments, felted mice, ceramic candle houses, rotary candle holders, silver jewelry, wooden kitchenware, mugs, musical ornaments and golf gifts. The Town of Georgetown's Shopping & Galleries page (https://georgetown-colorado.org/shopping-galleries/, last updated 16 July 2025) describes it differently, as a floral, gift and home decor shop with a year-round Christmas shop and European glass, fabrics and jewelry, at 608 Sixth Street, 800-746-7730.

The site's logo was uploaded in May 2025 and its page data names a customer-support number in Maryland, so whether the site belongs to the Georgetown shop is the first thing to settle.
