Georgetown Valley Candy Company makes its candy in small batches: caramel corn with real butter and cane sugar, fudge, brittles, caramels, divinity, traditional and artisan chocolates, and hard candy boiled and poured the old way. It also makes its own ice cream, scooped in the shop or hand-packed by the pint, quart or gallon, with three-gallon tubs on ten days' notice; signature flavors include Brownie Sundae, Turtle Fudge, Coconut Crisp, Black Walnut and Spearmint Chocolate Chip. Its site says it can no longer ship candy within Colorado because of the state's sales-tax rules, but ships to other states. The shop began in 1979 as a franchise of a Mackinac Island candy factory, was bought by Nina and Rube Goeringer in 1985, and is run by Scott and Rube Goeringer.
