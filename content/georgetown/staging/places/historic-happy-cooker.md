The Historic Happy Cooker on 6th Street has served, its site says, since 1974, and is known for all-day breakfast and specialty European waffles, with homemade comfort food made from original family recipes. Its history page names Derek ("Dirka"), its head chef and chief operations officer, with more than 20 years at the restaurant. Online orders are for pickup only, and table seating is not guaranteed; the site also has a catering page.

Address and phone were read on the restaurant's home, history and contact pages on 10 October 2026. The Town of Georgetown's Where to Eat page (https://georgetown-colorado.org/where-to-eat/, last updated 9 September 2026) lists "The Happy Cooker" at 412 6th Street with the same phone, so it is operating; it waits only on hours from the restaurant itself.
