The Town of Georgetown's Where to Eat page (last updated 9 September 2026) lists Scooters Smokehouse and Grill at 1416 Argentine Street, 720-955-6639, quoting the restaurant: "We know you can smell our smoke the moment you roll into town." Its Toast online-ordering page, read the same day under the name "Scooter's Smokehouse BBQ 1416 Argentine St", gives the same address and the phone 720-955-6629, lists every day of the week as closed and says online ordering is unavailable; the handoff packet's own notes called that page's hours display unreliable. The domain scooterssmokehousebbq.com shows a parked page.

The handoff packet describes, unconfirmed, a menu of brisket, pulled pork, sausage, poultry and ribs with classic sides. Nothing here was read on a page of the restaurant's own; the Town's page, read 10 October 2026, is the only current listing found.
