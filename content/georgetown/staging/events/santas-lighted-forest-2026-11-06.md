An evening round trip of about 60 minutes through a lighted forest, with Santa aboard every departure before Christmas. The railroad also runs Santa's Rocky Mountain Adventure by day over the same dates, a New Year's train from 26 December to 1 January and an Encore train on 2 and 3 January.
