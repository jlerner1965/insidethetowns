The Town's governing board meets at 6 pm on the second and fourth Tuesdays of the month at Town Hall, with a Zoom option whose link is on the second page of each agenda. This is the regular schedule from the Town's Boards and Commissions page; the Town posts cancellations and reschedulings on its news list (https://www.townofgeorgetown.us/newslist.php), so check there before going.
