## Who lives here

Lafayette is a home-rule city in southeastern Boulder County, governed by a seven-member City Council elected citywide in odd-numbered years, which chooses its own mayor; Council meets at 5:30 pm on the first and third Tuesdays at City Hall, 1290 South Public Road ([City Council](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/247/City-Council)). The 2020 Census counted 30,411 people, up from 24,453 in 2010 and 2,612 in 1960 ([State Demography Office census file](https://storage.googleapis.com/co-publicdata/historical-census.csv)). The town was platted by Mary Miller on 150 acres of her farm in 1888 and grew as a coal town ([This Date in Lafayette History](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/873/This-Date-in-Lafayette-History)); the [history article](/articles/lafayette-history/) has the rest.

## Housing

The City runs no rental program and no price survey of its own. What its Affordable Housing page gives: a for-sale program run with the City of Boulder, in which deed-restricted homes sell below market to income-qualified buyers who will live in them, with a City pre-qualification letter (valid six months) required before an offer and listings kept open 60 days; when read, it listed a four-bedroom at 775 Bedivere Circle for $402,200 and a three-bedroom at 1585 Sagrimore Circle for $262,100. Rentals at Willoughby Corner are managed by the Boulder County Housing Authority, 303-441-3929 ([Affordable Housing](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/342/Affordable-Housing)). The City adopted a rewritten Land Use Code and zoning map on September 15, 2026, in force from February 1, 2027 ([City news](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=10240)). A short-term rental must be the owner’s primary residence, licensed at $200 for two years with a safety inspection; a $100 festival license for City-recognized events is a pilot through June 1, 2027 ([Short-Term Rentals](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/3959/Short-Term-Rentals)). The listings in the sidebar show what is for sale.

## Schools

Lafayette is in the Boulder Valley School District. A new student is guaranteed a seat at the neighborhood school for the address, which the district’s [K-12 School Finder](https://www.bvsd.org/departments/enrollment/k-12-school-finder) shows; in Lafayette those are Lafayette, Ryan, Alicia Sanchez and Escuela Bilingüe Pioneer elementaries, Angevine Middle and Centaurus High, with Peak to Peak a K–12 charter. Choice enrollment to any BVSD school for 2027–28 has an on-time window from November 1 to December 18, 2026, with lottery offers about December 23 ([Choice Enrollment](https://www.bvsd.org/parents-students/enrollment-center/choice-enrollment)). The schools’ addresses and this year’s days off are on the [Families page](/families/).

## Getting around

Lafayette sits where US 287 crosses Baseline Road and South Boulder Road, between Boulder and I-25. RTD lists four bus routes with Lafayette in their names: the JUMP (Boulder / Lafayette / Erie, along Arapahoe Road), the 225 (Boulder / Lafayette via Baseline), the DASH (Boulder / Lafayette via Louisville) and the 228 (Lafayette / Louisville / Broomfield) ([RTD: find a schedule](https://rtd-denver.com/routes-services/find-a-schedule)); the JUMP’s timetable ends at the Lafayette Park-n-Ride, which the DASH timetable lists with 136 spaces ([RTD schedule PDFs](https://schedule-pdfs.rtd-denver.com/api/file/current/JUMP/Eastbound/2)). A standard fare is $2.75 for three hours and youth 19 and under ride free ([RTD fares](https://www.rtd-denver.com/fares-passes/fares)). Inside the city, Ride Free Lafayette is Boulder County’s free, on-demand, door-to-door shuttle, seven days a week from 7 am to 8 pm, booked at 833-456-3359, in its app or at rfl.ecolane.com ([Ride Free Lafayette](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/RideFreeLafayette)). The City plows primary and secondary snow routes, not every street, and property owners must clear their sidewalks within 24 hours of the last measurable snow ([Snow and Ice Control](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/222/Snow-and-Ice-Control)). The soft-surface [Coal Creek Trail](/places/coal-creek-trail/) runs 3.3 miles through town on a 14-mile county route, and the City counts more than 20 miles of trail and 1,640 acres of open space ([Open Space](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/160/Open-Space)).

## Services

- **Water, sewer and stormwater** are the City’s. About 80 percent of the drinking water is Boulder Creek basin water stored at Goose Haven and Baseline reservoirs, the rest Colorado-Big Thompson and Windy Gap water through Northern Water ([Water Resources](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/2780/Water-Resources)). Utility Billing is 303-604-3555; a home sale is transferred by the title company, renters use the Start and Stop Service forms, and mailed payments go to a Dallas lockbox from October 1, 2026 ([Utility Billing](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/776/Utility-Billing)). Drought Stage 1 has been in effect since April 10, 2026: two watering days a week, none from 10 am to 6 pm ([Drought Stage 1](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=9982)).
- **Trash, recycling and compost** come through the City’s Three Cart Program with Republic Services (303-286-2400), billed on the City utility bill: $19.32 a month in 2026 for a 32-gallon trash cart, $30.49 for 64 gallons and $41.67 for 96, each with weekly compost and every-other-week recycling included; 2027 rates rise to $19.95, $31.53 and $43.11 ([Three Cart Program](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/2420/Three-Cart-Program)).
- **Electricity and gas**: the City’s pages do not name the provider, but its Energy page points customers to Xcel Energy’s rebates and weatherization program ([Energy](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/1283/Energy)).
- **Police** is the Lafayette Police Department, 451 North 111th Street, 303-665-5571; to file a report or request an officer, Boulder County dispatch at 303-441-4444; emergencies 911 ([File a Police Report](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/1849/File-A-Police-Report)). **Fire** is the Lafayette Fire Department, an ISO Class 2 department with stations at 401 North 111th Street and 511 Exempla Circle, 303-665-9661 ([Fire Department](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/94/Fire-Department)).
- **Library** is the City’s [Lafayette Public Library](/places/lafayette-public-library/) at 775 West Baseline Road, open Monday to Thursday 10 to 8, Friday and Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday 1 to 5 ([Hours](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/1527/Hours-Location)).
- **Recreation** is the City’s [Bob L. Burger Recreation Center](/places/bob-l-burger-recreation-center/), $5.75 a day for adults, which voters agreed in November 2025 to renovate and expand with a $34 million share of a $40 million bond; design runs to April 2027 and construction 18 to 24 months from 2027 ([Lafayette Listens](https://lafayette-listens.com/capital-bond)). The Senior Center is at 103 South Iowa Avenue ([Senior Services](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/265/Senior-Services)).
- **Elections** are run by Boulder County by mail ballot, with a 24-hour drop box at the library and a vote center and drive-up box at the Southeast County Community Hub, 1755 South Public Road ([Elections](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/424/Elections)).

## Before you decide

- **Water.** The City has been in Drought Stage 1 since April and asked in October that lawn watering stop for the season; its draft Water Efficiency and Drought Response Plan will set what later stages mean ([Water Resources](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/2780/Water-Resources)).
- **Snow.** Not every street is plowed, and your sidewalk is your job within 24 hours ([Snow and Ice Control](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/222/Snow-and-Ice-Control)); the Senior Center’s [Snow Angels](/programs/snow-angels/) shovel for residents 55 and over who cannot.
- **The airport.** The City is challenging FAA flight-path changes at Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport and tells residents how to file noise complaints ([City news](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=9934)).
- **Construction.** Intrepid Fiber, a private company, is laying about 30 miles of fiber through town ([City news](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=9596)), and the recreation center rebuild starts in 2027.
