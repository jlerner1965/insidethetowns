Kukur Tihar, a day of Nepal’s five-day festival of lights, honors dogs with blessings, flowers and treats for their loyalty and companionship. The City’s Arts and Cultural Resources Department and Nepali Jatra hold it on Sunday, November 15, from noon to 2 pm; it is free, and dogs are welcome. The afternoon also includes Deusi Bhailo, the tradition in which performers go house to house singing, dancing and blessing families.
