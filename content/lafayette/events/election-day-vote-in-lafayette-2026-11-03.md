Lafayette has two Boulder County vote centers open on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, from 7 am to 7 pm: the Lafayette Public Library, 775 W Baseline Road, and the Southeast County Community Hub, 1755 S Public Road. Both take ballots, register voters and issue replacement ballots. The county’s 24-hour drop boxes at the same two addresses stay open until 7 pm (the Hub’s is drive-up; the library’s is not), and every ballot must be received, not postmarked, by then. Lafayette’s own question on the ballot is Issue 2A, which would extend an expiring property tax for Lamont Does Park through 2046 at no more than $713,000 a year, per the City’s ballot-measure page. The County’s Elections Division is at 303-413-7740.
