A winter walk on Coal Creek, Sunday, December 13, from 1 to 3 pm, on telling dormant plants apart by their shape and shade, on what grows under the snow, and on looking at the winter landscape with an artist’s eye: bring a camera, or a phone. Register on the City’s Engage site (3 to 30 people, all ages); the meeting point is Coal Creek at Elysian Field Drive. Bring layers, good shoes and something warm to drink. The City says the walk is wheelchair accessible as long as the trail is not covered in snow. The contact is Basil Binkley, 970-576-9209.
