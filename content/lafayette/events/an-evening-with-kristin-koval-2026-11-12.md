Colorado author Kristin Koval, a former attorney, talks about her debut novel *Penitence*, a literary thriller about family loyalty, redemption and forgiveness, on Thursday, November 12, from 6:30 to 7:30 pm in the library’s meeting room, with a book signing after. The library presents it with the support of The Read Queen Bookstore and Cafe on North Harrison Avenue.
