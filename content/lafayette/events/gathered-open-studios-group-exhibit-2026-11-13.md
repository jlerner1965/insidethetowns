The Collective’s winter show, "Gathered," hangs from November 13 to January 10, 2027: work by members of Open Studios, the Boulder County nonprofit that has connected local visual artists with the public since 1995, on what it means to gather objects, ideas, memories, people and places. It opens with a reception on Friday, November 13, from 6 to 8 pm, and the artists give a talk on Saturday, December 12, from 1 to 3 pm; Cultural Caravan’s [Behind the Music](/events/behind-the-music-cultural-caravan-2026-11-18/) is in the gallery on November 18 at 7. The Collective, the City’s arts center at 201 North Public Road, is open Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, Friday 10 to 7 and Sunday noon to 5, closed Mondays, and the City calendar lists it closed on Thanksgiving and the day after, on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, and on New Year’s Day. 303-661-1261.
