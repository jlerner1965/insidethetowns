The Collective’s next show, "Gathered – Open Studios Group Exhibit," hangs from November 13 to January 10, 2027, after the Día de los Muertos exhibit closes on November 8 and the gallery shuts for two days to install it. The Collective, the City’s arts center at 201 North Public Road, is open Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, Friday 10 to 7 and Sunday noon to 5, closed Mondays, and the City calendar lists it closed on Thanksgiving, the day after and New Year’s Day. The City’s page does not list an opening reception or an admission charge; the Collective’s exhibits are free to walk into. 303-661-1261.
