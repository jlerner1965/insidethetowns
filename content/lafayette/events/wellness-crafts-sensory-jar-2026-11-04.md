Kids and teens drop in any time during the hour at the table on the library’s upper level for a short mindfulness craft with Clinica Family Health & Wellness. November’s is a calming sensory jar, made from colorful and textured materials, to take home. The library runs one each month; December 2 is a gingerbread-themed self-affirmation craft.
