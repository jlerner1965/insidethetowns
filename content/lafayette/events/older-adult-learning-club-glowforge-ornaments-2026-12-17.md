Adults 50 and older cut winter-themed ornaments on the makerspace’s Glowforge laser cutter on Thursday, December 17, from 1 to 3 pm. Sign up through the Senior Center’s registration site, 303-665-9052 or olderadults@lafayetteco.gov. The makerspace then closes for maintenance from December 21 to January 1.
