"Celebrate our Thanksgiving role models by joining us for a walk along Coal Creek," the City’s listing says: an hour on Saturday, November 14, from 1 to 2 pm, on the wild turkeys that live along the creek, their behavior and adaptations and their place in the ecosystem. The walk takes 15 people and [the City’s Engage registration](https://engage.lafayetteco.gov/ActivityRegistration/78948054-f7be-4b62-8c64-d6f74447ad1d) now offers a waitlist in place of sign-up; join it in case a place opens. Engage puts the meeting point at Coal Creek and Elysian Field Drive. All ages are welcome, and the group walks up to a mile with stops, so wear comfortable shoes. The City’s Open Space program follows it with a winter plant-identification walk from the same spot on December 13.
