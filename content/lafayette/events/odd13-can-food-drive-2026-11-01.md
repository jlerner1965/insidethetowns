From November 1 through November 24, bring five non-perishable canned food items to the Odd13 taproom on East Simpson Street and get a free draft beer, per the brewery’s events page. The taproom is open Monday to Thursday 2 to 10 pm, Friday and Saturday noon to 10 and Sunday noon to 8.
