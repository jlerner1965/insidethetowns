Motus Theater and the Parent Engagement Network present *What Love Requires*, a new autobiographical monologue project of stories by parents of transgender and nonbinary adults, on Saturday, November 14, from 6:30 to 8:30 pm at The Collective. The monologists are Beth Leyba, Lynn Kutner and Cat Lindsey, with music by violinist Anthony Salvo.
