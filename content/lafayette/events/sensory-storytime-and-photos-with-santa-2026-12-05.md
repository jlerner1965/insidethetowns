A holiday Sensory Storytime with photos with Santa on Saturday, December 5, in the library’s lower-level Meeting Room, for kids ages 2 to 8 who are neurodiverse or have sensory needs. It runs twice, at 10:30 and at 11:30, 45 minutes each, with structured storytime, singing and books read by a board-certified behavior analyst, and sensory toys for any child who needs them. Each session takes 25 people, parents and children counted, and everyone attending registers separately on the library’s calendar: [10:30 session](https://lafayettepubliclibrary.libcal.com/calendar/main/SensorySanta2026_Dec5_A), [11:30 session](https://lafayettepubliclibrary.libcal.com/calendar/main/SensoryStorytime2026_Dec5_B).
