Books & Brews, a book club for adults 21 and over who read young-adult novels, meets at Odd13 Brewing on Thursday, November 19, from 7 to 8 pm; it meets on the third Thursday of each month, and next on December 17. The library carries it on its calendar as an independent club, not a library event, and Odd13’s events page lists it as "Book Club Meet Up." Neither page names the month’s book: for that, or anything else about the club, the library’s listing gives Sara C. at 303-868-0654.
