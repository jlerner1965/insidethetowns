A free pop-up mending circle in the gallery on Sunday, November 22, from 1 to 4 pm: bring a favorite piece of clothing that needs repair and learn to mend it visibly, from reinforced knees and elbows to decorated collars and hems and Japanese Sashiko stitching. It is a group of people sharing skills rather than a class. Non-stretchy fabric works best (denim, cotton or linen), and everything else is provided. It is for adults; anyone 16 or under comes with an adult who stays. Sign-up is on The Stitch Sitch’s Instagram (instagram.com/the.stitch.sitch). A Lafayette Downtown Development Authority grant pays for it.
