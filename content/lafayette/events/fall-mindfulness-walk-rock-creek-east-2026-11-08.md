A slow walk with a mindfulness practice along the Rock Creek trail on Sunday, November 8, from 2:30 to 4:30 pm, led by Basil B. of Growth Point for the City’s Open Space program. It is geared to the LGBTQIA2S+ community, and friends and allies are welcome. The trail is mild with little elevation change; the planned route is not wheelchair accessible, but the City says it will change the route if asked. Register on the City’s Engage site (3 to 30 people, log-in required); the contact is Basil Binkley, 970-576-9209. The trailhead is at 120th Street and Rock Creek Court, just south of Horizon Avenue, with no restroom.
