The City’s project team gives a neighborhood update on the coming construction at the Lafayette Service Center on Thursday, November 19, from 5:30 to 6:30 pm, with hot cocoa. It is one of three City facility projects paid for by the Capital Bond voters approved in 2025; site work is expected to start in April 2027, with construction and renovation through early 2028. The evening introduces the team, presents the project and shows parts of the design. The entrance is at Distel Drive and Avalon Avenue; follow the signs to parking. The meeting is indoors in an area without central heating, so dress for the weather and wear closed-toe shoes.
