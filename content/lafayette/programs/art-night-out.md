Art Night Out closes South Public Road between Emma and Cannon streets on the second Friday of each month from May through September for a street festival that the City describes as "live music, street performers, an art market with 50 vendors," food trucks and a beer, wine and cider garden, from 5 pm, with dancing on [Festival Plaza](/places/festival-plaza/) until 9. It is free. The City’s page thanks the town for the 2026 season and does not yet give 2027 dates; the Chamber’s Peach Festival in August and its [Holiday on the Plaza](/events/holiday-on-the-plaza-2026-12-05/) in December use the same plaza.
