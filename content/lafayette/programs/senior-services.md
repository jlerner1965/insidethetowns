Senior Services runs from the [Senior Center](/places/lafayette-senior-center/) at 103 South Iowa Avenue, weekdays from 8:30. Its page groups what it offers as activities, trips and tours, enrichment, fitness and wellness, with an Older Adult Artist Showcase and an Older Adults Resource Fair among its events, and it coordinates the [Snow Angels](/programs/snow-angels/) snow-removal program. With the library it runs the monthly Older Adult Learning Club in the makerspace: [Cricut stickers on November 19](/events/older-adult-learning-club-cricut-stickers-2026-11-19/), Glowforge ornaments on December 17 and 3D printing on January 21. The library’s Coffee & Conversation, free coffee and talk on first and third Saturdays from 10 to 11, is for older adults too. Fees and any age rule are not on the page; call 303-665-9052.
