The City counts 1,640 acres of open space and more than 20 miles of trails, and it staffs them partly with volunteers. **Trail Ambassadors** patrol the trails, answer visitors’ questions and report problems. **Open Space Stewards** do hands-on restoration: pulling noxious weeds, watering new plantings, planting natives and spreading seed. **Naturalists** identify and record plants and animals on iNaturalist or eBird, and some join Native Bee Watch, monarch monitoring or raptor-nest monitoring. **Shack volunteers** staff the interpretive station at [Waneka Lake](/places/the-shack-at-waneka-lake/) on weekend afternoons in the cold months and mornings in summer. **Event support** means tabling and guiding at the program’s walks, like the [turkey walk on November 14](/events/wild-turkeys-of-coal-creek-2026-11-14/). Apply on Engage; groups and one-offs go through the coordinator.
