B.A.S.E. picks children up from four elementary schools, Pioneer, Peak to Peak, Lafayette and Ryan, and brings them to the recreation center for the afternoon; a school gets a bus only with at least five enrolled children that day. The program follows the BVSD calendar, so there is none on days off (the City’s [School’s Out camps](/programs/schools-out-camps/) cover those). Monthly fees for 2026–27 run from $275 for two days a week to $515 for five for residents, $330 to $615 for non-residents, plus a $35 registration fee. Pre-registration for this year opened in April; ask youth@lafayetteco.gov about space now.
