The library’s weekday storytimes run at 10:30 am from August 18 to November 20: **Baby Storytime** on Tuesdays, early-literacy songs, rhymes, books and bounces for babies from birth to 24 months and their caregivers; **Family Storytime** on Wednesdays and Fridays, stories, songs and rhymes for toddlers through preschool age; and **Bilingual Storytime** on Thursdays, in English and Spanish for all ages. The room moves between the lower-level Meeting Room and the upstairs Kid Zone, so check the sign in the lobby. **Saturday Storytime**, for all ages, runs August 1 to December 26 at 10:30 unless a special storytime or event takes its place; on November 21 and December 19 [Lotería family bingo](/events/loteria-mexicana-family-bingo-2026-11-21/) follows it at 11:30.

**Sensory Storytime**, for ages 2 to 8 and designed for neurodiverse children and those with sensory needs, is a structured half hour of singing, books read by a board-certified behavior analyst and sensory toys, in the Meeting Room on Saturday, November 28, at 10:30. The storytimes page does not say when the winter session begins; the library is closed November 11, 26 and 27, December 24 and 25 and January 1.
