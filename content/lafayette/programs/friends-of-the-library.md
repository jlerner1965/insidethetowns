"Their mission is to support the Lafayette Public Library in Colorado," the library’s page says of the Friends, who run a year-round bookstore in the lobby and quarterly lobby sales with books from 50 cents to $3, and fund the Summer Reading Program and the Studio 775 Makerspace among other programs. Donations of books in good condition are welcome. The [holiday sale](/events/friends-holiday-book-sale-2026-12-04/) runs December 4 to 6, with $5 bags on the Sunday. Meetings are monthly in the library’s meeting room and open to anyone; membership is through folfco.org.
