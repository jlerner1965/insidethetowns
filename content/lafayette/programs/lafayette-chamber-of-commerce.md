The Chamber has been "the voice of our business community since 1954," its site says. A membership brings a listing with a link on lafayettecolorado.com, a business spotlight on its Facebook and Instagram at each renewal, event listings on the Chamber calendar, a ribbon cutting for an opening, referrals, four free inserts a year in a monthly e-newsletter that reaches more than 1,000 contacts, 10 percent off local digital billboard rates and a name badge. Members meet at after-hours mixers, Women’s Business Network luncheons and Coffee Talks, and may join the leads group. The 2026 dues table is on the benefits page; a second business under the same owner is half price. The office at [304 West Baseline Road](/places/lafayette-chamber-of-commerce/) is open weekdays 9 to 4.
