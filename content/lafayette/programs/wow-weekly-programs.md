WOW!’s calendar repeats a weekly set of programs, each run by a museum team member and included with admission or membership. **Littlest Learners**, Wednesdays from 9:30 to 10, is for infants and toddlers up to 3 with their caregivers. **Storytime** is Tuesdays at 10 and Thursdays at 11, half an hour for all ages. **STEAM to the MAX** is after-school science, technology, engineering, art and math on Thursdays from 3:30 to 4:15. **Science Spot** is Fridays at 10. One-offs on the November calendar: Story Craft with Korrie on Friday, November 13 at 10, and a Sensory Friendly Playtime listed for November 15, a day the calendar also shows the museum closed, so ask before going. The museum closes at 3 pm on November 7 for its 30th birthday fundraiser and on Thanksgiving Day.
