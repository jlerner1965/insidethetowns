The Youth Advisory Board is "composed of people ages 11–17 who reside or attend school within the City of Lafayette," the City says, and it wants students from every middle and high school and homeschoolers. It advises Council on youth matters, promotes civic engagement and leadership, plans events and activities and makes recommendations based on its own research and community dialogue. Meetings are the first Wednesday of the month at 6:30 pm at the library, open to the public, and only as needed in summer; the library’s calendar moved the November 4 meeting to the recreation center. Members serve September to August and reapply each year. The next application window is May 1 to June 30, 2027.
