Sister Carmen Community Center assists residents of Lafayette, Louisville, Superior and Erie who are in need, without discrimination. Its food bank and office are at 655 Aspen Ridge Drive and its thrift store at 701 Baseline Road; on the third Thursday of each month the center opens at 1 pm. Food bank shopping and donation hours are on its site, and the main line is 303-665-4342. To help, start on its [Give Time](https://sistercarmen.org/give-help/give-time/) page.
