The library’s Teen Team is for volunteers 14 and older. **Reading Buddies** mentor kids in kindergarten to third grade through reading and games on Thursdays from 4:30 to 5:30; **Writing Buddies** plan lessons, prompts and games for third to fifth graders on Wednesdays; **Spanish Buddies**, who must be proficient in Spanish, mentor third to fifth graders on writing on Tuesdays, after a training. Signing up for a Buddy program commits you to the whole session, and each training and meeting counts as service hours; the fall sessions ran from late September to late October, and the winter dates were not posted when this was read.

Year-round there are **Teen Service Sundays**, 1 to 3 pm shifts tidying, organizing and light shelving, and **Teen Service Events**: setup, cleanup and head counts at the Fall Mini-Con, summer Saturday events, the MLK celebration and author talks. Apply once through the Teen Team form; current volunteers add shifts on Engage as they are published.
