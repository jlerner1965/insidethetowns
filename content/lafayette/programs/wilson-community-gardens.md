The City’s community garden at [500 South Miller Avenue](/places/wilson-community-gardens/) rents in-ground beds from 6-by-12 to 20-by-20 feet and 2-by-8 raised beds for a season that runs March 1 to November 30. Residents pay $40 to $80 a plot and non-residents $48 to $96, put down half as a deposit that comes back in November if the rules are kept, and may rent two plots at most. Registration for 2027 opens January 7 for residents and January 14 for everyone, on WebTrac or at the recreation center. A plot must show weeding, planting or turned soil by April 15 or it can be given to someone else and the deposit kept.
