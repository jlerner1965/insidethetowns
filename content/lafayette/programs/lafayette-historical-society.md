The society has collected Lafayette’s history since 1976 and runs the [museum](/places/lafayette-history-museum/) in the Lewis House on East Simpson Street. **Members** pay $20 a year as individuals, $35 for a family, $125 for a business or $300 as a History Hero, which adds two behind-the-scenes sessions with staff a year; a $250 lifetime membership never renews, and every other kind expires December 31. **Docents** greet visitors and lead small tours for at least two two-hour shifts a month, with Saturday and Thursday shifts the current need. **Research volunteers** answer history requests, often from home, about two hours each. **Collections volunteers** catalog and care for the 4,000-plus objects in three-hour shifts, Tuesday to Friday, for at least four months. The board meets monthly at the library and the meetings are public (next December 10 and January 14, per the library calendar); the annual members’ meeting on November 7 is for members only.
