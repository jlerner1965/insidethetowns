The Arts Hub, a nonprofit at [420 Courtney Way](/places/the-arts-hub/), runs "a variety of performing and visual arts classes for all ages," from acting to dance, by semester, with sign-ups for spring 2027 linked from its site. Its youth programs are the **Arts Hub Thespians** for ages 11 to 16, the **Treble Singers** vocal ensemble, private lessons and camps; its **Adult Mainstage** holds auditions open to anyone. The youth cast of *Little Women* (ages 13 to 18, auditions November 2) rehearses Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from January 5, runs tech nightly from February 28, and plays Saturday matinees at 2 pm on March 6, 13 and 20, with a performance at the Senior Center on March 12. A coworking-with-childcare option for parents of infants and toddlers is on the site too.
