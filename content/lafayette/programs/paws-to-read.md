"Spend 15 minutes reading to a special therapy dog! They are cuddly, good listeners who would love to hear your stories," the library’s listing says. The dogs come from the Alliance of Therapy Dogs. Sessions are on Sunday afternoons, 1:30 to 2:30, in the Kid Zone: [November 8](/events/paws-to-read-2026-11-08/), [December 13](/events/paws-to-read-2026-12-13/), January 10 and January 24, with the 2027 series continuing monthly on the library’s calendar.
