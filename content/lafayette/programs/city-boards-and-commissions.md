**Serve on one.** The City’s boards are the Lafayette Cultural Arts Board, the Historic Preservation Commission, the IDEA Board (formerly the Human Rights Board), the [Youth Advisory Board](/programs/youth-advisory-board/), the Library Board, the Mental Health and Human Services Grant Program Advisory Committee, the Older Adults Advisory Board, the Open Space Advisory Board (LOSAB), the Planning Commission, the Public Art Committee and the Sustainability and Resilience Advisory Board. Applicants must have lived in the city for at least 12 months before appointment and be registered voters; residents under 18 who have lived here a year or attend a city middle or high school may apply to the Youth Advisory Board. The City recruits in the fall, with applications open August 24 to September 18 this year, interviews in October, Council appointments in November, terms beginning December 1 with annual training and first meetings in January 2027, and again in the spring.

**Sit in.** The meetings are public, and the City calendar lists them: the Open Space Advisory Board at 6 pm on the first Thursday (no December meeting), the Planning Commission on second and fourth Wednesdays at 6, the Public Art Committee at the Starkey Building and the Sustainability board at City Hall on November 12 at 6, the IDEA Board at the library on third Mondays at 6, the Cultural Arts Board at The Collective on November 19 at 6:30 and the Older Adults Advisory Board at the Senior Center on November 10 at 4:30. Dates move for holidays; the Historic Preservation Commission’s November meeting was rescheduled to the 5th. Agendas are on the City’s Archive page.
