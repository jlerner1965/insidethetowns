Lafayette is in the Boulder Valley School District. A new student is guaranteed a seat at the neighborhood school for the address, which BVSD’s [K-12 School Finder](https://www.bvsd.org/departments/enrollment/k-12-school-finder) and its [map of school locations](https://bvsd.finder.schoolmint.com/) show; the maps now reflect 2027–28 attendance areas, and a family near a boundary can email enrollment.helpdesk@bvsd.org. The schools in Lafayette: Lafayette Elementary (101 N Bermont Ave), Ryan Elementary STEAM School (1405 Centaur Village Dr), Alicia Sanchez Elementary (655 Sir Galahad Dr), Escuela Bilingüe Pioneer (101 Baseline Rd), Angevine Middle (1150 W South Boulder Rd), Centaurus High (10300 S Boulder Rd) and Peak to Peak Charter, K–12 (800 Merlin Dr), whose enrollment also runs through BVSD’s office.

**Choice enrollment** lets a family apply to any BVSD school. The on-time window is November 1 to December 18, 2026, in SchoolMint; seat offers from the lottery come on or about December 23, an offer must be accepted within 13 days and followed by registration, and wait lists move as space opens until August 31, 2027. Centaurus holds a [showcase night on November 10](/events/centaurus-choice-enrollment-showcase-night-2026-11-10/) and Friday tours. This year’s days off school are on this guide’s Families page.
