The athletics page lists the Warriors’ teams by season. **Fall**: cheer, cross country, dance and poms, girls’ field hockey, football, girls’ flag football, boys’ golf, gymnastics (on Broomfield’s team), boys’ soccer, softball, boys’ tennis and girls’ volleyball. **Winter**: boys’ and girls’ basketball, ice hockey (housed at Monarch), alpine and Nordic skiing (on Nederland’s team), girls’ swim and dive, boys’ wrestling and girls’ wrestling (with Broomfield). **Spring**: baseball, girls’ golf, boys’ lacrosse (with Monarch) and girls’ lacrosse (with Fairview), girls’ soccer, boys’ swim and dive (with Monarch), girls’ tennis, track and field and boys’ volleyball. Girls’ swim and ice hockey begin November 9 and basketball and wrestling November 16.

CHSAA lists Centaurus in the Granite Peaks league, in 4A for football and 6A for basketball. The official schedule is on ArbiterLive and the school’s own calendar; results are on [this guide’s sports page](/sports/). The first home wrestling dual is [December 3 against Fairview](/events/boys-wrestling-vs-fairview-centaurus-2026-12-03/).
