Lafayette’s City Council has seven members, "elected on a non-partisan basis in odd-numbered years": four seats are filled each election, the three top vote-getters for four years and the fourth for two, and Council chooses its mayor and mayor pro tem for two-year terms. The current mayor is Saul Tapia Vega, with Tim Barnes as mayor pro tem; three councilors’ terms end in November 2027 and four in November 2029. Meetings are at 5:30 on the first and third Tuesdays in Council Chambers, with streaming video; the City calendar shows them running to 9 pm, and a workshop on November 24.

To speak, attend and sign up for the Public Input period. A written comment, in English or Spanish, goes through the Public Input Form and, if received by 1 pm on the day, into that night’s packet, though it is not read aloud. By phone, call 1-877-853-5247 with meeting number 869 1163 2580 and press *9 during Public Input. Planning-application hearings use a separate testimony form. The City’s own ballot question this November, Issue 2A on the Lamont Does Park tax, is a mail-ballot election run with Boulder County; the [boards and commissions](/programs/city-boards-and-commissions/) are the other way in.
