Four roles were open on the library’s adult volunteer page when this was read. **Makerspace volunteers** help patrons in Studio 775 during drop-in hours with 3D print designs, laser cuts, the sewing machines and the Cricut, with training provided. A **Conversations in English facilitator**, a native English speaker, leads the Saturday 11 to 12:15 group on everyday topics. A **Conversations in Spanish facilitator**, an adult native Spanish speaker, leads the new Wednesday evening group, 6 to 7:15. A **Writers Workshop facilitator** leads the weekly drop-in writers’ group. The library prefers people who can commit to six months, and adults pass a background check before they start. The Lafayette Historical Society, the Friends of the Library and the City’s Open Space program take volunteers too.
