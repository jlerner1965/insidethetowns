The City’s youth sports page lists the programs by season. **Winter**: Tiny Tykes Basketball for kindergartners, Pee Wee Basketball for first and second graders, Youth Basketball for kindergarten through eighth grade, basketball clinics and indoor soccer. **Spring**: Tiny Tykes Soccer for 3-year-olds, Pee Wee Soccer for 4- and 5-year-olds, Youth Soccer K–8, tennis lessons for ages 4 to 14 and racquetball lessons for 9 to 15. **Summer**: tennis, T-Ball for 3 to 6, Soft Pitch Baseball for 7 and 8, baseball and softball for 9 to 11, and soccer, basketball and volleyball camps and clinics. **Fall**: soccer from Tiny Tykes to K–8, Flag Football for grades 1 to 8 and racquetball. The page gives no season dates, deadlines or prices; those are on WebTrac, where residents can usually register before non-residents, or from Lafayette Recreation at 303-665-0469. Lafayette residents on certain assistance programs get half off youth activity fees through the Reduced Rate Program.
