When Boulder Valley schools are closed, the City runs full-day camps at the recreation center from 8 am to 5:30 pm, sold a day at a time so a family can take one day or a whole break. The dates through spring: November 11 (Veterans Day), November 23, 24 and 25 (the conference days and the day before Thanksgiving), December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30, January 4 and 18, February 15 to 17 and March 15 to 19. Each costs $60 for residents and $72 for non-residents, and registration closes a week before the camp day; the 2027 dates open December 8 for residents and December 19 for everyone else. You get paperwork and ePACT forms at registration. Pack a lunch, two snacks and a water bottle. The school days off themselves are on this guide’s Families page.
