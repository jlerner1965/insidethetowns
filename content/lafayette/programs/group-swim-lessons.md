The City teaches group lessons in three tracks. **Aqua Baby and Aqua Tots**, for infants through 3 years (6 to 36 months), need one parent in the water with each child and swim diapers. **Dolphin 1 to 4** are for ages 3 to 5. **Levels 1 to 6** start at age 6, with Level 3 split into beginner and advanced. The page has a placement guide by age and skill, from putting a face in the water to swimming five yards alone.

Sessions run on Tuesdays and Thursdays (A: September 8 to October 1; B: October 6 to 29; C: November 2 to 19; D: December 1 to 17) and on Saturdays (A: September 5 to October 10; B: October 17 to November 21). The page does not list class times, prices or when registration opens; they are on WebTrac. The pool is being replaced in the center’s renovation, with construction from 2027.
