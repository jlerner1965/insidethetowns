Studio 775 runs on drop-in hours: Tuesday and Thursday 3:30 to 7:30 pm, Wednesday 1 to 5, and Monday 4:30 to 7:30 as the **Teen Maker Hangout** for grades 6 to 12. Eight patrons at a time, one project each per session, and each person checks in with a library card on every visit; before a first visit you need a regular library card and a completed policies and release form. The space may close for staffing, so the library asks you to check its Maker Calendar for cancellations. Workshops and skills open houses are listed on the Make & Create calendar.

The equipment, as the library lists it: Prusa 3D printers with Cura and Prusa Slicer, a Glowforge laser cutter, two Cricut cutters, a Brother sewing and embroidery machine, button makers in three sizes, a heat press, a manual die-cut machine, a metal stamping kit, a Canon inkjet and Adobe Creative Suite; any of it may be down for maintenance. Adults 50 and over have a monthly workshop here, the [Older Adult Learning Club](/events/older-adult-learning-club-cricut-stickers-2026-11-19/), through the Senior Center. The makerspace closes December 21 to January 1.
