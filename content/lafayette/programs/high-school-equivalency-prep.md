The Coal Creek Adult Education Center and the library run free high school equivalency preparation in the library’s Learning Lab on Tuesday evenings from 6 to 8. "Earning your High School Equivalency Diploma will improve your chances for employment and advancement as well as allow you to attend post-secondary training," the listing says. Sessions may start on the first or second week of each month and new students can join monthly; registration is through the calendar listing. The library’s calendar carries the Tuesday classes through December 15.
