The closed section is near the Lafayette Water Reclamation Facility, where the work builds floodplain improvements and a retaining wall to reduce flood risk. The City announced it on September 3 as a closure of about eight weeks from September 8. Its Cone Zone page, updated October 29, now lists "Coal Creek Trail Temporary Closure and Detour" with an expected completion date of December 7, 2026: "Originally 8 weeks but a delay in equipment delivery extended it several weeks." The detour is on the Cone Zone map, and part of it "is ungroomed and has an uneven surface," so cyclists, scooter and other wheeled users should dismount there. The contact is B2 Excavating, 720-703-9716.
