The closed section is near the Lafayette Water Reclamation Facility, where the work builds floodplain improvements and a retaining wall to reduce flood risk. The City’s notice does not describe the detour’s route; it is on the ConeZone map, and part of it "is ungroomed and has an uneven surface," so cyclists, scooter and other wheeled users should dismount there. Boulder County’s page for the trail adds a separate notice: from October 26 to November 6, heavy equipment crosses the trail near the Flagg Park trailhead junction, with flaggers and minimal delays.
