The closed section is near the Lafayette Water Reclamation Facility, where the work builds floodplain improvements and a retaining wall to reduce flood risk. The City’s notice does not describe the detour’s route; it is on the ConeZone map, and part of it "is ungroomed and has an uneven surface," so cyclists, scooter and other wheeled users should dismount there. The City’s Cone Zone page still lists it under Current Impacts, as "Coal Creek Trail Temporary Closure and Detour," from September 8 for eight weeks, with B2 Excavating, 720-703-9716, as the contact.
