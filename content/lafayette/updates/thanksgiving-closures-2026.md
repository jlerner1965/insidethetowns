The City calendar lists the [library](/places/lafayette-public-library/) and its book drops closing at 5 pm on Wednesday, November 25, and closed all day on Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27. [The Collective](/places/the-collective-community-arts-center/) and the Municipal Court are closed both days, and [Senior Services](/places/lafayette-senior-center/) lists Thanksgiving, November 26 and 27, among its holiday closures. Boulder County's page for Ride Free Lafayette names Thanksgiving among the eight holidays the shuttle does not run. The [Lafayette History Museum](/places/lafayette-history-museum/), which keeps its own hours, is closed for Thanksgiving and reopens at 10 am on Saturday, November 28.
