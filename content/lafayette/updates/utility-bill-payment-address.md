The Utility Billing page lists the new mailing address, effective October 1, alongside its older instruction to mail payments to 1290 South Public Road, without saying the old one is withdrawn, so this guide notes both. The rest of the page is unchanged: a $10 late fee once two bills are delinquent, $15 door-hanger and $35 shutoff fees inside city limits (double outside), and no checks accepted to reconnect service after a disconnection. Home sales are transferred by the title company; renters use the Start and Stop Service forms, and the City will not backdate a request.
