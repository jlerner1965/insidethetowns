The City's notice, posted November 4, lists the facilities closed on Wednesday, November 11: City Hall, [The Collective Community Arts Center](/places/the-collective-community-arts-center/), the [library](/places/lafayette-public-library/), the Municipal Court, the police records and administrative offices, the Public Works Service Center and [Senior Services](/places/lafayette-senior-center/). The [Bob L. Burger Recreation Center](/places/bob-l-burger-recreation-center/) and [Indian Peaks Golf Course](/places/indian-peaks-golf-course/) stay open, the course weather permitting. The City calendar adds that the library's book drops are locked from 8 pm on Tuesday, November 10, until 9 am on Thursday, November 12.
