Stage 1 targets a total demand reduction of up to 10 percent and an outdoor reduction of up to 17 percent. Its rules, from the City’s notice: limit outdoor watering to two days a week (the notice does not assign days); no watering between 10 am and 6 pm; no water waste, meaning no runoff onto sidewalks, curbs, driveways or streets; and no washing paved areas with a hose or power washer. On October 14 the City asked residents to end routine lawn watering voluntarily and winterize irrigation systems while the drought continues. About 80 percent of Lafayette’s water comes from the Boulder Creek basin and the rest from Northern Water’s Colorado-Big Thompson and Windy Gap projects, stored at Goose Haven and Baseline reservoirs, per the City’s Water Resources page.
