The question, set by Resolution 2026-19, asked whether the City could extend "without imposing a new tax" a property tax voters approved in 2014 and set to expire in December 2026, at up to $713,000 a year in total, through December 31, 2046, with the money for "capital improvements to, and the continued operation and maintenance of, Lamont Does Park and related facilities." City Council voted on October 20 to support it, alongside the county’s early-childhood mill levy, the five-member commissioners question and the Front Range passenger rail district. The county had counted 172,118 ballots across Boulder County by its update of 11:05 pm on November 6; its Statement of Votes, canvass documents and the audit follow before the result is certified.
