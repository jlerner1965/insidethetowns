The sweeping zones are divided by Baseline Road and Highway 287, and the City says the dates are approximate and may move for weather or emergencies. Leaves and clippings in the gutter block the storm drains, which is why raking them into the street is prohibited. Republic Services’ curbside leaf and branch pickups for Three Cart households use different lines, South Boulder Road and 287: Saturday, November 7, was the date for homes south of South Boulder Road; Saturday, November 14, is for those north of it and east of 287, and Saturday, November 21, for those north of it and west of 287.
