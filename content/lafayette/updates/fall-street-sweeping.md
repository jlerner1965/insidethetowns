kind: notice
stage: underway
summary: "The City sweeps every street this fall in four zones: south of Baseline and east of US 287 October 26 to November 6, south of Baseline and west of 287 November 9 to 20, north of Baseline and east of 287 November 16 to 27, and north of Baseline and west of 287 November 30 to December 11. Dates shift with weather."
affects: "Everyone who parks on a Lafayette street, and anyone raking leaves"
action: "Keep leaves and yard waste out of the street and gutters (blowing or raking them in is prohibited under the Stormwater Ordinance), and use Republic Services’ curbside leaf cleanups on November 7, 14 or 21 if you are on the Three Cart Program."

The sweeping zones are divided by Baseline Road and Highway 287, and the City says the dates are approximate and may move for weather or emergencies. Leaves and clippings in the gutter block the storm drains, which is why raking them into the street is prohibited. Republic Services’ curbside leaf and branch pickups for Three Cart households use different lines, South Boulder Road and 287: November 7 south of South Boulder Road, November 14 north of it and east of 287, November 21 north of it and west of 287.
