Start with the museum. [WOW! Children’s Museum](/places/wow-childrens-museum/), at 110 North Harrison Avenue a block east of Public Road, is open Tuesday to Saturday 9 to 5 and, since November 8, Sundays noon to 5; children 1 to 11 are $15, adults $9, babies under 1 free, and everyone is $2 off after 3 pm. The weekly programs come with admission: Littlest Learners for the under-3s on Wednesdays at 9:30, storytimes Tuesdays at 10 and Thursdays at 11, STEAM to the MAX after school on Thursdays and Science Spot on Fridays. It is closed Mondays and on Thanksgiving Day. Across the street, [The Read Queen](/places/the-read-queen-bookstore-and-cafe/), a new-and-used bookstore with a cafe, opens at 7 on weekdays and 8 at weekends.

## The library

The [library](/places/lafayette-public-library/) on West Baseline Road runs a [storytime](/families/#program-library-storytimes) at 10:30 most mornings: babies on Tuesdays, families (toddlers to preschool) on Wednesdays and Fridays, bilingual on Thursdays, through November 20, and a Saturday storytime for all ages through December 26. A [Sensory Storytime](/events/sensory-storytime-2026-11-28/) for ages 2 to 8 is on November 28, and a holiday one with [photos with Santa](/events/sensory-storytime-and-photos-with-santa-2026-12-05/) on December 5 takes registration. The week of December 14 is the holiday week, a different storytime each morning at 10:30: Winter Wonderland on Monday, Babies and Bells on Tuesday, Storytime with Santa on Wednesday the 16th, the bilingual Tradiciones on Thursday and Latkes and Lights on Friday, all in the lower-level Meeting Room on the library’s calendar. One or two Sundays a month, kids 5 to 10 read for fifteen minutes to a therapy dog at [Paws to Read](/families/#program-paws-to-read), next on December 13 and January 10 at 1:30; on November 21 and December 19 a bilingual [Lotería bingo](/events/loteria-mexicana-family-bingo-2026-11-21/) follows the Saturday storytime at 11:30; and on the first Wednesday of the month a drop-in [wellness craft](/events/wellness-crafts-self-affirmations-2026-12-02/) runs from 3:30 to 4:30, next on December 2. Older children get the [Studio 775 Makerspace](/families/#program-studio-775-makerspace), with 3D printers and a laser cutter, in drop-in hours Tuesday to Thursday afternoons (a child in fifth grade or below needs an adult over 17 with them), and Mondays 4:30 to 7:30 are the Teen Maker Hangout for grades 6 to 12. The makerspace closes December 21 to January 1.

## The recreation center and the lake

The [Bob L. Burger Recreation Center](/places/bob-l-burger-recreation-center/) charges $4 a day for children 2 to 17 and $5.75 for adults, with a $15 family rate for two adults and two children on weekend afternoons; the pool, the Rose Lueras Pool, closes half an hour before the building. The City teaches [group swim lessons](/families/#program-group-swim-lessons) there from 6 months up, with the year’s last session running December 1 to 17, and runs [youth sports](/families/#program-rec-youth-sports) by season: winter is basketball from kindergarten and indoor soccer. On school days off it runs [School’s Out camps](/families/#program-schools-out-camps), 8 am to 5:30 pm, $60 a day for residents; the dates follow Boulder Valley’s calendar: November 11, 23, 24 and 25, December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30, January 4 and 18, and February 15 to 17, each with a sign-up deadline a week ahead.

[Waneka Lake Park](/places/waneka-lake-park/), west of Public Road at 1600 Caria Drive, is the park for a whole afternoon: a loop trail, a nine-hole disc golf course on the east side, four pickleball courts, stocked trout fishing (a license from 16), and the boathouse, which rents paddleboats, kayaks, canoes and stand-up paddleboards by the half hour each summer, $10 to $18 for a paddleboat, with a 16-year-old required on any multi-person boat; low water ended the 2026 season early. No swimming. On weekend afternoons from September to April, 1 to 3, volunteers open [The Shack](/places/the-shack-at-waneka-lake/), a small nature station by the Greenlee Wildlife Preserve.

## Outside

The [Coal Creek Trail](/places/coal-creek-trail/) is soft-surface and flat, 3.3 miles through Lafayette on a 14-mile county trail, with a restroom at the [Flagg Park](/places/flagg-park/) trailhead; a section near the water reclamation plant has been closed for construction since September 8, with a signed detour, and the City now expects to finish on December 7; check its Cone Zone map before you go. The City’s Open Space program walks it with children welcome: [wild turkeys on November 14](/events/wild-turkeys-of-coal-creek-2026-11-14/), now down to a waitlist, and [winter plants on December 13](/events/jack-frosts-winter-botanizing-and-art-2026-12-13/), 1 to 3 pm, both from Elysian Field Drive, registration required. In Old Town, [Festival Plaza](/places/festival-plaza/) runs its splash-pad fountains from Memorial Day to Labor Day, 10 to 8, and the [Great Outdoors Water Park](/places/great-outdoors-water-park/) on East South Boulder Road is shut for the winter; its 2026 season ran from May 23 to Labor Day, at $7.50 a child at the gate for residents.

## The calendar

The City’s annual children’s events are in spring: a Leprechaun Gold Hunt at the recreation center in March, the Eggstravaganza at Waneka Lake with an egg hunt and the Bunny, and a Kids Fishing Derby at the lake in May. This fall and winter, besides the library, the Chamber’s [Holiday on the Plaza](/events/holiday-on-the-plaza-2026-12-05/) is Saturday, December 5, 2 to 6 pm, on Festival Plaza, and the Friends of the Library’s [holiday book sale](/events/friends-holiday-book-sale-2026-12-04/) fills the library lobby December 4 to 6, $5 a bag on the Sunday. The school days off are on the [Families page](/families/), with the City’s camps beside them.
