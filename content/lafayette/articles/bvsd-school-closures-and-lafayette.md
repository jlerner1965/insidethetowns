No Lafayette school closes. On September 22, 2026, the Boulder Valley School District’s Board of Education voted to approve Resolution 26-27, adopting what the district calls the Resilient Schools plan, its response to declining enrollment. The neighborhood schools the plan closes for 2027–28 are Birch, Douglass, Eldorado 6-8, Flatirons, Mesa and Monarch K-5. None is in Lafayette, and none of the closures, mergers or grade changes the plan lists is at a Lafayette school.

## What the plan does elsewhere

- **Boulder.** Douglass Elementary closes and its attendance area is divided among Coal Creek, Eisenhower and Heatherwood. High Peaks Elementary moves to the Douglass campus, and Community Montessori moves into High Peaks’ space on the Aurora 7 campus. Mesa Elementary closes and merges into the Bear Creek building. Flatirons Elementary closes and its area is divided between Whittier and Foothill.
- **Broomfield.** Birch Elementary closes and merges with Kohl in the Kohl building.
- **Superior and Louisville.** Eldorado PK-8 becomes an elementary school and Monarch PK-8 a middle school; Monarch’s elementary attendance area is divided among Fireside, Superior and Eldorado.

The district says relocating High Peaks, which it calls its most in-demand focus school, to the Douglass campus "will give more students, particularly those in east county, access to the focus school."

## What reaches Lafayette

**The lottery this winter.** Choice enrollment for 2027–28, the application to any school other than the one assigned to your address, has its on-time window from November 1 to December 18, 2026. This year the district puts students displaced by the plan first: "Students impacted by school closures or relocations will receive the highest lottery priority for non-charter schools," and the other priorities, which it lists as siblings, district employees and district residency, give no preference above that one. It applies to every non-charter school in the district. Within a priority group the lottery is not first-come, first-served, so an application on December 18 counts the same as one on November 1. Families hear about seat offers on or about December 23 and have 13 days to accept. Applications after December 18 are taken until August 31, 2027, and go to wait lists.

**Your neighborhood school.** A new student is guaranteed a seat at the school for the home address, and the district’s [K-12 School Finder](https://www.bvsd.org/departments/enrollment/k-12-school-finder) now shows the attendance areas for 2027–28. A family close to a boundary can ask the enrollment office to confirm its school at enrollment.helpdesk@bvsd.org.

**The dual-language review.** The plan makes no change to the district’s dual-language programs yet. Its team "identified some opportunities for improvement with the dual language programs in both Boulder and Lafayette" and proposes to spend the next year reviewing the PK-5 and 6-8 dual-language models in both cities: an internal analysis, a review of national research, and feedback from teachers, families and students. It will return to the Board "at a yet to be determined time" with recommendations, "which could include program and/or facility changes." The district’s page does not name the schools under review, and adds that dual-language programming "remains a district priority."
