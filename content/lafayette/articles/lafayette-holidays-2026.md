From Thanksgiving week to New Year’s Day, this is what Lafayette’s own calendars carry: the dated events first, then every closure and short day at the places residents use. Each line was read from the organizer’s page on November 10, and each event has a listing on the [calendar](/events/).

## What is on

- **Friday, November 13.** [Gathered](/events/gathered-open-studios-group-exhibit-2026-11-13/), the Open Studios exhibit at The Collective, 201 North Public Road, opens with a reception from 6 to 8 pm and hangs to January 10. The artists give a talk on Saturday, December 12, from 1 to 3 pm.
- **Through Tuesday, November 24.** Odd13 Brewing’s [can drive](/events/odd13-can-food-drive-2026-11-01/): five non-perishable cans at the taproom on East Simpson Street for a free draft.
- **Friday to Sunday, December 4 to 6.** The Friends of the Library’s [holiday sale](/events/friends-holiday-book-sale-2026-12-04/) in the library lobby: Friday and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, Sunday 1 to 3:30 pm, when $5 fills a bag.
- **Saturday, December 5.** The Chamber’s [Holiday on the Plaza](/events/holiday-on-the-plaza-2026-12-05/), 2 to 6 pm on Festival Plaza, 311 South Public Road. That morning the library holds a [Sensory Storytime with photos with Santa](/events/sensory-storytime-and-photos-with-santa-2026-12-05/) at 10:30 and again at 11:30, for ages 2 to 8, 25 people a session, registration required.
- **Thursday, December 10.** The City’s [winter wreath workshop](/events/winter-wreath-workshop-collective-2026-12-10/) at The Collective, 5:30 to 7 pm. Registration is down to a waitlist.
- **Sunday, December 13.** [Jack Frost’s winter botanizing walk](/events/jack-frosts-winter-botanizing-and-art-2026-12-13/) on Coal Creek, 1 to 3 pm, with registration on the City’s Engage site, and [Paws to Read](/events/paws-to-read-2026-12-13/) at the library, 1:30 to 2:30 pm.
- **Monday to Friday, December 14 to 18.** The library’s holiday storytimes, one each morning at 10:30: Winter Wonderland on Monday, Babies and Bells on Tuesday, Storytime with Santa on Wednesday, the bilingual Tradiciones on Thursday, and Latkes and Lights, for Hanukkah, on Friday.
- **Thursday, December 17.** The [Older Adult Learning Club](/events/older-adult-learning-club-glowforge-ornaments-2026-12-17/) cuts winter ornaments on the library makerspace’s laser cutter, 1 to 3 pm; sign-up is through the Senior Center.
- **Saturday, December 19.** [Lotería bingo](/events/loteria-mexicana-family-bingo-2026-12-19/), bilingual and for all ages, in the library’s Kid Zone, 11:30 to noon.

## What is closed

- **Thanksgiving, Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27.** The library closes at 5 pm on Wednesday the 25th and stays closed both days, with its book drops locked until 9 am on Saturday. Senior Services, The Collective and the Municipal Court are closed both days. The recreation center, WOW! Children’s Museum and Ride Free Lafayette are off on Thursday. The Lafayette History Museum reopens on Saturday the 28th at 10 am. The [Thanksgiving notice](/updates/#thanksgiving-closures-2026) has the rest.
- **Friday, December 4.** The Municipal Court is closed for staff training.
- **Wednesday, December 9.** The library closes at 1 pm for a City staff awards event, with its book drops open, and the recreation center closes at 2 pm that day.
- **Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24.** The library and The Collective are closed, and the library’s book drops are locked from 5 pm on Wednesday the 23rd until 9 am on Saturday the 26th. Senior Services and the Municipal Court close at noon, and the recreation center at 2 pm.
- **Christmas Day, Friday, December 25.** The library, the recreation center, Senior Services, The Collective and the court are closed, and Ride Free Lafayette does not run.
- **New Year’s Eve, Thursday, December 31.** Senior Services closes at noon, the recreation center at 2 pm and the library at 5 pm.
- **New Year’s Day, Friday, January 1.** The library, the recreation center, Senior Services and The Collective are closed, and Ride Free Lafayette does not run. The library’s book drops reopen at 9 am on Saturday, January 2.

Two longer closures sit inside those weeks. The library’s Studio 775 Makerspace is shut for maintenance from December 21 to January 1, and the Lafayette History Museum is closed from December 31 to January 2. The library’s closure list marks Thanksgiving, the day after, Christmas Day and New Year’s Day as City holidays as well.

On every other day of the season the recreation center keeps its regular hours, 5 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 8 am to 5 pm at weekends, and Ride Free Lafayette runs from 7 am to 8 pm.

## School and camps

Boulder Valley has no school on Monday and Tuesday, November 23 and 24, which are fall conference exchange days, and Thanksgiving break runs from Wednesday the 25th to Friday the 27th. Winter break runs from Monday, December 21, through Friday, January 1.

The recreation center’s School’s Out day camps cover most of those weekdays: November 23, 24 and 25, and December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30, 8 am to 5:30 pm, $60 a day for residents and $72 for everyone else. Each day is sold on its own, and registration for a camp closes a week before it. The next one after the break is Monday, January 4; sign-up for the 2027 dates opens on December 8 for residents and December 19 for non-residents.

## After Christmas: the tree

Households on the City’s Three Cart Program can put a Christmas tree out for removal in January, February and March. Republic Services, the City’s hauler, is at 303-286-2400.
