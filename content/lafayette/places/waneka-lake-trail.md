The trail loops 1.2 miles around the lake in [Waneka Lake Park](/places/waneka-lake-park/), on a mostly soft-surface path 8 to 10 feet wide, with ten fitness stations along the way, and enters from several points in the park and the streets around it. Dogs are welcome on leash. It connects with the Powerline Trail, Thomas Open Space, the Greenlee Wildlife Preserve and Waneka Landing. The park is open 6 am to 10 pm, with restrooms, picnic areas, a playground and parking.
