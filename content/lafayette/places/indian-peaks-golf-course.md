Indian Peaks is at 2300 Indian Peaks Trail, a Hale Irwin design that the City of Lafayette’s parks page lists among its facilities. The course’s site links tee times and a green-fees page but prints no hours, season or prices on its front page, so none are given here. Golf shop and tee times: 303-666-4706.
