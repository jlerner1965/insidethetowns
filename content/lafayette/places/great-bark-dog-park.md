The Dog Park Trail, the City’s facility page says, runs "from Rothman Open Space or Finch Street to the Great Bark Dog Park and back," with a loop trail inside the fenced park itself; the listing gives 119th Street as the address, hours of 4 am to 10 pm, parking, restrooms and a pet pick-up station, and 303-661-1306 as the contact. Since April 2019 the park has composted dog waste: use the compostable bags provided at the park, not plastic bags from home, and the separate dog-waste bins, which PetScoop empties; the City warns that contaminated recycling bins may lose their service.
