Lafayette has its own police department at 451 North 111th Street, open Monday to Friday 8 am to 5 pm at 303-665-5571. For an emergency call 911. To file a report by phone or have an officer come to you, the department’s page says to "call our Dispatch Center at 303-441-4444," the Boulder County dispatch line; lost property, theft, harassment and vandalism with no known suspect can be reported online through the City’s form, and a counter report can be filed at the department, where an officer is called in to meet you. Records requests, fingerprinting, VIN inspections, dog licenses and a prescription drug take-back are among the services on its page.
