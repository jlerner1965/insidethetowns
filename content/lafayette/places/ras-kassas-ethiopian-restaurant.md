Ras Kassa’s is at 802 South Public Road, with pickup on the east side of the building, and takes dine-in and take-out orders at 303-447-2919 or 303-604-6885. Its site shows two schedules at once, "3:00 to 9:00 pm every day" in one place and 11 am to 9 pm daily in another, so no hours line is given here; call first. The menu is on the site.
