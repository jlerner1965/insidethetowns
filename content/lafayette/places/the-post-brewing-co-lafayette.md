The Post’s Lafayette restaurant at 105 West Emma Street is also the home of The Post Brewing Company. It is open for dine-in, takeout and take-home Monday to Friday 11:30 am to 9 pm and Saturday and Sunday 10 am to 9 pm, with brunch on weekends from 10 to 2 and happy hour Monday to Friday from 2 to 6, dine-in only.
