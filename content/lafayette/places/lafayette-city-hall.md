City Hall, at 1290 South Public Road, is open to the public Monday to Friday from 8 am to 5 pm; the main line is 303-665-5588. It closes on Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, at noon on Christmas Eve, on Christmas Day and on New Year’s Day. Utility Billing (water, wastewater, stormwater and, for most homes, Republic Services trash) is here at 303-604-3555, with a payment drop box outside; the City Clerk’s office runs elections and the cemetery (303-604-4386). [City Council](/programs/city-council/) meets in the Council Chambers on the first and third Tuesdays at 5:30 pm, and most boards and commissions meet here too.
