Jeannot’s is at 2770 Arapahoe Road, Suite 124, in Atlas Valley at the west edge of town, open every day from 7 am to 3 pm. Everything is made in house, the site says, down to the English muffins, the croissant dough and the chocolate mousse; orange juice is pressed each morning, the menu changes with the seasons and uses Front Range ingredients when it can, and the signature drink is the Decadent Hot Chocolate.
