The Great Outdoors Water Park, at 500 East South Boulder Road, ran its 2026 season from May 23 to August 9 daily, 11 am to 6 pm, then weekends and Labor Day through September 7, with Sunset Swims from 6 to 8 pm on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings in June and July. Day passes are sold only at the gate: youth 2 to 17 and seniors $7.50 for residents and $9 for non-residents, adults $10 and $12, Sunset Swim $5 and $6. The park may close at short notice for weather; the weather line is 303-673-9207. The City had not posted 2027 dates when this was read.
