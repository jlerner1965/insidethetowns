Acreage sits at 1380 Horizon Avenue, Unit A, on the rise east of town. Its site describes the Acreage Food Truck menu, "crispy snacks, fresh salads, wraps, loaded nachos, burgers, sandwiches, and sweet treats," alongside Stem Ciders flights and seasonal ciders, and its posts mention street corn, pork wings and margaritas. The site gives a reservations line, 303-ACREAGE (227-3243), and a $25 bonus card with every $100 gift card bought from November 1 to December 31, but no opening hours, so none are listed here.
