William Oliver’s calls itself a Colorado pub and eatery: hand-cut applewood-smoked bacon that can be added to nearly anything (a "Pint of Bacon" comes with maple syrup), several vegetarian and vegan dishes, a bar of more than 400 whiskies and beers brewed in Colorado. Its site states it takes no phone calls at any location and hosts no live music; whisky tastings with brand ambassadors run regularly. The site did not list hours when read, so none are given here.
