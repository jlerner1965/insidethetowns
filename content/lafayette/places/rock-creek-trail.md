The Rock Creek Trail is a soft-surface trail that runs about 12 miles beside Rock Creek, from the Coalton Trailhead in Superior to the [Rock Creek East Trailhead](/places/rock-creek-east-open-space/) in Lafayette; 2.4 miles of it are in the city. There is limited parking at the Rock Creek West Trailhead on the east side of Exempla Circle, and a permanent restroom at the [Flagg Park Trailhead](/places/flagg-park/) on the regional system. The City lists biking, hiking, walking, running and cross-country skiing on its regional trails, and horseback riding on the Rock Creek Trail alone. With the [Coal Creek Trail](/places/coal-creek-trail/) it makes up the Coal Creek and Rock Creek regional trail system.
