The YMCA of Northern Colorado’s Lafayette branch, at 2800 Dagny Way, has an indoor ice rink, used for public skating, skating classes and hockey, and a pool with high and low dives, three slides and lap lanes, plus a seasonal splash park and baby pool. The building also has a fitness center, a basketball gym, an indoor running track, a synthetic turf field, a Pilates reformer studio, a group exercise studio, a sand volleyball court and pickleball. Rink and pool schedules are on the Y’s facility-hours page; the branch is 303-664-5455.
