WOW! is at 110 North Harrison Avenue, one block east of North Public Road and one block south of East Baseline Road. It is open Tuesday to Saturday 9 am to 5 pm and, from November 8, Sundays noon to 5. Admission from its visit page: children ages 1 to 11 $15, adults $9, infants under 1 free, and $2 off each admission after 3 pm ("End of Day Play"). No child visits without an adult and no adult without a child. Membership is optional.

Its calendar carries [weekly programs](/programs/wow-weekly-programs/) included with admission: Littlest Learners for ages 0 to 3 on Wednesday mornings, storytimes on Tuesdays and Thursdays, STEAM to the MAX after school on Thursdays and Science Spot on Fridays. The museum closes at 3 pm on November 7 for its [30th Birthday Bash](/events/wow-30th-birthday-bash-2026-11-07/), an adults-only fundraiser, and on Thanksgiving.
