Romero’s is a fenced, private off-leash dog park with a taproom at 985 South Public Road, "an ages 16 and up establishment" with beer, cider, mead, wine, kombucha and non-alcoholic drinks and rotating food trucks. Its site gives the hours loosely: Monday and Wednesday to Thursday from 4 pm to "9ish," Friday 2 to about 10, Saturday 12:30 to about 10 and Sunday 12:30 to about 7, with no Tuesday hours listed, so they are not set out as a line here. Dogs need a membership or day pass; the details are on the site.
