Festival Plaza, at 311 South Public Road, is open 6 am to 10 pm. Its splash-pad fountains run 10 am to 8 pm daily from Memorial Day to Labor Day, turned off during some events. The City holds Picnic on the Plaza here, free live music on Thursdays from noon to 1 in June, July and August, and [Art Night Out](/programs/art-night-out/), the free second-Friday street festival from May to September on Public Road between Emma and Cannon streets, 5 to 9 pm; the Chamber’s [Holiday on the Plaza](/events/holiday-on-the-plaza-2026-12-05/) is here on December 5. The plaza may not be used for private events; a public event needs a Community Event Permit filed 60 days ahead. The Starkey Building beside it, renovated in 2025, rents for meetings and small workshops through the City’s portal.
