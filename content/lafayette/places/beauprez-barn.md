Arthur Beauprez came from Belgium in 1907, worked at the Waneka Lake power plant and bought the family’s first 80 acres in 1917; the barn went up in 1926 and the house two years later. Three generations ran the farm for more than 70 years, known for Belgian draft horses, Hereford beef cattle and Holstein dairy cows, until the herd was dispersed in 1990 and the land was annexed into the city for Indian Peaks Golf Course and the neighborhoods around it. The City opens the barn for scheduled programs only, with guided tours through the year. Questions go to the Open Space superintendent, 303-661-1310.
