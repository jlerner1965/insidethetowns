The City’s skatepark sits at the [Bob L. Burger Recreation Center](/places/bob-l-burger-recreation-center/), 111 West Baseline Road. Its 19,000 square feet are 12,500 of street-style obstacles and 6,500 of bowls, built for bowl, plaza and obstacle riding. It is open daily from dawn to 9 pm; questions go to the recreation center, 303-665-0469.
