The Collective is the City of Lafayette’s community arts center, in a former church at 201 North Public Road, open Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, Friday 10 to 7 and Sunday noon to 5, closed Mondays. The Arts & Cultural Resources department programs it: the Día de los Muertos exhibit "Arcos y Portales" runs to November 8, and [Gathered, the Open Studios group exhibit](/events/gathered-open-studios-group-exhibit-2026-11-13/) follows from November 13 to January 10, 2027, after a two-day closure to install it. Single workshops are listed on its page (a winter wreath workshop on December 10 was full with a waitlist when read). Bilingual staff; arts@lafayetteco.gov.
