Lafayette Senior Services runs from the Senior Center at 103 South Iowa Avenue, open Monday to Thursday 8:30 am to 5 pm and Friday 8:30 to 4. Its page lists activities, trips and tours, enrichment, fitness and wellness programs, an Older Adult Artist Showcase and an Older Adults Resource Fair, and the volunteer-run [Snow Angels](/programs/snow-angels/) program, which clears snow for residents 55 and over who cannot. The page does not state an age of eligibility or a membership fee; call. Registration for its programs is on the City’s WebTrac site, and the monthly calendar is posted as a PDF on the Recreation site.
