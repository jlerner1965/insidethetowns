The Arts Hub is a 501(c)(3) at 420 Courtney Way, open by appointment, with "dynamic and diverse arts programming for all ages," in its own words. It runs performing and visual arts classes by semester, the Arts Hub Thespians theatre program for ages 11 to 16, the Treble Singers vocal ensemble, private lessons, camps, and an Adult Mainstage whose auditions are open to anyone regardless of experience; a part-time coworking-and-childcare option for parents of infants and toddlers is on the site too. Its fall production, *Little Shop of Horrors*, closed October 25; a youth cast of *Little Women* rehearses from January 5 for Saturday matinees on March 6, 13 and 20, 2027.
