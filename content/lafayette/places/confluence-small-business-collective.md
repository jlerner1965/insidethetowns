Confluence Small Business Collective, at 75 Waneka Parkway, is open to day users Monday to Friday from 8 to 5 through the first-floor lobby, staffed from 8:30 to 1, with round-the-clock access on its membership plans. It offers flex plans with desk reservations, meeting-room credits, the kitchen and phone booths; dedicated desks and keypad-locked private offices with a business mailing address; the Bison Conference Room for up to twelve around the table (sixteen on request), with a 96-inch screen; and an event space with a glass garage door for up to 50. Call (720) 484-6095 or email info@confluencesbc.com.
