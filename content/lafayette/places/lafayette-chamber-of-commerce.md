The Lafayette Chamber of Commerce is at 304 West Baseline Road, open Monday to Friday 9 am to 4 pm. It puts on the town’s big annual events, the Peach Festival on Festival Plaza, the Brew Festival, Independence Day fireworks and the December [Holiday on the Plaza](/events/holiday-on-the-plaza-2026-12-05/), and runs after-hours mixers, a Women’s Business Network luncheon, Coffee Talks and a leads group for members. [Membership](/programs/lafayette-chamber-of-commerce/) in 2026 starts at $290 a year for a business of up to two employees and $150 for a retired or non-business member. The Oatmeal Festival the Chamber ran each January for 27 years is not on its annual-events page.
