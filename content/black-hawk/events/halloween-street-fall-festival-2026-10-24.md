The last of the City's three free Fall Festival Saturdays (October 10, 17 and 24): the Tommyknocker Mine Maze, a pumpkin market, pumpkin decorating and fall games in the Livery Lot on Gregory Street, 11 am to 3 pm. Halloween Street's sculptures and photo stations line historic Gregory Street all month, and Halloween Night on October 31 runs to its own hours.
