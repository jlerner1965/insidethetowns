Black Hawk began with ore, water and the work of processing gold. The City's history dates the mining camp to May 1859, in the Gregory Diggings, when John H. Gregory's discovery drew prospectors to the narrow gulch between present-day Black Hawk and Central City. The two neighboring cities share a mining story but remain separate places.

## Start with the gulch

[Gregory Street Plaza](/places/gregory-street-plaza/) is the starting point for a walk through the historic part of town. The narrow streets and older buildings, set against the casino towers, show how a settlement fitted working mills, homes and businesses into a canyon.

Black Hawk Point was founded in 1860 and incorporated as the City of Black Hawk on March 11, 1864. It grew into a milling center for ore from the surrounding district, and a railroad connection to Denver followed in 1872. That industrial role sets Black Hawk apart from towns built chiefly around a mine or a railroad depot.

## Find the old church with a new job

The Presbyterian church built in 1863 survives as the City's Annex Building at 211 Church Street. Restored by the City, it now houses planning, finance and the Council Chambers: a working civic building, not a museum.

## Read the modern skyline

Colorado voters approved limited-stakes gaming in 1990, and casinos opened in Black Hawk in 1991. The City's history ties that change to renewed investment and restoration: the towers and the small historic buildings are two chapters of the same city's economy.
