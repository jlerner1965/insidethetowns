The Gilpin Historical Society's 90-minute walking tour of five historic Central City buildings, with guides telling the county's stories of murders, ghosts and other grim tales. Tours leave from the lower level of the Century Casino at 102 Main Street in Central City from 6:30 pm, a new one every 30 minutes until 8:30 pm, on October 16, 17, 23 and 24, 2026; check in at least 10 minutes early. Tickets are sold through Eventbrite.
