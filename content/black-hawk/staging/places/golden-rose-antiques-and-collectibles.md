Golden Rose Antiques & Collectibles is in the City of Black Hawk's building at 357 Gregory Street. On August 27, 2025 the City Council approved a commercial lease with Jeffrey Warner, doing business as Golden Rose Antiques & Collectibles, for that address, on the same terms as the plaza's other tenants; the minutes record that he and his family had run the store in Central City for the previous 17 years.

The HARD District's page lists the shop at 357 Gregory Street as pet- and family-friendly, with no description, phone or website. The packet that suggested this listing gave no hours or phone either.

Read in the City Council's minutes of August 27, 2025 and on the district's page (https://blackhawkharddistrict.com/what-to-do/golden-rose/) on 10 October 2026.
