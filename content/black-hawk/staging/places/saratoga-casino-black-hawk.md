Saratoga Casino Black Hawk is at 101 Main Street, at the bottom of the hill. Its dining page lists the Main Street Cafe (all-day breakfast, lunch and dinner, 10 am to 8 pm daily, with its own listing on this guide) and the Main Floor Bar, open 9 am to 2 am with craft beer, drafts, wine and cocktails.

The casino's visitor page says the Ramblin Express bus runs seven days a week from Lakewood, Arvada and Aurora with hourly or bi-hourly departures, that bus fares are reimbursed all day Monday to Thursday and 9 am to 5 pm on Fridays, and that the free Black Hawk and Central City tramway runs continuously seven days a week from mid-morning until early the following morning. The site gives no casino hours.

Address and phone are from the visitor page, read 10 October 2026. The site's newest dated post is from October 2023; the dining page carried an "October Special" without a year and its promotions page lists weekly specials by day of the week. Treat the listing as a starting point and call.
