The Gilpin Historical Society's museum at 228 E. First High Street in Central City is open daily 10 am to 4 pm from Memorial Day weekend through the end of September, and from October to Memorial Day weekend by appointment or through the society's tour office for groups of two or more. Admission is $10 for adults and free for children 12 and under. The society's Washington Hall tour office at 117 Eureka Street, open Tuesday to Sunday 10 am to 4 pm in the off-season, also runs tours of the Thomas House, the Teller House, the Opera House, St. James Church and the Weekly Register-Call print shop, $10 a person per building.

Facts are from the society's hours and locations page, read 10 October 2026; its events page the same day listed the 2026 season as May 23 to September 27.
