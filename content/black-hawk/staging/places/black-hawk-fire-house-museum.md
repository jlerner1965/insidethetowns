The Fire House Museum is a new brick building the City put up on Gregory Street Plaza when it built the plaza between 2019 and 2021, to house the City's 1935 Chevrolet fire truck and other historic Black Hawk Fire Department equipment and memorabilia.

The packet that suggested this listing also said to confirm interior access before a special trip; no page read says when, or whether, the inside is open.

Read on the City's Gregory Street Plaza page on 10 October 2026; the phone is City Hall's, from the same page.
