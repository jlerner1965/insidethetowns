Ted Garcia's gallery at 327 Gregory Street shows only his own work. His site describes it as a one-man gallery and invites visitors to First Fridays from 4 to 8 pm, with food and drinks; it lists commissioned work (people, motorcycles, animals) and other work (landscapes, waterscapes, sunrises, still lifes, flowers and minis), sells prints through a separate print site, and presents Plein Air 365x5, the 2,014 paintings he made outdoors every day from January 1, 2009 to July 11, 2014. Art classes are "coming soon".

The district page gives the gallery's number as 303-885-5112; Garcia's own contact page gives 303-670-2981 and a mailing address in Evergreen. The packet that suggested this listing gave 303-885-5112.

Read on the gallery's home and contact pages on 10 October 2026. The home page said "Next First Friday is September 4!", which fits 2026 but carries no year, so it is not counted as a date.
