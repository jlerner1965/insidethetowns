Copper Kitchen Pizzeria is in the City of Black Hawk's building at 307 Gregory Street, on Gregory Street Plaza. The City announced on February 26, 2025 that it had signed a lease with Bruce Boynton, owner of Pick Axe Pizza on Miner Street in Idaho Springs, and that many Pick Axe dishes would be served at Copper Kitchen, with stone hearth-baked pizzas; it then expected the restaurant to open in August 2025. At its meeting of August 27, 2025, the City Council, as the local licensing authority, approved a hotel and restaurant liquor licence for Hard Luck Black Hawk, LLC, doing business as Copper Kitchen Pizzeria, at 307 Gregory Street; the minutes record that the owners hoped to open by September 13. The Council's agenda of June 24, 2026 carried a contract for storage sheds for Black Hawk Commons and Copper Kitchen Pizzeria.

The HARD District's page, undated, describes made-to-order stone-hearth pizza, pasta, wings and desserts, and a full bar. The packet that suggested this listing said the same; neither gives hours.

The City's press release, the August 27, 2025 minutes (https://www.cityofblackhawk.org/media/9436) and the June 24, 2026 agenda (https://www.cityofblackhawk.org/media/10731) were read on 10 October 2026, with the district page (https://blackhawkharddistrict.com/what-to-do/copper-kitchen-pizzeria/).
