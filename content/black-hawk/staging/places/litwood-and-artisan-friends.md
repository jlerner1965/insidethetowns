LitWood & Artisan Friends is in Unit D of 221 Gregory Street. LitWood Creations, which runs it, describes itself as a veteran-owned local shop specializing in fractal-burned wooden art, and says the shop shows the work of more than 30 local artisans: cutting boards, home and garden pieces, candles and scents, bath products, knitwear and jewelry. It also sells online.

Its contact page gives store hours of Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 4 pm. The packet that suggested this listing gave the address as 221 Gregory Street without the unit.
