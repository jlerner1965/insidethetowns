The Monarch Chophouse is the casino's steakhouse, on the third floor near the guest elevators. Its page describes 28-day aged USDA prime beef, fresh seafood, Colorado lamb, table-side preparations and a wine list of more than 330 labels, and a three-course Seasonal Chef's Menu at $65 a person on Sunday to Thursday nights. Tables can be booked through OpenTable or by phone.
