The Buffet at the Monarch runs lunch on weekdays, dinner every night and brunch at weekends on the casino's third floor. Its page lists rotating live stations, a salad bar with homemade soups, a carving station, and lobster tail, shrimp, crab legs and made-to-order pho at dinner.

Prices from the same page: weekday lunch $28.99 for adults and $19.99 for children 4 to 10; dinner $53.99 Monday to Thursday and $58.99 Friday to Sunday ($27.99 and $42.99 for children); weekend brunch $58.99 and $42.99. An extra lobster tail or a pound of crab clusters is $9.99.

Hours, phone and prices are from The Buffet's page on Monarch's site, read 10 October 2026, which carried a "September Special" and a 2026 copyright line but no full date.
