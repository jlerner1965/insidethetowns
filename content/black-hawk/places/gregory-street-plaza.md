Gregory Street Plaza is the City of Black Hawk's pedestrian shopping and events street in what it calls the HARD District (History Appreciation and Recreation Destination), at an elevation of 8,535 feet. The City began building it in September 2019 and had the retail spaces ready by the end of 2021, moving and rebuilding several historic buildings to do it: the McAfee, Woodbury and Norton houses, the Bobtail Mine building and the Bunkhouse were restored, and the Rocky Mountain Evangelical Free Church, from about 1869, had its exterior refreshed. The plaza has brick paving, benches, signs on the town's history and a Howard four-faced reproduction post clock inscribed with the founders' names. A new brick building holds the Fire House Museum, which the City says displays historic firefighting equipment and memorabilia and its 1935 Chevrolet fire truck.

The City says the Bobtail Mine building will sell tickets and hold a small museum when it reopens the historic Bobtail Mine for tours; it gives no date, and no tours are listed. The Bunkhouse holds restrooms.

In October 2026 the City's calendar runs Halloween Street on Gregory Street: 69 Halloween sculptures, eight selfie stations, a Black Hawk Pumpkin Passport and the Tommyknocker Mine Maze, with a free fall festival on Saturdays October 10, 17 and 24 from 11 am to 3 pm and trick-or-treating on October 31. The plaza description was read on the City's Gregory Street Plaza page and the Halloween Street dates on the City's event page (https://www.cityofblackhawk.org/city-clerk/page/halloween-street-fall-festival), both on 10 October 2026.
