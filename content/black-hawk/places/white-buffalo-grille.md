The White Buffalo Grille is the Lodge Casino's sit-down restaurant, with USDA Prime steaks, seafood, desserts and what its page calls an extensive wine selection. It is open Thursday, Sunday and Monday from 3 to 9 pm and Friday and Saturday from 3 to 10 pm; the page lists no Tuesday or Wednesday hours. Happy hour runs 3 to 5 pm Thursday through Monday, with sliders at $11, a crab cake at $10, wings and shrimp cocktail at $9 and $2 off cocktails, beer and wine. Reservations are recommended, at 303-582-6375.

The packet that suggested this listing placed the dining room above the casino's bridge; the Lodge's page does not say so, and it is not confirmed.
