Fireside Kitchen is the Ameristar's American restaurant on the second floor, with fireplaces and wood in a lodge-style room. It serves three menus every day: breakfast from 7 to 11 am, an all-day menu from 11 am to 10 pm and a late-night menu from 10 pm to 5 am, so it closes only between 5 and 7 am. Breakfast includes house granola with Colorado honeycomb, steel-cut oats, buttermilk pancakes and a James Peak forest berry waffle at $12, and vanilla-bean French toast at $14. Carryout can be ordered from the page.
