Ameristar Casino Resort Spa Black Hawk is at 111 Richman Street. Its site lists a hotel, a casino with a baccarat parlor, a poker room and the Sportsbook, and eight places to eat and drink: the Richman Beer Garden, the Sportsbook, Club 38, JINYA Ramen Bar, Fireside Kitchen, the Timberline Grill, the Centennial Market Buffet and Bar 8042. Fireside Kitchen and the Timberline Grill are listed separately.

The amenities page describes a rooftop pool with indoor and outdoor hot tubs, open daily 7 am to 11 pm, with a movable glass wall that closes it in for the winter, and a 24-hour fitness room for hotel guests. The ara Spa is on the hotel's ninth floor and books by phone at 720-946-4300. The site's embedded data calls the casino open all day every day, and describes a 1,500-space parking garage; no page states casino hours.

Address and phone were read on the resort's Visit page on 10 October 2026, and the amenities and spa pages the same day. The promotions page listed events on "Tuesday, September 29", "Friday, October 30" and October 17, 24 and 31, which carry no year but fall on those weekdays in 2026. Under the owner's rule of 10 October 2026, a weekday and date that fall on that weekday in the current year count as a dated notice, so these are the evidence that the resort is operating; the hotel deals page also offered fall rates.
