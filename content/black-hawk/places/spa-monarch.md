Spa Monarch is inside the Monarch Casino Resort Spa on Main Street. Monarch's page describes more than 30,000 square feet: a Laconium lounge with heated, contoured seating, an Aqua lounge with an experiential shower, a brine inhalation and light therapy lounge, herbal steam rooms and a Mountain Stone Bath. The indoor infinity pool is on the 23rd floor, with an outdoor jetted spa and a seasonal sun deck with fire pits; the pool is open every day 6 am to 10 pm. Massages, facials, nails and body wraps are listed on the spa's booking page, and a stay-and-spa package adds a spa credit to a two-night stay in a Monarch Grand Room.

Day passes are sold by phone, online or at the spa's front desk. The spa amenities page says a pass gives four hours of facility use and access to the amenities, that passes are limited and subject to availability, that reservations are required and that blackout dates apply.

Hours and phone were read on the spa's page and its booking page on Monarch's site on 10 October 2026; both print "Open Daily 8am–9pm", though the spa page's embedded data gives shorter hours (9 am–7 pm Monday to Thursday, 9 am–9 pm Friday to Sunday), so call before an early or weekday-evening visit. The spa page cites its place as the top hotel spa in USA Today's 2026 10Best Readers' Choice Awards, and links a spa menu file named for September 2026.
