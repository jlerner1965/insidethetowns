Free drop-off of pumpkins, leaves, grass clippings, branches and other organic yard waste, run by the Town with its Sustainable Futures Commission and Boulder County Zero Waste, for Lyons and unincorporated Boulder County residents. Bring it loose, bagged or in a bucket and take the bags and buckets home; no plastic bags, rocks, dirt, lumber or fencing. Arrive any time from 9 am to noon, not before.
