Regular meeting of the Lyons Board of Trustees at Town Hall, which the Town says meets the first and third Monday of the month unless it is a holiday. The agenda posts to the Town's Agenda Center the week before, which is when the date is certain. Watch the livestream at townoflyons.com/livestream; audience comment is taken in person only.
