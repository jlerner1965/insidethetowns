The library's Community Room is a Boulder County vote center on Monday, November 2 (8 am to 6 pm) and Election Day, Tuesday, November 3 (7 am to 7 pm). Vote in person, drop off a mail ballot, register, or get a replacement ballot there; mail ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm on the 3rd, and anyone in line by 7 pm can vote. The library's calendar lists the room as a polling place on those two days only; the county's page gives the hours and the other vote centers, the nearest in Longmont.
