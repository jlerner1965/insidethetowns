The Town of Lyons holds its holiday artisan market at a new spot this year: Lyons Middle/Senior High School, 100 McConnell Drive, on Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 10 am to 4 pm each day, with Santa from 2 to 4 pm both days. Enter by the south entrance. Questions go to the Town's recreation office, 303-823-8250 or recreation@townoflyons.com. The Parade of Lights follows on Saturday evening.
