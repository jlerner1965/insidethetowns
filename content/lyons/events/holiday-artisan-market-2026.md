The Town of Lyons holds its holiday artisan market in the Lyons Elementary School gym on Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 10 am to 4 pm each day, with Santa from 2 to 4 pm both days. Enter by the back door on Stickney Street, past the playground. The Parade of Lights follows on Saturday evening.
