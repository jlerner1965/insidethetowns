The Town of Lyons’ Parade of Lights comes down Main Street on Saturday, December 5, at 6:30 pm, with Santa in the parade, followed by the second of the weekend's two drone shows, launched near Bohn Park and visible from Main Street. Students from the St. Vrain Valley School District's Innovation Center present both shows. Route and weather updates are on the Town's parade page.

A float or a spot in the parade is free, with prizes for the best entries. The Town takes sign-ups through the form on its parade page until November 30; its printable entry form asks for entries before November 25. Questions to 303-823-6622 ext. 35 or kmitchell@townoflyons.com.
