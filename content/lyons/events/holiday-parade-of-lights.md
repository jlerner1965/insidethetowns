The Lyons Holiday Parade of Lights is on Saturday, December 5, 2026, at 6:30 pm in downtown Lyons, followed by a drone show. The Town of Lyons runs it as the centerpiece of a holiday weekend that starts with the tree lighting on Friday, December 4.

## Schedule

- 10 am to 4 pm: the [Holiday Artisan Market](/events/holiday-artisan-market-2026/) at Lyons Middle/Senior High School, 100 McConnell Drive, with Santa from 2 to 4 pm.
- 6:30 pm: the Parade of Lights.
- After the parade: the second of the weekend's two drone shows.

The night before, Friday, December 4, at 5 pm, is the [tree lighting and caroling](/events/tree-lighting-and-caroling/) in Sandstone Park, with the first drone show.

## Where to watch

The Town's 2026 page calls the parade a tour through Lyons and has not posted this year's route, road closures or parking. It does say where to see the drone show: it launches near Bohn Park and will be visible from Main Street. Students from the St. Vrain Valley School District's Innovation Center present it.

## This year

There are two drone shows, one each night. The artisan market has moved to the high school for this year. The Town asks parade entrants to bring their "best snowy luau energy."

## Entering the parade

A float or a spot in the parade is free. Sign-up is open until November 30 through the form on the Town's parade page, and there are prizes for the best floats and entries.

## Before you go

The Town's page names no charge to watch and gives no weather or cancellation plan. Town Hall is at 303-823-6622.
