Regular meeting of the Lyons Board of Trustees at Town Hall, with public hearings on the draft 2027 budget and on Ordinances 1228 and 1229, the LaVern M. Johnson Park leases to Planet Bluegrass for the 2027 and 2028 RockyGrass and Folks Festivals, and Ordinance 1230, amending the flood damage prevention code. Watch the livestream at townoflyons.com/livestream; audience comment is taken in person only. Agendas post to the Town's Agenda Center the week before.
