The Town's Halloween afternoon on the Saturday before the day itself: kids' activities at Lyons Elementary from 4 to 5:30, the chili cook-off at MainStage from 4:30, the costume parade down Main Street at 6, and trick-or-treating along Main from 6:30 to 7.
