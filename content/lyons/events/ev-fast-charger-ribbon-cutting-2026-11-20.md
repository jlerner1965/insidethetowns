The Town of Lyons marks Jule's new DC fast charging station and battery energy storage system at Sandstone Park with a ribbon cutting. The Colorado Energy Office and members of the Jule team, an engineer and a technician among them, will be there to talk about the technology and the grant-funded project and to answer questions.
