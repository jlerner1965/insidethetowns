Denver's Museo de las Americas brings a hands-on workshop to the library: children learn about the art and storytelling of Carmen Lomas Garza, then paint a watercolor in her style around a favorite day of the year and the people they spend it with. Two sessions on Tuesday, November 10: the homeschool community meetup from 2 to 3 pm (registration appreciated, not required), and an after-school session from 3 to 4 pm for kindergarten through sixth grade, where seats are limited and registration is through the [library's calendar](https://lyons.librarycalendar.com/event/homeschool-community-meetup-family-storytelling).
