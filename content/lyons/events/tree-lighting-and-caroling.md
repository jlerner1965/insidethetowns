The Town of Lyons lights its tree in Sandstone Park on Friday, December 4, 2026, from 5 to 7 pm, with caroling by the Lyons School Choirs and a drone show afterward. The Town's calendar calls it the 4th Annual Tree Lighting Celebration. It opens a holiday weekend that runs through Sunday, December 6.

## Schedule

- 5 pm: the tree lighting celebration and caroling with the Lyons School Choirs, in Sandstone Park.
- Afterward: a drone show, the first of two this weekend.

The Town's calendar lists the evening from 5 to 7 pm. It does not give a time for the moment the lights go on or for the drone show.

## The drone show

This year there are two drone shows: one after the tree lighting on Friday and one after the Parade of Lights on Saturday. Both launch near Bohn Park and, the Town says, will be visible from Main Street. Students from the St. Vrain Valley School District's Innovation Center present them.

## The rest of the weekend

- Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 10 am to 4 pm: the [Holiday Artisan Market](/events/holiday-artisan-market-2026/) at Lyons Middle/Senior High School, 100 McConnell Drive, with Santa from 2 to 4 pm both days.
- Saturday, December 5, 6:30 pm: the [Holiday Parade of Lights](/events/holiday-parade-of-lights/), then the second drone show.

## Before you go

The Town's pages name no charge. They do not cover parking, road closures or what happens in bad weather. The Town of Lyons runs the event; Town Hall is at 303-823-6622.
