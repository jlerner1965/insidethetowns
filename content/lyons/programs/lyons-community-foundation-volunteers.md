The Lyons Community Foundation channels local giving into scholarships for Lyons students and grants for the nonprofits that serve the town. Its **Events Committee** puts the foundation at the Winter Wonderland Concerts (January and February), the Sandstone Concerts (July and August) and the Artisan Market, and always needs helpers. The **Scholarship Review Committee** meets at the end of the application season, in March and April, and its members are known only to the chair. The **Grants Review Committee**, made up of community peers, reads the Community Support Grant applications, typically in September, and recommends awards to the advisory board. To join the **advisory board**, email info@lyonscf.org.
