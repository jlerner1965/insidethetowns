Three standing games at Bohn Park, as the Town lists them: **pickleball** on the multisport court, free and open to all ages, on weekday times the Town posts (they changed on October 1); **soccer** on the multi-use field, free for teens and adults, Sundays at 9, weather permitting; and **basketball** for ages 16 and up on Thursdays from 7 to 8:30, September through May when school is in session, $5 a night paid to the instructor, with a waiver signed first.
