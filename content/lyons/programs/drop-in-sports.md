Three standing games, as the Town lists them: **pickleball** on the Bohn Park multisport court, free and open to anyone old enough to play, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9 to 11 am and Tuesday and Thursday 10 am to noon since October 1; **soccer** on the Bohn Park multi-use field, free for teens and adults, Sundays at 9, weather permitting; and **basketball** for ages 16 and up, Thursdays from 7 to 8:30, September through May when school is in session and not during school breaks, $5 a night paid to the instructor, with a waiver signed first. The Town lists basketball in the auxiliary gym at Lyons Middle/Senior High (use the gym door at the back of the building) through November 19 and in the Lyons Elementary School gym, 338 High St, from December 3 to May 27; it says some dates may vary, so check in before a first visit.
