Three standing games, as the Town lists them: **pickleball** on the Bohn Park multisport court, free and open to anyone old enough to play, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9 to 11 am and Tuesday and Thursday 10 am to noon since October 1; **soccer** on the Bohn Park multi-use field, free for teens and adults, Sundays at 9, weather permitting; and **basketball** for ages 16 and up in the auxiliary gym at Lyons Middle/Senior High (use the gym door at the back of the building), Thursdays from 7 to 8:30, September through May when school is in session and not during school breaks, $5 a night paid to the instructor, with a waiver signed first. The Town says some basketball nights move to the Lyons Elementary gym, so check in before a first visit.
