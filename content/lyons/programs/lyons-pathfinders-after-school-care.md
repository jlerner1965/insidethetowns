St. Vrain Valley Schools' Community Schools run licensed after-school care at Lyons Elementary for children from age five through fifth grade, from 2:50 to 6 pm on school days, with indoor and outdoor play and a snack. It is closed on days without school. An afternoon costs $20, with a discount for full-time attendance, and a drop-in booked with less than 48 hours' notice costs $40; registration is $50 for one child or $75 for a family. The district's late-start days have their own programming, at $30 a child.
