Lyons is the district’s only grades 6-to-12 school, so its middle-school teams share the building and the fields with the Lions. The school lists six: **club cross country**, **volleyball**, **boys’ basketball**, **girls’ basketball**, **wrestling** and **track and field**, each with a coach to email and a schedule document on the athletics page. Registration is two steps, the online form and then the participation fee, both on the district’s RevTrak site; the school does not post the fee on the page.
