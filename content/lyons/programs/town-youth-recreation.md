The Town’s recreation office runs a short list of youth programs a season, registered on its RecDesk site. The fall 2026 list, now finished: **10U girls’ fall-ball softball**, Wednesday practices at Bohn Ballfield from mid-August to October 14 with ten Saturday games on other towns’ fields and a season-end tournament October 17–18, $190; and **kids’ mountain biking** for beginner and intermediate riders, Fridays September 11 to October 16 at the Bohn Park dirt bike park, $195 for the six weeks, which the Town’s page marks “registration full”. The next season’s programs are not on the page yet. The Town says every youth program it offers has scholarship funding for local children in need; ask at recreation@townoflyons.com. What is next is on the [Parks and Recreation calendar](https://townoflyons.recdesk.com/Community/Calendar).
