The Town’s recreation office runs a short list of youth programs a season, registered on its RecDesk site. The fall 2026 list: **10U girls’ fall-ball softball**, Wednesday practices at Bohn Ballfield from mid-August to October 14 with about ten Saturday games on other towns’ fields and a tournament October 17–18, $190; and **kids’ mountain biking** for beginner and intermediate riders aged 8 to 14, Fridays September 11 to October 16 at the Bohn Park dirt bike park, $195 for the six weeks, which filled. The Town says every youth program it offers has scholarship funding for local children in need; ask at recreation@townoflyons.com. What is next is on the [Parks and Recreation calendar](https://townoflyons.recdesk.com/Community/Calendar).
