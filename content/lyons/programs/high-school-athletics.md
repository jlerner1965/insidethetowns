The school’s athletics page lists the Lions’ teams by season: **fall** cheer, cross country, football (eight-man), softball and volleyball; **winter** boys’ and girls’ basketball, cheer and wrestling; **spring** baseball and track and field. Each sport’s schedule is a spreadsheet linked from that page, with the coach’s email beside it. To play, a student registers on the district’s RevTrak site and uploads a current CHSAA physical. The records, the scores and the next games are on [the Lions’ page here](/sports/); the Lions Booster Club is at [lyonslions.org](https://www.lyonslions.org/).
