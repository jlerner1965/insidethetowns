The library’s weekly sessions for the youngest children, no registration: **Baby Storytime & Stay-and-Play** on Tuesdays at 10:30, songs, rhymes and stories for babies up to 18 months and their grown-ups, then open play in the Community Room; and **Toddler & Preschool Storytime & Craft** on Wednesdays at 10:30, music, movement and stories for ages 0 to 5. The library says storytime is on a break from October 27 through November 11; its calendar lists baby storytime again from November 17. Each is on the calendar as its own listing with the next date: [baby](/events/baby-storytime-november/), [toddler and preschool](/events/toddler-storytime-library/).
