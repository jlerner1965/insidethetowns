Creative Grounds is a nonprofit café, art studio and third space for youth on East Main. It describes itself as a place for kids to play, adults to unwind and anyone to try their hand at something creative. There are games, books and rock painting for kids and adults, no sign-up, Wednesday to Sunday from 8 am to 4:30 pm. The pottery classes, including the Saturday wheel-throwing course for ages 14 to 21 and the one-time Fun Friday classes, need a place booked on [its class page](https://creativegroundslyons.com/classes) and are on [the events page](/events/). More about the café is on [its listing](/places/creative-grounds/).
