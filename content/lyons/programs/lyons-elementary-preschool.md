Lyons Elementary runs its preschool under the school's principal, its preschool director and the St. Vrain Valley School District. Families join in one of three ways: tuition preschool in half days, Monday to Friday, with a morning or an afternoon option, at the rates on the district's preschool site; Universal Preschool Colorado, state-funded and free for up to 15 hours a week for eligible children who are 4 on or before October 1, by a UPK application; or special education preschool for children of 3 to 5 who qualify, with the district's Child Find team. Families register online. For other licensed care, the state's [Colorado Shines search](https://cdec.colorado.gov/for-families/find-child-care) lists every program by address or ZIP code.
