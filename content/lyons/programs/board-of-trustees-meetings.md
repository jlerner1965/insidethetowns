The Board of Trustees meets in person at Town Hall on the first and third Mondays of the month at 7 pm, and meetings usually run about three hours. Public comment on anything not on the night's agenda comes at audience business, at the start of every meeting: up to four minutes each, shortened when many people want to speak. You do not have to live in Lyons to speak. To write to the whole board, email TOL_BOT@townoflyons.com. Agendas and minutes are on the Town's Agenda Center; the next meeting, on [November 16](/events/board-of-trustees-2026-11-16/), holds the hearings on the 2027 budget and the Planet Bluegrass park leases.
