St. Vrain Valley Schools' open enrollment is fully online. Families apply from 6 am on December 1 through December 15, 2026, for a place at a school of their choice in 2027–28; admission is space-available, schools give their decisions by January 8, 2027, and families confirm by January 21. Applications after December 15 go on the requested school's wait list.
