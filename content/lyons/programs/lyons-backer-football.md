A volunteer club, not a Town program, listed on the Town’s registration page: contact football for middle-schoolers, with fifth graders admitted by prior approval. The 2026 season ran August 3 to October 7, four afternoons a week at Bohn Park with occasional Fridays. The fee was $75, cut in half after the club’s fundraising, and the club says it offers discreet financial help to those in need. Families buy the helmet, mouthpiece, socks and cleats; the club lends the jersey and shoulder pads.
