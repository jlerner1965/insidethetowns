The draft budget went to the Board on October 15 and is [online](https://www.townoflyons.com/DocumentCenter/View/3433/Town-of-Lyons_2027-Draft-Budget) and at Town Hall. Its budget message projects a property tax levy of 17.553 mills and about $1,043,500 in property tax, a 1.2 percent rise in sales tax over this year's projection, operating costs up about 3.7 percent on a $125,000 increase in the Sheriff's contract and $182,800 more for electric transmission, a staff reduction of 0.4 full-time equivalents, and capital money for the 3rd Avenue Safe Routes to School sidewalk, electric undergrounding and the North 5th Avenue sewer line. The two lease ordinances, read a first time on November 2, rent LaVern M. Johnson Park to Telluride Bluegrass Festival, Inc. for five days around each festival at $5,000 a day, 4.2 percent more than 2026, with 4 percent more in 2028, plus overflow parking fees at the 2nd Avenue Trailhead; staff expects $26,300 from RockyGrass and $21,300 from Folks Fest in 2027. Ordinance 1230 adds standards for below-grade crawlspaces and accessory structures in the flood hazard area and corrects the rule on the height of flood openings. The [November 16 listing](/events/board-of-trustees-2026-11-16/) has the meeting.
