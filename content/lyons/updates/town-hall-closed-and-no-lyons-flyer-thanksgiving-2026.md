The Town's calendar lists Town Hall, 432 5th Avenue, as closed for the Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27. Its usual hours are Monday to Thursday 8 am to 4:30 pm and Friday 8 am to noon, and utility bills can be paid [online](https://www.xpressbillpay.com/). The Lyons Flyer runs Monday to Friday except on New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, so there is no bus to or from Boulder on Thursday, November 26. The schedule and stops are on [Living here](/living-here/).
