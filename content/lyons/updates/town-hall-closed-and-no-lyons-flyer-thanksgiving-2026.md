The Town's calendar lists Town Hall, 432 5th Avenue, as closed for the Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27. Its usual hours are Monday to Thursday 8 am to 4:30 pm and Friday 8 am to noon, and utility bills can be paid [online](https://www.xpressbillpay.com/). The Lyons Flyer runs Monday to Friday except on New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, so there is no bus to or from Boulder on Thursday, November 26. The [library's calendar](https://lyons.librarycalendar.com/events/upcoming) has the Lyons Regional Library closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving and on Friday for Native American Heritage Day. [Boulder County](https://bouldercounty.gov/records/motor-vehicle/contact-information/) closes its government offices both days, Motor Vehicle and the [hazardous waste facility](https://bouldercounty.gov/environment/hazardous-waste/) among them. [UCHealth Urgent Care – Main Street](https://www.uchealth.org/locations/uchealth-urgent-care-longmont/), 2101 Main Street in Longmont, is open 8 am to 4 pm on Thanksgiving. The Flyer's schedule and stops are on [Living here](/living-here/).
