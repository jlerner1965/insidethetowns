The Town Administrator's report to the Board of Trustees, dated November 12, says a section of McConnell Drive was milled and overlaid that week, as the final project in the 2026 paving schedule. The Town's November update had planned the work, on the northeast part of the loop from about 326 to 302 McConnell Drive, for the week of November 16.
