The Town's November e-newsletter lists a new location for this year's Holiday Artisan Market: Lyons Middle/Senior High School, because of construction. The market runs Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The school's own site lists its address as 100 McConnell Drive. The Parade of Lights begins at 6:30 p.m. on Main Street on Saturday, December 5, the first day of the market.
