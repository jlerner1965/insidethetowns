The Town sweeps every street twice a year, in spring and fall, to clear debris, keep storm drains open and cut runoff into the river. The north side was swept on Thursday, November 19. On Friday, November 20, the contractor starts on Main Street and Railroad Avenue between 7 and 8 am, then works the south side from east to west, every street in between: the Stone Canyon neighborhood, the Lyons Valley Park neighborhood, 3rd Avenue, 4th Avenue, the Confluence neighborhood, Meily Street, Prospect Street and Ewald Street. The Town asks residents to park off the street or in driveways and to clear debris from the curb; the contractor does not sweep where something blocks the roadway. If the sweeper is on your street, move your vehicle out of its way.
