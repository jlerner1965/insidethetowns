The packet for the November 2 meeting carries three rate resolutions from the Engineering and Utilities Director. Resolution 2026-65 raises water rates by 2.4 percent, the Consumer Price Index figure the Municipal Code requires each year: the residential base rate goes from $30.15 to $30.87 a month and the first tier from $6.29 to $6.44 per 1,000 gallons, which staff put at $1.18 a month on an average bill of 3,000 gallons without irrigation. Resolution 2026-66 raises wastewater rates by the same 2.4 percent, the base from $26.10 to $26.73 and the usage charge from $15.60 to $15.97 per 1,000 gallons, $1.75 a month on the average bill. Resolution 2026-64 sets the stormwater fee at $12 a month per account, up from $10.36, a 15.8 percent rise that staff recommended over the Utility and Engineering Board's 6 percent to put about $17,000 a year more into stormwater work such as curb and gutter, after the budget survey ranked stormwater with roads as a priority. The Board's vote on each is recorded in the minutes of the meeting, on the [Agenda Center](https://www.townoflyons.com/AgendaCenter) once approved.
