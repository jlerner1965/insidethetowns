The Town’s notice of October 24: the drop box has moved from in front of Lyons Town Hall to in front of the Lyons Regional Library at 451 4th Avenue. Boulder County’s boxes are open from October 2 until 7 pm on Election Day. The library’s Community Room is also a vote center on Monday, November 2 (8 am to 6 pm) and Election Day (7 am to 7 pm); see [the Election Day listing](/events/election-day-2026-11-03/).
