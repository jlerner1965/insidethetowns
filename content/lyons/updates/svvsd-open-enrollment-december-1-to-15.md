St. Vrain Valley Schools' open enrollment for the 2027–28 school year is fully online. The application goes live at 6 am on December 1, 2026, and the window runs through December 15; the district says admission is space-available, schools give their decisions by January 8, 2027, and families confirm by January 21. Applications that come in after December 15 go on the requested school's wait list. Preschool registration for 2027–28 opens December 8, 2026, for every family; the district moved all its preschool windows to that day after the state changed its Universal Preschool timeline. Families apply on the district's preschool application, and those seeking a Universal Preschool place for a child who will be 4 on or before October 1, 2027, also complete the state's application, linked from the district's. Families eligible for continuity-of-care priority (returning preschoolers, siblings of district students and children of district staff) keep it by applying between December 8 and February 1.
