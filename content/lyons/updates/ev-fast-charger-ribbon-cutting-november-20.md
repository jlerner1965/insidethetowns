The Town's November newsletter announces an official ribbon cutting for Jule's new DC fast charging station and battery energy storage system at Sandstone Park, on Friday, November 20, at 10:30 am. The Colorado Energy Office and members of the Jule team, an engineer and a technician among them, will be there to talk about the technology, answer questions and explain the grant-funded project. The Town says the chargers had several technical problems over the past year, that Jule and eCAMION finished corrective work, and that the chargers have run consistently since its October update. It asks drivers to report any charging problem to the Town.
