Thirteen and a half miles of trail a mile west of Main Street. Bitterbrush (3.7 miles, difficult) climbs the rocky front to the Nelson Loop (2.2 miles) through ponderosa meadows and the Nelson house ruins; Nighthawk (4.7 miles, hikers and horses) is the quieter way up; Antelope is the short one. Elk in winter, wildflowers in June, and 64 parking spaces across two lots at the main trailhead, eight more at the Antelope trailhead. Bikes yes on Bitterbrush, Nelson and Antelope; dogs no, for the wildlife. The county plans its Nelson Loop prescribed burn for 2027 and closes the park on ignition days; it announces burn dates at least 24 hours ahead on its page.
