The outdoor beer garden at the A-Lodge, just off Main Street downtown, beside Fourmile Creek, which the lodge says runs right through its grounds. Craft beer and local spirits, food trucks on the calendar, a Monday open mic while the weather holds, and bands on Friday and Saturday evenings through the fall.
