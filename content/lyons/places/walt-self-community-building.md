The Town's community building has a main-floor meeting room and a lower-level meeting room. Groups that qualify under the Town's building use policy can reserve it by returning the policy and fee scale forms to the Parks, Recreation and Cultural Events department, 303-823-8250. As the Walt Self Senior Center it hosts the Town's [senior programs](https://www.townoflyons.com/845/Senior-Programming): the Redstone Cafe lunch on Tuesdays to Thursdays, the senior exercise group on Tuesdays and Thursdays and a monthly birthday celebration.
