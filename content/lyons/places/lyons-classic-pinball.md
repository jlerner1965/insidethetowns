More than 70 machines from seven decades in a Main Street storefront, with monthly open and women's tournaments, a Thursday-night league each quarter and a Fifth Sunday Classics tournament in the months that have one. Play is by day pass or membership, not by the quarter.

Open Thursday 5–10 pm, Friday 4–10 pm, Saturday noon–10 pm and Sunday noon–9 pm; closed Monday to Wednesday. In 2026 it is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve, and open for an event on New Year's Eve.
