The Town's campground inside the park, a five-minute walk from Main Street along the river, with the whitewater park and the playgrounds on the doorstep. RV sites with full hookups all year, tent sites from mid-April to mid-October, reservations through the Town. Bears walk the river corridor: food in the vehicle or the locker, never the tent.
