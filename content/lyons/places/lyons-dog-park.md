Ten fenced acres at the south end of [Bohn Park](/places/bohn-park/), kept by the Town and the only designated off-leash area in Lyons. It is free and open to everyone from dawn to dusk daily, unless it is closed for maintenance or an event. Park free on 2nd Avenue or in Bohn Park's paid lot at 199 2nd Ave, then walk the short trail due south; the Town warns that Google Maps sends drivers by 5th Avenue to a private road.

Dogs stay leashed until they are through the first gate. Every dog must be vaccinated and wear its Town licence ($30 for the first dog, $20 for each more; licences run May 1 to April 30). Three dogs a person at most, no human food, no dogs that are aggressive, ill or in heat, and children only with an adult. A bite or any other problem goes to Boulder County Animal Control, 303-441-4444.
