A town of two thousand with a nationally known music scene, and this is why. The ranch on the river at the west end of Main hosts RockyGrass in late July and the Rocky Mountain Folks Festival in August, both with on-site camping and both selling out; the Wildflower Pavilion carries a spring and fall concert series. Festival weekends fill every bed in town months ahead.
