The trail area that dries first in spring and stays walkable in winter. The Eagle Wind loop (about 2.5 miles), the Little Thompson Overlook (1.5 miles one way) and Indian Mesa (2.2 miles) all leave from one trailhead with a restroom and shelter. Rattlesnakes in summer, and the one county area here where leashed dogs are welcome.
