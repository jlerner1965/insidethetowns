Longmont's watershed around Ralph Price Reservoir, up County Road 80 west of town. The gated dam road is a two-mile walk along the North St. Vrain. Foot travel only, no dogs (the City has banned them since May 15, 2023), and fishing in the reservoir from May 1 to October 31, by a City of Longmont permit sold from April 1. The lot at the road end holds 60 vehicles and closes when full; a car parked outside it, along the road included, can be ticketed or towed.

From December 1 through March 31 the northwest corner of the preserve is closed to recreation for wintering elk: the Cook Mountain Trail and the way in from the Coulson Gulch Trail (Forest Service Trail 916).
