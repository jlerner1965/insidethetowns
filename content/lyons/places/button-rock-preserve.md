Longmont's watershed around Ralph Price Reservoir, up County Road 80 west of town. The gated dam road is a two-mile walk along the North St. Vrain. Foot travel only, no dogs, and fishing in the reservoir by a City of Longmont permit sold from April. The lot at the road end holds 60 vehicles and closes when full.
