The Lyons Regional Library District’s building, signed at the door as the Lyons Community Library; this guide uses the district’s name throughout. Built in 2019 after the flood put the old Depot library out of use. Story times, a makerspace, a LEGO club, a ukulele group, talks and the room where the Town's commissions meet. Cards are free to district residents.
