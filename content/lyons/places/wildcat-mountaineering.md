An outdoor gear shop on Main Street, established in 2024, on the way to Rocky Mountain National Park and the Indian Peaks Wilderness. It stocks camping, hiking and fly fishing equipment and gear for the highest and most technical summits, and runs a stewardship program on trail building and maintenance, route development and access in the St. Vrain canyons.
