The indoor hall on the [Planet Bluegrass](/places/planet-bluegrass/) ranch, where the concerts happen when the festival fields are quiet. Planet Bluegrass runs a Wildflower Concert Series there in spring and fall: seated shows, a full bar, and the bathhouse restrooms a short walk away. During RockyGrass and the Folks Festival in summer it is the second stage, the Wildflower Stage.

The fall 2026 series ran on Wednesday evenings from late September into early November; the dates and tickets are on Planet Bluegrass's Wildflower page, and the shows are listed on this guide's events pages as they are announced.
