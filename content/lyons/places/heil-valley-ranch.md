The big open space south of town. Picture Rock (5.2 miles, moderate) starts on the Lyons side and links to Wild Turkey (2.9) and Wapiti (3) for a long loop; the Overland, Schoolhouse and Lichen loops sit by the Wapiti and Corral trailheads off Lefthand Canyon Drive. On-trail only, for the wildlife habitat and the burn areas; no dogs.
