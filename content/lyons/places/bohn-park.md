The park has ball fields and the off-leash dog park, and the St. Vrain Trailhead is nearby.

The shelter/pavilion and the riverside picnic area can be reserved for groups, and picnic tables on summer weekends and holidays. Dry camping at the nearby Second Avenue trailhead, by reservation, is summer overflow for campervans.
