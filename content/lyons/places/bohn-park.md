The park has ball fields, a skate park, the Dirt Bike Park and the off-leash dog park, and the St. Vrain Trailhead on 2nd Avenue is nearby.

The multi-use sport court takes basketball, tennis and pickleball, 7 am to 7 pm year-round. Reserve it online by the hour at no cost, up to three hours a week per person and up to 7 days ahead, never the same day; bring your own equipment, a pickleball net included. The shelter/pavilion and the riverside picnic area can be reserved for groups, and picnic tables on summer weekends and holidays. Camping is not allowed in the park, but campervans can dry camp at the St. Vrain Trailhead on 2nd Avenue from mid-May through mid-September.
