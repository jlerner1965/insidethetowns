An herb and tea apothecary on the 300 block, stocked with responsibly harvested botanicals, teas and tinctures.

Loose-leaf green, black, oolong and white teas sit alongside house herbal blends, with bulk herbs sold by the ounce. Pots of tea and iced tea are served in the shop, and the shelves also carry books, botanical gifts, candles and locally made goods. The online Square store has a smaller selection than the shop.
