The clinic on High Street has new owners: Dr. James Shea, a veterinarian and certified veterinary acupuncturist who joined the practice full time in January 2025, and his wife Courtney, its practice manager. They mark the change with an open house on Sunday, November 1, noon to 3 pm, with raffle tickets for a gift basket, one for cats and one for dogs. Dr. Duncan stays on part time; hours remain Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm.
