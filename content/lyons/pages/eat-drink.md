  - place: moxie-mercantile
    for: "Breakfast before a hike"
    why: "Moxie Bread Co.'s shop at 442 High St, open 7 am to 4 pm every day: coffee, a rotating choice of its pastries, breads and sandwiches, and picnic bags for hikers on their way to Rocky Mountain National Park."
  - place: mudhut-lyons
    for: "Coffee on the drive"
    why: "Lyons' only drive-through coffee stand, the Town's visitor page says, at 4033 Ute Highway. Open 7 am to 3 pm daily, closed on Thanksgiving, November 26. Bring the dog: it hands out pup cups."
  - place: creative-grounds
    for: "Coffee with children"
    why: "A nonprofit café, pottery studio and third space for young people at 160 E Main St, open Wednesday to Sunday, 8 am to 4:30 pm. Coffee roasted by Ozo, syrups made in-house, pastries and sandwiches made each day, and games, books and rock painting for kids and adults."
  - place: indian-bites
    for: "Lunch, any day"
    why: "Curries, tandoori, biryani and saag at 432 Main St, open every day from 11 am, to 8 pm (8:30 on Fridays and Saturdays). Its site says every entrée is gluten-free, and dishes made with cream can be made with coconut cream instead."
  - place: smokin-daves-bbq-taphouse
    for: "Feeding a group"
    why: "Slow-smoked barbecue, sides made from scratch and rotating draft beers at 228 Main St, open daily from 11 am (to 7 pm Monday to Thursday, 8 pm Friday to Sunday). Part of a Colorado group serving barbecue since 2007; it takes reservations, parties and takeout."
  - place: mainstage-brewing-company
    for: "Kids and dogs along"
    why: "A brewery and pizza kitchen at 450 Main St, open since 2021, with live music and a big patio. Kids are welcome, and leashed dogs on the outdoor and covered patios. Orders are taken at the counter; there are gluten-free and vegan pizzas. Open daily from 11 am."
  - place: mojo-taqueria
    for: "Tacos, Tuesday to Sunday"
    why: "Tacos and Mexican dishes made from scratch, drawing on Oaxaca, Yucatán and Baja, at 216 E Main St. Dinner Tuesday to Thursday from 4 pm, lunch and dinner Friday to Sunday from 11 am; closed Mondays. Taco Tuesday, tortas on Wednesdays and fajitas on Thursdays."
  - place: spirit-hound-distillers
    for: "Craft cocktails"
    why: "The original Spirit Hound tasting room, at the distillery at 4196 Ute Highway: craft cocktails, bottles to take home and events every week. Its site lists Best of Boulder's distillery of the year eight years running."
  - place: lyons-locale
    for: "Wine, and paella Thursdays"
    why: "A wine and cocktail lounge at 442 High St, open Wednesday to Sunday, 4 to 9 pm, with the kitchen serving sandwiches, salads and bistro dinners until 8:30. Thursday is paella night: chicken or vegetable paella with a glass of house wine for $23."
  - place: marigold
    for: "Dinner out"
    why: "Northern Italian and southern French cooking at 405 Main St, dinner only, Wednesday to Sunday from 5 pm. Its site lists James Beard semifinalist nods for Best Chef: Mountain in 2024 and 2026, and a place on Wine Enthusiast's 2024 list of the best wine restaurants in America. Reservations are online."
