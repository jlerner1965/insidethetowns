From December 1 a power company buries lines along the Bitterbrush access road, and the County says the road may close during the work."
  - place: main-street
    for: "A first afternoon"
    why: "Quarry workers founded Lyons, the Town says, and several Main Street buildings date to the late 1800s. Its self-guided Historic District Walking Tour, online at Clio, tells their stories. The museum in the 1881 schoolhouse on High Street reopens on weekends in May."
  - place: lavern-m-johnson-park
    for: "The river, all year"
    why: "The Town's park, bounded by the North St. Vrain and sandstone cliffs: a quarter-mile whitewater park with eight features, playgrounds, picnic shelters and RV sites with full hookups all year. For the winter of 2027 the Ember N Ice sauna club runs hour-long sessions there, with a cold plunge in the creek."
  - place: rabbit-mountain
    for: "Hiking with a dog"
    why: "Unlike Hall Ranch and Heil Valley Ranch, it takes dogs, on a leash: Eagle Wind (2.5 miles), Indian Mesa (2.2) and Little Thompson Overlook (1.5) from one trailhead with 38 parking spaces. The County may close it on prescribed-burn days between November 15 and May 31, announced 24 hours ahead."
  - place: heil-valley-ranch
    for: "A longer loop"
    why: "County open space where Picture Rock (5.2 miles) links to Wild Turkey and Wapiti for a long loop, past a sandstone quarry worked from the 1890s to the 1960s. The Lichen and Schoolhouse loops are short and easy. On-trail use only, and no dogs."
  - place: button-rock-preserve
    for: "A creekside walk"
    why: "The City of Longmont's walk-in watershed about 7 miles west of Lyons: a 2-mile walk up the North St. Vrain to Ralph Price Reservoir, where eagles hunt over the water in winter, the City says. No dogs or bikes. The northwest corner closes December 1 to March 31 for wintering elk."
  - place: lyons-classic-pinball
    for: "A cold evening"
    why: "More than 70 machines from seven decades on Main Street, played on a day pass or membership instead of quarters, in an alcohol-free hall for all ages. Thursday 5–10 pm, Friday 4–10, Saturday noon–10, Sunday noon–9; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve."
  - place: lyons-dog-park
    for: "Dogs off the leash"
    why: "Ten fenced acres at the south end of Bohn Park, free from dawn to dusk and the only off-leash area in Lyons. Park on 2nd Avenue or in Bohn Park's paid lot at 199 2nd Ave; the Town warns that Google Maps sends drivers to a private road. Dogs must be vaccinated and licensed."
  - place: planet-bluegrass
    for: "Summer festivals"
    why: "Planet Bluegrass, at 500 West Main Street, hosts RockyGrass on July 23–25, 2027 and the 37th Rocky Mountain Folks Festival on August 6–8, 2027. Its Wildflower Pavilion holds concerts in spring and fall."
  - place: tubing-the-st-vrain
    for: "Summer on the river"
    why: "The St. Vrain flows through the heart of Lyons, and visitors come to tube it. Warning flags show the creek's status, the Town bans tubing above 450 cfs and requires life jackets for children under 13 through July 15; Ray's River Rentals, its official outfitter, rents tubes and jackets when conditions are safe."

These ten are for a first visit to Lyons, or a first year living here: where to walk, where to take a dog, what to do on a cold evening, and what to plan for next summer. Each one links to its page on this guide, with directions and the rules of whoever runs it.

**How we chose them.** Every pick is a place already in the directory below, and every fact above comes from the place's own page or its manager's, read on November 11, 2026: Boulder County Parks & Open Space, the City of Longmont, the Town of Lyons, Planet Bluegrass and Lyons Classic Pinball. Nobody pays to be listed. The order follows what most visitors come for: the trails and Main Street first, then the river park, then the places for a dog or a cold evening, and last the two summer draws, which are out of season now.

**What is not here.** The Lyons Redstone Museum is closed until May. Bohn Park, Sandstone Park and the library are in the directory below. For more on dogs and trails, read [Lyons hikes: where dogs are allowed](/articles/lyons-hikes-where-dogs-are-allowed/) and [Trails and the river](/articles/trails-and-the-river/); with children, [Lyons with kids](/articles/lyons-with-kids/). Conditions change by the day: the County posts burn closures on each park's page, and the Town's flags decide the river.
