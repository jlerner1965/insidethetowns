The Town of Frederick's roadwork page carries Dacono's notice at the top of its list: Colorado Boulevard closed between Highway 52 and Second Street from September 8 for a new roundabout at Sixth Street, expected to stay closed through December 1, with drivers asked to expect delays and follow the posted detour. Dacono's own notices add the rest. The Carbon Valley Veterans Wall of Honor dedication set for Sunday, November 8 at 10 am is canceled because the roundabout work limits access; the 2026 names are still being engraved on the wall and will be recognized at the 2027 ceremony. From Tuesday, November 3 the intersection of Glen Creighton Drive and Grandview Boulevard is restricted for about two weeks, with Grandview one-way westbound and the right turn from Glen Creighton onto Grandview, to reach Colorado Boulevard, still open. CDOT's [Colorado 52 resurfacing](/updates/#co-52-resurfacing-i-25-to-us-85) runs through December on the same page.
