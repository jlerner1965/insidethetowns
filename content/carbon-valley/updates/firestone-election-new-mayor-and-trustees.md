The Town of Firestone posts Weld County's unofficial results of its November 3 regular municipal election, as updated by the county on November 5 at 12:50 pm. For mayor, Scott Nahman had 3,885 votes and Scott Franks 3,176. For the five trustee seats, John Damsma had 3,818, Matt Holcomb 3,539, Kevin Botka 3,419, Troy Athmann 3,239, Michael W. Ayres 2,941, Joel Marsh 2,715, Cale Gould 2,470 and Joseph Jahan-Tigh 1,253. The three trustees with the most votes serve four-year terms and the next two serve two-year terms, which keeps the board's terms staggered; the mayor serves two years. Six seats were on the ballot this time because of earlier resignations and recalls. The Weld County Clerk and Recorder certifies the official results no later than November 25, and the new mayor and trustees take the oath of office after that. This is the board that will take up the Town's [2027 budget](/updates/#firestone-2027-budget-adoption-early-2027) in early 2027.
