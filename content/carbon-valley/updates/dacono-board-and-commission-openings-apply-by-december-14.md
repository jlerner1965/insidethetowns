The City of Dacono is looking for residents to serve on four of its boards and commissions: the Planning Commission (three openings), the Board of Adjustments (two), the Liquor Authority (two) and the Police Advisory Board (one). Applicants must be at least 18 and have lived in the City for at least 12 consecutive months immediately before the appointment. Applications are at Dacono City Hall or on the City's website, and go to City Clerk Valerie Taylor at City Hall, 512 Cherry Avenue, Building A, or vtaylor@DaconoCO.gov, by 5 pm on Monday, December 14. Council will hold interviews if it needs to, on a date not yet set, and makes the appointments at its meeting on Monday, January 11, 2027.
