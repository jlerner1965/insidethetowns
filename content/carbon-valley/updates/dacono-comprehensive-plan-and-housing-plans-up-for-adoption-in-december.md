After more than a year of work, the City of Dacono says its updated Comprehensive Plan, Housing Needs Assessment and Housing Action Plan are nearly done, and the Planning and Zoning Commission and City Council are expected to consider adopting all three at their public meetings in December 2026. The Comprehensive Plan sets the long-term vision for growth, including water supply; the Housing Needs Assessment looks at housing conditions and affordability gaps; and the Housing Action Plan lists possible steps to meet the needs it finds. The final drafts of the two housing plans have been out for public review since September, and the City had not set a hearing date. Council's regular meeting of November 23 is canceled. A special Council meeting on Wednesday, December 2, after the Urban Renewal Authority meeting at 6 pm in the Annex Building, 512 Cherry Avenue, Building C, has two items: a public hearing on Ordinance 1025, a sales tax credit for the property at the southwest corner of Highway 52 and Colorado Boulevard, and a public finance agreement for the Sharpe Farms Towne Center development.
