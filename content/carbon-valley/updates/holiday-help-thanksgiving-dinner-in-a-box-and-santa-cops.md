**Thanksgiving Dinner in a Box.** The Town, Frederick High's student council, the [Carbon Valley Help Center](/places/carbon-valley-help-center/), Rocky Mountain Christian Church and the Weld County Food Bank aim to give a complete Thanksgiving dinner to at least 100 Carbon Valley families. Requests closed on November 13 and food donations on November 18, and the Town's page says the donation list is completely filled. The boxes, with a turkey and the traditional sides, go out on Monday, November 23, from 10 am to noon at Rocky Mountain Christian Church's Frederick campus, 5860 Majestic Street; the Help Center says registered households get their pickup or delivery details from the organizers.

**Santa Cops and the Angel Tree.** The police department's program ([Santa Cops](https://www.frederickco.gov/959/Santa-Cops)) gives gifts to children from birth through 16 in families in financial hardship, referred by schools, churches, friends or relatives through an [English](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Police-7/Santa-Cops-Referral-Form-English-456) or [Spanish](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Police-7/Santa-Cops-Referral-Form-Spanish-291) form by 5 pm on Monday, November 30 (Kristy O'Hayre, 720-382-5711). To give, take a tag from the Angel Tree in the station lobby at 333 Fifth Street and return the gift with it, or leave a new, unwrapped toy, clothing item or other gift, weekdays 8 to 5, by the same deadline; host a drive; [donate online](https://checkout.square.site/merchant/Q6B65XEE4SVW6/checkout/EWV6KVPITYDY4AMCC2GCYEHQ); or mail a check to Frederick Police Department – Santa Cops, PO Box 639, Frederick, CO 80530. Officers and volunteers wrap in early December and deliver to the families.
