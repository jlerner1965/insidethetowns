Weld County says its offices are closed on Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27, for Thanksgiving; that includes the [Southwest Weld motor vehicle office](https://www.weld.gov/Government/Departments/Clerk-and-Recorder/Motor-Vehicle-Department/Contact-Us) at 4209 County Road 24.5 and the [elections office](https://www.weld.gov/Government/Departments/Clerk-and-Recorder/Elections-Department). The county's South Household Hazardous Waste Facility, 5500 Highway 52 in Dacono, normally open Wednesday, Friday and Saturday from 9 am to 4 pm, is closed on Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28, so its last Thanksgiving-week day is Wednesday, November 25. [UCHealth Urgent Care – Firestone](https://www.uchealth.org/locations/uchealth-urgent-care-firestone), 5965 Firestone Boulevard, is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26; its regular hours are 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 8 to 6 at weekends.
