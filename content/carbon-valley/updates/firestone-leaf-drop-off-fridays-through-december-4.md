The Town of Firestone is running free leaf drop-off days for residents at the Firestone Maintenance Facility, 7500 Weld County Road 20, on Fridays from November 6 through December 4, noon to 3 pm; November 20, November 27 and December 4 are the ones left. It takes leaves only: tree limbs and other woody yard waste are turned away. Staff are on site to direct traffic and help unload.
