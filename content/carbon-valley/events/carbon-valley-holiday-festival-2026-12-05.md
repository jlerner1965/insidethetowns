Dacono's evening of the three-town Carbon Valley Holiday Festival, at City Hall from 4 to 6 pm: pictures with Santa (bring a camera), goody bags for children, carriage rides, chili and sweet treats, and the Homyak student choir. Free. Firestone's festival at Miners Park and Frederick's Festival of Lights in Crist Park are the same day.
