The Firestone Police Department's trunk-or-treat, which the Town calls its most popular fall event: decorated trunks handing out candy, inflatables and obstacle courses, a trackless train, a word about community safety and food trucks (Emory Jane's Coffee, Infinitus Pie and Nacho Mamas Tacos), from 5 to 8 pm at the Firestone Regional Sports Complex.

The Town’s calendar entry still gives Miners Park, 170 Grant Avenue; its event page, the fuller and more recent listing (it names the food trucks), says the Sports Complex, and that is followed here.
