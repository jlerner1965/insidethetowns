The City of Dacono's part of the Carbon Valley Holiday Festival is Saturday, December 5, from 4 to 6 pm at Dacono City Hall, 512 Cherry Avenue. It is free and open to everyone.

## What's on

- **Tree lighting:** a countdown to the City's new color-changing Christmas tree light display.
- **Drone light show:** a holiday show of lighted drones, presented by the Innovation Center.
- **Santa**, for photos.
- **Horse-drawn carriage rides.**
- **Chili, hot chocolate and holiday treats**, and goody bags for children.
- **Carols** by the Homyak PK-8 student choir.

## The 2026 Dacono ornament

This year's ornament has a color-changing LED light. It costs $5 or the same value in nonperishable food, and everything raised goes to the [Carbon Valley Help Center](/places/carbon-valley-help-center/). It is on sale at City Hall during business hours and at the festival, while supplies last.

## One of three festivals that day

Dacono's evening is one of the three that make up the Carbon Valley Holiday Festival. [Firestone's](/events/carbon-valley-holiday-festival-2026-12-05/) runs from noon to 4 pm at Miners Park, and Frederick's [Festival of Lights](/events/festival-of-lights/) is 5:30 to 8 pm in Crist Park.
