The district’s Active Adults program paints bowls for the Carbon Valley Help Center, which feeds and supports families across the three towns: buy a bowl for $20 (cash, check, Venmo or PayPal), paint it, and it is kiln-fired and used at the Help Center’s event in November; the bowls are a donation and are not returned. Supplies are provided, the painting is seated, and registration in advance is required. Monday 12 October, 1 to 3 pm, in the Community Center’s community room.
