Día de los Muertos at the Fort Lupton Public & School Library's public branch, Monday, November 2, 5:30 to 7:30 pm: an evening of remembrance with the miniature ofrendas, or Day of the Dead altars, that individuals, families and organizations build at the library each year (this is the fifth year), a larger-than-life ofrenda dedicated to Frida Kahlo, traditional pan de muerto, and the library lit by candlelight. The altars are on display from October 26 to November 9. To contribute a miniature ofrenda, fill in the library's participation form in advance; the [Town's page](https://www.fortluptonco.gov/1157/Dia-de-los-Muertos) has the details.

> Join us for a special evening of remembrance and celebration as we honor the lives of loved ones who have passed. Explore beautiful ofrendas created by community members and experience our larger-than-life ofrenda dedicated to Frida Kahlo, celebrating her remarkable life, art, and cultural legacy.
