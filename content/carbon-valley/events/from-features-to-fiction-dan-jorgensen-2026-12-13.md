The author Dan Jorgensen talks at the library, Sunday, December 13, 2 to 3:30 pm, about how years of writing newspaper features in the Black Hills of South Dakota led to his historical mystery-thrillers, among them And the Wind Whispered, Rainbow Rock and Devil's Thumb, and to his adventure novel Killer Blizzard. He covers life as an author and getting a book published, and takes questions. For adults and high schoolers; registration opens on the library's page at 2 pm on November 29.
