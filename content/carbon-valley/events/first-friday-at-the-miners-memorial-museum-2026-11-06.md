The Town's history museum, in the original Frederick Town Hall moved into Crist Park, opens on the first Friday of the month from 5 to 7 pm. Inside: a diorama of the original town, an original wine press, the original mayor's chair, stained glass and a fireplace from the old Johnson Farm building, mining tools, a coal stove and old school desks. The Town's page lists no admission charge. The other remaining 2026 opening is Friday, December 4; otherwise the Town Clerk's office arranges tours (720-382-5581, townclerk@frederickco.gov).
