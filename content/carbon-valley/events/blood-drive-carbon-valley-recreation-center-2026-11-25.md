The American Red Cross takes blood donations at the Carbon Valley Recreation Center on Wednesday, November 25 from 9 am to 2 pm; the district's home page lists it among this month's events. Book a time at RedCrossBlood.org (sponsor code CarbonValley), by calling 1-800-733-2767 or in the Blood Donor App, and bring a photo ID or donor card. Donors get a free mini health screening.
