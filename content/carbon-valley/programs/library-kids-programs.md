**LEGO Club** supplies the bricks and sets a different challenge each month, or children build what they like; it is for ages 6 to 13 who can build on their own, and an adult must stay in the library with any child under 12. December's sessions are Saturday the 12th from 10 to 11:30 am and Wednesday the 16th from 4 to 5. **Science Fun** is an hour of activities, games and experiments for third and fourth graders on second Thursdays at 4:30, with registration. **Let's Draw!** is games, drawing techniques and a light snack for grades 2 to 5 on third Thursdays at 4. **Tween Craft Club** (ages 9 to 12, supplies provided) is first Tuesdays at 4, with registration. At **Paws to Read**, early readers 5 and up read for about ten minutes to a certified therapy dog, third Tuesdays at 6 pm; it is first come, first served and the library cannot take children under 5, siblings included.

Several of the library's pages carry a boilerplate note calling the session an online event, which contradicts the room each names; the sessions are in the building.
