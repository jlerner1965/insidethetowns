Erie Coffee Roasters describes itself as a micro-roaster and cafe located in Frederick, roasting fair-trade coffees from around the world on site. The ECR Cafe is its storefront: espresso drinks, drip and pour-over coffee, food from other Front Range makers and sandwiches on NoCo Sourdough focaccia. Beans are sold online and wholesale, and the site sells places in occasional Saturday roasting masterclasses, where you roast 250 g of green coffee of your choosing on the sample roaster and take it home; the next is on December 12, 2026, at $35 a slot.
