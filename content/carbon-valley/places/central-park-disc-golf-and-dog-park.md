The Town bought the 252-acre Central Park site in 2005 and is still settling its long-range plan, having ended a 2025 pre-development agreement with the United Soccer League early in 2026; for now the disc golf course (the Town links a course map) and Aisik’s Meadow dog park are its recreation. The Town publishes no park hours.
