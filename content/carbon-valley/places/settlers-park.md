Settlers Park is one of the Town of Firestone's larger parks, at 11447 Deerfield Drive. The Town lists a baseball and softball field, a basketball court, pickleball and tennis courts, volleyball, a playground, picnic shelters and tables, restrooms, a water fountain, parking and trails. It is one end of the [Firestone Trail](/places/firestone-trail/), which the Town says runs 12 miles from here to Coronado Park on the bed of the old coal railroad. Town shelters are first come, first served; a group of up to 49 that wants one held needs a park shelter permit, requested seven days ahead.
