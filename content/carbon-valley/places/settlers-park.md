The Town lists baseball and softball, a basketball court, pickleball courts, a tennis court, volleyball, a playground, picnic shelters and tables, restrooms, trails, parking and a water fountain. The History page names Settlers Park as the start of the Firestone Trail, which runs from here to Coronado Park. The Town publishes no park hours.
