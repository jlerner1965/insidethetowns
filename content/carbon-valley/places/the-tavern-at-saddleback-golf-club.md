The Tavern is the restaurant at Saddleback Golf Club, 8631 Frontier Street, open Thursday to Sunday from 11 am to 5 pm. The Town of Firestone's history says the course sits where the McKissick brothers opened Colorado's first production coal mine in 1872. The club also runs a Trackman simulator studio and lessons; call 303-833-5000.
