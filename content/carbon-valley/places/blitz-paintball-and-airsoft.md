Blitz runs scenario and recreational paintball fields and dedicated airsoft fields, with referees running organized games across every zone at once. Walk-ons play at weekends; weekday visits are by reservation for groups, and the park takes birthday, bachelor and bachelorette and team-building parties. It closes in heavy rain or snow and below 32 degrees, and around Christmas when it falls near a weekend, so call first; the waiver can be signed online before arriving.
