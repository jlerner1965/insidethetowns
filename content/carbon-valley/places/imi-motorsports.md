IMI bills itself as Colorado’s premier motorsports destination: an asphalt track shared by karts, motorcycles and drift cars and two dirt courses for motocross, ATVs and off-road vehicles. Walk-up race-kart sessions are listed at $50 for one 15-minute session or $85 for two; a day on the asphalt with your own kart or motorcycle is $50, drift cars $100 and the dirt track $30, with yearly passes. The complex also hosts corporate events, team building and birthday parties.
