Jacob H. Firestone Memorial Park, at 36 Grant Avenue, is named for the Ohio investor the Town is named after; the Town says Firestone was founded in 1908 to support the coal miners and their families. On August 29, 2009, the Board of Trustees dedicated the Firestone Centennial Clock, a time capsule and a brick plaza here, its pavers carrying the names of the project's supporters; the capsule is to be opened in 2059. The Town lists parking, picnic tables and a trail.
