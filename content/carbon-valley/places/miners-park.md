Miners Park is at 170 Grant Avenue in the old part of Firestone. The Town lists a picnic shelter and picnic tables, benches, horseshoe pits and a water fountain. It is where Firestone holds its part of the [Carbon Valley Holiday Festival](/events/carbon-valley-holiday-festival-2026-12-05/).
