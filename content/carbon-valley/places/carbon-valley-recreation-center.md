The district that serves Frederick, Firestone and Dacono runs its recreation centre on Fifth Street, with a lap pool (diving board, aquatic climbing wall), an activity pool with a play structure and lily pads, a steam room, a full-court gymnasium, an elevated indoor track, cardio and weight rooms and a fitness studio. The pool and outdoor fitness area close half an hour before the building. A Kid Zone runs weekday and Saturday mornings and Monday to Thursday evenings; holiday hours differ. The district’s Community Center and Gymnastics Center are separate buildings, and its Harvest & Holly Market fills the rec centre each November.
