The Frederick Police Department is at 333 Fifth Street (mail: P.O. Box 435). The department's number is 720-382-5700; the non-emergency dispatch line is 720-652-4222; emergencies are 911. Records requests and online crime reporting go through the Town's site, and the lobby, open weekdays 8 am to 5 pm according to the department's community programs page, holds the prescription-drug disposal box (no needles or liquids), the Angel Tree in November and the pet-license counter: dogs $5 a year, cats free, renewed each January with a current rabies vaccination.
