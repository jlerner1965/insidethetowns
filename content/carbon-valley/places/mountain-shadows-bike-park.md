Approved by the trustees in 2022 and built in two phases at once, a paved pump track and a dirt bike park, the park opened in September 2023. The Town describes asphalt and dirt pathways, intersections and turns meant to build off-road riding skills, as much a playground as a training ground, for bikes, skateboards and scooters of all abilities. Mountain Shadows Park next door has a playground, shelters and trails. The Town publishes no park hours.
