Gabe’s calls itself a family-owned Frederick restaurant, established in 2015, with breakfast and lunch served all day. It opens at 6 am on weekdays and 7 am at weekends, closing early (2 pm) on Tuesdays and Sundays. The cafe has added alcohol and a happy-hour menu, sells gift cards, takes pick-up orders and rents the room after hours.
