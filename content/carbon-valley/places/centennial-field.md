Centennial Field is where Dacono stages its biggest days. The one-day Music & Spirits Festival draws five to six thousand people with bands, a beer garden and spirits-tasting tent at the south end of the field, food trucks, vendors and a kids’ zone (the 2026 edition was on 1 August); the Boot Scoot’n Bash, presented by Colorado National Speedway, brings country music, line-dancing lessons and food trucks with free admission each June. Bring chairs and blankets, and expect wind. The City gives the field’s address on its vendor page.
