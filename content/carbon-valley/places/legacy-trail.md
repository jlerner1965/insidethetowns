The City annexed the former Union Pacific right-of-way in 2007 to build the trail, and the first alignment is finished: a ten-foot concrete path paired with a three-foot crusher-fines jogging path, with trailhead landscaping at the corner of Colorado 52 and Colorado Boulevard. When the full Dacono alignment is complete it will link six subdivisions with parks and shops as Dacono’s piece of the cooperative St. Vrain Legacy Trail.
