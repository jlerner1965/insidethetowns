Extended Stay America's Select Suites hotel in Firestone, at 11334 Business Park Drive, is built for stays of a few nights to several months. Every room has a full kitchen and free wi-fi, there is a guest laundry, and some rooms take pets, for a cleaning fee of up to $25 a night for the first six nights.
