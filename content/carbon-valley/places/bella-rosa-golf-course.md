Bella Rosa is a nine-hole regulation course of rolling fairways, bunkers, water and undulating greens, run by the Town of Frederick’s Golf Division, with a driving range, putting green and chipping area. The club lists rates from $19 for nine holes walking, with senior and junior rates, punch cards, cart and club hire and range buckets, and runs lessons, leagues, tournaments and glow-ball evenings. The range opens late on Mondays and Thursdays and closes early on Sundays and Wednesdays for mowing. On the course's rates page (read 2 November 2026) nine holes walking is $19 on weekdays and $24 Friday to Sunday and holidays, 18 holes $29 and $36, seniors 55 and over $16 for nine on weekdays, juniors 17 and under $12; a power cart is $15 for nine. The Town's Outdoor Adventures page says the course is open dawn to dusk all year, weather permitting.
