Bella Rosa is a nine-hole regulation course of rolling fairways, bunkers, water and undulating greens, run by the Town of Frederick’s Golf Division, with a driving range, putting green and chipping area. The club lists rates from $19 for nine holes walking, with senior and junior rates, punch cards, cart and club hire and range buckets, and runs lessons, leagues, tournaments and glow-ball evenings. The range opens late on Mondays and Thursdays and closes early on Sundays and Wednesdays for mowing.
