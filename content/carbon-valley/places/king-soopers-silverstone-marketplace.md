The largest grocery store in the Carbon Valley, as the Town puts it, at the northwest corner of Colorado Boulevard and Highway 52 in the Silverstone neighborhood. Besides the groceries it has a drive-thru pharmacy, a fuel center on site, sushi, a Murray's Cheese counter, a Starbucks, and pickup and delivery.
