The Firestone Trail follows the bed of the railroad that carried coal out of Firestone's mines until the last of them closed in 1947. The Town says it runs 12 miles from [Settlers Park](/places/settlers-park/) to Coronado Park, connecting neighborhood and regional parks, the St. Vrain Legacy Trail and the Colorado Front Range Trail. The Town's facility listing puts it at 100 Grant Avenue in the old town, with benches and picnic tables along the way.
