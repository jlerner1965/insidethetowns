Hart Park is in the older part of Firestone near the original townsite. The Town lists baseball and softball, a soccer field, a basketball court, volleyball, benches, picnic shelters and tables, restrooms, trails and a water fountain; shelters are first come, first served unless permitted. The Town publishes no park hours.
