The Carbon Valley Help Center provides food, resources and support to individuals and families in Frederick, Firestone and Dacono who are facing financial hardship; its testimonials mention rent and utility assistance grants as well as the pantry. The centre's events list shows office hours every Tuesday and Thursday from 9 am to 3 pm through May 2027. It is a partner in the Town of Frederick's Thanksgiving Dinner in a Box, and its twelfth annual [Empty Bowls fundraiser](/events/empty-bowls-fundraiser-2026-11-14/) is on November 14 at Frederick High School. Volunteers apply through a form on the centre's site; donations can be made online, and its "Adopt a Shelf" program keeps the pantry stocked. Email info@carbonvalleyhelpcenter.org.
