Founded by Gene and Gerda Heffley in 1965, the Speedway runs more than a dozen divisions on its three-eighths-mile asphalt oval every Saturday night from spring through fall, with a no-smoking, no-alcohol family section at the finish line, driver meet-and-greets in the hot pit after the races and free parking. The track lists regular-event admission at $17 for adults and $8 for ages 5 to 12, with under-fives free, and allows sealed soft drinks and small soft coolers. On race days the pit gate opens at 1 pm, practice at 3, qualifying at 5:30 and racing at 6:30 sharp; take I-25 exit 232 from Denver or exit 235 and Highway 52 from the north. The City calls Dacono its home.
