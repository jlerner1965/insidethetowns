The Town lists baseball and softball fields, parking, picnic shelters and tables, restrooms, trails, bins and a water fountain. Fields are reserved online through the Town’s facilities portal or by email to parkandfields@firestoneco.gov; shelters are first come, first served without a permit. The Town publishes no park hours.
