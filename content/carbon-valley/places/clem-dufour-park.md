The City calls Clem DuFour Park its main park and the focal point of its recreation, and its 2008 parks plan puts it at 11.5 acres with ball fields, a playground, picnic tables and parking. The splash pad’s pop-up fountains, jets and cascades run daily from 9 am to 8 pm between Memorial Day and Labor Day, weather permitting, and are never rented out; the covered pavilion (forty people, with power) can be booked by Dacono residents over 18 in four-hour blocks at City Hall, with no alcohol, glass, inflatables or grills. The Dacono Fall Fiesta moved here in 2026, and the City’s free Summer Splash is held here each July. The City gives its address as 99 Glen Creighton Drive on its Fall Fiesta page; its parks plan places it at Glen Creighton Drive and Glen Ayre Street.
