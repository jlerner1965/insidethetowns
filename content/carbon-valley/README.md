# Inside Carbon Valley (closed)

The Carbon Valley guide, carbonvalleyguide.com, covered Frederick, Firestone
and Dacono from 5 October 2026 and Fort Lupton from 12 October. The owner
closed it on 2 November 2026 (DECISIONS.md, "Black Hawk and Carbon Valley
close; Lafayette and Frederick open"). Frederick's listings moved to
`content/frederick/`, the Inside Frederick guide; the Firestone, Dacono and
Fort Lupton listings, articles, weekly notes and source registry were removed,
and git history keeps them (`git log -- content/carbon-valley/`) should any of
the three reopen. `images/` stays with its rows in IMAGE_LICENSES.csv, the hero
because the config still names it.

Every path on carbonvalleyguide.com, insidefirestone.com and
insidefortlupton.com redirects to insidethetowns.com (vercel.json). Nothing
here is built or audited.
