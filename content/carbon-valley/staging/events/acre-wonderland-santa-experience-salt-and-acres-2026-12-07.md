Salt & Acres' Santa visit in the Barn, with a cup of hot chocolate and the chance to take your own photos, on two December days. The farm's ticket page lists Sunday, December 7, 11:30 am to 2:30 pm, and Sunday, December 14, noon to 2 pm, but those dates fall on Mondays in 2026; the farm has not been asked which is right. Tickets are $10, or $25 for a family pass of up to four.
