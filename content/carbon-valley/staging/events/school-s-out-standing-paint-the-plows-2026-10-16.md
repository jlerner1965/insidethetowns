On a teacher-workshop day, children turn Firestone's snow plows into painted artwork before the first big snow, at Mountain Shadows Park. The Town provides the materials and asks only that painters dress for a mess; parents can paint alongside or cheer from the side. One of the Town's School's Out-Standing series.
