Firestone's part of the three-town Carbon Valley Holiday Festival: a holiday market of local makers and artists in Miners Park, hot cocoa, holiday music, free crafts for children and photos with Santa, from noon to 4 pm. Frederick's Festival of Lights in Crist Park and Dacono's festival at City Hall follow later the same day, so the valley's tradition is to dash from one to the next.
