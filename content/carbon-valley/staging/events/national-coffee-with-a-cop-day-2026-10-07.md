Drop in any time between 9 and 10:30 am to meet Firestone police officers over coffee at Chick-fil-A on Firestone Boulevard, ask questions and raise concerns. Coffee and light snacks are provided. National Coffee with a Cop Day has been held on the first Wednesday in October since 2016.
