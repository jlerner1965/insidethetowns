The last of the Town's 2026 tree-limb days: Firestone residents can bring limbs up to 12 inches across (no stumps, no other yard waste) to the maintenance facility on Weld County Road 20 between 7 and 11 am, where staff help unload, and take home wood chips from the chipping pile for gardens and tree rings. With emerald ash borer confirmed in Firestone in July, the Town takes extra care with the public mulch pile.
