Save money, share what you have, and find something new-to-you at our Kids Winter Clothing Swap! Bring gently used coats, hats, gloves, and other winter clothing your kiddos have outgrown, and browse the selection for something to keep them warm this winter.

Children’s clothing of all sizes is welcome! Please only donate items that are clean and in good condition so they can be enjoyed by another kiddo.

Winter coats and clothing may be dropped off at the library in advance or brought in before the swap begins at 4 PM.

After the swap, any unclaimed clothing that is not taken home will be donated to the Fort Lupton Food and Clothing Bank. Remaining coats will be available in the library lobby for swapping or donation throughout November.
