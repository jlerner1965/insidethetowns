A mud run of a few miles at Colorado National Speedway in Dacono, with about a dozen obstacles: mud pits no deeper than the waist, crawls and slides. It is not timed and there is no competition, so walking it is fine. The organizer says all ages are welcome, with rules for children.

The run starts at 8 am, with a pre-party from 7. Packet pickup is Friday, August 13, 4 to 8 pm; picking up on race day costs $5 a packet, cash. Bag check is $5 at the event, and the venue charges up to $20 for parking, cash only.
