Dacono BMX has run racing, practice and beginner programs for all ages since 2001, including regional and state qualifiers. Racing needs a USA BMX membership, with a free one-day trial for first-timers, and bikes and protective gear are available at the track. Schedules are posted on the track’s social media and USA BMX page; questions go to daconobmxtrack@gmail.com.
