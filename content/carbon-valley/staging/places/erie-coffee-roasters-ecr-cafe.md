Erie Coffee Roasters describes itself as a micro-roaster and cafe located in Frederick, roasting fair-trade coffees from around the world on site. The ECR Cafe is its storefront: espresso drinks, drip and pour-over coffee, food from other Front Range makers and sandwiches on NoCo Sourdough focaccia. Beans are sold online and wholesale.
