Frederick, Firestone and Dacono are three towns in a row east of I-25, north of Erie, with their old centres within about four miles of one another. This is a driving day rather than a walking one, with one exception: Fifth Street in Frederick holds the cafe, the park, the museum and the barbecue within two blocks, so park there once and walk those. Everything else is a short drive, and each stop below says which town it is in.

## 01 · Gabe's Cafe (Frederick)

Start on Fifth Street. [Gabe's](/places/gabes-cafe/) at 214 5th Street is a family-owned cafe, open since 2015, serving breakfast and lunch all day. It opens at 6 am Monday and Wednesday to Friday and at 7 am at the weekend, closing at 8 pm most days but at 2 pm on Tuesdays and Sundays. It takes pick-up orders and now serves alcohol.

*Getting there:* street parking on Fifth Street; leave the car here for stops 03 and 04 too. *Best for:* an early start any day; lunch on a Monday, when stop 04 is closed.

## 02 · Milavec Lake (Frederick)

Drive to the [Frederick Recreation Area](/places/frederick-recreation-area/) at 8201 Colorado Boulevard, the Town's 129 acres around Milavec Lake. A 1.26-mile crusher-fine loop circles the water, with 1.7 miles of paved trail connecting to it and to the neighbourhood trails beyond; all of it is open to walking and cycling. Fishing piers line the shore (a Colorado licence for anyone over 16; no ice fishing, because the aerators keep the ice unstable), and non-motorized boats can launch without a permit once the water is clear of ice. In winter the Town's page lists geese, ducks, herons, and bald and golden eagles, with the loop and the piers as the places to watch from. The Town lists restrooms, parking, picnic shelters and a playground behind the restroom by the main lot, and a 30,000-square-foot skate park at the south-west corner, open dawn to 10 pm.

*Getting there:* a few minutes' drive from Fifth Street; lot parking. *Best for:* the flat loop before lunch; birds in winter; the dog park.

## 03 · Crist Park and the Miners Memorial Museum (Frederick)

Back on Fifth Street, [Crist Park](/places/crist-park/) at 105 5th Street is the park at the entrance to downtown, with a playground, a gazebo, bocce courts, a sports field and a splash pad that runs from early June to late August (step on the yellow dome to start it). In the park stands the [Miners Memorial Museum](/places/miners-memorial-museum/), the original Frederick Town Hall moved here and made a Colorado Historic Landmark in 2012, where members of the Town's Historic Preservation Committee show the coal-camp exhibits. It opens the first Friday of each month from 5 pm to 7 pm, and otherwise by request through the Town Clerk's office (720-382-5500); the remaining 2026 openings are 6 November and 4 December. The Town holds Tiny Terror Town here in October and the Festival of Lights in December.

*Getting there:* on foot from the cafe. *Best for:* a first-Friday evening; the park on any day.

## 04 · Georgia Boys BBQ Smokehouse (Frederick)

Lunch is a block along at 141 Fifth Street. [Georgia Boys](/places/georgia-boys-bbq-smokehouse/) opened the Smokehouse in 2014 with a patio and a full bar that leans on local breweries. It is closed on Mondays; otherwise 11 am–8 pm Tuesday to Thursday and Sunday, 11 am–9 pm Friday and Saturday, with happy hour Tuesday to Friday from 2:30 pm to 5 pm. Reservations are by phone, for parties of eight or more only.

*Getting there:* on foot from Crist Park. *Best for:* lunch or an early dinner, any day but Monday.

## 05 · St. Vrain State Park (Firestone)

The Firestone afternoon. [St. Vrain State Park](/places/st-vrain-state-park/), at 3785 Weld County Road 24 1/2 on the north-west edge of Firestone just west of I-25, is Colorado Parks and Wildlife's pond-dotted park: 689 acres of land and 228 of water, 87 campsites, seven miles of trail, a boat ramp and nature trails around ten of the ponds, with views of Longs Peak. Day use runs 5 am–10 pm; the entrance station is staffed 9 am–5 pm; a daily vehicle pass is $10 to $15 and an individual day pass $4. Dogs on a leash. The Town of Firestone says it is the only state park annexed into a Colorado town.

*Getting there:* from I-25 exit 240, west on Colorado 119, then north on County Road 7, which becomes County Road 24 1/2. *Best for:* a longer walk, fishing, birds and an overnight.

## 06 · Settlers Park and the Firestone Trail (Firestone)

The alternative Firestone afternoon is the [Firestone Trail](/places/firestone-trail/), twelve miles on the bed of the railway that carried the town's coal, running from [Settlers Park](/places/settlers-park/) at 11447 Deerfield Drive to Coronado Park and linking the Town's parks with the St. Vrain Legacy Trail. The Town lists benches, picnic tables and bins along the route, and at Settlers Park pickleball and tennis courts, a basketball court, a playground, shelters that are first come, first served, restrooms and parking. Dacono's piece of the same Legacy Trail is a ten-foot concrete path on the old Union Pacific right-of-way, with its trailhead at Colorado 52 and Colorado Boulevard ([Legacy Trail](/places/legacy-trail/)); [Wing Shack](/places/wing-shack-dacono/) on 8th Street in old Dacono is open daily 10:30 am–10 pm if the day ends there.

*Getting there:* lot parking at Settlers Park. *Best for:* a walk or ride of any length, out and back.

## A simple first visit

1. Park on Fifth Street in Frederick and have breakfast at Gabe's.
2. Drive to the Frederick Recreation Area and walk the Milavec Lake loop.
3. Return to Fifth Street for Crist Park and lunch at Georgia Boys; on a Monday, eat at Gabe's instead.
4. Spend the afternoon in Firestone at St. Vrain State Park, with a vehicle pass, or on the Firestone Trail from Settlers Park.
5. On a first Friday, finish at the museum from 5 pm.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. The Town of Frederick puts itself 35 minutes from downtown Denver and 33 from Denver International Airport. From Longmont, Colorado 119 reaches I-25 at exit 240; from Boulder, Colorado 52. No public transit stop is confirmed on an official page read for this guide, so plan to drive.

All three towns are in Weld County. Each Town's own rules apply in its own parks; the state park is CPW's. Posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Town of Frederick lists restrooms and parking at the Frederick Recreation Area, with a crusher-fine loop around the lake and paved trails connecting to it. The Town of Firestone lists restrooms and parking at Settlers Park. Dacono's Legacy Trail is a ten-foot concrete path. CPW's page for St. Vrain State Park gives day-use hours and fees but does not describe accessible facilities, so none are claimed here.

Step-free entrances to the cafe and the smokehouse, accessible restrooms at Crist Park and the museum, and designated accessible parking on Fifth Street have not yet been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here.
