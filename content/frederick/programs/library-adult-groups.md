**Modern Romantics** discusses the latest romance novels on first Thursdays at 6 in Meeting Room 1, and **Page Turner's** takes on books across genres on second Thursdays at 11:30; both months' picks can be checked out at the front desk. The **Carbon Valley Quilters** welcome every skill level on second Wednesdays from 1 to 4 (bring your own machine and fabric; the pattern is provided); the November 11 session is cancelled and the next is December 9. A genealogist answers family-history questions one-on-one at the **Genealogy Drop-in**, third Wednesdays 1 to 3 (November 18, December 16). On Sundays there is **Drop-in Chess** from 1:30 and a **Silent Reading Hour** in the living room at 3. The **English Class** for anyone 16 or older meets Tuesdays from 9:30 to 11 in Meeting Room 2, sixteen weeks from November 3 to February 16, with registration done in class and no childcare.
