The fire district invites 3- to 5-year-olds and their grown-ups into a firehouse six times a year for a story and a look at the trucks, 10 to 11 am. Space is small, so only district residents may register, through the form on the page, which carries the visitor waiver, a photography notice and the story-time guidelines. Every 2026 date was full when this was read on November 2, including the last, November 5 at Station 4 on County Road 7; watch the page for 2027's dates. The stations used in 2026: Station 1 at 31 Walnut Drive and Station 5 at 3725 Glacier Way in Frederick, Station 3 at 6800 Tilbury Avenue in Firestone, and Station 4.
