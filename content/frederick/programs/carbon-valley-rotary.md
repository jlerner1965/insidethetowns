The club's site invites anyone interested in making a difference locally and globally to come to a meeting: Tuesdays from 7 to 8 am at the rec center on Fifth Street, with the last Tuesday of each month a breakfast at a local restaurant named on the club calendar. Its 2025–26 local projects are youth literacy, dictionaries for third graders, Little Free Libraries, the community Easter egg hunt at Hart Park (900-plus books and 12,500 candy eggs last April), a fire safety house, Adopt-A-Highway on Colorado Boulevard four times a year, support for Frederick High's I Have a Dream program and for suicide-prevention services, and the Amarok Society overseas. It raises money with a peach sale in late summer.
