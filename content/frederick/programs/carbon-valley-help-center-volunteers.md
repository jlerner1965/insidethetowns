The Carbon Valley Help Center at 150 Buchanan Avenue in Firestone provides food, resources and support to families in Frederick, Firestone and Dacono facing financial hardship. Its volunteer roles are the food pantry (sorting donations, stocking shelves, keeping the pantry organised), reception (welcoming visitors and connecting them with help) and events. Schedules are flexible. Apply through the form on the volunteer page; the center contacts you, matches you to a role and runs an orientation. Donors can give online or "adopt a shelf" to keep the pantry stocked, and the center's [Empty Bowls](/events/empty-bowls-fundraiser-2026-11-14/) lunch at Frederick High School on November 14 is its big fundraiser of the year. The Town of Frederick's [Thanksgiving Dinner in a Box](/updates/holiday-help-thanksgiving-dinner-in-a-box-and-santa-cops/) is run with the center.
