The district's group lessons follow a colour-coded ladder: StarBabies and StarTots for 6 to 35 months with a parent in the water; White (afraid of the water) to Red, Yellow, Blue and Green for ages 3 to 13, each placed by what the child can already do; and Stroke School for 6 to 16-year-olds who have finished Green and can tread water for 30 seconds. Session 3 of the fall runs Tuesdays and Thursdays from November 17 to December 17 or Saturday mornings from November 21 to December 19, skipping Thanksgiving week. Registration opens at 7 am on Monday, November 9 for district residents and Wednesday, November 11 for others, and closes at 5 pm on Thursday, November 12. Refunds are account credit, requested through CivicRec up to three days before the first lesson; there are no make-ups. Arrive five minutes early to sign in.
