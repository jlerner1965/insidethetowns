You do not need to be an expert, the district says: coaches get drills to run at practice, set their own practice days and times, coach the games and recruit an assistant if they want one. Requirements are an online concussion-in-sports course and a background check. The district's goals for its coaches are fundamental skills, teamwork, sportsmanship and fair play, and relationships among families across the three towns; the benefit is that a head coach's child plays that sport for free. Sign up through the youth sports catalog.
