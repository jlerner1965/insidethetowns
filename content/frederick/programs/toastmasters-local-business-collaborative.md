Toastmasters International's club finder lists one club in Frederick, The Local Business Collaborative (its own site still goes by the Ranch Raconteurs name): Thursday lunchtimes from 11:30 to 12:30 at 501 Walnut Street, with a Zoom option, in Area N05 of the Toastmasters district. The listing says guests are welcome, visits are free and there are no membership restrictions. Toastmasters' listing gives the ZIP as 80513; Frederick's is 80530.
