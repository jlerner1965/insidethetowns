**Toddler Social Time** is a guided class of play, music and storytelling to build social skills, held in the rec center's Kid Zone on Mondays: 1:30 to 2 for 1 to 2-year-olds, each with a guardian in the room, and 2:15 to 2:54 for 3 to 5-year-olds. The winter session runs November 2 to December 14 with no class on November 23; parents stay in the building. Registration closed October 29 and the district has not yet posted a later session.

**[Kid Zone](https://www.cvprd.com/kid-zone)** is the rec center's drop-off childcare for 6 months to 9 years while a parent is in the building: Monday to Saturday 8 am to 1 pm and Monday to Thursday 4 to 8 pm, two hours a day at most, $4 an hour for district residents and $6 for others, paid before drop-off; annual pass holders get the first hour free. Check-in is through the KidCheck app, no snacks or toys from home, and staff fetch you for a soiled diaper or fifteen minutes of crying.
