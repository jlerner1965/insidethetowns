The district's adult leagues are recreational and for 18 and over: [basketball](https://www.cvprd.com/adult-basketball), [volleyball](https://www.cvprd.com/adult-volleyball) and a [5x5x5 coed softball league](https://www.cvprd.com/5x5x5-coed-softball-league), each running seven weeks with the top teams playing a championship in the final week. Teams register online and pay half the fee up front, the rest by week two; captains get the schedule by email a week before the first game, and schedules are posted at cvprdathletics.com. [Drop-in sports](https://www.cvprd.com/drop-in-sports) run at the rec center, and the district rents its athletic fields. The 2026 season dates are on each league's page; call the sports office for what is next.
