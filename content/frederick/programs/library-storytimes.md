The library's storytimes are in the Storytime Room at 7 Park Avenue, Firestone, each half an hour. **Twinkle Babies**, for babies from birth to about 18 months, is Wednesdays at 10:30 and Thursdays at 9:30; it teaches songs, stories and what the library calls the fundamental tools for a baby's healthy development, and registering signs you up for the whole series (the Wednesday series runs October 21 to December 9, skipping November 11). **Tales for Tots**, for 18 months to 3 with a caregiver, uses stories, songs and fingerplays, Mondays at 9:30 and Thursdays at 10:30. **Music and Movement**, for 3 to 5, gets children and caregivers up and moving with instruments, Mondays at 10:30 and Tuesdays at 9:30. **Family Storytime**, books, songs and actions for 2 to 5-year-olds with longer attention spans (all welcome), is Tuesdays at 10:30 and Wednesdays at 9:30. Each series is listed through the second week of December; the library's calendar will show the winter dates. There is also a monthly evening [yoga storytime](/events/wiggle-worms-yoga-storytime-2026-11-10/) for 3 to 6-year-olds, which needs registration.
