The Carbon Valley Parks and Recreation District runs the youth leagues for Frederick, Firestone and Dacono: basketball from preschool to 8th grade, soccer from preschool to 6th, softball for 6 to 14, volleyball for 1st to 8th grade, baseball for 4 to 12, SuperTots (preschool-based play for 2 to 4-year-olds), non-competitive Skyhawks camps for 4 to 14 and cheer and dance through The Cheer and Dance Connection. Team requests (a coach, a friend, a practice day, a school) are first come, first served and not guaranteed; coaches set practice days; jerseys are included and players bring their own equipment; leagues for 8 and under are self-officiated and older divisions may travel. Schedules go up at cvprdathletics.com and the weather line is 720-531-3373. The season on now is [basketball](/programs/cvprd-youth-basketball/), with the winter leagues registering through December 7.
