The Frederick Market is not a weekly farmers' market but the vendor row at the Town's festivals: Frederick in Flight in late June, Chainsaws & Chuckwagons in July and Miners Day in September, all at Centennial Park, and the [Festival of Lights](/events/festival-of-lights-2026-12-05/) at Crist Park in December, where it runs 5:30 to 8 pm alongside the tree lighting. The Town supports local growers, farmers, artisans, crafters and non-profits; everything sold must be original and handcrafted by the vendor, and AI-generated art is not allowed. Times can change and approved vendors are told. Businesses outside the three categories can be event sponsors instead, through the [sponsor opportunities](https://www.frederickco.gov/1138/2026-Sponsor-Opportunities) page.
