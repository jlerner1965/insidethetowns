The Town runs its festivals on volunteers. Pick a time slot for an event or project on the [Frederick Volunteer Portal](https://frederickco.galaxydigital.com/) (the portal refused this guide's automated reader, so open it in a browser), join the [monthly volunteer newsletter](https://frederickco.us9.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=b15e60dc70599d821500d11b3&id=ac4740a117), or email the Events Team to get on the event volunteer list. Beer-garden shifts need TIPS training and an age of 21. One-time service projects, for Eagle Scout candidates, National Honor Society hours or a company's service day, go through the Communications and Engagement team. The Town also lists a Carbon Valley Volunteer Fair, held in February at the district's Community Center in Firestone with nonprofits from the Help Center and Habitat for Humanity to Meals on Wheels and the Weld Food Bank; the 2027 date was not posted on November 2. Other ways in through the Town: its [boards and commissions](/programs/town-boards-and-commissions/), the [Youth Commission](/programs/frederick-youth-commission/), and the police department's [Neighborhood Watch](/programs/neighborhood-watch-and-police-programs/).
