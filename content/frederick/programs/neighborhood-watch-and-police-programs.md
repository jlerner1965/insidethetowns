**Neighborhood Watch** organises residents by block, with captains who meet the police quarterly; 2026's meetings were at the Prairie Greens, Moore Farms and Bella Rosa clubhouses, and the 2027 dates had not been posted when this was read. Call 720-382-5700 to join. **Dog Walker Watch** asks anyone who walks a dog in Frederick to attend one training (Wednesdays at 10 am in June, July and August 2026) and keep an eye out; dogs must be vaccinated and licensed. **National Night Out** on the first Tuesday of August is neighbourhood gatherings from 5 to 7 pm, hosted by residents.

The department also offers a **Ride-Along** for citizens 18 and over by [request form](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Police-7/RideAlong-Request-Form-349); the **Blue Envelope**, kept in the glove box to tell an officer about a driver's needs during a stop; the **Blue Heart Initiative**, a voluntary sticker and optional profile for residents with visible or non-visible disabilities; **Vacation House Watch**, by form, for checks on an empty home; a green **prescription-drug disposal** box in the lobby (no needles or liquids); and the dial-711 relay for people with hearing or speech impairments. The lobby is open weekdays 8 to 5. In November the station is also where the [Santa Cops](/updates/holiday-help-thanksgiving-dinner-in-a-box-and-santa-cops/) Angel Tree hangs.
