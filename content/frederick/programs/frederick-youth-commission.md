The Youth Commission advises the Board of Trustees on matters affecting young people, developing and recommending policies, programs and services that bring youth into local government, and appoints a non-voting liaison to each of the Planning Commission, the Parks, Recreation, Open Space & Trails Commission, the Arts Committee, the Miners Day Committee and the Historic Preservation Committee. Members are Carbon Valley students in grades 8 to 12 with, the Town says, a vested interest in Frederick; they may not have a financial interest in anything that comes before the body. Ten seats were vacant when this was read. Apply online with the release form; the Board considers applications twice a year, generally in May and October, after a panel interview. The Town's meetings calendar lists the commission at 9 am on the second Saturday of each month.
