The Board of Trustees created the scholarship in 2008. It pays $2,000 a year, directly to the school each August, for the first four years of eligibility within six years of the award, as long as the student stays enrolled, pursues a degree and keeps a 3.0. Applicants must be Frederick residents graduating from a St. Vrain Valley high school, an accredited Colorado high school or home-school program, or a high school in the Frederick feeder, with a college-preparatory record and excellence in something beyond the classroom: drama, music, art, student government, athletics, science, leadership or service. The Town's page says nominations for students outside the St. Vrain district open in December 2026; the 2026 deadline for them was March 4, and the Scholarship Commission's interviews follow. The Scholarship Committee is one of the Town's [resident committees](/programs/town-boards-and-commissions/).
