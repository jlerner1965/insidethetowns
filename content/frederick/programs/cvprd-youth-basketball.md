The district's winter leagues for 5th/6th grade coed and 7th/8th grade boys are Junior Nuggets partner leagues: one 90-minute practice a week from the week of January 5 and games on Friday evenings or Saturdays from January 15 to February 27 against Carbon Valley, Mead, Berthoud and LaSalle teams, so some travel. Registration closes Monday, December 7; the fee is $127 for district residents and $191 for others, jersey included. Leagues for ages 8 and under are refereed by coaches and parents; 9 and up use trained officials. Schedules are posted at [cvprdathletics.com](https://www.cvprdathletics.com).

The fall season is already running: Mini-Dribblers (coed, age 4), kindergarten to 2nd grade, 3rd/4th grade boys and girls and, new this year, 7th/8th grade girls play Saturdays from November 7 to December 19, with nothing the week of Thanksgiving. Registration for it closed in September. Not every address in the three towns is in the district; the district checks residency through the Weld County property portal (look for "Carbon Valley Rec" under tax authorities).
