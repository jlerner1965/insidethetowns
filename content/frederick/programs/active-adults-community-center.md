The recreation district's programs for adults 55 and over run from the [Community Center](/places/carbon-valley-community-center/) at 151 Grant Avenue, Firestone, open weekdays 8 to 4; anyone can drop in to look around. The weekly drop-ins are Morning Joe coffee, crafts and conversation, mahjong, pinochle, hand-and-foot cards, Diamond Dots (bring your own) and a veterans' coffee hour. The dated programs on the district's page when this was read ran through October (bingo, a Medicare talk, bead and garland classes with the library, blood-pressure checks with the fire district, a virtual-reality Eyes of Freedom exhibit) plus the Book Club's new time, Tuesday, November 3 at 9:30, reading *The House of Broken Angels*; November's and December's list, with the December holiday event, goes out in the Active Adults newsletter. The Town's senior resources page adds the Weld County Friendly Fork lunch, served at the center at noon on Tuesdays and Thursdays with to-go meals, reserved by 11 am the previous business day (303-833-3660 or seniors@cvprd.com), and SilverSneakers classes at the rec center. Rides to all of it, for residents 60 and over and others who qualify, come through [Via Mobility](https://viacolorado.org/services/get-started/), weekdays 8 to 3, 303-444-3043.
