The district's mini camps cover the first three weekdays of St. Vrain Valley Schools' two long breaks: **Camp Gobble Gobble** on Monday to Wednesday, November 23 to 25 (its registration closed November 11) and **Camp Frost** on December 28 to 30. Both run 8:30 to 4:30 at the rec center for ages 5 to 12 and cost $180 for district residents and $270 for everyone else. Camp Frost registration opens Monday, November 2 for district residents and November 9 for non-residents and closes December 9. The district's parent manual is linked from the camps page. The rec center's pool keeps school-break hours on the same days: open swim from 11 am, slide from 1 pm.
