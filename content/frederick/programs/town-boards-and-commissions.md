**Serve on one.** The Town fills its boards twice a year, generally in May and October: apply online (residents only, and no financial interest in anything that comes before the body), interview with a panel of Board representatives, and the Board of Trustees appoints at a meeting. The fall 2026 window closed September 4 with interviews September 8 to 17 and selections October 7; applications after that wait for 2027. Open seats on November 2: two on the Parks, Recreation, Open Space & Trails Commission and one on the Historic Preservation Committee. Terms on the Arts Committee and PROST are three years with no limit.

**Sit in.** The [Planning Commission](https://www.frederickco.gov/289/Planning-Commission) (five members, reviewing annexations, zoning, plats and the land use code) meets at 6:30 pm on the first and third Thursdays as needed at Town Hall. The [Arts Committee](https://www.frederickco.gov/284/Frederick-Arts-Committee), which runs the Art in Public Places program, meets second Tuesdays at 4:30 at Town Hall, and the [Historic Preservation Committee](https://www.frederickco.gov/286/Historic-Preservation-Committee), which keeps the Miners Memorial Museum, at 2:30 the same day. [PROST](https://www.frederickco.gov/288/Parks-Recreation-Open-Space-Trails-Commi) meets second Wednesdays at 6:30 at the Bella Rosa Golf Course clubhouse, in person and online. Agendas go up the week before on the Town's [Agenda Portal](https://www.frederickco.gov/1100/Agenda-Portal). The Board of Trustees itself meets first and third Wednesdays at 7 pm, work sessions at 5:30, and can be emailed at BOTInfo@frederickco.gov. Teens have their own body, the [Youth Commission](/programs/frederick-youth-commission/).
