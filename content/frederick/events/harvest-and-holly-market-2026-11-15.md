The recreation district's indoor holiday market fills the Carbon Valley Recreation Center with more than forty local boutiques, artisans and crafters, Sunday, November 15 from 11 am to 3 pm. The district's page lists a kids' corner (a fall photo booth, harvest placemats, pumpkin crafts and princess meet-and-greets by Magical Moments), food trucks outside, live music from Wichita Run and dance performances by the Legacy Dancers. Every 2026 vendor space has sold; applications for 2027 open next August. No admission price is listed.
