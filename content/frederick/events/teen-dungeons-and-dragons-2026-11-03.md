A tabletop Dungeons & Dragons session for middle and high school students in the library's Storytime Room, the first Tuesday of the month from 3:45 to 6:45 pm. Registration is required and the session was full with a waiting list open when this was read on November 2; the form asks whether you have played before. The next session is December 1. The library's page also carries a note calling it an online event, which contradicts the room it names, so check with the library at 7 Park Avenue before going.
