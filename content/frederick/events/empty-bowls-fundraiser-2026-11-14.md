The Help Center's twelfth Empty Bowls lunch, Saturday, November 14 from 11 am to 2 pm at Frederick High School: soups, salads, breadsticks and desserts, a kids' menu, free carnival games, a raffle and a silent auction, and every ticket includes a hand-painted bowl to keep. Proceeds go to the center's food pantry and assistance for Carbon Valley families. Advance tickets are sold on the event page and in person at the center, 150 Buchanan Avenue, Firestone, Tuesdays and Thursdays 9 to 3; 303-833-6626.
