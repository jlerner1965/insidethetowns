Firefighters read to 3- to 5-year-olds and show them the station, Thursday, November 5 from 10 to 11 am at Station 4 on County Road 7. It is free and open only to residents of the Frederick-Firestone Fire District, because space is small. This is the last of the district's six 2026 sessions, and on November 2 the district's page marked every one of them, this one included, as full; the 2027 dates were not yet posted.
