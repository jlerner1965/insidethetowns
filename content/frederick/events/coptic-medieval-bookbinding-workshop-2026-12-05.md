A hands-on afternoon at the library, Saturday, December 5, noon to 4 pm, on the history of bookbinding and the Coptic tradition in particular: folded sheets sewn together in a chain stitch, which leaves the spine open and lets the pages lie flat. You bind a Coptic-style book of your own to take home. No experience or supplies needed.
