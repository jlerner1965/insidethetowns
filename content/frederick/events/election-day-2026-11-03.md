Weld County runs a Voter Service and Polling Center at the Carbon Valley Recreation Center, 701 5th Street, on Monday, November 2 from 8 am to 5 pm and on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, from 7 am to 7 pm: register or update a registration, get a replacement ballot, vote in person or drop off a mail ballot. The county's 24-hour ballot box outside the rec center takes ballots until 7 pm on November 3, with no postage needed. The nearest others are at Dacono Council Chambers, 512 Cherry Avenue (the same two days), and the Southwest Weld County Services Complex, 4209 Weld County Road 24 1/2 near Longmont, which has been open weekdays since October 19 and has its own 24-hour box.

Ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm, the Town's [Election Day notice](https://www.frederickco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=775) says. Frederick voters have two Town questions on the ballot: [2D](https://www.frederickco.gov/393/Elections), a one percent sales and use tax from January 1, 2027 for streets and transportation, which the Town estimates would raise about $3.8 million in its first full year and cost the average household about $10 a month, and 2E, which, if 2D passes, allows up to $26 million of debt with a repayment cost of up to $57 million. Weld County Elections: 970-304-6525, elections@weld.gov.
