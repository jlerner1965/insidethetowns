The Carbon Valley Parks and Recreation District runs its programs for adults 55 and over from the Community Center at 151 Grant Avenue, Firestone, open weekdays 8 am to 4 pm; anyone can drop in during business hours to look around. The district lists social, fitness, educational and wellness programs, day trips, seminars and special events, and the Town of Frederick's senior resources page adds the Friendly Fork lunch served at noon on Tuesdays and Thursdays (reserve by 11 am the prior business day at 303-833-3660; suggested donation $4 for 60 and over). The center is closed on Memorial Day afternoon, Labor Day afternoon, the day after Thanksgiving and the district's other holidays. Contact: 303-833-3660 x123, seniors@cvprd.com.
