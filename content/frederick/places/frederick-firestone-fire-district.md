The Frederick-Firestone Fire District's administrative office is at 8426 Kosmerl Place in Frederick, 303-833-2742 for anything that is not an emergency. Its community programs include a blended CPR/AED and first-aid course, a child and babysitting safety class for teens, car-seat checks, fire-extinguisher training, Story Time at the Firehouse for 3- to 5-year-olds (district residents only; six sessions a year at the stations, which include Station 1 at 31 Walnut Drive, Station 4 at 10706 Weld County Road 7 and Station 5 at 3725 Glacier Way in Frederick) and the Santa Run, three evenings of Santa touring the neighbourhoods on a fire engine in December, with the 2026 routes due on the district's site in late November. The district's board meets at the office; its calendar lists the classes with dates.
