The Town's voter information page names the Weld County Elections Office's three locations, and this is the one nearest Frederick: 4209 County Road 24 1/2, Longmont, 720-652-4202 (the others are in Greeley and Fort Lupton). For the November 3, 2026 election the county's [voting locations list](https://www.weld.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/departments/clerk-and-recorder/documents/elections/2026-general-voting-and-drop-box-locations.pdf) has it open as a Voter Service and Polling Center on weekdays from October 19 to November 2 (8 am to 5 pm), Saturday October 31 and Election Day 7 am to 7 pm, with a 24-hour ballot box outside. Weld County is based in Greeley, thirty miles off, so this complex is where many of Frederick's county errands go; check the county's site for which departments keep hours here.
