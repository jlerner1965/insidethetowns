The Town's information page puts the tax at about $1 on a $100 taxable purchase and estimates about $4 million a year, aimed at four priorities: Highway 52, congestion and safety, road quality, and sidewalks, crossings and trails. Bonding, the page says, would let larger projects start sooner; its ranked list of 35 potential projects, in 2026 dollars, is headed by a $19 million mill-and-overlay of Bella Rosa Parkway with shoulder improvements, the Colorado Boulevard and Bella Rosa Parkway intersection ($1.6 million) and work on Silver Birch Boulevard, and totals about $88.6 million. The Town says the revenue would be spent notwithstanding the state revenue limit. Voters moved regular Town elections to November of even-numbered years; the next is November 7, 2028. Where and how to vote is on the [Election Day listing](/events/election-day-2026-11-03/).
