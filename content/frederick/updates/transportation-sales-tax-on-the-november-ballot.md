Weld County's unofficial count, in its summary report of 12:51 pm on November 5, had Ballot Issue 2D at 3,485 votes for and 4,516 against, and Ballot Issue 2E at 2,740 for and 5,162 against. The Town's Elections page says the preliminary results indicate voters have not approved either measure. 2D asked to raise the Town's sales and use tax by 1.0 percent from January 1, 2027, estimated at about $3.8 million in the first full year, for streets, intersections, paving, sidewalks and regional projects such as State Highway 52 and its frontage roads; the Town's information page put it at about $1 on a $100 taxable purchase and about $10 a month for the average household. 2E, which depended on 2D passing, would have let the Town borrow up to $26 million, with a maximum repayment cost of up to $57 million, in general obligation bonds for street and transportation projects. The count stays unofficial until the county certifies it.
