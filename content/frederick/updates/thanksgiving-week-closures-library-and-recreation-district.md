High Plains Library District lists Thanksgiving Eve, November 25, as a 5 pm closing and Thanksgiving, November 26, as a full closure. Its list of branches includes the Carbon Valley Regional Library. The Carbon Valley Parks and Recreation District lists the Recreation Center, Community Center and Gymnastics Center as closed on Thanksgiving. On the day after Thanksgiving, the Recreation Center is open 5:30 am to 9 pm, while the Community Center and the Gymnastics Center are closed. The district also has a Fall Break Gymnastics Closure from November 24 to 27, with no classes and no open gyms.
