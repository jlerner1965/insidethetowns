The November 4 agenda listed the first hearing as "No Action – Action will occur after the second public hearing on 12/02/2026." The finance director's presentation, built from work sessions on September 16, October 7 and October 21, puts the General Fund at $23.85 million of spending against $21.54 million of revenue, with police the largest line at $9.49 million; the Town's sales and use tax stays at 3.5 percent and its property tax at 6.555 mills. Across all funds, $22.54 million is capital outlay, most of it in the Capital Facilities Fund, $22.41 million is personnel and benefits and $19.68 million is services and operations. Staff recommend 5.5 new positions: two school resource officers ahead of the new high school, a part-time victims advocate, two parks, open space and trails technicians and a year-round golf employee. The packet also proposes 2027 fee-schedule changes, which the Board would approve separately. Staff present the final proposed budget at the December 2 hearing.
