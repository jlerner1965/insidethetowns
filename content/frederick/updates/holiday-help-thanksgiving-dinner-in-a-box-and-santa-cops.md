**Thanksgiving Dinner in a Box.** Each household in need submits one [online form](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Community-Relations-9/Thanksgiving-Dinner-in-a-Box-2026-Englis-451) (a [Spanish form](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Community-Relations-9/El-programa-de-Cena-de-Accin-de-Gracias--450) too) by Friday, November 13, and anyone can nominate a family. Food goes to the Town Hall lobby, 401 Locust Street, weekdays 8 to 5 by Wednesday, November 18; on November 2 the Town's tally still needed, out of 100 each, 84 cans of yams, 85 boxes of instant potatoes, 92 cans of cranberry sauce, 69 gravy mixes, 91 tins of fried onions, 92 bags of brown sugar, 52 cans of corn and 91 salt-and-pepper sets, plus $665 toward the $1,000 for pies, butter and rolls (cash, check or [online](https://checkout.square.site/merchant/Q6B65XEE4SVW6/checkout/566KQPSZB675CXNPUYTDP5GG)). The page does not give the pickup date.

**Santa Cops and the Angel Tree.** The police department's program gives gifts to children from birth through 16 in families in financial hardship, referred by schools, churches, friends or relatives through an [English](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Police-7/Santa-Cops-Referral-Form-English-456) or [Spanish](https://www.frederickco.gov/FormCenter/Police-7/Santa-Cops-Referral-Form-Spanish-291) form by 5 pm on Monday, November 30 (Kristy O'Hayre, 720-382-5711). To give, take a tag from the Angel Tree in the station lobby at 333 Fifth Street and return the gift with it, or leave a new, unwrapped toy in the collection box, by the same deadline; host a drive; [donate online](https://checkout.square.site/merchant/Q6B65XEE4SVW6/checkout/EWV6KVPITYDY4AMCC2GCYEHQ); or mail a check to Frederick Police Department – Santa Cops, PO Box 639, Frederick, CO 80530. Officers and volunteers wrap in early December and deliver to the families.
