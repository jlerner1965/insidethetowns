Stage 1 is the mildest of the stages in the Town's [2026 Water Shortage Contingency Plan](https://www.frederickco.gov/DocumentCenter/View/33040/2026-Water-Shortage-Contingency-Plan) and rests on education and voluntary compliance: the Town's recommendations are no irrigation between 10 am and 6 pm and no more than three watering days a week, toward a 10 percent reduction in demand. The page lists no penalties. The Town's conservation programs, a lawn-replacement rebate, irrigation-controller, toilet and clothes-washer rebates and Garden In A Box among them, are linked from the same page. The declaration still stood on the Town's home page on November 2.
