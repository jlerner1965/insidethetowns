The Town of Frederick has a snowplow that needs a name and is asking children to supply it. The 2026 Name-A-Snowplow Contest is open to children ages 5 to 11 who live in Frederick, and each child may submit one name. Names must be appropriate and family-friendly, and a child's full name and other personal information should be left out of the proposed name. The winning name will be displayed on the snowplow. The Town will honor the winner at a ceremony, where the winner gets to climb into the cab and pose for photos. Entries are due by 11:59 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, through the form on the Town's contest page.
