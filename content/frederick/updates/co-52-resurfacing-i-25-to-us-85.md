The Town's roadwork page carries CDOT's schedule: daytime work (6 am to 6 pm) through August, then overnight work from 7 pm to 6 am, Sunday night through Friday morning, once construction moved east of Ridgeway Boulevard, with lane shifts and single-lane alternating traffic. The southbound US 85 off-ramp to CO 52 may close nightly Sunday to Thursday, with traffic sent to CO 7. The project adds bridge repairs, drainage-ditch repairs and guardrail upgrades to the new pavement in travel lanes, turn lanes and shoulders. Project line 720-743-0775, co52improve@gmail.com. Separately, the Town's own Ridgeway Boulevard widening between Highway 52 and Penrose Boulevard, with its northbound detour via Colorado Boulevard, Tipple Parkway and Wheatland Boulevard, was scheduled to run about four weeks from August 31; the page does not say it is done. Live conditions on the state highways are at [COtrip](https://cotrip.org/).
