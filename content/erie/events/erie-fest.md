Being Better Neighbors' festival of the cultures of the people who live in Erie, at Coal Creek Park: traditions, food, music and art from around the world, with family activities, food trucks, dance and music on stage. Presented in 2026 with the Town of Erie and the Bob Braudes and Nancy Clairmont Family Fund.
