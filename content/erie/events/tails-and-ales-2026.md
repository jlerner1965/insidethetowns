The dog party at the Boneyard: a Chamber beer garden, food from Downtown Fingers and Maximinas, music by Titonic, and a free swag bag for the first 100 families. Free to get in; food and drink by card only. Dogs off leash inside the fenced park, on leash outside it. Parking is on the natural-surface lot east of the park.
