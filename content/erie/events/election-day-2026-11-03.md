The Erie Community Center is a vote center for both counties, run with Weld County: open through Monday, November 2 (closing at 5 pm) and on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 7 am to 7 pm. Vote in person, drop off a mail ballot, register, or get a replacement ballot there, whichever county you live in. The 24-hour drop boxes: Weld County’s at the community center, 450 Powers St, and Boulder County’s at Meadowlark School, 2300 Meadow Sweet Lane, in the staff and bus lot off Front Range Road at Laramie Lane. Ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm; anyone in line by 7 pm can vote.
