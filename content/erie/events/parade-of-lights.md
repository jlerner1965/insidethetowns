Erie's Parade of Lights is on Friday, December 4, 2026: activities downtown run from 4:30 to 8:30 pm, and the lighted parade starts at 6:30 pm, heading north on Briggs Street from Maxwell Avenue to Cheesman Street. The Erie Chamber of Commerce runs it, and it is free.

## Schedule

- 4 to 6 pm: school choirs sing inside Orchard Community Church, 470 Briggs Street, which has free hot chocolate, snacks and restrooms.
- 4:30 to 6 pm: Santa and Mrs. Claus on Briggs Street.
- 4:30 to 6:30 pm: free carriage rides.
- 6:30 pm: the parade begins. Santa is at the end of it.

There is open skating at the Erie Ice Rink, 575 Kattell Street, a second hot-chocolate tent from Calvary Bible Church on Briggs, and food for sale on Wells Street and Briggs Street. No candy is handed out from the parade this year, for safety. Lost-children and first-aid tents are next to the DJ by Fox Dog Coffee on Briggs.

## Route and where to watch

The parade travels north on Briggs Street from Maxwell Avenue to Cheesman Street. Walkers leave at Cheesman for Coal Creek Park; floats continue north to Evans Street.

## Road closures and parking

- 2 to 10 pm: no parking on Briggs from Maxwell to Evans, or on Kattell Street from Moffat to Cheesman.
- 4 to 10 pm: Briggs closed from Anderson Street to Evans, and Kattell from Moffat to Cheesman.
- 5:45 to 10 pm: Briggs fully closed from Perry Street to Evans, with every cross street.

From 6:30 pm there is no vehicle access in or out east of Briggs. The Post Office can be reached until 6 pm by Kattell, Moffat and the alley.

Park on residential streets, at Erie Methodist Church on Holbrook Street, or in the Town Hall lot at Holbrook and Cheesman. Not in the alleys. The few accessible spaces, behind Orchard Community Church at Moffat Street and in the lot south of Coal Creek Park on Kattell, are first come, first served; be parked by 5:45 pm, and you cannot move your car during the parade.
