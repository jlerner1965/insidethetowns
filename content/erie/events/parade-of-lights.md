The Chamber's holiday night downtown, 4:30 to 8:30. School choirs sing inside Orchard Community Church at 470 Briggs from 4 to 6, with free hot chocolate, snacks and restrooms; Santa and Mrs. Claus are on Briggs Street from 4:30 to 6, and free carriage rides run from 4:30 to 6:30. There is open skating at Coal Creek Park, a hot-chocolate tent from Calvary Bible Church on Briggs, and food for sale on Wells and Briggs. The lighted parade steps off at 6:30 and runs north on Briggs Street from Maxwell Avenue to Cheesman Street. Briggs is closed to traffic from Perry Street to Evans Street from 5:45. Free.
