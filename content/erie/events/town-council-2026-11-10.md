Regular meeting of the Erie Town Council in the Council Chambers at Town Hall. The agenda for this one sets the start at 7:30 pm, or right after the Urban Renewal Authority meeting, which opens the evening in the same room at 6:30; Council usually meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays at 6:30. The agenda includes the resolution adopting the 2027 budget and a public hearing on the Coal Creek Property rezoning. The agenda gives the link to watch or comment online, erieco.gov/CouncilMeeting; agendas post at least 24 hours ahead.
