The Town's Independence Day fireworks, on July 3, launched from the roof of the Erie Community Center with the crowd in Community Park next door. In 2026 event parking opened at 6 pm, food trucks and vendors ran through the evening, and the show went up at about 9, the time depending on the light and the weather. Free. The Community Center campus, its pickleball and tennis courts and every parking lot there and at the park closed from 1 pm, and the skate park at dusk.
