The farm's autumn season: a corn maze, the pumpkin patch, barrel train rides, bounce mats and weekend entertainment, September 23 to October 31, closed Tuesdays. Timed-entry tickets online, priced by the day: through October 25, Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays are $18, Fridays $25 and weekends $32, with anytime and at-the-door tickets up to $50; October 26 to 30 drops to $13.50 to $26.25, and Halloween is $16 to $21. Children three and under free. Last entry 4:30 on Mondays and Wednesdays, when the farm closes at 6. Tickets are limited on peak weekends and sell out.
