The farm's autumn season: a corn maze, the pumpkin patch, barrel train rides, bounce mats and weekend entertainment, September 23 to October 31. Timed-entry tickets online: in the early-bird weeks weekdays are $13.50 and weekends $24; from October 3 weekdays are $18 and weekends $32; children three and under free. Last entry 4:30 on Mondays and Wednesdays; closed Tuesdays and Fridays by day. Tickets are limited on peak weekends.
