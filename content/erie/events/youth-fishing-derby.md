A morning at Thomas Reservoir for anglers aged 5 to 12 of any skill: stations for casting, handling fish and fishing from the bank, prizes for the biggest fish, a casting contest and a completed booth passport, and a free pole for the first 100 children. Free, with no registration. Anyone 16 or older who fishes needs a Colorado fishing licence. Presented with Colorado Parks & Wildlife and Arbor Anglers.
