Erie Police's evening in Community Park on the night communities across the country hold theirs, Tuesday, August 4 in 2026. It had about 20 attractions: a helicopter landing, K9 demonstrations, SWAT and bike patrol officers, drones, dispatchers, firearm safety demonstrations, sack races, a bounce house and trucks to climb on.
