An evening of classical music from the Town's Cultural Arts series and the Cultural Caravan. The violinist Njioma Grevious leads violinist Alex Gonzalez, violist Jordan Bak, cellist Josh Halpern and pianist Elliot Wuu in works by Germaine Tailleferre and Rhiannon Giddens, with Mozart and Schumann. Erie Parks & Recreation and Erie Rotary provide refreshments. Seats are reserved through [the Cultural Caravan](https://www.culturalcaravan.org/events-1/njioma-grevious-friends-at-the-erie-community-center).
