The 13th annual Erie Brewfest fills Coal Creek Park with more than 20 breweries (Bootstrap, Left Hand and WeldWerks among them), live music from Wandering Roads, food and craft vendors and games. Ages 21 and up; rain or shine; no pets. $45 online through October 2, $50 on the day, $5 for designated drivers; every ticket includes a commemorative glass. Proceeds go to the Erie Chamber of Commerce.
