Construction trucks, emergency vehicles and more parked in the Community Center's west lot for children to climb into, honk and steer. The first hour, 8:30 to 9:30, is a low-sensory hour with lights and sounds limited; the event proper runs 9:30 to noon, and in 2026 an airplane flew over at 10:30. Free, with food and coffee trucks selling on site.
