The Erie Historical Society opens the Wise Homestead Museum on Jasper Road for its Christmas morning on Saturday, December 12, 10 am to 2 pm, with tours of the turn-of-the-century farmhouse and museum, hot chocolate, hot cider and Christmas cookies. Admission is free; donations are appreciated. The society's page lists the entertainment: the Red Hawk Tone Chime Choir of fourth and fifth graders around 10 to 10:30, Erie High School's choral groups Topaz and CR5 around 11 to 11:30, and the Weld County Ramblers playing bluegrass from noon to 2.
