The Erie Historical Society opens the Wise Homestead Museum on Jasper Road for its Christmas morning on Saturday, December 12, 10 am to 2 pm, with tours of the turn-of-the-century farmhouse and museum, hot chocolate, hot apple cider and Christmas cookies. Admission is free; donations are appreciated. The society says there will be live entertainment and has not yet announced the acts.
