The Town's Veterans Day program at the Community Center: doors and refreshments at 10:30, a flag ceremony, a speaker and the unveiling of this year's Hometown Hero banners at 11, and the Erie High School chorale with military songs and recognition of service members at 11:30. Past and current service members are welcome in uniform. Free; pre-register online through the Town's Amilia system.
