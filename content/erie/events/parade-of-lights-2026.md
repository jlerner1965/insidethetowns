The Chamber's holiday night downtown. From 4 to 6: school choirs inside Orchard Community Church at 470 Briggs with hot chocolate and snacks, free carriage rides, Santa and Mrs. Claus, open skating at Coal Creek Park, and a hot-chocolate tent from Calvary Bible Church. The lighted parade steps off at 6:30 and runs north on Briggs Street from Maxwell Avenue to Cheesman Street. Free.
