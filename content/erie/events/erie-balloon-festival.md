The Erie Chamber of Commerce's hot air balloon weekend, the 28th in 2026: balloons inflate from 6 am and lift off between about 6 and 7:30 each of the three mornings, weather permitting, with a balloon glow on the Saturday evening from 7:30 to 9:30. The launch field is at Weld County Road 5 and Colliers Parkway; the Denver Gazette's guide gave it as free to attend, with free parking at Erie High School and Soaring Heights.
