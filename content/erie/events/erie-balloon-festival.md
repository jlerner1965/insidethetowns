The Erie Chamber of Commerce's hot air balloon weekend, next on July 9 to 11, 2027 (the 28th was July 10 to 12, 2026). The field opens at 5 am; balloons inflate at 6 and most are flying by 7 each of the three mornings, weather permitting, with a balloon glow on the Saturday evening at about 8. The launch field is at Weld County Road 5 and Colliers Parkway, just west of Erie High School. The festival's own site gives it as free to attend, with free parking at Erie High School and Soaring Heights K-8 and a free Erie Bee shuttle.
