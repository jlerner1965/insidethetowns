A 250-metre wooden velodrome that opened in 2013, closed and fell into disrepair after a 2019 sale, and has been restored by a volunteer campaign begun in 2020. Its site lists daily training, weekly racing, pickleball courts booked by appointment, and movie nights, concerts and festivals for Erie families.
