The public buildings in Erie do not all take the same days off: the Community Center keeps regular hours on Veterans Day, and the library is closed. Each list below is the one its own operator publishes.

## Erie Community Center

450 Powers Street. The Town's holiday-hours page for the Center gives:

| Day | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11 | Regular hours |
| Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26 | 6 am–1 pm |
| Day after Thanksgiving, Friday, November 27 | Regular hours |
| Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24 | 6 am–1 pm |
| Christmas Day, Friday, December 25 | Closed |
| New Year's Eve, Thursday, December 31 | 6 am–1 pm |
| New Year's Day, Friday, January 1 | Closed |

Regular hours are Monday to Thursday 5 am–9 pm, Friday 5 am–7 pm, Saturday 7 am–7 pm and Sunday 8 am–5 pm. The Town's page says these holiday hours are for the Community Center only.

## Erie Community Library

400 Powers Street. The High Plains Library District's 2026 closures apply to every branch, Erie included:

- **Closed:** Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11; Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26; Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24; Christmas Day, Friday, December 25.
- **Closing early, at 5 pm:** Wednesday, November 25, and New Year's Eve, Thursday, December 31.

Regular hours are Monday to Thursday 9 am–8 pm, Friday and Saturday 10 am–5 pm and Sunday 1–5 pm.

## The ice rink at Coal Creek Park

575 Kattell Street. The Town's season runs from Friday, November 27, 2026 to February 15, 2027.

- **November 27 to December 20:** Tuesday 5–7:45 pm, Thursday 6:15–7:45 pm, Friday 5–9:30 pm, Saturday and Sunday 11 am–1 pm and 5–9:30 pm. Closed Monday and Wednesday.
- **December 21 to 24 and December 28 to 31:** 10 am–noon and 4–9 pm.
- **Closed:** Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Admission is $10 for adults and $8 for children 12 and under, and the price includes skate rental and skate trainers. No reservations; credit or debit card only, no cash. The Town's weather hotline for closures and delays is 303-926-2550, option 4.

## Town offices, the Recycling Center and police records

The Town's list of observed holidays for the rest of the year: Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11; Thanksgiving Day and the day after, Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27; Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, Thursday and Friday, December 24 and 25; New Year's Eve, Thursday, December 31; and New Year's Day. Town Hall's regular hours are Monday to Friday, 8 am–5 pm.

For Veterans Day the Town has posted two closures in its Alert Center. The Recycling Center at 1000 Briggs Street is closed on Wednesday, November 11, and back to regular operations on Thursday, November 12. Police records services are closed on the 11th and resume the next business day at 8 am. The Recycling Center's regular hours are 8 am–4 pm, seven days a week, "holiday and weather permitting," and it is for Erie residents with proof of residency; later holiday closures go up in the Alert Center.

Town Council does not meet on November 24 or December 22: the Town's calendar lists both regular meetings as canceled. A special meeting is on the calendar for Tuesday, December 1, at 6 pm, and the next regular meeting is Tuesday, December 8.

## Trash and recycling pickup

The Town does not collect trash. Its utility providers page says "The Town of Erie does not manage any residential waste collection services," so ask your hauler, or your HOA if it contracts one, about pickup in a holiday week.

## On the days themselves

- **Veterans Day:** the Town's [Veterans Day Celebration](/events/veterans-day-celebration-2026/) is at the Community Center on Wednesday, November 11, 11 am to 12:15 pm. Pre-registration closed on November 4.
- **Thanksgiving morning:** [The Pilgrimage Run](/events/the-pilgrimage-run-2026-11-26/), a 2 mile and 4 mile run, starts at the Community Center at 9 am on Thursday, November 26.
- **The first Friday of December:** the Chamber's [Parade of Lights](/events/parade-of-lights/) is downtown on Friday, December 4, 4:30 to 8:30 pm, with the parade at 6:30.
