Erie built its parks for families and it shows: playgrounds across the neighborhoods, a splash pad and a winter ice rink downtown, a pump track that hosts a world-championship qualifier. This is how to use them.

## Downtown: Coal Creek Park

[Coal Creek Park](/places/coal-creek-park/), a block off Briggs Street on Kattell, is the one to know first. Maddie's Playground is the custom butterfly-shaped one; the splash pad runs from Memorial Day weekend to mid-September; the ice rink's 2026–27 season runs from November 27 to February 15, with admission of $10 for adults and $8 for children 12 and under, skates included. Two shelters. It is also where Brewfest and the Parade of Lights happen. [Sweets Ice Cream](/places/sweets-ice-cream/), next door to the Old Mine on Briggs, has been scooping Colorado-made Little Man and Glacier ice cream since 2015 and is the walk afterwards.

## The big one: Erie Community Park and the Community Center

[Erie Community Park](/places/erie-community-park/) on Powers Street is 49 acres beside the Community Center and the library: three playgrounds, the Revolution pump track that hosted a UCI Pump Track World Championship qualifier, a skate park, a sledding hill and walking trails. Open daily 6 am to 10 pm; the pump track is open from dawn to dusk.

The [Erie Community Center](/places/erie-community-center/) on the same site has an indoor pool with a lazy river and a hot tub, a climbing wall, a gym and an indoor track. Day passes for anyone. Weekdays from 5 am, Saturday from 7, Sunday from 8.

## The library

The [Erie Community Library](/places/erie-community-library/), between the Community Center and the park, is open seven days and runs a program most of them: a Saturday family storytime and an adaptive storytime on first Saturdays, baby open play, a chess club and a Book Dragons club for grades 3 to 5, Code Club on first Thursdays, a family LEGO club one Sunday a month, Pokémon hangouts, science explorers for K to 2, and Tales 2 Tails on first and third Tuesdays, where children read to a dog. The [events page](/events/?category=family) has the dates.

## The neighborhood playgrounds

Each is a little different, which matters to a four-year-old. [Star Meadows Park](/places/star-meadows-park/) on Flatiron Meadows Boulevard has a space-themed playground, a human sundial and mister boulders for hot days. [Serene Park](/places/serene-park/) on Colliers Boulevard has a wooden treehouse playground and outdoor fitness equipment. [Lehigh Park](/places/lehigh-park/) on Eichhorn Drive has a playground, a multi-purpose field, two shelters and walking trails. [Arapahoe Ridge Park](/places/arapahoe-ridge-park/) on Powell Street has a playground, a shaded picnic area and a pickleball and tennis court. [Longs Peak Park](/places/longs-peak-park/) on Wheat Berry Drive has a playground, a ballfield, a basketball hoop and what the Town calls amazing mountain views; it is named for the Longs Peak Coal Company. All sunrise to sunset.

## Fishing without a boat

[Thomas Reservoir](/places/thomas-reservoir-loop/) on 119th Street is about a mile of flat trail around a stocked ten-acre reservoir in the middle of town, with bank fishing for rainbow trout and bluegill and benches for the ones not fishing. [Erie Lake](/places/erie-lake/) at the south-west edge has about 1.2 miles of concrete and crusher-fines path, open again since July after the dam repairs; the Town is asking anglers to hold off there until the lake refills, and the entrance and parking lot off Highway 287 are permanently closed, so park on the Arapahoe Ridge streets the Town lists.

## Autumn: Anderson Farms

[Anderson Farms](/places/anderson-farms/), the working farm on County Road 3¼ east of town, ran its 2026 Fall Festival from September 23 to October 31: a corn maze, a pumpkin patch, barrel trains, and Terror in the Corn after dark for the older ones. The farm's site says it is currently not open to the public for any activities.

## The dog

[Reliance Park](/places/reliance-park/) at the north end of Briggs has the Boneyard, a 7.75-acre fenced, double-gated dog park with separate areas for large and small dogs, plus a small playground and a ballfield.
