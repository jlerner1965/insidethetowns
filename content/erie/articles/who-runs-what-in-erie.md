Most towns have one county, one school district and one set of offices. Erie has two of the first two and a service map that does not line up with either. The town runs its own water and police; the fire trucks, the library and the schools belong to districts that answer to nobody at the town hall; and which of those you get can change from one side of a street to the other.

This is which body to approach for what, and where the boundaries actually fall. It is not a substitute for checking a specific address — it is a list of the things worth checking.

## The county line is a real line

The Town of Erie [says it plainly](https://erieco.gov/2505/Regional-Partners): "The Town of Erie lies within two counties — Boulder and Weld Counties." County Line Road is the boundary. West of it is Boulder County; east of it is Weld. At the 2020 census 58 per cent of Erie's residents lived on the Weld side and 42 per cent on the Boulder side, so neither is a minority worth rounding away.

What changes with the side you are on:

- **Which county you file with** — property taxes, recording, assessor's valuation and appeals.
- **Which school district you are in** — see below; this is the one people discover late.
- **Which sheriff covers unincorporated land** near you, and which county court your case is heard in.
- **Which county ballot** you get, alongside the town's own questions.
- **Even the streetlights.** The town notes on its [utility providers page](https://www.erieco.gov/1284/Utility-Providers) that streetlights are "now under split ownership," with the Town having "assumed ownership and maintenance responsibilities for streetlights located primarily on the Boulder County side of Erie." If a light is out, which number to ring depends on where it stands.

## Schools: two districts, and the split is not the county line exactly

Most of Erie is [St. Vrain Valley Schools](https://www.svvsd.org/), based in Longmont — Erie, Red Hawk and Black Rock elementaries, Soaring Heights PK-8, Erie Middle and Erie High. The Boulder County side is [Boulder Valley](https://bvsd.org/), where Meadowlark PK-8 is the neighbourhood school.

Two things follow. The district you get depends on the address, not the town, so a house four streets from another can feed a different high school. And the district boundary and the county line are drawn by different bodies for different reasons, so "I am in Boulder County" is not by itself an answer about schools. Ask the district to confirm an address before you make an offer; a listing is not a source.

## What the town actually runs

Erie is a home rule municipality with a town hall on Briggs Street, and it runs more than most towns its size.

- **Water and wastewater.** Unusually, the town treats its own. The [water and wastewater division](https://www.erieco.gov/1047/Water-Wastewater) operates the Lynn R. Morgan water treatment facility and the town's own water reclamation facility, its reservoirs, pump stations and storage tanks. Your water bill comes from the town, and so does the person who fixes the main.
- **Police.** The [Erie Police Department](https://www.erieco.gov/246/Police-Department) is the town's own force, covering the whole municipality in both counties. In an emergency, call 911.
- **Parks, trails and recreation**, including [Erie Community Park](/places/erie-community-park/), [Coal Creek Trail](/places/coal-creek-trail/) and the [Erie Community Center](/places/erie-community-center/).
- **Planning, zoning and building permits**, and the [Erie Municipal Airport](/places/erie-municipal-airport/).

## What the town does not run

These are separate governments with their own boards, their own budgets and, in most cases, their own line on your tax bill.

- **Fire and emergency medical response: [Mountain View Fire Rescue](https://www.mvfpd.org/).** Not a town department. It covers "approximately 250 square miles including Boulder, Dacono, Erie, Mead, Niwot, Superior and unincorporated areas of Boulder and Weld counties" — which is why the engine that arrives in Erie may have come from a station in another town.
- **The library: [Erie Community Library](/places/erie-community-library/)**, run by the [High Plains Library District](https://www.mylibrary.us/erie/), whose district is centred on Weld County. A library card is a district matter, not a town one.
- **Electricity and gas.** Depending on the address, electricity is United Power or Xcel and gas is Xcel or Black Hills Energy. The town's [utility providers list](https://www.erieco.gov/1284/Utility-Providers) names them and notes it "is not all-inclusive."
- **Trash and recycling.** There is no municipal collection. Households arrange it with a private hauler; the town lists several, including composting.

## Metropolitan districts, and the part realtor guides skip

Much of Erie built since 2000 sits inside a metropolitan district — a local government formed to finance the streets, water mains and parks of a subdivision, repaid by a mill levy on the houses inside it. They are legal, common across the Front Range, and entirely normal. They also mean two houses with the same list price can carry materially different annual costs, and the difference does not appear in the listing.

Before you commit to an address, find out whether it is in one, what the levy is, and how long the debt runs. Colorado's Department of Local Affairs publishes [a map of every special district in the state](https://gis.dola.colorado.gov/CO_SpecialDistrict) and [the underlying list](https://data.colorado.gov/Local-Aggregation/All-Special-Districts-in-Colorado/7rar-v5z8), including metropolitan, fire, library and water districts. The county assessor for the side you are on can tell you what is actually levied on a parcel.

We are not in a position to price a particular house, and anyone who tells you a metro district is nothing to worry about is not either. It is a question with a documented answer; ask it.

## The short version

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Water, sewer, police, parks, permits | Town of Erie |
| Fire and ambulance | Mountain View Fire Rescue |
| Schools | St. Vrain Valley, or Boulder Valley on the Boulder County side |
| Library | High Plains Library District |
| Electricity, gas, trash | Private providers; varies by address |
| Property tax, recording, elections | Boulder or Weld County, depending on the side |
| Subdivision infrastructure debt | A metropolitan district, in much of newer Erie |
