The Town's November update on Downtown Erie: "Little Grove opened for business at 512 Briggs Street on November 7." The Town has followed it since June, when its update said Little Grove had signed a lease for a new kids play center and coffee bar at 512 Briggs St.; the July update called it a children's play center and coffee bar still in planning, and the September update had it under construction.
