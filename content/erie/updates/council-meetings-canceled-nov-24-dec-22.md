The Town's agenda portal lists the Town Council meeting and the Urban Renewal Authority meeting on Tuesday, Nov. 24 as canceled, both set for 6:30 p.m. in Council Chambers at 645 Holbrook Street. It shows the same two meetings canceled on Tuesday, Dec. 22. The Town's calendar also shows the Planning Commission meeting on Wednesday, Nov. 18 as canceled. Before the Nov. 24 gap, the Council holds a study session on Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 6 p.m. at 645 Holbrook Street. After it, the portal lists a study session on Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 6 p.m. and a regular Council meeting on Tuesday, Dec. 8 at 6:30 p.m. in Council Chambers.
