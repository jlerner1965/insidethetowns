The Town's agenda portal lists the Town Council meeting and the Urban Renewal Authority meeting on Tuesday, Nov. 24 as canceled, both set for 6:30 p.m. in Council Chambers at 645 Holbrook Street. It shows the same two meetings canceled on Tuesday, Dec. 22. The Town's calendar lists a Council special meeting on Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 6 p.m., then a regular Council meeting and an Urban Renewal Authority meeting on Tuesday, Dec. 8 at 6:30 p.m.
