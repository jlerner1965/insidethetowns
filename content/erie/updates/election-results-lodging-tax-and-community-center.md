The Town's results page now shows the combined count for both counties: 15,105 votes cast on 3E and 15,110 on 3F. The lodging tax, as the Town's sample ballot question words it, is 3.5 percent of the price of any hotel, motel, bed-and-breakfast or short-term rental stay of 30 days or less, from January 1, 2027, with the money for general purposes including parks, capital infrastructure and public safety; the Town's lodging-tax page expected about $150,000 a year from the roughly 50 short-term rentals it had identified, before any hotel. The Community Center question would have raised the Town's sales and use tax by 0.501 percent from January 1, 2027 and allowed up to $32.5 million in debt, repaid at up to $52.6 million, for an expansion and renovation the Town costed at about $32 million; it failed, 42 to 58 percent. The same page shows the Council results: Bob Braudes over Andrew Sawusch in District 1, Ben Hemphill over Delaney Dreckman and Dipak Basnet in District 2, and Amy Becker over Brian O'Connor in District 3. The counties' own counts went the same way on both questions: Boulder County's, last updated at 12:59 am on November 4, had 3E at 4,124 to 2,406 and 3F at 2,764 to 3,759, and Weld County's summary report of 11:35 pm on November 3 had 3E at 4,897 to 3,678 and 3F at 3,603 to 4,984. The results are official after the Canvass Board approves them in late November.
