Boulder County's unofficial count, last updated at 5:23 pm on November 4, and Weld County's summary report of 5:03 pm the same day together give 3E 9,782 for and 6,819 against (Boulder 4,407 to 2,611, Weld 5,375 to 4,208) and 3F 6,915 for and 9,689 against (Boulder 2,954 to 4,054, Weld 3,961 to 5,635); both counties went the same way on both questions. The lodging tax, as the Town's sample ballot question words it, is 3.5 percent of the price of any hotel, motel, bed-and-breakfast or short-term rental stay of 30 days or less, from January 1, 2027, with the money for general purposes including parks, capital infrastructure and public safety; the Town's lodging-tax page expected about $150,000 a year from the roughly 50 short-term rentals it had identified, before any hotel. The Community Center question would have raised the Town's sales and use tax by 0.501 percent from January 1, 2027 and allowed up to $32.5 million in debt, repaid at up to $52.6 million, for an expansion and renovation the Town costed at about $32 million; it failed, 42 to 58 percent. The Town's [results page](https://www.erieco.gov/2688/2026-Election-Results), last updated on the morning of November 4, shows the Council results: Bob Braudes over Andrew Sawusch in District 1, Ben Hemphill over Delaney Dreckman and Dipak Basnet in District 2, and Amy Becker over Brian O'Connor in District 3, and says the results are official after the Canvass Board approves them in late November.
