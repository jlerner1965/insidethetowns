The Town's rules, from its Short-Term Rentals page: the applicant must own the property, one short-term rental is allowed per parcel, occupancy is two people per bedroom plus two, and a responsible agent must answer the phone around the clock and be at the door within an hour when the police ask, with the address, permit number and agent's contact posted inside the main entrance. In residential Old Town, a proposed rental within 250 feet by sidewalk of an existing one needs a special review use, with a neighborhood meeting and hearings before the Planning Commission and Town Council, and permits there go first come, first served. A permit is valid for two calendar years from January 1 of the year it is issued, expires on December 31 of the following year, and can be renewed in a 30-day grace period after that. Standard and special-event permits both cost $200; the special-event kind, which a renter can hold with the owner's notarized permission, is valid only in periods Council designates by resolution, and the Town says the resolution for the Sundance Film Festival goes to Council on November 10.
