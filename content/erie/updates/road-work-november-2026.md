The Town's alerts for November: Mears Broadband, working for Comcast, is boring a new fiber line along the west side of County Line Road between Moffat and Balcolm streets from Thursday, November 12, through Friday, November 20. The west sidewalk is closed through the work, and pedestrians are asked to use the Moffat and Balcolm crosswalks. Colorado Boring Company is laying a fiber line between the Town's water plant and its tank site west of Weld County Road 7, on sidewalks and tree lawns near Vista Ridge, with no road closures, through November 25. Crack sealing continues on streets around town through November 30.
