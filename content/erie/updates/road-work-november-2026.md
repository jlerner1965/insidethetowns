The Town's alerts for November: southbound County Line Road north of Cheesman Street drops to one lane from 9 am to 3 pm, November 9 to 13, while Xcel Energy replaces a utility pedestal at the Conoco station, 4200 County Line Road. In Northridge, Town crews install speed humps and pavement markings on Fletcher Drive November 9 to 13, 8 am to 3:30 pm, with partial lane closures and flaggers and no full closures. On Weld County Road 5, Neilson Underground starts installing fiber lines on Tuesday, November 10, at Erie Parkway and works north toward Weld County Road 10, from 9 am to 2:30 pm to stay clear of school drop-off and pick-up; the Town's notice gives single-lane closures in sections north of Erie Parkway and no full closures, no work on Veterans Day, and up to two weeks of work that may run into the week of November 23. Colorado Boring Company is laying a fiber line between the Town's water plant and its tank site west of Weld County Road 7, on sidewalks and tree lawns near Vista Ridge, with no road closures, through November 25. Crack sealing continues on streets around town through November 30. The valve replacement on Mountain View Boulevard in Vista Ridge is complete: the Town's project page says so in its November 6 update, with all travel and bike lanes reopened.
