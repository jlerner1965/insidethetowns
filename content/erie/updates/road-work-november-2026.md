The Town's alerts for November: southbound County Line Road north of Cheesman Street drops to one lane from 9 am to 3 pm, November 9 to 13, while Xcel Energy replaces a utility pedestal at the Conoco station, 4200 County Line Road. In Northridge, Town crews install speed humps and pavement markings on Fletcher Drive November 9 to 13, 8 am to 3:30 pm, with partial lane closures and flaggers and no full closures. On Mountain View Boulevard between Ridgeview and Fairway Pointe drives and Village Vista Drive, a Town contractor is replacing six water valves, with weekday lane closures from 8 am to 4:30 pm through November 17. Colorado Boring Company is laying a fiber line between the Town's water plant and its tank site west of Weld County Road 7, on sidewalks and tree lawns near Vista Ridge, with no road closures, through November 25. Crack sealing continues on streets around town through November 30.
