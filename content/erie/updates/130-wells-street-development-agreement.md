The Town's October update on Downtown Erie: planning continues for the redevelopment of the urban renewal authority's property at Wells and Kattell streets, about 10,000 square feet of new restaurant and retail space, and the Town and the developer are still refining the proposed development agreement and incentive package, with consideration scheduled for December 8. The authority approved an agreement with Rearview Mirror Concepts for the sale and development of the lots at 130 Wells St. and 570 Kattell St. in December 2025, and an amendment to the project's tax-increment reimbursement agreement on May 26, 2026.
