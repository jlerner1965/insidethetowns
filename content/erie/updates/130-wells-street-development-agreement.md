The Town's November update on Downtown Erie keeps consideration of the 130 Wells development agreement and incentives on the calendar for December 8. Its October update describes the project: redevelopment of the urban renewal authority's property at Wells and Kattell streets with about 10,000 square feet of new restaurant and retail space, the Town and the developer still refining the proposed development agreement and incentive package. The authority approved an agreement with Rearview Mirror Concepts for the sale and development of the lots at 130 Wells St. and 570 Kattell St. in December 2025, and an amendment to the project's tax-increment reimbursement agreement on May 26, 2026.
