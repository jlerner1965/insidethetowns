The Town's October update on Downtown Erie: construction continues at 656 Kattell St. on Johnny Bechamel's, a new Italian restaurant with a three-season patio, which is targeting an opening in late November. The Town's earlier updates describe homemade pasta, pizza and sandwiches, and a patio facing Coal Creek Park; the urban renewal authority approved up to $725,000 in tax-increment reimbursement for the build in March. The restaurant's own Erie page says it is opening in Erie this fall and takes newsletter sign-ups for the details.
