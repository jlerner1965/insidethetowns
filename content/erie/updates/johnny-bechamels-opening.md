The Town's November update on Downtown Erie says Johnny Bechamel's is targeting an opening at 656 Kattell Street on December 3; its October update had said late November, with construction continuing on the new Italian restaurant and its three-season patio. The Town's earlier updates describe homemade pasta, pizza and sandwiches, and a patio across from Coal Creek Park; the urban renewal authority approved up to $725,000 in tax-increment reimbursement for the build in March. The restaurant's own Erie page says it is opening in Erie this fall and takes newsletter sign-ups for the details.
