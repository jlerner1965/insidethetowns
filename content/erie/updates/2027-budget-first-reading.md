The Town's announcement says the recommended budget "prioritizes maintaining what the Town currently owns, while managing the impacts of growth and economic change," with reduced revenue projections behind it, and that Council may direct changes before it adopts a final version. The document itself is on the Town's [budget page](https://www.erieco.gov/Budget); the meetings can be watched at [erieco.gov/WatchMeetings](https://www.erieco.gov/WatchMeetings). This guide takes no side.
