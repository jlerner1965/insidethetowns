The Town's announcement says the recommended budget "prioritizes maintaining what the Town currently owns, while managing the impacts of growth and economic change," with reduced revenue projections behind it, and that Council may direct changes before it adopts a final version. The first reading was item VI.A on Council's October 27 agenda; the November 10 agenda lists "A Resolution of the Town Council of the Town of Erie Adopting the 2027 Budget" under general business. The document itself is on the Town's [budget page](https://www.erieco.gov/Budget); the meetings can be watched at [erieco.gov/WatchMeetings](https://www.erieco.gov/WatchMeetings). This guide takes no side.
