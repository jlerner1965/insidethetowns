The Town's announcement of October 22: the urban renewal authority bought the corner for $585,000 in 2024, chose Gaia Development in April 2026, and on September 22 approved the disposition, development and reimbursement agreement for a $7.3 million building. Besides Malbec, the rest of the retail space is to go to an existing Erie business moving to a larger space, which the Town does not name. Plans include landscaping and an outdoor gathering area. [Piripi](/places/piripi/), the team's existing restaurant, is on Briggs Street.
