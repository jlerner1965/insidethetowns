Cider and pizza in the two-storey 1889 brick building at the heart of the block: house-made hard ciders and beer, artisan pizzas and smoked wings, a happy hour from 3 to 5 with a dollar off drafts, and a calendar of weekly events, from Sporcle trivia on Tuesday evenings and live music on Wednesdays and Sundays to a second-Thursday bluegrass jam and a fourth-Thursday open mic. Closed Mondays.
