Christine Twining's zero-waste store on Cheesman Street, which she started, her site says, to get away from plastic waste and packaging. The shelves, as its site lists them: kitchen, cleaning, laundry and bath products to refill into your own containers (jars are sold too), bulk spices, grains, legumes, baking goods, snacks, nuts and teas, and gifts. The site also takes special orders, including Moxie Bread Co. Closed Mondays.
