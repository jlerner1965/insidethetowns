A coin and bullion dealer in the new building on Wells Street, next door to Erie Social Club. Its site lists gold, silver, platinum and palladium coins and bars, U.S. and world coins, and jewelry, and the shop also buys from customers, including inherited collections. The site says it has been in business since 2009. Open weekdays 10 to 5 and Saturday 10 to 2; closed Sunday.
