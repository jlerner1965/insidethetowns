A coffee shop at the south end of Briggs Street, in the same building as the Dugout, that calls itself "Erie's living room": espresso drinks, pastries, and room to stay and work. The shop says it roasts with Silver Canyon Coffee in Boulder and sells its single-origin beans (Colombian, Ethiopian and Sumatran among them) by the bag, roasted weekly. Open from 6 am on weekdays, 7:30 on weekends; online ordering is on its site.
