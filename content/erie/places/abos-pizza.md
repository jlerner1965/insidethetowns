The Erie shop of Abo's, the New York-style pizzeria that started in Boulder in 1977 and now has nine on the Front Range. The dough is made fresh daily and baked in deck ovens, and by the shop's allergen notice it has no peanuts, eggs or milk products in it; the pesto may contain walnuts and pine nuts. Delivery has a $20 minimum, and the shop caters. Sundays it opens at 4.
