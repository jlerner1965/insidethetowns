An 8.7-acre neighborhood park on Highlands Circle with a large playground with a hill slide and a zipline, two ballfields, a multi-purpose field, a pickleball and tennis court, a picnic shelter and walking trails; street parking, and a portable restroom from March 15 to November 15. A flag collection box is here. It is named for the Clayton Coal Company, which mined from 1920 to 1942 in the square of land that now holds Erie High School.
