Eighteen holes over varied ground beside the Coal Creek Open Space, with a practice basket, a shelter with two tables and a link to the Coal Creek Regional Trail. The course is open to everyone, first come, first served, from sunrise to sunset.

The trailhead lot north of the Erie Parkway bridge has 25 spaces and 3 accessible ones, and you can only turn right into it from Erie Parkway. The course is also a short walk from [Coal Creek Park](/places/coal-creek-park/) and [Serene Park](/places/serene-park/), and reachable from the [Coal Creek Trail](/places/coal-creek-trail/). The portable restroom is there from March 15 to November 15. The Town posts closures on the course page and by text or email alert.
