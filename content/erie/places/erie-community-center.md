The Town's recreation center on Powers Street, next to the library and Erie Community Park: an indoor pool with a lazy river and hot tub, a full-court gymnasium, an indoor running track, a 15-foot bouldering wall and a 32-foot climbing pinnacle (roped climbing without staff needs the Center's belay certification), racquetball courts, a fitness floor, meeting rooms and the Columbine Lounge for seniors. Open to everyone regardless of residency; day passes and memberships at the desk. Hosts the Town's Veterans Day Celebration.
