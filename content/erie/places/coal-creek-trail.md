Coal Creek bisects Erie from north to south and the Town's 400-acre open space along it, in three units, carries the Coal Creek Regional Trail: 4.7 miles of concrete, 2.9 of soft surface and 2.5 of crusher fines, with benches and pet-waste stations. At the south end it connects to the City of Lafayette's Coal Creek Trail system. Trail access at Reliance Park (900 County Road 1.5), the Leon A. Wurl Service Center, the Coal Creek Disc Golf Course and Lehigh Park. Sunrise to sunset; dogs on leash; coyotes about; no horses.
