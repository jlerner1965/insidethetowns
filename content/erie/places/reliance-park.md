A 19-acre park at the north end of Briggs Street on County Road 1.5, with a ballfield, a small playground, walking trails and the Boneyard, a 7.9-acre double-gated dog park with fenced areas for large and small dogs, open sunrise to sunset. The Coal Creek Trail's north trailhead is here, and Tails & Ales fills it every September.
