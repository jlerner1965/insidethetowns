A 9.2-acre park on Skyline Drive with four outdoor pickleball courts, open for drop-in play and reservations, a ballfield, a multi-purpose athletic field, a shelter and a walking trail, with a paved parking lot and a portable restroom from March 15 to November 15. There is no playground. A flag collection box is here. It is named for the Columbine Mine, which ran from 1920 to 1946, employed about 350 men and produced more than seven million tons of coal.
