A 22.2-acre park on Tynan Drive with two ballfields, a cricket pitch set in the native grass, a playground, a shelter and walking trails, a dirt parking lot and a portable restroom from March 15 to November 15. The fields are open for drop-in play; teams rent them from March 15 to November 15.
