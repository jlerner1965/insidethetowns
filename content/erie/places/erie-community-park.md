The big park on Powers Street that shares its site with the Community Center and the library: a ballfield complex, multi-purpose fields, three playgrounds, the Revolution pump track that hosted a UCI Pump Track World Championships qualifier in May 2026, a skate park open 6 am to 10 pm, a sledding hill, reading nooks, eleven rentable shelter wings and a walking loop. Restrooms are open mid-March to mid-November.
