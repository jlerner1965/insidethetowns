The big park on Powers Street beside the Community Center: a ballfield complex, multi-purpose fields, three playgrounds, the Revolution pump track, a skate park open 6 am to 10 pm, a sledding hill, reading nooks, eleven rentable shelter wings and walking trails. Restrooms are open mid-March to mid-November.
