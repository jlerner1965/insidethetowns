Railbike rides on the 4.2-mile Boulder Valley Mine Route, which the company describes as Colorado's second-oldest railroad tracks. The classic ride, sunset departures and weekday-morning rides with per-person pricing run in season; Rails and Ales, for 21 and over, is a sunset ride with a stop at The Old Mine Factory for snacks, cider, beer and trivia. In the fall a shorter two-mile Haunted History ride runs down to the ghost depot and back, and in winter there are a daytime Christmas ride on the full route, with Santa at some departures, and a two-mile illuminated Christmas ride. Rides are booked online, priced by day and time, and the holiday rides have limited seats.
