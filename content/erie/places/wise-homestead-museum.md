Oliver and Adaline Wise came from Wisconsin in 1869, patented the land in 1870 and built the Western Victorian farmhouse that the Erie Historical Society opened as a museum in 2007, one of the oldest frame houses in Boulder County. Open Saturdays 10 to 2 from May through September and by appointment the rest of the year; the Society also runs Biscuit Day in spring and A Homestead Christmas in December.
