Globally inspired tapas upstairs in the 615 Briggs building: European cured meats, Spanish octopus, small plates meant for the table, and a paella day once a month, by reservation only. Most dishes are marked gluten-free on the menu; ask about cross-contact. Reservations through the restaurant's website.
