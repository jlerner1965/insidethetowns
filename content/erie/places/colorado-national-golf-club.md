A public championship course on Vista Parkway, run by Heritage Golf Group, with tee times booked online. It is the home course of the University of Colorado's men's and women's golf teams and has hosted NCAA regionals, USGA and PGA of America events and a Korn Ferry Tour Monday qualifier. Master's Restaurant serves lunch and dinner seven days a week, and the club hosts weddings, corporate outings and charity events.
