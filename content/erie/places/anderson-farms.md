A working farm on County Road 3¼ east of town that opens each autumn for one of the Front Range's biggest fall festivals, September 23 to October 31 in 2026: a corn maze, a pumpkin patch, barrel train rides, bounce mats and entertainment by day, and the Terror in the Corn haunted attraction on Thursday to Sunday nights. Timed-entry tickets online; children three and under free.
