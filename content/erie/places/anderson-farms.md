A working farm on County Road 3¼ east of town that opens each autumn for one of the Front Range's biggest fall festivals, September 23 to October 31 in 2026: a corn maze, a pumpkin patch, barrel train rides, bounce mats and entertainment by day (closed Tuesdays; Fridays are priced between the weekday and weekend rates), and the Terror in the Corn haunted attraction on Thursday to Sunday nights. Timed-entry tickets online; children three and under free.

Read on 2 November 2026, the farm's site says it is not open to the public for any activities, and gives no date for the next season; its 2026 U-Pick Sunflowers ran on select days from August 1 to 23 and the Fall Festival from September 23 to October 31. The office keeps Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, hours from November 1 to July 31.
