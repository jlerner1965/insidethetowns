A 12.2-acre park in the Vista Ridge area with a ballfield and a multi-purpose field, a basketball hoop, a playground, a shelter with four tables and two grills, walking trails and, in the Town's words, amazing mountain views. It takes its name from the Longs Peak Coal Company, which worked the area east of County Line Road and south of Jay Road from 1892 to 1901.
