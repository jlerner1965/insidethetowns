A 9.3-acre neighborhood park on Flora View Drive in Colliers Hill with open turf fields, native areas, a large playground, a loop trail, two large shelters, seating, a full-size basketball court, a sand volleyball court, lawn game areas and a parking lot. The shelters are first come, first served. The Colliers Hill Metro District owns and maintains the park until the Town takes it over in 2027, at the end of its two-year warranty; the amenity center and pool next door are the metro district's, not part of the park. The name comes from the banded, iron-rich rock dug up on the site.
