A 250-metre wooden velodrome designed by the track architect Peter Junek, which opened in 2013, hosted national and international races for five years, and fell into disrepair after it was put up for sale in 2019. A volunteer campaign begun in 2020 has restored it. It now runs daily training and weekly racing, books pickleball courts by appointment on its site, and puts on movie nights, concerts and festivals for Erie families. Membership and events are on its site.
