An 8-acre park on Lombardi Street with a playground that includes a challenge course, a multi-purpose athletic field, a shelter and walking trails, and plenty of street parking. The permanent restroom is open 6 am to 10 pm from March 15 to November 15. The park is named for the Crescent Mine, which was working in 1905 near Jay Road, just west of the Sullivan Ditch.
