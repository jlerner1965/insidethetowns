Daily balloon flights in season, May through November, over the Rocky Mountain ranges and the plains. Fair Winds takes private parties and large groups, with group discounts, and flies promotional and instructional flights. Each flight comes with a champagne toast (non-alcoholic drinks too), a T-shirt, a pin and a framed certificate with a photo. Flights and gift certificates are booked on its site.
