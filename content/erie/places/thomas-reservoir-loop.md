A 28-acre Town open space around a 10.7-acre reservoir on 119th Street: a mostly flat loop of about 0.8 miles in concrete and crusher fines, plus soft-surface spurs, with bank fishing for stocked rainbow trout, bluegill and largemouth bass, benches and grass. Dogs on a six-foot leash and out of the water.
