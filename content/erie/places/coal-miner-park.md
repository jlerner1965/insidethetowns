A 0.8-acre park close to downtown with a large picnic shelter (nine tables and two grills), a sand volleyball court with a net and a playground; a parking lot and some street parking, and a portable restroom from March 15 to November 15. The playground is closed for replacement from October 15 to late November 2026. The shelter can be rented from March 15 to November 15 through the Town's Amilia store.
