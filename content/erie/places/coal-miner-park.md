A 0.8-acre park close to downtown with a large picnic shelter (nine tables and two grills), a sand volleyball court with a net and a playground; a parking lot and some street parking, and a portable restroom from March 15 to November 15. The playground closed on October 15, 2026 for replacement; the Town expects the new one at the end of November or in early December, and the volleyball court is to be upgraded after that. The shelter can be rented from March 15 to November 15 through the Town's Amilia store.
