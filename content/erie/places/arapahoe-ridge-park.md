A neighborhood park on Powell Street on the Boulder County side of town, with a nautical-themed playground, big shade trees and picnic areas.

The 5.5-acre park also has a ballfield, a multi-purpose field and courts for drop-in tennis or pickleball. Parking is on the street. The picnic area is shaded by trees, with no roofed shelter, and is first come, first served. Restrooms are open seasonally, March 15–November 15, 6 am–9 pm. Drop-in play on the field is allowed when it is free; organized use needs a permit.
