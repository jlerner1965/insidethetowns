The society was founded in 1984 "to preserve Erie, Colorado's history". It keeps the [Wise Homestead Museum](/places/wise-homestead-museum/), a farmhouse built by an Erie settler in 1870, holds Erie Biscuit Day each fall and hosts talks by local historians. Its next public day is [A Homestead Christmas](/events/a-homestead-christmas-wise-homestead-museum-2026-12-12/) on December 12.

**Join.** "Any person interested in the history of Erie, Colorado may become a member." Membership is $20 for one person, $25 for a family and $50 for a business or institution, and the society says members get discounts on books, research materials, fees, programs and workshops, and invitations to members-only events. Sign up on the [Membership page](https://www.eriehistoricalsociety.org/membership/) or by mail.

**Volunteer.** The [Volunteer page](https://www.eriehistoricalsociety.org/volunteer/) has a form to be told of openings. The roles it lists are museum docent in summer, collections, landscaping and yard work, gardening and board member.
