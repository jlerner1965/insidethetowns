The Community Center's wall runs climbing classes in seven-week blocks. **Kids Rock**, ages 6 to 8, is open to young climbers at any level and teaches teamwork, balance, coordination, climbing commands and technique, Tuesdays or Wednesdays at 4:30. **Big Kids Rock**, 9 to 12, meets Wednesdays or Thursdays at 5:30 or Sundays at noon, and **Next Level Rock**, 12 and up, Wednesdays at 6:30. The November–December classes began the week of October 27 and run to mid-December; the Wednesday Kids Rock and Big Kids Rock classes are full and take a wait list, and the others had places on October 22. Each is $80 to $100.
