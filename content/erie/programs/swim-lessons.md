The Town teaches the American Red Cross levels in half-hour classes: **parent and child** for 6 months to 3 years with a grown-up in the water, **Preschool 1 to 3+** for ages 3 to 5 at about one teacher to five children, and **Levels 1 to 5** and a **stroke clinic** for school-age swimmers. The session on the registration site now is Saturday mornings, October 31 to December 12 with no class over Thanksgiving weekend, $36 to $45 for the six weeks; many of its classes are full and take a wait list. The Town's page also describes teen (12 to 18) and adult classes, and private lessons booked a month at a time, with registration opening a week before each month; call the swim lesson hotline at 303-926-2562 for those. Open swim times are on the [pool schedule](https://www.erieco.gov/2139/Pool-Schedule).
