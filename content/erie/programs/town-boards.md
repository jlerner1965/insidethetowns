**Sit in.** The meetings are at [Town Hall](/places/erie-town-hall/) at 6:30 pm unless the Town's calendar says otherwise, and agendas are online no later than 24 hours ahead. The Planning Commission meets in the Council Chambers on the first and third Wednesdays and takes written public comment at planning@erieco.gov. The advisory boards meet in person in the Community Room: Sustainability on the first Wednesday, Open Space and Trails on the second Monday, Tree on the second Wednesday, Airport Economic Development on the third Thursday and Historic Preservation on the fourth Monday. The Board of Adjustment meets only when it has a case. Coming up: the [Tree Advisory Board on November 11](/events/tree-advisory-board-meeting-2026-11-11/), the [Planning Commission on November 18](/events/planning-commission-2026-11-18/), the [airport board on November 19](/events/airport-economic-development-advisory-board-meeting-2026-11-19/) and [Historic Preservation on November 23](/events/historic-preservation-advisory-board-meeting-2026-11-23/).

**Serve.** The Town Council appoints the members. The Planning Commission, the Open Space and Trails board, the Tree board, the airport board and the Board of Adjustment are for Town residents, and terms run four years, three on the Board of Adjustment. The Town says appointments to vacancies "are made by the new Council following the election", and at other times when a seat opens. The board with the vacancy recommends an applicant, the Council votes at a regular meeting, and the Town Clerk tells every applicant the result. Apply through the Town's [application page](https://www.erieco.gov/1419/Apply-to-an-Advisory-Board), which has one form for each board.
