Troop 73 is a girls' Scouts BSA troop with a focus on STEM and outdoor adventure skills: camping and hiking year-round, an outdoor activity every month and service projects in the community through the year. The Scouts lead, with adult leaders for support; at least two adults, one of them a woman, are at every activity, and parents of a Scout take the youth-protection training before she starts. The troop draws girls from Erie, Firestone, Frederick, Dacono, Longmont and Mead. Questions go to troop73g@gmail.com; its site gives no cost.
