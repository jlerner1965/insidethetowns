The school lists five sports and a club, each sport with its own registration window: **girls' volleyball** (August), **boys' basketball** (September 21 to October 5), **girls' basketball** (November 2 to 16), **wrestling**, coed (January 11 to 24, 2027) and **track and field**, coed (February 22 to March 7, 2027), with **cross country** run as a club. Soccer for this age is through the City of Longmont, and the school points to the towns of Erie, Frederick and Longmont for football, soccer and swimming. Coaches set the practice schedules and hand them out on the first day. The school follows the high-school eligibility rule: two F's and a student sits out the next week's competitions.
