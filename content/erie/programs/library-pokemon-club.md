The library's answer to a day off school: an afternoon of battles and card trading for grades 4 to 8, [November 24](/events/pokemon-club-2026-11-24/) in Thanksgiving week and [December 22](/events/pokemon-club-2026-12-22/) in winter break. Sign up for each one separately. For younger fans the library also holds Pokémon Hang Out, an unstructured meet-up for elementary-age kids with no guidance on play or trades, on Saturdays [November 28](/events/pokemon-hang-out-2026-11-28/) and [December 26](/events/pokemon-hang-out-2026-12-26/) at 2 pm.
