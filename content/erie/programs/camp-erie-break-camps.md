Camp Erie is the Town's licensed childcare camp, mostly a summer program, with short camps on the school breaks. This fall and winter there are three, each Monday to Wednesday: **Thanksgiving Break Camp**, November 23 to 25 (register by November 13); **Winter Break Camp I**, December 21 to 23 (by December 11); and **Winter Break Camp II**, December 28 to 30 (by December 21). Campers rock climb, swim, play gym games and make crafts between 9 and 4, and drop-off from 7:30 and pick-up until 5:30 cost nothing extra. Pack a swimsuit and towel, a sack lunch, a water bottle and two snacks.

Each camp is $125 to $180 on the registration site, which does not say which rate is whose. Erie households in a federal assistance program can apply for the [Camp Erie scholarship](https://www.erieco.gov/2605/Recreation-Scholarship-Programs), half off per child. The Town says it also runs a camp in spring break; it is not yet listed.
