The Town runs its own recreational leagues for every skill level, with two or three practices or games a week and volunteer parents as coaches. **Winter basketball** for 3rd to 6th graders is next: 10U and 12U boys' and girls' divisions, teams practicing once a week on a weeknight and playing Saturday games against Erie and Carbon Valley teams, some of them away. The registration site lists the teams from January 11, 2027 at $145, $116 for residents. The fall league for the youngest players, 5U Little Dribblers through 2nd grade, runs from early November to mid-January (the Town's youth sports page, read October 26).

After that come the spring leagues: **flag football** for ages 5 to 13 (March to May; the fee includes a jersey and a mouth guard), **soccer** from Little Kickers under 5 to U15 (April and May; families supply shin guards), girls' **softball** 8U to 18U with the Indian Peaks Girls Softball Association (March to July, with travel), and **baseball** for ages 4 to 14 from T-ball up (late May to early August). The Town's page also lists contracted programs and local competitive clubs, from Coal Creek Little League to the Erie Tigers Wrestling Club.
