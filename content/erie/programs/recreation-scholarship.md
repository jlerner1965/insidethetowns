The Town and Weld County United Way fund a credit of up to $200 a year for each eligible person, applied to the family's Amilia account for programs, classes and memberships at the Community Center. It does not cover daily admission, punch passes, monthly memberships, private swim lessons, personal training or rentals. To qualify, an applicant lives in Erie (a Town water bill or a property tax statement is the proof) and takes part in a federally funded program: free or reduced school lunch under the federal rules, Medicaid, SNAP, WIC, SSI or SSD, or Section 8 or public housing, shown with a dated letter rather than a card. Applications run from January 1 to November 30 or until the money is gone, and the credit expires on December 31. The same form covers the **Camp Erie scholarship**, half off camp per child, and a **therapeutic recreation** scholarship of up to $200, which also considers applicants from outside Erie.
