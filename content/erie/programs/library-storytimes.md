The library's weekly sessions in the storytime room, by age. **Twinkle Babies**, a lap-sit storytime for babies up to 18 months and their grown-ups, Mondays at 9:30 and 1:15 and Wednesdays at 9:30; it is the one weekly storytime that takes registration, for the whole eight-week session, and the 9:30 groups are full with a wait list. **Tales for Tots**, for 18 months to 3, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9:30 and Wednesdays at 10:30. **Music & Movement**, instruments and moving together for ages 3 to 5, Tuesdays at 10:30. **Family Storytime**, for toddlers and preschoolers with everyone welcome, Mondays at 10:30 and Thursdays at 4:15. The drop-in sessions are first come, first seated, and the library asks families to arrive early.

On the first Saturday of the month there are two more: [Adaptive Storytime](/events/adaptive-storytime-2026-11-07/) at 9:30, a smaller group with movement, music and sensory play for neurodivergent children and others who learn best that way, siblings welcome, one registration per family; and [Saturday Family Storytime](/events/erie-saturday-family-storytime-2026-11-07/) at 10:30 for 5 and under. [Baby Open Play](/events/baby-open-play-2026-11-19/), drop-in sensory play for babies and their grown-ups, is on a Thursday afternoon once a month at 1:15.
