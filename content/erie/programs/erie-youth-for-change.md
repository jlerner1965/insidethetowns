A youth-run committee that connects Erie's teenagers with the Town. Members live in Erie or go to a school in Erie, serve from September to May, and decide what they work on: they set the year's focus and goals, schedule and run the meetings, and organize the events and projects, with Town staff as liaisons who document volunteer hours. Active members get a free annual pass to the Erie Community Center, granted in January. Middle schoolers are welcome to help at the group's events. Apply with the Town's 2026–27 application.
