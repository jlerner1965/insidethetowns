Fifteen teams from towns we cover are still alive in Colorado's 2A to 5A football playoffs. Three are unbeaten (Fort Collins, Evergreen and Elizabeth, each 10-0), and Roosevelt, in Johnstown, is the top seed in 4A. Most of them play Friday, November 13; Mead, Montrose, Golden and Elizabeth play Saturday.

The 2A final is Saturday, November 28, at CSU Pueblo's ThunderBowl. The 3A, 4A and 5A finals are Saturday, December 5, at CSU's Canvas Stadium in Fort Collins, [per CHSAA](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/09/09/fall-2026-state-championship-dates-locations-announced). Below: this weekend's games, then a short brief on each team, read from CHSAA's brackets and the season's coverage on November 13.

## 5A

### Fort Collins Lambkins (No. 4, 10-0)

The only unbeaten team left in 5A, averaging more than 42 points a game, [the Coloradoan reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/northern-colorado-teams-could-win-112929041.html). The Lambkins closed the regular season by beating Rocky Mountain 52-17. No. 4 is the highest 5A seed for a Fort Collins city team since Rocky Mountain was No. 2 in 2008. A win sends them to a home quarterfinal against Grandview on November 20.

**Standout:** quarterback Jace Garcia was the state's No. 2 passer and Preston Mozer its No. 6 receiver as of CHSAA's September 5 roundup.

### Grand Junction Tigers (No. 8)

Grand Junction was 8-1 and ranked in the state's top 25 before a 48-10 loss to Roosevelt in the last week of the regular season. Its second-round opponent, Ralston Valley (8-3), beat Castle View 34-24 in the first round.

## 4A

### Roosevelt Roughriders, Johnstown (No. 1, 9-1)

Roosevelt lost its opener, then won nine straight, four of them against other playoff teams, and allowed 34 points over its last seven games. It beat 5A Grand Junction 48-10 and won 43-14 at Loveland. Montbello, the opponent, edged George Washington 24-17 in the first round.

**Standout:** in a 56-0 win over Longmont, the defense allowed 73 total yards and senior linebacker Thomas Dunn returned an interception 85 yards for a touchdown, [per High School On SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/colorado/colorado-high-school-football-top-25-state-rankings-oct-15-2026-01m4z1e0pzh8).

### Loveland (No. 5, 9-1)

Loveland started 6-0, and its only loss is the 43-14 game against Roosevelt. The Coloradoan calls its defense "sneaky-good." Columbine beat Durango 56-27 to get here.

**Standout:** dual-threat quarterback Jackson Gebhardt.

### Monarch Coyotes, Louisville (No. 4, 9-1)

Monarch hasn't lost since Broomfield in its second game, and it won the 4A Front Range League title by beating Golden 49-7.

**Standout:** the defense. In a 52-9 win over Boulder it had 8 tackles for loss, 4.5 sacks, 9 quarterback hurries and 5 takeaways, 4 of them interceptions, [per High School On SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/colorado/colorado-high-school-football-top-25-state-rankings-oct-22-2026-01m5h15wktyp).

### Douglas County Huskies, Castle Rock (No. 13, 9-2)

The Huskies ended the regular season with a 35-7 loss to unbeaten Ponderosa, then won a 33-30 first-round game against Cheyenne Mountain.

**Standout:** Myles Coxe ran for 310 yards and three touchdowns in that first-round win, [per High School On SI](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/colorado-high-school-football-top-150006221.html).

### Montrose Red Hawks (No. 2, 8-2)

Montrose plays Air Academy for the second time in three weeks. It won the regular-season finale between them 46-14.

### Golden Demons (No. 9)

Golden shut out Westminster 21-0, lost 49-7 to Monarch with the 4A Front Range League title at stake, and beat Fruita Monument 24-6 in the first round. Mesa Ridge (8-2) had a first-round bye.

## 3A

### Evergreen Cougars (No. 5, 10-0)

Evergreen won the 3A Foothills League title and the Mountain Bowl rivalry trophy. This is a rematch: it beat Pomona 42-0 on October 16.

### Timnath Cubs (No. 7)

Timnath opened with a 28-0 shutout of Berthoud and started 7-0, then lost its last three regular-season games, to Holy Family, Mead and Fort Morgan. Friday is the first home playoff game in school history. The Coloradoan says its defense "borders on dominant." Eagle Valley beat Palmer 47-0 in the first round.

**Standout:** quarterback Jackson Craig and running back Makai Major.

### Severance Silver Knights (No. 11, 8-3)

Severance started 1-2, including a 19-6 loss to Timnath, and comes in on a seven-game winning streak, each win by at least 18 points. Its defense has allowed 20 points in a game once all season. It beat Lewis-Palmer 47-14 in the first round.

### Palisade Bulldogs (No. 3, 9-1)

Palisade opened with a 45-20 win over Lewis-Palmer and hosts Fort Morgan at Stocker Stadium.

### Fort Morgan Mustangs (No. 14)

Fort Morgan upset Timnath, then No. 6, in late October, and beat Summit 35-14 in the first round.

### Mead Mavericks (No. 4)

Mead lost to 4A Lutheran and to Holy Family, then won its last five, including 28-7 at Timnath on October 15. It has finished runner-up three times in the past decade. Rifle beat Cañon City 26-20 in the first round.

## 2A

### Elizabeth (No. 1, 10-0)

Elizabeth had a first-round bye and beat Weld Central 36-13 in the second round.

**Standout:** quarterback Joaquin Aviles threw for 135 yards and a touchdown and ran for 102 yards and another score against Weld Central, [per High School On SI](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/colorado-high-school-football-top-150006221.html).

The 3A to 5A quarterfinals are November 20 and 21 and the semifinals November 28. In 2A, the semifinals are November 21. CHSAA had not posted 2026 ticket details for the finals when we checked; last season's 3A to 5A finals sold through GoFan, payment was electronic only, and one ticket covered all three games, per [CHSAA's postseason page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/2021/10/31/FB_1031215231.aspx).

## Out of the playoffs

Berthoud (3A) lost 21-18 to Pueblo South, Windsor (3A) 17-13 to Silver Creek, Frederick (4A) 10-3 to Air Academy, Longmont (4A) 34-31 to Vista Ridge, Fossil Ridge of Fort Collins (5A) 49-21 to Regis Jesuit, Castle View of Castle Rock (5A) 34-24 to Ralston Valley, Fruita Monument (4A) 24-6 to Golden, Durango (4A) 56-27 to Columbine, and Fort Lupton (2A) 56-22 to Strasburg. Erie, Niwot and Lyons are not in this year's brackets.
