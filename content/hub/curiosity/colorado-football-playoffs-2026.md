### Elizabeth High School (Elizabeth)

No. 1 seed · 11-0 · Class 2A

**Last game:** beat No. 8 Alamosa 26-7 in the quarterfinals on Saturday, November 14, scoring 12 points in the fourth quarter, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/14/alamosa-at-elizabeth).

**Next game:** No. 4 Delta (11-0), semifinal, Saturday, November 21. Elizabeth is the home team on [CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/21/delta-vs-elizabeth), which gives the time as to be announced and names no venue.

**Why this matchup matters:** both teams are unbeaten. Delta beat No. 5 Eaton 43-13 in the quarterfinals; its closest games were its first two, 13-7 at Eagle Valley and 29-22 at Meeker. Delta has allowed 7.9 points a game and Elizabeth 6.5, per CHSAA's game page.

**Season context:** Elizabeth has allowed 72 points in 11 games, with four shutouts. Its closest game was 27-17 over The Classical Academy on October 23; The Classical Academy plays Wellington in the other semifinal.

**Player to watch:** Brody Aldridge averages 28.8 yards a punt return, first in Colorado, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/21/delta-vs-elizabeth); Delta's Mack Lockhart averages 155.3 rushing yards a game, 12th. Quarterback Joaquin Aviles threw for 135 yards and a touchdown and ran for 102 yards and another score against Weld Central in the second round, [per High School On SI](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/colorado-high-school-football-top-150006221.html).

**If they win:** the 2A final on Saturday, November 28, at 5 pm at the Neta and Eddie DeRose ThunderBowl at CSU Pueblo, against No. 2 The Classical Academy or No. 3 Wellington. The stadium is from [CHSAA's championship announcement](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/09/09/fall-2026-state-championship-dates-locations-announced) and the time from its [2A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/2a).

### Evergreen High School (Evergreen)

No. 5 seed · 11-0 · Class 3A

**Last game:** beat No. 12 Pomona, the defending 3A champion, 21-7 on Friday, November 13. It was Evergreen's second win over Pomona this season and its first postseason win since 2022, and Grady White's interception return for a touchdown with 3:40 left sealed it, [per CHSAA](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/11/14/football-second-round-quarterfinals-results-2026).

**Next game:** at No. 4 Mead (9-2), quarterfinal, Saturday, November 21, at 1 pm. Evergreen is the away team: [CHSAA reported](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/11/13/evergreen-football-remains-perfect-in-knocking-out-defending-3a-state-champs) that Evergreen would be on the road if Mead beat Rifle, and Mead won 56-7. The day and time are from CHSAA's [3A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/3a), which names no venue.

**Why this matchup matters:** Evergreen has allowed 35 points in 11 games, 3.2 a game, with six shutouts, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/13/pomona-at-evergreen). Mead has won six straight since a 37-28 loss at Holy Family on September 25; its other loss was 21-0 at Lutheran on September 11.

**Season context:** Evergreen's closest game was 16-14 at Conifer on October 23. Conifer, the No. 9 seed, is also in the quarterfinals, against No. 1 Palmer Ridge.

**Player to watch:** James Mauro averages 24.9 yards a punt return, third in Colorado, and Will Kelker 0.6 interceptions a game, ninth, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/13/pomona-at-evergreen).

**If they win:** a semifinal on Saturday, November 28, against No. 1 Palmer Ridge or No. 9 Conifer, per CHSAA's [3A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/3a). CHSAA has not set the time or site.

### Fort Collins High School (Fort Collins)

No. 4 seed · 11-0 · Class 5A

**Last game:** beat No. 13 Northfield 39-34 at French Field on Friday, November 13. The teams traded the lead five times in the first half, and Northfield scored with 4:08 left to get within five, [per CHSAA](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/11/14/football-second-round-quarterfinals-results-2026).

**Next game:** No. 5 Grandview (8-3), quarterfinal, Friday, November 20. As the higher seed, Fort Collins would stay home, [the Coloradoan reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fort-collins-football-could-stay-224710600.html); CHSAA's [5A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/5a) lists the game for 6:30 pm at French Field.

**Why this matchup matters:** Fort Collins is the only unbeaten team left in 5A. Grandview beat No. 12 Regis Jesuit 19-13 on November 12, three weeks after losing to it 24-21. Its other losses were 10-6 to Northfield on September 25 and 17-0 to Cherry Creek on October 30, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/12/regis-jesuit-at-grandview).

**Season context:** the Lambkins average 42.2 points a game, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/13/northfield-vs-fort-collins), and five of their wins were by single digits, the latest over Northfield. No. 4 is the highest 5A seed for a Fort Collins city team since Rocky Mountain was No. 2 in 2008, the Coloradoan reports.

**Player to watch:** quarterback Jace Garcia averages 304.7 passing yards a game, third in Colorado, and receiver Preston Mozer 97.7 receiving yards, 12th, per CHSAA's game page.

**If they win:** a semifinal on Saturday, November 28, against No. 1 Cherry Creek or No. 9 Ralston Valley, who play Friday at 7 pm at Stutler Bowl, [per CHSAA](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/11/14/football-second-round-quarterfinals-results-2026). CHSAA has not set the semifinal's time or site.

More: [Fort Collins town page](/fort-collins/)

No. 5 seed · 10-1 · Class 4A

**Next game:** No. 4 Monarch (10-1), quarterfinal, Saturday, November 21, at 2 pm, per CHSAA's [4A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/4a), which names no venue.
