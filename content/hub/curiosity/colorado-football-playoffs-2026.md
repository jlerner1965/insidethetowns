### Douglas County High School (Castle Rock)

No. 13 seed · 9-2 · Class 4A

**Next game:** at No. 4 Monarch (9-1), second round, Friday, November 13, 7 pm. Douglas County is the away team. The game is at Spangenberg Field in Lafayette, per Monarch's [GoFan ticket listing](https://gofan.co/event/7049726).

**Why this matchup matters:** Douglas County beat No. 20 Cheyenne Mountain 33-30 in the first round. Monarch has not lost since a 24-18 loss at Broomfield on September 4. Both beat Dakota Ridge: Douglas County 28-11, Monarch 21-0.

**Season context:** Douglas County's two losses were 29-27 to Chaparral and 35-7 to unbeaten Ponderosa on October 30.

**Player to watch:** Myles Coxe ran for 310 yards and three touchdowns in the first-round win, [per High School On SI](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/colorado-high-school-football-top-150006221.html). Asher Schembri averages 167.3 rushing yards a game, 13th in Colorado, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/13/douglas-county-at-monarch).

**If they win:** a quarterfinal on November 20, the date CHSAA's [4A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/4a) gives, against No. 5 Loveland or No. 12 Columbine. CHSAA has not set the time or site.

### Elizabeth High School (Elizabeth)

No. 1 seed · 10-0 · Class 2A

**Next game:** No. 8 Alamosa (7-3), quarterfinal, Saturday, November 14, 1 pm. Elizabeth is the home team. Venue not yet confirmed: [CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/14/alamosa-at-elizabeth) names none.

**Why this matchup matters:** It is a rematch of August 27, when Elizabeth beat Alamosa 35-7. Alamosa has won five straight since a September 18 loss to La Junta, the last 31-22 over Forge Christian in the second round.

**Season context:** Elizabeth has allowed 65 points in 10 games, with four shutouts, and beat No. 2 The Classical Academy 27-17 on October 23. It had a first-round bye, then beat Weld Central 36-13.

**Player to watch:** quarterback Joaquin Aviles threw for 135 yards and a touchdown and ran for 102 yards and another score against Weld Central, [per High School On SI](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/colorado-high-school-football-top-150006221.html).

**If they win:** a semifinal on Saturday, November 21, against No. 4 Delta or No. 5 Eaton, per CHSAA's [2A bracket](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/2a). CHSAA has not set the time or site.

### Fort Collins High School (Fort Collins)

No. 4 seed · 10-0 · Class 5A

**Next game:** No. 13 Northfield (7-4), second round, Friday, November 13, 7 pm, at French Field. Fort Collins is the home team. [Tickets on GoFan](https://gofan.co/event/7052319); the game streams on the [NFHS Network](https://www.nfhsnetwork.com/events/fort-collins-high-school-fort-collins-co/gam012542f1f1), a paid subscription.

**Why this matchup matters:** Fort Collins is the only unbeaten team left in 5A. Both teams beat Riverdale Ridge, Northfield 41-10 in the first round and Fort Collins 44-27 on October 23. Northfield also won 10-6 at Grandview, Fort Collins' quarterfinal opponent with a win.

**Season context:** the Lambkins average 42.5 points a game, and four of their wins were by single digits. No. 4 is the highest 5A seed for a Fort Collins city team since Rocky Mountain was No. 2 in 2008, [the Coloradoan reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fort-collins-football-could-stay-224710600.html).

**Player to watch:** quarterback Jace Garcia averages 302.0 passing yards a game, fourth in Colorado, and receiver Preston Mozer 94.2 receiving yards, 17th, [per CHSAA's game page](https://chsaanow.com/sports/football/games/2026/11/13/northfield-vs-fort-collins).

**If they win:** a quarterfinal on Friday, November 20, against No. 5 Grandview, which beat Regis Jesuit 19-13 on November 12, [per CHSAA](https://chsaanow.com/news/2026/11/12/football-quarterfinals-second-round-live-updates-2026). As the higher seed, Fort Collins would stay home, [the Coloradoan reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fort-collins-football-could-stay-224710600.html); CHSAA has not set the time or site.
