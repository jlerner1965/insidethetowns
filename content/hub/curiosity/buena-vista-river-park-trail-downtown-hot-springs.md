Start at the Community Center, 715 East Main Street: [parking is free](https://www.buenavistarec.com/communitycenter/), and its outside restrooms are open from 7 am to 10 pm every day. River Park is beside it at the end of East Main, on the Arkansas River, and the nearly flat Arkansas River Trail, 0.6 mile, makes a loop along the river with the Whitewater and Rock 'n Roll trails. Then soak: all three hot springs near town take walk-ins, and two take no reservations at all.

This is for anyone spending a few hours in Buena Vista, with children or a dog. In November the Heritage Museum and the Depot are closed for the season, and the river is cold.

## River Park

The Town owns River Park, [800 East Main Street](https://www.buenavistaco.gov/2240/Parks-Department). The [parks page](https://www.buenavistarec.com/parks/) gives its hours as dawn to 10 pm; the Town's 2023 park rules say dawn to dusk.

- **The whitewater park:** five main structures running downstream into the South Main neighborhood. The Town's map names the waves, from the top: Uptown, Staircase, Pocket, Downtown and Lo Do. CPW says it has [surf waves](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-river-sections) for boats and boards.
- **By the bridge:** a boathouse and changing room, a map kiosk and the trailhead at the Whipple Bridge, on the Town's [2023 map](https://www.buenavistarec.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/BV-River-Park-Map-3.23.23.pdf).
- **In the park:** a disc golf course, pump track, skate park, dog park, ball fields and courts, and a six-sided pavilion with four picnic tables.
- **In South Main:** the Boulder Garden & Rocks & Ropes park, with climbing boulders and access to the whitewater park, and the Town Square, among shops and restaurants.
- **No alcohol** on Town property.

## The river trails

| Trail | What it is |
|---|---|
| Arkansas River Trail | The uppermost trail on the river's west bank: nearly flat, 0.6 mile |
| Whitewater Trail | Closest to the river, with views of the paddlers; good for fishing and wildlife |
| Rock 'n Roll Trail | To the boat ramp, where many rafting trips begin |
| River Walk | An easy path from South Main to the recreation area north of River Park; in winter, sometimes a bald eagle |
| Whipple Main Route | Across the River Park bridge: the old Hack Road, 300 feet up in about a mile to County Road 304 |

All five are on the [Town's trails page](https://www.buenavistarec.com/trails/). The Whipple trails are the town's most popular hike, with views of the Collegiate Peaks. The [main route](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/whipple-trail-main-route-21081) is dirt, shared by the BLM and the Town, and dogs go on a leash.

## Walking East Main to the river

From the stoplight at Highway 24, East Main runs to the river:

1. **McPhelemy Park,** at the stoplight: the Legacy Stage, Concerts in the Park and Paddle on the Pond. The Depot & Caboose, 116 U.S. Highway 24 North, is [closed for the season](https://www.buenavistaheritage.org/locations-and-hours).
2. **The xeric gardens:** [11 gardens](https://www.buenavistaco.gov/2493/Main-Street-Xeric-Gardens) beside the curbs at East Main's three main intersections.
3. **Historic Town Hall,** 210 East Main: [built in 1883](https://www.buenavistaco.gov/2198/Historic-Town-Hall) as the Bank of Buena Vista, with stone plaques reading "Bank" and "1883".
4. **The Historic Chaffee County Courthouse,** 506 East Main, home of the Buena Vista Heritage Museum: closed for the season.
5. **The Community Center,** 715 East Main: free parking, the outside restrooms on the north side, and a playground with swings, slides, a jungle gym and musical play equipment. Showers are $2 for 4 minutes, in quarters only.
6. **River Park,** at the end of the street.

## Parking

- **The Community Center:** ample free public parking.
- **The River Park lots:** the 2023 map marks parking at the boathouse at the end of East Main, at Walton Loop, the ball fields, South Main and Ramsour.
- **RVs and trailers** may stay in the Town's right of way [no more than 72 hours](https://www.buenavistaco.gov/2603/RVs-and-Camping), and nobody may sleep in an RV parked there or on other Town property.

## Dogs

- **On a leash** [no longer than 20 feet](https://www.buenavistaco.gov/2625/Dogs) on public property and in Town parks; an electronic collar does not count.
- **Not on** the Town's playing fields.
- **Off the leash:** the dog park across from the Community Center, with a small-dog area and a large-dog area, and the Town's off-leash area at the Rodeo Grounds.

## The river in November

- **The water is cold:** Colorado's waters stay [under 70 degrees](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-river-sections) most of the year, and cold-water shock can drown strong swimmers.
- **Life jackets:** state law requires one for everyone on a boat, and children under 12 wear one at all times on any paddleboard, boat or kayak.
- **The boating season** ends in late summer or early fall; some sections stay boatable all year, with [cold and possible portages](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-activities).
- **Wading anglers:** the river has strong currents even where it looks calm.

## Two ways to spend the day

### A short visit

1. Park at the Community Center.
2. Walk into River Park and loop the Arkansas River, Whitewater and Rock 'n Roll trails.
3. Come back past the playground and the restrooms.

### Half a day

1. Start at McPhelemy Park and walk East Main to the river.
2. Cross the River Park bridge and climb the Whipple Main Route to County Road 304 for the view of the Collegiate Peaks.
3. Drive to one of the hot springs.
