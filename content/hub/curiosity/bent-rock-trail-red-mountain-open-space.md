## Why go

Larimer County's [Red Mountain Open Space](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain) is 15,780 acres of deep crimson and tan rock, rolling grassland and sandy washes, with over 15 miles of multi-use trails. The Bent Rock Trail is one way to see it on foot, and there is no entrance fee.

- [Red Mountain Open Space brochure, 2023](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/brochure_-_red_mountain_open_space_english_-_august_24_2023.pdf) (Larimer County, PDF)

## Distance and difficulty

The Bent Rock Trail is 2.0 miles on the map in the county's [2023 brochure](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/brochure_-_red_mountain_open_space_english_-_august_24_2023.pdf). That figure is for the trail itself; on the map, connecting trails link it to the parking lot.

The county's [2009 brochure](https://fcmod.org/trails/tour1/brochure_redmountain.pdf) listed it as hiking only and rated it easy.

## Getting there

The trailhead is on County Road 21, 25 miles north of Fort Collins, [Larimer County says](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain).

- **Take County Road 21.**
- **Not Red Mountain Road** off North Highway 287: there is no access to the open space from it.
- **Not Harriman Road** off Interstate 80: no access from it either.

## When it's open

Open March 1 through November 30 each year. Closed December 1, 2026, to March 1, 2027.

On open days it is open [sunrise to sunset](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain), for day use only. There is no entrance fee.

## Hunting

This fall, deer hunting by special permit runs through November 30, on weekdays only, according to the county's [hunting page](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain/hunting). There is no hunting on weekends, so plan your visit for a Saturday or Sunday.

## Before you go

- **Check the county's [Red Mountain page](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain)** on the day you go.
- **Watch the weather.** Fast-moving storms and high winds are common, the county's [2023 brochure](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/brochure_-_red_mountain_open_space_english_-_august_24_2023.pdf) says.
- **Bring water and food.** There is [no water at the trailhead](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain), and the brochure says to carry plenty of water and extra food.
- **No dogs.** [Dogs are not allowed](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/red-mountain) anywhere in the open space.

## The detail to tell a friend

Take County Road 21, not Red Mountain Road. Go on a weekend. Carry all your water. Leave the dog at home.
