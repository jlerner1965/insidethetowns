The Sundance Film Festival's first year in Boulder runs Thursday, January 21, to Sunday, January 31, 2027. [The festival says](https://festival.sundance.org/how-to-fest/attend-in-person) there is no public parking at its Boulder venues, and both it and [the City of Boulder](https://bouldercolorado.gov/getting-around-boulder-sundance-film-festival) ask anyone coming from outside the city to park once at a Park-n-Ride and ride in. That makes the towns around Boulder a workable base, if you know which bus runs, how late, and on which days. This is that, town by town.

## What is settled, and what is not

Settled: the dates, and [the venue list](https://festival.sundance.org/venues/). There are eleven film venues, among them the Boulder Theater, eTown Hall, Chautauqua Auditorium, the Dairy Arts Center, Cinemark Century, and Macky Auditorium on the University of Colorado campus. The festival's headquarters is at The Rink, 1722 14th Street, and its information desk and box office is at 1303 Pearl Street. The headquarters is one block from RTD's Downtown Boulder Station, where every RTD bus below ends.

Settled: [RTD will run five routes free](https://www.rtd-denver.com/sundance) from January 21 to 31, with longer hours. Two matter here. BOLT, between Longmont and Boulder past Niwot, runs 6:30 a.m. to 2:30 a.m. JUMP, on Arapahoe toward Lafayette and Erie, starts at 5:30 a.m. on weekdays and 6:15 a.m. on weekends. The others, HOP, SKIP and a temporary route to Chautauqua called CHAT, move people between venues inside Boulder.

Not published yet: the trip-by-trip timetables for those free buses, and the city's full transportation plan (it says more "will be released in the coming months"). RTD also changes its regular schedules on January 3, 2027. So this page gives the routes, the stops and the parking, and no departure times: for those, use [RTD's festival page](https://www.rtd-denver.com/sundance), which is where the timetables will appear. The festival expects its film schedule in early to mid-December, and single film tickets go on sale January 8.

A regular RTD fare, on the routes that are not free, is $2.75 for a three-hour pass or $5.50 for a day pass.

## Niwot

Niwot is on the BOLT. The stop is the Park-n-Ride at CO 119 and Niwot Road, on the Diagonal Highway, and RTD lists that lot among those where anyone parks free for 24 hours. During the festival the BOLT is free and runs until 2:30 a.m.

To stay: the [Niwot Inn & Spa](https://insideniwot.com/places/niwot-inn/) is the only place to stay on Second Avenue, in Old Town.

The Diagonal is a construction zone through the festival: [CDOT's project](https://www.codot.gov/projects/co119mobility) between Foothills Parkway and Airport Road is scheduled to finish in spring 2027, with speed cameras and doubled fines in the work zone.

More on the town: [Inside Niwot](https://insideniwot.com/).

## Longmont

Longmont is the other end of the BOLT, so it has the same free bus with the same late hours. Board at the 8th and Coffman Park-n-Ride, 775 Coffman Street: 97 spaces, free. Longmont's local buses (routes 323, 324, 326 and 327) are free all year and reach that Park-n-Ride.

Driving the Diagonal from Longmont, know that every left turn at CO 119 and Hover Street is closed until late 2027, and so is the right turn from southbound Hover toward Boulder.

Transfort's FLEX bus also links Longmont and Boulder, on weekdays and Saturdays, but its published schedule ends January 17, 2027.

To stay: the town page lists [Hotel Longmont](https://www.hotellongmont.com/) and the [Super 8 by Wyndham Longmont/Twin Peaks](https://www.wyndhamhotels.com/super-8/longmont-colorado/super-8-longmont-twin-peaks/overview).

More on the town: [Longmont](/longmont/).

## Lafayette

Three RTD routes run from the Lafayette Park-n-Ride, 1080 South Public Road (136 spaces, free), to Downtown Boulder Station: JUMP by Arapahoe, DASH by Louisville and South Boulder Road, and the 225 by Baseline. Only JUMP is free during the festival; DASH and the 225 are regular fare, and RTD has announced no extra service on them.

RTD has not said whether JUMP's longer festival hours run as far as Lafayette.

Inside Lafayette, Boulder County's Ride Free Lafayette is a free on-demand bus, 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily, that will take you to the Park-n-Ride. The [City of Lafayette's own festival page](https://www.lafayetteco.gov/4365/2027-Sundance-Film-Festival) points visitors to the same lot.

By car, [CDOT is rebuilding three intersections on CO 7](https://www.codot.gov/projects/co7intersectionlafayette) in Lafayette, at Willow Creek Drive, Park Lake Drive and 95th Street, from October 2026 to late 2027.

More on the town: [Lafayette](/lafayette/).

## Erie

Erie has one bus to Boulder, the JUMP, from the Erie Community Center, where the Town sets aside 15 parking spaces for riders. The Town puts the ride at about 40 minutes. JUMP is free during the festival.

Not every JUMP bus goes as far as Erie, and RTD has not said whether its longer festival hours cover the Erie end of the route. Check the trips marked for Erie on RTD's schedule before you plan a late film.

The other way in is Lafayette: [the Town of Erie suggests](https://www.erieco.gov/2513/Transit-Services) the Lafayette Park-n-Ride, where DASH and the 225 also run, and Boulder County's free Ride SouthEast links Erie and Lafayette from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day.

More on the town: [Inside Erie](https://insideerie.com/).

## Lyons

Lyons has no RTD bus. The Lyons Flyer, run for Boulder County, is free and needs no pass, but it runs on weekdays only, four trips each way, and not on county holidays. From 4th and Main it leaves at 6:10 a.m., 7:50 a.m., 12:50 p.m. and 5:15 p.m. and reaches Broadway and Walnut in downtown Boulder 31 minutes later. The last bus back passes Broadway and Walnut at 6:06 p.m. No evening, weekend or festival service has been announced.

So from Lyons an evening screening or a weekend day means driving US 36 to a Park-n-Ride or a paid lot. The festival says city-owned downtown lots will be two hours free and then a $20 flat rate on January 21 to 24 and 29 to 31.

To stay: [A-Lodge Lyons](https://insidelyons.com/places/a-lodge-lyons/) on Main Street and [Stone Mountain Lodge & Cabins](https://insidelyons.com/places/stone-mountain-lodge/) on North St. Vrain Drive.

More on the town: [Inside Lyons](https://insidelyons.com/).

## Nederland

RTD's NB bus runs between the Nederland Park-n-Ride, 300 Jackson Street (75 spaces, free), and Downtown Boulder Station. NB is not one of the free festival routes, so the regular fare applies, and RTD has announced no extra service on it.

Check the last bus up the canyon on RTD's schedule before an evening film.

It is January, and the road is a canyon: CDOT's road conditions are at [COtrip.org](https://www.cotrip.org/) or by phone at 511.

More on the town: [Nederland](/nederland/).
