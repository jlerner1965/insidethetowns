For a soak with no planning, go to Old Town Hot Springs, 136 Lincoln Avenue, downtown: it [takes no reservations](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/faqs/), opens every day but Thanksgiving and Christmas, and is steps from the free bus stop at Lincoln and 3rd Street. Strawberry Park Hot Springs, about 7 miles out at 44200 County Road 36, needs a [reservation for every visit](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/pool-reservation/), in 2-hour slots that open 30 days ahead, paid in cash on arrival. From November 1 to May 1 its operator says you need four-wheel drive with snow tires to get there, or a shuttle, which includes your entry.

Old Town is the family choice, with water slides and a climbing wall. Strawberry Park is natural pools in the woods, adults only after dark, when swimwear is optional.

## Old Town or Strawberry Park

| | Old Town Hot Springs | Strawberry Park Hot Springs |
|---|---|---|
| Where | 136 Lincoln Avenue, downtown | 44200 County Road 36, about 7 miles; 15 to 20 minutes |

| Children | All day; the Heart Spring and the Icebox are 18 and over | Daytime only; 18 and over after dark |
| Swimwear | Required | Optional after dark |
| Dogs | None on site | None on the property, or left in cars |
| Getting there | The free bus to Lincoln and 3rd; two parking lots | 4WD with snow tires, November 1 to May 1, or a shuttle; no Uber or Lyft |

## Old Town Hot Springs

- **Holidays:** [closed](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/contact/) on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day; open 8 am–9 pm on November 27 and December 26; 5:30 am–4 pm on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, with the pools from 6.
- **Your pass:** sign the guest waiver online on the day, and keep your receipt to come back in the same day. A day pass covers the [whole facility](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/rates/): nine pools and a cold plunge, the gym for ages 10 and up, and the 37-foot [climbing wall](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/climbing-wall/).
- **Water slides:** two 230-foot slides, $7 for the day on top of admission, open [4–7 pm on weekends](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/water-slides/) from November 28. Riders must be 44 inches tall and strong swimmers.
- **Bring:** a towel and a swimsuit; street clothes and underwear are not allowed in the pools, and everyone showers with soap first. Babies wear non-disposable swim diapers. Lockers downstairs are free for the day, and coolers are fine, but no glass or alcohol.
- **Children** under 10 are watched by someone 15 or older, outside the pools.
- **Cold days:** the pools [close](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/safety-guidelines/aquatics/) at 10 below zero. Before 9:30 am they open only at zero or above, and after 9:30 at 5 below or above. There are no refunds for weather.
- **Crowds:** in winter late afternoons and evenings are busiest, and so are Fridays, Saturdays and holidays; mornings and weekdays are [quieter](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/soaking-pools/).

### Parking at Old Town

The [main lot](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/parking/), upper and lower, fills at busy times, and takes no overnight or oversized vehicles. Lot C, where the old tennis courts were, is the easiest: off the alley on 3rd Street between Oak and Pine streets. The City does not allow parking on Old Fish Creek Falls Road.

## Strawberry Park Hot Springs

- **Booking:** a reservation is the only way to be sure of a parking space. Slots last two hours; to come back later, book again. If a time shows no availability, it is sold out. Book each person in your party; there are no group discounts.
- **Pay:** in cash on arrival. No cards, no checks, no ATM.
- **After dark** the pools are clothing-optional and for 18 and over until they reopen the next morning. The operator gives no clock time for dark.
- **Rules:** no food, glass, gum, smoking or alcohol, and bags are checked. No photos or video after dark, and only low-intensity red light; the FAQ suggests a headlamp or low flashlight for the walk from the car to the cabins.
- **On site:** restrooms and a heated cabin for changing; no lockers, showers or indoor rest areas. No cell service or Wi-Fi.
- **Dogs:** not allowed [anywhere on the property](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/), and not in your car either.
- **Vehicles:** standard-size only; no RVs or trailers, and no Uber or Lyft passengers.

### The road in winter

From November 1 to May 1 the operator [says](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/finding-us/) Routt County law requires a 4WD vehicle with snow tires; two-wheel drive is not allowed even with chains, and cables or straps do not count as chains. The FAQ allows AWD too. The last two miles are unpaved, steep and winding, though maintained, and the State Patrol and the Sheriff's Office enforce the traction laws. Without the right car, take a shuttle.

## The shuttles to Strawberry Park

A shuttle ticket [includes your pool entry](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/shuttles/), so do not book the springs as well. The shuttles run even when the road is closed to cars, and have seats when the springs are sold out online.

## If Strawberry Park is sold out

1. **Take the next open slot:** there is no waitlist.
2. **Book a shuttle,** or call Sweet Pea if its time shows unavailable online.
3. **Stay the night:** a [lodging booking](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/lodging/) includes the pools. Stays open 90 days ahead, single nights about 30.
4. **Go to Old Town,** which takes no reservations.

## By bus to Old Town

Steamboat Springs Transit is [free](https://www.steamboatsprings.net/166/Transit), and the nearest stop is Lincoln and 3rd Street. Its summer service runs through November 27, 2026. The City has not yet posted its 2026–27 [winter schedule](https://www.steamboatsprings.net/861/Winter-Schedule-Routes). Pets ride only in an airline-style carrier; service animals are welcome.

## In summer: walk in

Forest Service Trail 1169, the Hot Springs Trail, runs [2.4 miles](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/hot-springs-7323) to Strawberry Park from the lot it shares with the Mad Creek Trailhead on Elk River Road, which is [free](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/mbrtb/recreation/trails/hot-springs-trailhead-trail-1169). Hikers check in with the attendant and pay at the springs. The trail allows dogs; the springs do not.

- **Old Town:** [oldtownhotsprings.org](https://oldtownhotsprings.org/contact/) for the day's hours and cold-weather closures.
- **Strawberry Park:** its [reservation page](https://strawberryhotsprings.com/pool-reservation/), and cash.
