Leadville is surrounded by places people left. Some are on the hill east of town. One is over a pass that closes for the winter, and one can only be walked to. They are sorted here by direction from town, because the first question is which road, and whether it is open.

One of them is closed as this is written. [CDOT shut Colorado 82 over Independence Pass](https://www.codot.gov/news/2026/november2026news/co82-independencepass-close-for-winter) for the winter as of November 3, 2026, which puts Independence out of reach from Leadville until the pass reopens in spring.

## East: the hill

The mining district is on the hill east of town. The camps on it had names, and the trail through it warns of open shafts and private land on either side. The two entries here are the two ways to see it that someone has opened to the public: the county's paved [Mineral Belt Trail](https://lakecountyco.gov/382/Parks-and-Recreation-Facilities) and the Matchless Mine.

## South: Clear Creek Canyon and Twin Lakes

Vicksburg and Winfield are on one road up one canyon, three miles apart. Interlaken is across the water from [Twin Lakes village](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/psicc/recreation/twin-lakes-historic-district), which the Forest Service describes as preserved, with many of its log cabins standing. People live there; it is on the way, not on the list.

## Over the pass, north and west

Independence, Camp Hale and the Hagerman Tunnel are the three that take the most planning: a seasonal highway, a site with standing closure orders, and a road for high-clearance vehicles.
