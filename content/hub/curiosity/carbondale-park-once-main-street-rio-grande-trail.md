Park once at RFTA's Carbondale Park & Ride, beside Highway 133: it is [free for up to 24 hours](https://www.rfta.com/how-to/park-ride/), and the paved Rio Grande Trail leaves from it. Walk the trail across the highway at the Village Road light; it runs alongside downtown, and 8th, 7th, 4th and 2nd streets lead south to Main Street. When you are done, the free Carbondale Circulator brings you back to the car every 15 minutes, 5:02 am to 9:16 pm, every day.

This is for a day visit from Glenwood Springs, Aspen or further, with children or without. The Town's two parks on the Crystal River are a ride away on the Downtowner, Carbondale's free on-demand van.

## Where to park

The Park & Ride has 155 spaces. RFTA's rules for all its lots:

- **Free** all day.
- **No overnight parking,** and no more than 24 hours a visit.
- **No storing** cars, bikes, e-bikes or scooters.
- **Towing,** at the owner's expense, for breaking the rules or parking outside a marked space.

Pitkin County's [trail map](https://www.pitkincounty.com/DocumentCenter/View/2295/Rio-Grande-Trail-Map) lists the Park & Ride as the trail's parking in Carbondale.

The Circulator times are RFTA's [winter 2026–27 timetable](https://www.rfta.com/wp-content/uploads/RFTA_Bus_Schedules_Winter_Carbondale_Circulator_CC.pdf); the Downtowner and WE-cycle are on the [Town's transit page](https://www.carbondalegov.org/community/local_transit.php).

## The Rio Grande Trail

The trail runs [3.9 paved miles](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/rio-grande-trail-12843) through Carbondale. RFTA, which owns and maintains it, says it is mostly asphalt, 8 to 10 feet wide, and open to walkers, cyclists, wheelchair users and riders.

- **Downvalley, toward Glenwood Springs:** 5.3 miles from the Carbondale Park & Ride to the CMC Park & Ride, past the historic Satank Bridge. In winter RFTA [plows this stretch](https://www.rfta.com/trail-information/) after more than 3 inches of snow.
- **Upvalley, toward Aspen:** 4.0 miles to the Catherine Bridge trailhead. Beyond it, the section from Catherine Bridge to Rock Bottom Ranch closes to all use from 5 pm on November 30 to 5 pm on April 30, with a signed detour.
- **Restrooms:** the trail's vault toilets are near Cattle Creek and Catherine Store Bridge.
- **Dogs:** [on a leash](https://www.rfta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/CorridorRulesRegs-2.pdf) at all times, and never on the wildlife section between Rock Bottom Ranch and Catherine Bridge.

## The Crystal River parks

- **Stairway Park,** 3.93 acres on Oak Run Road between Crystal Road and Greystone Drive: a trail, picnic table and fire pits.
- **Riverfront Park,** 4.44 acres beside the river south of Stairway Park: soft-surfaced trails.

Both are Town parks, [open 6 am to 10 pm](https://www.carbondalegov.org/departments/parks_&_recreation/parks.php). Dogs are not allowed in them.

## On Main Street

- **The Plaza at 4th and Main:** benches and a bike rack, with the farmers' market and special events.
- **The Carbondale Clay Center gallery,** 135 Main Street: [open](https://www.carbondaleclay.org/about-the-gallery) Monday to Friday 10 am–5 pm and Saturday 11 am–4 pm.
- **The Carbondale Arts gallery,** inside The Launchpad at 76 South 4th Street: [open](https://www.carbondalearts.com/gallery) Monday to Friday 10 am–5 pm. Its Deck the Walls holiday market is open to the public almost every day through December 24.
- **Centennial Sundial Park,** at the north corner of Main and 8th: a sundial sculpture, a picnic table and benches.
- **Restrooms and a playground:** the Town lists restrooms at Triangle Park, Village Road and 8th Street, which has a playground, and at Sopris Park, Snowmass Drive at Second Street.

## Three ways to spend the day

### A short visit

1. Park at the Park & Ride.
2. Walk the trail across Highway 133 at the Village Road light, then down 4th Street to the Plaza and Main Street.
3. Ride the Circulator back to the car.

### Half a day

Add the Crystal River parks on the Downtowner, the playground at Triangle Park, or a guided tour of the [Thompson House](https://www.carbondalehistory.org/thompson-house), 301 Lewies Lane, built in the 1880s by one of Carbondale's first homesteaders. Tours are by appointment; the Historical Society is on 970-414-1078. The log cabin of 1887 at the Jail & Cabin park, Weant Boulevard and Highway 133, is the Society's museum, [open in summer only](https://www.carbondalehistory.org/explore).

### If the weather turns

- **The Recreation Center,** 567 Colorado Avenue: [open](https://www.carbondalerec.com/recreation-center/hours-of-operation/) Monday to Thursday 6 am–9 pm, Friday 6 am–7 pm, Saturday 8 am–5 pm and Sunday 10 am–4 pm; closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The [climbing wall](https://www.carbondalerec.com/recreation-center/climbing-wall/) comes with a day pass; climbers 14 and under climb with a certified climber 18 or older.
- **The galleries** on Main Street and 4th Street.
- **The Powers Art Center,** 13110 Highway 82, about a mile past the Highway 133 light from Glenwood: [closed through November](https://www.powersartcenter.org/planyourvisit), then open from December 1, Tuesday to Friday 11 am–5 pm and Saturday 11 am–3 pm. Admission and parking are free, with restrooms inside.
- **Closed this winter:** the Town's [ice rinks](https://www.carbondalerec.com/gus-darien-arena/ice-arena/), which have no opening date, and the [Aquatics Center](https://www.carbondalerec.com/aquatics/), until spring 2027.

## Coming by bus

The VelociRFTA BRT and the Valley Local stop at the Carbondale Park & Ride. From 5:02 am to 9:16 pm the Local does not go into downtown, so change to the Circulator.

- **Winter schedule:** [November 23, 2026, to April 18, 2027](https://www.rfta.com/wp-content/uploads/RFTA_Bus_Schedules_Winter_VelociRFTA_BRT.pdf), daily. A midday BRT takes about 15 minutes from Glenwood's 27th Street Station and about 50 from Rubey Park in Aspen.
- **Fares:** [exact cash](https://www.rfta.com/wp-content/uploads/RFTA_Bus_Schedules_Winter_Riders_Guide.pdf), $2 from Glenwood Springs and $4 from Aspen, or 25 percent less in the RFTA Tickets app. Seniors 65 and older and children 5 and under ride free; ages 6 to 18 pay $1.
- **Bikes:** none on the buses from late November to early April.

## Dogs

- **On the trail:** on a leash, and never between Rock Bottom Ranch and Catherine Bridge.
- **In Town parks:** not allowed, except at Glassier Park, Weant Boulevard and Glassier Drive; Hendrick Dog Park, Holland Drive at Hendrick Road, with a fenced run; and Delaney Nature Park.
