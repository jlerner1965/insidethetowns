Park free on Sackett Avenue across from Riverside Park and walk: the paved Riverwalk, the playground and amphitheater, the Salida Whitewater Park by the boat ramp, then up F Street into the old downtown. Allow about 90 minutes. For a half day, follow the paved Monarch Spur Trail west from the river, 3.9 miles there and back on the state's trail database, or add the library and the museum.

This is for anyone with a morning or an afternoon in Salida, with children or a dog. Every lot named here is free and the river paths are paved. The one public restroom on the route with an official listing is at the Whitewater Park, by the boat ramp.

## Where to park

The City's [downtown parking map](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/74d89f7783ac4efb8a358ad6a23707dd), last updated in September 2025, shows free lots and three-hour streets and no paid parking:

| Lot | Where | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Sackett Avenue | Across from Riverside Park | Free; four ADA spaces south of E Street; EV chargers at the playground |
| Headwaters lot | N. G Street and Sackett Avenue, by the original skatepark | Free and paved, with two EV chargers |
| Boat Ramp lot | East of Sackett Avenue, south of the SteamPlant | Free |
| F Street bridge | The unpaved areas north-west and south-east of the bridge | Free |
| Monarch Spur lot | W. 1st Street between N. I and G streets, south of the Alta station | Free; at the trail, for the half day |

Most of the downtown streets around F Street are three-hour parking. The City asks drivers not to park in the bridge area on F Street.

## The 90-minute loop

1. **Riverside Park.** From Sackett Avenue, the park's paved [Riverwalk](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/salida-riverwalk-20907), 0.4 mile, runs along the Arkansas; leashed dogs are allowed. The park has the amphitheater built in 2005 and a playground, [FIBArk's pages](https://www.fibark.com/history) say, and spectator seating along the bank from 2003.
2. **The Salida Whitewater Park.** At the Salida Boat Ramp, by the SteamPlant at 220 W. Sackett Avenue. The City manages it, and [Colorado Parks and Wildlife](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-salida-vallie) lists a restroom, changing facilities and picnic tables there.
3. **The visitor center.** The [Arkansas Headwaters Recreation Area](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area) center at G Street and Sackett Avenue sells state park passes and fishing licenses, Monday to Friday, 8 am–5 pm.
4. **F Street.** Walk back past Riverside Park to the F Street bridge and up F Street into the historic downtown, then return along Sackett.

## Restrooms and places to sit

- **Restroom:** Colorado Parks and Wildlife lists a [restroom and changing facilities](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-salida-vallie) at the Salida Whitewater Park, by the boat ramp. It gives no hours or season.
- **Seats by the river:** spectator seating along the bank, built in 2003, and the amphitheater in Riverside Park, built in 2005, [FIBArk says](https://www.fibark.com/history).
- **Picnic tables:** at the Whitewater Park, with grills, CPW lists.
- **Indoors:** the Salida Regional Library, 405 E Street, a block off the river and open every day (hours under Half a day).

## Half a day

- **The Monarch Spur Trail.** Beyond the Riverwalk the paved [Monarch Spur](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/monarch-spur-21032), 1.8 miles, and the [Milk Run](https://trails.colorado.gov/trails/milk-run-21031), half a mile, carry on out of town. The state's trail database gives the [whole route](https://trails.colorado.gov/routes/167226) from downtown as 3.9 miles there and back, paved, gentle and wheelchair-friendly. All three paths allow leashed dogs and are managed by the City.
- **The Salida Regional Library,** 405 E Street: open every day, [9 am–7 pm](https://www.salidalibrary.org/) Monday to Friday, 9 am–5:30 pm Saturday and 1–5 pm Sunday. Its fall storytime, for ages 0 to 6, is on Wednesdays at 10 am.
- **The Salida Museum,** 406½ W. Rainbow Boulevard: [Friday to Sunday, noon to 4 pm](https://www.salidamuseum.org/); $5 for adults, $3 for seniors, $1 for children.

## With children

- **The playground** is in Riverside Park, by the Riverwalk.
- **Storytime** for ages 0 to 6 is at the library on [Wednesdays at 10 am](https://www.salidalibrary.org/events/).
- **The Salida Museum** charges $1 for children, Friday to Sunday.

The Arkansas is cold and fast. Colorado Parks and Wildlife's [rules](https://cpw.state.co.us/sites/default/files/dam/iv4cywcl7k/ahra-state-park-brochure.pdf) say anyone under 13 swimming in it must wear a life jacket, and children under 12 must wear one at all times on any paddleboard, boat or kayak. It warns that even calm sections have powerful currents, and asks parents to watch children closely near the water. Late May and early June are usually when [runoff is highest](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-river-sections).

## With a dog

The paved river paths allow dogs on a leash, on the state's trail database. The [Chamber says](https://salidachamber.org/dog-friendly-salida/) Salida keeps its parks for children, with "Dogs Not Allowed in Park" signs, and that dogs may come along the river, on sidewalks and on hiking trails on a leash; Loyal Duke's Dog Park on Holman Avenue is the place for them off the leash.

## Before you go

- **Parking:** the City's [downtown parking map](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/74d89f7783ac4efb8a358ad6a23707dd).
- **The river:** the gauge reading on [Salida's page](/salida/) and CPW's [Arkansas Headwaters](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area) pages.
- **Weekdays and weekends:** the visitor center is closed at weekends, and the museum opens only Friday to Sunday.
