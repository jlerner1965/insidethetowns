You can do Crested Butte without a car. Mountain Express, the free bus between the town and Mt. Crested Butte, runs every 15 minutes from 7:10 am to midnight from November 25, 2026, to April 4, 2027, and every 40 minutes until then. Parking in town is free: two hours downtown, and long-term in the public lots. At the ski area the Main Lot costs $20 Monday to Thursday and $30 Friday to Sunday and on holidays, and two lots are free.

This is for anyone staying in town or on the mountain this winter. The ski area plans to open on November 25, the date [Vail Resorts announced](https://news.vailresorts.com/2026-08-18-Is-Winter-About-to-Show-Off-Vail-Resorts-Announces-2026-27-Opening-Dates-as-Super-El-Nino-Builds-Anticipation-for-Great-Powder) in August, subject to weather and conditions.

## The free bus

[Mountain Express](https://www.mtnexp.org/) charges no fare and runs 365 days a year. Its fall schedule, every 40 minutes from 7:35 am to 11:20 pm, runs through November 24. From November 25 the [winter Town Bus](https://www.mtnexp.org/schedules-routes/winter-2/town-shuttle/) loops through town and up to the mountain every 15 minutes:

| Stop | Leaves at | First and last bus |
|---|---|---|
| Old Town Hall | :10, :25, :40, :55 | 7:10 am to 11:40 pm |
| Whiterock and 2nd | :11, :26, :41, :56 | 7:11 am to 11:41 pm |
| 6th and Belleview | :14, :29, :44, :59 | 7:14 am to 11:44 pm |
| 4-Way, toward the mountain | :00, :15, :30, :45 | 7:15 am to 11:45 pm |
| Mountaineer Square, Mt. Crested Butte | :00, :15, :30, :45 | 7:30 am to midnight |
| 4-Way, back into town | :06, :21, :36, :51 | 7:36 am to 11:36 pm |

The bus stops only at its stops. The ones on Whiterock Avenue at 3rd, 4th and 5th streets are on request: tell the driver. Every bus is wheelchair accessible.

On the mountain, [FirstTracks](https://www.mtnexp.org/firsttracks/) vans give free rides on request to and from the base area from November 25 to April 4, from 7:15 am to 12:15 am on the winter map, by app or at 970-713-4070. They also reach the Snodgrass trailhead.

## Dogs, skis and strollers

- **Skis and snowboards** ride inside; [hold them](https://www.mtnexp.org/riding-the-bus/) during the trip. Bikes do not go inside.
- **Strollers** go on if they fold.
- **Dogs** are welcome on Mountain Express if they are [registered](https://www.mtnexp.org/dogsonbus/) with the Town, Mt. Crested Butte or your own town, on a leash and in a muzzle; free soft muzzles are on every bus. Bring proof of registration. On a crowded bus you may be asked to wait for the next.

## From Gunnison and the airport

The Gunnison Valley RTA's [free bus](https://www.gunnisonvalleyrta.com/gunnison---crested-butte-bus-information) runs from Gunnison to the 4-Way stop in Crested Butte and the Mt. Crested Butte Transit Center. It [does not serve the airport](https://www.gunnisonvalleyrta.com/free-bus-schedule), carries only carry-on luggage, and allows no animals but service animals. Its winter 2026–27 timetable is not yet posted; real-time arrivals are in the Transit app.

The Gunnison–Crested Butte Regional Airport is [28 miles from town](https://www.gunnisonvalleyrta.com/gunnison-valley-air-service-information). Alpine Express runs a shuttle that needs a reservation, and private shuttles can be booked. Uber cannot pick up or drop off at the airport: it has not signed the county's operating agreement.

## Parking in town

[Park Crested Butte](https://www.parkcrestedbutte.com/townofcrestedbutte), the Town's parking operator, sets out the rules:

- **Downtown:** free for two hours, and nobody may park on Elk Avenue longer than that.
- **Public lots:** free, long-term.
- **East end, December 19, 2026, to April 7, 2027:** Elk Avenue from the tennis courts to Seventh Street, and Teocalli and Butte avenues from 5th to Seventh, need a permit, with no overnight parking except for residents of the street. Short-term-rental guests can ask Park Crested Butte for a permit by email.
- **Fines:** a winter parking ticket is [$50](https://www.parkcrestedbutte.com/violations).

The Town's own winter rules still apply. Public restrooms are at the 4-Way parking lot and at the old Town Hall, 2nd and Elk Avenue, the [Land Trust says](https://cblandtrust.org/lower-loop-recreation-area/).

## Parking at the ski area

| Lot | Cost | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Main Lot, 32 Emmons Road | $20 Monday–Thursday, $30 Friday–Sunday and holidays, 7 am–3 pm | Pay at the station or by phone; no overnight parking |
| Snowmass Lot, Snowmass Road south of The Plaza | Free | Unpaved; no overnight parking |
| Matterhorn Lot, Gothic Road north of the Main Lot | Free | No overnight parking |

A 2026–27 season parking pass is $500 and does not promise a space. [Park Crested Butte](https://www.parkcrestedbutte.com/crestedbuttemountainresort) points out the other way up: park in town and take the free bus.

## A day without skiing

- **Crested Butte Nordic,** 620 2nd Street, by the Whiterock and 2nd bus stop: the building is [open daily](https://www.cbnordic.org/contact) from 9 am to about 5 pm. A [trail pass](https://www.cbnordic.org/trail-passes) for its 55 km of groomed trails is $30 a day for adults, $10 for 18 and under and $10 for a dog. The trails stay closed to all uses until grooming starts.
- **Free trails:** about 10 km at Town Ranch and on the Rec Path need no pass, for skiing, snowshoeing, walking and fat biking, with your dog; on the Rec Path, on a leash.
- **The Old Rock Library,** 504 Maroon Avenue, a block off Elk in an 1883 sandstone schoolhouse: [open](https://www.gunnisoncountylibraries.org/crested-butte-library/) Monday to Friday 10 am–6 pm, Saturday 10 am–4 pm and Sunday 1–5 pm.
