No train goes to Silverton until 2027: the 2026 Silverton season ended on November 1, and the railroad has not published next year's dates. From November 20 the Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad runs two winter trains from its depot at 479 Main Avenue: the [Cascade Canyon Winter Train](https://durangotrain.com/excursions/cascade-canyon-winter-train/) by day, on select dates to April 30, 2027, and [The Polar Express](https://durangotrain.com/events/the-polar-express-train-ride/) in the evening, to January 2, 2027. Book online or on 970-247-2733, and park in the railroad's lot at 209 West College Drive: $10 a car in 2026, and $6 for The Polar Express.

The flood of November 1 washed out about 300 feet of roadbed north of the Hermosa Creek bridge. In its [November 12 update](https://durangotrain.com/travel-update/) the railroad said it was on schedule to run both winter trains by November 20.

## The Cascade Canyon Winter Train

This winter it runs November 20–25 and 27–29, and December 5, 6, 11–13, 18–24 and 26–31; the [railroad's page](https://durangotrain.com/excursions/cascade-canyon-winter-train/) lists the dates from January to April. One train a day leaves at 9:15 am and reaches Cascade Canyon at 11:15, where there is a fireside area. It leaves at 12:15 pm and is back in Durango at 2:30.

- **Fares** are round trip per person and include the 8 percent Historic Preservation Fee. Children under 4 ride free on a lap. The other cars cost $134 to $215, and some are for adults 21 and over.
- **Food:** a concession car with hot and cold drinks and snacks. Boxed lunches must be ordered at least a day ahead, at checkout or by phone.
- **Be seated 30 minutes before departure,** or the railroad [may give your seats](https://durangotrain.com/ride-us/important-information/) to people waiting.

## The Polar Express

The railroad's 2026 calendar, on its [event page](https://durangotrain.com/events/the-polar-express-train-ride/), sets the departures:

| Nights | Departures |
|---|---|
| Most nights | 4:55, 6:30 and 8 pm |
| Saturdays December 5, 12 and 19, and every night December 20–24 | 4:35, 6, 7:35 and 9:10 pm |
| December 14 and 15 | 4:55 and 6:30 pm |
| No train | Thanksgiving, November 30, December 1, 2, 7 and 8, and Christmas Day |

Cars come in coach, deluxe and first class, all heated and decorated. Prices run in three tiers, value, standard and peak, set by the night. The [FAQ](https://durangotrain.com/the-polar-express-faqs/) says to book once your date is fixed, because some times and cars sell out quickly, though there are often seats on the day; the widest choice is [before Christmas](https://durangotrain.com/ride-us/winter/).

- **Arrive** 20 minutes before departure to see the train steam in, and no earlier than half an hour before. Final boarding is 10 minutes before.
- **Restrooms** are on the train, with family-size portable restrooms next to the museum.
- **Wheelchairs:** one coach has a hydraulic lift.

## Booking, changes and refunds

- **Book** online at durangotrain.com or on 970-247-2733 or 877-872-4607. The card is charged when you book, every seat is assigned, and a ticket on your phone is [all you need](https://durangotrain.com/faqs/).
- **Changes and cancellations** are by phone only; the online system cannot make them.
- **Regular trips:** a cancellation fee of $14 an adult and $7 a child, no refund within 5 days of the trip, and none for no-shows.
- **The Polar Express:** a refund less the same fees up to 60 days before; after that, none. You can move to another date in the same calendar year, before your departure, for $7 a ticket. The railroad makes no exceptions for weather, illness or road closures.
- **Weather:** the trains run as scheduled except in severe weather or bad track conditions.
- **Wheelchair seating:** the fleet has one wheelchair-accessible car; book it ahead on 877-872-4607.
- **Dogs:** service animals only. The railroad asks you not to leave a pet in a parked car; Durango has pet boarding.

## Where to park

| Where | Cost | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| [Railroad lot](https://durangotrain.com/dsngrr-parking/), 209 West College Drive, at U.S. 550 | In 2026, $10 a car and $20 an RV or trailer; $6 for The Polar Express | Pay online, by phone or by card at the lot; a ticket for each day; no overnight camping |
| [City meters](https://www.durangoco.gov/332/Parking) downtown | Free after 6 pm on weekdays, at weekends and on holidays | Coins, a card ($1 minimum) or ParkMobile; no parking on the street from 2 to 5 am |
| [Transit Center lot](https://www.durangoco.gov/1889/Municipal-Lots), 250 West 8th Street | $5 a day on weekdays; free after 5 pm and at weekends | Double-length spaces for RVs |

The railroad keeps an unloading zone on Main Avenue near the depot, so you can drop your party before parking. The City's holidays with free meters include Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day, and a disability placard parks free at any meter except the 30-minute ones.

## By bus

- **The trolley** runs up Main Avenue from the Transit Center to North Animas View Drive [every 20 minutes](https://www.durangoco.gov/348/Routes-Maps) from 7 am. The last one leaves the Transit Center at 8:40 pm. It is [$1 a ride](https://www.durangoco.gov/347/Rates) in exact change, or $3 for a day; children 5 and under ride free.
- **In the evening,** [DuranGO! microtransit](https://www.durangoco.gov/1724/DuranGO-Microtransit) picks up at the curb from 5 pm to midnight, $2 a ride, booked and paid by card in its app.
- **No buses** on Thanksgiving, the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day or New Year's Day, the [City says](https://www.durangoco.gov/354/No-Service-Days).
- **Pets** are not allowed on Durango Transit; service animals are.

## The summer train to Silverton

In 2026 the [Silverton train](https://durangotrain.com/ride-us/) ran from May 2 to November 1: 3½ hours each way over 45 miles, with about two hours in Silverton, 9¼ hours in all. The 2027 dates, times and fares are not yet published. The railroad is selling [2027 season passes](https://durangotrain.com/hot-deals/), $350 for adults and $320 for children, through May 31, 2027.

## If the train is sold out

- **The railroad museum,** at the south end of the roundhouse by the depot: [free](https://durangotrain.com/dsng-museum/), open 10 am–4 pm every day the train runs and Wednesday to Sunday otherwise; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.
- **The Animas River Trail:** the City's paved path, [nearly 7 miles](https://www.durangoco.gov/555/Trails) along the river, through town and past downtown. Dogs stay [on a leash](https://www.durangoco.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14089/ART-Orientation), and bikes keep under 10 mph.
- **Santa Rita Park,** on the river and the trail in south Durango: [restrooms all year](https://www.durangoco.gov/553/Parks).
