The honest list of campgrounds in Carbon Valley has one name on it. St. Vrain State Park is in Firestone, it is open all year, and it is the reason this page exists. The other two entries are places to plug in an RV in Longmont, included because someone will ask.

None of the three towns lists a campground or overnight RV parking in its own parks.

## What to know about St. Vrain this fall

Half of it is shut. The park is repaving the south campground road, and [its own notice](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/st-vrain-state-park) says the full-hookup sites, numbers 42 to 87, have been unavailable since October 13, 2026. That leaves the electric loops on the north side. The park has not said when the south side reopens.

The other thing is the pass. A campsite reservation does not cover the vehicle: the park requires a vehicle pass on top.

## Where you cannot camp

- [Union Reservoir](https://longmontcolorado.gov/union-reservoir-nature-area/activities-at-union/), Longmont: the City says camping is not available. The old campsites were turned into day-use space in 2018.
- Adams County's parks, Riverdale Regional Park in Brighton among them: [the county's rules](https://adamscountyco.gov/our-county/parks-open-space-cultural-arts/rules-and-regulations/) make overnight camping unlawful, except RV permits issued for a county event.
