Berthoud has no campground of its own. The Town's [reservoir park](https://berthoud.org/facilities/facility/details/Berthoud-Reservoir-Park-Open-Space-17) lists trails, fishing and parking, and no camping. What it has is three county reservoirs stacked up the foothills to the west, and a state park to the north in Loveland. All four are open every month of the year, which is not true of much camping in Colorado.

## One county, one set of rules

Carter Lake, Flatiron and Pinewood are all run by Larimer County, so they share a price table, a booking site and a rulebook. Three things catch people out.

The camping fee is not the whole price. Every vehicle also needs a [daily entrance permit](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/permits), and each trip carries a reservation fee.

You cannot drive up, pick a site and pay later. The county's [policy](https://www.larimercamping.com/article.do?assetGuid=e028b92d-fc6d-4890-a30c-5964a3ba32b0) is a reservation and a permit before you set up, though both can be bought the same day, including by a QR code at the site.

Rates change with the night. A weekend night costs more than a weekday and a holiday more again, from April through September.

Boyd Lake is the state's, with its own pass and its own booking site.
