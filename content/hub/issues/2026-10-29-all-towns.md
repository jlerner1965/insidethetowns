## New on the guides

- **Erie** [Bridge Refill Market](https://insideerie.com/places/bridge-refill-market/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — A zero-waste refill shop on Cheesman Street: bring a jar for cleaning, laundry and bath products, and buy bulk spices, grains, snacks and teas by weight.
- **Erie** [Cristos Coffee](https://insideerie.com/places/cristos-coffee/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — A coffee shop on South Briggs Street that opens at 6 on weekdays, with espresso, pastries and its own whole-bean bags, roasted with Silver Canyon Coffee in Boulder.
- **Golden** [Adorne Artisan Jewelry & Goods](https://insidegolden.com/places/adorne-artisan-jewelry-and-goods/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Jewelry and gift shop on Washington Avenue, home to Sarah DeAngelo Jewelry, which is made in the studio on site, and to goods from independent makers. Also does permanent jewelry. Open daily 10 am to 6 pm.
- **Golden** [Alyth Active](https://insidegolden.com/places/alyth-active/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Women-owned activewear and athleisure brand, designed in Colorado, with a store on Washington Avenue. Bottoms, tops, sports bras and maternity wear for women, plus a men’s line.
- **Golden** [Bentgate Mountaineering](https://insidegolden.com/places/bentgate-mountaineering/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Climbing, backcountry ski and bike shop on Washington Avenue since 1994, with rentals and demos, bootfitting, a ski tune shop and bike service. Closed Saturdays.
- **Golden** [Bivy Beer Bar](https://insidegolden.com/places/bivy-beer-bar/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Beer bar on 12th Street with 20 rotating taps of Colorado and out-of-state craft beer, Coors always on, plus wine, cocktails and a menu of salads, hot dogs and brats. Trivia, bingo and story nights. Closed Mondays.
- **Golden** [The Buffalo Rose](https://insidegolden.com/places/buffalo-rose/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Bar, restaurant and concert venue at Washington Avenue and 12th Street, under the Welcome to Golden arch, on a site in use since 1859. Weekend brunch, a Sky Bar under a retractable glass roof, and shows in its event center.
- **Golden** [Gold Mine Cupcakes](https://insidegolden.com/places/gold-mine-cupcakes/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Cupcake shop on 12th Street, baking from scratch each day: eight everyday flavors plus seasonal, gluten-free and vegan cupcakes. Closed Tuesdays, and it closes early if it sells out.
- **Golden** [Golden Pantry & Thrift](https://insidegolden.com/places/golden-pantry-and-thrift/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Nonprofit thrift store on Ford Street, open to the public on weekdays, selling secondhand clothing and home goods. The proceeds fund the group’s food pantry and crisis help for Golden neighbors.
- **Golden** [Golden Sweets](https://insidegolden.com/places/golden-sweets/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Family-owned soda fountain, ice cream and chocolate shop on Washington Avenue, with more than 40 flavors of locally made ice cream, malts and shakes, chocolates and nostalgic candy. Open daily 11 am to 9 pm.
- **Golden** [Miners Alley Performing Arts Center](https://insidegolden.com/places/miners-alley-performing-arts-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Professional theater in the old Meyer Hardware building near 11th and Washington: mainstage plays, a young audiences season and a lobby bar, with no seat more than six rows from the stage.
- **Golden** [Miners Saloon](https://insidegolden.com/places/miners-saloon/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Saloon on Miners Alley styled after Golden’s mining days, with 24 taps, more than 50 beers in bottles and cans, craft cocktails and wine, and a menu of platters, small plates and paninis. Open until midnight Fridays and Saturdays.
- **Golden** [Snarf’s Sandwiches](https://insidegolden.com/places/snarfs-sandwiches/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — The downtown Golden shop of the Snarf’s sandwich chain, on Washington Avenue next to Woody’s Pizza. Open daily 10 am to 7 pm, with a gluten-free menu, patio seating, catering, pickup and delivery.
- **Golden** [Thai Gold](https://insidegolden.com/places/thai-gold/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Thai restaurant on 14th Street downtown: curries, noodles, fried rice and soups, most of which can be made vegetarian, vegan or gluten free. Closed between lunch and dinner on weekdays; open all day on weekends.
- **Golden** [The Golden Diner](https://insidegolden.com/places/the-golden-diner/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Breakfast diner on 12th Street since 2009, with a scratch kitchen: pancakes, omelets and scrambles alongside a braised pork Benedict and a Colorado loco moco. Open daily from 7 am to about 2 pm.
- **Golden** [Toad&Co Golden](https://insidegolden.com/places/toad-and-co-golden/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Family-owned partner store of the Toad&Co clothing brand on Washington Avenue, established in 2017, with the brand’s women’s and men’s clothing, footwear and accessories.
- **Golden** [Truly BoHotique](https://insidegolden.com/places/truly-bohotique/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — Women’s clothing boutique at Washington Avenue and 14th Street, open since 2014 and stocked mainly with woman-owned brands: apparel, shoes, jewelry, accessories, beauty and wellness products, and gifts.
- **Johnstown** [Animal Crackers Feed](https://insidejohnstown.com/places/animal-crackers-feed/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — The feed and pet-food store on Parish Avenue, inside the Ace Hardware: food for dogs, cats, chickens, wild birds and small animals.
- **Johnstown** [Wing Shack](https://insidejohnstown.com/places/wing-shack-johnstown/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — The Northern Colorado wing chain's Old Town shop on North Parish Avenue: wings in the house sauces, fries and sides, with online ordering.
- **Lyons** [Creative Grounds](https://insidelyons.com/places/creative-grounds/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-10-29) — A nonprofit café, pottery studio and hangout for young people at the east end of Main: Ozo coffee, house-made syrups, pastries and sandwiches, and pottery classes through December.
