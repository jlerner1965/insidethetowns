## New on the guide

- [Acupuncture & Natural Medicine Center](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/acupuncture-and-natural-medicine-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — An acupuncture and natural medicine practice at 426 Fifth Street in old-town Frederick, listed in the Town’s business directory; hours are not published there.
- [Carbon Valley Active Adults & Community Center](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/carbon-valley-community-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The recreation district’s center for adults 55 and over, on Grant Avenue in old Firestone: weekday drop-in coffee, cards and crafts, talks, day trips, special events and the Friendly Fork lunch on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- [Carbon Valley Gymnastics Center](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/carbon-valley-gymnastics-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The recreation district’s gymnastics building on Frederick Way: a competition floor, an in-ground tumble track into a foam pit and preschool equipment, with classes from age 2 and open gym on Friday mornings and Saturday middays.
- [Carbon Valley Help Center](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/carbon-valley-help-center/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The Carbon Valley’s nonprofit food pantry and assistance office, on Buchanan Avenue in old Firestone, open to the public on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9 to 3; it takes volunteers and donations and runs the November Empty Bowls lunch at Frederick High.
- [Carbon Valley Regional Library](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/carbon-valley-regional-library/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — Frederick’s library, a High Plains Library District branch on Park Avenue in Firestone: open seven days, with storytimes most mornings, teen and adult clubs, two meeting rooms, four study rooms and a vending cafe.
- [Coalridge Park](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/coalridge-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — A neighborhood park in the Coal Ridge subdivision west of downtown: a playground and swings, a multi-use field, benches and picnic tables. The Town publishes no park hours.
- [Confluence Park](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/confluence-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — A small park on Drake Way in the newer neighborhoods east of Colorado Boulevard, with picnic shelters and tables. The Town publishes no park hours.
- [Countryside Park](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/countryside-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — A neighborhood park in the Countryside subdivision: a playground and swings, a multi-use field, picnic shelters, tables and benches. The Town publishes no park hours.
- [Erie Coffee Roasters: ECR Cafe](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/erie-coffee-roasters-ecr-cafe/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — A micro-roaster and cafe in Frederick (the name notwithstanding), roasting fair-trade coffee on site and serving espresso, pour-overs and focaccia sandwiches made in house, weekday mornings and Saturday.
- [Frederick-Firestone Fire District](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/frederick-firestone-fire-district/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The fire and EMS district for Frederick, Firestone and the county around them, headquartered on Kosmerl Place; non-emergency 303-833-2742. It runs CPR, car-seat and babysitting classes, firehouse story times and the December Santa Run.
- [Frederick Police Department](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/frederick-police-department/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The Town’s police station on Fifth Street: 720-382-5700 for the department, 720-652-4222 for non-emergencies and 911 for emergencies. The lobby, open weekdays 8 to 5, is where pet licenses, Santa Cops tags and the prescription drop box live.
- [Frederick Skate Park](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/frederick-skate-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — About 30,000 square feet of poured concrete at the south-west corner of the Frederick Recreation Area: an 18,500-square-foot street course, an egg bowl, a 10,000-square-foot flow bowl and two 12-foot full pipes, unsupervised and lit, open dawn to 10 pm.
- [Frederick Town Hall](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/frederick-town-hall/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The Town’s offices on Locust Street, a block off Fifth Street: the Town Clerk, the Board of Trustees’ chambers and the front counter, weekdays 8 to 5. Utility billing is at 311 Fifth Street.
- [Mirror Image Brewing](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/mirror-image-brewing/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — A small brewery and pizza kitchen on Iris Parkway off Colorado Boulevard, with a beer garden and a September music festival of its own; open from 3 pm on weekdays and from noon or 11 at weekends.
- [Southwest Weld County Services Complex](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/southwest-weld-county-services-complex/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — Weld County’s south-west office, on County Road 24 1/2 just west of I-25 near Del Camino: the county elections office’s Longmont-area branch, a Voter Service and Polling Center and a 24-hour ballot drop box, and the county building closest to Frederick.
- [St. Vrain State Park](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/st-vrain-state-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — Colorado Parks and Wildlife’s pond-dotted park just west of I-25 at Exit 240, a few minutes from Frederick: 689 acres of land and 228 of water, 87 campsites, seven miles of trail, fishing and bald eagles in winter. A daily vehicle pass is $10 to $12 for Colorado plates.
- [Ziggi’s Coffee](https://insidefrederickco.com/places/ziggis-coffee-frederick/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-05) — The Colorado coffee chain’s drive-thru on Colorado 52 west of Colorado Boulevard: espresso drinks, blenders and smoothies, teas, sodas, locally made breakfast and lunch items and a kids’ menu. Hours are not on the location page.
