## New on the guide

- [Esh's Grocery Market](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/eshs-grocery-market/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A discount grocery store on Pointe Plaza Drive selling at 40 to 60 percent off retail, with new deals every day.
- [Lil Monsters Playground](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/lil-monsters-playground/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — An indoor playground for children up to 12: $15 for all-day play, one grown-up free with each child, socks required.
- [Main Event](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/main-event-windsor/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The indoor entertainment center on Fairgrounds Avenue: bowling, an arcade, laser tag, virtual reality and billiards, with food and a 3–6 pm happy hour.
- [PizzaVino](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/pizzavino-windsor/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Rustic Italian pizza and a long wine list on Fairgrounds Avenue, with a dine-in happy hour Monday to Thursday 3 to 5 and Friday to Sunday 11 to 4.
- [Roma](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/roma-windsor/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A full-service Italian restaurant on Main Street for pizza and pasta, with a daily drink special and delivery in Windsor.
- [Safeway](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/safeway-windsor/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Windsor's Safeway on west Main Street at 11th Street, open every day from 6 am to 11 pm, with a pharmacy, a cake shop and pickup or delivery orders.
- [Twin Staves](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/twin-staves/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A whiskey-forward cocktail lounge on 4th Street, from the people behind Ridge River and Larado whiskeys, with a list of mocktails; open Tuesday to Saturday from 4.
- [Windsor Ace Hardware](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/windsor-ace-hardware/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The locally and family-owned hardware store on west Main Street, with 45,000 items in stock; open seven days.
- [Windsor Community Playhouse](https://insidewindsorco.com/places/windsor-community-playhouse/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Windsor's community theater on East Garden Drive, staging a season of plays with Friday and Saturday evening shows and Sunday matinees; single and season tickets.
