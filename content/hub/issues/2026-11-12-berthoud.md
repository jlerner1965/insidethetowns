## New on the guide

- [Berthoud Animal Hospital](https://insideberthoud.com/places/berthoud-animal-hospital/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A full-service vet clinic for dogs and cats on Mountain Avenue: wellness exams, vaccinations, dental care, spay and neuter, surgery and emergency consultations, weekdays 8 to 5:30.
- [Coyote Ridge Veterinary Clinic](https://insideberthoud.com/places/coyote-ridge-veterinary-clinic/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A vet clinic on Grand Market Avenue that takes new clients, keeps same-day appointments open and sees sick and urgent cases; open Saturday mornings.
- [Domino's Pizza](https://insideberthoud.com/places/dominos-pizza/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The Domino's on Grand Market Avenue for pizza delivery and carryout, open until 11 pm, and midnight on Friday and Saturday.
- [Forbici Barber Werx](https://insideberthoud.com/places/forbici-barber-werx/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A barber on Mountain Avenue that books online: haircuts, skin fades, beard trims, straight-razor shaves, kids' cuts and women's cuts.
- [Hays Market](https://insideberthoud.com/places/hays-market/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Berthoud's family-run grocery store on West Mountain Avenue, open every day from 6 am to 9 pm; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Easter.
- [Jessie's Pub and Sports Bar](https://insideberthoud.com/places/jessies-pub-and-sports-bar/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A pub and sports bar on the main block of Mountain Avenue, with cocktails, a happy hour, two pool tables and sports on eight TVs.
- [Muddy Mutt](https://insideberthoud.com/places/muddy-mutt/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Dog grooming by appointment on Mountain Avenue, seven days a week from 8 am to 7 pm; the entrance is down the alley at the back.
- [O'Reilly Auto Parts](https://insideberthoud.com/places/oreilly-auto-parts/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The auto parts store on West Mountain Avenue, with free battery, alternator and starter testing; open every day.
- [Osito's](https://insideberthoud.com/places/ositos/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The newest restaurant downtown, on 5th Street in the space La Casita had.
- [Sirenhouse](https://insideberthoud.com/places/sirenhouse/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A hair salon on Mountain Avenue downtown for cuts, color, extensions, curly cuts and nails, by appointment; free haircuts for anyone going through chemotherapy.
- [SIX Barbershop](https://insideberthoud.com/places/six-barbershop/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A barbershop for men and boys on Mountain Avenue: cuts, beard and mustache trims and straight-razor shaves, booked online or by phone, with walk-ins when a chair is free.
- [The Learning Experience](https://insideberthoud.com/places/the-learning-experience-berthoud/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A child care center and preschool on Lake Avenue for children from six weeks to five years, open weekdays from 6:30 am to 6 pm.
