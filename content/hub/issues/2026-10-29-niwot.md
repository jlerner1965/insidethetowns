**Inside Niwot** · October 29, 2026

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, and ballots must be received by 7 pm; postmarks don't count. The incorporation questions are on the ballot for registered voters inside the proposed boundary. Boulder County offices are closed Tuesday, but the Elections Division and the vote centers stay open. Saturday morning is the Great Pumpkin Party in Old Town, 10 am to 2 pm.
