**Inside Lyons** · October 29, 2026

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3. The Lyons Regional Library is the vote center, 7 am to 7 pm, and the county's ballot drop box now sits in front of the library rather than Town Hall; ballots must be in by 7 pm. On Wednesday the Boulder Philharmonic plays Country Music: Explored at the Wildflower Pavilion, doors at 6 pm. The library's story time is on a break through November 11.
