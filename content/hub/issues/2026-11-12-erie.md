## New on the guide

- [Abo's Pizza](https://insideerie.com/places/abos-pizza/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — New York-style pizza on Austin Avenue from a Front Range pizzeria founded in Boulder in 1977, with delivery, carry-out and catering.
- [Boulder Valley Velodrome](https://insideerie.com/places/boulder-valley-velodrome/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A 250-metre wooden cycling track, one of two in the country that meet Olympic standards, restored and running again with daily training, weekly racing, pickleball courts and family events.
- [Clayton Park](https://insideerie.com/places/clayton-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — An 8.7-acre park on Highlands Circle with a big playground (a hill slide and a zipline), two ballfields, a pickleball and tennis court, a shelter and walking trails.
- [Coal Miner Park](https://insideerie.com/places/coal-miner-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A small park near downtown on Cheesman Street with a large picnic shelter and a sand volleyball court; its playground is being replaced until late November 2026.
- [Colorado National Golf Club](https://insideerie.com/places/colorado-national-golf-club/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A public championship golf course on Vista Parkway, home course of the University of Colorado's golf teams, with Master's Restaurant open for lunch and dinner seven days a week.
- [Colorado Railbike Adventures](https://insideerie.com/places/colorado-railbike-adventures/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Pedal-powered railbikes on the 4.2-mile Boulder Valley Mine Route, with sunset, weekday-morning and Christmas rides and a shorter Haunted History ride in the fall; booked online, priced by day and time.
- [Columbine Mine Park](https://insideerie.com/places/columbine-mine-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A 9.2-acre park on Skyline Drive with four outdoor pickleball courts for drop-in play or reservations, a ballfield, a multi-purpose field and a shelter. No playground.
- [Country Fields Park](https://insideerie.com/places/country-fields-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A 22.2-acre park on Tynan Drive with two ballfields, a cricket pitch in the native grass, a playground, a shelter and walking trails.
- [Crescent Park](https://insideerie.com/places/crescent-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — An 8-acre park on Lombardi Street whose playground has a challenge course, with a multi-purpose field, a shelter, walking trails and a permanent restroom in season.
- [Fair Winds Hot Air Balloon Flights](https://insideerie.com/places/fair-winds-hot-air-balloon-flights/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Hot air balloon flights from a launch site on US 287, daily in season from May through November, for private parties and groups of any size; booked online.
- [Ironstone Park](https://insideerie.com/places/ironstone-park/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — A 9.3-acre park in Colliers Hill with a large playground, a loop trail, two shelters, a full basketball court, a sand volleyball court, lawn games and open turf fields.
- [Mountain Kids Erie](https://insideerie.com/places/mountain-kids-erie/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — The Erie gym of Mountain Kids, a Louisville gymnastics and dance school: gymnastics and ninja classes, camps and birthday parties for children.
- [Si Señor! Real Mexican Food](https://insideerie.com/places/si-senor/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-11-12) — Locally owned Mexican restaurant on Village Vista Drive serving breakfast, lunch and dinner, with a daily happy hour from 2 to 5.
