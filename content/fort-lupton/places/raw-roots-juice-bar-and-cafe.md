A Natural Way wellness center, at 333 Denver Avenue, lists Raw Roots as its café. The menu runs from smoothies and smoothie bowls, fresh juices, coffee, espresso and tea to breakfast, pizzas, paninis, sandwiches, salads, soups and desserts, and the café makes its organic sourdough breads, pizzas, cinnamon rolls and naturally sweetened treats in-house. Vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free and no-refined-sugar choices are marked on the menu; smoothies start at $6.95. It is closed Sunday and Monday and closes early on Friday and Saturday.
