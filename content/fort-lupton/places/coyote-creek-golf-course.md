Coyote Creek is owned and operated by the City of Fort Lupton and open to the public: 18 holes, par 71, 6,412 yards, laid out by Matt Eccles with large greens, wide fairways, several lakes and multiple sets of tees. The clubhouse has a pro shop, snack bar and dining room, and there is a grass driving range and a tournament pavilion; the course runs men’s and ladies’ associations, a summer junior golf academy and outings. The course posts summer hours of 5:45 am to 8:30 pm and winter hours of 9 am to 4 pm, daily; tee times are booked online or at 303-857-6152 (course home page, https://www.coyotecreekgc.com/).
