Coyote Creek is owned and operated by the City of Fort Lupton and open to the public: 18 holes, par 71, 6,412 yards, laid out by Matt Eccles with large greens, wide fairways, several lakes and multiple sets of tees. The clubhouse has a pro shop, snack bar and dining room, and there is a grass driving range and a tournament pavilion; the course runs men’s and ladies’ associations, a summer junior golf academy and outings. The course’s contact page posts summer hours of 5:45 am to 8:30 pm and winter hours of 9 am to 4 pm, daily, and on 10 October 2026 its home page (https://www.coyotecreekgc.com/) gave the winter hours, Monday to Sunday 9 am to 4 pm, as the pro shop’s current hours; tee times are booked online or at 303-857-6152.

The course page (https://www.coyotecreekgc.com/golf/course, read 10 October 2026) sets a dress code: collared shirts for men, with no cutoffs or other athletic shorts, collared or sleeved shirts for women, and no tank tops for anyone. Besides the blue, white, gold and red tees, from 6,412 down to 5,166 yards, it has junior and family tees with their own scorecard.
