Cannery Park is a small City of Fort Lupton park, 0.4 acres at Fifth Street and Fulton Avenue. The city lists a playground, picnic tables, benches and a drinking fountain; it is free and open sunrise to sunset, and Parks & Recreation answers at 303-857-4200.
