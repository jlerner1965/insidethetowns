The City of Fort Lupton lists Community Center Park at the Recreation Center’s address as an 11.2-acre free park open sunrise to sunset. Its listed features are a skate park, a playground, a soccer field and other playing fields, a bike trail and walking path, a pavilion, picnic shelters and tables, restrooms, a drinking fountain and parking; the shelters can be reserved (call 303-857-4200). The Fort Lupton Public & School Library keeps a StoryWalk picture-book trail here, and its news posts name the book currently on display (city news, https://www.fortluptonco.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=652). The city’s park rules (https://www.fortluptonco.gov/1017/Parks) ask for dogs on a 10-foot leash and ban alcohol, glass, fires and motor vehicles.
