Railroad Park is one of the City of Fort Lupton’s larger parks: 8.6 acres that the city describes as running from Highway 52 north to Ninth Street, with its listed address at Fourth Street and Pacific Avenue. The city lists a baseball and softball field, a soccer field and other playing fields, a basketball court, sand volleyball, horseshoe pits, a playground, a bike trail and pedestrian path, and picnic areas with shelters, tables and benches. It is free and open sunrise to sunset; Parks & Recreation answers at 303-857-4200.
