House of Smoke began in 1973, when Gordon Barsness and his family, who had come to Fort Lupton from Iowa, bought their first smoker and started smoking turkeys and chickens; son Jim took over in 1981, and the business still works from its original building, now tripled in size (https://houseofsmokeinc.com/about). It lists more than three hundred items, from bison, elk, venison, wild boar and smoked turkeys to thirty kinds of wild-game sausage and jerky, and ships coast to coast. The deli (https://houseofsmokeinc.com/deli/) sells sandwiches, meat trays and burger specials, a wild boar and Hatch chile burger on Wednesdays and a bison or elk burger on Fridays. It is open weekdays only, 8 am to 3 pm.
