The library is a City of Fort Lupton department, and its pages link to the High Plains Library District and the Weld RE-8 School District. Beyond books it offers a makerspace, a Library of Things, technology and printing, downloads and streaming, and community and study rooms that can be reserved; its autumn program news lists after-school Level Up! sessions, Cereal, Centers & Stories mornings for elementary-age children, a family Healthy Plate cooking class and Lotería. It opens 9 am to 8 pm Monday to Thursday and 9 to 5 on Friday and Saturday, closed Sunday. The 2026 closure list on the hours page includes Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday and Christmas Day, with 5 pm closings on November 25 and December 24 and 31.
