Lancaster Park is a 13-acre City of Fort Lupton park on Lancaster Avenue, northeast of Longview Drive. The city lists basketball, a playground, a walking path, picnic tables and benches, with free admission and hours of sunrise to sunset. The city’s general park rules (https://www.fortluptonco.gov/1017/Parks) ask for dogs on a 10-foot leash and ban alcohol and glass.
