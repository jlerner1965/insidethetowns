The City of Fort Lupton lists Pearson Park as 13 acres on Highway 52, west of Highway 85. Its features, by the city’s list, are a baseball and softball field and other playing fields, a playground, restrooms, a walking path and bike trail, open space, a scenic overlook, picnic tables and benches. The park’s own page gives no hours; the city’s general park rules (https://www.fortluptonco.gov/1017/Parks) open all parks from dawn to dusk and ask for dogs on a 10-foot leash.
