The City of Fort Lupton calls Denver Avenue its historic main street and points to its many early-20th-century commercial buildings; the city keeps its own register of designated historic properties, and a Historic Preservation Board meets on the first Thursday of the month. One storefront with a published history is the St. John building at 410 Denver Avenue, an 1889 mercantile now home to Wholly Stromboli (https://www.whollystromboli.com/our-story/). Along the same stretch are Lucky Shot Sports Bar & Grill at 327, A Natural Way at 333 and Raw Roots Juice Bar & Cafe at 337. The City Museum at 453 First Street, in the 1929 former public library, is included on the city’s Fort Lupton Historic Tour.
