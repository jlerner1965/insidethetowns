Salt & Acres sits on County Road 25 north of Highway 52, about 12 miles east of I-25. Its Tasting Room serves a seasonal food and drink menu with the property’s own Ampersands Wines label (https://saltandacres.com/pages/the-tasting-room), with brunch from 10 am to 1 pm on Saturday and Sunday; tables can be reserved on OpenTable, and fire pits by email or text. It is open Thursday evening and Friday to Sunday, last food orders an hour before closing, and says private and curated events sometimes change its hours, so it suggests calling ahead or booking on OpenTable. It also runs a shop, hosts weddings and private parties, and holds Secret Supper paired dinners and, in October 2026, a harvest market.
