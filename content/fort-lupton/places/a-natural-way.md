A Natural Way is a holistic wellness practice and shop led by traditional naturopath Julie A. DeVisser. The shop side carries organic vitamins, herbs and essential oils, Nature’s Sunshine products and its own mushroom coffee, mocha and tea; the practice offers services from nutritional counseling and herbal medicine to biofeedback scans (https://anaturalwayco.com/). Its café, Raw Roots Juice Bar & Cafe, is at 337 Denver Avenue.
