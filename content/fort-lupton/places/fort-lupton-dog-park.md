The City of Fort Lupton’s dog park is at 12285 Highway 52, free and open sunrise to sunset. Besides the dog park itself, the city’s listing for the site names open space, trails and a walking path, restrooms, a playground, a baseball and softball field, picnic tables and benches. The city’s general park rules (https://www.fortluptonco.gov/1017/Parks) ask owners to clean up after their dogs.
