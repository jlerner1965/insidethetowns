The South Platte Valley Historical Society has built a history park of about 100 acres along the South Platte River, a quarter mile west of Highway 85 off County Road 14½ on Historic Park Way. Its centerpiece is a reconstruction of the trade fort Lancaster Lupton, a former U.S. Dragoons lieutenant, began in 1836 to trade with the Arapaho and Cheyenne; volunteers started rebuilding it in 2003 and dedicated it in 2011 (https://www.spvhs.org/luptons-fort.html). A path leads on to a restored 1850s trapper cabin, the Independence School of 1875 and the Donelson homestead house of the 1860s (https://www.spvhs.org/the-grounds.html). Members get in free and the Society suggests a $5 donation for visitors 13 and over; it also stages rendezvous and other events through the year, and a new visitor center with restrooms is due to open this fall.
