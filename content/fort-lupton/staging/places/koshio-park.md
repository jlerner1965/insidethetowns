The City of Fort Lupton is rebuilding Sam Koshio Memorial Park, a 2.1-acre park at First Street and McKinley Avenue, after 17 bilingual community meetings on the design. The plans include an ADA-compliant playground with a zipline and inclusive play equipment, a large community pavilion, a walking trail, renovated restrooms, more shade and seating, bilingual signage, better lighting and new landscaping; the existing gazebo is being kept. Funding partners are The Great Outdoors Fund, Oxy, the Colorado Health Foundation and the Weld Community Foundation.
