The Fort Lupton Recreation Department opened the 48,000-square-foot Recreation/Community Center in 2004, and it won Recreation Management’s Innovative Design Award in 2005. It has a 4,060-square-foot zero-depth-entry leisure pool with a lazy river, lap lanes, a 139-foot water slide, a vortex pool and a hot tub; a 27-foot rock-climbing spiral; an elevated two-lane running track around the gym; a 3,600-square-foot fitness center, an aerobics room and a cardio balcony with spin bikes; and a teen center. Community Center Park, with its skate park and fields, shares its South Harrison Avenue address.

Re-read on 10 October 2026: the Fall 2026 Activity Guide gives building hours of Monday to Friday 5 am to 9 pm and Saturday and Sunday 8 am to 5 pm, and pool hours of Monday to Friday 5:30 am to 8:30 pm, Saturday 8 am to 4:30 pm and Sunday 11:30 am to 4:30 pm, with open swim (slide and play features on) from 11:30 am on weekdays. Its 2026 holiday list closes the center on Thanksgiving (November 26 and 27), Christmas Day and New Year’s Day 2027, with 5 pm closings on December 24 and 31; the About Us page’s own holiday panel still says November 28/29 for Thanksgiving. The About Us page sets the age rules: 10 and over may use the facility without supervision, 7 to 9 need someone 12 or over in the building, 6 and under need direct supervision, and the weight and cardio areas are for 15 and over (13 and 14 with an adult). The packet’s description (leisure pool, fitness spaces, running track, climbing feature, classes and parties) agrees with these pages; the listing waits only on the Friday closing.
