Whether you’re taking your kids to the Trick-or-Treat Trail, admiring the Día de los Muertos altar set up by the library, enjoying the live dancers, or hopping on the kiddie train or hayrides at dusk, there’s so much to enjoy at Pumpkinfest at the Fort on Saturday, October 10! Come enjoy this free event from 3:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. 
 
- Día de los Muertos Display by the Fort Lupton Public & School Library: Visitors are welcome to bring a disposable, non-returnable photo of a departed loved one to place on the display. 
- Fort Tours provided by the South Platte Valley Historical Society. 
- Hayrides at dusk courtesy of Willy Taylor and family. 
- Live Dance Performance at 5:15 p.m. 
- Food trucks available for dinner purchase.
