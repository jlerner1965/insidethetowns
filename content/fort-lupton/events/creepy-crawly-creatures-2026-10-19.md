A hands-on hour with live snakes and spiders in the library's community room, for elementary-age children and the adults who bring them: meet the animals up close and look at real molts and shed skins under magnifying lenses. It is that week's session of the library's weekly Level Up! program for children, and part of its Spooktacular October.
