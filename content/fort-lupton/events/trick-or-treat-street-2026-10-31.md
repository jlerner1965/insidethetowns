The Chamber of Commerce's annual Halloween trick-or-treating downtown, from 4 to 6 pm on Denver Avenue and 4th Street, where local businesses hand out candy from booths and car trunks. There are costume contests for children, adults and pets, and a contest for the decorated booths.
