A week before Christmas: shops on Second Avenue and Cottonwood Square stay open until 8 pm on a Thursday, for a gift you can take home that night and dinner or a drink on the way. Free. The Business Association's page names the businesses open that evening: Basin Design + Build, Cimmini's, Few of a Kind, Fortezza, Fritz Family Brewers, Inkberry Books, Little Bird, Love Ice Cream, the Niwot Inn & Spa, Niwot Market, Niwot Natural Medicine (until 7 pm), the Old Oak Coffeehouse, Osmosis Gallery, The Wheel House and Wise Buys Antiques. The last Stroll of the season is Monday, December 21.
