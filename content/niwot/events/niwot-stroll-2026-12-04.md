The first Niwot Stroll of the season falls on the First Friday Art Walk: shops on Second Avenue and Cottonwood Square stay open until 8 pm for holiday shopping, with the art walk along the way. Free. The Business Association's page names the businesses open that evening: Basin Design + Build, Cimmini's, Few of a Kind, Fortezza, Fritz Family Brewers, Inkberry Books, Little Bird, Love Ice Cream, the Niwot Inn & Spa, Niwot Market, the Old Oak Coffeehouse, Osmosis Gallery, The Wheel House and Wise Buys Antiques. Three more Stroll evenings follow, each 5 to 8 pm: [Thursday, December 10](/events/niwot-stroll-2026-12-10/), [Thursday, December 17](/events/niwot-stroll-2026-12-17/) and [Monday, December 21](/events/niwot-stroll-2026-12-21/).
