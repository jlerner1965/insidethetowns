The first Niwot Stroll of the season falls on the First Friday Art Walk: shops on Second Avenue and Cottonwood Square stay open until 8 pm for holiday shopping, with the art walk along the way. Free. The Business Association's page names the businesses open that evening: Basin Design + Build, Cimmini's, Few of a Kind, Fortezza, Fritz Family Brewers, Inkberry Books, Little Bird, Love Ice Cream, the Niwot Inn & Spa, Niwot Market, the Old Oak Coffeehouse, Osmosis Gallery, The Wheel House and Wise Buys Antiques. More Stroll evenings are planned for Thursday, December 10, Thursday, December 17 and Monday, December 21; the page gives no times for those yet.
