An afternoon open house held by the Niwot Cultural Arts Association in support of its project to restore Eddie Running Wolf’s carved tree sculptures and to build a tipi-style canopy to protect them. Learn about the carvings and the plan, and how to give. The Association’s site is the source for the date and time; it does not list a cost.
