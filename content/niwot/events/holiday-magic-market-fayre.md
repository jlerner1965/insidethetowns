Holiday shopping with local businesses and pop-up vendors at Niwot Hall and around town, with Santa and Mrs. Claus from 1 to 3, live music and carriage rides. The Niwot Business Association's page says the market is held at Niwot Hall on the 5th and the 12th of December, from 10 to 4. Its calendar carries Saturday, December 5, and Niwot Hall's calendar lists the Holiday Market on both Saturdays, December 5 and December 12, 10 am to 4 pm.
