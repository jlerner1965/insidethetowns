Monthly evening art walk run by osmosis art & architecture with the Niwot Cultural Arts Association. Shops across Old Town and Cottonwood Square stay open late with art displays and refreshments, and free craft stations run for children aged five to twelve. Most businesses open from 5 pm; closing times vary.
