Tuesday-night trivia at the taproom on the corner of Second Avenue. The Business Association calendar lists it weekly, 6:30 to 8:30 pm, and The Wheel House's own calendar lists it every Tuesday through December 29, the holiday weeks included (from 6:30 pm; the bar's entries show an hour). Check the bar's calendar in a holiday week before you count on it.
