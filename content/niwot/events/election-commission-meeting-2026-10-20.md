The court-appointed commission that administers the November 3 incorporation and charter commission election meets at Niwot Hall. The hall's calendar lists the meeting for Tuesday, October 20 from 2 to 3 pm and describes it as a public meeting for observation only. The commission's own meetings page adds meetings as they are noticed and had not yet posted this one when this listing was checked, so confirm there before setting out.

The commission publishes the ballot content and procedure and takes no position on the question; Boulder County's Clerk and Recorder runs the coordinated election itself.
