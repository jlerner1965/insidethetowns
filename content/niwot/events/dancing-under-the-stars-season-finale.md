The Niwot Business Association’s free Friday-night dance series closes its 2026 season. A free lesson at 7 pm, then social dancing with a DJ from 7:45 pm, outdoors in Cottonwood Square. The series has run every Friday since June 12; the organizer’s schedule gives September 18 as the last night.
