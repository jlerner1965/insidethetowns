Monthly gathering of Niwot Community Connection, a group for people 50 and older, downstairs at Niwot Hall: an update on local news and the group's interest groups, then a presentation by Genevieve Barnett, a licensed wildlife rehabilitator, on Colorado bat species, the threats they face and how to live alongside them. Free and open to the public. The hall's calendar lists Thursday, October 8 and books the room from 9 am to noon; the program runs 10 to 11:30. The next gathering is listed for [November 12](/events/niwot-community-connection-2026-11-12/).
