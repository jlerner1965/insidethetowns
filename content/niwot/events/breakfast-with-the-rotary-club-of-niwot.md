Thursday-morning meeting of the Rotary Club of Niwot at Niwot Hall, 7:30 to 8:30 am, with a guest speaker and complimentary breakfast and coffee. The club meets on the first, second and third Thursdays of the month and welcomes members of the community as guests. Its own calendar lists club meetings on October 8 and October 15; October 22 is an evening club social and October 29 a committee meeting at Old Oak Coffeehouse, so neither is shown here. The November breakfasts have [their own listing](/events/breakfast-with-the-rotary-club-of-niwot-november/).

On October 8 the speaker is Kathy Trauner of the Niwot Cultural Arts Association, on the return of Niwot's tree carvings.
