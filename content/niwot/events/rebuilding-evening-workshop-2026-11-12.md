A 90-minute introductory evening with Rebuilding Seminars, the Niwot-based program for people recovering from divorce or the end of a relationship: Thursday, November 12, 6:30 to 8 pm at 195 Second Avenue, the organizer's own address and Niwot Hall's. Owner, therapist and facilitator Bob Manthy walks through the Rebuilding blocks of relationship recovery and what the ten-week Rebuilding Seminar offers, which next runs on Sunday evenings in Niwot from January 17 to March 21, with tools to take away and use at once. The organizer's enrollment page prices the evening at $30; enroll there or call 720-600-2763.
