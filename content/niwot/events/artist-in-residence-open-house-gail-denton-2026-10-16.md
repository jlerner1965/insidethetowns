Open house with artist-in-residence Gail Denton and other artists at Niwot Hall: a public showing of Denton's plein air and studio paintings, with Denton demonstrating her painting process and serving refreshments. Each piece is for sale, with 30 percent of proceeds going to Niwot Hall. The hall's calendar lists Friday, October 16 from 4 to 7 pm.
