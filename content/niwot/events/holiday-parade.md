Niwot's Annual Holiday Parade is on Saturday, November 28, 2026, starting at 11 am on Second Avenue, with Santa in the parade and at the Grange afterward until 1 pm. It is the morning after Enchanted Evening, when the community tree is lit.

## Schedule

- 11 am: the parade starts.
- After the parade, until 1 pm: Santa is in the Grange, now called Niwot Hall, to take children's Christmas wish lists.

The Niwot Business Association's calendar gives Saturday, November 28, 11 am to 1 pm. The description on the same page still says "Nov 29th", which is a Sunday this year; the calendar date is the one to go by.

## Route and where to watch

The parade marches down Second Avenue from Murray Street to Niwot Road. Anywhere along Second Avenue between the two is on the route. The Business Association lists the location as downtown Niwot, Second Avenue and Cottonwood Square.

## Before you go

The listing names no charge or ticket to watch. It does not give road closure times, parking, a weather plan or how to enter the parade. The Niwot Business Association runs the event, and its contact page is at niwot.com/contact.

The night before, Friday, November 27, is [Enchanted Evening](/events/enchanted-evening/), from 6 to 9 pm.
