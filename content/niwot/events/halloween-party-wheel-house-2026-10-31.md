The Wheel House's first Halloween party, sponsored by Suerte Tequila, runs through the day in two halves. The kids' party is noon to 4 pm, with face painting from noon to 2, piñatas and a kids' costume contest at 4. The adult party starts at 5, with a Suerte tequila promotion from 6 to 8, a raffle, themed cocktails and an adult costume contest at 8:30. Wendy Woo, a Colorado singer-songwriter with her own band, plays from 7 to 10 pm.
