The Niwot Trot is Niwot Elementary's 5K run and walk through downtown Niwot, and the 17th annual race was held on Sunday, October 4, 2026, at 9 am from Whistle Stop Park. The school's Parent Teacher Advisory Council (PTAC) runs it, and anyone can enter to run, walk, stroll or trot. The PTAC has not posted a date for 2027.

## The course

A 3.1-mile loop on the back roads and gravel paths of downtown Niwot, which the PTAC calls fast and flat. The 2026 race used the same course as 2025. Race miles count toward the 100 Mile Club.

## Race morning in 2026

There was free coffee before and after the race and free ice cream sandwiches from Cold Stone Creamery at the finish. The race ended with an awards ceremony: medals for every youth finisher, awards for the overall male and female winners and for the fastest boy and girl in each grade, and a raffle.

## Parking

The PTAC says parking is extremely limited and asks people to walk, bike or carpool. There is no parking on Murray Street between First and Second avenues or along First Avenue.

## Signing up and questions

Sign-up for 2026 was online, through the link on the PTAC's race page. Sponsorship sales closed on September 5, and the PTAC asks businesses that want to sponsor next year's race to get in touch. Questions go to the race director at niwottrot@niwotptac.org.
