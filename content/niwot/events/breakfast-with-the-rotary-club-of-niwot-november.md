The Rotary Club of Niwot's Thursday-morning meetings at Niwot Hall in November, 7:30 to 8:30 am, with a light breakfast included: a club business meeting on November 5, then club meetings with a speaker on November 12 and 19. The club meets on the first three Thursdays of the month and welcomes guests from the community. November 26 is a committee meeting at Old Oak Coffeehouse, not a club breakfast. Dates are from the club's own calendar.
