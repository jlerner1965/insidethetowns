Closing open house for painter Gail Denton's residency at Niwot Hall, with her plein air and studio paintings on show, a painting demonstration and refreshments. Each piece is for sale, with 30 percent of proceeds going to Niwot Hall. The hall's calendar lists Saturday, October 17 from 2 to 5 pm; the Courier's Hall column gave 2 to 4 in one place, so come early.
