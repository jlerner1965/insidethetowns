Niwot’s morning-long Independence Day celebration in Old Town and Cottonwood Square: free family activities including a fun run and bike decorating, and the parade. The Niwot Business Association’s calendar lists Sunday, July 4, 2027, from 7:30 am to noon, but its description still refers to the 2026 edition (America’s 250th birthday) and the Niwot Mile registration it links is 2026’s. Treat the time and program as last year’s until the organizer publishes the 2027 details.
