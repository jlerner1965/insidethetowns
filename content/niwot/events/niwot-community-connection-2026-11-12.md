Monthly morning gathering of Niwot Community Connection, a group for people 50 and older, downstairs at Niwot Hall: local news, updates from the group's interest groups and a guest presentation. Free and open to the public. Niwot Hall's calendar lists Thursday, November 12 and books the room from 9 am to noon; the October session ran 10 to 11:30, and the program for November had not been announced when this listing was checked.
