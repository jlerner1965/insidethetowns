Monthly morning gathering of Niwot Community Connection, a group for people 50 and older, downstairs at Niwot Hall: local news, updates from the group's interest groups and a guest presentation. Membership is free and open to anyone in the Niwot area over 50. Niwot Hall's calendar lists Thursday, November 12, downstairs, 9 am to noon.
