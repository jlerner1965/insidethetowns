The last Niwot Stroll of 2026: shops on Second Avenue and Cottonwood Square stay open until 8 pm on the Monday before Christmas for one more evening of holiday shopping in Old Town. Free. The Business Association's page names the businesses open that evening: Basin Design + Build, Few of a Kind, Fortezza, Fritz Family Brewers, Inkberry Books, Little Bird, Love Ice Cream, the Niwot Inn & Spa, Niwot Market, the Old Oak Coffeehouse, Osmosis Gallery, The Wheel House and Wise Buys Antiques.
