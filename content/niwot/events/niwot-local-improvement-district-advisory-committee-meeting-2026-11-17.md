November's meeting of the advisory committee for the Niwot Local Improvement District is on the third Tuesday. The County Commissioners' advance agenda for November 9 to 20 lists it for Tuesday, November 17 at 7 pm at the Niwot Inn, 342 Second Avenue. The county's page for the district says members of the public are invited to attend the committee's meetings; for the agenda, email the county's staff liaison, Mark Ruzzin, at mruzzin@bouldercounty.gov.
