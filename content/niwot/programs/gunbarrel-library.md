Gunbarrel Library is Boulder Public Library's branch at 6500 Lookout Road, open Monday to Thursday 9 am to 7 pm, Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday noon to 5, with free parking; its phone is 303-441-3100. The library's Storytimes link goes to its [events calendar, filtered to storytimes](https://calendar.boulderlibrary.org/events/?r=days&n=7&t=%255B%2522Storytime%2522%255D).
