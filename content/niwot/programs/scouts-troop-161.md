The hall's calendar lists Troop 161 on Monday evenings, and the Left Hand Valley Courier has reported for years on the troop's Eagle Scouts, its fiftieth anniversary and the girls' troop 161G the Grange approved in 2021. The meeting time is the hall's booking, and the way in is to ask the Grange.
