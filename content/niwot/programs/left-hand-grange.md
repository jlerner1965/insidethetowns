[Niwot Hall](/places/niwot-hall/) is the Left Hand Grange hall, and the Grange is the membership organization behind it: its join page says new members are always welcome and essential to keeping the Grange a strong part of Niwot, with a National Grange benefits package on top. The hall's calendar is the clearest picture of the town's week, and not everything on it is public: a line marked "Private" is a rental, "Rotary Club of Niwot" is the club's own meeting with guests by its rules, and the [hall's monthly meeting](/events/niwot-hall-board-meeting-2026-11-11/) on the second Wednesday is the Grange's business meeting. The Niwot Hall Next Century Project is the Grange's plan to revitalize the building; its site gives a donate page but no current status.
