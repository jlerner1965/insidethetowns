St. Vrain's Community Schools run licensed care in the school building. The **Niwot Cougar Club** takes children from five years old through fifth grade before and after school, 7 am to 6 pm, at $16 a morning or $20 an afternoon, or $32 and $40 for a drop-in day. **Pre-K care** takes 3- to 5-year-olds from 7:30 am to 5:30 pm, at $45 a day for a child in St. Vrain's preschool and $55 for one who is not, with a two-day weekly minimum. The registration fee is $50 for one child or $75 for a family.

**School days off.** Both programs are closed on non-school days. Community Schools runs care on non-school days at a few schools across the district, aiming for one each in Longmont, Erie and the Frederick and Firestone area, and the school's page says the details come from the program manager about a month before each one, so ask before a day off rather than assume. The district's [2026–27 calendar](https://svvsd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/26-27-SVVSD-4_8_26-2.pdf) has the days off, which are also in the list on this page. On the district's late-start Wednesdays (November 4 and December 2 this fall, when classes begin two and a half hours late) the program runs special care for its families at $30 a child.
