Colorado Shines, run by the Colorado Department of Early Childhood, is the state's quality rating system for early learning programs, and its Find a Program search, on the home page, looks for child care by a work or home address and a distance. The site says to contact a program directly, or to call the Colorado Shines Child Care Referral Line at 877-338-2273 for more information.
