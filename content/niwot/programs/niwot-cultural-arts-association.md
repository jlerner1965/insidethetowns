Formed in 2009 to fund and organize the arts in Niwot: it built and keeps the [Children's Park](/places/niwot-childrens-park/) and [Whistle Stop Park](/places/whistle-stop-park/), runs the [Sculpture Park](/places/niwot-sculpture-park/) and the Rock & Rails concerts, and sponsors the Why Not Niwot? show. Its current project is a tipi-style canopy for the restored tree carvings, with a $275,000 goal and a [donation page](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/eddie-running-wolf-tree-carving-restoration-and-relocation). The next public date it is part of is the [First Friday Art Walk](/events/first-friday-art-walk-2026-12-04/) on December 4. Its site does not publish a membership or a volunteer list, so a first inquiry goes through the contact form.
