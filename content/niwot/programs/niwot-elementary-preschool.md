St. Vrain Valley Schools runs preschool at 25 of its elementary schools, Niwot Elementary among them, for 3- and 4-year-olds. The district's 2026–27 location list gives Niwot a morning session from 9 to 11:40 am and an afternoon session from 12:55 to 3:35 pm; it lists no full-day session at Niwot. Every classroom, the district says, is led by a licensed teacher with a bachelor's degree in early childhood education.

The district's rates for 2026–27 are $640 a month for part-time preschool. Colorado's Universal Preschool program pays for up to 15 hours a week for a child who was four on or before October 1 of the school year, and its application is part of St. Vrain's own; placements are first come, first served. Children who are not yet ready for a classroom, or who need a longer day, can use the school's licensed pre-K care, which is listed separately on this page under the parents' section. Sources: the district's [preschool page](https://www.svvsd.org/departments/early-childhood/preschool/) and [2026–27 registration and locations](https://www.svvsd.org/departments/early-childhood/preschool/2026-2027-preschool-registration/), and its [2026–27 calendar](https://svvsd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/26-27-SVVSD-4_8_26-2.pdf) for the first day of preschool, all read October 26, 2026.
