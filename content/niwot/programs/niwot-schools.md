The neighborhood schools are Niwot Elementary, Sunset Middle School in Longmont and Niwot High; the district's [maps and boundaries](https://www.svvsd.org/schools/maps-boundaries/) page and its school options sites say which address goes where, and open enrollment across the district is common. Niwot High offers tours every Tuesday and Thursday at 1:30, scheduled by phone, and has an [Education Foundation and Booster Club](https://nhs.svvsd.org/about/education-foundation-booster-club/). The Cougars' fall seasons, football, soccer, volleyball, softball and cross country, are on [the sports page](/sports/), read from MaxPreps. The 2026–27 calendar: Thanksgiving break November 23–27, winter break December 21 to January 1, spring break March 15–19.
