An all-volunteer nonprofit of households, founded in 1990, that speaks for residents to Boulder County and runs National Night Out, the veterans' banners and the tractor show. Membership brings quarterly updates on community issues, surveys, the May cleanup day for junk, yard waste and electronics, and invitations to the Fourth of July parade and the ice cream socials. The board meets on the second Wednesday of the month at 7 pm at [Niwot Hall](/places/niwot-hall/), and its meetings page says members are encouraged to attend and the public is welcome; to speak or put something on the agenda, contact the board a week ahead. Its site had not posted the November date when this was checked.
