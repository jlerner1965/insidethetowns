A Boulder County improvement district funded by a 1% sales tax in the business area; the advisory committee recommends spending to the County Commissioners, who are its board. Its October 6 meeting approved $5,885 toward the Great Pumpkin Party and $9,201 for the holiday lighting, by the Left Hand Valley Courier's account. The county's page lists the committee on the first Tuesday of the month at 7 pm at the Niwot Inn, 342 Second Avenue, and says each meeting is confirmed on the Commissioners' two-week advance agenda; the agenda for November 2 to 13 lists no Niwot LID meeting. The next listed meeting is December 1.
