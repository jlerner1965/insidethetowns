A Boulder County improvement district funded by a 1% sales tax in the business area; the advisory committee recommends spending to the County Commissioners, who are its board. Its October 6 meeting approved $5,885 toward the Great Pumpkin Party and $9,201 for the holiday lighting, by the Left Hand Valley Courier's account. The county's calendar still lists the [November meeting on November 3](/events/niwot-local-improvement-district-advisory-committee-meeting-2026-11-03/), Election Day; the Courier reported the committee moving it to November 17, and the county's page had not changed when this was checked, so check the Commissioners' agenda before going.
