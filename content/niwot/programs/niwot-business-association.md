A members' organization for businesses in Niwot rather than a club a resident joins, but the one that puts on the events residents turn up to: the [Great Pumpkin Party](/events/great-pumpkin-party-2026/) on October 31, [Enchanted Evening](/events/enchanted-evening-2026/) the day after Thanksgiving, the [Holiday Parade](/events/holiday-parade-2026/) and the December [Holiday Magic Market Fayre](/events/holiday-magic-market-fayre-2026/). Its [monthly meeting](/events/niwot-business-association-monthly-meeting-2026-11-10/) is on the hall's calendar; the site does not say whether non-members may sit in. Member benefits include the directory listing, ribbon cuttings and discounted sponsorship of Rock & Rails.
