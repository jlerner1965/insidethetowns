The Left Hand Water District says it will be closed for the Thanksgiving holiday from 12 noon on Wednesday, November 25, through Friday, November 27, 2026. Its 24-hour answering service stays available for emergencies through the district's main line, 303-530-4200, and payments can be made at any time online through Xpress Bill Pay, from the Pay My Bill button on the district's site.
