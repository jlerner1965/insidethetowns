RTD's BOLT schedule for September 27, 2026 to January 2, 2027 says its services follow a Sunday/Holiday schedule on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. BOLT runs between Downtown Boulder Station and 23rd and Main in Longmont, with a stop at CO 119 and Niwot Road in both directions.
