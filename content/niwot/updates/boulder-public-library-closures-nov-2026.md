Boulder Public Library's Gunbarrel Library page, at 6500 Lookout Road, carries two closures for every location: Friday, November 20, for staff training, and Thursday, November 26, for Thanksgiving. Its regular hours are Monday to Thursday 9 am to 7 pm, Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday noon to 5, and its phone is 303-441-3100.
