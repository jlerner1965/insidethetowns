The district's page sets the window: December 1 to 15 is when applications for a school other than the neighborhood school are submitted online. Families get an approval or denial on the family dashboard by January 8, 2027, and anyone approved visits the requested school and confirms there by January 21. After the window the district keeps taking applications on a rolling basis, and a denied or late application goes on the requested school's waitlist, with an appeal available under board policy. Niwot's neighborhood schools are Niwot Elementary, Sunset Middle in Longmont and Niwot High; the guide's [Families page](/families/) has the district's maps and the 2026–27 breaks.
