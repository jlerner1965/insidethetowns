Boulder County's hours-of-operation page lists these holiday closures for county government offices: Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov. 26, and the day after, Friday, Nov. 27; Christmas Eve, Thursday, Dec. 24, closed at noon; Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25; New Year's Eve, Thursday, Dec. 31, closed at noon; and New Year's Day, Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. The county's home page also carries the Thanksgiving closure notice. General county office hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and the page notes that certain offices and departments keep different hours.
