The county's Niwot Trails page carries the notice under "Niwot Loop Trailhead and Trail Closure": later this winter, crews will repair two bridges near the Niwot Loop Trailhead, and the trailhead and trail will be closed during construction. Access to the recycling drop-off area will remain open, and repair dates will be announced when they are available. The page links a closure map. It lists two other trailheads on the Niwot Trails, Lefthand Valley Grange, with 30 parking spaces and a restroom, and Monarch, with 10.
