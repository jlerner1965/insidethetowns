In the county's first unofficial count, posted at 8:41 pm on election night, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 546 votes for and 1,466 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 666 for and 1,269 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 559 to 1,411, the property tax 531 to 1,446, the marijuana sales tax 751 to 1,236, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 632 to 1,331, and the debt authorization 585 to 1,392. Boulder County posts further unofficial counts on November 4, 5 and 6 and a ninth-day count by mid-day on November 13, runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
