In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 1:32 pm on November 13, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 738 votes for and 2,121 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 916 for and 1,813 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 743 to 2,045, the property tax 715 to 2,080, the marijuana sales tax 1,052 to 1,766, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 911 to 1,861, and the debt authorization 820 to 1,973. Boulder County's posting schedule says results typically do not change after November 13; the county runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
