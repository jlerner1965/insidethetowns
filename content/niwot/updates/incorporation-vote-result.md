In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 5:23 pm on November 4, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 641 votes for and 1,789 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 798 for and 1,540 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 651 to 1,728, the property tax 622 to 1,765, the marijuana sales tax 895 to 1,506, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 773 to 1,600, and the debt authorization 700 to 1,689. Boulder County posts further unofficial counts at 5 pm on November 5, on November 6 if needed and around mid-day on November 13, runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
