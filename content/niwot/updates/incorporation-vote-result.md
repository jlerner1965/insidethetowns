In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 5:06 pm on November 5, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 670 votes for and 1,922 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 834 for and 1,649 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 678 to 1,851, the property tax 645 to 1,893, the marijuana sales tax 947 to 1,607, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 817 to 1,701, and the debt authorization 733 to 1,804. Boulder County posts further unofficial counts on November 6 if needed and around mid-day on November 13, runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
