In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 12:59 am on November 4, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 606 votes for and 1,668 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 747 for and 1,439 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 620 to 1,611, the property tax 590 to 1,644, the marijuana sales tax 844 to 1,403, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 713 to 1,508, and the debt authorization 657 to 1,579. Boulder County posts further unofficial counts at 5 pm on November 4 and 5, on November 6 if needed and around mid-day on November 13, runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
