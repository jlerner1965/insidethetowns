In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 11:05 pm on November 6, Ballot Question 300, incorporation, had 697 votes for and 2,047 against; Question 301, the nine-member home rule charter commission, 872 for and 1,750 against; and the five fiscal issues that would have funded a town were all behind: the sales and use tax 706 to 1,968, the property tax 675 to 2,006, the marijuana sales tax 990 to 1,710, the authority to retain and spend all revenue 863 to 1,795, and the debt authorization 772 to 1,908. Boulder County updates the unofficial results by mid-day on Friday, November 13, after the deadline for curing ballots and for military and overseas ballots, runs its risk-limiting audit on November 17 and certifies the result at the canvass board's meeting on Tuesday, November 24; the Election Commission then certifies the canvassed results to the Boulder County District Court, and its FAQ says that if incorporation is not approved the process ends and no municipality is created. The guide's [election explainer](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) sets out what each question asked.
