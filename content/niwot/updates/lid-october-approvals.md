By the Courier's account of the October 6 meeting, the committee approved $5,885 of the Business Association's $7,500 request for the [Great Pumpkin Party](/events/great-pumpkin-party-2026/), covering a hayride, a magic show, a petting zoo, the haunted house, county permits and advertising, and unanimously approved $9,201 for holiday lighting, toward which the association puts $500. A third request, $17,881 toward the $21,450 Holiday Magic series, was presented without a reported decision. The committee moved its November meeting to the 17th because the first Tuesday is Election Day, and will skip December unless something comes up. The county's minutes were not yet posted when this was checked; the Courier is the record here, and the [county's page](https://bouldercounty.gov/government/boards-and-commissions/niwot-local-improvement-district/) is where the official one will appear.
