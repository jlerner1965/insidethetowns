The CO 119 Safety, Mobility and Bikeway project, under construction since September 2024 and scheduled to finish in spring 2027, builds the Diagonal Bikeway with an underpass at Niwot Road, new signals and bus rapid transit stations. This week's notices on its project page: the 63rd Street median between the two directions of CO 119 is closed to all traffic from Sunday, October 18 at 7 pm to Sunday, October 25, for signal, roadway and concrete work, with detours by CO 52 northbound and Jay Road southbound; and on Thursday, October 22 the Oxford Road and 83rd Street medians close from 7 am to 5 pm, one at a time, with southbound drivers bound for Oxford Road sent on to the Niwot Road median to turn back north.
