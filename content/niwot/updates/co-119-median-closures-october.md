The CO 119 Safety, Mobility and Bikeway project, under construction since September 2024 and scheduled to finish in spring 2027, builds the Diagonal Bikeway with an underpass at Niwot Road, new signals and bus rapid transit stations. Its project page's notices for the week of October 18: the 63rd Street median between the two directions of CO 119 was closed to all traffic from Sunday, October 18 at 7 pm to Sunday, October 25, for signal, roadway and concrete work, with detours by CO 52 northbound and Jay Road southbound; and on Thursday, October 22 the Oxford Road and 83rd Street medians closed from 7 am to 5 pm, one at a time, with southbound drivers bound for Oxford Road sent on to the Niwot Road median to turn back north.
