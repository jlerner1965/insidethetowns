## What you are moving to

Niwot is a census-designated place of about 4,300 people in Boulder County, off the Diagonal Highway roughly halfway between Boulder and Longmont. Two blocks of railroad-era brick storefronts on Second Avenue, a second cluster of shops at Cottonwood Square, neighborhoods of mostly single-family homes around them, and open space and farmland where the sidewalks stop. It has no town government, no mayor and no town hall; Boulder County and a set of special districts provide the services, and volunteer groups run the calendar. That may change: on November 3, 2026 voters inside a proposed boundary decide whether to incorporate. [The election guide](/articles/2026-incorporation-election/) explains what is on the ballot without taking a side.

## Housing

Most of Niwot is detached single-family homes on lots larger than you will find in Boulder, with a few townhome and condominium clusters near the commercial blocks. Inventory is small in a place this size, so what is for sale at any moment is a handful of listings. The links in the sidebar go to the major listing sites; we do not publish prices here because they would be out of date within a month. Property tax and any future municipal taxes are covered in the election guide.

## Schools

Niwot is in the [St. Vrain Valley School District](https://www.svvsd.org/). The neighborhood schools are Niwot Elementary on Niwot Road, Sunset Middle School in Longmont, and Niwot High School, whose campus is just south of town on 79th Street. The district’s site has boundaries, enrollment and transportation; open enrollment across the district is common, so check the current rules rather than assuming an address decides everything.

## Getting around

The Diagonal Highway (CO 119) is the road that matters: about twenty minutes to Boulder or Longmont in normal traffic, forty-five to fifty to downtown Denver, and about forty-five to Denver International Airport by way of I-25 or E-470. RTD’s BOLT bus between Boulder and Longmont stops at Niwot Road. Within Niwot, most errands are walkable or bikeable; the [LoBo Regional Trail](/places/lobo-regional-trail/) connects to both cities for anyone who commutes on two wheels.

## Water, sewer, trash, fire

Because there is no town, each of these comes from a different body, and it pays to know which one before you close.

- **Drinking water** is from the [Left Hand Water District](https://lefthandwater.gov/).
- **Sewer** is from the [Niwot Sanitation District](https://niwotsd.colorado.gov/) where its lines run, roughly seven square miles around town; elsewhere, homes are on septic. Check the address with the district.
- **Trash and recycling** are arranged household by household with a private hauler. There is no municipal service.
- **Fire and emergency medical** response is [Mountain View Fire Rescue](https://www.mvfpd.org/); law enforcement is the [Boulder County Sheriff](https://bouldercounty.gov/safety/sheriff/).
- **Roads, snow, building permits and land use** are Boulder County departments. [Who runs what in Niwot](/articles/who-runs-what-in-niwot/) links to the right page for each.

## Living here

Summer is Rock & Rails on Thursday evenings beside the caboose at [Whistle Stop Park](/places/whistle-stop-park/), Friday dancing in Cottonwood Square, and the Fourth of July parade. Fall is the Great Pumpkin Party and the Niwot Trot. The evening after Thanksgiving is Enchanted Evening, when the tree is lit and Santa arrives by sleigh; the parade is the next morning. None of it is run by a government. The [Business Association](https://niwot.com/), the [Cultural Arts Association](https://niwotarts.org/) and the [Community Association](https://niwot.org/) organize it, and the quickest way to meet people is to volunteer for one of them. ## Before you decide

Spend a Saturday here. Coffee on Second Avenue, the walk to the caboose and out along the trail toward the foothills, lunch on a patio at Cottonwood Square. [The walking-order guide](/articles/explore-niwot-in-walking-order/) is the itinerary, and it takes an afternoon.
