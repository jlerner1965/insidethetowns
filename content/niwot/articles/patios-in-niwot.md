Niwot has more outdoor seating than a town of four thousand has any right to, and most of it faces something worth looking at. Five places, and the conditions each one suits.

## For a summer evening: the Niwot Tavern

The [Niwot Tavern](/places/niwot-tavern/) has a covered patio on the corner of Cottonwood Square, facing the square and the bronze bear sculptures. The cover means a July afternoon is bearable and a passing thunderstorm is not the end of dinner. Open daily from 11 to 9.

## For a warm lunch: Raza Fresa

Next door on the square, [Raza Fresa](/places/raza-fresa/) puts its tables under umbrellas along the walkway. Dine in, take out or eat outside, which in Niwot is possible most of the year. Sunday to Wednesday 11 to 8:30, Thursday to Saturday until 9.

## For October: Cimmini's

[Cimmini's](/places/cimminis/), on the quieter 300 block of Second Avenue, has a heated front patio, which extends the outdoor season well past the point where the others have gone inside. Scratch-made Italian, open since January 2022; lunch and dinner Wednesday to Friday, brunch and dinner at the weekend. Menus and hours are on the restaurant's site.

## For a band: The Wheel House

The [Wheel House](/places/the-wheel-house/), at the west end of Second Avenue, is a taproom with a back patio, musicians on stage most nights of the week and trivia on Tuesdays. It opened in September 2020 and added its own kitchen in February 2026. Closed Mondays; from 11 Tuesday to Saturday, 3 on Sundays.

## For the dog: Fritz Family Brewers

[Fritz Family Brewers](/places/fritz-family-brewers/) is a few minutes south on 79th Street, a family brewery known for clean German-style lagers, and it takes children in the taproom and dogs on the patio. Weekdays from 3, Friday from 1, weekends from noon.

## When they are open right now

Each place's own page says whether it is open at this moment, worked out from its posted hours, and the [Eat & Drink](/eat-drink/) page has an "Open now" filter for the whole list. Hours change fastest of anything on this site; the restaurant's own page is the record.
