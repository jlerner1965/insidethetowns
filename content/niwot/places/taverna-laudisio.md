The Laudisio family, long known in Boulder, took over the two-story historic building at the west end of Second Avenue when 1914 House closed, and opened in June 2026. The cooking is straightforward family Italian. The Left Hand Valley Courier covered the opening; hours are on the restaurant’s own site.
