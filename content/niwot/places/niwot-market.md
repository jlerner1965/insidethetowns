Niwot’s grocery, independent since 2002, open every day from 7 am to 8:30 pm. Produce, the [deli counter](/places/niwot-market-deli/) for breakfast burritos and made-to-order sandwiches, prepared foods and pet supplies, plus [Sachi Sushi](/places/sachi-sushi/) at the back, [The Nook](/places/the-nook/) for gifts, and a second [Belle Terre Floral](/places/belle-terre-floral/) counter. It is in Cottonwood Square, the shopping center on Niwot Road, a walk from [Old Town](/places/old-town-second-avenue/); hours and phone are read from the market’s own site.
