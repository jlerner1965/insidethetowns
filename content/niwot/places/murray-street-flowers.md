A backyard-scale flower grower whose cart stands at the corner of First Avenue and Murray Street, across from the Children’s Park, from April to early autumn. The grower’s page says the cart closed for regular hours on October 3 and opens on occasion through the holiday season, with pop-up dates posted on Instagram; look for it again in spring.
