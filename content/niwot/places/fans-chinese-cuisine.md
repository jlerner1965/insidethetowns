The square’s Chinese kitchen, in the south-west corner next to [Niwot Wheel Works](/places/niwot-wheel-works/). Open every day from 11 am to 8:15 pm: dine in, take out, or have it delivered, and call ahead on holidays. The menu is on [its own site](https://www.fanschinesecuisine.com/). [Niwot Market](/places/niwot-market/) and the rest of Cottonwood Square are across the parking lot.
