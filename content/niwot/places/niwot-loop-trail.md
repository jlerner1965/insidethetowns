A flat, easy loop on Boulder County trails around the edge of town, starting from the county’s Niwot Loop Trailhead on 79th Street south of Niwot Road. It links to the LoBo Regional Trail and crosses the open fields east of the built-up blocks. The county’s Niwot trails map shows the connections; dog and closure rules are the county’s. (The Niwot Trot 5K, which used these trails for years, has run a different course through downtown since 2024.)
