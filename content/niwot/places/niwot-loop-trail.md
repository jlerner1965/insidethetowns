A flat, easy loop on Boulder County trails around the edge of town, starting from the county’s Niwot Loop Trailhead on 79th Street south of Niwot Road. It links to the LoBo Regional Trail and crosses the open fields east of the built-up blocks. The county’s Niwot trails map shows the connections. (The Niwot Trot 5K, which used these trails for years, has run a different course through downtown since 2024.)

The County rates all seven named Niwot trails easy, so the choice is length: Niwot Hills 0.6 miles, Cougar 0.8, 95th Street 1.0, Somerset 1.1, Overbrook 1.3, and Hidden Star and the Legend Ridge Loop 1.5 each; some marked sections of Hidden Star are closed to horses. Dogs are allowed on all seven, on a leash. Three trailheads serve them: Niwot Loop, Lefthand Valley Grange (the one with a restroom and a group shelter) and Monarch. Trailheads close at sunset and parking is not allowed overnight, though the regional trails that pass through, the LoBo among them, stay open to commuters around the clock. Rated easy is the County’s word for grade and surface, not a promise that a route is step-free or stroller-friendly.
