A movement studio in Cottonwood Square, across the parking lot from Niwot Market. Its schedule runs vinyasa, slow-flow and restorative yoga, guided and silent meditation sittings, qigong and dance, plus workshops, series, intensives, sound baths and guest teachers, and drop-ins and socials for the community around it.

Classes are booked through the studio’s own schedule, which is the record for what is on and when; nothing from it is listed here as an event. The walk-in front desk keeps its own hours, Monday 9 am to 2 pm, Tuesday 8:30 am to 2 pm, Wednesday 10:30 am to 3 pm and Thursday 9 am to 4 pm, which are not the class times.
