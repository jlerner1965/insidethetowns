A contemporary gallery in a renovated 1910 brick building on Second Avenue, showing more than forty Colorado artists and opening each new show on a First Friday Art Walk. Osmosis Architecture shares the building.

Rotating shows span ceramics, sculpture and painting. The “Why Not Niwot?” competition offers the winning local artwork as cards or prints; the website lists a 2026 winner.
