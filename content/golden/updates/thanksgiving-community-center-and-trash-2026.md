The Town's Golden Community Center page lists Thanksgiving as a closed day and gives Black Friday hours of 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26 this year, so the center is closed that day and opens at 8 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 27. The Town's trash page says 5280 Waste Solutions observes Thanksgiving Day, and a pickup that falls on the holiday is delayed one day.
