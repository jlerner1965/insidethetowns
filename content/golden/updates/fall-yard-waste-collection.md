The City runs the collection twice a year, once in spring and once in fall, for residents in its Pay-As-You-Throw program. The truck takes grass clippings, pine needles, leaves, weeds, branches, sawdust and ground or chipped wood; branches go in bundles under 4 feet long and 18 inches across, and because A1 Organics no longer accepts natural-fiber string, yarn or rope, the City says to bag branches or leave them in an organized pile without twine. The limit is 3 cubic yards a household, about a 4-foot cube or 21 kraft paper bags of up to 30 gallons. The material is composted, so no trash, plastics, glass, dirt, cactus, tree stumps, branches over 4 inches across, ashes, animal waste, treated lumber, logs, old hoses or broken flower pots, and anything loose, in plastic bags or outside the organics cart is not picked up. Questions about year-round organics collection go to 5280 Waste Solutions, 303-277-8727.
