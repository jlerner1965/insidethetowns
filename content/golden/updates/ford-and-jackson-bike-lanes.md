The City's October 20 update on its Guiding Golden project page says construction is largely complete, with the new 25 mph speed limit signs and minor punch-list items still to finish; City staff will collect data on the streets this winter and spring and post the results. The project puts a protected bike lane on Ford and Jackson streets from 14th to 24th, set off from traffic by a concrete curb with flexible posts, with ADA curb ramps along both streets, a raised intersection at Jackson and 24th and changes to the intersection at Ford and 14th. Ford stays two lanes; Jackson is two lanes except between 15th and 18th, where it narrows to one, and at 17th and Jackson a shared bus and bike lane lets RTD buses and the Ore Cart stop without blocking traffic. The speed limit on both streets between 14th and 24th drops from 30 to 25 mph. About 84 parking spaces come out on Ford and 37 on Jackson, with replacement parking behind the curb on Ford between 18th and 19th; the City's hauler uses the alleys where it can, and on Ford the buffer holds trash carts clear of the bike lane.
