The county's release says the piles are the woody debris stacked during its forest health and wildfire mitigation work, and that burning them in a controlled setting cuts the fuel for a large, unplanned wildfire and improves habitat. Trained fire crews run each burn with the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office and the local fire district, only on days with suitable weather, air quality and at least four inches of snow, and monitor it until it is declared fully out. Signs go up at the park and on variable message boards; smoke and flames may be visible during a burn and residual smoke can hang around for a short time. If visibility drops below five miles for smoke, the county says the air has reached a level that may be unhealthy. White Ranch is the one park on the list near Golden; the [White Ranch Park](/places/white-ranch-park/) page has the two trailheads and the campground's winter closure.
