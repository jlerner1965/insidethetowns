Jefferson County Parks & Open Space announced the opening of the Huntsman Trailhead in Clear Creek Canyon Park for November 6, 2026; the park's page lists it at 25651 US Highway 6, and the County's trail page puts the Huntsman Segment west of Tunnel 1. The County's release says the trailhead has expanded parking, restrooms, a shaded gathering area and wayfinding signs, and that across US 6 there are four new bridges, 1.5 miles of 10-foot-wide ADA-accessible trail and five new creek access points, with better access to bouldering and climbing areas. The same release says the trail through the canyon, formerly the Peaks to Plains Trail, is now named the Clear Creek Trail. County open space is open from one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset.
