The Golden Police Department's alert says City email addresses end in @cityofgolden.gov, and lists the other signs: a sender whose address doesn't match earlier messages from the City, a request to reply for a personalized payment link, pressure to act quickly, and advice not to call the City, your bank or the police. The City does not accept cryptocurrency at all, and the alert says anyone told to withdraw cash and convert it to cryptocurrency should stop and call their bank.
