**Serve on one.** The City fills its boards once a year: applications open near the beginning of February and close in mid-March, interviews are on the last Tuesday of March, and the Mayor appoints with a majority vote of Council. Every term ends in April. Most boards require members to live in the city, and a Golden mailing address is not proof; the City points to Jefferson County's address lookup, under "Municipal Status". The full list and each board's members are on the [Boards & Commissions page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/government/city_leadership/boards_and_commissions.php).

**Sit in.** Each board meets in public on a fixed day, most in Council Chambers. Among them: the Arts & Culture Commission on first Mondays at 6 pm, the Downtown Development Authority on third Mondays at 5:30, the Mobility & Transportation Advisory Board on fourth Thursdays at 6, the Community Sustainability Advisory Board on fourth Wednesdays at 6, and the Citizens Budget Advisory Committee at 7:30 am on third Wednesdays from March to October. Dates move for holidays or a missing quorum, so check the [Public Meetings page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/government/public_meetings.php) before going; the sustainability board's November and December meetings, for one, have both moved to December 8.
