The [Golden History Museum](/places/golden-history-museum/) is the City's own, and its volunteers sign a City of Golden volunteer application and consent to a background check, except corporate groups and other one-day helpers. The museum is recruiting **Visitor Services volunteers** for the admissions desk, one shift a week or more; after checking guests in, you may guide visitors through the galleries, research the collections or help staff with projects. **Junior Summer Camp Aides**, 13 and older, help run camp activities for at least two weeks and apply online, interview and attend a training first; the Student & Family Programs Coordinator takes questions. The museum's volunteer profiles describe people who welcome visitors and keep the [History Park](/places/golden-history-park/)'s chickens and bees.
