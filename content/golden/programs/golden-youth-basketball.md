The Golden Youth Basketball Association has fielded Golden-area teams for more than three decades, coached by volunteers. Teams register as a group through their head coach, who then emails each player an invitation; a child who is not on a team can use the **Need a Team** registration, open through October 31, and the league places as many as it can as coaches, roster space and grade levels allow. The 2026–27 season runs from November 2 to February 28.

Last season's [information page](https://www.goldenyouthbasketball.com/content/37288/202526-Winter-League-Info) describes how it works: teams for boys and girls in 3rd to 8th grade in the Jefferson County Youth Basketball Conference, organized by high-school area, with all levels of players welcome; about two practices a week in Golden-area school gyms; games Monday to Saturday, home games mostly at Tony Grampsas, with Thanksgiving week and winter break off; and tryout-only feeder teams for 7th and 8th-grade boys headed for Golden High. Players are not placed first come, first served, and returning teams may stay together; the league's [team formation policy](https://www.goldenyouthbasketball.com/content/6252/Team-Formation-Policy) explains the rest.
