**Golden Dance Arts** is the City's youth dance program, for kids and teens who dance already or want to try: ballet, tap, jazz, lyrical, hip hop and more, taught by age with a focus on technique, musicality and confidence. Families register for a whole season at a time, fall, winter/spring or summer, and the City asks them to read its refund and cancellation policy before enrolling. The Community Center's **pottery** classes run for every age from kids to seniors, on different days and at different skill levels; the instructor can say which class fits. Current class times, dates and fees are listed on the City's registration site, linked from the [programs page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/programs.php).
