[Foothills Art Center](/places/foothills-art-center/) lists its youth classes on its classes page; three are coming up, all with Amanda Pfister. The **Gingerbread House Workshop**, Saturday, December 5, decorates sturdy cardboard houses with candy, paint and craft supplies, no baking; kids 7 and up can be dropped off while parents shop at the Astor House, or families can build together. **Winter Art Escape** is two one-day classes for ages 7 to 12 on Monday and Tuesday, January 4 and 5, both days off for Jeffco students: drawing, painting and mixed media on winter themes, with a new set of projects on the second day. Each day starts at 9 am and is booked on its own.

The art center's youth summer classes are open only to members at its Catalyst level and up, with 48 hours' early registration for Explorer members and above, and carry no refunds.
