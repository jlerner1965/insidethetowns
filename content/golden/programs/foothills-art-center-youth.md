[Foothills Art Center](/places/foothills-art-center/) lists its youth classes on its classes page; this fall there are two. **Teen Clay Winter Gnomes**, for 12 and up, builds a clay gnome step by step over two Sunday afternoons, November 8 and 15, with Brittany Bell; the art center says it suits every skill level. The **Gingerbread House Workshop** with Amanda Pfister, Saturday, December 5, decorates sturdy cardboard houses with candy, paint and craft supplies, no baking; kids 7 and up can be dropped off while parents shop at the Astor House, or families can build together.

The art center's youth summer classes are open only to members at its Catalyst level and up, with 48 hours' early registration for Explorer members and above, and carry no refunds.
