Two monthly programs for young children at the [Colorado Railroad Museum](/places/colorado-railroad-museum/), whose 15-acre rail yard holds more than 100 locomotives and cars. On the **first Tuesday** a guided tour of the yard follows children's books, *The Little Engine That Could*, the Little Red Caboose and Donald Crews' *Freight Train*, from 10:30 to 11:30; tour tickets are $3 for everyone 2 and up, and admission is extra. On the **third Tuesday** staff and volunteers read railroad stories and lead a make-and-take craft, recommended for 4 to 6-year-olds and included with admission; the museum also posts a Story Time & Craft series on YouTube.

The museum runs a week of train rides, pop-up exhibits and crafts over spring break; it has not yet announced the 2027 dates. Its Christmas train, [The Polar Express Train Ride](/events/the-polar-express-train-ride-2026/), runs evenings from November 6 to December 23, with tickets sold separately.
