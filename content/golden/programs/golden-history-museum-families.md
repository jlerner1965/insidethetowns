The City's history museum runs its school-break programs on Jeffco Public Schools' days off, for children 6 to 11, at the [Golden History Park](/places/golden-history-park/): science, art and history, the park's chickens, and cooking in the historic Pearce/Helps cabin. The next is **Survival Days with Hutch** over Thanksgiving week, Monday and Tuesday, November 23 and 24, with the Colorado survivalist Aaron "Hutch" Hutchings: survival supplies, navigation, map making and knife safety on Monday; fire, shelter, tracking and rope on Tuesday. No experience is needed. Space is limited and registration closes at noon on Friday, November 20. The museum has not posted its winter-break programs; its e-news announces each one.

The [Golden History Museum](/places/golden-history-museum/) on 10th Street is free, Tuesday to Saturday from 10 to 4:30, and has a hands-on gallery for families with young children. Its fall after-school club, the Garden Lab, finished in October. The museum, the library and the art center host a drop-in [Monster Mash](/events/monster-mash-2026-10-31/) for costumed children on October 31.
