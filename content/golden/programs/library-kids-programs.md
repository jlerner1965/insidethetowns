The Golden Library's calendar for school-age children and tweens. **Paws for Reading** pairs a child with a dog, cat or rabbit from the Denver Pet Partners therapy program for a 15-minute read-aloud; children must be able to read on their own, and registration is recommended. **Pokémon Club** meets in the meeting room once a month for an hour of play: beginners can borrow the library's starter decks, experienced trainers bring their own 60-card deck, and trades happen only with a parent present. **LEGO Builders** on November 19 puts out all the library's LEGO plus one extra building activity, such as KEVA planks or Snap Circuits. **Tween Scene** on November 8 is "Weave Got This!": build a loom and weave a bookmark, no experience needed.

On Tuesday, November 24, the Thanksgiving-week family movie is *Ratatouille*, 2 to 4 pm, with popcorn; bring a blanket or a pillow. Jeffco schools are out that week.
