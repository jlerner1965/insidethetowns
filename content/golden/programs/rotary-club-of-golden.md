"Please join us for one of our weekly meetings. We would be glad to meet you," the club's page says. The meeting is Tuesday mornings at The Club at Rolling Hills: breakfast at 7, which the club pays for, and the program at 7:30. Its projects in and around Golden include food security, housing and mental wellness, the Ethics in Business awards, Warren Tech mock interviews and youth exchanges. The club's events page also lists evening talks on Zoom; the next is November 5 at 6:30 pm, Mark Gerzon on "American PTSD: Diagnosis & Prescription"; sign up from the event page. Membership goes through the membership chair, and the club's online application form was not loading when this was checked.
