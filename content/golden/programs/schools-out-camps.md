The Golden Community Center runs a day camp on the weekdays of Jeffco Public Schools' breaks, for children 5 to 12: gym games, outdoor play, walking field trips, swimming when the pool is available, arts and crafts, reading, puzzles and board games. The rest of this school year's dates are **November 23 and 24** (the page lists no camp on the Wednesday of Thanksgiving week), **December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30**, **January 4 and 5**, and spring break, **March 22 to 26**, whose registration opens in January. The page lists no camp on Jeffco's single days off (January 18, February 12 and 15, March 29, April 30).

It is a state-licensed program, so a current health appraisal signed by a medical provider and an immunization record must be on file before a child attends. The programs page gives the hours as 8:30 am to 4:30 pm and the price as $60 for residents and $65 for non-residents; the City's 2026 youth programs [parent handbook](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/Youth%20Programs%20Parent%20Handbook%202026%20published.pdf), written for summer camp, gives the same rate per child per day, a one-time $50 registration and sunscreen fee, and drop-off from 7:30 to 9 with pick-up from 3:30 to 5. Ask the recreation office which applies to the break camps. Residents who need help with fees can apply to the City's [Equity & Access program](https://co.accessgov.com/community-center/Forms/Page/community-center/equity-and-access-application) at least a month before registration opens.
