The Front Porch is the older adults' room on the upper floor of the [Golden Community Center](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/index.php), just off 8th Street, and the City calls it a place "to meet new people, gather with friends, enjoy a cup of coffee, read a magazine, relax". It is open weekdays from 8 am to 2 pm, and its activities are drop-in, "no need to register". It also runs outdoor adventures, health programs, special events, games, clubs and sports teams, and the City's online registration lists the classes for active adults. Front Porch Friends, the quarterly conversation about what the room offers, meets at 10:30 am on the second Tuesday of January, April and July 2027. The Community Center supports SilverSneakers and Renew Active, and to join the Front Porch email list you write to Suzanne at slinder@cityofgolden.net.
