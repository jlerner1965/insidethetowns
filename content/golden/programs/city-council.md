**Speak to Council.** Four members are elected from wards, two from districts and the mayor at large. At a meeting you can sign up to share your ideas with Council. Comments on an agenda item go to the public-comment address on the [Council's page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/government/city_leadership/city_council.php); they are printed with the agenda if they arrive after it is posted (generally by 5 pm the Thursday before) and by 3 pm on the meeting day. A separate address takes questions on topics not on the agenda, and staff answer within 48 hours. Emails received by the end of a week are published the following Monday; write "Do not publish" in the subject line to ask that yours be left out. For a pothole or a park question, the City asks you to use [Fix It!](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/services/contact_us.php) instead.

**When.** The City's calendar lists the regular meetings on the second and fourth Tuesdays; in December the second meeting moves to Tuesday, December 15, at 6:30 pm. Agendas, minutes and recordings are on the [Public Meetings page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/government/public_meetings.php).

**Meet councilors outside a meeting.** The City runs Coffee with Councilors, each with a different topic, from a water treatment plant tour to an Ore Cart ride-along. The next on its calendar is the holiday Cookies with Councilors bake-off on Saturday, December 5, 10 to 11 am, in the Bear Creek Room at the Golden Community Center, 1470 10th St.
