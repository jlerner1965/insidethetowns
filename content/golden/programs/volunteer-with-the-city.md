**Habitat Helpers.** [DeLong Park](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/get_involved/delong_park_weedbusters.php) is the City's first organic park, kept without synthetic fertilizers, herbicides or pesticides, and volunteers pull its weeds by hand once a week under the Parks staff, usually on a weekday morning. The City trains you; gardening experience helps. Volunteers 16 and over can be assigned on their own, and younger ones come with a parent or guardian.

**Adopt-A-Street.** An individual, family or group [adopts a stretch of road](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/get_involved/adopt-a-streetprogram.php) for one to three years and picks up litter along it once a month. The Streets Division supplies vests and bags, collects the bags (give it a business day's notice) and puts a sign with your name at each end. Adopters report their volunteer hours at least once a year.

**Elsewhere.** The [Golden History Museum & Park](https://www.goldenhistory.org/get-involved/volunteer/) recruits its own volunteers, and the City's page lists about forty local organizations that take them, among them A Little Help, BGOLDN, Foothills Animal Shelter, the Golden Farmers Market, Golden Pantry & Thrift, Hunger Free Golden and Stewards of Open Space. The creek-corridor trash pickups return in spring 2027.
