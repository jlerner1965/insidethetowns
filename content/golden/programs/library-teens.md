**Teen After Hours** is a monthly evening at the library for teens in 6th to 12th grade only; the library asks families to keep to the age limit. On November 13, from 6 to 8, it is "Go Nuts for Donuts!": learn basic crochet stitches and make an amigurumi donut buddy while eating donuts, then take home the buddy, a hook and extra yarn. No crochet experience is needed; registration is on the library's calendar, for 20 places, with a wait list once they fill.

The **Teen Advisory Board** meets in the Golden Library's meeting room to plan teen events and services there; the library is looking for a wide range of teens, bilingual volunteers among them. Apply with the volunteer application on the library's home page or ask at the desk. **Ukulele Thursdays**, an hour for teens and adults, is next on Thursday, December 3, from 6 to 7. The library's Teen Art Exhibition ends with a [closing reception on November 15](/events/golden-teen-art-exhibition-reception-2026-11-15/), 12:30 to 1:15, with snacks and the teen artists.
