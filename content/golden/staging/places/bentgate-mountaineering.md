Bentgate was founded in downtown Golden in 1994 and says it has been under the same ownership for 30 years. The Washington Avenue store carries gear for climbing, backcountry and resort skiing, hiking, camping and cycling, including e-bikes and gravel bikes.

Its rental and demo department covers backcountry safety gear, e-bikes, gravel bikes and ski gear, and the shop does bootfitting, custom footbeds, ski tuning and bike service. The store's hours page lists it closed on Saturdays and on July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day; transit stop 24413 is at the front door.
