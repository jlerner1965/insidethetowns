The Coors Brewery Tour is a 90-minute look at malting, brewing and packaging at the Coors Brewery, with old photos, neon signs, cans, bottles and other archive memorabilia. Reservations open 25 days ahead at midnight Mountain Time and often sell out. All visitors, including those going only to the gift shop, park at the tour lot at 502 14th Street (southeast corner of 13th and Ford) and take a shuttle to the brewery.

The tour, hospitality lounge and Coors & Co. gift shop are closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays, on the 2026 holiday and maintenance dates the site lists (including October 1–5, November 26–27, December 24–25 and December 31), and on most major holidays.
