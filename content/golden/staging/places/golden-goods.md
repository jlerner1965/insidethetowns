Golden Goods has been a family-owned store in downtown Golden since 2013, run by Nick, De and Todd Aichholz. It sells souvenirs such as mugs, stickers and wooden bears, Golden, Colorado and Coors apparel, toys and games including a large Lego selection, old-fashioned candy, drinks, snacks and sauces, and poly furniture such as Adirondack chairs and rockers. In summer it sells River Run tubes and water gear for Clear Creek.
