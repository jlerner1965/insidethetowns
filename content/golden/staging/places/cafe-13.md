Cafe 13 is in the historic armory building and runs a scratch kitchen: breakfast is served all day, and lunch (sandwiches, salads, soup and green chile) starts at 11 am. You order food, drinks and pastries at the counter and the staff bring them to your table; lines for food can get long, and the patio window serves to-go coffee, pastries, grab-and-go items and bread. Gluten-free bread and vegan options are available, and bakery items can be pre-ordered with a week's notice.
