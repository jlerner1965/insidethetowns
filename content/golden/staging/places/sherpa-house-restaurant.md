Sherpa House opened in spring 2009 and serves Nepalese, Sherpa, Tibetan and Indian food. The building is modelled on a traditional Sherpa home in Nepal's Solu Khumbu district, with a living room, shrine room, gallery room, courtyard patio and garden, and artifacts brought from owner Lhakpa Sherpa's home village of Syangma. It also hosts events in its backyard event center.
