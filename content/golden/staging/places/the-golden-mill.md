The Golden Mill is a food hall in a former flour and feed mill at 1012 Ford Street, one block north of the Coors Brewery. Its kitchens include Sushi Sora, Republik of Chicken, Rolling Smoke BBQ, Tacos al Chile and Happy Cones ice cream, alongside a tap wall, trivia nights and live music. It does not take tips.

Paid parking is nearby at the Clayworks lot, 612 10th Street, and at the Gold Office Building, 607 10th Street, on weekday evenings and weekends; free parking is at the Clear Creek lot, 304 10th Street.
