Goosetown Station opened in 2021 in one of the last original buildings of Goosetown, one of Golden's first neighborhoods, settled by German immigrants. The bar and stage sit in a yard among grape vines, with group seating and a calendar of live music and food trucks from Thursday to Saturday.
