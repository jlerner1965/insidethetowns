Peak Cycles is a full-service bike shop in the historic Foss Building at 13th and Washington, offering sales, repairs, rentals, demos and bike fits for all ages. It sells Specialized (including S-Works), Giant and Liv, and is the storefront for BikeParts.com, whose local customers can pick up web orders at the shop.
