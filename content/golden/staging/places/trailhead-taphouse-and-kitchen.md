Trailhead Taphouse & Kitchen is at the corner of Miners Alley and 12th Street, just off Washington Avenue. Its owners, Brett and DeAnn Wieber, moved to Colorado from Wisconsin in 1999 and also run Windy Saddle Café. The kitchen leans Cajun, with gumbo, jambalaya, red beans and rice, fried alligator and shrimp po' boys, plus burgers, salads and cheese curds; the taps carry Colorado craft beer, with Coors Banquet, Coors Light and Blue Moon year round.

It is a sports bar too: it shows every Green Bay Packers game, and Broncos games, on its screens. It takes no reservations, offers call-in takeout except on very busy evenings, and does not deliver.
