Charlie and Janine Sturdavant, both geologists, started Golden City Brewery in 1993 in the old machine shop behind their historic Golden home, a few blocks from the Colorado School of Mines, and it still runs from the same place: the carriage house is the tasting room and the backyard is the beer garden. The site calls it the second-largest brewery in Golden and says it is currently majority woman-owned.
