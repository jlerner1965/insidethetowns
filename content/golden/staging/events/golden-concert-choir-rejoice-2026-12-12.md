The Golden Concert Choir's holiday concert, Rejoice, with Denver Brass 5: festive and sacred choral music by Randol Bass, Gabrieli, Biebl, Daniel Pinkham, Gwyneth Walker and others, including Biebl's Ave Maria. It is the choir's first season at Common Flame, the former Jefferson Unitarian Church on West 32nd Avenue. Tickets are sold online through the choir's page (bring the receipt) or at the door by cash, check or card.
