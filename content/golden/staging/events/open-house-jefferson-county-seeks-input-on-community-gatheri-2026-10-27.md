Jefferson County Parks and Open Space is conducting an evaluation to determine where the County should invest its resources to support community gathering and provide opportunities for equine, youth, and agricultural activities. Potential investment locations include the Jefferson County Fairgrounds and Fehringer Ranch (another County property). This study will encompass a full exploration of scenarios that include reimagining or reinvesting in the current Fairgrounds property to modernize, as well as scenarios that involve development of community facilities at Fehringer Ranch.
