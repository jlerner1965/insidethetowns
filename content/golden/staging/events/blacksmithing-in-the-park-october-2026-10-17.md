				<h2>Blacksmithing every third Saturday</h2>
<p>Have you always wanted to learn about blacksmithing but you’ve had trouble finding a good option? Join our volunteer every third Saturday at Golden History Park for a fun morning of observation and learning about blacksmithing. Our recreated blacksmith shop, complete with historic tools and coal-fired forge, will transport you to the 1800s. Bring your technical questions and your camera and we’ll take care of the rest.</p>
<p>After you’re done at the park, swing by Golden History Museum to see our <a href='https://www.goldenhistory.org/visit/history-museum/current-exhibits/'>latest exhibits</a>, open Saturdays until 4:30 p.m. Admission is free to the Park and the Museum.</p>
<h2>Varied history opportunities await you</h2>
<p>A variety of programs are offered throughout the year. <a href='https://www.goldenhistory.org/get-involved/membership/'>Consider a membership</a> to open the doors of special program pricing and other benefits like 10% off in the museum gift shop.</p>
<p>Lots of fun history opportunities await you in the Golden History Museum & Park <a href='https://goldenhistory.pastperfectonline.com/'>online collection</a>. We have approximately 15,000 items in our collection and you’re sure to find something that interests you about Golden.</p>
