Fossil Trace opened in July 2003, with Table Mountain to the north and the foothills to the west. Triceratops footprints and other fossils can be seen beside the 12th green, and an exhibit just inside the clubhouse doors tells the story of the tracks and trace fossils found when the course was dug; old clay-mining machinery stands around the course, which the City runs. Tee times for the 2026 season are booked online, and the golf shop is at 303-277-8750. Footprints at Fossil Trace, the restaurant, is open year round to golfers and everyone else; reservations, required for 16 or more, at 303-277-8755.

The course is replacing its pump station and irrigation. From September 10, 2026 to May 2027 one hole at a time is closed, so play is 17 holes at adjusted rates, and from November 2026 to August 2027 the 17th plays shorter while the new pumphouse is built. The whole facility closes on Thanksgiving Day, December 24 and 25, and from December 31 to January 8 for renovations.
