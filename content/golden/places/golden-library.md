The Golden Library opened in 1996 beside the Golden History Museum, on the Clear Creek Trail; in summer the Golden Farmers Market fills the lot next door. It is a full-service branch with study rooms, a holds locker, a walk-up book return and a meeting room for 46 that can be booked free up to 60 days ahead. Curbside pickup of holds runs Monday to Saturday 9 to 5 and Sunday 12 to 5: park in a curbside space and call the number on the sign. The library's own lot is small, and the City's lot just west of it gives two hours free to library users who register their cars.

The library closes for Veterans Day on November 11, Thanksgiving and the day after, November 26 and 27, and December 24 and 25, and at 5 pm on December 31. Its storytimes and its programs for school-age kids and teens are on the [Families page](/families/).
