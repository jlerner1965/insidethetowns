Truly BoHotique opened on December 13, 2014, to support female entrepreneurship, and carries mostly woman-owned brands, many of them local, along with a few larger names such as Free People. It sells women's apparel, shoes, accessories, jewelry, beauty and wellness products and gifts, and a small selection for babies and kids.

The shop is on the southwest corner of Washington Avenue and 14th Street, next to the Higher Grounds coffee shop.
