The Clear Creek Trail, formerly the Peaks to Plains Trail, is a planned 65-mile route from the South Platte Trail in Denver to the headwaters of Clear Creek at Loveland Pass. Jefferson County Parks & Open Space is building the canyon section: the first segment, three miles of 10-foot-wide concrete trail beside the creek, two in Jefferson County and one in Clear Creek County, opened in summer 2016, and that segment's final three quarters of a mile in fall 2017, and the 1.75-mile Gateway Segment opened in August 2021, connecting with downtown Golden along Clear Creek and running west to Tunnel 1 beside US 6.

The Gateway Trailhead at the canyon entrance has a bike repair station and a drinking fountain with a ground-level bowl for dogs. West of Tunnel 1, the CCR Trailhead segment opened in November 2025, with parking for 42 cars, five bridges and 1.5 miles of ADA-accessible trail that connects to the Gateway Segment. The Huntsman Trailhead, at 25651 US 6, opened on November 6, 2026, with restrooms and a shaded gathering area; across US 6 from it are four more bridges, another 1.5 miles of 10-foot-wide ADA-accessible trail and five creek access points. With that open, the county counts a little less than 6 miles still to build of the trail's 13.5 miles in Jefferson County.

The Huntsman opening is from the county's news release of November 5, 2026 (https://www.jeffco.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2680). Hours are the county open space hours from the JCPOS Regulations page (https://www.jeffco.us/1583/Regulations).
