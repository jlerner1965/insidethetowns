An indoor climbing gym on Golden Ridge Road: roped climbing on walls up to 45 feet, bouldering with an overhanging arch and a top-out boulder, a lead cave, and campus and system boards. There is also a yoga studio with views of Lookout Mountain, a fitness floor with weights and cardio equipment, locker rooms with showers, a gear shop and lounges. Open weekdays 6 am to 11 pm, Saturdays 8 am to 8 pm and Sundays 8 am to 6 pm.
