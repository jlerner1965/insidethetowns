Tony Grampsas sits on the east side of North Table Mountain: 63 acres with one of Golden's two off-leash dog parks (with dog hydration stations), pickleball courts whose lights go off at 10 pm, baseball fields, a multi-use field, a playground with a tire swing, and restrooms. The 1.3-mile Tony Grampsas Trail runs along the west side of the park, often beside the Church Ditch, to its north end; it is unpaved and easy, open to bikes and horses, and starts east of the Tony Grampsas Gym on Salvia Street, on Easley Road, or at West 50th Avenue and Quaker Lane.

The Golden Bike Park, inside the complex, was built by the City with the Colorado Mountain Bike Association, the International Mountain Bicycling Association and local volunteers, the Golden Bike Park Group, who still maintain it. Its main jump and flow trail starts with a single line at the top and gets bigger and harder on the way down, with a rollable option on every feature; about halfway it splits into beginner, intermediate and advanced lines that meet again at the climbing trail. Park hours are the City's for its outdoor parks.
