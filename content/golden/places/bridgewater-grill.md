Bridgewater Grill is the restaurant at The Golden Hotel, 800 Eleventh Street, open from 8 am every day until 9 pm Sunday to Wednesday and 10 pm Thursday to Saturday. The dinner menu runs from smoked trout dip and mussels to Colorado filet, salmon and rib-eye, and the seasonal patio looks over Clear Creek toward the foothills, where kayakers pass and hang gliders launch from Lookout Mountain.

Happy hour is Sunday to Thursday, 3 to 6 pm, except during special events and holidays (The Golden Hotel's Bridgewater page, https://thegoldenhotel.com/bridgewater-grill/). Reservations are taken by phone or online, but not for the patio, which is first come, first served. Diners get up to two hours of free parking in the hotel lots.
