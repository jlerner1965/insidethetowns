Vanover Park sits beside Clear Creek across from the Coors Brewery. It has a playground, a climbing structure, a first-come picnic pavilion and table, checkers and chess boards, and a short walking loop with benches. In summer it is the last exit point for people tubing down the creek, and the city names it as the take-out for paddlers leaving the Clear Creek Whitewater Park.

It connects to the Tucker Gulch and Clear Creek trails. Hours and creek-corridor rules (no alcohol, glass, smoking or most grills; dogs on leash) are from the city's Park Rules page; the parking note is from the city's Clear Creek Whitewater Park page.
