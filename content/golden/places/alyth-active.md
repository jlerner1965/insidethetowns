Alyth Active designs its activewear and athleisure in Colorado and is women owned. Its Golden store, at 1224 Washington Avenue, is one of two; the other is on Old Town Square in Fort Collins.

The store sells women's bottoms, tops, sports bras and maternity wear, a men's line of tops and bottoms, and accessories, along with a "Mines x Alyth" collection.
