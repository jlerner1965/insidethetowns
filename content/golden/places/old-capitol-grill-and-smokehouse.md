Old Capitol Grill & Smokehouse serves house-smoked meats and classic American food with signature cocktails and local beer, at the bar or in private booths, at the corner of 12th Street and Washington Avenue. It is open seven days a week; the hours above are the posted seasonal hours, which the restaurant says may change with the season.

Walk-ins are welcome; parties of 10 or more should contact the restaurant. The patios are dog-friendly, and card payments carry a 3% surcharge.
