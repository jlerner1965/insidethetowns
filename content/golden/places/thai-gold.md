Thai Gold cooks curries, noodle dishes, fried rice, soups and stir-fries to order, with spice on a scale of 0 to 10. Most of the menu can be made vegetarian, vegan or gluten free on request, and the restaurant says all of its curry bases are vegan and gluten free.

On weekdays it serves lunch from 11 am to 2:30 pm and dinner from 4 to 9 pm; on Saturdays and Sundays it is open from 11 am to 9 pm. Orders for pickup or delivery go through the online ordering on its site, and it caters corporate events, private parties and large group orders.
