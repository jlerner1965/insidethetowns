The Golden Diner opened in 2009 and runs a scratch kitchen, making its sauces, pancake mixes and hash browns in house and its donut holes to order. The menu runs from diner classics such as pancakes, omelets and scrambles to dishes like a braised pork Benedict and a Colorado loco moco, and it serves Bloody Marys and mimosas.

It is open every day from 7 am. The diner gives its closing time as "2pm-ish".
