Bivy pours from 20 rotating draft lines, a mix of Colorado craft beer and beers from out-of-state breweries, and always keeps Coors on tap. It also serves Old World wine, cocktails and non-alcoholic drinks, and the food menu is salads, hot dogs and brats. Happy hour is 2 to 5:30 pm, Tuesday to Friday.

Owners Kim, a Golden native, and Cody set it up as a place to stop after a hike, ride or climb. Regular events include trivia, chess club, pint nights and bingo, and Campfire Stories, an open-mic night for tales from the trail.
