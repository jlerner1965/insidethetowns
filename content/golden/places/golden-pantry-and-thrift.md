Golden Pantry & Thrift is a Golden nonprofit that runs a food pantry and gives financial help to neighbors in crisis. Its thrift store, open to everyone, sells secondhand clothing and home goods, and the proceeds support the pantry, a crisis fund and its other programs.

The store is open Monday to Friday, 10 am to 4 pm, and closed most holidays. The food pantry itself serves accepted clients only.
