Golden Sweets calls itself Golden's hometown soda fountain. It scoops ice cream from Magill's in Lakewood and Sweet Action in Denver, more than 40 flavors in all, and makes malts, milkshakes and sundaes.

It also sells chocolates from makers near and far, boxed as a gift or by the bag, along with nostalgic candy, gifts and souvenirs. The shop is family owned and operated.
