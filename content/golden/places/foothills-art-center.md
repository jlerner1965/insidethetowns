Foothills Art Center has championed art in Golden since 1968. Its exhibitions, at Astor House, 1133 Arapahoe Street, are free and usually open seven days a week: Monday to Saturday 10 am to 5 pm and Sunday noon to 5 pm. The galleries are closed until November 20 while the 51st Holiday Art Market is installed; the market runs November 20 to December 29 and is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

Classes are held at the Creative Campus, 809 15th Street, which is open only to students and studio artists. The center also offers memberships and space rentals.
