Foothills Art Center has championed art in Golden since 1968. Its exhibitions, at 1133 Arapahoe Street, are free and open seven days a week: Monday to Saturday 10 am to 5 pm and Sunday noon to 5 pm.

Classes are held at the Creative Campus, 809 15th Street. The center also offers memberships and space rentals.
