Ulysses Sports Complex sits on a mesa overlooking Golden and the Platte Valley. Recent improvements gave it new multi-use athletic fields, an inclusive playground with accessible play features, a picnic shelter (first come, first served; no reservations), public art, expanded parking and new paths. It also has the Ulysses Dog Park, off leash, with a dog hydration station; a 21,500-square-foot skate park whose lights go off at 10 pm; baseball fields; an open lawn; and a 0.67-mile fitness and walking loop with benches that connects to the North Ulysses Trail. The restrooms are open 5 am to 10 pm, and the park keeps the City's outdoor park hours.
