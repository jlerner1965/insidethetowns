Bentgate has been outfitting climbers, skiers and cyclists from Washington Avenue since 1994. The store carries gear for climbing, backcountry and resort skiing, hiking, camping and cycling, including e-bikes and gravel bikes.

It rents climbing gear, bikes, e-bikes, skis and ski boots, runs ski demos, and does bootfitting, ski tuning and bike service. The shop is closed on Saturdays and on the holidays its hours page lists; RTD stop 24413 is at the front door.
