Adorne is the shop and studio of jewelry designer Sarah DeAngelo, known for hand-stamped sterling silver pieces with genuine gemstones, made in house; the studio is in the shop, so you can see where the work is done. The shelves also carry gifts from independent makers.

It offers permanent jewelry: a chain chosen in the shop and micro-welded on at the wrist, ankle or neck, with no clasp. Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are encouraged and booked customers are served first, so there can be a wait.
