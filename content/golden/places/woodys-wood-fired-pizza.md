Woody's has been making wood-fired pizza and wings on historic Washington Avenue, one block west of the Coors Brewery, for more than 30 years. It is open every day from 11 am to 11 pm for dine-in, with take-out from 11 am to 9 pm and online ordering for take-out and delivery. It also runs a food truck and hosts events.
