Jason Stengl and Brian Hutchinson, who met in 2004 at the Mountain Sun breweries in Boulder, opened Cannonball Creek in January 2013 on a property on the north side of Golden. The name is the original name of Clear Creek, which runs through the center of Golden. The brewery lists Great American Beer Festival and World Beer Cup medals on its site.

The taproom at 393 Washington Avenue has food trucks, and dogs are welcome on the patios. The brewery says to check its Facebook page for updates to its hours.
