White Ranch is the biggest park Jefferson County Parks & Open Space runs, with more than 20 miles of natural-surface, multi-use trails over gentle and rugged ground. The East Trailhead is at 21827 West 56th Avenue and the West Trailhead at 25373 Belcher Hill Road. A 0.9-mile stretch of the Longhorn Trail is bike-only and one-way, part of the county's designated-use pilot.

It is one of two county open space parks with camping: Sawmill and Sourdough Springs campgrounds have semi-primitive, tent-only, walk-in sites by online permit, bought at least 24 hours ahead, $12 a site a night, for up to three nights in a row and six nights in any 30 days. Sourdough Springs closes for the winter: the park's page says November 1 to March 31, and the county's [alerts page](https://www.jeffco.us/1531/Alerts-Closures) says December to March. Trails cross the property except for two Sensitive Areas; one of them, Ralston Buttes, can be visited only by permit.

Hours are from the JCPOS Regulations page (https://www.jeffco.us/1583/Regulations); the mud-closure note is from the JCPOS Alerts & Closures page and the campground closure from the park's own page.
