Toad&Co Golden is a local, family-owned partner store of Toad&Co, the clothing company headquartered in California, and was established in 2017. It carries the brand's women's and men's clothing, from dresses, flannels and sweaters to jackets and pants, along with footwear and accessories.

Toad&Co says all of its clothing is made with at least 80 percent sustainable fibers or certified fabrics, and that it gives 1 percent of sales to nonprofits. Orders placed on the store's website can be picked up in the shop.
