New Terrain's Golden taproom on Table Mountain Parkway pairs a changing lineup of exploratory beers with its scenery, a beer garden with rotating daily food trucks, weekly live music and events such as Monday yoga. It also pours a range of non-alcoholic options, from kombucha to sodas and sparkling water. The brewery opened a second taproom in Palisade in October 2026.
