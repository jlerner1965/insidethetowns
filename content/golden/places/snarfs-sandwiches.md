Snarf's downtown Golden shop is on Washington Avenue, next to Woody's Pizza. Alongside its main menu it has a gluten-free menu, and it takes orders for pickup, delivery and catering. There is patio seating.

Snarf's pitches it as a place to pick up a picnic before fishing, kayaking or tubing on Clear Creek, and it offers a discount for Colorado School of Mines students; ask about it when you order.
