The City of Golden's recreation center on 10th Street. The City lists its amenities as indoor leisure and lap pools, cardio and weight equipment, a gymnasium, an indoor running track, and licensed school-age and preschool camps. The Front Porch, the City's program for older adults, is in the same building.

**The pools.** The pool area, hot tub and sauna included, closes half an hour before the building. The leisure pool opens at 10 am on weekdays, 7 am on Saturday and 8 am on Sunday. The lap pool's schedule changes with swim lessons and swim team, so check the City's [Indoor Pools page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/pool_area.php) for the day's lanes; the [gym schedule](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/gym_schedule.php) is on its own page. The hot tub and sauna are for guests 14 and older.

**Getting in.** Daily admission, 10-visit punch cards and annual passes; Golden residents show proof of residency for the resident rate. This guide's [passes, pools and Child Watch](/families/#program-community-center-passes) entry has the prices. Child Watch, for ages 1 to 7, runs Tuesday 8:30 am to 12:30 pm and 2:30 to 4:30 pm and Wednesday 8:30 am to 12:30 pm.

**Holidays.** Closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The day after Thanksgiving it is open 8 am to 7 pm, and on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve 5:30 am to 2 pm.

**Parking.** The largest lot is off 10th Street, at the front desk. From May 1 through October 1 the lots are reserved for Community Center members Friday through Sunday; a parking permit is activated at check-in.
