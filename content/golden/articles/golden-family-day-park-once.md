Park once at the City's free Clear Creek Lot, 304 10th Street, and the whole short plan is on foot: the creek path, two parks with restrooms, a playground, the History Park's cabins and the free Golden History Museum. Allow about 90 minutes for the creek and a half day with a museum; the Colorado Railroad Museum is a separate stop by car. The City's parking site says the [Clear Creek Lot is free at all times](https://www.parkgoldenco.com/faq).

Tubing on the creek is for summer only, and the flags decide it. Everything below was read on the City's, the county's or each museum's own page on November 18, 2026.

## Where to park

| Where | Cost | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Clear Creek Lot, 304 10th Street | Free at all times | The City's parking site says so; it is the start of this plan |
| North Garage, 702 12th Street, or South Garage, 1250 Jackson Street | First two hours free, then $2.50 an hour, to $10 a day | Paid from 7 am to 5 pm every day, holidays included. Register your plate even for the free two hours |
| On the street downtown | Free for two hours before 5 pm, and free after 5 pm | No registration; the two-hour limit is enforced, and the signs say which days it applies |

Garage and lot parking is paid [by license plate](https://www.parkgoldenco.com/paid-parking) at a pay station or by QR code, with a 49-cent fee on each payment. A time-limit or paid-parking ticket is [$50 the first time](https://www.parkgoldenco.com/violations).

## About 90 minutes: the creek

The creek parks are open from an hour before dawn to an hour after dusk, under the City's [park rules](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/parks_trails/park_rules/index.php): dogs on a leash, no alcohol or glass, and no grills.

- **Parfet Park, 725 10th Street:** Golden's oldest park, from the early 1900s, with shade trees, picnic tables, a slide and a [restroom](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/business_detail_T31_R176.php).
- **Golden History Park, 11th and Arapahoe:** 1800s cabins, a barn, a chicken coop, a two-seat outhouse and the 1876 Guy Hill schoolhouse, across the creek from the museum. The [grounds](https://www.goldenhistory.org/visit/history-park/) are free and open 5 am–11 pm; you look in through the windows, since the buildings open only now and then. A walking guide and map is a dollar in the park, and leashed dogs are welcome.
- **Lions Park, 1300 10th Street:** the large, accessible [playground](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/business_detail_T31_R17.php), with slides and swings, and a restroom, next to the Community Center.
- **The whitewater course:** about seven city blocks on the north bank of Clear Creek, across from Lions Park. The City says it is [unsupervised](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/business_detail_T31_R162.php), with no fee, and everyone in the water is there at their own risk.

**In summer,** the creek flags on [Visit Golden's creek page](https://www.visitgolden.com/plan-your-visit/creek-info/), which the City's whitewater page sends readers to, decide the water. Flags start in June, and green, the creek season, usually comes from late June to mid-July. Yellow means unsafe for anyone under 18, and red closes the creek to swimming and tubing. Tubers must be out by Vanover Park, where a dam lies just past the take-out.

## Half a day: add a museum

| Museum | Cost | Open | For children |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Golden History Museum](https://www.goldenhistory.org/visit/history-museum/), 923 10th Street | Free | Tuesday–Saturday, 10 am–4:30 pm | A hands-on gallery for young children; one floor, wheelchair accessible; picnics on the creek-side patio |
| [Mines Museum of Earth Science](https://museum.mines.edu/), 1310 Maple Street | Free | Daily, 10 am–4 pm | A moon rock from Apollo 17, and a free rock or mineral for taking part in the scavenger hunt |
| [Colorado Railroad Museum](https://coloradorailroadmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/), 17155 W. 44th Avenue | $12 adults, $10 seniors 60+, $5 ages 2–17; under 2 free | Tuesday–Sunday, 9 am–5 pm; closed Mondays | Locomotives, cars and cabooses in the outdoor railyard |

The History Museum is on the walk: it is next to the Golden Library on 10th Street. The Mines Museum's [parking](https://museum.mines.edu/parking/) is free at weekends and $10 a day on weekdays, so on a weekday walk there or use a downtown garage.

The Railroad Museum is a drive, off Colorado 58 at I-70 Exit 265, with its own lot. It has no general admission on the evenings of its Polar Express train rides, which run November 6 to December 23 in 2026, or on Day Out with Thomas dates. After a big snowfall, the museum asks visitors to [call ahead](https://coloradorailroadmuseum.org/timetable/train-rides/), 303-279-4591; its Saturday train rides return in mid-January 2027.

## Cold or wet: four places indoors

- **Golden History Museum:** free, Tuesday to Saturday (above).
- **Mines Museum of Earth Science:** free, every day (above).
- **Golden Library, 1019 10th Street:** Monday–Thursday 9 am–8 pm, Friday and Saturday 9 am–5 pm, Sunday noon–5 pm. The library's own lot is small; the City lot west of it gives [two hours free](https://jeffcolibrary.org/locations/gn/) once you register your car.
- **Golden Community Center, 1470 10th Street:** a 91-degree leisure pool, from zero depth to 3 feet 6 inches, with a 130-foot spiral slide. A day pass is $4.75 to $10.25 by age and residence. An adult must be in the water with any child under 6, at most three under-6s to an adult, and riders must be 48 inches tall to take the slide alone. The [slide is closed](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/pool_area.php) on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 4 to 6:30 pm for swim lessons.

## Thanksgiving week

The History Museum is closed on Wednesday, November 25, and Thursday, November 26. The Railroad Museum, the Mines Museum and the Community Center are closed on Thanksgiving Day, and the Golden Library on Thanksgiving and the Friday after.
