Three trail areas sit in the foothills west and north-west of downtown Golden, and two governments run them. Jefferson County Parks & Open Space has [Apex Park](/places/apex-park-trailhead/), [White Ranch Park](/places/white-ranch-park/) and, on Lookout Mountain, the [Lookout Mountain Nature Center and Preserve](/places/lookout-mountain-nature-center-and-preserve/) and [Windy Saddle Park](/places/windy-saddle-park-trailhead/). The City and County of Denver has [Lookout Mountain Park](/places/lookout-mountain-park/), around Buffalo Bill's grave, as one of its mountain parks. North Table Mountain and the Clear Creek Trail are in [A mesa hike or a canyon walk](/articles/mesa-hike-or-canyon-walk/). Rules, distances and hours are as each posted them, read on November 11, 2026.

## Apex Park: check the date

Apex has 702 acres and 10 miles of trail on the County's [park map](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/9361), on the slope between Highway 93 and Lookout Mountain Road. The [park page](https://www.jeffco.us/1190/Apex-Park) says mountain bikers come for its technical trails and the steep climb up Lookout Mountain. The map grades almost every trail more or most difficult. The Apex Trail up Lena Gulch, Pick-n-Sledge, Grubstake, Bonanza, Argos and Sluicebox are multi-use every day.

Six trails run on a calendar, and the County says you must know the date to visit safely. Enchanted Forest and Magic Mountain are directional trails; Poco Calle, Rocker, Paydirt and Smelter connect them. On even dates the six are bike-only, with no hikers or horses. On odd dates they are for hikers and horses only, with no bikes. The map's arrows send riders one way on Enchanted Forest and Magic Mountain, west to east and downhill, and mark the four connectors multi-direction; the park page says riders go one way, downhill and east, on even dates "on all trails". Follow the signs at each junction.

The trailhead at 121 County Highway 93 has, on the map, parking, a restroom, seasonal drinking water, a bike repair station, picnic areas and accessible facilities. The West Access on Lookout Mountain Road is marked as an access point with no parking. Access to the park and the Grubstake Trail from the water tank access road is closed for good, for habitat restoration; use the Kinney Run Trail to reach the main trailhead. Apex is one of three County parks that close completely for mud at some times of year.

## White Ranch and the Belcher Hill Trail

White Ranch is the largest park in the County system. Its [map](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/9387/White-Ranch-Park-Map), last revised in August 2023, counts 3,953 acres and 21.5 miles of multi-use trail; the [park page](https://www.jeffco.us/1437/White-Ranch-Park) says 20-plus. The East Trailhead is at 21827 West 56th Avenue, with parking, a restroom and a picnic area, and the West Trailhead is at 25373 Belcher Hill Road, with a horse trough and corral.

The Belcher Hill Trail starts at the East Trailhead and climbs west into the park, and many of the park's other trails branch from it. The map labels its first stretch 1.1 miles and grades its segments more and most difficult. Hikers, bikes and horses share it. One stretch of the Longhorn Trail is bike-only and one-way: the park page calls it 0.9 miles, and the map calls it Middle Longhorn, 1.0 miles, and says hikers and horses are prohibited on it.

White Ranch is one of two County parks with camping: Sawmill and Sourdough Springs, ten walk-in, tent-only sites each, by online permit bought at least 24 hours ahead, $12 a site a night. Sourdough Springs is closed from November 1 to March 31 on the park page; the [Alerts & Closures](https://www.jeffco.us/1531/Alerts-Closures) page says December to March. The east side of the park is subject to partial mud closures, and the Ralston Buttes Sensitive Area can be entered only by permit.

## Lookout Mountain

**Denver's park.** Lookout Mountain Park, at 987 Lookout Mountain Road, is home to the Buffalo Bill Museum and Grave and is open all year, from an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset. The museum's [amenities page](https://buffalobill.org/visit-us/park-amenities-and-things-to-do/) gives free parking, with none overnight, and a free observation deck a short walk from Buffalo Bill's grave. A one-mile trail from the museum lot leads to the Nature Center and connects with Windy Saddle and the Beaver Brook Trail. Denver's [park map](https://www.denvergov.org/files/assets/public/parks-and-recreation/documents/trails/lookoutmtn_parkmap.pdf) marks the Buffalo Bill Trail hiker-only and shows a restroom by the picnic shelter. Denver's [park rules](https://denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/3/parks-and-recreation/documents/dpr_parkuserulesregulations.pdf) require a leash of six feet or less on trails and allow horseback riding only in designated areas.

**The County's preserve.** The Lookout Mountain Nature Center at 910 Colorow Road is open Friday to Monday, 10 am to 4 pm, and closed Tuesday to Thursday; its naturalist programs are free. The preserve's trails are hiker-only and open daily on the County's hours. The [Nature Center page](https://www.jeffco.us/1281/Lookout-Mountain-Preserve-and-Nature-Cen) calls the preserve 110 acres; the County's March 2026 map says 100, with 2.8 miles of trail, among them the Forest Loop, Meadow Loop and Lorraine Lodge trails. The lot has 129 spaces, first come, first served, shared with Boettcher Mansion, and the County asks visitors to carpool.

**Windy Saddle.** The trailhead at 1277 Lookout Mountain Road starts the Beaver Brook Trail, which the County's [brochure](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/14152/Beaver-Brook-map-brochure) describes as hike-only and 9 miles one way across rugged cliff terrain, with few trail markers and little or no cell coverage; it crosses private land and other jurisdictions on its way to Denver's Genesee Park. Mountain bikers use the Chimney Gulch Trail, with an access at 1678 Lookout Mountain Road.

The County's alerts page lists no February-to-July wildlife closures at Apex, White Ranch or Lookout Mountain.

## Vanover Park, on the creek in town

[Vanover Park](/places/vanover-park/) is the City of Golden's small park at 1019 Ford Street, beside Clear Creek across from the Coors Brewery. The [City's page](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/business_detail_T31_R175.php) lists a playground and climbing structure, a first-come picnic pavilion, chess and checkers boards and a walking loop with benches, and it connects to the Tucker Gulch and Clear Creek trails. In summer it is the last exit for tubers and the take-out for the Clear Creek Whitewater Park, and there is parking. Under the City's [creek corridor rules](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/parks_trails/park_rules/index.php) it opens an hour before dawn and closes an hour after dusk, with no smoking, alcohol, glass or charcoal grills, and dogs on a leash.

## The Ancient Palms Trail is on South Table Mountain

The Ancient Palms Trail is not in the foothills. It is on [South Table Mountain Park](/places/south-table-mountain-park/), the County mesa on Golden's east side, where the [map](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/9383) published in May 2026 draws it as a multi-use trail across the mesa's north-east corner, from the Basalt Cap Loop past the Croc Tooth Trail and below Carl's Point. The County is building a hike-only link between the Jawbone and Welch Ditch trails from summer 2026 into 2027 and asks cyclists and horse riders to keep using the Tertiary and Ancient Palms trails. The Camp George West Trailhead at 1219 Kilmer Street has parking, a restroom and a picnic area; the neighborhood accesses on the east side have little or no parking.

## Which to choose

- **A technical ride, or a hike without bikes, close to town:** Apex; its six calendar trails are for riding on even dates and walking on odd ones.
- **A long day on a big trail network, or a night in a tent:** White Ranch, by the Belcher Hill Trail from the East Trailhead; Sourdough Springs campground closes for the winter.
- **Children, a short walk and a view:** the Nature Center from Friday to Monday, and the walk to the grave and the deck at Lookout Mountain Park.
- **A long hike with no bikes:** the Beaver Brook Trail from Windy Saddle.
- **A playground and the creek:** Vanover Park.

On County land a dog is on a leash of 10 feet or less and its waste is carried out; Class 1 e-bikes may use natural-surface trails open to bikes, Class 2 only paved trails and Class 3 only roads and parking lots; everyone yields to horses and cyclists yield to walkers; and groups are capped at 14 hikers or 8 riders. A car left in a County lot outside park hours without a camping permit can be fined. Jeffco's [Parks & Recreation](https://www.jeffco.us/3159/Parks-Recreation) page says its 27 parks have no entry fees. Mud, wildlife and campground closures are posted on the County's [Alerts & Closures](https://www.jeffco.us/1531/Alerts-Closures) page, and violations go to Jeffco dispatch at 303-980-7300.
