Three holidays move trash day in Golden between now and the new year, and the City, the library and the museums each keep their own holiday calendar. These are the dates each has posted, read on November 10, 2026.

## Trash, recycling and compost

The City's hauler, 5280 Waste Solutions, observes five holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. If your pickup day falls on one of them, it is delayed one day, the City's [When Is Trash Day?](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/services/trash_recycling_compost/when_is_trash_day_.php) page says. Veterans Day is not on that list.

The City's [calendar](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/calendar.php) gives the three delays ahead:

- **Thanksgiving week**, a recycling week: no pickup on Thursday, November 26. Service runs a day late for the rest of the week, on Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28.
- **Christmas week**, a recycling week: no pickup on Friday, December 25. Friday's service moves to Saturday, December 26.
- **New Year's week**, a compost week: no pickup on Friday, January 1. Friday's service moves to Saturday, January 2.

The same page has the pickup maps by neighborhood. Questions about a cart or a missed pickup go to 5280 Waste Solutions at 303-277-8727.

## City offices and the Council

City administrative offices are closed on Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, on Thanksgiving Day and on the Friday after it, November 27, by the City's calendar; police and fire stay on duty. City Hall at 911 10th Street is otherwise open Monday to Friday from 8 am to 4:30 pm.

The City Council meets on [November 24](/events/city-council-2026-11-24/), [December 8](/events/city-council-2026-12-08/) and [December 15](/events/city-council-2026-12-15/). The City's calendar has no meeting on the fourth Tuesday of December, the 22nd, and the Council's agenda puts the final public hearing on the 2027–2028 budget on December 8.

## The Community Center

The [Golden Community Center](/places/golden-community-center/) at 1470 10th Street stays open on Veterans Day. Its [holiday hours](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/visit_us.php):

- Thanksgiving, November 26: closed
- Black Friday, November 27: 8 am to 7 pm
- Christmas Eve: 5:30 am to 2 pm
- Christmas Day: closed
- New Year's Eve: 5:30 am to 2 pm, with the leisure pool open from 8 am
- New Year's Day: closed

On other days it is open 5:30 am to 8 pm Monday to Thursday, 5:30 am to 7 pm on Friday, 7 am to 6 pm on Saturday and 8 am to 4 pm on Sunday. The pool area closes half an hour before the building.

## The library

The [Golden Library](/places/golden-library/) at 1019 10th Street is closed on Veterans Day, November 11, on Thanksgiving Day and the day after, November 26 and 27, and on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, by the Jefferson County Public Library's [closures schedule](https://jeffcolibrary.org/holiday-closures/) for 2026. Its regular hours are 9 am to 8 pm Monday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm on Friday and Saturday and noon to 5 pm on Sunday.

## Museums

- The [Golden History Museum](/places/golden-history-museum/), open Tuesday to Saturday from 10 am to 4:30 pm, lists Thanksgiving Eve, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day among its closed days.
- The [Mines Museum of Earth Science](/places/mines-museum-of-earth-science/), open daily from 10 am to 4 pm, is closed on Thanksgiving Day and on December 24, 25, 26 and 31 and January 1.
- The [Colorado Railroad Museum](/places/colorado-railroad-museum/), open Tuesday to Sunday from 9 am to 5 pm, is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.
- The [Buffalo Bill Museum and Grave](/places/buffalo-bill-museum-and-grave/), open Tuesday to Sunday from 10 am to 5 pm in winter, is closed on Thanksgiving, the Friday after it, Christmas and New Year's Day.
- [Foothills Art Center](/places/foothills-art-center/)'s [Holiday Art Market](/events/foothills-art-center-holiday-art-market-2026/) at the Astor House runs November 21 to December 29, Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 5 pm and Sunday from noon to 5 pm. It closes at 2 pm on Christmas Eve and is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

## Getting around

The City's [Ride](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/getting_around/ride.php) page says the free Ore Cart shuttle runs Monday to Saturday with no service on federal holidays, and its [Ore Cart](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/services/ore_cart/index.php) page says the shuttle does not run on designated Colorado School of Mines holidays. RTD lists no [Golden FlexRide](https://www.rtd-denver.com/routes-services/other-services/flexride/flexride-service-areas/golden) service on Sundays or holidays.

## No school

Jeffco Public Schools' [calendar](https://www.jeffcopublicschools.org/calendar) has Thanksgiving Break from Monday, November 23 to Friday, November 27. The Community Center's [School's Out camps](https://www.cityofgolden.gov/community/golden_community_center/programs.php) for ages 5 to 12 run from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, at $60 a day for residents and $65 for others, on November 23 and 24, December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30, and January 4 and 5. The history museum's [break program](https://www.goldenhistory.org/learn-do/school-break/) on November 23 and 24, two days of survival skills for ages 6 to 11, runs from 9 am to 4 pm at $80 a day for members and $95 for others.

## The events

Thanksgiving morning has [The Great Turkey Chase](/events/great-turkey-chase/) from Parfet Park at 8:30 am, and the Golden Mill serves a free or pay-what-you-can [community lunch](/events/thanksgiving-community-table-golden-mill-2026-11-26/) from 11 am to 3 pm. In December come the [Candlelight Walk](/events/olde-golden-candlelight-walk-2026-12-04/) on the 4th, the [Holiday Parade](/events/olde-golden-holiday-parade/) on the 12th and [Jingle on the Avenue](/events/jingle-on-the-avenue/) on the 19th.
