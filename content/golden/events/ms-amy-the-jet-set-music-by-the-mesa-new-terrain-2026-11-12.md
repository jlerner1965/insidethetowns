The Jet Set, a Front Range bluegrass band led by Ms Amy that took second place in the 2025 RockyGrass band competition, plays New Terrain Brewing's Music by the Mesa series on Thursday, November 12 from 6 to 9 pm. The series is free live music every Thursday in the taproom on Table Mountain Parkway.
