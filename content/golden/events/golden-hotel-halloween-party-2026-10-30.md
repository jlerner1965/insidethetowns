A family Halloween party at the hotel's Clear Creek Pavilion, timed to follow Trick or Treat Street on Washington Avenue: a Halloween Zumba performance by the Golden Zumba Squad, a costume contest the audience votes on, free caramel apple and s'mores bars and pumpkin spice soft-serve, pumpkin carving, coloring, games and prizes. Food and drinks are for sale, and guests who register get two hours of free parking.
