Downtown Golden's Halloween afternoon on Washington Avenue: music, dance performances, games and candy from local businesses for trick-or-treaters of all ages in costume. The City's calendar lists the event on Washington Avenue from 10th to 13th Street and says the avenue will be closed to traffic between 11th and 14th streets.
