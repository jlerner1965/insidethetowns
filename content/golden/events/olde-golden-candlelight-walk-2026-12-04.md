Golden opens its holiday season with a candlelit walk down historic Washington Avenue, singing carols, from 15th Street at 6:30 pm to Parfet Park, where a half-hour holiday performance starts at about 6:50 and Santa helps switch on the 45,000 lights along Clear Creek and downtown at about 7:20. Bring a candle: keepsake battery candles are sold at Baby Doe's Clothing & Accessories from November 27, and local nonprofits sell wax candles for $1 to $3, mostly cash, from 5 to 6:30 pm on the night at Parfet Park, the Golden Welcome Center and the corners of Washington Avenue at 12th, 13th and 14th. Afterward Golden History Park has a luminaria walk, blacksmith demonstrations, a bonfire, dancing and free cocoa from the Golden Lions Club, and the History Museum and the library stay open late. A free shuttle runs from 5 to 9 pm from the Splash lot at 3151 Illinois St., dropping off at 18th and Washington and picking up on the north side of 10th Street across from Parfet Park.
