The December second-Tuesday meeting of the Golden City Council, preceded by a study session, in Council Chambers at City Hall; the City's calendar lists it from 5 to 8 pm. Agendas are posted to the City's meetings page, generally by 5 pm the Thursday before; written comments on agenda items go to publiccomments@cityofgolden.net by 3 pm on the day of the meeting, and residents can also sign up at the meeting to speak.
