The December second-Tuesday meeting of the Golden City Council, preceded by a study session, in Council Chambers at City Hall. The City's meetings page lists the study session at 5 pm and the regular business meeting at 7 pm; the City's calendar shows the evening as 4:30 to 8:30 pm. The final public hearing on the 2027–2028 budget, with a vote on adopting it, is scheduled for this meeting. Agendas are posted to the City's meetings page, generally by 5 pm the Thursday before; written comments on agenda items go to publiccomments@cityofgolden.gov by 3 pm on the day of the meeting, and residents can also sign up at the meeting to speak.
