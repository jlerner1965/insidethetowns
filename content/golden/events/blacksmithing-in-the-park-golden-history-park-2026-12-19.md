A volunteer blacksmith works the coal-fired forge in Golden History Park's recreated blacksmith shop on the third Saturday of the month, Saturday, December 19 from 9 am to noon, for anyone who wants to watch and ask questions. Admission to the park is free, and the Golden History Museum is open that day until 4:30 pm, also free.
