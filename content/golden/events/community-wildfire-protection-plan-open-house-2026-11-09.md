The City of Golden is getting a new Community Wildfire Protection Plan, and this educational session explains the process and how to prepare for wildfire, with City staff, community partners and the Ember Alliance, the Colorado nonprofit that specializes in writing these plans. It begins with a presentation by the Ember Alliance, then residents can meet the partners, pick up resources and talk with neighbors. The City will record the presentation and Q&A and make them public afterward.
