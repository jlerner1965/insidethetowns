The City of Golden is drawing up a new Community Wildfire Protection Plan, and this open house explains the process and how to prepare for wildfire, with City staff, community partners and the Ember Alliance, the Colorado nonprofit helping write the plan. It opens with a 30-minute recorded presentation, then residents can talk with the partners, pick up resources and meet neighbors.
