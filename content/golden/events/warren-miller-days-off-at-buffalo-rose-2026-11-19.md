The 77th Warren Miller ski film, Days Off, hosted by Katie Burrell, follows skiers on the days they are not competing, from heli-skiing in Alaska to Hokkaido, Deer Valley and Revelstoke. Part of the net ticket proceeds goes to the Share Winter Foundation, and the Buffalo Rose says prices go up two weeks before the show.
