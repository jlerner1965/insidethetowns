Golden's Candlelight Walk is on Friday, December 4, 2026: the walk sets off at 6:30 pm from Washington Avenue and 15th Street and ends at Parfet Park, where Santa helps switch on the city's 45,000 holiday lights at about 7:20 pm. People carry candles and sing carols down Washington Avenue. It is the first of the three Olde Golden Holidays events.

## Schedule

- 5 to 6:30 pm: wax candles on sale along the route.
- 5 to 9 pm: free shuttle from the Splash lot.
- 6:30 pm: the walk begins at Washington Avenue and 15th Street.
- About 6:50 pm: a 30-minute holiday performance in Parfet Park.
- About 7:20 pm: Santa helps switch on the lights along Clear Creek and through downtown.

Afterward, Golden History Park has a luminaria walk, blacksmith demonstrations, a bonfire, dancing and free cocoa from the Golden Lions Club. The Golden History Museum and the Golden Public Library stay open late with activities. Many Washington Avenue shops are open during the walk.

## Candles

Bring your own, or buy one. Keepsake battery candles are sold at Baby Doe's Clothing & Accessories from November 27 while they last. On the night, local nonprofits sell wax candles, and possibly glow sticks, at Parfet Park, the Golden Welcome Center and the corners of Washington Avenue at 12th, 13th and 14th streets. Most are $1 to $3, cash only.

## Shuttle and parking

Park at the Splash lot, 3151 Illinois Street, and take the free shuttle, which runs from 5 to 9 pm. It drops off at the top of the hill at 18th Street and Washington Avenue, near the start. The pickup going back is at the bottom of the hill, on the north side of 10th Street across from Parfet Park.

The walk is free. Visit Golden's page does not give street closure times, a weather plan or a rule on pets. The Golden Welcome Center is at 1010 Washington Avenue, 303-279-2282.
