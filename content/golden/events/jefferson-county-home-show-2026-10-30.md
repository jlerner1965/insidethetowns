A free three-day home show at the Fairgrounds, with remodeling contractors and home-improvement companies to meet and get quotes from: kitchens, baths, roofing, windows, siding, heating and cooling. Because it falls on Halloween weekend, children in costume can trick-or-treat at participating exhibitors' booths. Admission is free with a ticket reserved on the show's site.
