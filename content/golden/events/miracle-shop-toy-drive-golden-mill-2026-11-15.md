The Golden Mill collects new, unwrapped toys for The Miracle Shop, which supplies holiday gifts to local children, from Sunday, November 15 through Thursday, December 10. Bring a toy during opening hours (11 am to 9 pm Sunday to Thursday, to 10 pm Friday and Saturday) and the Mill gives you a free drink. Read from the Golden Mill's event page.
