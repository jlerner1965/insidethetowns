A fundraising dinner show for Golden's hometown orchestra: the JSO Winds play holiday favorites while a three-course dinner from chef Chris Zekos and the Buffalo Rose kitchen is served, and the JSO Brass close the night. Doors open at 5 pm, seating for dinner starts at 6, the first set begins at 6:30, and the music ends by 9. Tickets include both sets and dinner, not drinks, and are sold in pairs; for a single seat, email information@buffalorose.net. All ages.
