The fourth-Tuesday business meeting of the Golden City Council, in Council Chambers at City Hall, two days before Thanksgiving. The City's calendar lists it from 5 to 8 pm; check the posted agenda for the start time. Agendas are posted to the City's meetings page, generally by 5 pm the Thursday before; written comments on agenda items go to publiccomments@cityofgolden.net by 3 pm on the day of the meeting, and residents can also sign up at the meeting to speak.
