Golden's holiday parade runs along Washington Avenue from 10th Street to 13th Street, starting at 10:30 am, with this year's theme, Western Winter Wonderland: floats, elves, marching bands, Victorian carolers, antique cars, the Grinch, Buffalo Bill and Santa on the fire truck. Free carriage rides follow from noon to 2 pm, picking up on 12th Street just west of Washington Avenue, and new, unwrapped toys for The Miracle Shop can be dropped at Parfet Park from 11 am to 4 pm. The organizers suggest arriving early for parking and a spot, and leaving chairs and pets at home; the garages on Jackson Street between 11th and 13th are closest to the route.
