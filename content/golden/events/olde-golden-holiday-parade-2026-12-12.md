Golden's holiday parade runs along Washington Avenue from 10th Street to 13th Street, starting at 10:30 am, with elves, marching bands, floats, Victorian carolers, the Grinch and Santa. Downtown's restaurants, galleries and shops are open for lunch and browsing afterward.
