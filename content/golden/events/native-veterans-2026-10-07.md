An evening talk at the Golden History Museum on Native American military service, from the code talkers of the First World War to today's veterans, given by Steven LaPointe of the Sicangu Lakota Nation with the American Indian Veterans of Colorado and the Sister Nations color guard. Tickets are $12, or $5 for museum members, through the City's WebTrac registration.
