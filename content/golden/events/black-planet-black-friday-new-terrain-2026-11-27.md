New Terrain Brewing's annual Black Friday release of Black Planet, its imperial Belgian porter with vanilla and tangerine, in the taproom on Table Mountain Parkway on Friday, November 27 from noon to 9 pm. This year the brewery is releasing only a barrel-aged edition, B.A. Black Planet, aged in Leopold Bros. 9-Year Three Chamber Rye whiskey barrels, on draft and in 330 ml bottles at $8 each, at its Golden and Palisade taprooms when each opens that day.
