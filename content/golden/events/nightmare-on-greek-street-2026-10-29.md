The Mines fraternities and sororities open the houses on West Campus Road for a family trick-or-treat on the Thursday before Halloween: pick up a passport at the check-in tables, play each house's kid-friendly games for candy, and finish the passport for a large candy bar or Play-Doh while they last. A haunted house at the Alpha Phi house, run with the Golden Fire Department, has three scare levels and is for 18 and over or with a parent or guardian. West Campus Road is closed from Elm Street; Mines suggests parking in Lot D or the McNeil parking deck, free after 5 pm outside reserved spaces.
