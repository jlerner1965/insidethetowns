Golden has two Jefferson County vote centers on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 7 am to 7 pm: City Hall, 911 10th St, and the county’s elections office in the Laramie Building, 3500 Illinois St, with a third at the Mines Park Community Center, 2001 Infinity Circle, on the 2nd and 3rd. Vote in person, drop off a mail ballot, register, or get a replacement ballot. The 24-hour drop boxes in town are at City Hall, the School of Mines (1400 Illinois St), the Laramie Building (drive-up) and the Courts and Administration building, 100 Jefferson County Pkwy (drive-up), until 7 pm. Ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm; anyone in line by 7 pm can vote.
