The world premiere of Miners Alley's Halloween show for young audiences: curious cavemen discover zany zombies, and the children in the seats join battles and solve puzzles to save the day, in a story billed as not too spooky. Two shows on Halloween, at 11 am and 1 pm, and a final one on Saturday, November 7; tickets on the theater's box office.
