The Olde Golden Holiday Parade is on Saturday, December 12, 2026, at 10:30 am on Washington Avenue, from 10th Street up to 13th Street through downtown Golden. It is free to watch. Visit Golden lists one parade this year, on this date.

## Schedule

- 10:30 am: the parade begins at 10th Street.
- 11 am to 4 pm: drop a new, unwrapped toy for The Miracle Shop at Parfet Park.
- Noon to 2 pm: free carriage rides, picking up on 12th Street just west of Washington Avenue, by Bella Colibri.

Children can also leave letters to Santa at the Golden Welcome Center.

## What is new in 2026

The parade has a theme again: Western Winter Wonderland, which the organizers describe as the Old West meets the North Pole. Expect floats, elves, Victorian carolers, marching bands, antique cars, the Grinch, Buffalo Bill, and Santa on the fire truck. Entries compete for a Judge's Favorite and a People's Choice award.

## Route and where to watch

The parade starts at 10th Street and continues up Washington Avenue to 13th Street. The organizers suggest getting to town early for a viewing spot and dressing for the weather. They ask people not to bring chairs or pets, because the sidewalks get crowded.

## Parking

The garages on Jackson Street between 11th and 13th streets are the closest to the route. The organizers say to get to town early to be sure of parking. Visit Golden's page does not give street closure times or say what happens in bad weather.

The Olde Golden Holidays Committee runs the parade. The Golden Welcome Center is at 1010 Washington Avenue, 303-279-2282.
