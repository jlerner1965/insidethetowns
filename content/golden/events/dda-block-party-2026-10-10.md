The Golden Downtown Development Authority's block party in the public lot on the north side of 12th Street, between the Astor House and Miners Alley: artist demonstrations and live music from Miners Alley Performing Arts Center and Foothills Art Center, and food from Miners Saloon and Xicamiti Taqueria. All ages, with no alcohol served, and a chance to meet the DDA and hear what it is doing downtown.
