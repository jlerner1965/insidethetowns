A volunteer blacksmith works the coal-fired forge in Golden History Park's recreated blacksmith shop for anyone who wants to watch and ask questions. The museum bills it as a third-Saturday morning, but its page puts November's session on Saturday, November 28, from 9 am to noon. Admission to the park is free, and the Golden History Museum is open that day until 4:30 pm, also free.
