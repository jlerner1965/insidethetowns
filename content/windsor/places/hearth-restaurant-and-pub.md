The gastropub a few steps off Main Street on 4th, with patio dining. The menu is built around pizza, such as a buffalo chicken with bechamel, the Woodsman with wild mushrooms and white truffle oil, and a bacon and fig, plus burgers (the Meister double smash on Wagyu patties, the "Hipster" with gorgonzola) and a short list of entrees including an 8-ounce filet mignon, pan-seared cod with risotto and Scottish salmon. Four taps rotate, and the bar makes its own cocktails, among them a Huckleberry Lemonade and a Windsor Manhattan.

Happy hour runs Tuesday to Sunday from 3 to 5:30, and all night on Mondays. Weekdays it opens at 3 for dinner only; Friday, Saturday and Sunday it opens at 11. Online ordering and delivery are on the website.
