Windsor's original park, a block south of Town Hall on Locust Street, with the kind of mature cottonwoods and maples a park only gets by being there for a long time. The Town lists a playground, pickleball courts, a basketball court, restrooms and a water fountain, and three distinct shelters with tables and grills that seat from 16 to 48 and can be reserved online. Cornhole boards, foosball, ping pong and volleyball equipment round out a family afternoon.

It is the picnic and birthday-party park for the downtown blocks and the pickleball courts are the busy corner. It is ADA accessible.
