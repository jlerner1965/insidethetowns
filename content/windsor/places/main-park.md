Main Park is just south of Town Hall on Locust Street. The Town lists a playground, pickleball courts, a basketball court, restrooms and a water fountain, and picnic shelters with tables that seat from about 16 to 48 and can be reserved online. Cornhole boards, foosball, ping pong and volleyball round out a family afternoon.

It is ADA accessible.
