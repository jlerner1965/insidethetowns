The Town of Severance’s Parks & Trails map (Ayres, map 12, dated 13 April 2026) lists Severance Dog Park as park 14, 2.0 acres, and places it around the pond between the Great Western Trail and Tailholt Avenue, south of Ponderosa Drive and north of Community Park. The Town’s Parks & Recreation page names the park and says its parks are spread through the subdivisions, each with its own amenities, but neither the page nor the map gives a street address or the amenities, so the address above is the map’s position. Parks & Recreation answers 970-686-1218.
