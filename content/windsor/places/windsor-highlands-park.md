The Town park serving the Highland Meadows neighborhood on Windsor's Larimer County side, west of the old town and close to I-25. The Town lists playgrounds (with a dragon play feature for the small ones), a basketball court, a sand volleyball court, a baseball and softball backstop, picnic tables with grills, and trails that run through a nature area with rock formations. There are two reservable shelters: the gazebo has two tables seating about 16, with electricity and a grill, and the second shelter is similar.

It is ADA accessible, and shelters are booked online through the Community Recreation Center.
