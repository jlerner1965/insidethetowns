A Town park on Highland Meadows Parkway. The Town lists playgrounds (with a dragon play feature), a basketball court, a sand volleyball court, a baseball and softball backstop, picnic tables with grills, and trails that run through a nature area with rocks. There are two reservable shelters: the gazebo has two tables seating about 16, with electricity and a grill, and the second shelter is similar.

It is ADA accessible, and shelters are booked online.
