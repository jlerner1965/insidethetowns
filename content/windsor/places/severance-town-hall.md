The Town of Severance's offices stand at the corner of Weld County Road 74 and Timber Ridge Parkway and are open Monday to Friday, closing for lunch from noon to 1 pm; for after-hours drop-offs there is a box on the north side of the building. The Town's Community Gardens page sends would-be gardeners for the Tailholt Community Garden here, and the Great Western Trail Authority's About page says its board meets here on the first Thursday of each month at 5:30 pm.
