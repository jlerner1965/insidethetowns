One of William Oliver's Colorado pubs, on the 500 block of Main. The dining area and bar share one room, with a dartboard and an emphasis on conversation. There is no live music or loud television. The whiskey list is the draw; the bar otherwise pours only Colorado-made gin, rum, vodka, brandy, absinthe and tequila alongside Colorado craft beers, and the menu uses hand-cut, thick-sliced applewood-smoked bacon liberally, with vegetarian options.

Two things set this location apart: the Snug, a semi-private room for six to eight that works for a meeting, and a rear Biergarten for outdoor seating, where dogs are welcome. The pub takes no reservations and does not answer the phone at any location; you walk in. The site's contact page gives one set of normal hours for its pubs, 11 am to 2 am every day, says closures for private events are posted on its Google listing, and lists the days it always closes: New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
