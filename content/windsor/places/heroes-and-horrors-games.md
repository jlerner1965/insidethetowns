The game store on the 1200 block of Main, beside the Ace Hardware. It stocks the Magic: the Gathering, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh and One Piece card games (and claims one of the largest collections of Magic singles in northern Colorado), role-playing games including Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder and Shadowrun, Warhammer 40k and Age of Sigmar miniatures, plus comics, manga, graphic novels and collectible figures.

The game room seats 16 and holds more than 160 games for open play and the shop's weekly events; it is currently by reservation, Tuesday to Thursday 2 to 9 and Friday 2 to 6. Open afternoons and evenings Tuesday through Friday, from noon on weekends, and closed Monday.
