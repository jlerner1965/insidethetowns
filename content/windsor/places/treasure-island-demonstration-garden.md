A volunteer-and-Town garden started in 2007 on an acre along the Poudre River Trail, next to the Eastman wetlands. Half of it grows produce for the local food pantry; the other half is a demonstration of what thrives in Windsor's climate with less water: vegetables and herbs, roses, dahlias, annuals and perennials, a crevice garden and Plant Select varieties, with companion planting and xeriscaping shown as they are done.

It is open to a quiet stroll off the trail, guided tours and classes, and volunteers are welcome. The Town's administrative hours for the garden are Monday to Friday 7:30 to 4:30.
