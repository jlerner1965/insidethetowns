The main library of the Clearview Library District, which serves about 43,000 people in Windsor, Severance and west Greeley, is on 3rd Street a block from Main. Beyond the catalog of books, movies and digital downloads it offers computers, laptops, printing and 3D printing, children's areas and programs, meeting rooms that can be reserved including after hours, test proctoring, notary service and seasonal tax help. Programs run most days and are listed on the district's events calendar.

The district also runs the Severance Library at 5 Timber Ridge Parkway, a bookmobile with neighborhood and school stops, a small outpost inside the Community Recreation Center on 11th Street, and its administrative offices at 1194 W Ash Street. Open 9 to 7 Monday through Thursday, 9 to 5 Friday and Saturday, and 1 to 5 on Sunday.
