A vintage and thrift boutique on the corner of 5th and Main, the second location of the shop on West Oak Street in Old Town Fort Collins. The stock is curated vintage clothing, upcycled and refurbished furniture and decor, and goods from local makers, including the shop's own handmade pressed-flower resin jewelry.

The website does not publish hours for the Windsor shop; check its social pages or call before making a trip.
