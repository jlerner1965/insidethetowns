The Town's largest piece of open space, 110 acres of ridge and grassland on the west side of Windsor, kept as habitat with trails through it. The main loop is 1.4 miles and rated easy to intermediate for hiking and mountain biking, on soft-surface paths, with the kind of wildlife viewing a quiet ridge gives in the early morning. Only a few acres are maintained; the rest is left natural.

Access is from Coyote Gulch Park at the same address on Aladar Drive, with on-street parking. The park itself has a playground with climbing rocks and swings, a basketball court, bocce, a self-service bike maintenance station, shelters with electricity, tables and a water fountain, so it works as the trailhead and the picnic afterward.
