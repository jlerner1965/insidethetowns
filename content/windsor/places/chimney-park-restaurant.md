Chef-owner Jason Shaeffer's restaurant has been the special-occasion table in Windsor since 2007, in a Main Street building from 1895 that was renovated for its fifteenth anniversary in 2022. The kitchen sources local, seasonal and organic products from Colorado and the region; the wine list held a Wine Spectator award every year from 2009 through 2024, and the restaurant carries the DiRoNA award and has twice made OpenTable's national list. The main room seats 40, the Nook 12, and the whole restaurant can be booked for parties of up to 65.

In November 2026 the website says the restaurant "remains closed following our kitchen fire" and that it will reopen in November 2026, without a confirmed grand-reopening date; it asks guests to join its email list on the Contact tab for the announcement. Reservations, when they resume, are through OpenTable.
