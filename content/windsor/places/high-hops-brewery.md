High Hops shares its building on Highway 392 with The Windsor Gardener and its greenhouse, and with The Heart Distillery. The taproom's seating is for 21 and over, but families can walk the greenhouse, and the brewery pours its own root beer for younger visitors. Fall hours are Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 5 pm, with the last pour 30 minutes before closing; it is closed Mondays. Call 970-674-2841.
