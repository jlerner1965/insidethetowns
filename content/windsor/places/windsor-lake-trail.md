The trail circles the perimeter of Windsor Lake, 2.25 miles around according to the Town, starting from Boardwalk Park at 100 N 5th Street.

On the north side of the lake the trail reaches the Windsor Lake Dog Park, where dogs can get in the water; everywhere else on the loop they must be leashed. From the lake the Town's #2 Ditch Trail, part paved and part soft surface, heads west toward Kyger Reservoir for anyone who wants more than one lap.
