A brewery and restaurant in Highland Meadows, the neighborhood on the Larimer County side of west Windsor, one of four breweries in Windsor alongside Peculier Ales downtown, Mighty River on Fairgrounds Avenue and High Hops at the Windsor Gardener. The Windsor Area Chamber lists it as "Brewery and Restaurant" at this address and phone number.

Hours, the tap list and the menu are on the brewery's own site and change with the season; call 970-262-9225 before driving out.

Online ordering and a dine-in waitlist are on the site too. The smokehouse menu includes a smashburger and wings that are smoked, then fried, with pretzel bites and Brussels sprouts among the dishes to share; cocktails and mocktails are poured alongside the house beer. For private events there is a separate taproom, the mezzanine or the whole building.
