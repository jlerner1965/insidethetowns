The Windsor Area Chamber of Commerce works from an office on the 400 block of Main Street, in the middle of the downtown shops. Its online member directory, searchable by category (restaurants and food, shopping and specialty retail, arts and entertainment, lodging) and shown on a map, is the most reliable local list of businesses, with addresses and phone numbers for each; the chamber also runs an events calendar, member-to-member deals and the usual networking and ribbon cuttings.

For a visitor it is the place to ask what is new downtown; the office can be reached by phone or at information@windsorchamber.net.
