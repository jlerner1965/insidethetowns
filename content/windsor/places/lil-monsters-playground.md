An indoor playground on Automation Drive for children from six months old. Open play is $15 a child for the whole day, with one grown-up free per child and $3 for each extra adult; a 10-visit punch pass is $125 and never expires. Everyone wears socks, grown-ups included. It also hosts birthday parties.
