An indoor playground on Automation Drive for children up to 12 years old. Open play is $15 a child for the whole day, with a baby who is not yet walking free alongside a paying child, one grown-up free per child and $3 for each extra adult; a 10-visit punch pass is $125 and never expires. Everyone wears socks, grown-ups included. It also hosts birthday parties.
