Wing Shack grew from Greeley into a chain across Colorado and Wyoming, and its Windsor shop is on the 1200 block of Main Street at the west end of town, among the hardware store and the motel rather than the downtown blocks. The menu is wings in the house sauces with the usual sides; the company describes itself as "pro-chicken and anti-spam," runs a rewards program and an app, and takes online orders for pickup.

Open 11 am to 10 pm seven days a week, which makes it one of the later kitchens in Windsor.
