The drop site at the Windsor Public Services Campus, 922 N 15th Street, is open Fridays through Sundays from 8 am to 3 pm until Sunday, November 15, weather permitting, for Windsor residents only, which leaves one last weekend, November 13 to 15; the Town runs it so residents can recycle leaves and branches beyond its Fall Clean-Up event. Payment is by credit card, no cash: $10 a load for a car, SUV or pickup, $15 for a single-axle trailer, $20 for a tandem-axle trailer, and $25 or $30 for a vehicle and trailer together. Bring proof of residency. Leaves and branches go in separate roll-off bins or piles, limbs more than four inches across are not accepted, bags of any kind, compostable ones included, are not allowed, and you empty your own load; the last loads are taken 30 minutes before closing. The Town's own page is windsorco.gov/YardWaste, and [Living here](/living-here/) lists the yard-waste rebate alongside it.
