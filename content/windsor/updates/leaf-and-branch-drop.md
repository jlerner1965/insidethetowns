The drop site at the Windsor Public Services Campus, 922 N 15th Street, was open Fridays through Sundays from 8 am to 3 pm, weather permitting, from Friday, October 16, to Sunday, November 15, for Windsor residents only; the Town ran it so residents could recycle leaves and branches beyond its Fall Clean-Up event. Payment was by credit card, no cash: $10 a load for a car, SUV or pickup, $15 for a single-axle trailer, $20 for a tandem-axle trailer, and $25 or $30 for a vehicle and trailer together, with proof of residency. Leaves and branches went in separate roll-off bins or piles, limbs more than four inches across were not accepted, bags of any kind, compostable ones included, were not allowed, and the last loads were taken 30 minutes before closing. The Town's own page is windsorco.gov/YardWaste, and [Living here](/living-here/) lists the yard-waste rebate alongside it.
