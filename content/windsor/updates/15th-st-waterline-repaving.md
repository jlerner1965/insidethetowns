The Town says the new water main along 15th St. is installed and active, and crews have moved to the last phase of the project, patching and repaving the roads the work touched. The intersection of 15th St. and Walnut St. was fully closed on Wednesday, Nov. 11, from about 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. From Monday, Nov. 16, the Town's Nov. 10 update (still its latest on Nov. 17) has crews milling and overlaying the road at the north end near Main St. (CO 392) and working south through the week. Work locations and traffic impacts will change with each day's production, and flaggers will be on Walnut Dr. to Lakewood Dr. to help residents reach their homes. The Town's Oct. 22 notice listed southbound 15th St. from CO 392 to Westwood Dr. as closed Oct. 21 through Nov. 19, with residential and business access kept and through traffic detoured. The Town expects the work to finish before Thanksgiving, weather permitting.
