The Town of Severance's watering rules, in place since 2003, set the lawn-watering season by date: lawn watering is allowed from April 1 through November 30 and not at all from December 1 through March 31 of any year. Until November 30 the usual schedule applies to everyone served by the Town: even house numbers water on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, odd numbers on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday, and homeowner-association common areas, multi-family buildings, businesses, churches and government on Sunday, Wednesday and Friday, with no outdoor watering between 11 am and 5 pm. Hand watering of flowers, shrubs and trees by a hose with a shut-off valve is allowed each day during permitted hours. The schedule does not apply to approved non-potable systems, which the Town lists in Hunters Crossing, The Overlook, Severance Shores, The Reserve, Settler's Landing and Tailholt, or to homes served by the North Weld County Water District. Town Hall, 970-686-1218, has the water restriction resolution and the variance permits for newly seeded or sodded lawns.
