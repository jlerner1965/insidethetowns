The Town cut the ribbon on Monday, October 26, at 2 pm at Ridgeline Middle School, 1100 N 15th Street, for the new trail connection at the corner of North 15th Street and Harmony Road. The 15th St. Trail Improvements project added walkways and safety measures at that intersection to improve pedestrian access and connectivity; it was budgeted in the Town's 2026 Capital Improvement Program and follows the Windsor Open Space & Trails Master Plan and the Comprehensive Plan. The Town's Project Connect page tracked the work through the year: the intersection reopened to traffic in June while crews finished up, and the trail's safety railing was slated for installation the week of August 10, after which the trail would open to the public. The Town posts its capital projects at windsorprojectconnect.com, and its trails map is on [Living here](/living-here/).
