The town's road race, starting and finishing at the Island at Pelican Lakes in Water Valley, with a distance for everyone from the one-mile Fit & Fun Run to the full marathon. Waves go out from 7 in the morning, the fun run at 10 and awards at 11, and a post-race party with food, drink, entertainment, a vendor fair and a kids' zone runs from 9 to 2. Late packet pickup opens at 6 am at the start line; Saturday pickup is 3 to 6 pm in a tent on the Island. This year's race supports the Banner MD Anderson Cancer Center. Registration closes at 11:59 pm on race day; the Chamber's listing puts the fun run at 11, but the race's own page gives 10.
