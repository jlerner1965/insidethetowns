The town's road race, starting and finishing at the Island at Pelican Lakes in Water Valley, with a distance for everyone from the one-mile Fit & Fun Run to the full marathon. Waves go out from 7 in the morning, the fun run at 11, and a post-race party follows with food, drink, entertainment and a kids' zone. This year's race supports the Banner MD Anderson Cancer Center. Entry fees are on the registration site.
