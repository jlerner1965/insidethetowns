The second reception for Trifecta!!!, Windsor's twelfth annual community art show at the Town's Art & Heritage Center in the 1909 town hall on 5th Street: the poetry award winners are announced, the Trifecta Poetica perform, and an open mic follows. The show itself hangs from September 11 through Sunday, October 25. Admission to the Art & Heritage Center is always free; it is open Thursday 3 to 7 and Friday to Sunday noon to 4.
