The Town's dog social moves to the Historic Halfway Homestead for its October edition: activities, vendor booths, free demos and a designated off-leash area, with dogs leashed everywhere else. Clean-up bags are provided and owners answer for their animals. The Town asks for donations of supplies for its partner, NoCo Humane. The Homestead is Windsor's first historic landmark site, built around the Halfway House, the oldest structure in town, which began as a stagecoach stop on the Overland Trail cutoff.
