An hour of coffee and conversation with Mayor Julie Cline at the Community Recreation Center, on the morning the building celebrates its tenth birthday with free admission. No agenda and no sign-up on the Town's listing; coffee is provided and residents and business owners alike are invited. The Town holds it on the second Friday of each month unless noted otherwise.
