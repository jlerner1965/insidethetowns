The Town's Halloween afternoon on the lake shore: trick-or-treating around Boardwalk Park, bounce houses and kids' activities, free crafts at the Art & Heritage Center and the market pavilion, face painting on the History Museum lawn, animal scooters, stilt walkers, food trucks and a DJ. Noon to 4 on the Saturday before Halloween. The Town's calendar lists it as free; the Town is also looking for volunteers aged 16 and over for the day.
