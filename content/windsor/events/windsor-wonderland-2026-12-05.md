The Town's holiday evening at Boardwalk Park, as its page sets it out: Santa arrives by train at 4, the Mayor speaks and lights the tree at the depot at 4:15, Santa photos start at 4:30, food vendors sell on 5th Street from 4 to 7, and a parade goes at 6:30. Around it are Christmas crafts, face painting, a scavenger hunt, fire pits with s'mores, a live ice-sculpture carving and holiday music. The Town's page gives the date as December 5, 4 to 7 pm, without printing the year; December 5, 2026 is a Saturday, and the Town's other autumn pages carry dates that fall on their stated weekdays only in 2026.
