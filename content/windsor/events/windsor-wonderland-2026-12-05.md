The Town's holiday evening at Boardwalk Park, as its page sets it out: Santa arrives by train at 4, the Mayor speaks and lights the tree at the depot at 4:15, Santa photos start at 4:30, food vendors sell on 5th Street from 4 to 7, and a parade goes at 6:30. Around it, from 4:30 to 7, are Christmas crafts, face painting, a scavenger hunt, fire pits with s'mores on 5th Street, a live ice-sculpture carving at the History Museum and holiday music from the Windsor Middle School and Windsor High School choirs. The parade starts at the roundabout and runs down the train depot parking lot.
