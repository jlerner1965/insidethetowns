October's edition of the library's Thursdays at the Library series for children up to five: hands-on, monster-themed crafts and activities that stay on the not-so-scary side of Halloween. It runs open-house style from 10 to 11:30, so come when it suits and allow about half an hour to get round everything. Free, no registration. Questions to catherine@clearviewlibrary.org.
