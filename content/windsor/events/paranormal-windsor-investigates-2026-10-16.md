The second night of the Town museums' after-dark program, this time in the 1909 town hall on 5th Street that is now the Art & Heritage Center, a building that once held the police station, two jail cells and the fire truck. A museum educator and the paranormal investigator James Montez lead it; dark rooms, steep stairs and confined spaces are promised. Two sessions, 7:30 and 9:30; $50 with advance registration through the Town.
