A Sunday soup lunch at Bethel Lutheran Church for Stepping Stones of Windsor, the nonprofit that works to prevent homelessness in the Weld RE-4 district by helping households facing eviction or utility disconnection and pointing them to local resources. It runs on grants, donations and volunteers; the lunch is free, and donations are what it is for. The Chamber's listing gives the church without a street address.
