Thanksgiving morning 5K, run or walked, on the paved cart and walking paths of Pelican Lakes Golf Course through Water Valley, finishing by the lake. Chip timing, a long-sleeved shirt, coffee, cocoa, beer and seltzer, breakfast bites, and local gift cards and homemade pastries for the age-group and overall winners. The run starts at 9 am and the walk at 9:05; the fee rises after November 20. The organizers ask runners to bring non-perishable food for the Weld County Food Bank.
