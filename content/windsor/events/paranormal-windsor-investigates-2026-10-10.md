Two of Windsor's oldest buildings explored after dark with a museum educator and the paranormal investigator James Montez, including the 1882 train depot that was moved to the History Museum grounds beside Windsor Lake. Expect dark rooms, paranormal experiments, steep stairs and confined spaces. Two sessions on the night, 7:30 and 9:30; $50 and advance sign-up through the Town's registration system. The same program runs at the Art & Heritage Center on October 16.
