The first Wellness in Windsor Health Fair, put on by the Town with AesthetiKa Wellness and Med Spa at the recreation center, for adults 18 and over: fitness demos, chats with personal trainers, InBody scans, bloodwork and hormone consults, hearing, neurofeedback and physical-therapy screenings, oral cancer screening, vaccines from the Weld County health department, and booths from nutritionists, mental-health providers, spas and others. Saturday, October 17, 9 am to 2 pm; the Chamber's listing calls it free to the community.
