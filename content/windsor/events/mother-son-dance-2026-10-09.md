The Town's Mother Son Decades Night Dance: dress in your favorite decade, from the 50s to the 00s, for retro hits, themed activities and light refreshments. Recommended for boys aged 2 to 12, and grandmothers, aunts and family friends are welcome as substitutes. Registration closes a week before the dance; call the recreation center front desk on 970-674-3500. The Town's page does not list the price.
