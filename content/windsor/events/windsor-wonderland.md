Windsor Wonderland is Saturday, December 5, 2026, from 4 to 7 pm at Boardwalk Community Park, 100 N. 5th Street: Santa arrives by train at 4, the Mayor lights the tree at 4:15 and the parade goes at 6:30.

## Schedule

- **4 pm:** the event opens and Santa arrives on the Great Western Railway train.
- **4 to 7 pm:** food vendors on 5th Street.
- **4:15 pm:** the Mayor's speech, the holiday proclamation and the tree lighting, at the train depot.
- **4:30 pm:** Santa photos start at the train depot.
- **4:30 to 7 pm:** Christmas crafts and face painting at the Art & Heritage Center; a scavenger hunt at the Art & Heritage Center and the History Museum, with winners emailed afterward; fire pits and s'mores on 5th Street, the s'mores provided by Traverse Christian Church; a live ice-sculpture demonstration at the History Museum; and the Windsor Middle School and Windsor High School choirs at the Market Pavilion and on 5th Street.
- **6:30 pm:** the parade.
- **7 pm:** the event closes.

## Parade route and where to watch

The parade starts at the roundabout and continues down the train depot parking lot. The tree lighting and Santa photos are at the depot too, so the depot is the place to be at 4:15 and again at 6:30.

## This year

The Town's theme for this year's event is "Christmas Vacation".

## Who runs it

The Town of Windsor's Parks, Recreation & Culture department, at 970-674-3500; its weather line is 970-674-3530. Groups that want to join the parade apply through the form on the Town's Windsor Wonderland page.
