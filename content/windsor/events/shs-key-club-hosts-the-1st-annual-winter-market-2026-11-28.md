The first Winter Market at Severance High School, hosted by the school's Key Club: Saturday, November 28, from 9 am to 3 pm at the school on Hidden Valley Parkway, open to the public. The school's listing promises more than 50 vendors, with handcrafted woodworking, metal and laser-engraved pieces, holiday items, baked goods, pet products, puzzles, books, tumblers and hair bows, plus face painting for children and local food trucks through the day. Santa is on site from 11 am to 3 pm. The Key Club asks the town to come out and support its students and the local small businesses selling.
