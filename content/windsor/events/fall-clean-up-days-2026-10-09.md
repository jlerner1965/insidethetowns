The Town's annual two-day drop-off at the Public Services Campus on 15th Street: residents drive up, unload their own materials at the corresponding stations and drive on. Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10, 9 am to 3 pm each day. The Town's calendar listing does not say what is and is not accepted, so check it before loading the truck.
