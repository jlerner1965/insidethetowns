The Town's annual two-day drop-off at the Public Services Campus on 15th Street: residents drive up, unload their own materials at the corresponding stations and drive on. Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10, 9 am to 3 pm each day. Bring the entry voucher printed on the back page of the fall Windsor Matters magazine, or a driver's license or utility bill as proof of residency. Accepted: general household trash; yard and garden waste, grass clippings and branches up to four inches across; scrap metal; up to four car tires without rims per voucher; stoves, ovens, washers, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, grills and bicycles. Not accepted: mixed recycling, hazardous waste, electronics, propane tanks and gas cylinders, refrigerators or anything else containing freon, construction debris, pallets, vehicles, commercial or tractor tires, light bulbs, mirrors, window glass and other non-recyclable glass, animal carcasses, mattresses and large furniture.
