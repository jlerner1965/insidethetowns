The Clearview Library District's Windsor branch runs its storytimes on the Every Child Ready to Read practices, reading, writing, talking, singing and playing, with plenty of moving. **Tuesdays**: Read, Rhyme, and Romp at 10, an active storytime for ages 0 to 5, then Stay and Play, open-ended playtime, from 10:30. **Fridays**: Babies and Books at 9:30, a lap-sit storytime for birth to 2, then Stay and Play for babies and toddlers at 10. **Every other Wednesday** at 10 (October 28, November 11 and 25, December 9), Adaptive Storytime with ASL is sensory-friendly and built for children of all abilities; it takes registration to keep the group small. On the other Wednesdays, **Lil' STEAM** (ages 3 to 5, November 4 and 18, December 2 and 16) pairs a story with a science or art activity, and **Messy Monster Monday** (ages 2 to 5, November 2 and 16, December 7) is process art; both register each child. **Thursdays at the Library** is an open house from 10 to 11:30 on select Thursdays, November 12 and December 10 next. The library's StoryWalk at Eastman Park Pond changes its book every other month, with a storytime there on December 17. Readers can also sign up for 1000 Books Before Kindergarten at the desk or online.
