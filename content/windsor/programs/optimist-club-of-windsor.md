The club calls itself "a group of people who care about kids" and says it has been "working with kids in Windsor, Colorado since 1973". Its project pages cover Kindness Week, the Easter Egg Hunt, an essay contest, an oratorical contest, Kids Speak Out, Bike Night, the Duck Race, Safe Halloween, Santa's Toy Shop and scholarships.

**Visit.** The club meets in person on Thursdays at 7:30 am at the [Windsor Community Recreation Center](/places/community-recreation-center/). Each meeting has "a new speaker or program", and the club's page says "Feel free to join us as our guest this week!"

**Join.** "The club is open to all adults", the club says. Its Become a Member page gives three first steps: attend a meeting, come to an event, or speak to a current member. It also mentions a mentorship program for new members. Reach the club at 970-795-2301 or windsoroptimistclub@gmail.com.
