Weld RE-4, the school district for most of Windsor and all of Severance, enrolls preschoolers who are 3 or 4 years old on or before October 1. Families first complete the state's Colorado Universal Preschool Program application. An automated email follows with the next step: the required documents, proof of residency among them, go in the district's portal, and the child's identification is shown in person at the school. The district's registration help desk answers at 970-686-8045.
