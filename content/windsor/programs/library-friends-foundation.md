The Friends & Foundation is a 501(c)(3) that supports the [Clearview Library District](/places/clearview-library/). Its Support Us page says donations pay for "library programs, events, resources, and initiatives that go beyond the district's operating budget", and names author talks, summer reading and staff support programs among them.

**Volunteer.** The page says volunteers help at events, support fundraising, assist with community outreach and lend behind-the-scenes support. It asks people to complete the volunteer interest form on the [Support Us page](https://cldfriends.org/support/) to hear about upcoming opportunities. The library district's own volunteer page says the group "is always looking for volunteers to help support the library through fundraising efforts".

**Other ways in.** The same page takes donations and has an interest form for businesses that want to sponsor an event. The site also has a Become a Friend membership page. The group's email is friends@cldfriends.org.
