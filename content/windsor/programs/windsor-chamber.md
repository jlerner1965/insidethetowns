The Chamber's home page lists its regular gatherings under "Get Involved":

- **New Member Mixer**: first Wednesday of the month, 4 to 6 pm, at the Chamber office.
- **Wake Up Windsor**: first Friday of the month, 8 to 9:30 am, at rotating locations.
- **Strength of Windsor**: second Tuesday of the month, 4 to 6 pm, at The Mill Tavern; registration required.
- **Windsor's Leading Edge**: second and fourth Thursdays, 8:30 to 10 am, at The American Legion.
- **Women of Windsor**: third Tuesday of the month, 5:30 to 7:30 pm, at Tiho's; registration required because of capacity.

Dates and places for each one are on the Chamber's [event calendar](https://business.windsorchamber.net/events/).

**Membership.** The Chamber's [membership levels page](https://www.windsorchamber.net/membership-levels/) lists business levels from Foundation, at $300 for up to 5 employees, and a nonprofit level at $225 for up to 15 employees. Every level includes a directory listing, member pricing for networking events, access to the Leads Group, and eligibility "to attend and volunteer at signature events".

**Ambassadors.** The Chamber's Ambassadors welcome new members and attend ribbon cuttings, grand openings and Town festivals. The Ambassadors page says there is a waiting list to join, and to contact the Chamber.
