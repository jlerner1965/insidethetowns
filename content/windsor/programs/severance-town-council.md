**Go to a meeting.** The Town's page says the Council "meets in the Severance Town Hall's Council meeting room at 5:00 p.m. on the second and fourth Tuesday of each month for a Work Session and Regular Council meeting", and the regular meeting follows the work session or starts by 6 pm. The Town's calendar lists meetings on November 10 and December 8, and marks November 24 and December 22 as canceled. Agendas and minutes are on the Town's [Agendas & Minutes page](https://www.townofseverance.org/129/Agendas-Minutes).

**Speak.** The Town's public input sheet, which covers the Town Council and the Planning Commission, says to sign in with the Town Clerk to comment, speak into the microphone and state your name and address for the record. Meetings are recorded and posted on the Town's website and YouTube page, and a person who joins by Zoom may appear by name in the recording.

**Write.** The mayor's welcome on the same page says residents who cannot attend can reach the Council by email; the address for the mayor and the whole Council is towncouncil@townofseverance.org. For a disability accommodation at a meeting, the Town asks for a call to 970-686-1218 at least 72 hours ahead.
