The Town teaches swimming on the Starfish Aquatics Institute's program, with instructors trained and certified by the institute. **StarBabies** (6 months to 3 years, with an adult in the water) is about comfort in the water and water safety for the grown-up. **Swim School** (3 to 8) works through five levels, from White, for children not yet comfortable putting their face in, to Green, which readies them for strokes; children move up at their own pace. **Stroke School** (5 to 12) is for children who can already swim a length and tread water for 30 seconds, and teaches the four strokes. The registration site lists half-hour classes on Monday and Wednesday or Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 5:10 pm, and on Saturday mornings from 8:10, in blocks running to November 21; most weekday blocks are $60 for residents and $75 for others, and four Saturdays $30 and $37.50. Private lessons are also offered. The activity pool is closed to open swim from 5 to 7 pm Monday to Thursday and 8 to 10 am on Saturdays while lessons run.
