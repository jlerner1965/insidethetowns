The bookmobile carries its own collection, books, DVDs, video games and graphic novels with new titles each month, which stays on board rather than going to the branches. Borrow straight off the bus, return anywhere in the district, and put a hold on anything in the catalog with "Bookmobile" as the pickup to have it brought to the stop nearest home. A library card can be had on board; any Colorado resident can get one with a photo ID and proof of address, with a parent signing for children under 14. The bins outside take returns, including Weld RE-4 and Windsor Charter Academy school library books. The weekly round, as the calendar lists it: **Mondays** Grandview Elementary at 2:30, then Northern Lights Park, Windshire Park and Covenant Park; **Tuesdays** Mountain Shadows Community Park at 10:30 am, then Orchard Hill Elementary at 2:30, Ravina Park, Greenspire and The Ridge Park; **Wednesdays** Poudre Heights Park at 2:30, Founders Green Park, Cherry Park at RainDance and Pelican Farms Park; **Thursdays** Hollister Lake Elementary at 2:30, Overlook Park in Severance (650 Audubon Blvd) at 3:30, Village East Park and Promontory Community Park; **Fridays** Range View Elementary at 2:30. Each stop is half an hour, an hour at Mountain Shadows.
