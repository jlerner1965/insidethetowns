Windsor High's winter sports are **boys' basketball, girls' basketball, girls' swimming, unified basketball and wrestling**, and registration for 2026–27 opened October 19 at 8 am. The athletics page links everything a family needs: the winter registration instructions, the Arbiter Sports family login, the CHSAA physical (valid for a calendar year and uploaded to the student's profile), the athletic handbook and the district's refund policy. Students from another school who play for Windsor High file a non-WHS student form before their first practice. The district requires every athlete to have accident insurance and points families to K & K Insurance's student plans. The Wizards' records, scores and next games are on [this guide's sports page](/sports/); the booster club is at [wizardboosters.com](https://www.wizardboosters.com/).
