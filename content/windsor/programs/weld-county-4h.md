4-H in Weld County is run by Colorado State University Extension, with more than 50 projects to choose from, from model rocketry, photography, cooking and woodworking to dogs, horses and market livestock. Young people join a club, pick their projects, and enroll each year through 4-H Online, which opened for 2026–27 on October 1 and closes at 10:59 pm on March 1, 2027. The county fee, $50 for a member and $40 for a Cloverbud, covers insurance and the project manuals; clubs may charge small dues. The county's list for the Severance and Windsor area has one club, the **Severance 4-H Club**, with five co-leaders reached at severance4h@gmail.com; it meets on the second Monday of the month at Calvary Severance Church. Members taking a market or breeding animal project for the first time need the Colorado Meat Quality Assurance class; the county's fall session is October 25. The Weld County Extension office is on the second floor of the Exhibition Building, 525 N 15th Ave in Greeley.
