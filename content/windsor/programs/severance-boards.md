**[Citizen Advisory Board](https://www.townofseverance.org/159/Citizen-Advisory-Board).** Seven citizens of the Town and one alternate, serving two-year terms. The Town says the board is there "to advocate for and inform the community by providing research and recommendations on local issues to the Town Council". It meets at 6 pm on the first Wednesday of each month. The Town's page lists one member seat as vacant, with a term that ends in December 2026. The page does not give an application form; call Town Hall at 970-686-1218 to ask about the seat.

**[Planning Commission](https://www.townofseverance.org/165/Planning-Commission).** A chairperson, a vice-chair and five members, appointed by the Town Council to four-year terms. The commission reviews land use applications under the Severance Municipal Code and advises the Council on planning items. It meets at 6 pm on the third Wednesday of each month. To comment at a Planning Commission meeting, the Town's public input sheet says to sign in with the Town Clerk and state your name and address for the record.

Agendas and minutes for both are on the Town's [Agendas & Minutes page](https://www.townofseverance.org/129/Agendas-Minutes). Teens have their own body, the [Youth Council](https://www.townofseverance.org/413/Youth-Council).
