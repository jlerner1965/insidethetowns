WISE stands for Windsor Inspiring Successful & Engaged Professionals. The Chamber calls it "a collaborative network designed to elevate young professionals in Northern Colorado through meaningful connection, growth, and community impact". It is "open to both Windsor Chamber Members and non-members".

**Who it is for.** The Chamber's list: people "between the ages of 21 and 43 ish" who want to meet other young professionals, are interested in personal and professional growth, and want "to make an impact in your community".

**What it costs.** $75 a year for each member who belongs to the Chamber and $125 a year for each non-member. The page says membership "covers most events". Businesses can buy a plan that sends different staff to each event: $475 for up to 3 spots, $900 for up to 6 and $1,450 for up to 10. WISE membership is separate from Chamber membership.

**How to join.** Use the join link on the Chamber's [WISE page](https://www.windsorchamber.net/get-involved-windsor-area-chamber-of-commerce/wise-ypn/), or email katie@windsorchamber.net with questions. Upcoming WISE events are on the Chamber's event calendar.
