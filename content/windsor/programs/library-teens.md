The library's teen calendar across both branches. **In Windsor**: Teen Dungeons & Dragons, a monthly campaign with the library's Dungeon Master and a chance to learn the basics, every other Saturday from noon to 4, no registration; **Teens Cook**, making and eating something together once a month, [November 2](/events/teens-cook-spice-cookies-2026-11-02/) and December 7 from 4:30 to 6 (registration required); after-hours **video gaming** on Fridays November 13 and December 11, 5 to 7; **Teen DIY** crafts, on November 3 glazing mugs at Chill Potz on Main Street; and Common Ground, an evening to meet other teens. **In Severance**: the **Teen Cookbook Club**, which cooks a recipe from a featured book, on Tuesdays November 10 and 17 and December 8 and 15 from 4:30 to 5:45 (registration required), and Teen DIY on December 1. The **Teen Advisory Group** helps plan the library's programs and events and counts toward volunteer hours. Adulting 101, a monthly workshop, is on professional communication on November 12.
