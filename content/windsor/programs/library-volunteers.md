The library district's volunteer page lists three ways in.

**Teens, 12 to 18.** Teen volunteers "can earn credited volunteer hours and gain work experience". The work includes shelving and straightening materials, preparing activities for programs, and helping with programs and special projects. Email the Teen Librarian at amy@clearviewlibrary.org.

**Youth services, 18 and over.** Volunteers prepare crafts for children's programs, pull materials from the collection, help at children's programs and events, and keep the toys in the children's area clean and in order. They do not plan or run programs. Apply on the library's [youth services volunteer form](https://clearviewlibrary.org/form/youth-services-volunteer), or email the Youth Services Supervising Librarian at becca@clearviewlibrary.org.

**Other adults.** The page says "New adult volunteer applications are currently on pause as our team works to reimagine the opportunities we are able to offer our volunteers", and asks people to check back. It points adults to the Clearview Library District Friends & Foundation, which "is always looking for volunteers to help support the library through fundraising efforts".

**The board.** The Library Board of Trustees "regularly meets on the last Thursday of every month at 5:30 p.m." It has five trustees and an alternate, with liaisons from the Weld RE-4 School District, the Town of Windsor, the Town of Severance and the City of Greeley. The [board's page](https://clearviewlibrary.org/about-us/learn-more/board-of-trustees) links the meeting schedule, packets and videos.
