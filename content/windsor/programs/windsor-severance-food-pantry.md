The pantry serves residents of the Weld RE-4 school district, which takes in Windsor and Severance. Each visit brings a box of about 30 pounds of non-perishable food, a bag of flour, sugar and other staples, a toiletry bag and frozen meat, with more for larger families; clients may come once every four weeks. In summer it also gives out fresh produce from the Treasure Island Demonstration Garden on Tuesday and Thursday mornings. The pantry hosts the Weld Mobile Food Truck on the first Wednesday of every month at 1020 Walnut Street, with food handed out from 10 am. Call 970-329-1588.
