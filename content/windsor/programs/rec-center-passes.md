The Town's 85,000-square-foot center on 11th Street has two gyms, fitness rooms, an indoor track and the indoor pool: three lap lanes, a lazy river, an enclosed water slide (40 inches to ride), a zero-depth play pool and a hot tub. Admission covers all of it. The play features and the slide come on at 10 am; the activity pool is closed to open swim from 5 to 7 pm Monday to Thursday and 8 to 10 am on Saturdays for swim lessons, though the lap pool and hot tub stay open. **Day passes** are $5 under 5, $6 under 21, $7 for adults and $6 for 62 and up, or $98 for 20 visits; a **family membership** for two adults and two children under 21 is $693 a year or $69 a month, with $72 a year for each extra child, and active military and veterans get 20 percent off. **Childcare** for children from 12 months to 9 years, while a parent uses the center, is $3 a child for members and $5 otherwise, up to 90 minutes, weekdays 8 am to 1:15 pm, Monday to Thursday 5 to 8 pm and Saturdays 8 to noon; bring the signed waiver the first time. Preschool open gym is $1 for members and $3 otherwise.
