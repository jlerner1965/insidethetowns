Windsor's recreation basketball teaches the fundamentals in a team setting, and every player gets a reversible Nuggets jersey and one ticket to a Nuggets game. The **girls' season** (kindergarten through 8th grade) and the boys' kindergarten and 1st-grade pee-wee divisions are under way, October 19 to December 12. The **boys' winter league** for 2nd through 8th grade is open for registration now: divisions for each grade from 2nd to 6th and one for 7th and 8th, practices from the week of January 18, games on Saturdays from January 30 to March 6, $140 for Windsor residents and $165 for others. The registration site also lists a boys' league for 9th to 12th grade, January 11 to February 28, with the price shown at checkout. Schedules go up on the Town's [Team Sideline site](https://www.teamsideline.com/sites/windsor/home) during the season, and the game-day weather line is 970-674-3530.

Over winter break, a two-day **Winter Basketball Camp** for 1st to 8th graders runs December 28 and 29, 9 to 11 am at the Community Recreation Center, $75, with coach Bruce Dick. For the youngest, **Start Smart** basketball for ages 4 and 5, with a parent at every session, registers from early December to mid-February and meets in March. Windsor families who qualify by income can apply for reduced fees, and Severance residents under 18 for up to $100 toward youth sports; see the [Reduced Fee Program](/families/#program-reduced-fee-program).
