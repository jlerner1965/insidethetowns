The library's passes, each reserved online for a date a month or less ahead, as the library describes them: the **Butterfly Pavilion** (two free, then $5 each for up to three more), the **Climbing Collective** in Greeley, Longmont and Loveland (all day for two, gear not included), the **Colorado Model Railroad Museum** in Greeley, the **Denver Botanic Gardens** and Chatfield Farms (up to six, daytime only), the **Fort Collins Museum of Discovery** (up to four), the **Gardens on Spring Creek** (up to eight, not Garden of Lights), the **History Colorado Center** (one adult free and $5 off for up to three more; under-18s get in free), **Lil Monsters Playground** (two children and two adults, all day, good for seven days), the **Loveland Museum**, the **Museum of Art Fort Collins**, and **Rogue Play** trampoline and ninja center in Greeley (four people at open play, with a signed waiver and trampoline socks). Any Colorado resident can get a Clearview card with a photo ID and proof of address.
