The Town of Severance lists Community Park at 100 Waterfowl Way and says on its facility page that it is under construction, with opening expected in late fall, weather permitting. The Town Christmas page and the Town's Community Events calendar set Town Christmas there on December 12, 2026, 2 to 5 pm, with Santa and Ms. Claus, vendor booths, horse-drawn carriage rides and a fireworks show to close.
