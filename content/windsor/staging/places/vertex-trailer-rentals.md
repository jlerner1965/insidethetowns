A new, locally owned trailer rental lot on East Collins in Eaton. There is no counter: you book on the website, Vertex confirms the trailer and the price by email and sends a lockbox code, and you pick up and return at any hour. Delivery can be requested, priced by distance.

The fleet on the site is seven trailers: an 18-foot utility trailer, a 16-foot enclosed trailer, 12- and 14-foot dump trailers, a 20-foot deckover, a 22-foot car hauler and a 36-foot gooseneck. Rates as posted run from $95 a day for the utility trailer to $225 for the gooseneck, with weekly and monthly rates. Chains and binders come with the flatbed, car hauler and dump trailers, and a winch or adjustable hitch can be added. You need a driver's license, a vehicle rated to tow the loaded trailer, the right hitch ball and a working 7-pin plug, and a card for the deposit.
