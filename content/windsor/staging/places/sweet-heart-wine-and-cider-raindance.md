Sweet Heart Winery, of Loveland and Fort Collins, is opening Sweet Heart Wine & Cider at RainDance, 2191 Covered Bridge Pkwy. The winery calls it the flagship taproom for its hard ciders (Climb Hard, Patriot Press, Sweetheart and Black Bear), with a full kitchen, weekend brunch and live music; a limited menu comes first, and full restaurant service follows the public opening. Families are welcome, with children staying with an adult, and leashed dogs are welcome outdoors. A two-story event center of about 5,000 square feet is planned beside it for summer 2027.
