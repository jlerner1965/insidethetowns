Here is what closes between Thanksgiving week and the first week of January, and what is on, from the pages the library district, the two towns and the school district publish.

## The libraries

The Clearview Library District runs the Windsor Library at 720 3rd Street and the Severance Library at 5 Timber Ridge Parkway. Its [closures list](https://clearviewlibrary.org/about-us/learn-more/hours-locations) for the rest of 2026:

- **Wednesday, November 25:** early closing at 2 pm.
- **Thursday, November 26:** closed for Thanksgiving Day.
- **Friday, December 4:** the Windsor Library closes early, at 3 pm, to prepare for the Clearview Frostival.
- **Friday, December 18:** early closing at 1:30 pm.
- **Thursday and Friday, December 24 and 25:** closed for Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
- **Thursday, December 31:** early closing at 2 pm for New Year's Eve.

On other days the regular hours hold. The Windsor Library is open Monday to Thursday 9 am to 7 pm, Friday and Saturday 9 am to 5 pm and Sunday 1 to 5 pm. The Severance Library is open Monday to Thursday 10 am to 6 pm and Friday and Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, and is closed Sunday.

## Town of Severance offices

The [Town's calendar](https://www.townofseverance.org/calendar.aspx) closes Town Hall, Public Works and the administrative offices at the Police Department on these days:

- **Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27**, for Thanksgiving.
- **Thursday and Friday, December 24 and 25**, for Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
- **Friday, January 1**, for New Year's Day.

The Town Council's second meetings of November and December, on November 24 and December 22, are canceled on the same calendar. Its December meeting is [Tuesday, December 8](/events/town-council-public-meetings-oct-dec-2026/), with a work session at 5 pm and the regular meeting at 6 pm at Town Hall, 3 S. Timber Ridge Parkway.

Trash and recycling in Severance are not a Town service. The Town says independent companies provide them, some under a contract with a homeowners association or metro district, so the holiday-week pickup day comes from your hauler.

## Windsor's recreation center

The Town of Windsor's calendar lists two closed days this season for the [Community Recreation Center](/places/community-recreation-center/) at 250 N 11th Street: Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, and New Year's Day, Friday, January 1. Parks, Recreation & Culture's number is 970-674-3500.

## Schools

The Weld RE-4 School District's [calendar](https://weldre4.org/en-US/calendar) has these days off:

- **Friday, November 20:** no school for elementary students; teachers report.
- **Monday to Friday, November 23 to 27:** no school, fall break.
- **Friday, December 18:** no school; teachers report.
- **Monday, December 21 to Tuesday, January 5:** no school, winter break.
- **Wednesday, January 6:** no school; teachers report.

This is Weld RE-4's calendar; a family in another district should check its own. Camp Windsor, the Town of Windsor's day camp for school-out days, is on the [Families page](/families/#program-camp-windsor).

## What is on

- **Thursday, November 26:** the [Windsor Turkey Trot 5K](/events/windsor-turkey-trot/) at Pelican Lakes Golf Course, 1600 Pelican Lakes Point. The run starts at 9 am and the walk at 9:05. Entry is $43.40 through November 20 and $48.70 after, and $22.20 for ages 1 to 12.
- **Saturday, November 28:** the Severance High School Key Club's first [Winter Market](/events/severance-high-school-winter-market-2026-11-28/), 9 am to 3 pm at the school, 1200 Hidden Valley Parkway, with more than 50 vendors and Santa from 11 am to 3 pm.
- **Saturday, December 5:** [Windsor Wonderland](/events/windsor-wonderland/) at Boardwalk Park, 4 to 7 pm. Santa arrives by train at 4, the Mayor lights the tree at the depot at 4:15, and the parade goes at 6:30.
- **Saturday, December 12:** the [Santa Catch 5K and 10K](/events/santa-catch-5k-10k-2026-12-12/) from Boardwalk Park; the 10K starts at 9 am and the 5K at 9:30.
- **Saturday, December 12:** Severance's [Town Christmas](/events/town-christmas-2026-12-12/) at Community Park, 2 to 5 pm, with Santa and Ms. Claus, horse-drawn carriage rides and fireworks planned to close.
- **Saturday, December 19:** [Wreaths Across America](/events/wreaths-across-america-2026-12-19/) at Lakeview Cemetery, 10 to 11 am. Volunteers lay wreaths on veterans' graves; wreaths and instructions are provided.
- **Thursday, December 31:** [New Year's Eve at Bruce's Bar](/events/new-years-eve-bruces-bar-2026-12-31/) in Severance, 11 am to 9 pm.
