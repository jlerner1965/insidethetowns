The Longmont Museum's galleries closed for more than a year for a major expansion and renovation, and the City set the free Grand Reopening for Saturday, October 17. The nearly $10 million expansion adds 70 percent more public gallery space. New on reopening were DreamLab, a permanent hands-on exhibit in Lila's Children's Gallery with a dragon castle and a crystal cave; "Frida Kahlo: An Intimate Portrait," 98 photographs with letters and notes from Kahlo's private collection, in the new Bill Stewart Feature Gallery; and "Women Artists: Four Centuries of Creativity." More than 450 donors gave to the capital campaign, the Stewart Family Foundation more than $6 million of it. The museum's Plan Your Visit page gives hours of Monday to Saturday 9 am to 5 pm and Sunday 1 to 5 pm, with most Thursday evenings to 9 pm, and admission of $12 for adults and $8 for youth, under 2 and members free, $1 with a qualifying EBT or SNAP card, and free on the second Saturday of each month.
