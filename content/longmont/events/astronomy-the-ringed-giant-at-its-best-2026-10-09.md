Saturn at its best: a talk at the picnic shelter at Rabbit Mountain (the Ron Stewart Preserve), then stargazing in the parking area through the Longmont Astronomical Society's telescopes. Bring a chair or blanket, a jacket and a red flashlight. Registration required with Boulder County Parks & Open Space.
