The Longmont Senior Center turns 50 with a day-long open house on Tuesday, November 17, 8 am to 8 pm. A welcome lunch runs from 11:30 to 1:30, with Longmont Museum staff showing Longmont artifacts from 1976 to 2026 and library staff on the city's history over the same years. Through the day the center screens "Where We Meet: Stories of Healing and Hope," a 17-minute film about Longmont's Elder Exchange with members of the Northern Arapaho tribe from the Wind River Reservation in Wyoming, and the galleries show old Go catalogs and Senior Center artifacts. The afternoon brings an ice cream social with cake and music.
