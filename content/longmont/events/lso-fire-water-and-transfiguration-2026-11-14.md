The second masterworks concert of the Longmont Symphony's 60th season, at 7 pm on Saturday, November 14 at Vance Brand Civic Auditorium on the Skyline High School campus. Elliot Moore conducts Smetana's The Moldau, Strauss's Death and Transfiguration and the world premiere of Massimiliano Messieri's Saxophone Concerto with soloist Alex Sebastiannuto, which the orchestra is recording live. Ticket prices are not on the season page; buy through the orchestra's ticketing link.
