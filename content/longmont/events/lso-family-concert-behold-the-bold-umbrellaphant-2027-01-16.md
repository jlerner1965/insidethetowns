The Longmont Symphony's family concert at 4 pm on Saturday, January 16 at Vance Brand Civic Auditorium: Lucas Richman's Behold the Bold Umbrellaphant, set to Jack Prelutsky's children's poems, plus the Longmont Youth Symphony playing side by side with the orchestra in the finale of Tchaikovsky's Fourth Symphony. The winner of the orchestra's November concerto competition also performs. Ticket prices are not on the season page; buy through the orchestra's ticketing link.
