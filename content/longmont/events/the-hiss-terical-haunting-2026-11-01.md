Garden of the Cats' Hiss-terical Haunting, a TICA-sponsored championship and household-pet cat show with 14 judges in 14 rings, at the Boulder County Fairgrounds, Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November, open to the public 9 am to 4 pm both days. There are also information booths and vendors. Entry is $10, $25 for a family of four and $5 for military. Garden of the Cats, a nonprofit animal-welfare group that works in Southern Colorado, gives part of the proceeds to a shelter or rescue.
