A guided tour of Las Barracas, a historic farm labor camp near Longmont, with a Boulder County Parks & Open Space cultural resources specialist: the Japanese American farmers who ran the farm, the Mexican and Mexican American families who lived and worked there, and the work to document and preserve it. For Hispanic Heritage Month; registration required, and the location is sent on registration.
