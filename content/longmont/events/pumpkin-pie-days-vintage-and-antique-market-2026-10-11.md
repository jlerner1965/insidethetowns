The St. Vrain Historical Society's autumn vintage and antique market: more than 80 dealers selling antiques and collectibles in the Exhibit Building at the Boulder County Fairgrounds, Saturday 10 October 10 am to 5 pm and Sunday 11 am to 4 pm (society members get in at 9:30 am on Saturday). Admission is paid at the door; it and the café fund the society's historic preservation and education work in Longmont.
