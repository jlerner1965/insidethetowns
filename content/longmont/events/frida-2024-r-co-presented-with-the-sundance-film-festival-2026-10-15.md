The Longmont Museum and the Sundance Film Festival screen Frida (2024, R, 87 minutes), Carla Gutiérrez's documentary about Frida Kahlo told in the artist's own words, followed by a conversation with Gutiérrez and filmmaker MG Evangelista. A meet-and-greet with the filmmakers and festival staff starts at 6 pm.
