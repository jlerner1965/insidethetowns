The Longmont Lights Holiday Parade, themed Merry Memories this year, steps off from Roosevelt Park at 5 pm on Saturday, December 12, 2026, and runs down Main Street with lighted floats, bands and dancers. The City lists it from 5 to 7 pm.

## Route and where to watch

The City's event page links to the altered route it has used while Coffman Street is under construction, from 2024 through 2026. The parade is staged around Roosevelt Park, crosses Coffman Street on Longs Peak Avenue to Main Street, turns right and heads down Main, turns left at 4th Avenue, comes back up Kimbark Street and returns to the park along Longs Peak Avenue. Anywhere on Main Street between Longs Peak and 4th avenues, or on Kimbark Street on the way back, is on the route.

## Road closures and parking

The City's route notice says Main Street is closed down to 3rd Avenue for every parade, though the route turns at 4th. That notice, written in 2024, put the start of parking restrictions and road closures at noon on the day of the holiday parade. The City has not posted closure times for this year.

## Santa and the floats

The City's rules allow one Santa in the parade, supplied by the sponsors. Nothing is thrown from floats, candy included. Towed floats carry at least 4,000 lights each, and all entries are eligible for prizes. NextLight sponsors the parade.

## Entering, and who runs it

City of Longmont Recreation Services, 303-651-8404. The deadline to enter a float is Tuesday, December 1, and proof of insurance is due by December 10. The holiday lights in Roosevelt Park are on nightly from late November into early January.
