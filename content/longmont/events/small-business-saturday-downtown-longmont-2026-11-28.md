Downtown Longmont's Small Business Saturday on November 28, with roaming holiday characters, surprise giveaways, a warming station, free gift-wrap supplies and snacks for shoppers. The holiday page adds that shoppers can collect a set of 12 historic Longmont postcards, one free with purchase at each participating business.
