Light Up Longmont turns on downtown's holiday lights on Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 5 to 7 pm, starting at the Roosevelt Park Ice Pavilion and walking with Santa to the 6th Avenue Plaza tree and St. Stephen's Plaza. Downtown Longmont's page lists no admission charge.

## Schedule

- **5 to 7 pm:** fire pit and hot cocoa at the Ice Pavilion in Roosevelt Park, 700 Longs Peak Avenue.
- **5:30 pm:** Santa and a cast of holiday characters light Roosevelt Park.
- **6 pm:** they light the tree at the 6th Avenue Plaza.
- **6:30 pm:** they light St. Stephen's Plaza, at 5th Avenue and Main Street.
- **6 to 7 pm:** music, crafts and cookies at St. Stephen's Plaza.

## Where to be

Start at the Ice Pavilion for the first lighting at 5:30, then follow Santa and the characters on foot to the other two stops. Each lighting is half an hour after the last, so you can also skip the walk and wait at the 6th Avenue Plaza for 6 pm or at St. Stephen's Plaza for 6:30.

## New in 2026

Downtown Longmont calls this a new format for its holiday lighting: one evening that moves between three places instead of a single ceremony. It opens the downtown holiday calendar, which runs to the Longmont Lights parade on Saturday, December 12.

## Who runs it

Downtown Longmont, the Longmont Downtown Development Authority, whose office is at 515 Kimbark Street, Suite 106. Its page gives no parking, road closure or weather information for the evening.
