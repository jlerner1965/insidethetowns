A guided tour in Spanish of Las Barracas, a historic farm labor camp near Longmont, with a Boulder County Parks & Open Space cultural resources specialist: the Japanese American farmers who ran the farm, the Mexican and Mexican American families who lived and worked there, and the work to preserve it. For adults 18 and over; registration required, and the location is sent on registration.
