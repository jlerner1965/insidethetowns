A vegan-friendly market of local food and goods vendors at the Oskar Blues taproom in Longmont, hosted by Flora + Fauna Experiences on the second Monday of each month this fall; the November date is Monday, November 9 from 5:30 to 8:30 pm. Proceeds support Luvin Arms Animal Sanctuary, and pre-registering enters a 7 pm drawing for a goodie bag. No admission is mentioned.
