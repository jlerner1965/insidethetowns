CyclErie, the Erie bike and e-bike shop, opens its second shop on Main Street with a ribbon cutting at 4 pm alongside the Longmont Area Chamber of Commerce. Stay on after to meet the sales and service teams, tour the shop, and test-ride e-bikes and bikes from Aventon, Specialized, Woom and Giant through Roosevelt Park, a block away. There are non-alcoholic drinks, door prizes and a raffle. Free, and everyone is welcome.
