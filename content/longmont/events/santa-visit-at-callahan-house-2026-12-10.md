Santa visits the holiday-decorated Callahan House, 312 Terry Street, on Thursday, December 10. Space is limited and visits are assigned by lottery: enter online from November 9 to 15, or call 303-774-4700. Free; donations are appreciated. To see the decorations without Santa, the [Holiday Open House](/events/holiday-open-house-at-callahan-house-2026-12-03/) is on Thursday, December 3.
