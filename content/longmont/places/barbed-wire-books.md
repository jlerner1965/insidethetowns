Barbed Wire Books stocks new and used books with an emphasis on local and Colorado-interest titles, and its Western Americana section is a big one. It also carries maps of the area and guides to local flora, fauna, geology and weather. Barb Dwyer, the store's spokesperson figure, holds court in the front window promoting the books of the moment.
