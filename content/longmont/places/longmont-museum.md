The City-run museum's collection includes more than 17,000 objects and 10,000 photographs relating to Longmont and the St. Vrain Valley. A nearly $10 million expansion added gallery space, hands-on experiences and places to gather; a Frida Kahlo exhibition and a new hands-on children's gallery, DreamLab, open with the galleries. The galleries were closed for the construction until the free all-day Grand Reopening on Saturday, October 17, 2026 (9 am to 5 pm, ribbon-cutting at 9); from that day the City's [fall museum brochure](https://longmontcolorado.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/f26_museum.pdf) gives regular hours of Monday to Saturday 9 am to 5 pm and Sunday 1 to 5 pm, with most Thursdays open to 9 pm, closed November 26, and admission of $12 for adults and $8 for youth, under 2 and members free, $1 with a qualifying EBT or SNAP card, and free days on November 14 and December 12 (read 14 October 2026). It also hosts concerts, films, talks and classes for adults and children.
