The City-run museum's collection includes more than 17,000 objects and 10,000 photographs relating to Longmont and the St. Vrain Valley. A nearly $10 million expansion added gallery space, hands-on experiences and places to gather; the galleries, closed for the construction, reopened with a free all-day Grand Reopening on Saturday, October 17, 2026, along with a new hands-on children's gallery, DreamLab, and a Frida Kahlo exhibition that runs through January 31. The City's [Plan Your Visit](https://longmontcolorado.gov/museum/about-the-museum/plan-your-visit/) page gives regular hours of Monday to Saturday 9 am to 5 pm and Sunday 1 to 5 pm, with most Thursday evenings open to 9 pm, and admission of $12 for adults and $8 for youth, under 2 and members free, $1 with a qualifying EBT or SNAP card, and free on the second Saturday of each month (read 19 October 2026); the [fall museum brochure](https://longmontcolorado.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/f26_museum.pdf) adds that it is closed November 26 (read 14 October 2026). It also hosts concerts, films, talks and classes for adults and children.
