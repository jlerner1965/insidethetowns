Rosalee's sells lunch slices from 11 am to 2 pm and runs happy hour from 2 to 6 pm. It is a small shop with a limited number of takeout time slots, so it suggests calling early (phone lines open at 11 am); Friday and Saturday 5–7:30 pm pickups fill up first, and it encourages prepaying by card over the phone. It stays open until 8 pm or until the dough sells out.
