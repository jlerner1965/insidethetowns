Now run as the Supper Club @ Johnson's Station, it serves burgers, crispy chicken and upscale American plates, with a full bar of classic cocktails, house specials and a long beer list. Happy hour is Tue–Sun 3–5 pm, and the restaurant calls itself family-friendly. Table reservations are on Resy, and it books private events.
