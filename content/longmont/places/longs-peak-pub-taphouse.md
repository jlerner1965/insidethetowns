Longs Peak Pub opened in September 2014 with the same menu as its Mountain Sun siblings and 21 beers on tap, more than a dozen of them Mountain Sun ales. It also pours seven wines and Mountain Sun margaritas on tap. A spacious patio with cornhole makes it an easy family stop.
