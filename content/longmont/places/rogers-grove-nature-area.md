The 54.9-acre nature area beside the St. Vrain Greenway includes Fairgrounds Lake, trails, a shelter, an outdoor amphitheater, demonstration gardens and an apple grove whose fruit is free to harvest. Fishing is open to all ages and belly boats are the only boats allowed. Dogs are allowed on leash. Several Art in Public Places works by Robert Tulley stand in and near the grove.
