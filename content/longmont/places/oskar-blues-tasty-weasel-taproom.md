The Tasty Weasel opened in February 2009, and you can see parts of the brewery working from the taproom (there are no tours). Trivia runs Tuesdays 6:30–8:30 pm, six-packs are $6 on Sundays, and there is live music in the taproom plus food trucks through the month; otherwise it's pre-packaged snacks or bring your own food. It's all ages and first come, first served, and leashed, well-behaved dogs are welcome on the patio and inside.
