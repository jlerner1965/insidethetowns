Longmont Theatre Company has put on plays since 1957 and added the Longmont Performing Arts Center at 513 Main St in 1990; its 2026–27 season has six productions, and tickets are sold online and at the box office, which opens an hour before each ticketed show. The LTC Stages Performing Arts Academy runs classes and monthly workshops in acting, improv and stagecraft for young performers. The venue is wheelchair accessible with designated seating, and there is an accessible drop-off zone in front on Main Street.
