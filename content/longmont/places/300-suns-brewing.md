Besides the house beers there's cider, wine and local canned cocktails, and the kitchen makes its food from scratch, including a kids' menu. Dogs are welcome on the deck. Tuesday's Taps & Apps happy hour (3–6 pm) takes half off appetizers and 300 Suns beer, and the events calendar lists regular local live music.
