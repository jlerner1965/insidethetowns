The menu has many vegetarian and gluten-free options plus hot lunch sandwiches and big salads served all day; everything is made from scratch except cheeses, breads and some meats. Baristas make coffee drinks, and there is a full bar. The Longmont location takes reservations by email only for groups of 5 or more on weekdays, and before 10 am on weekends.
