Boulder County Parks & Open Space runs this farm site focused on 1900 to 1925, with a furnished 1909 farmhouse, two barns, an outhouse and milk house, an heirloom garden and chickens, pigs, draft horses and sheep on site April through October. Admission is free, and groups of 8 or more can request a private tour. Picnics are welcome, pets are not (service animals excepted), and all buildings and trails are wheelchair accessible. The McIntosh Lake trail can be reached from the site only during open hours.
