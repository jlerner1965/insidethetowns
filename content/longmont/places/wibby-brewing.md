Wibby brews only lagers, using German malts and long cold conditioning without filtration, and its site lists GABF medals and Brewery of the Year honors. The taproom keeps a weekly lineup: a free fun run Mondays at 6:30 pm, free bingo Thursdays at 6 pm, and sushi from Temaki Tornado on Fridays. Live bands and ticketed shows fill the event calendar, and reservations go through reservations@wibbybrewing.com.
