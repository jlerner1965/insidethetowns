The building was a car dealership, roller rink and service station before the Pumphouse opened in May 1996, and despite the theme it was never a fire station. Happy hour runs Monday to Friday 3–6 pm and again nightly from 9 pm to close, with a beer of the day and daily drink deals. On Saturdays and Sundays it opens at 10 am for brunch and college and NFL football.
