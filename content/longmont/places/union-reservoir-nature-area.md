The 736-acre reservoir offers wakeless boating, paddleboarding, fishing for all ages with a fishing pier, picnic shelters, a playground and sand volleyball. The City says the people swim beach is closed for the season while the dog beach stays open year-round unless there is ice on the water. With water levels the lowest for the time of year since 2012, the City ended the 2026 boating season early, at 6 pm on November 8, for every kind of watercraft; there is no water access from then until February 28. The park itself stays open, 7 am to 6 pm through February, and closes to all entry on Thanksgiving, the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Entry is $10 per vehicle per day ($15 on summer weekends and holidays) plus $5 per watercraft, and the Colorado State Parks Pass is not accepted.
