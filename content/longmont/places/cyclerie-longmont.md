CyclErie, the community bike shop on Briggs Street in Erie, opened this second store on Main Street in downtown Longmont. It sells e-bikes and bikes from Aventon, Specialized, Woom and Giant, services and tunes up whatever you already ride, and takes trade-ins. Test rides head out from the door into Roosevelt Park, one block away, and the downtown bike lanes; walk in while the shop is open, or book a time online and a bike will be fitted and waiting.
