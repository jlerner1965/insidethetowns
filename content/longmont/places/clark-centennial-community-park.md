One of Longmont's largest parks at 48.5 acres, it is home to the indoor Centennial Pool, the Lashley Street Station activity rooms and the Youth Center. It also has a disc golf course, ball and soccer fields, pickleball, a roller hockey rink, a track, paved trails and an accessible playground. Dogs are allowed on leash; the City notes park restrooms are winterized starting Oct. 1.
