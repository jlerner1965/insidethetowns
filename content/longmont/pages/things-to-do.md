  - place: longmont-museum
    for: "Any weather"
    why: "The City's museum of art and history, now open after a nearly $10 million expansion that added 70% more gallery space. Frida Kahlo: An Intimate Portrait runs to January 31, and DreamLab is a new hands-on children's gallery. Adults $12, youth $8, free on second Saturdays; closed November 26."
  - place: mcintosh-lake-nature-area
    for: "A lake loop walk"
    why: "A 3.5-mile trail loops the whole lake, with a spur to the County's Agricultural Heritage Center. Open from an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset. Dogs on a leash, no swimming. Ice buoys now mark the shoreline kept off-limits for nesting and migrating birds."
  - place: st-vrain-greenway
    for: "Walking or cycling"
    why: "About 8 miles of paved and gravel trail along the St. Vrain from Golden Ponds to Sandstone Ranch, linking Roger's Grove, Izaak Walton and Dickens Farm. Open around the clock to people on the move. No dogs on the last mile, east of the County Line trailhead; the City's Trail Status Map shows closures."
  - place: roosevelt-community-park
    for: "Skating in winter"
    why: "Longmont's oldest community park, three city blocks with a rose garden first planted in 1945. The Longmont Ice Pavilion, an outdoor rink under the park's pavilion at 725 8th Ave, is open for the season from November 20, with public skating, hockey and lessons through the winter, weather permitting."
  - place: union-reservoir-nature-area
    for: "Dogs, all year"
    why: "A City reservoir whose dog beach stays open all year unless the water is iced. Low water ended boating early, at 6 pm on November 8, and the water stays closed to February 28. The park is open 7 am to 6 pm through February; $10 a vehicle, and the Colorado State Parks pass is not accepted."
  - place: longmont-theatre-company
    for: "A December night out"
    why: "A theatre company since 1957, at the Longmont Performing Arts Center at 513 Main St since 1990. Its holiday show, The Best Christmas Pageant Ever, runs December 4 to 13, and Agatha Christie's The Unexpected Guest follows in February."
  - place: golden-ponds-nature-area
    for: "Fishing and herons"
    why: "Four ponds on 88 acres, with 2.6 miles of trails, an accessible fishing pier and a view of Longs Peak, at the western trailhead of the St. Vrain Greenway. Great blue herons and cormorants are easy to spot, the City says. Belly boats only; dogs on a leash."
  - place: sandstone-ranch
    for: "Restless kids"
    why: "A 313-acre community park and nature area on Highway 119, a mile east of County Line Road: an adventure playground, a skate park, sports fields, a fishing pond, trails and a visitor center. The park is open 5 am to 11 pm, the nature area an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset."
  - place: dickens-farm-nature-area
    for: "Near Main Street"
    why: "Fifty-two acres on St. Vrain Creek between Main and Martin Streets, with an accessible nature playground, a beginner bike skills trail and slow water play areas. A free float course starts at Main Street for your own tube, kayak or paddleboard, at your own risk. Parking is off Boston Avenue."
  - place: agricultural-heritage-center
    for: "A first Saturday"
    why: "Boulder County's free farm museum at 8348 Ute Highway, set in the years 1900 to 1925: a furnished 1909 farmhouse, two barns and an heirloom garden. From November to March it opens only on the first Saturday of the month, 10 am to 5 pm. Farm animals are there April to October."

These ten are for a first visit to Longmont, or a first winter living here: what to see on a cold day, where to walk, where to take the dog and the children, and one night out. Each links to its page on this guide, with hours, fees and directions.

**How we chose them.** Every pick is a place already in the directory below, and every fact above comes from the page of whoever runs it, read on November 11, 2026: the City of Longmont, Boulder County and the Longmont Theatre Company. Nobody pays to be listed. The order follows what a visitor most likely wants first: the expanded museum, the lake loop and the greenway, then the winter rink and the dog beach, a night at the theatre, and last the quieter places for anglers, children and a Saturday on the farm. They spread across town, from Golden Ponds in the west to Sandstone Ranch in the east.

**What is not here.** Clark Centennial Park, the fishing pond at Izaak Walton kept for children 14 and under, Roger's Grove and Boulder County's Pella Crossing are in the directory below.
