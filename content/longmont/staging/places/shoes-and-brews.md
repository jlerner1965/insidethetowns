The brew side of Shoes & Brews is a relaxed taproom pouring a rotating list of craft beer, cider, wine and non-alcoholic options, with usually around four of its own beers, brewed on site by brew master Roger Anderson and not distributed anywhere else. The free, all-paces Run Club leaves every Thursday at 6 pm year-round and hangs out afterwards, which is why Thursday hours run to about 8 pm. The taproom also hosts trivia, live music and community events, and the site describes leashed dogs and kids playing board games.
