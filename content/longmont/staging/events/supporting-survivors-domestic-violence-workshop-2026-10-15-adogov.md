Library Conference Room
No registration necessary
This program is in English

  
This presentation provides helpful tools and guidance for supporting individuals experiencing domestic violence. Some key topics include relationship dynamics, warning signs of abuse, and local resources. Participants will learn ways to communicate effectively with survivors and offer support with safety planning, in personal and professional settings. This workshop is designed for anyone wanting to gain the insight and skills needed to become a safe, supportive resource for survivors.
