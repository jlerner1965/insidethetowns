Unquiet Study Area

This program is in English and Spanish
Registration is required

  
Join us and make a book nook that will fit in your pocket! Learn the basics of miniatures and some other neat tips and tricks to a small world, and to celebrate the spooky month. 
  
Every month or so we meet and do a different, beginner-friendly craft. All supplies are provided, and no experience is required. These nights are open to anyone age 17 or older. This program is generously supported by the Friends of the Longmont Library.
