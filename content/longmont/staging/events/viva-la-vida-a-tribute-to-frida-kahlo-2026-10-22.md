A diverse group of passionate artists, poets, and musicians descend upon the Stewart Auditorium stage for an evening celebrating Frida Kahlo’s bold spirit, fierce individuality, and enduring creative influence. Through live performance and personal expression, they’ll explore love, loss, identity, humor, and the courage to create—each in their own voice. Join us for a spirited tribute to Frida and the many ways her legacy continues to inspire. 
  
Calling all Poets, Dancers, Performance Artists, Musicians and more!
 
  
DEADLINE EXTENDED! We’re looking for folks with a passion for Frida to join us in celebrating her legacy by performing short 3-5 minute pieces. Poetry/spoken word, dance, short theater pieces, monologues, storytelling, performance art, comedy, just about everything and anything will be considered. Contact stewartauditorium@longmontcolorado.gov for more info.
