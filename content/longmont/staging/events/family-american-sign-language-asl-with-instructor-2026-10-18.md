Library Meeting Rooms
No registration required
For children age 5+ and their grown ups
This program is presented in American Sign Language (ASL)
This event happens monthly, every third Sunday

  
This American Sign Language (ASL) class is held once per month on the third Sunday from 2-3PM. It’s focused towards children ages 5 and up (with a caregiver) and perfect for families, even those who have never signed before! 
  
A highly-qualified instructor is teaching these fun classes. Each month will be a new theme with vocabulary instruction, games to practice, and lots of fun! 
  
While the emphasis of these Family class will be to learn ASL, voice-off is not a requirement. Instructors are willing and able to interpret if needed for families with Deaf members in attendance
