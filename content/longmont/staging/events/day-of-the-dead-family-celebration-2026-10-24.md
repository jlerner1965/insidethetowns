Longmont's Day of the Dead family celebration, in its 26th year and, by the museum's account, the longest-running public one in Colorado, returns as an outdoor street festival downtown at 4th Avenue and Kimbark Street: traditional music and dance, face painting, sugar-skull decorating, crafts, food trucks, a kids' play zone, local vendors and a procession of gigantes. Free.
