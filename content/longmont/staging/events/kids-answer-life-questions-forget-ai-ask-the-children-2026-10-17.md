About this Interactive Program 
																			In September, adults in the community submitted “big life questions” for kids to answer. Select questions will be explored by kids during two creative arts workshops on Saturday, Oct. 10 and 17. Their responses will be displayed  (anonymously) in November. Have fun!  
																	
							
						
					
				
			
		
			
	




	
		
					
		
			
	
		
			
									
						
							
								


For K-5th graders
Led by Dandelion Arts Collective
Library Meeting Rooms



  
An art-making workshop in response to questions that people in the Longmont community has asked, like: 
  

How do you make the world’s best sandwich?
What makes someone a good friend?
Who is a hero?
and more!

  
Through theatre and movement games, visual art, generative stories, and informal interviews kids will create a collection of answers to curated questions from the Longmont community that celebrate their imagination, creativity, and insights. 
  
After the workshop, kids’ creations and words will be shared anonymously at the Library through an exhibit celebrating the wisdom of kids and our shared humanity. This program is generously supported by the Friends of the Longmont Library. 
  
This workshop is run by Dandelion Arts Collective staff.
