Outlaw Production Collective marks twenty years of its Books on the Chopping Block series with a "Best of the Banned" evening at the Longmont Public Library: actors read from the American Library Association's list of most challenged books, then the audience talks about them. Part of the library's Banned Books Week; registration required.
