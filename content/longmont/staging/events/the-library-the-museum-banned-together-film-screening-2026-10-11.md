A free screening at the Longmont Museum of Banned Together, a documentary about three students in Beaufort, South Carolina, and their adult allies fighting to return 97 books pulled from their school libraries. Shown by the library for Banned Books Week, with resources on the freedom to read before and after. Free; reservations recommended.
