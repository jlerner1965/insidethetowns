A panel at the Longmont Museum on Latino life in Boulder County, its culture, politics and history, with Leticia Abajo, Lesly Fajardo-Feaux, Jose Jimenez, Marinela Maneiro Goodwin and Lafayette Mayor Saul Tapia Vega, moderated by Dr. Perla Delgado of the "I Have A Dream" Foundation of Boulder County. Free, reservations recommended; English to Spanish interpretation.
