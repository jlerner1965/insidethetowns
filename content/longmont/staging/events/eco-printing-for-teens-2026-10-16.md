Teen Room
for 6th-12th graders
This program is in English

  
Learn how to turn real leaves and flowers into eco-prints. We will use actual leaves and flowers from the library’s learning garden to make art. Participants will use hammers to print real flowers onto paper or fabric. This is an opportunity for teens to get to know other teen crafters and artists, work with their hands, and learn how to use natural materials for art and printing.
