Longmont has two lakes you can put a boat on, and the City runs them under different rules. [McIntosh Lake](/places/mcintosh-lake-nature-area/) at 1929 Harvard Street, on the west side of town, is a nature area with a trail round it and no gate. [Union Reservoir](/places/union-reservoir-nature-area/) at 461 County Road 26, east of town, is a 736-acre reservoir with an entrance station, a fee and a ranger office (303-772-1265). What follows is what the City publishes for each.

| | McIntosh Lake | Union Reservoir |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | No fee listed | $10 a vehicle; $15 weekends and holidays, Memorial Day to Labor Day; $2 on foot or bike |
| Hours | 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset | By season (below); gates shut on five winter holidays |
| Boats | Carry-in, non-motorized only, from the designated ramps | Wakeless boating, trailered boats included; $5 a canoe, kayak or board, $10 a trailered boat |
| Swimming | Prohibited | Swim beach closed for the 2026 season; a wading area ran May 23 to September 19 |
| Dogs | On a leash at all times | Off leash only at the dog beach; leashed everywhere else |
| Trail | 3.5-mile loop, gravel | None listed on the City's page |
| Winter water | No seasonal closure listed | No water access December 1 to February 28 |

## McIntosh: the walk, and a quiet put-in

The City's [McIntosh Lake page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/facilities/mcintosh-lake-nature-area/) lists gravel trails, picnic areas, restrooms, fishing for all ages and boating for carry-on, non-motorized boats only. The 3.5-mile loop round the whole shore was finished in 2004, with a spur to Boulder County's Agricultural Heritage Center and a concrete stretch along the east side between Flanders and Dawson parks; the trailhead lot is off Harvard Street. Buoys mark off a wildlife area in the north-west corner.

The boating rules are short. Canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, belly boats, sailboards, sailboats and any small boat that can be carried to the water are allowed, only from the designated boat ramps, and the City asks paddlers to stay ashore if the lake is already crowded. Everyone aboard needs a life jacket, and children 12 and under wear theirs the whole time. Pool toys and single-chambered inflatables such as inner tubes are out. Swimming is prohibited, dogs must be leashed at all times, and glass is not allowed. The City adds that McIntosh is a reservoir in the ditch system, so its level depends on snowpack and irrigation priorities.

Hours are those of every Longmont nature area under the City's [rules page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/parks-and-natural-resources/rules-and-regulations/): one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset, seven days a week, unless posted otherwise. Neither page lists an entry fee for McIntosh.

## Union: the gate, the fee, the season

Union is the lake for a trailered boat, a sail, a windsurfer, a grill, or a dog that wants to swim. The City's [activities page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/union-reservoir-nature-area/activities-at-union/) allows wakeless boating and bans jet skis; any boat launched from a trailer or carrying a motor, electric trolling motors included, must pass an aquatic nuisance species inspection and arrive clean, drained and dry. All boats come off the water 30 minutes before closing. The same life-jacket rule as McIntosh applies. Alcohol and glass are prohibited, and there has been no camping since 2018. Fishing is from most of the shore or a boat, with an accessible pier; walleye and wiper carry a daily limit of 5. In October 2026 the City notes low water and suggests larger trailered boats check the level before buying a pass.

The [fees and hours page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/union-reservoir-nature-area/fees-and-hours-at-union/) sets the gate: 6 am–8 pm from March 1 to May 31, 6 am–9 pm from June 1 to August 31, 6 am–8 pm from September 1 to October 31, and 7 am–6 pm from November 1 to February 28. In 2026 the reservoir closes early, at 6 pm, on October 14, and the gate shuts at noon on July 4 for capacity. It is closed to all entry on Thanksgiving Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. From December 1 to February 28 there is no water use at all, including ice fishing; the daily fee still applies and access is walk-in.

Admission is $10 a vehicle, $15 on weekends and holidays from Memorial Day through Labor Day (the City counts Juneteenth, July 3, 4 and 5 among them), $2 a person on foot or by bicycle, $5 for each canoe, kayak, paddleboard, raft or sailboard and $10 for a trailered boat. The Colorado State Parks Pass is not accepted; Union is not a state park. A 2026 season pass is $85 for a resident's first car and $200 for a non-resident's, valid through February 28, 2027; a personal watercraft needs its own daily or season pass on top. A season pass gives no preferred entry, and when the lot is full it is one in, one out.

## Swimming and dogs

The people's swim beach at Union is closed for the whole 2026 season. The City's [May 18 release](https://longmontcolorado.gov/news/union-reservoir-swim-beach-closed-for-2026-season-sunset-pool-opening-in-time-for-memorial-day-weekend/) blamed anticipated low water, and offered instead a designated wading area up to three feet deep from May 23 to September 19, on a natural clay and rock bottom, at your own risk and with children supervised, plus an expanded sand beach. Swimming anywhere else at Union counts as open-water swimming and is allowed only in lifeguarded sessions for ages 16 and over, June through mid-August, on Tuesdays and Wednesdays 6:15–7:30 pm and Saturdays 8:30–9:45 am, at $10 a person, registered in advance. At McIntosh there is no swimming at all.

Union's dog beach is on the south-east shore past the swim area, and it is the one place at either lake where a dog is off the leash and in the water. The City's fees page says it remains open year-round, and the activities page that it stays open in 2026, with waste going straight into the cans or dumpster. Dogs are leashed everywhere else in the park. At McIntosh the rule is a leash at all times and no swimming for anyone, dogs included.

## So, which day

A walk, a run, a leashed dog, a fishing rod or a kayak off the roof, with nothing to pay: McIntosh. A motor or a trailer, a sail, a dog that needs to swim, a grill and a shelter, or a wading toddler between late May and mid-September: Union, with the fee and the gate hours. In winter McIntosh keeps its trail and hours; Union's gate stays open to walkers and its water is closed until March 1.
