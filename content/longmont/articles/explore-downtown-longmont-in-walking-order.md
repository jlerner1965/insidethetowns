Longmont is a city of nearly 100,000, and this guide does not try to cover it. It keeps to the 200 to 700 blocks of Main Street and adds one lake by car. Leave the car on or near Main Street and walk; the lake is the drive at the start or the end of the day.

## 01 · Breakfast on Main

[Tangerine](/places/tangerine-longmont/) at 379 Main Street serves breakfast, brunch and lunch cooked from scratch in an 1886 building, 7:30 am–1:30 pm on weekdays and until 2 pm at the weekend, with a full bar; weekday reservations are by email and only for groups of five or more. For something quicker, [Ziggi's Coffee](/places/ziggis-coffee-main-street/) at 400 Main Street opens at 6 am every day with locally made breakfast and lunch.

*Getting there:* park on or near Main Street. *Best for:* a start before the shops open at 10.

## 02 · The shops

[Composition Shop](/places/composition-shop/) at 356 Main Street is open daily 10 am–6 pm, with events and six book clubs; [Barbed Wire Books](/places/barbed-wire-books/) at 504 Main Street, also daily 10–6, sells new and used books with a deep Colorado and Western Americana section, plus area maps and nature guides. [Maker General](/places/maker-general/) at 381 Main Street is a craft shop for textiles and fiber, with kits and workshops; it is closed on Monday and opens only 10–2 on Tuesday.

*Getting there:* on foot along Main Street. *Best for:* a trail map or a present; a Wednesday to Sunday if you want Maker General's full hours.

## 03 · Lunch on Main

[Pelo's Deli and Provisions](/places/pelos-deli-and-provisions/) at 630 Main Street makes deli sandwiches, soups and salads from 11 am Tuesday to Sunday and is closed on Monday. [Rosalee's Pizzeria](/places/rosalees-pizzeria/) at 461 Main Street sells slices 11 am–2 pm Tuesday to Saturday and stays open until 8 pm or until the dough sells out; it is closed Sunday and Monday. [Flavor of India](/places/flavor-of-india/) at 370 Main Street does lunch combo plates Tuesday to Sunday and is closed Monday. On a Monday the choices are [Mike O'Shays](/places/mike-oshays/) at 512 Main Street, open from 11 am every day, and the [Pumphouse Brewery](/places/pumphouse-brewery/) at 540 Main Street, a brewpub since 1996, open from 10:45 am on weekdays and 10 am at the weekend.

*Getting there:* all on Main Street between the 300 and 700 blocks. *Best for:* noting the day: three of the five are closed on Monday.

## 04 · An evening on Main

The [Longmont Theatre Company](/places/longmont-theatre-company/) has staged its seasons at the Longmont Performing Arts Center, 513 Main Street, since 1990; the 2026–27 season has six productions, and tickets are sold online and at the box office, which opens an hour before each ticketed show and not during the day, so book before you come. [Küper Wine Bar](/places/kuper-wine-bar/) at 704 Main Street pours by the glass and the flight from 3 pm Wednesday to Sunday, closed Monday and Tuesday, with reservations through Tock.

*Getting there:* the same parking spot. *Best for:* a Wednesday to Saturday evening, when the theater, the wine bar and the restaurants are all open.

## 05 · McIntosh Lake, by car

[McIntosh Lake Nature Area](/places/mcintosh-lake-nature-area/) is on the west side of the city, with a trailhead lot off Harvard Street at 1929 Harvard Street. The City's page lists a 3.5-mile gravel loop around the whole lake, finished in 2004, with a spur to Boulder County's Agricultural Heritage Center, picnic areas and restrooms, open one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset. Carry-on, non-motorized boats launch only at the designated ramps, with a life jacket for everyone aboard; swimming is prohibited and dogs stay on a leash. The [lakes guide](/articles/mcintosh-versus-union/) sets McIntosh beside Union Reservoir, with the fees and gate hours, so look there if you want the water rather than the loop.

Two alternatives for the same afternoon, both a drive from Main Street. The [Left Hand Brewing tasting room](/places/left-hand-brewing-tasting-room/) at 1265 Boston Avenue has poured there since 1993: Monday to Thursday 12–9 pm, Friday and Saturday 11 am–9 pm, Sunday 11 am–8 pm, with a food truck every day during tasting room hours and brewery tours; kids are welcome, and dogs only on the west patio or in the seasonal Garden next door. The [Longmont Museum](/places/longmont-museum/) at 400 Quail Road reopens its galleries with a free all-day Grand Reopening on Saturday, October 17, 2026; until then the City says the building is open only during education and auditorium or courtyard program hours, so do not drive over for the galleries before that date.

*Getting there:* by car; the lot is off Harvard Street. *Best for:* the loop walk in the hour before sunset; a paddle with a life jacket.

## A simple first visit

1. Park on or near Main Street, on a Tuesday to Saturday if you can.
2. Breakfast at Tangerine or coffee at Ziggi's.
3. Browse Composition Shop, Barbed Wire Books and Maker General from 10.
4. Lunch at Pelo's, Rosalee's or Flavor of India.
5. Drive to McIntosh Lake for the loop, or to Left Hand; from October 17, the museum.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. Main Street is US 287: Loveland is 16 miles north and downtown Denver 34 miles south, about 45 minutes. Colorado 119, the Diagonal, runs 15 miles south-west to Boulder and 5 miles east to I-25. Transfort, the City of Fort Collins' transit agency, runs the FLEX regional bus between Fort Collins, Loveland, Berthoud, Longmont and Boulder on weekday and Saturday timetables, and says it connects to RTD's buses in Longmont and Boulder; the current timetables are on [Transfort's FLEX page](https://www.ridetransfort.com/routes/flex) and [RTD's site](https://www.rtd-denver.com/). Parking rules and road work are the City of Longmont's, and posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Longmont Theatre Company states that its venue is wheelchair accessible with designated seating and that there is an accessible drop-off zone in front on Main Street. At McIntosh Lake the City lists restrooms, gravel trails, a concrete trail section along the east shore and a trailhead parking lot off Harvard Street; it does not describe the loop as accessible, and that is not claimed here. Left Hand's page refers parking and its dog policy to its FAQ and says nothing about step-free entry. Sidewalks, curb ramps, public restrooms and designated accessible parking on Main Street have not been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here.
