A costumed paddleboard float on Evergreen Lake for witches, warlocks and other magical creatures, run by the recreation district. Bring your own board or rent one from the district; registration is per board, and children under 8 ride free with an adult. The last boat goes out at noon, there is a drone group photo on the water at 11:30 am, and coffee and waffles are sold on site.
