The Center for the Arts Evergreen's monthly walk past the town's public sculptures, led by a trained docent who tells the stories of the pieces and the people who made them. November's route is Downtown Evergreen, meeting outside Java Groove; plan on about an hour on foot. Free, all ages, no RSVP.
