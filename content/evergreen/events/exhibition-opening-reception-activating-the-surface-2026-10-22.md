The opening of Activating the Surface: Exploring the Expressive Qualities of Paint, a show by the Of Chaos and Order Art Collective (Richard Rodriguez, Elizabeth Rouland, Dan Oakleaf, Sascha Ripps and Hazel Weckbach). The reception is free. The exhibition runs through November 28, with the artists in the gallery on Saturdays from October 31 to November 21, 11 am to 1 pm, and a closing reception and artist talk on November 28.
