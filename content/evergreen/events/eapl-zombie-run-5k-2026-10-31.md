The 12th annual Halloween 5K for the Evergreen Animal Protective League, starting and finishing at the Buchanan Rec Center ball fields and following the walking trails around Bergen Park. Costumes are encouraged, with a costume contest for runners and pets, refreshments and music afterward. Race-day registration and packet pickup run 8:30 to 9:45 am; online registration closes at 10 am on October 30.
