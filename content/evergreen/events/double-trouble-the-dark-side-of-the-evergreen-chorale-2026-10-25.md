The 90-voice Evergreen Chorale's Halloween-season concert under artistic director Christine Gaudreau, with music by John Williams, Eric Whitacre and Ola Gjeilo, including selections from Harry Potter, accompanied by piano, string quartet, oboe, English horn and percussion. The Ralston Valley High School Select Choir opens the program. The same concert plays in Arvada on Friday, October 23.
