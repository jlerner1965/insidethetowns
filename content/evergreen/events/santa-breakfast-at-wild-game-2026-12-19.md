The Evergreen Area Chamber's Santa Breakfast at The Wild Game: breakfast, holiday crafts and one-on-one time with Santa, with photos texted to your phone. Two seatings, each ticketed separately: 8:30 to 10 am and 10:30 am to noon. Tickets are $28 for adults and children 1 and over, sold online only and non-refundable, with no day-of tickets or walk-ins; children under 1 are free but must still be registered. One of two Santa Breakfasts this year, with Sunday, December 6 at Hiwan Barn.
