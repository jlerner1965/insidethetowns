December's Second Saturday in downtown Evergreen is the holiday shopping edition, with shops and galleries open late for gifts. On the second Saturday of each month the shops and galleries of downtown Evergreen stay open late, 4 to 7 pm, for a sip and stroll with treats, sales and discounts at participating businesses. Free; Downtown Evergreen posts details on its Facebook and Instagram pages.
