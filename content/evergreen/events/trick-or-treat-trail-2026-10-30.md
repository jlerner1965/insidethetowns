The recreation district's Halloween evening on the Buchanan Park ball fields: a trail of candy booths to trick-or-treat through from 4 to 8 pm, inflatables and food trucks, then E.T. on an outdoor screen at sunset as the last Movie in the Park of 2026. Every trick-or-treater needs a $5 trail ticket; the movie and the other activities on the fields are free.
