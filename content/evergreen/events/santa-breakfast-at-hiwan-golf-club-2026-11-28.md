The Evergreen Area Chamber's Santa Breakfast at Hiwan Golf Club: breakfast, a craft at the table, and time with Santa, who visits each family, hears wish lists and poses for photos. Tickets are $25 a person, sold online only and non-refundable, with none at the door, and every child over age 1 needs a ticket. One of four Saturday Santa Breakfasts at different Evergreen restaurants between November 28 and December 19.
