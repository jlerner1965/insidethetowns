November's Second Saturday in downtown Evergreen, themed Gather in Downtown, Giving Thanks, runs alongside the Evergreen Creative District's Mountain of Thanks festival the same day. On the second Saturday of each month the shops and galleries of downtown Evergreen stay open late, 4 to 7 pm, for a sip and stroll with treats, sales and discounts at participating businesses. Free; Downtown Evergreen posts details on its Facebook and Instagram pages.
