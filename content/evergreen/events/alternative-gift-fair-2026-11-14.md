Evergreen's volunteer-run Alternative Gift Fair, now past 25 years, fills Evergreen Lutheran Church on Highway 73 on Saturday, November 14 from 9 am to 4 pm and Sunday, November 15 from 9 am to 3 pm. Every vendor is a charity or nonprofit selling crafts and gifts, and the charities keep all of their sales; a bake sale and a youth cafe run by the supporting congregations cover the fair's costs. Admission is not charged.
