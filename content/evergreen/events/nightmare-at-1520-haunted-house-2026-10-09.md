The recreation district's haunted house in North Buchanan Park, open 7 to 9:30 pm on selected October nights; follow the signs from 1521 Bergen Parkway. It is rated PG-17, children must be supervised, and "anti-scare sticks" are handed out to anyone who wants fewer jump scares. Tickets are $20, or $50 for unlimited entry that night, and cost more at the door.
