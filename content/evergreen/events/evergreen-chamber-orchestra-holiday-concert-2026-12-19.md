The Evergreen Chamber Orchestra's family holiday concert: Jingle Bells sing-alongs, selections from Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker, Silent Night, the winner of the orchestra's Young Artists Solo Competition, and the Evergreen Chorale's Altezza with director Christine Gaudreau. Santa visits before the 3 pm performance, and the program repeats at 6 pm. Livestream tickets are also sold.
