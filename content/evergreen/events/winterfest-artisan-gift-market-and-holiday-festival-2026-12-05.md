The Center for the Arts Evergreen's free holiday market, with Colorado artists and artisans at indoor tables and outdoor tents, the annual member exhibition for sale and a ceramics sale upstairs in the studio. There are free photos with Santa, crafts and face painting, food trucks, a hot cocoa bar, live music and free gift wrapping; it goes ahead snow or shine. Park at Bergen Park Church next door or the RTD park-and-ride across the street.
