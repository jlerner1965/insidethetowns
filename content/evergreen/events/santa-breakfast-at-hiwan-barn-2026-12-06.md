The Evergreen Area Chamber's Santa Breakfast at Hiwan Barn, the building with The Woodcellar, Bivouac Coffee and Dandelion's Cafe: breakfast, holiday crafts and one-on-one time with Santa, with photos texted to your phone. Two seatings, each ticketed separately: 8 to 9:30 am and 10 to 11:30 am. Tickets are $28 for adults and children 1 and over, sold online only and non-refundable, with no day-of tickets or walk-ins; children under 1 are free but must still be registered. Parking out front is limited; there is free street parking and a dirt lot across the street at the disc golf course. One of two Santa Breakfasts this year, with Saturday, December 19 at The Wild Game.
