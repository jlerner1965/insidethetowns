The natural-surface walking and jogging loop around Evergreen Lake, kept by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District on Denver's Dedisse Park land. The Lake House page gives it as 1.3 miles; the district's Evergreen Lake & Trail page says 1.25.

The district warns that parts of the loop are not up to ADA standards and are hard going for wheelchairs and strollers. Dogs stay on a leash; Jefferson County Animal Control enforces it. Questions go to the Lake House, 720-880-1300.
