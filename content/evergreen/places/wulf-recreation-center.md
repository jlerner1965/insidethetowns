The Evergreen Park & Recreation District's other recreation center, on Olive Road. Inside are a 25-yard, six-lane pool, a gym for basketball, volleyball and pickleball, a gymnastics gym, a dance studio, a 1,600-square-foot fitness area, meeting rooms and the Playschool.

Pool hours are shorter than the building's: 5:30 am to 3 pm on weekdays and 7 am to 3 pm at weekends. Outside, Wulf Park, owned by Jefferson County Public Schools and leased by the recreation district ([Wulf Park](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/wulf-park), read 6 October 2026), has four tennis courts, a skate park, a playground and a mile-long asphalt fitness trail.
