Center for the Arts Evergreen is a nonprofit arts organization on Rocky Village Drive. Besides its Main Gallery it hangs smaller rotating shows by local artists in the Community Room and Board Room galleries, and the work in those rooms is almost always for sale.

It also runs art classes, an artist-in-residence program, concerts, a film series and festivals, and keeps a temporary sculpture walk and a permanent sculpture collection. The hours above are its public hours as the site lists them.
