The Jefferson County Public Library branch for Evergreen, set into the hillside just south of the historic downtown. The building dates from 1989 and was redesigned, renovated and reopened in 2024.

It is a full-service branch with holds pickup, a walk-up book return, a 24-hour ballot drop box and electric-vehicle charging. The library says the branch stays open during the current roof construction; the branch page posts holiday closures.
