A breakfast and lunch cafe on Highway 74 downtown that has been open for more than 30 years under several owners; Erin Myers and Valerie Hartford bought it in 2024. The cafe says its sauces, dressings, gravy, soups and bloody mary mix are made in house.

Hours are 7:30 am to 2 pm every day. On the first Friday of each month there is a pop-up night with seatings at 5 and 7 pm.
