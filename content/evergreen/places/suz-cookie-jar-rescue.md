A downtown bakery run by a mother and daughter, now serving breakfast and lunch as well as baked goods. It makes custom cakes for weddings and birthdays, hosts parties, and offers "adoptable" cookie jars.

Closed Mondays; open 7 am to 3 pm Tuesday to Thursday, 7 am to 5 pm Friday and 9 am to 4 pm at weekends. Online ordering is on its site.
