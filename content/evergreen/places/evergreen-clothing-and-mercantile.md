A clothing, gift and souvenir store on Highway 74 that says it has been a stop in downtown Evergreen for 15 years. It carries apparel from t-shirts, sweaters and hoodies to hats and socks, plus jewelry, art, luggage, purses, candy and local gifts.

It opens at 10 am every day, closing at 5 pm Sunday to Friday and 6 pm on Saturday; on the second Saturday of each month it stays open until 7 pm.
