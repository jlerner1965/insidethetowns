A Jefferson County Open Space heritage park and museum on Meadow Drive. The park is open every day for self-guided outdoor tours; the museum opens Thursday to Sunday, noon to 4:30 pm, and its free guided tours, offered during open hours, usually take about 45 minutes.

The museum stays open on holiday weekends except Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, Christmas weekend and New Year's weekend. Call the museum on 720-497-7650.
