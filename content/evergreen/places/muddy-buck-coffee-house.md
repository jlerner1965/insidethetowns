A coffee house in downtown Evergreen, next to the Little Bear on Highway 74, that roasts its own coffee. Its own site describes house-baked pastries, savory breakfast and lunch options, and a small bar stocked with wine, craft beers and cocktails, in what it calls a rustic room in historic Evergreen. Bagged coffee and a monthly coffee club are sold online.
