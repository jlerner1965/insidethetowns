A pub on Highway 74 pouring craft beer with pub fare, dine-in or carryout, plus growler and half-growler fills, cocktails and canned beer to go. The patio opens when the weather allows.

Happy hour runs 3 to 5 pm every day. Thursday is family game night with free bingo at 6 pm, Saturday brings free live music from 5:30 to 7:30 pm, and Sunday is service-industry night. Its posted fall and winter hours are Wednesday to Friday 3 to 9 pm, Saturday noon to 9 pm and Sunday noon to 8 pm.
