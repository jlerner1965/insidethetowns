A small park owned and managed by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District at Welch Avenue and Highway 74 in Kittredge. It has a playground, an asphalt fitness trail and a picnic shelter with two tables and an outlet, booked through the district's park operations office.

The district allows access to Bear Creek only along the posted path and only during park hours. Children under 12 must be with an adult, dogs must be leashed and waste picked up. The district's page refers problems to the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office, 720-497-7850.
