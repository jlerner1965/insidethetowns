A locally owned ice cream shop on Meadow Drive that serves Little Man Ice Cream by the scoop, along with milkshakes and malts, sundaes and pints to take home.

It opens at 3 pm on weekdays and at noon on Saturday and Sunday, and closes at 8:30 pm every day.
