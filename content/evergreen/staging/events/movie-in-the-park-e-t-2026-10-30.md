The last of the recreation district's 2026 Movies in the Park, shown outdoors on the Buchanan Park ball fields on Friday, 30 October. Activities start at 4 pm and the film, E.T., begins at sunset once the screen is visible. Free to attend, with inflatables, yard games, face painting and food trucks; it shares the evening with the ticketed Trick or Treat Trail.
