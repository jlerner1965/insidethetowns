A free public talk in the Evergreen Speaks! series from Evergreen Newcomers and Neighbors. Drs. Benjamin Sax and David Krueger take up how Americans can hold difficult conversations across religious and political lines, what is and is not antisemitism in debate over the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, and how to listen better in heated exchanges. Thursday, 19 November, 7 to 8 pm, with discussion to follow.
