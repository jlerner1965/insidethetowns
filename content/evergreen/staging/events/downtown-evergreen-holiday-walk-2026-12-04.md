The 34th annual Holiday Walk, Downtown Evergreen's official start to the holiday season, on Friday, 4 December from 5 to 9 pm. It opens with the tree lighting at Evergreen Lake House with Santa and Mrs. Claus, then moves back downtown, where Main Street closes to traffic for photos with Santa, performances by local music and dance groups, fire pits, s'mores and late shopping. The organizer says a detailed schedule is still to come.
