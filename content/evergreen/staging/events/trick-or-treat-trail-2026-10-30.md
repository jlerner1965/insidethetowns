The recreation district's annual Halloween evening on the Buchanan Park ball fields: a trail of candy booths to trick-or-treat through, inflatables and food trucks, followed by a free outdoor screening of E.T. as the October Movie in the Park. A ticket is needed for each trick-or-treater; everything else on the fields is free. Runs 4 to 8 pm on Friday, 30 October.
