The Evergreen Players' fall production, a 90-minute, gender-bending, quick-changing comic take on Bram Stoker's Dracula by Gordon Greenberg and Steve Rosen, directed by Paul Newman, at Center Stage on Fireweed Drive. The run is 9 to 25 October with evening shows at 7:30 pm and Sunday matinees at 2 pm; this is the 7:30 pm performance on Saturday, 17 October. Tickets are listed at $15–30 by the chamber and $20–30 by the Players.
