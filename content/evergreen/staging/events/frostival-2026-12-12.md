The recreation district's daytime holiday gathering at Evergreen Lake on Saturday, 12 December, 11 am to 3 pm. The Lake House is open with a bar serving festive drinks, and there are crafts, live music and a holiday market for the whole family. The district is taking vendor and volunteer applications on its own page; no admission price is given.
