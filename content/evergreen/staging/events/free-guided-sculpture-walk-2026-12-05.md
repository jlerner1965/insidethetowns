The last of the Center for the Arts Evergreen's monthly docent-led sculpture walks for 2026, on Saturday, 5 December at 10:30 am, meeting at the Center on Rocky Village Drive. About an hour of walking past the public sculptures near the Center, with the stories of the pieces and their makers. Free, all ages, no RSVP needed.
