Active4All's New Year's Day plunge into Evergreen Lake, 10 am to noon on Friday, 1 January 2027. Come in a New Year's costume, bring friends, and warm up afterwards with hot drinks and treats. Most proceeds go to INSPIRE, the Evergreen programme providing inclusive recreation for people with disabilities. Pricing is on the organizer's page.
