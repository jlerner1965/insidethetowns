A Halloween afternoon at the Hiwan Museum run by Jefferson County Parks & Open Space: spooky storytelling in English, Spanish and a sensory-friendly version, crafts, games and prizes, with a costume parade at 2:30 pm. Saturday, 24 October, 1 to 4 pm. Free, all ages, no registration; children must be accompanied by an adult.
