The Evergreen Players' holiday tradition: six actors from the company's master ensemble play every part in the Players' own adaptation of Dickens' A Christmas Carol, directed by Kathleen Davis, at Center Stage on Fireweed Drive. The Saturday 7:30 pm performance on 19 December; the run is 18 to 20 December. Tickets are listed at $20–30 by the chamber and $15–25 by the Players.
