The Center for the Arts Evergreen's annual holiday market and festival on Saturday, 5 December, 10 am to 4 pm, with Colorado artists and artisans at indoor tables and outdoor tents, free photos with Santa, a children's craft station, face painting, food trucks, a hot cocoa bar and live holiday music. The CAE member exhibition is on view and for sale, a ceramics sale runs upstairs in the studio, and gift wrapping is complimentary. Snow or shine; parking next door at Bergen Park Church and at the RTD park-and-ride.
