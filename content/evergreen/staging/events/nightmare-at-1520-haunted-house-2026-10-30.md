The Evergreen Park & Recreation District's haunted house in Buchanan Park, open 7 to 9:30 pm on select October nights: 9, 10, 15, 16, 17, 22, 23, 24, 29, 30 and 31 October. Follow the signs from 1521 Bergen Parkway. It is rated PG-17, children must be supervised, and anti-scare sticks are offered for anyone who wants fewer jump scares. $20 general admission or $50 for unlimited entry, with a higher price at the door.
