A costumed paddle on Evergreen Lake run by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District: bring your own paddleboard or rent one from the district, and come dressed as a witch, warlock or other mythical creature. The float runs 10 am to 1 pm, with the last boat out at noon and a drone group photo on the water at 11:30. Registration is per board; children under 8 ride free with an adult. Coffee and waffles are sold from trucks on site.
