Opening reception for Activating the Surface: Exploring the Expressive Qualities of Paint, an exhibition by the Of Chaos and Order Art Collective (Richard Rodriguez, Elizabeth Rouland, Dan Oakleaf, Sascha Ripps and Hazel Weckbach) at the Center for the Arts Evergreen. Thursday, 22 October, 4 to 7 pm, free and open to the public, with the gift shop open late. The show hangs through 28 November.
