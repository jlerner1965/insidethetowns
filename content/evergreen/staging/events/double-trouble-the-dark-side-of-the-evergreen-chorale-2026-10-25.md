The 90-voice Evergreen Chorale's Halloween-season concert, a slightly spooky programme of John Williams, Eric Whitacre and Ola Gjeilo, including music from Harry Potter and a Haunted House set, with string quartet, oboe, English horn and percussion under Christine Gaudreau. Ralston Valley High School's select choir opens. Sunday, 25 October at 3 pm at Evergreen Lutheran Church.
