The Center for the Arts Evergreen's monthly docent-led walk past the town's public sculptures, on Saturday, 7 November at 10:30 am. This month's route is Downtown Evergreen, meeting outside Java Groove. About an hour of walking, with the stories of the sculptures and their makers. Free, all ages, no RSVP needed.
