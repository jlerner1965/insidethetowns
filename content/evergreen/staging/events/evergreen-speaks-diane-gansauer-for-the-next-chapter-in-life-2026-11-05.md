A free public talk in the Evergreen Speaks! series run by Evergreen Newcomers and Neighbors. Long-distance hiker and life-story celebrant Diane Gansauer speaks on travelling lightly through life's later chapters: empty nests, retirement, loss and downsizing. TEDx-style short presentation followed by questions and discussion. Thursday, 5 November, 5:30 to 6:30 pm.
