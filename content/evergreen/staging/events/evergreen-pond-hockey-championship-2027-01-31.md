The 15th annual Evergreen Pond Hockey Championship, run by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District on rinks groomed on Evergreen Lake, with check-in at the Lake House. Teams of up to eight play four-on-four in competitive, intermediate and novice divisions over the weekend of 30 and 31 January 2027, with finals on the Sunday. The Lake House opens at 5:30 am and play begins around 7:30 am each day. Team registration is $750; the page says nothing about a charge to watch.
