The Evergreen Park & Recreation District's family skating day on Evergreen Lake, the largest Zamboni-groomed outdoor rink in North America, from noon to 6 pm on New Year's Eve. Open to all ages; tickets are $25 for anyone 5 and older and sold on the day, first come first served, and children 4 and under skate free. Food trucks sell food and soft drinks on site. Skating depends on the ice, so check the district's site before going.
