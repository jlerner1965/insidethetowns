Evergreen is an unincorporated community in Jefferson County at about 7,100 feet on Bear Creek, thirty to forty minutes from Denver by I-70 and Colorado 74. The stops below are a short drive apart along Highway 74 and Upper Bear Creek Road, so this is a driving order rather than a walk. Park first at the lake lot on Upper Bear Creek Road; in boating season the district runs a shuttle from Stagecoach Park when that lot fills, but boating and the shuttle are closed for 2026.

## 01 · Evergreen Lake and the trail

[Evergreen Lake](/places/evergreen-lake/) at 29612 Upper Bear Creek Road is Denver's, part of its Dedisse Mountain Park, and is run by the Evergreen Park & Recreation District from the Lake House on the shore. The [lake trail](/places/evergreen-lake-trail/) is a natural-surface loop that the district gives as 1.3 miles on one page and 1.25 on another, passing an observation boardwalk over the wetlands; park and trail are open sunrise to sunset, dogs stay on a leash, and nobody swims, because the lake is the community's drinking water. Boat rentals have ended for the season and the district says summer boating resumes in 2027; skating starts only when the ice is in, so call the boating and skating hotline, 720-880-1391, before any winter visit. The boats, the ice, the prices and the rules are in [Evergreen Lake through the seasons](/articles/evergreen-lake-through-the-seasons/); this page is the walk.

*Getting there:* the lake lot on Upper Bear Creek Road, west of downtown. *Best for:* the first hour, on foot, in any season the trail is clear.

## 02 · Downtown on Highway 74: the meal

Downtown Evergreen is the run of storefronts along Highway 74 on Bear Creek, a short drive east of the lake. [Wildflower Cafe](/places/wildflower-cafe/) at 28035 is breakfast and lunch, 7:30 am–2 pm every day. [Beau Jo's](/places/beau-jos-evergreen/) at 28186 serves its mountain-pie pizza on a Bear Creek patio from 11 am daily, to 8 pm Sunday to Thursday and 9 pm Friday and Saturday. [Lariat Lodge Brewing](/places/lariat-lodge-brewing-evergreen/) on Fireweed Drive has a full kitchen, 11 am–9 pm daily. [Suz Cookie Jar Rescue](/places/suz-cookie-jar-rescue/) at 27975 does breakfast and lunch from 7 am Tuesday to Friday and 9 am at weekends, closed Monday. [Revival Brews](/places/revival-brews/) at 27845 is closed Monday and Tuesday and opens at 3 pm Wednesday to Friday, noon at weekends.

*Getting there:* by car from the lake; the shops and kitchens are on Highway 74. *Best for:* lunch, with the day of the week checked first.

## 03 · Meadow Drive

Meadow Drive runs off the downtown strip. [The Colorado Creamery](/places/the-colorado-creamery/) at 27905 scoops Little Man ice cream from 3 pm on weekdays and from noon at weekends, to 8:30 pm. [Mountain Home](/places/mountain-home/) at 27965 is a furniture and decor store in a 6,000-square-foot historic barn, open 10 am–5 pm daily, and [Sundance Gardens](/places/sundance-gardens/) at 27866 is the garden center for high-altitude and animal-resistant plants, open year round.

*Getting there:* a short drive or walk from the Highway 74 storefronts. *Best for:* ice cream after lunch, and the barn.

## 04 · Hiwan Heritage Park & Museum

Further along Meadow Drive at 28473, [Hiwan Heritage Park & Museum](/places/hiwan-heritage-park-museum/) is Jefferson County Open Space's history stop. The grounds are open every day for self-guided outdoor tours; the museum opens Thursday to Sunday, noon–4:30 pm, closed Monday to Wednesday, and its free guided tours run during those hours and usually take about 45 minutes. In 2026 the county's Hiwan Halloween is on October 24, 1–4 pm, free, with storytelling in English, Spanish and a sensory-friendly version.

*Getting there:* by car, on Meadow Drive. *Best for:* a Thursday-to-Sunday afternoon; on other days, only the grounds.

## 05 · The county trails, for a longer day

Two Jefferson County Open Space parks are close. [Alderfer/Three Sisters Park](/places/alderfer-three-sisters-park/), near the middle of Evergreen, has the Three Sisters rock formations, old ponderosa stands and, the county says, more trails per acre than any of its foothills parks; the rebuilt [West Trailhead](/places/alderfer-three-sisters-west-trailhead/) at 31537 Buffalo Park Road has a bigger lot and a new restroom, and Denver's Dedisse Trail links the lake's park to these trails. [Elk Meadow Park](/places/elk-meadow-park/) is where the county says its elk are most likely to be seen, with meadow loops from the [Stagecoach trailhead](/places/elk-meadow-stagecoach-trailhead/) and the 4.7-mile, 2,100-foot climb to Bergen Peak. Both parks are open one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset, dogs on leash; closures are posted on the county's [alert page](https://www.jeffco.us/AlertCenter.aspx?CID=25).

*Getting there:* by car; each trailhead has its own lot. *Best for:* a second half-day, or a morning before the lake.

## A simple first visit

1. Park at the lake lot on Upper Bear Creek Road and walk the loop.
2. Drive east to Highway 74 for lunch; Suz is closed Monday and Revival Monday and Tuesday.
3. Ice cream on Meadow Drive, from 3 pm on weekdays or noon at weekends.
4. On a Thursday to Sunday, the Hiwan museum tour in the afternoon.
5. With more time, a trail at Alderfer/Three Sisters or Elk Meadow.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service and [CDOT's COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) before you set out. From Denver it is about thirty to forty minutes by I-70 and Colorado 74. No bus service is claimed here, because no official timetable has been read for it.

Evergreen is unincorporated, so parking, road work and trail closures are set by Jefferson County, the recreation district and the state highway department rather than a town. Posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The recreation district says sections of the lake trail are not to ADA standards and are hard going for wheelchairs and strollers. Jefferson County says full ADA accessibility is not available at Hiwan because of the historic buildings and grounds; some areas of the house are accessible, it offers ADA binders with photographs and content for the spaces that are not, and a digital tour marked ADA accessible, and it asks visitors to contact the museum in advance. The county's Alderfer West Trailhead has a new restroom, but its page does not say whether it is accessible, so that is not claimed.

Step-free entrances along Highway 74 and Meadow Drive, and accessible parking at the lake, have not been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here; contact a business directly before relying on it.
