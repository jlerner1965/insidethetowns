On November 18, 2026, [Evergreen Lake](/evergreen/places/evergreen-lake/) is between seasons. Boating is closed until 2027, and skating has not opened: the district says it will post the 2026–27 season [once it has an opening day](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/public-ice-skating). The 1.3-mile lake trail and fishing, with a state license, are open all year, sunrise to sunset.

Before any visit, call the boating and skating hotline, 720-880-1391. The Evergreen Park & Recreation District says it [updates it daily](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake) and every time the lake closes. The lake belongs to Denver, as part of Dedisse Park, which [Denver says](https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Parks-Recreation/Mountain-Parks/About-Denver-Mountain-Parks) it bought as the Dedisse Ranch in 1919. The district runs the lake from the Lake House at 29612 Upper Bear Creek Road, 720-880-1300.

## What runs when

| Season | At the lake | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Winter, once the ice is in | Skating rinks, skate rental, hockey | 2026–27 not yet open; no opening day announced |
| Summer (in 2026, Memorial Day, May 25, to Labor Day) | Boat rentals and private boats, with a free shuttle | Resumes in 2027; dates not yet posted |
| After Labor Day | Boat rentals at weekends only | Closed for 2026 |
| All year, sunrise to sunset | The trail, fishing, the picnic area | Open |

## Park once at the lake

The lake lot is [open to everyone](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/boat-rentals), first come, first served, and fills quickly at weekends and on holidays; the district closes it when it is full. Then it runs a shuttle from [Stagecoach Park](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/stagecoach-park), at Stagecoach Boulevard and Highway 74: in summer, free, 10 am–5 pm while the boats are out, and in skating season at weekends and on holidays, from the lower lot by the turf field. Portable toilets are by the lot in boating season and near the warming hut in skating season.

## Winter: the ice

The district has posted its [2026/2027 skating prices](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/public-ice-skating): $15 a person for the day, $20 with skate rental, and free for children 4 and under. Rental skates run from toddler 8 to men's 16. A $200 season pass and a $120 ten-visit punch pass are on sale online only, with no refunds, because the district cannot promise opening days or ice quality. Open skating takes no reservations, and the price is the same wherever you live, since the park is Denver's.

- **Hours:** set by the weather and the ice; the hotline has them.
- **On the ice:** skates stay on the groomed rinks and shoes on the banks; hockey stays on rinks 1 to 5; no dogs on the ice or in the warming hut.
- **Off the rinks:** the district checks the ice [only on its rinks](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/ice-safety-at-evergreen-lake); anywhere else on the lake is at your own risk.
- **Food:** Muddy Buck sells refreshments on the weekends the rinks are open.

## Summer: the boats

[Rental boats](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/boat-rentals) are first come, first served, with no reservations, and the last boat goes out an hour before closing. In 2026 a single kayak or a stand-up paddleboard was $25 an hour, and a canoe or a double kayak $40. Everyone wears a life jacket, and a child under 13 goes out with an adult who signs the waiver. Leashed dogs may ride along, but nobody swims, people or pets: the lake is the community's drinking water.

Private boats need a day pass and an inspection for aquatic nuisance species at the washing station by the lot, and no motor but an electric trolling motor. The district's [lake rules](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/seasonal-lake-rules) put the longest private boat at 15 feet and its [own-boat page](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/bring-your-own-boat-personal-watercraft-information) at 17. After Labor Day, rentals run at weekends only and the hours vary.

## All year: the trail and the fish

The [lake trail](/evergreen/places/evergreen-lake-trail/) is a natural-surface loop round the lake, [1.3 miles](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake-house) on the Lake House page and 1.25 on the district's [park page](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake-trail). Parts of it are not to ADA standards and are hard going for wheelchairs and strollers. Dogs stay on a leash, and Jefferson County Animal Control enforces it.

[Fishing](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/fishing) for the stocked rainbow trout needs a Colorado fishing license, though [anglers 15 and under](https://cpw.state.co.us/fishing) fish free. Use artificial bait only, no live minnows, and barbless hooks; don't fish off the boat docks or around the Lake House, except from its accessible pier.

## Toward downtown

A shared-use path, the Evergreen Lake North Trail, runs [beside Highway 74](https://www.codot.gov/projects/archives/co74-evergreen-lake-trail) on the north side of the lake, from Upper Bear Creek Road to the dam. The [Evergreen Area Chamber](https://evergreenchamber.org/visitors-guide-copy/) describes the walk from the Lake House along a boardwalk and through the wetlands toward town, by the high path or over the dam. In April the state transportation department said resurfacing on Highway 74 between the lake and Lair o' the Bear Park would [run through fall 2026](https://www.codot.gov/news/2026/april-2026-news/co74-bearcreek-resurfacing-begins-may), with sidewalk detours downtown.

The [Hiwan Heritage Park & Museum](https://www.jeffco.us/1251/Hiwan-Heritage-Park), 28473 Meadow Drive, is a short drive. The museum is open Thursday to Sunday, noon to 4:30 pm, with free guided tours of about 45 minutes, and the park is open every day. It closes on Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, and on the Christmas and New Year's weekends.

## A longer walk: Alderfer/Three Sisters

[Alderfer/Three Sisters Park](/evergreen/places/alderfer-three-sisters-park/) is Jefferson County's 1,131 acres of rock formations and old ponderosa pine, with 16.7 miles of trail on the county's map, the most trails per acre of any foothills park, [the county says](https://www.jeffco.us/980/Alderfer-Three-Sisters-Park). Start from the East Trailhead, 30357 Buffalo Park Road, or the West Trailhead, 31537 Buffalo Park Road; both have restrooms. The county map gives the distance between each junction rather than loop totals. Denver's Dedisse Trail links the lake's park to Alderfer's trails.

Jefferson County's [rules](https://www.jeffco.us/1583/Regulations): dogs on a leash of 10 feet or less, and the park is open from one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset. The [Pioneer Trail](https://www.jeffco.us/1218/Elk-Meadow-Park) connects Evergreen Lake with Elk Meadow Park and Bergen Park.

## Before you go

- **The lake:** the hotline, 720-880-1391, for whether the ice or the boats are open and the day's hours.
- **Skating news:** the district's [skating page](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/public-ice-skating), where the opening day will be posted.
- **County trails:** Jefferson County's [alerts and closures](https://www.jeffco.us/1531/Alerts-Closures).
- **Highway 74:** [COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/), for roadwork between the lake and downtown.
