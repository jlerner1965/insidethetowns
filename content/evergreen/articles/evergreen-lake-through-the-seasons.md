[Evergreen Lake](/places/evergreen-lake/) is not Evergreen's. It belongs to the City and County of Denver as part of Dedisse Mountain Park, which [Denver's parks department](https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Parks-Recreation/Mountain-Parks/Mountain-Park-Descriptions) says it acquired as the Dedisse Ranch in 1919, when Evergreen was already a summer retreat; the park also takes in Evergreen Golf Course, the Dedisse Trail that links to the Jefferson County trails in [Alderfer/Three Sisters Park](/places/alderfer-three-sisters-park/), and a historic log and stone shelter above the water. The Evergreen Park & Recreation District runs what happens on the lake from the Lake House at 29612 Upper Bear Creek Road (720-880-1300), and because the land is Denver's, its skating prices are [the same for everyone](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/public-ice-skating), resident or not. What runs when is below, as the district posted it on October 7, 2026.

| When | What the district runs |
|---|---|
| Summer (the 2026 holiday hours ran from Memorial Day to Labor Day) | Boat rentals and private-boat launching; a shuttle from Stagecoach Park on weekends and holidays |
| After Labor Day | Rentals on weekends only, hours vary; closed for 2026 as of October 7 |
| Winter, when the ice is in | Skating rinks, skate rental, clinics, pick-up hockey, ice-fishing gear |
| All year, sunrise to sunset | The 1.3-mile trail, fishing with a state license, the picnic area |

One rule covers every season: call the boating and skating hotline, 720-880-1391, before you leave. The district says it is [updated daily](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake) and every time the lake closes.

## Summer: the boats

[Rental boats](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/boat-rentals) are first come, first served, with no reservations. In 2026 the hourly rates were $25 for a single kayak or a stand-up paddleboard, $40 for a canoe (up to three people) or a double kayak, and $50 for an XL paddleboard that carries four or a swan pedal boat for two adults and two children. Life jackets, from baby to adult XXL, come with the boat and stay on the whole time; a child under 13 needs an adult of 18 or over to sign the waiver and go out with them. Leashed dogs may ride along, but nobody swims, people or pets, because the lake is the community's drinking water. The last boat goes out an hour before closing. On the 2026 holidays, Memorial Day through Labor Day, the boats ran 9 am–7 pm, and on the three summer-concert days they stopped at noon.

You can bring your own boat, on the terms of the district's [personal watercraft page](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/bring-your-own-boat-personal-watercraft-information). Every private boat checks in at the washing station by the parking lot for an aquatic nuisance species inspection and a day pass, $10 Monday to Thursday and $15 Friday to Sunday, or a $100 season pass per vessel that expired on October 1, 2026. No trailered launching, no gas engines, no sailboats and no pool inflatables. Bring a life jacket or rent one for $10. The pages disagree on size: the [lake rules](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/seasonal-lake-rules) say private boats should be no longer than 15 feet, the personal watercraft page says none larger than 17.

The lot fills on summer weekends and holidays. During boating operations the district runs a free shuttle, 10 am–5 pm, from [Stagecoach Park](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/stagecoach-park) at 3229 El Pinal Drive, at Stagecoach Boulevard and Highway 74, and closes the lake lot once it is full. Summer camps, school outings and clubs that intend to park at the lake must file an EPRD Park Permit Application first.

## Fall

After Labor Day the rentals go to weekends only and the Lake House page says the hours vary. On October 7, 2026 the boat rentals page said boating operations were closed for the season and the shuttle was done for the year, and the Lake House page said summer boating resumes in 2027. The exception the district still lists is its Witches & Warlocks paddle on the morning of October 10, 2026. Fishing and the trail carry on.

## Winter: the ice

Skating depends entirely on the ice, and the district [will announce](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/public-ice-skating) the 2026–27 season once it has an opening day. Last winter's prices were $15 a person for the day, $20 with skate rental and free for children 4 and under; rental skates run from toddler 8 to men's 16 with no half sizes, in hockey or figure styles, and hand sharpening is $10. A rink can be rented for $150 an hour for up to 15 people, and a $200 season pass and a $120 ten-punch pass sell online only. Open skating takes no reservations, hockey stays on rinks 1 to 5, and skate helpers are free. There are no refunds, because the district cannot guarantee opening days or ice quality: in 2025–26 every holiday date from Christmas Eve to Martin Luther King Day was closed for lack of ice and the 15th pond hockey tournament was canceled.

On the ice, by the [winter rules](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/seasonal-lake-rules): skates stay on the groomed ice, shoes stay on the banks, no dogs on the ice or in the warming hut, no sticks or pucks on the main rink, no glass, no alcohol in the park, no open fires, no drones, and skating only in open hours. Weekend and holiday shuttles run from the lower lot at Stagecoach Park, by the turf field. The district's [winter programs](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake-winter-programs-activities), all due back for 2026–27, include skating and hockey clinics for ages 4 to 12, pick-up pond hockey on Sundays for 13 to 18 and Thursdays for adults, 5–7 pm as the lake permits, and ice-fishing kits for $75 for two hours. The district does not manage or monitor ice fishing itself; anyone on the ice to fish needs a Colorado license and is there at their own risk.

## All year: the trail, the fish, the Lake House

The [lake trail](/places/evergreen-lake-trail/) is a natural-surface loop that the district gives as [1.3 miles](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake-house) on its Lake House page and 1.25 on its [park page](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/evergreen-lake-trail), passing an observation boardwalk over the wetlands; the lake itself is 40 acres on one page and 55 on the other. Parts of the trail are not to ADA standard and are hard going for wheelchairs and strollers. Dogs stay leashed, and Jefferson County Animal Control enforces it. The park and trail are open sunrise to sunset.

[Fishing](https://www.evergreenrecreation.com/fishing) for the stocked rainbow trout needs a Colorado fishing license, artificial bait only, no live minnows, from sunrise to sunset, and not around the Lake House except from the accessible pier, nor off the boat docks. The two-acre picnic area has ten tables, all first come; the district writes no reservations for Dedisse Park. No charcoal grills at any time, no grilling at all, even propane, during a fire ban, and no tents or canopies. Portable toilets and drinking fountains are by the lot. Commercial photo shoots need a $25 permit, and drones are banned on all district property.

The Lake House, the old boat and warming house, is rented for weddings and meetings the year round; in winter it becomes the skating center.
