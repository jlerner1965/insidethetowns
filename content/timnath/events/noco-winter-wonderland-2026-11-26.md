Colorado Youth Outdoors' drive-through holiday light show, in its seventh year: a one-mile drive past 22 moving light displays and more than a million tree lights, with Santa, Mrs. Claus and the elves, seen from your car. It opens on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, and runs Thursday to Sunday through December 13, then every night from December 17 to New Year's Eve. Tickets are for timed entry, from 5:15 to 8:30 pm, on the show's ticket page. Sales are final, but a ticket can be exchanged for another night, including when weather cancels an evening.

The show is on Colorado Youth Outdoors' campus on County Road 36, just outside Timnath, with a Fort Collins address. Every ticket supports the nonprofit, whose mission is to strengthen relationships through outdoor recreation. Groups buying ten or more tickets can call 970-663-0800 for a discount.
