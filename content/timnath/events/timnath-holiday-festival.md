Timnath's Holiday Festival is Saturday, December 5, 2026, from 11 am to 1 pm at Timnath Community Park, with photos with Santa, school bands and choirs, ice sculptures, an artisan market and food trucks.

## What is there

The Town's list of activities: arts and crafts, photos and a visit with Santa, hot drinks, cookies, ice sculptures, holiday characters and reindeer visits.

Performing are the Timnath Middle High bands, the Resurrection Christian School choir, the Timnath Middle High poms and The Studio's teams. The Town's page gives no set times for them.

The food trucks are BIGS Meat Wagon, Momma Perez, Scrooge Maki and Sunny Stack Pancakes.

## Parking and shuttles

Park at the Community Park, or parallel park on River Pass Road, Twin Bridge Drive and in the surrounding neighborhoods. Two shuttle stops serve the festival:

- River Pass Road and Saddle Horn Drive
- Twin Bridge Road and Wishing Well Drive

The shuttles are six- and eight-passenger golf carts driven by volunteers. The turnaround by the playground works as a drop-off zone.

## Accessibility and contact

The Town of Timnath runs the festival; Town Center's number is 970-224-3211. For a disability accommodation, the Town's ADA coordinator is Susan Bieber, at the same number, extension 1404. Santa photos are posted for download afterward through a link on the Town's festival page.
