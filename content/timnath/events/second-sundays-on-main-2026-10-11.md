The season's last second Sunday brings all three Old Town markets together from 10 to 2: the Timnath Farmers Market at the Feed & Grain, the Bloem Artisan Market of local makers and fine art, and the District Market of beauty, home and fashion brands, with the Hidden Stem open all day.
