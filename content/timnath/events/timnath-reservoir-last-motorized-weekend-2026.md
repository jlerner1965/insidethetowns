Motorized boats are allowed on Timnath Reservoir on Thursdays to Sundays from April through October, 7 am to 8:30 pm, with a Town permit ($300 a year or $75 a day). This is the season's final Thursday-to-Saturday window; paddling, fishing and the shore stay open through the winter, 7 to 7.
