The Town's free autumn festival on the Town Center grounds: live music, an artisan market, Colorado beer, food trucks, and free activities for the family, from pumpkin decorating and face painting to bounce houses, inflatable games, trampolines, slides and a toddler zone. Food and drink for sale; proceeds benefit the Children's Speech & Reading Center. The Community Park splash pad closes for the season after this weekend.
