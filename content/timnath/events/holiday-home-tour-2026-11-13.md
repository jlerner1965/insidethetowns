A self-guided tour of local homes, each decorated for the season by a professional interior designer, held by the Crawford Child Advocacy Center to support kids and families. This year's designers are Change Everything, Hutch Home Co., Interior Design Concepts, Salt House Interiors and The Styled Entry. General tours run Friday, November 13, from 2 to 7 p.m. and Saturday, November 14, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., at your own pace. A VIP preview and reception the evening before, Thursday, November 12, from 5:30 to 8 p.m., adds appetizers, refreshments and early access to the homes. Tickets are sold through the Center's Holiday Home Tour page, which also links directions to the Harmony Club clubhouse.
