The Town's holiday afternoon at Community Park: photos with Santa, performances from local bands and choirs, ice sculptures, artisan vendors and food trucks, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. (the Town moved the time up an hour from its earlier noon-to-2 listing). The activity, performance and food truck lists were still marked coming soon on November 1. Park in the Community Park lot or on nearby streets; volunteer golf-cart shuttles run from two pickup points.
