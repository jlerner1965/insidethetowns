A cafe on South Colorado Boulevard on the east side of town, in a barn built in 1920 as a dairy milking barn and since restored. It roasts its own coffee by hand and sells it by the bag for brewing at home; the shop has a brewed-coffee menu and a menu of baked goods, with mobile ordering for pickup. Open seven days from 7 am to 3 pm.
