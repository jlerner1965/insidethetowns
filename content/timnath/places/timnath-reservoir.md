Timnath's own lake, about 580 to 600 acres with a six-mile perimeter and the Rockies on the western horizon, owned by the New Cache La Poudre Reservoir Company for irrigation and leased by the Town for recreation: fishing (Colorado license required), swimming, kayaking and paddleboarding, motorized boating Thursday to Sunday from April to October 1, and hiking, biking and picnicking on the shore. Water levels rise and fall with irrigation demand.

It is not open to every visitor. Permits are sold only to Timnath residents, residents of the Town's growth management area, and Colorado residents with a Colorado fishing license; anyone else comes as a permit holder's guest. Everyone needs a permit displayed on the car: annual shore permits are free for Timnath households and $65 for growth-area households and anglers, single-day shore passes $8 to $10, non-motorized boat permits $20 to $35 a year, and motorized boat permits, for residents only, $300 a year or $75 a day. Day-use shore and paddle permits are sold at the reservoir, by card. Open 7 am to 7 pm or dusk outside summer, 6 am to 8:30 pm or dusk from the Friday before Memorial Day through Labor Day. Leashed dogs; no alcohol, camping or fires. Blue-green algae has been confirmed here in past years, usually late summer into early fall, and the Town does not treat it.

Address, phone, hours, fees and rules were read on the Town's reservoir page on 10 October 2026, and who may buy a permit on its Reservoir Permits and Colorado Anglers pages the same day; the anglers page says "Colorado resident anglers only" and allows one permit per fishing license. Permit questions go to (970) 971-8850.
