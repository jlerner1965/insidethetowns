The other half of the Feed & Grain building: a coffeehouse with handcrafted coffee and tea, a soda fountain, breakfast and lunch bites and sweets, plus a general store of artisan gifts, local goods, gourmet groceries and gluten-free baking, the Fine & Funky boutique and the Fairview event room. The Timnath Farmers Market sets up here on second and fourth Sundays from May to October.
