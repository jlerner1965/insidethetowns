A small gated park on Weitzel Street beside the river, 4.5 acres with a fishing pond, climbing structures, picnic areas and soft-surface trails, and the town's trailhead onto the Poudre River Trail. It borders open space and a reservoir belonging to the New Cache la Poudre Irrigation Company. Dawn to dusk.
