The Town's main park, about 25 acres on Summerfields Parkway south of Bethke Elementary: a splash pad running daily 8 to 8 from May 15 until the Fall Festival, multi-age playgrounds and climbing structures, six pickleball courts, two tennis courts, a basketball court, an amphitheater, sheltered picnic areas, a dog park at the east end and open fields. The courts are first come, first served, but Town-sponsored classes, events, programs and tournaments have priority on them. The west restroom building is open all year; the east one is seasonal. The December Holiday Festival is here, and the east side was the site of the Poudre River Trail's completion celebration on 13 June 2026. Dawn to dusk; dogs on a leash outside the dog park.

The address, hours, amenities and court rule were read on the Town's park page on 10 October 2026; the June celebration is on Larimer County's Poudre River Trail page, read the same day.
