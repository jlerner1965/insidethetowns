The Town's main park, about 25 acres on Summerfields Parkway: a splash pad running daily 8 to 8 from mid-May until the Fall Festival, multi-age playgrounds and climbing structures, six pickleball courts, two tennis courts, a basketball court, an amphitheater, sheltered picnic areas, a dog park and open fields. Courts are first come, first served. The December Holiday Festival is here, and the east side was the finish line for the Poudre River Trail's completion celebration in June 2026. Dawn to dusk.
