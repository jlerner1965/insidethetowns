Timnath's brewery opened in 2018 in the old Colorado Feed & Grain elevator on Main Street, with a taproom, indoor and outdoor seating, its own beers on tap and a Fort Collins taproom too. A food truck parks out front most days, and the week’s trucks are on the brewery’s calendar; there is music bingo and live music on the calendar, and a run club meets on Monday evenings. Kids and dogs welcome; leashed dogs on the main floor and patio, not upstairs. Outside food is allowed, and the brewery asks that outside drinks be limited to water. The lot is entered from 3rd Avenue, with more parking along Main Street and in an overflow lot southeast of Kern and 3rd.

The parking and outside-food rules were read on the brewery's Timnath home page on 10 October 2026. That page gives Sunday hours of 11 am to 8 pm, while its contact page, read the same day and the source of the hours above, gives 11 am to 9 pm. The two pages disagree.
