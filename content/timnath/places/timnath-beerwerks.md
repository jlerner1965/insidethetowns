Timnath's brewery opened in 2018 in the old Colorado Feed & Grain elevator on Main Street, with a taproom, indoor and outdoor seating, its own beers on tap and a Fort Collins taproom too. Food trucks park out front Wednesday to Sunday (4 to 8 on weekdays, noon to 8 on weekends), there is music bingo and live music on the calendar, and a run club meets on Monday evenings. Kids and dogs welcome.
