A neighborhood liquor store on Innovate Place, with beer, wine and spirits, tastings and monthly specials.

Orders can be called in ahead, staff carry purchases out at no charge, and they will help with wine pairings, beer choices and cocktails. The website lists a 10 percent case discount on wine and liquor. Tastings are held on set dates.
