The Town of Timnath's administrative building on Signal Tree Drive, where the Town Council meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays at 6 pm. The police department is at its own station at 5601 E Harmony Road.

At Council meetings, public comment on items not on the agenda is limited to three minutes per person. Public hearings on the business agenda take comment after the staff and applicant presentations, and anyone present can ask for a consent-agenda item to be pulled for discussion. The Town publishes agendas and a public-participation guide.
