The Town of Timnath's administrative building on Signal Tree Drive, where the Town Council meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays at 6 pm, and the grounds for the September Fall Festival. The police department is at 4800 Goodman Street beside it and Poudre Fire Authority's Station 8 at 4800 Signal Tree Drive.
