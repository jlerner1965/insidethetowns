The Town of Timnath's administrative building on Signal Tree Drive, where the Town Council meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays at 6 pm, and the grounds for the September Fall Festival. Poudre Fire Authority's Station 8 is at 4800 Signal Tree Drive beside it; the police department moved to its own station at 5601 E Harmony Road in December 2022.

At Council meetings, public comment on items not on the agenda is limited to three minutes per person. Public hearings on the business agenda take comment after the staff and applicant presentations, and anyone present can ask for a consent-agenda item to be pulled for discussion. The Town publishes agendas and a public-participation guide.
