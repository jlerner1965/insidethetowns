An Asian-fusion restaurant in the Signal Tree Drive center by the Town Center, which it dates to 2025. The menu runs to pho, ramen, udon, wonton noodles, lo mein, fried rice and rice bowls such as galbi and teriyaki chicken, and a list of small plates it calls tapas: a bao trio of roast duck, pork belly and char siew, potstickers, crab cheese wontons, dynamite shrimp and rock salt wings. Sake and house-made cocktails; dine in or takeout, Monday to Saturday.
