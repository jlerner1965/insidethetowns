The Town's youth cooking classes are taught by Sapna Von Reich, and every recipe is vegan and vegetarian friendly. Three are listed for the holidays:

- **Festive Appetizers**, Sunday, November 15: full, with a wait list.
- **Cool Christmas Treats**, Saturday, December 12, 1 to 3 pm: "yummy edible treats" that "make good homemade gifts for family, friends and teachers". Enrollment ends December 3.
- **Holiday Snacks**, Sunday, December 20, 1 to 3 pm.

Each is $32, for ages 6 to 13, in the Timnath Community Room. Register on [the Town's RecDesk site](https://timnath.recdesk.com/Community/Program).
