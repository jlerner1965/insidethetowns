**Speak to the Council.** Timnath's council is the mayor and four members, all elected at large. After roll call, time is set aside for anyone to speak on a topic that is not on the agenda, three minutes a person; items with a public hearing take comment after the staff presentation. A comment emailed by 5 pm the day before goes to the council and into the minutes, though it is not read aloud. Meetings are at Town Center and stream on the Town's YouTube channel; the [Agendas & Minutes page](https://timnath.org/agendas-minutes/) has the address for written comments. The next meetings are [November 10](/events/town-council-2026-11-10/) and [November 24](/events/town-council-2026-11-24/).

**Serve on a board.** The [Boards and Commissions page](https://timnath.org/boards-and-commissions-2/) describes four: the **Planning Commission**, seven volunteers appointed by the council, which reviews development requests, rezonings and most land-use requests; the **Finance Committee**, seven members who review the Town's financial health and meet quarterly; the **PROST committee**, which advises the council on parks, recreation, open space and trails and meets every other month ([application](https://timnath.org/prost-committee-member-application/)); and the new **Main Street Board of Directors**, formed to guide the downtown program.
