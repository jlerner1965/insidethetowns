Four listings on the Town's registration site: a 40-minute private lesson for ages 4 to 8, a 60-minute private lesson, 60-minute semi-private lessons and 60-minute small-group lessons. The listings say junior players can work on recreational tennis, fundamentals or a competitive pathway, and that adults of all levels are welcome. Fees are per participant and are on each listing's Fees tab on [RecDesk](https://timnath.recdesk.com/Community/Program).

Lessons depend on the instructor's availability and the weather; the Town says participants are told of any cancellation or rescheduling.
