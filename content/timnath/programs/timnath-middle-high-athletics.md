The school's [Sports Registration page](https://tmh.psdschools.org/athletics/sports-registration) is the one place to start. Each athlete registers online through Poudre School District, turns in a current physical, and pays the district participation fee; the school says "a new Gold 'Permit to Practice' Card is required for every athlete, for every sport", and that all fees must be paid before a card is issued.

Winter registration opened on October 15 for both levels. As the page lists the season:

- **High school boys' basketball**: tryouts November 16 to 18, practice from November 19.
- **High school girls' basketball**: practice starts November 16.
- **High school boys' wrestling**: first official practice November 16, with Gold cards due that day.
- **Middle school boys' basketball**: October 26 to December 16.
- **Middle school girls' basketball**: January 11 to March 3, with Gold cards due January 11.
- **Middle school wrestling**: first official practice January 11.

Scores and schedules for the Cubs are on this guide's [sports page](/sports/).
