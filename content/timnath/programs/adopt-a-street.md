The Town's program "is open to everyone in Timnath—individuals, groups, neighborhoods, businesses, and organizations". A group adopts a street or trail section and keeps it clean for at least one year, and may carry on for as long as it likes if it meets the program's requirements. The [Town's page](https://timnath.org/adopt-a-street/) has the program overview, the safety guidelines and the release forms for volunteers over and under 18.
