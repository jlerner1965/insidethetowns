The Town's winter league "is here to emphasize skill development, teamwork, and sportsmanship", in the Town's words, and runs teams for boys from 2nd grade through 8th. Timnath partners with the Town of Windsor's recreation department: games are on Saturdays in Windsor, on Windsor's schedule, and each team practices twice a week for an hour in Timnath, on days its volunteer coach picks. The season's dates on the listings run from January 18 to March 6, 2027.

Each grade has its own listing on [the Town's RecDesk site](https://timnath.recdesk.com/Community/Program), at $110, and enrollment ends January 11, 2027. The Town's [Recreation Programs page](https://timnath.org/recreation/) lists the rest of the year: soccer in spring, baseball in summer, flag football, soccer and basketball in fall.
