At its October 27 meeting, the Poudre School District board voted to close Timnath Elementary on July 1, 2028, and assign students in its neighborhood boundary to Linton and Bamford elementaries, unless the Town of Timnath secures funding for the full cost of a new elementary school, owned and operated by the district, before the district's first round of school choice applications opens, no later than November 5, 2027, and the district and the Town sign an intergovernmental agreement on their roles by January 31, 2027. On November 3 the Town said the Council is moving forward with an independent community task force to look at ways of keeping elementary students in Timnath, with $25,000 from the current budget and $25,000 planned for 2027. The task force will set its own direction and make its own recommendations, and the Town's page has an interest form for residents who want to take part.
