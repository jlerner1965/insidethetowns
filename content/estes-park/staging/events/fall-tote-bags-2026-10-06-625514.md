Celebrate autumn's crisp colors by turning a simple canvas bag into a wearable work of art! Join us in the Makerspace for a mess-free, tie-dye alternative using permanent markers and rubbing alcohol.

When you apply isopropyl alcohol to Sharpie designs on fabric, the ink breaks down and bleeds across the canvas, creating soft, watercolor-style gradients and vibrant tie-dye bursts. Whether you draw detailed fall foliage, geometric patterns, or abstract bursts of pumpkin spice tones, you will leave with a custom tote ready for market runs, book hauls, or everyday autumn outings.
