A Day of the Dead evening at the Estes Park Event Center, on the program of the Estes Chamber's Latino Alliance and the Estes Arts District. It follows the Arts District's Día de los Muertos art crawl, a month-long show around town with a map, which runs from October 2 to November 3. The chamber's page gives the date, time and place and lists the program as free; it has not yet posted further details.
