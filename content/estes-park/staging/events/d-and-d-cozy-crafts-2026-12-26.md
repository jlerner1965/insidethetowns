A Dungeons & Dragons-themed craft morning in the Estes Valley Library's Makerspace on Saturday, December 26, 10 am to 12:30 pm, for tweens and teens aged 10 to 17: colour a dice tray, make a "dice potion", and battle a monster together with D&D-style skill checks, with hot chocolate, themed music and a fake fire. No experience with the game is needed.

Registration is required and space is limited; it opens at 10 am on Saturday, November 14, on the library's calendar. The library lists a second session on Wednesday, December 30.
