The fourth First Peoples Festival, January 14 to 17, 2027: a competition powwow, the First Peoples Film Festival, the First Peoples Fashion Show, musical guests and a Native art market whose vendors all come from federally recognized tribes. Free. The Town's page says the 2027 schedule will be posted in November.
