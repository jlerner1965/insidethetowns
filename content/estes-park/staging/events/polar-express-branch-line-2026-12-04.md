All aboard!

Join us for a reading of The Polar Express, followed by a trolley ride to view the holiday lights, wave to Santa, ring jingle bells, and sing along to holiday songs. We'll then return to the Library's Hondius Community Room for refreshments, including hot chocolate, cookies, and a gift to take home.

Due to popularity and seating limitations, we request a maximum of two adult trolley riders per family. All family members are encouraged to join us for the book reading, cookies, and cocoa. Registration should include all trolley riders.

Please contact youthservices@estesvalleylibrary.org with any cancellations so we can accommodate as many children as possible at this highly anticipated Estes Valley holiday tradition.

This event would not be possible without the support of the Estes Park Newcomers Club and sponsorship from the Estes Valley Sunrise Rotary Club and the Friends of the Library Foundation. Thank you!
