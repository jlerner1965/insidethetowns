The MacGregor family homesteaded here, and their home, built in the late 1800s, keeps its original furnishings, photographs and belongings. Visitors can take a docent-led museum tour, a museum-and-barns tour or a self-guided audio tour (bring a phone and headphones). The ranch also runs nature camps for children and sells its own Black Angus beef.
