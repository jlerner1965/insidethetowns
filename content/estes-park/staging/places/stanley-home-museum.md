The Stanleys' 1904 house, which predates The Stanley Hotel by about four years, is run by The Historic Stanley Home Foundation, a nonprofit formed in 2016. Tours are guided only, last about an hour and a half and take at most eight guests; adult tickets are $25, seniors, veterans and active military $15, and children 8 and under free. The first floor is accessible by ramp; the second floor is not, and guides narrate it with photographs instead.
