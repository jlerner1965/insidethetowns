Lumpy Ridge pours from two places: the original brewery in a converted gas station on Route 7, open daily from noon to 8 pm, and the Publick House in downtown Estes Park at 203 Cleave St., open Thursday and Friday 2 to 8 pm and Saturday and Sunday noon to 8 pm. Pets are welcome on its outdoor patios; food comes from visiting food trucks or you bring your own snacks, and beer goes home in cans.
