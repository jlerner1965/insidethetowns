A paved four-mile loop around the lake, crossing Town of Estes Park, Larimer County and Bureau of Reclamation land and managed by the Estes Valley Recreation and Park District, which rates it easy. Get on it from the Visitor Center, parking garage or Fisherman's Nook lots, or from the paid-entry lots at Cherokee Draw, Wapiti Meadows and the Lake Estes Marina; restrooms and drinking fountains are at Cherokee Draw. Dogs are welcome on a leash.
