Rocky Mountain National Park's Hiker Shuttle leaves from outside the visitor center for the park's Park & Ride on Bear Lake Road; tickets are reserved on Recreation.gov and a park entrance pass is also needed. The Estes Valley Library's parking page points drivers to the free parking garage just south of the visitor center.
