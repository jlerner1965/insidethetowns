Kind Coffee's shop sits on the edge of town within walking distance of the Visitor Center and its shuttles, with a full food and drink menu; the company sells organic, fair-trade, shade-grown coffee. It asks visitors to keep their distance from the elk and deer that come into the green space beside the shop.
