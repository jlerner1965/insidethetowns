A lodge on Big Thompson Avenue with a hot tub, breakfast, barbecue areas, a gazebo and a children's play area. Balconies, views and bed arrangements depend on the room type, described on the lodge's accommodations pages.

Check-in is after 4 pm and check-out at 11 am. The front desk is open 7 am–7 pm in the off-season and until 10 pm in the peak summer months, so call ahead for a late arrival. The lodge's special offers page advertises 20% off stays through March 2027.

Hours and amenities were read on the lodge's [hotel information](https://www.estesdiscoverylodge.com/hotel-information), [amenities](https://www.estesdiscoverylodge.com/amenities), [contact](https://www.estesdiscoverylodge.com/contact-us-1) and [special offers](https://www.estesdiscoverylodge.com/special-offers) pages on 11 October 2026.
