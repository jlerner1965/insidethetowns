Brownfield's has been a seasonal business since 1956, selling souvenirs, shirts and outdoor gear and filling the store with new stock each season; it says it means to stay seasonal. Store hours are 10 am to 8 pm, Monday to Saturday, and it closes for the season in early November. Staff hand out hiking tips for Rocky Mountain National Park.
