Brownfield's has been a seasonal business since 1956, selling souvenirs, shirts and outdoor gear and filling the store with new stock each season; it says it means to stay seasonal. It is open from Memorial Day weekend until early November. It has now closed for the 2026 season: its site says the store is closed until its Rummage Sale on Saturday, November 7, at 10 am. Staff hand out hiking tips for Rocky Mountain National Park.
