Brownfield's has been a seasonal business since 1956, selling souvenirs, shirts and outdoor gear and filling the store with new stock each season; it says it means to stay seasonal. It is open from Memorial Day weekend until early November. It has closed for the 2026 season, and its site says it reopens in spring 2027. Staff hand out hiking tips for Rocky Mountain National Park.
