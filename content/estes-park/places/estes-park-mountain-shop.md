Since 1986 the shop has sold equipment and clothing, rented gear for summer, camping and winter (including adaptive equipment), and run guided rock climbing, hiking and snowshoe trips. Its indoor climbing gym has more than 4,500 square feet of terrain, including a 600-square-foot bouldering wall. Open 8 am to 8 pm daily.
