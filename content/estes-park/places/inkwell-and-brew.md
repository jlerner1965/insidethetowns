A shop for people who still write by hand, selling fountain and calligraphy pens, journals, hand-pressed papers and letterpress cards, with a coffee bar serving cappuccino, cortado, pour-over and cold brew. It is open seven days a week, and its site says to check its social media (@inkwellbrew) for last-minute closures for weather or other emergencies.
