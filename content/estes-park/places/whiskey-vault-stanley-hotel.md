The whiskey bar at The Stanley Hotel, stocked with single-barrel and special bottlings, most of them from runs of fewer than 200 bottles, and a menu of bar food. Private, guided tastings take place in the vault itself, and ticketed tastings with distilleries appear on its events list. The hotel says its bars are open to visitors as well as guests.

The open-to-visitors note is from the hotel's [FAQ](https://www.stanleyhotel.com/faqs/).
