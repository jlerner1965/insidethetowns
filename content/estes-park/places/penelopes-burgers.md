A burger place on West Elkhorn Avenue whose menu offers beef, bison or elk patties, gourmet brats and dogs, and fries cut by hand every day from fresh potatoes. Sides include fried pickles and mac and cheese bites; there is also elk chili, loaded baked potatoes, Rocky Mountain oysters, shakes, malts and old-fashioned sodas. Open 11 am to 8 pm every day.
