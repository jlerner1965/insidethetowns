The Estes Park branch of Smokin' Dave's, which also has restaurants in Denver, Longmont, Lyons and Greeley. The meats are hand-rubbed and slow-smoked, the draft list rotates between local and classic beers, and there is online ordering for pickup and catering for Estes Park and nearby mountain towns. Open 11 am to 8 pm every day.

The list of locations is on the group's [home page](https://smokindavesbbq.com/).
