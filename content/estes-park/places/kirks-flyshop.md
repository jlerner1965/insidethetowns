A downtown fly shop across Elkhorn Avenue from Town Hall that sells flies and gear, publishes a local fishing report and books guided fishing trips and other mountain adventures. Its site suggests parking in the lot by the Dairy Queen and walking back a couple of shops. The posted winter hours are 8 am to 5 pm; there is also a toll-free line, (877) 669-1859, and the shop ships gear to the lower 48 states.

The shop's contact page gives winter hours of 8 am to 5 pm and no days of the week or summer schedule; call (970) 577-0790 before a special trip.
