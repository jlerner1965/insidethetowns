Park Tavern describes its food as modern pub fare rooted in tradition and made from scratch: craft burgers, reimagined classics and familiar comforts, served with cocktails, wine and craft beer. The patio is dog friendly, the tavern takes reservations, and its site lists its events and a Tavern Mercantile. Open every day from 11 am to 9 pm.
