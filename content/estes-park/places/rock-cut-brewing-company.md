A downtown brewery and taproom on West Riverside Drive, at the base of Prospect Mountain, that pitches itself as a stop on the way back from the national park. It has seating inside and out, a patio where dogs are welcome, and crowlers and specialty bottles to take away. Trivia runs every Tuesday from 7 to 9 pm; the brewery's [events calendar](https://www.rockcutbrewing.com/events-2/) lists the rest.
