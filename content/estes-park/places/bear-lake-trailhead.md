The trailhead at the top of Bear Lake Road, which climbs 9.2 miles from its junction with Trail Ridge Road near the Beaver Meadows Entrance; the park says the destination is popular enough that the lot fills early all year, especially on winter weekends and holidays. A marked path leads from the lot to the lake; the park lists the Bear Lake loop at 0.6 mile, Nymph Lake at 0.5, Dream Lake at 1.1, Emerald Lake at 1.7 and Flattop Mountain at 4.2 miles one way. The park expects snow on the Bear Lake Trail from late October to early June and advises traction devices and waterproof boots.
