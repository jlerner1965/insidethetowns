The Stanley's evening steakhouse serves premium cuts, seafood and seasonal Colorado dishes; the bar opens an hour before the dining room, at 4 pm. It has a private dining room for groups of 16 to 48, and the hotel strongly recommends reservations. Non-guests are welcome, as at all the hotel's restaurants.

Reservation advice and the open-to-visitors note are from the hotel's [FAQ](https://www.stanleyhotel.com/faqs/).
