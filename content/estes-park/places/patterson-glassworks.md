A hand-blown glass studio and gallery on the western side of downtown, recognizable by its garden of glass decorations. Visitors can watch the glassblowers at work and buy functional and decorative pieces. It is open 9 am to 4:45 pm every day, but asks visitors to call for hours in January and February; parking in front is free, near the river, Performance Park and public restrooms.
