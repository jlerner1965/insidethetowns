An outdoor store on Moraine Avenue selling gear and clothing for hiking, fishing and hunting, renting gear, teaching fly tying and running guided fly fishing, hiking and snowshoe trips. Its hours follow the seasons: 9 am to 5 pm daily in spring and fall, 8 am to 6 pm daily in summer, and in winter 9 am to 4 pm every day, except that the shop closes on Wednesdays in January and February. The owner confirmed the listing by email on 9 October 2026 and corrected the winter hours; everything else here was as he has it.
