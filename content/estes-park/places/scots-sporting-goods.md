An outdoor store on Moraine Avenue selling gear and clothing for hiking, fishing and hunting, renting gear, teaching fly tying and running guided fly fishing, hiking and snowshoe trips. Its hours follow the seasons: 9 am to 5 pm daily in spring and fall, 8 am to 6 pm daily in summer, and in winter 9 am to 4 pm on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday to Saturday, 9 am to 2 pm on Sunday, closed Wednesday.
