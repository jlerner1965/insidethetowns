A Rocky Mountain National Park trailhead at 7,840 feet. From Bond Park in downtown Estes Park, drive 1.5 miles on MacGregor Avenue/Devils Gulch Road to the Lumpy Ridge sign, turn left and go 0.3 mile. The park's trail list gives Gem Lake as 1.6 miles one way with about 1,000 feet of gain.

Directions are from the park's [McGregor Mountain campsite page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/mcgregor-mountain.htm); distances from its [list of hiking trails](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/list_hiking_trails.htm); the permit and pass rules from the [timed-entry](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm) and [fees](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fees.htm) pages, both of which name Lumpy Ridge.
