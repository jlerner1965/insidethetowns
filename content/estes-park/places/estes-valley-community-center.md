The Estes Valley Recreation and Park District’s community center on Community Drive: lap and leisure pools, a gymnasium, weight and cardio equipment, fitness classes and personal training, youth and adult sports, a golf simulator, a children’s area it calls the Cubz Den, and venues for events. Daily admission, punch passes and memberships are sold at the front desk, and the district says to stop by for a tour.

The district’s page gives the building’s hours as Monday to Friday 6 am to 8 pm and Saturday 9 am to 6 pm, and gives Sunday twice, as 9 am to 6 pm in one place and noon to 5 pm in another, so they are not listed as a schedule here; call before a Sunday visit. Aquatic hours vary by pool and are their own schedule, with age and supervision rules of their own. The center closes for Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year’s Day and closes early on December 9 and December 31.
