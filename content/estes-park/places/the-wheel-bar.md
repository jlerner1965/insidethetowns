A bar in the center of downtown on East Elkhorn Avenue that says it has opened every day but Christmas since 1945. Inside are two pool tables, shuffleboard, a dart board, foosball, arcade games and a digital jukebox; the house claim is its Bloody Mary. Parking is out back, across the river from the patio, and there is a private room upstairs and a lower bar for parties.
