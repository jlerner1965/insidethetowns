Two neighboring storefronts on East Elkhorn Avenue: a wildlife gallery and a home store. The stock runs from wildlife sculpture, paintings and handmade knives to Pendleton throws, pipes and tobacco, watches, lighting, crystal glassware and Spode dinnerware, and much of it is also sold online. Open 10 am to 6 pm Monday to Saturday and 11 am to 6 pm on Sunday.
