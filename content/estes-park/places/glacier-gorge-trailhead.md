The start of the Glacier Gorge trails, at 9,175 feet, and in the park's words one of its most popular hiking areas all year. One-way distances on the park's corridor page: Alberta Falls 0.8 mile, Lake Haiyaha 2.1, Mills Lake 2.7, The Loch and Jewel Lake 3, Sky Pond 4.2 and Black Lake 4.7 miles. From Beaver Meadows Visitor Center, take US 36 west about 1.3 miles to Bear Lake Road, then follow it about 8 miles.

Directions and the towing note are from the park's [Glacier Gorge wilderness campsite page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/glacier-gorge.htm); permit hours from the [timed-entry page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm); shuttle dates from the [shuttle page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/shuttle-buses-and-public-transit.htm).
