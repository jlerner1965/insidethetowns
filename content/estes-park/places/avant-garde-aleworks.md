A family-founded craft brewery on Dunraven Street, off Highway 7 behind Napa Auto Parts, with a taproom and patio. The taps rotate with the seasons and include gluten-free seltzers and non-alcoholic drinks, and cans and crowlers go home. Food trucks come through the week, Tuesday is vinyl night, and the open mic is on the first and third Wednesdays of the month. Seating is walk-in; groups of 20 to 200 can book the event space.
