Run by the Town of Estes Park's Events Division, the complex offers large indoor and outdoor event space and equestrian facilities, with on-site concessions. The division's calendar of signature events runs from the First Peoples Festival in January and the Wine & Chocolate Festival in February through Bigfoot Days in April, the Wool Market in June and the Rooftop Rodeo in July. It also books Town venues elsewhere, including Bond Park, Performance Park, George Hix Riverside Plaza, Mrs. Walsh’s Garden and the O’Connor Pavilion; email events@estes.org.
