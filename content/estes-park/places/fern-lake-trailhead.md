The Fern Lake trail starts where the road along the edge of Moraine Park ends, at 8,155 feet. The park lists The Pool at 1.7 miles, Fern Falls at 2.5, Fern Lake at 3.8, Spruce Lake at 4.7 and Odessa Lake at 4.9 miles one way. From Beaver Meadows Visitor Center, take US 36 west to Bear Lake Road, follow it 1.3 miles, turn right on Moraine Park Campground Road and then left at the sign for the riding stable and trailheads.

Directions are from the park's [Fern Lake campsite page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fern-lake.htm); permit hours from the [timed-entry page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm); shuttle stops from the [shuttle page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/shuttle-buses-and-public-transit.htm).
