The shop has made its Estes Park Salt Water Taffy since 1935 and says it is the only store in town where you can watch taffy being made. Batches are cooked as needed, so there is no fixed schedule; the shop suggests calling in the morning to find out when. It is open all year, daily from 9 am to 6 pm at least, and often longer; boxes can be shipped from the store or ordered online.

The founding year and the machine are from the shop's [home page](https://originaltaffyshop.com/).
