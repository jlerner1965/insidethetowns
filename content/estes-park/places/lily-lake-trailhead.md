One of the park's outlying areas, on Highway 7 south of town. The trail by the lake has benches, a viewing dock, two bridges and a boardwalk through a wetland that is good for spotting wildlife, with views of Longs Peak, Lily Mountain, Twin Sisters Peak and the Mummy Range; the park's page lists it as accessible for people with mobility impairments. The park's timed-entry and fees pages both name Lily Lake among the outlying areas they cover. The Forest Service also suggests this lot for the Lily Mountain Trail, a quarter mile to the north.

Read on the park's [Lily Lake trail page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/lily-lake-trail-wedding-site.htm), [timed-entry page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm) and [fees page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fees.htm), and the Forest Service's [Lily Mountain Trail page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/trails/lily-mountain-trail).
