A National Park Service park with more than 350 miles of hiking trail; Trail Ridge Road crosses it, 48 miles one way, and climbs to 12,183 feet. Entrance passes are sold at the staffed entrance stations (cards only; the park is cashless) or online at Recreation.gov, and timed-entry reservations ($2 processing fee) are sold only through Recreation.gov, not at the gates. The park's Information Office answers 970-586-1206 year-round: 8 am–4 pm daily in summer, and in winter 8 am–4 pm on weekdays and 8 am–noon at weekends; the recorded Trail Ridge Road line is (970) 586-1222. The address above is the park's mailing address.
