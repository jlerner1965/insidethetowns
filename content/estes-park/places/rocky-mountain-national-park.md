A National Park Service park with more than 350 miles of hiking trail; Trail Ridge Road crosses it, 48 miles one way, and climbs to 12,183 feet. Entrance passes are sold at the staffed entrance stations (cards only) or online at Recreation.gov, and timed-entry reservations ($2 processing fee) are sold only through Recreation.gov, not at the gates. The park's Information Office answers 970-586-1206 year-round: 8 am–4 pm daily in summer, and in winter 8 am–4 pm on weekdays and 8 am–noon at weekends; the recorded Trail Ridge Road line is (970) 586-1222. The address above is the park's mailing address.

An entrance pass is required in the park's outlying areas too, including Lumpy Ridge, Lily Lake, Wild Basin and the East and North Inlet trailheads. Most entrance passes can be bought with cash at the Rocky Mountain Conservancy nature stores in the Beaver Meadows, Fall River and Kawuneeche visitor centers. Annual and lifetime passes ordered online can take two to four weeks to arrive by mail; for a trip sooner than that, buy in person.
