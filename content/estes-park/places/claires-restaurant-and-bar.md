Claire's has been on Park Lane since 1992 and cooks a menu that changes with the seasons around a set of staples, with a full bar. It opens at 11 am on weekdays and at 9 am on Saturdays and Sundays for breakfast until noon, and closes at 8 pm every day. It is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas, and takes reservations, parties and catering orders.
