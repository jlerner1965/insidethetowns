The trailhead and Wild Basin Ranger Station sit at the end of Wild Basin Road, about 2 miles in from Highway 7, at 8,500 feet. To get there from Beaver Meadows Visitor Center, go about a mile east on US 36, take Mary’s Lake Road 2.4 miles to Highway 7, turn right and follow it 9.1 miles to Wild Basin Road; drive past Wild Basin Lodge and bear right to the entrance station. The park's trail list gives Copeland Falls at 0.3 mile, Calypso Cascades 1.8, Ouzel Falls 2.7, Ouzel Lake 4.9, Bluebird Lake 6 and Thunder Lake 6.8 miles one way.

Directions are from the park's [North St. Vrain campsite page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/north-st-vrain.htm); distances from its [list of hiking trails](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/list_hiking_trails.htm); the road note from [Park Roads](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm); permit and pass rules from the [timed-entry](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm) and [fees](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fees.htm) pages.
