A Canyon Lakes Ranger District trailhead in the Roosevelt National Forest, 7 miles east of Estes Park and 13 miles west of Lyons. The Lion Gulch Trail drops at first, then climbs about three miles beside a stream to Homestead Meadows, where information boards tell the story of each homestead; visiting all of them adds 2.8 miles. Horses are allowed (certified weed-free hay only), motorized equipment is not, and historic artifacts are to be left where they lie.
