An independent bookshop downtown that sells books for readers of all ages, publishes local reading recommendations and holds author and community events, some after hours. It runs a Fall Trolley Event and book-donation drives (in April, the Month of the Child, and again in July and September) to get books to local children, and sells online through Bookshop.org and audiobooks through Libro.fm.

The shop's history goes back to a log cabin built for the Forest Service in 1907. Books moved into the front parlor of the Macdonald family home in 1928 and gradually filled more of the house; the store's history page traces its generations of ownership.
