Right now, a day in [Rocky Mountain National Park](/places/rocky-mountain-national-park/) needs only an entrance pass: $30 a car for one day, sold at the gates by card. In the timed-entry season, which the park's [2024 plan](https://www.nps.gov/romo/getinvolved/day-use-visitor-access-strategy.htm) sets from late May through mid-October, it also needs a reservation for the busiest hours, or a plan to arrive outside them. The 2027 dates and hours are not yet announced: the [timed-entry page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm) still gave 2026's on November 18, 2026.

This matters to anyone driving in from Estes Park in summer: in 2026, a car without a reservation was turned around during the reserved hours. It also matters to anyone bringing a dog: dogs are not allowed on any park trail, so a dog's hike is outside the park.

## What to book

| Your day | What to book | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Any day of the year | An entrance pass: $30 a car for one day, $35 for seven, $70 for the park's annual pass | At the gate (cards only) or Recreation.gov |
| Late May to mid-October: Trail Ridge Road, Lily Lake, Lumpy Ridge, Wild Basin | Timed Entry (in 2026, 9 am–2 pm) | Recreation.gov, $2 |
| Late May to mid-October: Bear Lake Road (Moraine Park, Sprague Lake, Park & Ride, Glacier Gorge, Bear Lake) | Timed Entry + Bear Lake Road (in 2026, 5 am–6 pm) | Recreation.gov, $2 |
| Summer, leaving the car in town | Hiker Shuttle tickets from the Estes Park Visitor Center, plus an entrance pass | Recreation.gov |
| A night in a park campground | Nothing more: the campground reservation includes timed entry | Recreation.gov |

The [fees page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fees.htm) adds a $100 fee for each visitor 16 or older who does not live in the United States, unless they enter on an annual or America the Beautiful pass. Children under 16 need no pass.

## How the reservations worked in 2026

The park announced the 2026 system on [February 18, 2026](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/rocky-mountain-national-park-announces-2026-timed-entry-reservation-system.htm). These were its rules; 2027's may differ, since the plan lets the park change the season, the hours and the release dates each year.

- **Sales:** on the first of each month at 8 am for the month ahead, from May 1, with 40 percent of each day's reservations held for 7 pm the night before.
- **Where:** only on Recreation.gov, its app or 877-444-6777. Never at the gates or visitor centers.
- **Cost:** a $2 processing fee, not refunded, on top of the entrance pass.
- **One a day:** one reservation a vehicle a day, with the holder there and photo ID.
- **The window:** enter within your two hours, then stay as long as you like. On Bear Lake Road, leaving and coming back is allowed only after 2 pm.
- **Parking:** a reservation does not hold you a parking space.

## A day with a reservation

Arrive early: the park says lines at the entrance can take 30 minutes or longer. On Bear Lake Road the [Bear Lake lot](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/exploring-the-bear-lake-road-corridor.htm) is often full by 9 am, and Glacier Gorge fills early too. In 2026 the park's answer was Park & Ride, 5.2 miles up the road, and the free Bear Lake shuttle from there every 10 to 15 minutes, 6:30 am to 7:30 pm.

The park's distances for the short walks:

|---|---|---|---|
| Bear Lake loop | [Bear Lake](/places/bear-lake-trailhead/) | 0.6 mile, a loop | 71 feet |
| Nymph Lake | Bear Lake | 0.5 mile one way | 231 feet |

## If you can't get one

Reservations sell out, the night-before ones fastest. In 2026 the park offered these ways in without one:

1. **Go early or late.** Enter before 9 am or after 2 pm for the rest of the park, and before 5 am or after 6 pm for Bear Lake Road.
2. **Try the night before.** Log on to Recreation.gov at 7 pm the evening before your day.
3. **Leave the car in town.** The [Hiker Shuttle](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/shuttle-buses-and-public-transit.htm) left the Estes Park Visitor Center eight times from 8:30 am to 1:30 pm, daily May 22 to September 7 and at weekends to October 18; the last bus back left Park & Ride at 6 pm. Tickets were $2 a reservation for up to four people, and every rider also needs an entrance pass. The park will post 2027's schedule in spring 2027.
4. **Ride a bike.** Cyclists did not need a timed-entry reservation in 2026.

Turned around at 9 am? Give the middle of the day to the town, such as the Lake Estes loop below, and drive in after 2 pm.

## In winter, now to late May

No reservation is needed, and the entrance pass still is. [Trail Ridge Road](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/trail-ridge-road-closes-to-through-travel-for-winter-2026-2027-season.htm) closed to through travel on November 13, 2026, at Many Parks Curve on the east side; it typically reopens in the last week of May. Bear Lake Road is [open all year](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm), with snow and ice, and Colorado's traction law can apply. There are no shuttles in winter, and the Bear Lake lot still fills by 9 am, most of all on weekends and holidays.

From October to early June the park says to [expect snow-packed trails](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/plan-your-winter-springtime-visit-to-rocky.htm); traction devices are advised, and snowshoes may be needed. A [prescribed burn](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/news/rocky-mountain-national-park-prepares-for-possible-prescribed-burn.htm) between the Beaver Meadows Visitor Center and Bear Lake Road may happen any time this winter, with traffic control on US 36 and Bear Lake Road.

## Where dogs can hike

Pets are [not allowed](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/pets.htm) on any trail, tundra or meadow in the park, in visitor centers or on its buses. On a leash no longer than six feet they may go along roads, in parking areas, campgrounds and picnic areas. In winter the closed stretches of Trail Ridge Road and Old Fall River Road are open to walkers and leashed dogs, on the road only.

For a real walk, go outside the park:

| Trail | Manager | Distance or size | Dogs | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Lake Estes Trail](/places/lake-estes-trail/) | [Estes Valley Recreation and Park District](https://evrpd.com/parks-and-trails/) | Paved 4-mile loop, rated easy, joined from the Visitor Center, parking-garage or Fisherman's Nook lots or the paid lots | On a leash | $8 a car for the day at the paid lots: Cherokee Draw, Wapiti Meadows and the marina |
| Hermit Park Open Space | [Larimer County](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/hermit-park) | 1,362 acres, about 4 miles southeast of downtown on US 36 | On a leash of 10 feet or less, never left alone | $10 a vehicle; a permit for every visit, on foot too. Open March 1 to December 20 |
| [Lion Gulch Trail](/places/lion-gulch-trailhead/) | [USDA Forest Service](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/lion-gulch-trailhead) | A three-mile climb to Homestead Meadows, from the trailhead 7 miles east of town on US 36 | On a hand-held leash | None |

The Lake Estes loop's lots at Cherokee Draw have restrooms and drinking fountains. Hermit Park closes for the winter on December 21.

## Before you go

- **Reservations and 2027 dates:** the [timed-entry page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm). The 2026 system was announced in February.
- **Roads:** the park does not post road status on its website. Call the recorded Trail Ridge Road line, 970-586-1222.
- **Questions:** the Information Office, 970-586-1206; in winter, 8 am–4 pm on weekdays and 8 am–noon at weekends.
- **Trails and closures:** the park's [conditions page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/conditions.htm).
