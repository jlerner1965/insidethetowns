Estes Park sits at about 7,700 feet at the east entrance to Rocky Mountain National Park, reached by US 34 from Loveland or US 36 from Lyons and Boulder, an hour and a half to two hours from Denver. One main thing a day, plus a meal, is the realistic plan: altitude, weather, park reservations and booked tours each take time. Park once at the Town's free parking structure at 691 North St. Vrain Avenue, by the Visitor Center where US 34 meets US 36: 415 spaces on four levels, with accessible spaces and EV charging. The Town says downtown is under ten minutes on foot from it, over the pedestrian bridge across the Big Thompson River and through the lighted tunnel under US 36 to the Riverwalk. Downtown lots are paid from May 22 to October 18 in 2026 and free after that until late May.

## 01 · Elkhorn Avenue

Elkhorn Avenue is the main street, and its stops are a walk, not a drive. [Macdonald Book Shop](/places/macdonald-book-shop/) at 152 East Elkhorn opens at 9 am every day. [The Taffy Shop](/places/the-taffy-shop/) at 121 West Elkhorn has made salt water taffy to the same recipe since 1935; open daily 9 am–6 pm, all year. [Estes Perk](/places/estes-perk/) at 145 East Elkhorn is coffee and breakfast by day and a cocktail lounge by night, 8 am–9 pm. At the west end, [Patterson Glassworks](/places/patterson-glassworks/) at 323 West Elkhorn lets you watch the glassblowers, 9 am–4:45 pm daily, with free parking out front near the river and public restrooms; call ahead in January and February.

*Getting there:* on foot from the parking structure. *Best for:* the first hour in town, a bookshop and a coffee.

## 02 · Riverside Drive: the tramway

The [Estes Park Aerial Tramway](/places/estes-park-aerial-tramway/) at 420 East Riverside Drive has run since 1955 and carries riders 2,630 feet up to the 8,708-foot summit of Prospect Mountain in about five minutes. The 2026 season ends on November 1: from October 12 the hours are 9 am–6 pm, with the last tram up forty minutes before closing. Tickets are sold by arrival time, $39.95 for adults and $24.95 for ages 6 to 17, children 5 and under free; the 57-space lot charges $7.95 for four hours and can fill. [Rock Cut Brewing Company](/places/rock-cut-brewing-company/) at 390 West Riverside, at the foot of the same mountain, is open 11 am–10 pm daily.

*Getting there:* a short drive from downtown, or the tram's own lot. *Best for:* the afternoon slot, through November 1 in 2026; after that, give the afternoon to the Stanley instead.

## 03 · Moraine Avenue: the meal

Moraine Avenue, US 36 on its way to the Beaver Meadows entrance, is where this day puts lunch. [Bird & Jim](/places/bird-and-jim/) at 915 Moraine has been open since 2016, with seasonal menus and a wine list picked by the owner, a sommelier; it opens at 11 am every day and closes about 9 pm, and reservations are encouraged. [Smokin' Dave's BBQ & Brew](/places/smokin-daves-bbq-estes-park/) at 820 Moraine serves slow-smoked meats 11 am–8 pm every day.

*Getting there:* by car; the restaurants have their own parking. *Best for:* the meal the rest of the day is built around.

## 04 · The Stanley Hotel

[The Stanley Hotel](/places/the-stanley-hotel/) at 333 East Wonderview Avenue was built by F.O. Stanley in 1909 and is open to visitors who are not staying. Its historic, ghost, Shining and whiskey-vault tours are each sold as 60-minute walking tours, booked on the hotel's site; the hotel says most last 60 to 75 minutes. Day visitors pay $10 to park. [Cascades](/places/cascades-restaurant-stanley-hotel/) serves breakfast from 7 am through a late menu, and [MacGregor Chophouse](/places/macgregor-chophouse/) does dinner from 5 pm with reservations strongly recommended. Only trained service animals are allowed in the buildings.

*Getting there:* by car, a short drive north of downtown off US 34. *Best for:* a booked afternoon tour on a day without the park.

## 05 · The national park, on another day

[Rocky Mountain National Park](/places/rocky-mountain-national-park/) begins at the west edge of town. An entrance pass is needed at every hour of the year, and in 2026 a timed-entry reservation was needed as well from May 22 to October 12, or to October 18 for the Bear Lake Road corridor. The 0.6-mile loop at [Bear Lake](/places/bear-lake-trailhead/) and the accessible loop at [Sprague Lake](/places/sprague-lake-trailhead/) are the short walks. [The first-visit guide](/articles/first-visit-to-rocky-mountain-national-park/) has the pass, the reservations, the shuttles and the weather; read it before the park day.

*Getting there:* US 36 to the Beaver Meadows entrance or US 34 to Fall River. *Best for:* a day of its own.

## A simple first visit

1. Park at the Town's free structure at 691 North St. Vrain Avenue and walk to the Riverwalk and Elkhorn Avenue.
2. Browse the bookshop, the taffy shop and the glassworks; coffee at Estes Perk.
3. Lunch on Moraine Avenue at Bird & Jim or Smokin' Dave's.
4. In the afternoon, the tramway (through November 1 in 2026) or a booked Stanley tour, not both.
5. Keep the national park for a separate day, with the pass and the reservation sorted first.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service and [CDOT's COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) before you set out. From Denver it is about an hour and a half to two hours; Loveland by US 34 and Lyons by US 36 are nearer. The Town's free seasonal transit, [The Peak](https://estespark.colorado.gov/transit), runs from late May through late October: in 2026 the Red Route along Elkhorn Avenue runs daily 9 am–9 pm through October 18.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Town says its parking structure has accessible spaces and EV charging, that every Peak route is ADA accessible, and that riders may ask for a route deviation of up to three-quarters of a mile by calling 970-577-3959. The tramway says it has ADA-accessible restrooms at both terminals, a ramp to board, cars that take wheelchairs including large electric ones, and short flat paved paths from the accessible parking to the tram and from the summit platform to the cafe. The Stanley says accessible accommodation and public spaces are available throughout the property. In the park, Sprague Lake's loop is described by the park as wheelchair and stroller accessible.

Curb ramps and step-free entrances along Elkhorn Avenue, and the Riverwalk's surface, have not been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here; contact a business directly before relying on it. Corrections and additions to this section are welcome and [published with their date](/contact/).
