Estes Park's places for children split between the national park on the town's west edge and the recreation district's buildings and parks in town. October is the hinge of the year here: the park's summer programs have closed, Trail Ridge Road is about to, and the indoor pools, the library and the playgrounds carry the winter. Here is what there is, with the hours, ages and prices each operator publishes, read on October 7, 2026.

## Rocky Mountain National Park with children

The park's free [Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm) program is the place to start. Pick up a free activity booklet at any visitor center, in English or Spanish, in three versions: ages 5 and under, 6 to 8, and 9 and up; children are sworn in and then work through it. The booklets can also be printed at home from the same page. The park's Junior Ranger Headquarters at Hidden Valley, about 7 miles up Trail Ridge Road from the Beaver Meadows or Fall River entrances, and the activities at the Moraine Park Discovery Center are closed for the season; the park suggests both places for families to explore on their own in the meantime.

On the east side, the Beaver Meadows Visitor Center on US 36 and the Fall River Visitor Center on US 34 are both [open year-round](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/kidsyouth/index.htm), with restrooms, an information desk and Junior Ranger booklets; Fall River has wildlife exhibits. The 23-minute park film, Spirit of the Mountains, [plays daily](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/ranger-led-programs.htm) at Beaver Meadows. Ranger-led programs are free (the entrance fee still applies), mostly in summer, with occasional winter programs at the visitor centers.

[Entrance](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fees.htm) is $30 for a car for one day or $35 for seven, paid by card at the entrance stations; anyone on foot or by bike pays $15 a day, but children under 16 need no pass of their own. US fourth graders can get a free America the Beautiful pass. Timed-entry reservations end for 2026 on October 12, and on October 18 for Bear Lake Road ([NPS](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/timed-entry-permit-system.htm)). Trail Ridge Road closes from about mid-October to late May, while Bear Lake Road stays open all year ([park roads](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm)).

Two short walks suit small legs and wheels. The loop around [Sprague Lake](/places/sprague-lake-trailhead/) on Bear Lake Road is wheelchair and stroller accessible, with benches, lookouts, 27 picnic tables and a vault toilet open all year; the park gives it as [0.8 mile](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/exploring-the-bear-lake-road-corridor.htm) on one page and [0.7 mile](https://www.nps.gov/places/sprague-lake.htm) on another. The [Lily Lake](/places/lily-lake-trailhead/) trail off Highway 7, which the park lists as [accessible](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/lily-lake-trail-wedding-site.htm), has benches, a viewing dock, two bridges and a boardwalk through a wetland, with vault toilets at the lot.

## Elk, and the rule of thumb

Fall is the rut, and the [Park Service's elk rules](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/nature/elk.htm) are worth teaching children before you go. Keep at least 75 feet, about two bus lengths, from elk and bighorn sheep. The test is the "rule of thumb": hold out your arm with your thumb up, close one eye, and if your thumb does not hide the animal, step back until it does. Feeding or harassing wildlife is illegal. During the rut, off-trail walking in Horseshoe Park, Moraine Park, Upper Beaver Meadows, Harbison Meadow and Holzwarth Meadow is closed from 5 pm to 10 am.

For young anglers, [anyone 16 or older needs a Colorado fishing license](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/fishing.htm) in the park, and children 12 and under may use worms or preserved fish eggs in park waters that are open to fishing and not catch-and-release.

## The community center and its pools

The Estes Valley Recreation and Park District's [Estes Valley Community Center](https://evrpd.com/community-center/), at 660 Community Drive (970-586-8191), has indoor pools, a gymnasium and a track. Its own page gives two sets of hours, Monday to Friday 6 am to 8 pm in both, with Saturday 9 am to 6 pm and Sunday noon to 5 pm in one and both weekend days 9 to 6 in the other, so call before a Sunday morning swim. Children 3 and under need an adult in the water within arm's reach, and children must be 9 to be unsupervised in the pools, lobby, track and gym.

The [aquatics center](https://evrpd.com/aquatics/) has a lap pool, a dive well, a leisure pool with a lazy river and a hot tub. Open swim in the leisure pool is 11 am to 1 pm on weekdays, 5 to 6:30 pm on Monday and Wednesday, 4 to 6:30 pm on Friday, 1 to 5 pm on Saturday and 10 am to 5 pm on Sunday; the dive well has open swim 11 to 1 on weekdays and 10 to 5 on Sunday. The lap pool closes for renovation from October 12 to November 6. The district also runs Red Cross swim lessons and a rec swim team.

A [day visit](https://evrpd.com/community-center-memberships/) is $6 for residents aged 3 to 17 and $8 for adults; for non-residents, $11 and $15. A week's family membership, for up to two adults and three children under 18 at one address, is $65 for residents and $115 for others.

For ages 4 to 6, [Cubz Den](https://evrpd.com/cubz-den/) is the district's child-watch program, 3 to 5:30 pm in six-week sessions on Tuesday and Thursday or Monday, Wednesday and Friday, with an indoor playground, crafts, music and dancing. The district's [youth sports](https://evrpd.com/youth-sports-and-activities/) are open to non-members through a CivicRec account: this fall, soccer at Stanley Park, Jr. Nuggets basketball from October 19, gymnastics for ages 5 to 10, and a Bobcat Camp day for kindergarten to fifth grade on Monday, October 19, from 7:45 am to 3:45 pm.

## Stanley Park and the Lake Estes Trail

[Stanley Park](https://evrpd.com/stanley-park/), nearly 100 acres beside Lake Estes, has a playground, always open, with slides, forts, rope walls, a zip line and swings, plus the new Lawson Skatepark, a dirt mountain bike track for all ages and abilities, ballfields and courts. Its lots are closed for paving until about late October, the district says; park in the asphalt lot east of the tennis courts, north of Manford Avenue, or at Cherokee Draw and walk in through the Highway 36 underpass ([notice](https://evrpd.com/)).

The [Lake Estes Trail](https://evrpd.com/parks-and-trails/) is a paved, easy 4-mile loop around the lake, reached from the Visitor Center, the parking garage and the Fisherman's Nook lot, or from paid lots at Cherokee Draw, Wapiti Meadows and the marina. Restrooms and drinking fountains are at Cherokee Draw, and dogs on leash are welcome.

## Lake Estes Marina and Hermit Park

The [marina](https://evrpd.com/marina/) at 1770 Big Thompson Avenue rents paddle boats ($40 an hour for four), canoes ($30), kayaks ($30 to $35), pedal carts ($35 to $45) and bikes ($15 an hour). Everyone aboard wears a life jacket, and a boat's driver must be 16. Through October 31, boat rentals run 9 am to 3 pm on weekdays except Tuesday, when there are none, and 8 am to 4 pm on weekends, weather permitting (970-586-2011). The page lists two different sets of store hours, so call ahead. After October 31 the published season resumes with weekends from April 1.

Larimer County's [Hermit Park Open Space](https://www.larimer.gov/naturalresources/parks/hermit-park), about 2 miles southeast of town off US 36, is 1,362 acres of ponderosa pine and wet meadow with cabins, tent and RV camping, a group pavilion and trails for hiking, riding and mountain biking. It needs an entrance permit and is open from March to mid-December.

## Up Prospect Mountain

The [Estes Park Aerial Tramway](/places/estes-park-aerial-tramway/) runs daily through November 1, from 9 am to 6 pm from October 12 with the last car up at 5. Tickets are $24.95 for ages 6 to 17, free for 5 and under and $39.95 for adults, and are sold by arrival time, online or at the desk; the cars are enclosed and wheelchair and walker friendly, and from the summit a 0.75-mile hike reaches the West Summit at 8,868 feet ([tram FAQ](https://estestram.com/faq)).

## The library's programs

The [Estes Valley Library](/places/estes-valley-library/) holds its storytimes at the community center's Cubz Den. On its calendar, [Baby Storytime](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/event/17607727) (books, songs and rhymes for infants) is at 10 am on Thursdays, and [Storytime](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/event/17607730) for children 6 and under with a grown-up is at 10:30, with Saturday sessions at 10:45 and 11:15; from late November the [calendar](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/ical_subscribe.php?src=p&cid=19315) lists them in the library's Hondius Community Room.

Older children register for most things. [Friday Kids Club](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/event/17016909), for ages 6 to 12, makes crafts at the YMCA of the Rockies' Mootz Craft and Design Center; [Teen Maker Mornings](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/event/17391872), for 10 to 17, use the Makerspace's laser cutter, button maker and soldering irons; and [Tween Dragon Slayers](https://calendar.estesvalleylibrary.org/event/15412961), Dungeons & Dragons for 10 to 13 at the Estes Park Memorial Observatory, is full; the teen librarian is taking names by email for a possible second group. The library's one-off workshops for children and teens are on the [events page](/events/), among them [Halloween trick-or-treating](/events/halloween-trick-or-treat-2026-10-31/) in its lobby from 5 to 8 pm on October 31.

## Closed until next summer, and the holidays

The [MacGregor Ranch](https://www.macgregorranch.org/) has closed tours of its family homestead museum until next summer, as have the park's Junior Ranger Headquarters and the Moraine Park Discovery Center. In November the Town's holiday events begin downtown: the free [Tree Lighting](https://eventsinestes.com/tree-lighting/) in Bond Park on Saturday, November 21, from 3:30 to 6, with candy canes, ornament-making, Santa and Mrs. Claus, carriage rides and a train ride, the tree lit at 5:30; and the [Catch the Glow Parade](https://eventsinestes.com/catch-the-glow-parade/) down Elkhorn Avenue at 5:30 pm on November 27, the day after Thanksgiving.

The Town of Estes Park's own website, which covers its museum and visitor center, and the YMCA of the Rockies' site refused automated reads, so they are not covered here.
