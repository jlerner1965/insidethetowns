Estes Park's Catch the Glow Parade comes down Elkhorn Avenue on Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving, starting at 5:30 pm. It is free to watch.

## What is in it

The Town lists more than 40 entries: lighted floats built by the Town and by local nonprofits, marching bands, fire trucks and specialty vehicles. It says up to 30,000 people line Elkhorn Avenue, and that Santa is downtown in the afternoon before the parade; its page gives no time or place for him.

## Where to watch

The parade runs along Elkhorn Avenue through downtown. Visit Estes Park's guide to the night suggests standing near Bond Park or the Visitor Center, or at the end of the route for more room, and picking Town Hall as a meeting spot if your group gets separated. It advises layers, blankets and folding chairs. By November 12 the Town had not published this year's route map, start and end points, or an end time.

## Parking and road closures

Parking in the Town's downtown lots is free in the off-season, late October through late May. Visit Estes Park says to arrive early, because parking goes fast, especially around the Visitor Center garage. The Town had not posted this year's road closures by November 12; it publishes them as news releases at estespark.colorado.gov/news.

## Who runs it

The Town of Estes Park, through its events office at the Estes Park Events Complex, 970-577-3902. Floats are entered by nonprofits only, and applications closed on November 1.
