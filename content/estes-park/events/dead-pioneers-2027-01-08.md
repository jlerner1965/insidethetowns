The Denver punk band Dead Pioneers plays a two-night residency at the Stanley, presented by 102.3, with Speedy Ortiz and Cheap Perfume playing sets both nights. Standing general admission, $50 a night, 21 and over. The show time had not been posted on November 1; check the Stanley's page.

The after-parties, film screenings, poetry and Gregg Deal's performance piece The Punk Pan-Indian Romantic Comedy are for overnight hotel guests only. The nights are listed one by one: Friday, January 8 (this listing) and [Saturday, January 9](/events/dead-pioneers-2027-01-09/).
