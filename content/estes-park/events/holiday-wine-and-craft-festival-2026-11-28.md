The seventh Estes Park Holiday Wine Festival fills the Events Complex on Rooftop Way on Saturday, November 28, from 11 am to 5 pm, the day after the Catch the Glow parade. Admission is $40 with wine tasting, $5 for anyone 6 and over who is not drinking, and free under 6. The festival is presented by Estes Park Wine Festival, 970-218-4545.
