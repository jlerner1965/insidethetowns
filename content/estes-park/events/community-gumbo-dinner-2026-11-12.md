The seventh annual community gumbo night, a "Friendsgiving" supper open to everyone: Louisiana-style gumbo with bread and butter, cooked by Carl Robicheaux of Elkhorn Law and volunteers, served in the Tavern at the Legion or packed to go, with a cash bar. Gumbo is limited, so pre-order through the chamber's page; online orders close at 3 pm on the day.
