DeVotchKa's annual costume ball in the Stanley's Concert Hall, two nights over the Halloween weekend, billed this year as a celebration of the music of Little Miss Sunshine. The Slavic Sisters perform aerial acts in the Pavilion beforehand, and a DJ plays an after-party in the Billiards Room at 11:30 pm. Standing room only, 21 and over.
