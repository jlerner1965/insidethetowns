A two-hour songwriting workshop for adults, beginners included, led by the Lyons-based songwriter Nadine Sekerez in a classroom at the Estes Valley Community Center: finding song ideas, structure and starting points, then writing a song together as a group. Bring an instrument if you play one. Registration is required and seats are limited.
