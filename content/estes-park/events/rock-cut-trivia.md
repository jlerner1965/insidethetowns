Rock Cut Brewing runs trivia every Tuesday from 7 to 9 pm, with original questions by its trivia host Ashley: a picture round, an audio round, pop culture, dealer's choice and a category chosen by the previous week's winner. The brewery's calendar lists the series through late May 2027. No charge was posted.
