Frozen Dead Guy Days returns to the Estes Park Events Complex Friday to Sunday, April 9 to 11, 2027, with coffin races, costumes and bands. The 2027 lineup has not been announced; 2026 brought Pigeons Playing Ping Pong, Andy Frasco & The U.N. and Gasoline Lollipops. The guest of honor is Grandpa Bredo Morstøl, born in Norway in 1900, on ice since 1989, kept in Colorado since 1993 and at home in the Stanley Hotel's ice house since August 2023. The festival is all ages; in 2026 kids under 2 got in free and ages 2 to 12 needed a child ticket. Tickets are sold through Eventim, linked from the festival's own site, and 2027 prices will be announced at on-sale.
