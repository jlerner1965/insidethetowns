The library's annual jigsaw swap in the Atrium: bring your old puzzles and trade them for gently used ones, with no puzzles that have missing pieces. Without one to swap, you can take up to three free puzzles, plus any you trade. Drop in any time from 11:30 am to 5 pm, and bring a tote bag.
