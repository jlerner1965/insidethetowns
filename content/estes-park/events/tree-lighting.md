The Town of Estes Park's 16th annual Lighting of the Trees is on Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 3:30 to 6 pm in Bond Park, with the trees lit at 5:30 pm. It is free.

## Schedule

The afternoon opens at 3:30 pm in Bond Park, on Elkhorn Avenue next to Town Hall. Before the lighting there are carolers and local performers, Santa and Mrs. Claus, photos with holiday characters, carriage rides, an electric train for all ages, hot chocolate, candy canes and take-home ornament craft kits for kids. Local shop vendors will be there too.

The Estes Park 8th Grade Choir sings in the run-up to the lighting, and Ballet Renaissance's youth dancers perform excerpts from The Nutcracker, weather permitting. The giant spruce in Bond Park is lit at 5:30 pm, and the event ends at 6.

## New in 2026

The Town says the park's lighting has been redesigned this year with Visit Estes Park, on an animal theme that runs through Bond Park. Visit Estes Park says the display then stays on every evening from 5 to 9 pm, free.

## Parking

Parking in the Town's downtown lots is free in the off-season, late October through late May; overnight parking needs a permit all year. The Town's release mentions no road closures for the ceremony.

## Who runs it

The Town's Events Department. Questions go to Kayleigh Schissel at kschissel@estes.org. Police Department Auxiliary members will take monetary donations at the event for Blue Santa, which delivers to disabled, shut-in and elderly residents in mid-December.
