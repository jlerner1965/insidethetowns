A new family- and stroller-friendly 5K run or walk that goes ahead of the Catch the Glow Parade on its route, starting at 600 W. Elkhorn Avenue and finishing at the Estes Park Event Center, where runners can watch the floats pass. Entry includes a beanie, wearable lights and food after the race; it raises money for the Estes Chamber of Commerce's Economic & Workforce Development Council. The chamber suggests parking at the Event Center and taking the free shuttle to the start.
