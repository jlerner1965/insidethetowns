The Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce's listing for Theo's Toys, read 10 October 2026 (last changed in July 2026), describes a mom-and-pop toy store named after the owners' son, next to the Penny Arcade at 934 Manitou Avenue, Unit 103, selling new high-tech toys, old classics and brands such as Melissa & Doug, and calls it Manitou's only toy store.
