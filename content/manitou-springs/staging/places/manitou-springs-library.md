The Manitou Springs Library is a branch of the Pikes Peak Library District. It is open 10 am to 6 pm Monday through Thursday and 9 am to 5 pm Friday and Saturday, and closed Sunday. The district's programs (All Pikes Peak Reads, author events, the Summer Adventure, the Winter Adult Reading Program) are listed on its events pages, filterable by branch.
