The City of Manitou Springs' Parks & Recreation page, read 10 October 2026, says the Manitou Incline is owned and managed by the City of Colorado Springs and sends visitors to the Colorado Springs Incline page for details and reservations. That page's site asks automated readers to stay out, so nothing on it was read.

Two operators nearby say how they handle Incline parking: the Cog Railway's FAQ says its lot is not for Incline hikers, who should use the Iron Springs Chateau lot or the Manitou Springs public lots, and the Iron Springs Chateau sells daytime hiker parking at $15 a car, cash only, valid four hours, with limited reservations May to August (both read 10 October 2026).

From the handoff packet, unconfirmed: the Incline gains about 2,000 feet in less than a mile, with the descent by Barr Trail; reservations and a waiver are required through the City of Colorado Springs; no pets are allowed on it; and its base is beside the Cog Railway at the upper end of Ruxton Avenue.
