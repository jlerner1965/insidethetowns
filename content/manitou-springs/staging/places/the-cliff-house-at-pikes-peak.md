The Cliff House at Pikes Peak began as a stagecoach stop and became a mineral-springs resort; it has 54 guest rooms and suites decorated in the style of the late 1800s, a dining room, the Red Mountain Bar & Grill, a wine cellar of 800 bottles with sommeliers on staff, a veranda facing Pikes Peak, and wedding and meeting spaces. The hotel describes itself as AAA Four Diamond. Its toll-free line is 888-212-7000.
