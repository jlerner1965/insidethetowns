The Manitou Cliff Dwellings let visitors walk inside and touch the dwellings, with two museums of cultural displays overlooking Manitou Springs. The site is open year-round, weather permitting: 9 am to 5 pm in April through October, 9 am to 4:30 pm in March, 9 am to 4 pm in November and 10 am to 4 pm in December, January and February, and the park stays open 30 minutes after the last admission. It closes on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Admission is $12.75 plus tax for adults 12 and over, $8 for children 4 to 11, $10 for seniors 60 and over and free for children 3 and under; groups of ten or more pay less.
