The City of Manitou Springs' Parks & Trails page, read 10 October 2026, lists the Red Mountain Trail among its four trails (with the Creek Walk, Intemann and Iron Mountain trails) and the Red Mountain Open Space among its three open spaces, and links an interactive hiking trails map app. Its rules for all City trails: no motorized vehicles on trails, no smoking on trails or in City parks, stay on designated trails, and bring a leash and bags for a pet. The City's Adopt-a-Park page lists the trail in its 2026 program.

From the handoff packet, unconfirmed: an out-and-back of about 2.5 miles with a steady climb; the City's trail map shows the access point, and dog rules are posted.
