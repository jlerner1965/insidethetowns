The Manitou Springs Penny Arcade fills eight buildings downtown with more than 400 coin-operated video, pinball and mechanical machines, some more than a century old, plus pool tables, kiddie rides, photo booths, Skee-Ball and a 12-horse race Derby game made in England. The Derby is 49 years old and often under maintenance; during fall hours the arcade aims to run it daily from noon to 6 pm, and asks players to call or text ahead. The snack bar has reopened with paninis, smoothies, fresh lemonade and limeade, and British 99 ice cream. The single ATM dispenses $10 bills for a fee of $3 or more; surprise bags of swag, a toy, candy, coupons and quarters cost $13 (with $10 in quarters) or $23 (with $20).

It is open every day except Christmas. Fall hours, 8 September to 5 November, are Monday to Thursday 11 am to 8 pm and Friday to Sunday 11 am to 9 pm; winter and spring hours, 6 November to 20 May, are Monday to Thursday 11 am to 7 pm and Friday to Sunday 11 am to 8 pm; summer hours, 20 May to 6 September, are 10 am to 10 pm daily. On "Game Changer" Mondays, 4 to 7 pm, a $25 bag ($15 in quarters, candy, swag and snack-bar coupons) benefits a local nonprofit: The Commons at the STACC on 12 October, the Manitou Springs Community Foundation on 19 October, CRANE Creative Alliance on 26 October and Silver Key Senior Services on 2 November. The arcade's news page lists 2025 coverage of its sale to a new owner after 92 years.

Phone, hours and the Game Changer dates were read on the arcade's home page and Game Changer page on 10 October 2026; the general manager takes texts only at 719-659-5371. Neither these nor the arcade's other pages give a street address; 930 Manitou Ave is the Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce's listing (and the handoff packet's), so the listing waits for the arcade to confirm it.
