The Broadmoor Manitou and Pikes Peak Cog Railway leaves from its depot at 515 Ruxton Avenue for the summit of Pikes Peak. Its site describes year-round operation with many regular daily departures and a round trip of about three hours (the home page says three and a half), and asks riders to arrive 30 minutes before departure and allow about four hours in all. Departure dates and times, and fares, are on its Purchase Tickets and Rates pages rather than on the pages read here, so there is no hours line on this listing.

Address, phone, season and trip length from the railway's [home page](https://www.cograilway.com/) and [schedule page](https://www.cograilway.com/ride/schedule/), read 10 October 2026. Apart from a 2026 copyright line, neither page carries a dated notice.
