The Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce's listing (read 10 October 2026, last changed in April 2025) calls Patsy's "the only permanent open-air concession stand in Colorado", famous since 1903 for gourmet popcorn and salt-water taffy, and lists homemade ice cream and soft serve, smoothies, cotton candy, pretzels, Nathan's hot dogs, chili cheese fries, funnel cakes, fresh-squeezed lemonade and slushies, with the Penny Arcade directly behind it. The chamber's springs page says Navajo Spring's font is on the back outside wall of the store, once the bottling works' sampling room.

Patsy's Candies' own site, read the same day, says Patsy Mehaney settled in Manitou Springs in 1903 and started the company, now run by the fourth generation of the Niswonger family from its factory at 1540 S 21st St in Colorado Springs, and invites visitors to "our famous historical landmark in Manitou Springs". It gives no hours, address or phone for the Manitou stand. The handoff packet (unconfirmed) says service and offerings are seasonal.
