Choose the plan first, then the parking: in Manitou Springs each one starts somewhere different. The Cog Railway needs a ticket booked ahead, and its $20 lot across from the depot opens 30 minutes before your train. The Incline needs a reservation from the City of Colorado Springs and has no parking at its base. A downtown family visit needs no booking at all.

The free Route 33 shuttle, now called The ZEB Manitou Ride, links all three: daily from 6 am to 6 pm, every 30 minutes, until March 27, 2027, according to Mountain Metro's [route card](https://manitousprings.org/manitou-free-shuttle/). In snow or ice it may not be able to serve Ruxton Avenue, where the Cog and the Incline are.

## The three plans

| Plan | Book ahead | Where to park | Or ride |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cog Railway | Yes: tickets online for a set departure; walk-ups only if seats are left | The Cog lot across from the depot, 515 Ruxton Avenue: $20, cards only, from 30 minutes before your train | Route 33 to The Incline/Cog Railway |
| Manitou Incline | Yes: a reservation from the City of Colorado Springs for every climb | Iron Springs Chateau, 444 Ruxton Avenue: $15 a car for 4 hours, cash only, first come. Never the Cog lot | Route 33 to The Incline/Cog Railway |
| Downtown | No | A City lot, or the Metro District's Wichita lot, 708 Manitou Avenue: $3 an hour, $20 for 24 hours | On foot, or Route 33 along Manitou and Ruxton avenues |

## Plan 1: the Cog Railway

Book online; the railway says [walk-up tickets](https://www.cograilway.com/ride/rates/) at the depot are subject to availability. Without a ticket there is no parking at the depot, and the Cog asks you [not to drive up Ruxton Avenue](https://www.cograilway.com/the-new-experience/parking/) hoping to buy one.

- **Parking:** the Cog's lot across the street, $20, opens 30 minutes before your departure; the kiosks take cards, no cash. It is first come, first served and not guaranteed, takes no buses, RVs or trailers, and is not for hiking, even with a train ticket.
- **Cog and town in one day:** the Cog suggests a City lot (Hiawatha Gardens, the Dillon Mobility Hub, Wichita, Cañon or Smischny), which it says generally costs $10 to $20, and Route 33 up Ruxton.
- **Arriving:** no more than 30 minutes before departure; boarding starts 20 minutes before, and everyone must be seated [10 minutes before](https://www.cograilway.com/ticket-purchase-terms/).
- **The trip:** about three hours round trip, the railway's [FAQ](https://www.cograilway.com/about-us/faq/) says, 1 hour 10 minutes up and about 40 minutes at the top. It runs all year, with fewer departures in fall and winter, and severe weather or deep snow can stop a train short of the summit.
- **At the top:** the Pikes Peak Summit Visitor Center, run by the City of Colorado Springs, with food, [restrooms and water](https://www.cograilway.com/the-new-experience/pikes-peak-visitor-center/), cashless. The only restrooms on the trip are there and at the depot. The summit is at 14,115 feet and about 30 degrees colder than the depot.
- **Children:** every passenger needs a ticket; the railway does not recommend the ride for babies under 6 months, and strollers and baby carriers are not allowed on the train. No pets.

## Plan 2: the Manitou Incline

The Incline is run by the City of Colorado Springs, and its [Incline page](https://coloradosprings.gov/manitouincline) is where you book. On November 17, 2026, it called for a free reservation and a newly signed waiver for every climb, for parties of up to eight, and gave the hours as 6 am–3 pm from November through April and 6 am–6 pm from May through October. Pets are not allowed, carried ones included.

- **Parking:** not in the Cog's lot, which [the Cog says](https://www.cograilway.com/about-us/faq/) is not for Incline climbers. [Iron Springs Chateau](https://ironspringschateau.com/parking.home.html), 444 Ruxton Avenue, sells hiker parking at $15 a car for 4 hours, cash only, first come, first served.
- **Or the shuttle:** Route 33's top stop is The Incline/Cog Railway, unless snow or ice stops it reaching Ruxton Avenue.
- **The climb:** about a mile up with about 2,000 feet of climbing, the [Manitou Springs Chamber](https://manitousprings.org/where-to-play/manitou-incline/) says. Going down the steps is not allowed: come down by Barr Trail.

## Plan 3: a downtown family visit

Nothing here needs booking. The [Metropolitan District's](https://www.manitouspringsmd.live/parking) Wichita lot, 708 Manitou Avenue, and its Smischny lot charge $3 an hour, rising to $20 for 24 hours.

- **The springs:** the [Mineral Springs Foundation](https://mineralspringsfoundation.com/) has restored or developed eight of the town's mineral springs with the City and landowners, and its brochure has a map of them.
- **Soda Springs Park, 1016 Manitou Avenue:** the City's 1.9-acre park on Fountain Creek, where the playground was rebuilt with inclusive equipment in 2025.
- **Memorial Park, 502 Manitou Avenue:** the City park in the same block as the Manitou Art Center and the Heritage Center.
- **Miramont Castle Museum:** self-guided tours of 45 minutes to an hour; [open](https://www.miramontcastle.org/castle-hours) Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am–3:30 pm, closing at 4. Admission is [$15 for adults](https://www.miramontcastle.org/), $13 for seniors 60 and over, $10 for ages 4 to 12, and free for 3 and under. Route 33's Twin Spring/Miramont Castle stop is nearby.
- **The Penny Arcade:** more than 400 coin-operated video, pinball and mechanical games, open every day; in winter, [11 am–7 pm](https://manitouspringsarcade.com/) Monday to Thursday and 11 am–8 pm Friday to Sunday. Its one ATM gives $10 bills, for a fee.

## If the first choice falls through

- **The Cog is sold out:** do not drive up Ruxton Avenue for a walk-up seat. Switch to the downtown plan, which needs no booking; the Incline needs its own reservation.
- **It snows:** Route 33 may skip Ruxton Avenue. The Cog gives no weather refund once a train reaches the summit, at least 25 percent back if it cannot, and 50 percent if it cannot reach Mountain View Siding.
- **Plans change:** Cog tickets can be changed or refunded free more than 7 days ahead; within 7 days, a reschedule or store credit costs $20 for the booking.
- **Thanksgiving:** Miramont Castle is closed on Thanksgiving Day.
