SALUS has made bath and body care in Fort Collins since 2004: bath bombs, shower bombs, Tub Taffy, bar and liquid soaps, scrubs, lotions and creams, body oils, lip care and deodorant. On its customizable products the customer picks the product and the scent, or a blend of scents, and the company mixes, pours and finishes it fresh. It has two stores, one at 240 Walnut Street in Fort Collins and this one in Suite 105 at 906 Manitou Avenue, open 10:30 am to 6 pm every day.
