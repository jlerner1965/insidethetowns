The Broadmoor Manitou and Pikes Peak Cog Railway leaves from its depot at 515 Ruxton Avenue for the 9-mile ride to the summit of Pikes Peak at 14,115 feet. Its FAQ says the climb takes 1 hour and 10 minutes, with about 40 minutes at the top, and the round trip about 3 hours (the home page says 3.5); it asks riders to arrive 30 minutes before departure and to allow about four hours in all. The train runs all year, with tickets released a few months ahead. It does not stop on the way up, is heated but not air-conditioned, and is not recommended for infants under six months or anyone with a history of cardiac or respiratory problems.

Online reserved seats start at $67 for adults and $57 for children, and the Engineer's View front-row seats at $96 and $86; walk-up or phone tickets start at $86 and $76, plus taxes and fees. Changes made more than seven days ahead are free; within seven days a reservation can be rescheduled or turned into store credit for a $20 fee. The railway's lot and on-street parking cost $20, there is no free parking in Manitou Springs, and the railway suggests the City's lots and the free Route 33 shuttle; its lot is not for Manitou Incline hikers.

Address, phone, season and trip length from the railway's [home page](https://www.cograilway.com/) and [schedule page](https://www.cograilway.com/ride/schedule/), fares from its rates page and the rest from its FAQ, read 10 October 2026; the address and phone were read again on its [ticket page](https://www.cograilway.com/purchase-tickets/) on 14 October 2026. Departure times vary by date and sit in the booking calendar on that page, which is why no timetable is given here; apart from a 2026 copyright line, none of the pages carries a dated notice.
