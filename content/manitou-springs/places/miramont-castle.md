Miramont Castle is owned and operated by the Manitou Springs Historical Society. The castle is open 10 am to 3:30 pm Tuesday through Sunday, and 9 am to 4:30 pm seven days a week from Memorial Day to Labor Day. Admission is $15 for adults 13 to 59, $13 for seniors 60 and over, $10 for children 4 to 12 and free for children 3 and under, with $2 off for military, veterans and first responders with ID. The Queen's Parlour Tearoom seats Tuesday through Sunday at 11 am and 1 pm only and is closed Mondays.

The society's events page is dated through 2027: a Halloween High Tea in late October 2026, a Victorian Christmas High Tea over Thanksgiving weekend, Poe Night in January 2027 and a Mother's Day tea in May, at $65 a ticket with reservations through OpenTable, plus the opening of a postage-stamp exhibit on 16 and 17 October 2026.
