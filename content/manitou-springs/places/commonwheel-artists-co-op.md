Commonwheel Artists Co-op is a member-run gallery and shop at 102 Cañon Avenue, open daily 10 am to 6 pm. Its gallery show changes monthly: "KOSAI: The Hue of Light" runs 2 October to 2 November 2026, with the next show from 6 November to 27 December. The co-op also runs a Holiday Market, a Summer Market and the Commonwheel Arts Festival on Labor Day weekend, with 2026 festival pages on its site, and is marking its 50th anniversary.

Address and phone from the co-op's [contact page](https://www.commonwheel.com/contact-us.html); hours and the show dates from its [home page](https://www.commonwheel.com/), both read 10 October 2026.
