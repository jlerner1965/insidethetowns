The Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce, Visitors Bureau & Office of Economic Development has its office at 354 Manitou Avenue, where its staff and volunteers help visitors with Manitou Springs and the Pikes Peak region. For the town's mineral springs it gives out a walking-tour map and a chart of each spring's mineral content, and sells collapsible stainless-steel cups, $3 each or two for $5, cash only, to support the Mineral Springs Foundation. The bureau also mails a printed visitor guide free on request. The chamber started the Emma Crawford Coffin Races in 1995; the 2026 race is on Saturday, October 24.

Address and phone were read on the chamber's contact page, the springs map and cups on its Mineral Spring Water page, and the 2026 race date on its coffin races page, all on 10 October 2026. None of the pages read gives the office's opening hours, so call before making a special trip.
