The Manitou Outpost at 807 Manitou Avenue calls itself the largest gift store on Manitou Avenue. It sells Colorado souvenirs, T-shirts, hoodies and hats, contemporary Native American jewelry in turquoise, silver and stone, magnets, ornaments, keychains and postcards, pocketknives and trail gear, pottery and Colorado-themed decor, local art, crystals and collectibles, with fudge made in the store daily (free samples) and ice cream cones. The store is pet friendly. It belongs to the Colorado Retail Collection, which it describes as eight stores in Colorado Springs and Manitou Springs, rooted in Colorado Springs since 1929.

Address, phone and hours were read on the store's home page on 10 October 2026. The page carries no date; the Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce's listing for the store, with the same address and phone, is the second source that it is operating.
