The Manitou Incline is a demanding stair climb above Ruxton Avenue, managed by the City of Colorado Springs. The City requires a free reservation and a new signed waiver for each visit, for parties of up to eight; there is no access without a reservation. Hours are 6 am–6 pm from May 1 through October 31 and 6 am–3 pm from November 1 through April 30, and use outside those hours is prohibited. Pets are banned, including animals carried in backpacks, and the City asks climbers to take their rubbish back out.

Plan the descent and parking before starting; the City’s page has live parking information and trail guidance. Nearby operator lots have their own restrictions, and a Cog Railway ticket does not make its lot Incline parking.

Reservations, waiver, group limit, seasonal hours and the pet rule were read on the [City’s Incline page](https://coloradosprings.gov/manitouincline) on 11 October 2026; [Visit Colorado Springs](https://www.visitcos.com/things-to-do/outdoors/manitou-incline-near-colorado-springs-colorado/) gives the same reservation, hours and pet rule, and the [Cog Railway’s FAQ](https://www.cograilway.com/about-us/faq/) says its lot is not for Incline hikers.
