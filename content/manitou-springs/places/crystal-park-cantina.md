Crystal Park Cantina, established in 2011, is a family-owned restaurant at 178 Crystal Park Road cooking what it calls Grandma Anne's home-style recipes from Mexican cuisine. Its margaritas use only 100% agave tequilas and fresh juices, and it always has local craft beers and wines. The cantina says reservations are never needed.

The top floor is a semi-private room for parties of 18 to 35, booked through the site, and a party-to-go service sends margaritas and food out in large servings for pickup. Online ordering is linked from the site.

Address, phone and hours were read on the cantina's home page on 10 October 2026. The page shows nothing dated; the Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce's listing, with the same address, phone and Tuesday-to-Sunday hours, is the second source that it is operating.
