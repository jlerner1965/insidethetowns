Red Dog Coffee is a family-owned cafe at 739 Manitou Avenue serving barista-made espresso drinks, breakfast and lunch. Its beans come from Colorado Coffee Merchants, which air-roasts them; sandwiches are made while you wait with Boar's Head meats and cheeses; and muffins, tarts, cookies, brownies and coffee cake are baked in-house each day, with some gluten-friendly items. The site says the cafe was started by the people behind Pikes Peak Chocolate and Ice Cream at 805 Manitou Avenue, and its home page was advertising October drink specials when read.

Address, phone and hours were read on the cafe's Hours + Contact page, and the coffee, food and bakery details on its other pages, on 10 October 2026. The site carries no full date; the Manitou Springs Chamber of Commerce lists Red Dog Coffee at the same address and phone, the second source that it is operating.
