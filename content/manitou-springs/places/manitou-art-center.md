The Manitou Art Center is a nonprofit with galleries, classes, makerspace access and memberships at 513 and 515 Manitou Avenue. Gallery hours are 10 am to 6 pm Wednesday and Thursday and 10 am to 5 pm Friday and Saturday; it is closed Sunday through Tuesday. On its annual holiday schedule it closes on Thanksgiving, Christmas, December 26 and New Year's Day and closes at 2 pm on December 24 and 31. Its calendar lists a Dancing for the Arts fundraiser in the Intemann Gallery on 17 October 2026.
