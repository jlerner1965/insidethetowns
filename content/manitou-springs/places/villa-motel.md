Villa Motel on Manitou Avenue has rooms and cottages. Most rooms have a hairdryer, coffee maker and microwave-refrigerator combination, the operator says; the cottages do not have the same set. Check-in starts at 3 pm and check-out is 11 am. There is no 24-hour desk, so arrivals after 9 pm must be arranged with the front desk when booking. The motel has an on-site guest laundry and a heated outdoor pool, open from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend. Cancellation terms for direct bookings differ from those for reservations made through travel platforms.

The address and phone were read on the motel’s [contact page](https://villamotel.com/contact-us/), and arrival and amenity details on its [FAQ](https://villamotel.com/faq/) (updated 8 June 2026), on 11 October 2026.
