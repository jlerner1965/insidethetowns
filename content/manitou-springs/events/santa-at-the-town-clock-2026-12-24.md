Santa Claus visits the Wheeler Town Clock on Manitou Avenue on Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, the last stop in the Chamber's downtown holiday calendar. The Chamber's 2026 holiday page gives the date but no time; check it or call the Visitors Center on 719-685-5089 nearer the day.
