The AdAmAn Club has climbed Pikes Peak at the end of every year since 1922: about thirty members and guests leave the Barr Trail trailhead in Manitou on December 30, sleep at Barr Camp, flash mirrors from timberline on the 31st and fire fireworks from the 14,115-foot summit at the stroke of midnight on New Year's Eve. On a clear night the display is visible across the Front Range, and Manitou Springs, at the foot of the mountain, is as close as anyone gets. There is nothing to buy and no gathering place is designated; dress for the cold and check the weather.
