Watch parties of classic frights at the Heritage Museum on three October Sundays at 6 pm: "The Cat and the Canary" on October 11, "The Ghost Breakers" on October 18 and "Rebecca" on October 25. The museum's calendar gives 6 to 9 pm for the October 18 and 25 nights and a 6 pm start only for October 11; it lists no price.
