Watch parties of classic frights at the Heritage Museum on three October Sundays at 6 pm: "The Cat and the Canary" on October 11, "The Ghost Breakers" on October 18 and "Rebecca" on October 25. The museum's calendar gives 6 to 9 pm for the October 18 and 25 nights and a 6 pm start only for October 11; admission is free.

Read from the Heritage Center's [events calendar](https://manitouspringsheritagecenter.org/events/) on 10 October 2026.

The museum's individual event listings, read on 11 October 2026, give doors at 5:30 pm and special features at 5:45 pm before each 6 pm film.
