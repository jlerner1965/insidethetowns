The [Iron Springs Chateau](/places/iron-springs-chateau/)'s adult Halloween special: the Rocky Horror Picture Show screened with a live shadow cast of eight Chateau Players acting it out in front of the film. Six nights, Friday and Saturday, October 16 and 17 and 23 and 24, and Thursday and Friday, October 29 and 30. The Chateau bills it as an adult-themed show.

The Chateau's own pages give the six dates but no separate time for this run; the times here are its usual ones, doors at 7 pm and the show at 7:30, so check with the box office (719-685-5104) before you go. Its prices page warns that special-event prices may be higher than the melodrama's.

Dates read on the Chateau's [home page](https://ironspringschateau.com/index_files/1home.html) and its [Rocky Horror page](https://ironspringschateau.com/index_files/1auditions-Rocky.html), and the usual curtain on its [tickets page](https://ironspringschateau.com/index_files/1tickets.html), on 11 October 2026.
