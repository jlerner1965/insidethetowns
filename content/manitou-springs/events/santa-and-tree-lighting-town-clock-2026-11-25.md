Manitou Springs lights its Christmas tree at the Wheeler Town Clock on Wednesday, November 25, the night before Thanksgiving, from 5 to 7 pm, with Santa Claus on hand. It opens the downtown holiday season: the Chamber's letters-to-Santa box at the clock takes letters from the same day through December 20, the Elf on the Shelf hunt starts in the shops, and the Christmas Parade follows on December 6. The Chamber's page gives no price; the Visitors Center is on 719-685-5089.
