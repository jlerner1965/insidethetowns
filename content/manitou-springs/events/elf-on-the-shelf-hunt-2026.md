The Chamber's fourth Elf on the Shelf Hunt hides three elves in downtown shops and restaurants, moving them to three new places each week for four weeks from Wednesday, November 25, until noon on Sunday, December 20. Photograph all three and email the pictures to events@manitouchamber.com to enter that week's drawing for $25 in Manitou Money; winners are drawn on Wednesdays and announced on Fridays. Free; the shops set their own hours.
