Commonwheel's gallery show for November and December is its Holiday Market, a group show of handmade work by open call, in the co-op's gallery at 102 Cañon Avenue from Friday, November 6, through Sunday, December 27. The shop is open daily from 10 am to 6 pm and admission is free, as with any gallery visit. The co-op's pages give no opening reception; the shop is on 719-685-1008.
