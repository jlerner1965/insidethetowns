The [Manitou Art Center](/places/manitou-art-center/) opens its three galleries in the evening on the first Friday of every month, with live music and a signature cocktail: Friday, December 4, 5 to 8 pm. The galleries are free. The one before is [November 6](/events/first-friday-manitou-art-center-2026-11-06/).

Read on the center's [events page](https://www.manitouartcenter.org/events) on 11 October 2026.
