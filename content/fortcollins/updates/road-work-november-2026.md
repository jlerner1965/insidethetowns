The City lists two current impacts on major roads. On Harmony Road between McMurry and Timberline, lanes shift and close for the construction of the Power Trail Harmony Crossing, with the detour posted at Horsetooth and Trilby. On Horsetooth Road between Shields and Manhattan, lanes shift for a third-party natural gas line replacement, with varied detours. The City lists both as running until the projects are complete; smaller neighborhood work is on its FCTrip map.
