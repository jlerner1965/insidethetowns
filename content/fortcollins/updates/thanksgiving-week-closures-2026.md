Poudre School District's adopted [2026-27 calendar](https://www.psdschools.org/about/calendars-schedules) has no school for any grade from Monday, November 23 through Friday, November 27, and Colorado State's [fall recess](https://thehub.colostate.edu/registration-records/registration-dates/) runs November 21 to 29. The City [closes all its offices](https://www.fortcollins.gov/Event-Folder/2026-Events/2026-Q4/Thanksgiving) on Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27 and resumes regular hours on Monday, November 30. Its recreation facilities all close at 2 pm on Wednesday, November 25 and are closed on Thanksgiving Day; on Friday the [Edora Pool Ice Center](https://www.fortcollins.gov/Venues/Edora-Pool-Ice-Center-EPIC) and the Northside Aztlan Community Center keep normal hours, the Senior Center opens only for the [Holiday Artisan Market](/events/holiday-artisan-market-2026-11-27/), and all other recreation facilities are closed. [The Gardens on Spring Creek](/places/gardens-on-spring-creek/) are closed on Thanksgiving and the day after. [Poudre Libraries](https://poudrelibraries.org/locations/) close all three branches at 1 pm on Wednesday, November 25 and all day on Thanksgiving, and are open on Friday. [Transfort](https://ridetransfort.com/hours-and-locations/) lists Thanksgiving Day among the holidays with limited service hours and routes, and the MAX timetable marks November 26 as reduced service. Larimer County counts both days among its [2026 holidays](https://www.larimer.gov/hr/employee-relations/holiday-pay-schedules/holiday-schedule), and its vehicle licensing office at 200 West Oak Street keeps its weekday hours except on holidays.
