The City's curbside page gives the season for weekly yard trimmings collection as April 1 to November 30 and says there is no collection from November 30 to April 1. Until then the cart takes leaves, lawn clippings, garden waste, untreated wood, twigs and small branches under three inches across, put in loose with the lid fully closed; bags, paper ones included, food scraps and treated wood are not accepted, and a cart with an open lid or an overflow is not collected. Through the winter Republic Services still takes yard waste as a bulk pickup, at $4 for a 32-gallon bag, arranged at 970-416-2012; the City notes that bulk pickups go to the landfill and are not composted. The cart is part of the City's contract with Republic for single-family homes and buildings of seven or fewer units, and a household on any cart size but the smallest can decline yard trimmings service and save $5 a month.
