A comfort-food restaurant next to the Armstrong Hotel, open since 2004 and best known for burgers: The Classic, The Choice City and The Black & Blue at $20, the Truffle Grove at $25 and the Horsetooth at $26. Sandwiches such as the Butter Chicken Parm and the Tandoori Turkey Melt are $17 to $19, and the weekday lunch special (sandwich, chips and a fountain drink) is $18 from 11 to 3.

Lunch runs 11 to 4 and dinner 4 to 9 every day except Tuesday. Reservations are through OpenTable; takeout and DoorDash are available.
