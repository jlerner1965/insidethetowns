A comfort-food restaurant next to the Armstrong Hotel, open since 2004 and best known for burgers. At lunch The Classic, The Choice City and The Bacon & Blue start at $20 and the Truffle Grove and The Spice Route at $21; at dinner The Classic and The Choice City start at $23 and the other three are $25. Lunch sandwiches run $16 to $20, with the Butter Chicken Parm at $19 and the Tandoori Turkey Melt at $18.

Lunch runs 11 to 4 and dinner 4 to 9 every day except Tuesday. Reservations are through OpenTable; takeout and delivery are available.
