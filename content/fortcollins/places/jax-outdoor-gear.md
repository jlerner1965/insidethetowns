The outfitter Fort Collins sends everyone to before a trip up the Poudre: footwear and clothing, fishing and hunting, camping, cycling, winter sports, climbing and boating, from more than 140 brands, with military surplus alongside. There is an indoor archery range and an on-site coffee bar, and a pond outside to drink it by. Jax is a family-owned Northern Colorado company; it has supported Idea Wild for more than twenty years and its Coats and Boots program now reaches every school in the district. The Farm & Ranch store is further north at 1000 N US Hwy 287.
