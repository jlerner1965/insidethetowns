The brewpub on the square has been brewing on site since 1989, and it is the easiest place to take a mixed group: full dinners at the Brewpub at #5 Old Town Square, with burgers, wings, fish and chips, bangers and mash and the pub's own rotating ales, lagers and stouts. Billiards, at #7 Old Town Square, Suite 101, is open Wednesday and Thursday 5 to 10 pm, Friday 4 pm to midnight, Saturday 11 am to midnight and Sunday 11 am to 9 pm, with pool tables, board games and pizza. Both rooms use the same phone number.
