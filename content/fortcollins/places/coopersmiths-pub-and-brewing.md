The brewpub on the square has been brewing on site since 1989, and it is the easiest place to take a mixed group: full dinners on the Pubside at #5 Old Town Square, with burgers, steaks, pizza, wings and the pub's own rotating ales, lagers and stouts. The Poolside billiards room at #7 Old Town Square, Suite 101, opens Wednesday to Sunday from 3 pm (to 10 on Wednesday and Thursday, 11 on Friday and Saturday, 9 on Sunday) with pool tables, board games and casual food. Both rooms use the same phone number.
