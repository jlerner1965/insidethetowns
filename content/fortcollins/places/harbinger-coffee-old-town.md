Founded in 2012 by Jonathan Jarrow, Harbinger is the city's established specialty roaster, and this is its downtown room, across College Avenue from the Armstrong Hotel. Expertly roasted coffee and a welcoming atmosphere are the stated aims; the roastery itself is at the south shop at Harmony and Lady Moon. Open daily 7 am to 4 pm.
