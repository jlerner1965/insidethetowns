A working trial garden where growers' new annuals are planted out and judged against each other in the High Plains climate, which for a visitor means a city block of flower beds at their showiest from late May through October, beside the [University Center for the Arts](/places/university-center-for-the-arts/) on Remington Street. The perennial beds are there year round and cool-season plantings carry into early spring. Open daily to the public in daylight hours, free. The department of horticulture runs it; the gazebo can be reserved for weddings.
