The city's record shop, and a used bookstore, comic shop and curiosity cabinet at the same time: used and vintage books including antique volumes, vinyl, CDs, cassettes, DVDs and Blu-rays, action figures, sheet music and posters, plus stereo equipment and stereo repair. Everything is bought, sold and traded in person, with buying between 11 am and 6 pm; there is no online store, which is the point. Open seven days, 10 am to 7 pm.
