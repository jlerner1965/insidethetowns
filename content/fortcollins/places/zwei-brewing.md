The lager house of the south side, on South Mason: traditional German-style lagers and weissbiers made with care, with contemporary craft styles alongside for the hop drinker. An independent brewery (Brewers Association certified), with a taproom, a patio and food trucks. Open from 11 daily, to 9 on weekdays and Saturdays and 7 on Sundays.
