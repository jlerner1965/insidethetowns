The natural area at the Harmony Road interchange on the Fort Collins side of the river: about four miles of paved and natural-surface trail looping the reclaimed gravel ponds and Rigden Reservoir, fishing for bass, bluegill, perch and sunfish, more than 80 bird species including white pelicans, and the ruins of the Strauss Cabin, one of the area's earliest log houses. Leashed dogs, horses and non-motorized boats allowed; no e-bikes on the unpaved sections. Open 5 am to 11 pm.

It was listed on the Timnath guide until 10 October 2026, when the Census Bureau's address lookup placed 4109 East Horsetooth Road inside Fort Collins and the listing moved here; the old address redirects.
