Odell started in 1989 and moved to this East Lincoln Avenue site in 1994, where it now runs a 125-barrel brewhouse, three fermentation cellars and its own keg, can and bottle lines. The taproom is where locals bring visitors for the flagship lineup, 90 Shilling (amber, 5.3%), Lagerado (4.5%), the IPA (7%) and Hazer Tag (hazy IPA, 6%), on a big dog-friendly patio facing the foothills.

A food truck is on site daily; the rotation includes La Piadina, NoCo Pizza, Shell on Wheels, Scrooge Maki, Las Catrinas and others. Brewery tours run Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays at 1 and 3 pm.
