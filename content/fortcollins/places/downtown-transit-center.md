The northern hub of Transfort's routes, including MAX, on North Mason Street. A full-service customer service counter, printed maps and schedules, rider alerts, restrooms, lost and found, an indoor waiting area, bus shelters and bike racks, and Transfort's administrative offices. The counter is open Monday to Friday 8 to 5 and closed weekends and City holidays; routes and schedules are on ridetransfort.com. Riding Transfort is currently fare-free. The South Transit Center at 4915 Fossil Boulevard (Monday to Friday 9 to 3) and the CSU Transit Center at the north end of the Lory Student Center are the other two.
