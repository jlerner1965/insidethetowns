The east-west trail, 6.6 paved miles following Spring Creek from Spring Canyon Community Park under the foothills to the Poudre River east of Timberline Road, where it joins the Poudre River Trail. Restrooms and drinking water at Cottonwood Glen, Rolland Moore, Spring and Edora parks. Edora Park, where the creek winds through beside the trail, is a central place to start; the Spring Canyon end has the Inspiration Playground and the dog park. Open 5 am to 11 pm.
