Seven miles of natural-surface trail on the first ridge west of the city, with a flat 1.8-mile loop around Dixon Reservoir for an easy walk and the southern end of the Foothills Trail, 9.6 miles north through Maxwell to Reservoir Ridge, for something harder; the City warns that biking the Foothills Trail is for the experienced cyclist. Fishing and non-motorized boats on the reservoir, horses welcome with trailer parking, dogs on leash. Picnic tables and seasonal restrooms. Open daily 5 am to 11 pm, no fee.
