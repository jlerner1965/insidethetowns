Inspiration Playground, an adaptive playground and one of the most popular in the city, with a splash pad of touch-sensor spouts and fountains beside it (9 am to 7 pm, seven days a week). Around it: a dog park, a skate park and bike pump track, ball fields and turf fields, tennis, basketball, volleyball, horseshoe pits, a fitness course, sport courts for pickleball with your own net, a reservable shelter, and the Veterans Plaza of Northern Colorado. The Spring Creek Trail starts here. Two lots off Horsetooth Road by the playground and dog park and a third off Overland Trail on the northwest side. Open daily 5 am to 11 pm.
