The concert venue at 204 South College in Old Town, booked by Z2 Entertainment (aggietheatre.com now points to Z2's own site). The calendar runs through indie, rock, electronic, country, blues, jazz, hip-hop, reggae and comedy. The box office is open Monday to Friday 10 to 5, and on Wednesday to Friday event nights from doors until 30 minutes after the headliner goes on; tickets are sold online through Tixr. The room is also rented for private events.
