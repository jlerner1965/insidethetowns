Colorado State's football stadium opened on campus in July 2017, on the south side of the university between Pitkin and Lake streets: an 812,000-square-foot building that houses the Iris and Michael Smith Alumni Center, classrooms and the Collaborative for Student Achievement as well as the field. It holds about 36,000 guests for commencement and runs a clear-bag policy with mobile lockers outside. Beyond game days, the campus itself is worth the walk: the Oval, Moby Arena and the Lory Student Center, where Transfort's CSU Transit Center sits at the north end.

On a game day the parking lots and tailgating open six hours before kickoff and the Ram Walk tailgate concert off Meridian Avenue starts four hours before; will call is at the Rams Ticket Office on the stadium's northwest side off Pitkin Street, open four hours before kickoff. Clear bags only and metal detectors at the gates. Tickets (800) 491-RAMS.
