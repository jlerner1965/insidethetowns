Twelve acres of curated gardens run by the City on Centre Avenue, with a Butterfly House in a tropical setting and a paved main loop of just over half a mile that is wheelchair accessible (a chair can be borrowed free, first come first served). The year runs on events: the Spring Plant Sale, the Live at The Gardens summer concerts, Pumpkins on Parade in October, Garden of Lights (December 11 to 27 this year), and the Northern Colorado Bonsai Show.

The Gardens are on winter hours with reduced admission: open Monday to Wednesday and Friday to Sunday 10 to 4, last entry 30 minutes before closing, and closed Thursdays and on July 4, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve and Day, and New Year's Eve and Day. During Garden of Lights the daytime hours are 1 to 4. Winter admission is $6.50 for adults and $5.50 for ages 2 to 17, students with ID, seniors 65 and over and military; members free. Free parking in the lot off Centre Avenue and along Botanical Lane. Only trained service animals inside.
