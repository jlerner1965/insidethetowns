Science and local history under one roof on Mason Court. Exhibits on First Peoples, the Music Experience, Wildlands, Agriculture and Astronomy; the 360-degree OtterBox Digital Dome Theater; the Heritage Courtyard with four buildings of regional historical importance; and an archive and collections room. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9 to 5, closed Mondays and most major holidays. Adults $15.50, youth 2 to 18, students and seniors $12.75, under 2 and members free.
