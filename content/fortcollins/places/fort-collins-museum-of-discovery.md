Science and local history under one roof on Mason Court. Exhibits include First Peoples, the Music & Sound Lab, Wildlands & Wildlife and Food, Forage, & Farm; the 360-degree OtterBox Digital Dome Theater shows films and planetarium programs; and the Archive is free to visit. The Heritage Courtyard and its historic cabins are not on Mason Court: they are next to the Center for Creativity at the old Carnegie Library, 200 Mathews Street, and the museum books guided group visits there. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9 to 5, closed Mondays and most major holidays. Adults $15.50; youth ages 2 to 12, students with ID and seniors $12.75; under 2 and members free. Tickets for a specific date can be bought online, and the museum’s [plan-your-visit page](https://fcmod.org/plan-your-visit/) has parking and access details.
