Science and local history under one roof on Mason Court. Exhibits on First Peoples, the Music Experience, Wildlands, Agriculture and Astronomy; the 360-degree OtterBox Digital Dome Theater; the Heritage Courtyard with four buildings of regional historical importance; and an archive and collections room. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9 to 5, closed Mondays and most major holidays. Adults $15.50; youth ages 2 to 12, students with ID and seniors $12.75; under 2 and members free. Timed-entry tickets can be reserved online, and the museum’s [plan-your-visit page](https://fcmod.org/plan-your-visit/) has parking and access details.
