The Avenir keeps and shows historic apparel and textiles, a collection of roughly 20,000 artifacts from across cultures and centuries, in the University Center for the Arts East on Lake Street, a block from the main [University Center for the Arts](/places/university-center-for-the-arts/). Exhibitions change through the year. Free and open to the public, Tuesday to Friday from 11 am and Saturdays from 10 am; closed on university breaks and holidays.
