Several places in one building at the north end of Linden Street. The Cafe does a new take on farm-to-fork American classics from 7 am to 9 pm; the Market, Bakery and Coffee Shop keeps the same hours for coffee, fresh-baked pie and a treat to take home; The Cache is a modern Colorado steakhouse of steaks, chops, wine and whiskey, open Tuesday to Saturday from 4 to 9. Everything closes Monday.

The Teaching Kitchen runs hands-on classes (listed from $99) and ticketed evenings such as murder mystery dinners and Dungeons & Dragons nights. Guest parking is complimentary.
