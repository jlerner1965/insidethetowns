A small-batch, bean-to-bar maker, "honest chocolate" with no added chemicals or shortcuts, from ethically sourced cacao roasted and ground locally. The shop sells single-origin dark bars (a Vanuatu 2024 bar is $10.50), spirit bars such as the American Whiskey at $17, truffles at about $5.25 each, chocolate bitters, and the signature French-style hot chocolate that makes it a winter stop. The site does not publish hours; the owners, Tyler and Megan, invite people to stop by the shop.
