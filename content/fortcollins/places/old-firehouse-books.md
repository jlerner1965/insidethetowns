The locally owned independent bookshop, named for the historic firehouse it occupies, carrying both new and used titles with a staff that knows science fiction, literature and mystery particularly well. New books not in stock can be special ordered, and used books brought in earn trade credit. It is a member of the American Booksellers Association and the Mountains and Plains Independent Booksellers Association, and the downtown events calendar fills with its author evenings and panels. Open Sunday to Wednesday 10 to 6 and Thursday to Saturday 10 to 7. The trade desk takes no books in November and December.
