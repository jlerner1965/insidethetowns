An American brasserie with French and American-inspired cooking from regional ingredients, built to feel like a busy market: you walk through the wine display to choose a bottle for dinner and ask the staff about pairings. The Bowerbird coffee bar opens at 6 am every day. On weekdays breakfast (bagel and lox $21, French toast $13, Denver omelet $17) runs 7 to 3 and lunch (the FoCo burger $20, grilled cheese with tomato bisque $16) 11 to 3; brunch takes their place on Saturday and Sunday from 9 to 3. Happy hour is every day from 3 to 6, dine-in only. Dinner is 5 to 9 Sunday to Thursday and 5 to 10 on Friday and Saturday, with mains such as guajillo salmon at $36 and a prime New York strip at $55. Reservations through OpenTable.
