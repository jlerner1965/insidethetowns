An American brasserie with French and American-inspired cooking from regional ingredients, built to feel like a busy market: you walk through the wine display to choose a bottle for dinner and ask the staff about pairings. The Bowerbird coffee bar opens at 6 am; breakfast (bagel and lox $21, French toast $13, Denver omelet $17) runs 7 to 3, lunch (the FoCo burger $21, grilled cheese with tomato bisque $16) 11 to 3, and happy hour is every day from 3 to 5 with a $9 old fashioned. Dinner mains include guajillo salmon at $38 and steak béarnaise at $54; brunch is Saturday and Sunday. Reservations through OpenTable.
