The two-story building on the east side of Library Park is the district's Old Town branch. Computers and Wi-Fi, copying, printing, faxing and scanning, a laptop kiosk, 3D printing, meeting and study rooms, an Outdoor Story Stroll through the park, and the Old Town Nook run by the Friends of the Library. Open Monday to Thursday 9 to 8, Friday 9 to 6, Saturday 9 to 5 and Sunday noon to 5.
