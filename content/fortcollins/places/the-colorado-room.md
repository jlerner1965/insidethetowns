Gourmet sliders and American bar food made with Colorado ingredients, locally sourced drinks on tap, and a rotating show of regional artists' work that is for sale, with a cut of every purchase going back to Fort Collins causes. The poutine, sweet potato fries, wings and chicken tenders are what people come for. Open every day 11 am to 10 pm.
