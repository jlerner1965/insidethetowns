A neighborhood brewery a block off College Avenue, with a beer garden and patio that stay shady and a tap list that runs past a dozen house beers. Postcard Pizza serves Detroit-style pies on site Thursday through Sunday evenings, and the weekly trivia night is Thursday at 7 pm. The brewery says it does not host regular live music, so this is the quiet-conversation choice among the downtown taprooms.
