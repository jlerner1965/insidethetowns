The house was built in 1904 for Alfred Augustus Edwards at a cost of $6,000, to a design by Montezuma Fuller in the neoclassical Denver foursquare style of the time, and was expanded into a bed and breakfast in 1993. Rooms, a gourmet breakfast, and Mountain Avenue to walk down into Old Town. Reservations through the inn's site.
