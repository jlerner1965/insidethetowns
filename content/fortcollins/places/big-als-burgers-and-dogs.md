The cheap, quick Old Town lunch: fresh, never-frozen patties, the signature 60/40 bacon burger (60 percent beef, 40 percent bacon), all-beef and Chicago dogs, a homemade veggie burger, hand-cut fries in truffle and cajun versions, and hand-scooped milkshakes. The daily happy hour from 2 to 5 pm sells the 60/40, the Chicago dog or any shake for $5. Same family as Austin's American Grill and Comet Chicken.
