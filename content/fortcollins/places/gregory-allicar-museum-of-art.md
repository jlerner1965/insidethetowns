The university's art museum occupies galleries inside the [University Center for the Arts](/places/university-center-for-the-arts/) on Remington Street. Beside its permanent-collection galleries it installs three or four temporary exhibitions a year, and the collection runs to more than 6,000 works of art; through December 13, 2026 the Griffin Foundation Gallery holds *Ralph Steadman: And Another Thing*, 138 original works from the Gonzo illustrator's sixty-year career. Admission is always free. Open Wednesday to Saturday from 10 am, until 7:30 pm on Thursdays, and Sunday afternoons; Mondays and Tuesdays are reserved for class visits. The museum closes for university holidays and recesses: November 21 to 29, 2026 for fall break and December 14, 2026 to January 19, 2027 for winter break. Free two-hour street parking around the building.
