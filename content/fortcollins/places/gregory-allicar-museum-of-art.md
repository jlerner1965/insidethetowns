The university's art museum occupies galleries inside the [University Center for the Arts](/places/university-center-for-the-arts/) on Remington Street. It mounts eight or nine exhibitions a year, drawn from a collection of some 3,000 objects and from visiting shows; through December 13, 2026 the Griffin Foundation Gallery holds *Ralph Steadman: And Another Thing*, 138 original works from the Gonzo illustrator's sixty-year career. Admission is always free. Open Wednesday to Saturday from 10 am, until 7:30 pm on Thursdays, and Sunday afternoons; Mondays and Tuesdays are reserved for class visits. Free two-hour street parking around the building.
