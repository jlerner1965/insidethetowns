The Old Town seafood house: an active oyster bar, fresh sustainable fish with local produce, handcrafted cocktails, wine and local beer. Mains run to grilled wild Alaskan salmon at $47 and New England sea scallops at $49, while the oyster bar end of the menu is where happy hour earns its keep, deviled eggs at $4, chargrilled oysters at $5 each and an $8 oyster shooter. Happy hour is daily from 3:30 to 5:30 and all evening on Tuesdays.
