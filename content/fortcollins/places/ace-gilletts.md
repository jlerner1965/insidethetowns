Downstairs in the Armstrong Hotel, the lounge is the dress-up cocktail room of Old Town: a proper cocktail program, an evening-only schedule and a happy hour from 5 to 6:30 pm. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

The Ace Gillett's cafe upstairs at 239½ South College is the daytime side, open Wednesday and Thursday 9 to 5, Friday and Saturday 9 to 9, and Sunday 9 to 2.
