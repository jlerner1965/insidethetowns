The student union is where a visitor to campus ends up: the CSU Bookstore for the green and gold, a food court of more than fifteen venues, an information desk, a theatre and the plaza outside that fills for Homecoming and move-in. It sits at the center of campus on the Center Avenue Mall, a short walk from the Oval and from the MAX stop on the east edge of campus. The hours shown are the building's posted hours for 2026–27; it posts separate hours for university breaks.
