The student union is where a visitor to campus ends up: the CSU Bookstore for the green and gold, a food court of more than fifteen venues, an information desk, a theatre and the plaza outside that fills for Homecoming and move-in. It sits at the centre of campus on the Center Avenue Mall, a short walk from the Oval and from the MAX stop on the east edge of campus. The building posts its hours by term; the hours shown are the summer 2026 posting, so check the site in term time.
