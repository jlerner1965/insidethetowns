Spring Creek meanders through Edora Park alongside the trail, and the Edora Pool Ice Center (EPIC) sits at its edge. An 18-hole disc golf course, a skate park, tennis courts (pickleball with your own net), ball fields, basketball, horseshoe pits, fishing, a playground and a community garden; the Edora Veteran Memorial, built in 1987, honors soldiers of the two World Wars, Korea and Vietnam. Shelter #1 holds 150 and can be reserved. Two large lots off Riverside Avenue, one by EPIC and one by the disc golf course, and a small one on the west side near the playground. Open daily 5 am to 11 pm.
