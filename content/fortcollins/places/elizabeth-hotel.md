An Autograph Collection hotel on Chestnut Street built around music: 164 rooms, a lending library of wall-mounted guitars and a vinyl record library for guests, The Emporium brasserie, rooftop jazz at the Sunset Lounge and live music at Magic Rat. Check-in 4 pm, check-out 11 am. Up to two pets per room (100 pounds each) for a $75 non-refundable fee; self and valet parking in the Fire House Alley structure next to the hotel, $25 a day.
