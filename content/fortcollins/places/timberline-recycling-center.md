Two yards side by side on South Timberline Road. The Everyday Recyclables Yard is free and open seven days a week, 6 am to 8 pm from April through October and 7 to 6 from November through March, for bottles, cans, cardboard, glass, paper and plastic bags. The Hard-to-Recycle Materials Yard charges $5 to enter, plus fees on some materials, and is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 5 from April through October and 8 to 4 from November through March, for electronics, batteries, paint, motor oil, antifreeze, wood waste, yard trimmings and aggregates. You do not have to live in Fort Collins to use it, and commercial customers are welcome at both yards. 970-829-6337 on site; 970-221-6600 for general recycling questions.
