Fort Collins is a city of 170,000, and this page keeps to the part of it you can walk: Old Town, the blocks around College Avenue and the square, with the museum and the River District a few minutes north. Horsetooth, the reservoir and the open space in the foothills west of town, is a drive and a separate morning; the [route chooser](/articles/horsetooth-route-chooser/) sorts out the County's permit and the trailheads.

Park once. The City runs three garages downtown, at 100 Remington Street (Old Town), 165 Chestnut Street (Firehouse Alley) and the corner of Laporte Avenue and Mason Street (Civic Center). The first hour is free and each hour after it $1, all three are free on Sundays and City holidays, and they are gateless, so start a session at the kiosk or in the Parkmobile app however long you stay. On-street parking in the core is limited to two hours.

## 01 · Breakfast on Walnut Street

The [Silver Grill Cafe](/places/silver-grill-cafe/) at 218 Walnut Street has served breakfast and lunch since 1933, which makes it the city's oldest restaurant: cinnamon rolls, pancakes, skillets and hash browns, daily 7 am–2 pm. On the same block, [Old Firehouse Books](/places/old-firehouse-books/) at 232 Walnut sells new and used titles in the old firehouse and publishes its hours on its own site.

*Getting there:* a few minutes on foot from any of the three garages. *Best for:* the first meal of the day and a book for later.

## 02 · Old Town Square

[Old Town Square](/places/old-town-square/), off College Avenue, is the pedestrian plaza the rest of downtown is arranged around, built in 1983–84 and rebuilt in 2015 by the Downtown Development Authority: a stage, a splash pad whose jets run from May through September, a communal fireplace and patios on every side. This fall it holds FoCo Fall Fest (October 9 to 11) and the Chili Cook Off (October 24). [CooperSmith's Pub & Brewing](/places/coopersmiths-pub-and-brewing/) at 5 Old Town Square has brewed on site since 1989 and opens at 11 am every day, to 9 pm Sunday to Wednesday and 10 pm Thursday to Saturday; its billiards room at number 7 opens Wednesday to Sunday from 3 pm. On College Avenue itself are [Harbinger Coffee](/places/harbinger-coffee-old-town/) at 236 S College, daily 7 am–4 pm, and [The Cupboard](/places/the-cupboard/), the kitchen store at 152 S College, family-owned since 1972.

*Getting there:* the square opens off College Avenue. *Best for:* lunch, a seat by the fireplace, the shops along College.

## 03 · The Museum of Discovery

Mason Street runs a block west of College, and at its north end, at the corner of Cherry and Mason, is the [Fort Collins Museum of Discovery](/places/fort-collins-museum-of-discovery/), 408 Mason Court: science and local history under one roof, with the OtterBox Digital Dome Theater and a courtyard of historic buildings. It is open Tuesday to Sunday 9 am–5 pm and closed Monday and on most major holidays, Thanksgiving and December 24 and 25 among them; adults $15.50, youth, students and seniors $12.75, under 2 free, and the dome $6 on top for non-members. The archive, the rooftop observation deck, the store and the cafe are free. The museum has its own free parking with overflow on Cherry Street, and puts the Civic Center garage a short walk to the south and Transfort's [Downtown Transit Center](/places/downtown-transit-center/) a block or two down Mason.

*Getting there:* on foot up Mason Street, or drive this leg and use the museum lot. *Best for:* a wet afternoon, children, the dome.

## 04 · Linden Street: pie and the brewery

Linden Street leaves the square to the north-east and runs into the River District. [Ginger and Baker](/places/ginger-and-baker/) at 359 Linden is a pie shop, bakery, market, cafe and steakhouse in one building, Tuesday to Sunday 7 am–9 pm and closed Monday. At 500 Linden, [New Belgium Brewing](/places/new-belgium-brewing/), the brewery that made Fat Tire, opens its taproom daily noon–8 pm with a different food truck each day and a lawn for the big events; tours are booked ahead on its site. Check its adjusted hours before a special trip: in 2026 it closes at 6 pm on October 17 and December 31, and is closed on November 10, 11 and 26 and December 24 and 25.

*Getting there:* on foot along Linden from the square. *Best for:* an afternoon beer, pie to take home, the lawn when there is music.

## A simple first visit

1. Park in one of the City garages: free for the first hour, free all day on Sunday.
2. Breakfast at the Silver Grill, then the bookshop.
3. Cross the square, with lunch at CooperSmith's or a coffee on College.
4. Walk up Mason to the museum on a day it is open, which is any day but Monday.
5. Finish on Linden at Ginger and Baker or on New Belgium's lawn.

Horsetooth does not fit into this day. Give it its own morning, with the [permit and the routes](/articles/horsetooth-route-chooser/) sorted out first.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. Fort Collins is about an hour and ten minutes from Denver on I-25 off-peak, twenty minutes from Loveland and thirty-five from Greeley. Bustang runs to Denver Union Station from the Downtown Transit Center, and Transfort's city buses and the MAX line on Mason Street are currently fare-free. Posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Museum of Discovery states the most: push-button main doors, a drop-off area in front, four accessible parking spaces outside the cafe entrance, an elevator to the second and third levels, a wheelchair to borrow at the welcome desk, an accessible stall and a baby-changing station in every restroom, companion restrooms in the Music and Sound Lab, three wheelchair spaces in the dome theater and sensory kits to check out free. Transit Center restrooms and an indoor waiting room are listed by Transfort.

The City's parking page says nothing about accessible spaces in the garages, and the restaurants, the square and the brewery have not published step-free details, so none is claimed here.
