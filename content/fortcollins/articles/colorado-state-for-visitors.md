Colorado State University had 34,412 students in the fall of 2025, the most it has ever had, which makes the campus between Laurel Street and Prospect Road the largest single thing in a city of 170,000. It is also the easiest part of Fort Collins to visit badly: the parking rules are the university's, not the city's, and a football Saturday rearranges the whole west side. This is what a visitor needs.

## Seeing the campus

Guided tours leave from the Admissions building at 711 Oval Drive, the brick building at the head of the Oval, Monday to Saturday; you register online and the registration comes with a free parking pass, which is the only free weekday parking on campus. A self-guided tour works any day. Without a tour, parking is by pay station or the ParkMobile app, Monday to Friday; the lots in the middle of campus fill, and the university's own advice is that there is almost always space in Moby lot 195 or lot 740. The garages are enforced every day. From downtown or Midtown the simpler way in is the [MAX](/places/downtown-transit-center/) bus down the Mason corridor, which is fare-free and stops at the campus.

The [Lory Student Center](/places/lory-student-center/) is the place to aim for once you are there: the bookstore, the food court and the information desk. The [Flower Trial Garden](/places/flower-trial-garden/) on Remington Street is the prettiest free hour on campus from late May to October.

## Game day

Football is at [Canvas Stadium](/places/canvas-stadium/) on the south edge of campus. The lots and tailgating open six hours before kickoff, the free Ram Walk tailgate concert off Meridian Avenue starts four hours before, will call at the ticket office on the stadium's north-west side opens four hours before, and the gates have metal detectors and a clear-bag rule. The 2026 home games left are San Diego State on October 24 at 4:30 pm and Boise State on November 7 at 4 pm, with a final home date on November 28 whose opponent and time are not yet set.

Basketball and volleyball are at [Moby Arena](/places/moby-arena/) on Plum Street, where the lots open two hours before tip-off and will call one hour before. The women's home schedule has published times from late October; the men open at home against Montana State on November 2, with tip-off times still to be announced. Tickets for everything: (800) 491-RAMS.

## The arts, and two free museums

The [University Center for the Arts](/places/university-center-for-the-arts/) on Remington Street, the old high school, holds the concert hall, the organ hall, the theatres and more than 250 performances a year, most of them free or $15 or less. Inside it, the [Gregory Allicar Museum of Art](/places/gregory-allicar-museum-of-art/) is free Wednesday to Sunday; a block east, the [Avenir Museum](/places/avenir-museum-of-design-and-merchandising/) of dress and textiles is free Tuesday to Saturday. The one campus event the rest of the city plans around is the [Halloween Organ Extravaganza](/events/halloween-organ-extravaganza-2026-10-31/), three sold-out shows on October 31.

## The dates that change the city

Move-in week in late August, Homecoming and Family Weekend in early October (October 1 to 4 in 2026, around the Oregon State game), and the two commencements: Friday December 18, 2026, with the Graduate School ceremony at Moby Arena at 11 am, and May 14 and 15, 2027. On those weekends hotels fill from Loveland to Wellington and Old Town restaurants get busy, so book a room early, reserve a table where you can, or come the week after.
