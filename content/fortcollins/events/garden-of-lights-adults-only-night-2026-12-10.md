New this year, the Gardens open the Garden of Lights season with an adults-only evening on Thursday, December 10, the night before the public run begins. Doors open at 6 p.m., the entrance gates close at 7 and the evening ends at 8. It is for ages 18 and over, with two live musical acts, a cash bar and a food truck. Tickets are $15 through the Gardens' page, with 20% off for members.
