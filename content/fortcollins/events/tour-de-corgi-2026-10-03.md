The 12th annual corgi parade. Registration opens at 9 at Civic Center Park, the costume contest is at 10, the parade sets off through Old Town at noon and the day wraps at 2. Wristbands are sold on the day; there is also a registration evening on Friday, October 2, 5:30–7:30 pm at Visit Fort Collins.
