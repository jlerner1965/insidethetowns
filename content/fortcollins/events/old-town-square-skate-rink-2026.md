The synthetic-surface rink in Old Town Square is back from November 25 through January 24, with free admission and free skate rentals in child and adult sizes. Regular hours are Fridays 4 to 7, Saturdays noon to 6 and Sundays noon to 5, with extra weekday hours over Thanksgiving week and the Poudre School District winter break; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Group reservations: aruffer@fcgov.com; questions 970-999-0475.
