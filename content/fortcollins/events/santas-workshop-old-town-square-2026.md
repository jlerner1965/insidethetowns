Free visits with Santa in his Old Town Square workshop. Hours are noon to 6 p.m. on open days: November 27 to 29, December 4 to 6, December 9 to 13, then every day December 14 to 24 (noon to 4 on Christmas Eve). Families may take their own photos; printed portraits are sold on site. The DDA says lines are shortest early in the season, and posts a live Square webcam. Questions: 970-999-0475.
