The last of Wildcraft Farmers Markets' summer-season second-Saturday markets at Foothills, 10 am to 2 pm: produce, specialty foods and makers, with live music and free children's activities. The market turns twenty this year and is recording shoppers' stories for an anniversary video at this one. The indoor winter season follows, every Saturday from November 7.
