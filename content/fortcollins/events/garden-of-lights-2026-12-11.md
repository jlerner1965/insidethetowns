The Gardens' holiday light walk runs December 11–23 and 26–27, with Santa at the Everitt Pavilion from 5 to 8 p.m. and the Ice Queen in the Ice Forest from 5:30 to 7:30 each night. Entry is timed, with slots from 5 to 8 p.m. and the grounds closing at 9; prices vary by date and time, $12 to $16 for adults and $8 to $12 for ages 3 to 17, free for 2 and under, with $1 SNAP/EBT tickets by phone or on site. Tickets are sold through the Gardens' page, which advises buying early to get the entry window you want. A rivers-of-light display is new in 2026; an adults-only evening on December 10 opens the season.
