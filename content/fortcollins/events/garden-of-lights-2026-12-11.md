The Gardens' holiday light walk returns December 11–23 and 26–27, with visits from Santa and an ice princess on some nights. Entry is timed, with slots from 5 to 8 p.m. and the grounds closing at 9; prices vary by slot, starting at $12 for adults and $8 for children, with $1 SNAP/EBT tickets by phone or on site. Tickets go on sale Monday, November 2 at noon, and the Gardens say some slots sell out. Santa is there through December 23, the Ice Forest returns and a rivers-of-light display is new in 2026; an adults-only evening on December 10 opens the season.
