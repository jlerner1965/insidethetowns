The Homecoming and Family Weekend game: the Rams' first conference game as a Pac-12 member, and the fifth game of their season, against Oregon State at Canvas Stadium, kicking off at 4 pm on USA Network. The weekend around it runs October 1 to 4, with the Festival on the Oval and Friday Night Lights on the Friday and the Homecoming 5K on the Oval on the Saturday morning (homecoming.colostate.edu). Kickoff and network from CSU's own schedule.
