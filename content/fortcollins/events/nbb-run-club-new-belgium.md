New Belgium's weekly run club takes off on a roughly three-mile route on Wednesday nights at 6, and runners get $1 off a beer afterward. The start point moves; each week's route and meeting spot are posted on the club's Strava group and @newbelgium.runclub on Instagram. Dates are listed through December 23.
