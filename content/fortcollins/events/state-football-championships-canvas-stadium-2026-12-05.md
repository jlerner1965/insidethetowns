Colorado's three largest football classes play their state finals back to back at Colorado State's Canvas Stadium: 3A at 10 am, 4A at 2 pm and 5A at 6 pm, the kickoffs on CHSAA's [3A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/3a), [4A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/4a) and [5A](https://chsaanow.com/brackets/football/2026/state/5a) brackets. The finalists come out of the semifinals on November 28.
