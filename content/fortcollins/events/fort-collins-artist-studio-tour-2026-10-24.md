A free, self-guided weekend tour of working artists' studios around the city, Saturday 10–5 and Sunday 12–5, using the online map of participating artists. A preview exhibit runs October 14–25 at the Center for Creativity, 200 Mathews St, with an opening reception there on Friday, October 16, 6–8 pm.
