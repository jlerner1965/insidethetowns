Berthoud sits on US 287 between Loveland and Longmont, about fifteen minutes from either. Mountain Avenue is the town's main street and runs straight through the grid, with Town Hall at the west end and the museum at the east, so leave the car on or near it and walk; the Main Street program's own advice is to park once. The reservoir is the one stop that needs the car again.

## 01 · Grandpa's Cafe

Breakfast first, at the west end of Mountain Avenue a block from Town Hall. [Grandpa's](/places/grandpas-cafe/) opens at 7 am every day and serves breakfast all day, with lunch specials on weekdays and a fish fry on Fridays. It closes at 8 pm except on Tuesdays, when it shuts at 2 pm. The same kitchen runs an Indian menu, Colorado Curry, from 2 pm to 8:30 pm Wednesday to Monday.

*Getting there:* 903 Mountain Avenue; street parking. *Best for:* the first meal of the day, or a late one on any day but Tuesday. [Cornerstone Cafe](/places/cornerstone-cafe/) at 3rd and Mountain is the diner alternative, Tuesday to Saturday 7 am–2 pm.

## 02 · Fickel Park and Town Park

Walk east. [Fickel Park](/places/fickel-park/) at 620 Mountain Avenue is the shaded block with the sculptures: the bear is the landmark, and the Town's page lists picnic tables, a lighted tennis court and portable restrooms. Some of the sculptures rotate, so the set changes between visits. A block further, at 7th and Mountain, [Berthoud Town Park](/places/berthoud-town-park/) is the town's oldest park, rebuilt around a 12,000-square-foot fully inclusive playground and a 6,000-square-foot natural-stone water feature with fifteen waterspouts and a button-activated waterfall. The Town shuts the water for the season at Oktoberfest, the first Saturday of October, so in 2026 it is a playground and lawn until spring. The Saturday market sets up on the lawn.

*Getting there:* on foot along Mountain Avenue. *Best for:* a picnic, children, and the sculptures.

## 03 · The main block and the murals

The brick storefronts between 7th Street and the museum are where the shops, the coffee house and the brewery are, and the [mural walk](/places/downtown-mural-walk/) runs through them. Look down as well as up: the leaf motif cast into the sidewalks and worked into the lamp posts comes from the ironwork of the local blacksmith A.G. Bimson, whose shop is the next stop. The Hometown Heroes banners line the avenue. A free Art in Public Places brochure is at Homestead Fine Art Gallery, City Star Brewing and The Rancher's Wife.

*Getting there:* on foot. *Best for:* coffee, browsing and the murals. [Who is on the block](/eat-drink/).

## 04 · The Pioneer Museum

The [Little Thompson Valley Pioneer Museum](/places/pioneer-museum/) at 224 Mountain Avenue is built around the 1893 Bimson Blacksmith Shop, a native-sandstone building on the National Register, with exhibits on the railroad, the sugar-beet farms, the schools and the families of the valley. It keeps afternoon hours only: Wednesday to Saturday, 1–4 pm; adults $5, seniors and youth 12 to 18 $3, children 11 and under free. The Historical Society's page says most areas of the museum are handicapped-accessible. The Homesteaders mural is on the building. The Society's replica of John Bunyan's 1933 observatory is an exhibit during museum hours and opens for public stargazing on the first Friday of each month.

*Getting there:* the east end of the walk. *Best for:* the hour after lunch, Wednesday to Saturday.

## 05 · Berthoud Reservoir

Now the car. The [Berthoud Reservoir Loop](/places/berthoud-reservoir-loop/) at 2480 North Berthoud Parkway is a flat 1.2-mile walk around the water, opened to the public in 2020 with a restroom, parking, paddle-craft access and fishing. Free, dogs on leash, foothills to the west. For more water, [Carter Lake](/places/carter-lake/) is ten minutes north-west and is Larimer County's: boat ramps, campgrounds, shoreline trails and a swim beach from Memorial Day to mid-September, with an entrance permit required at the gate. In August 2026, Colorado Parks and Wildlife confirmed New Zealand mudsnails in the lake, so clean, drain and dry boats, paddleboards and fishing gear, and expect a boat leaving the lake to need inspection before it goes into other water. [Roberts Lake](/places/roberts-lake/), off Bunyan Court in town, is the small option, with a walking path and a fishing dock.

*Getting there:* a short drive north from downtown; the Town page lists parking. *Best for:* an easy loop at any time of year; paddling and fishing.

## 06 · City Star Brewing

Finish back on Mountain Avenue if the hours fit. [City Star](/places/city-star-brewing/) at 321 opens at 3 pm on weekdays and noon at weekends, and closes at 8 pm Monday and Sunday, 9 pm Tuesday to Thursday and 10 pm Friday and Saturday. Food trucks park outside most days, trivia is on Tuesdays at 6 pm, a social run leaves the brewery on Thursdays at 6 pm, and there is live music on Friday and Saturday evenings. The beer garden is behind the storefront; dogs are welcome.

*Getting there:* on the main block, between stops 03 and 04. *Best for:* the end of the day; a weekend afternoon.

## A simple first visit

1. Park on or near Mountain Avenue.
2. Breakfast at Grandpa's; on a Tuesday, go before 2 pm.
3. Walk east through Fickel Park, Town Park and the main block to the museum, timing it for 1–4 pm, Wednesday to Saturday.
4. Drive to the reservoir and walk the loop.
5. Come back to City Star after 3 pm on a weekday or noon at the weekend.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. Loveland and Longmont are about fifteen minutes on US 287, Fort Collins about twenty-five, and Denver about fifty minutes by I-25. Transfort's FLEX bus stops in Berthoud on its run between Fort Collins, Loveland, Longmont and Boulder, on weekdays and Saturdays; check [Transfort](https://www.ridetransfort.com/routes/flex) for the current timetable.

Berthoud is a Larimer County town with a small part east of the county line in Weld, so a sheriff's or county notice can come from either. Posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Town's page for Berthoud Reservoir lists the park as ADA accessible, with restrooms and parking; Roberts Lake has an ADA-accessible fishing dock. The Town Park playground is described as fully inclusive, and Fickel Park has portable restrooms. The Historical Society says most areas of the Pioneer Museum are handicapped-accessible.

Sidewalks and curb ramps along Mountain Avenue, step-free entrances to the shops and cafes, and designated accessible parking downtown have not yet been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here.
