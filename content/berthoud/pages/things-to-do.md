picks:
  - place: downtown-mural-walk
    for: "A first afternoon downtown"
    why: "A free, self-guided walk past the murals, rotating sculptures and the Bimson leaf cast into the sidewalks and lamp posts along Mountain Avenue and its side streets. Pick up the Art in Public Places brochure at Homestead Fine Art Gallery, City Star Brewing or The Rancher's Wife."
  - place: pioneer-museum
    for: "A winter afternoon"
    why: "The Historical Society's museum in A.G. Bimson's 1893 stone blacksmith shop, on the National Register, with the Meadow Hollow School from the valley Carter Lake now covers. Open Wednesday to Saturday, 1 to 4 pm; adults $5, ages 12 to 18 and seniors $3, children 11 and under free."
  - place: berthoud-recreation-center
    for: "A snowy day"
    why: "The Town's recreation center at Waggener Farm Park: a lap pool, a lazy river and a water slide, a climbing wall, and a gym with a walking track and six pickleball courts. A drop-in visit is $7 for adults and $5.50 for youth and seniors. Closed on Thanksgiving Day."
  - place: berthoud-reservoir-loop
    for: "Easy walk, any season"
    why: "The Town opened Berthoud Reservoir to the public in 2020 with a walking trail, fishing, paddle-craft access, parking and a restroom, and lists the site as ADA accessible. It is at 2480 North Berthoud Parkway."
  - place: carter-lake
    for: "A bigger day out"
    why: "Larimer County's 1,100-acre reservoir in the foothills, three miles long, with fishing all year (no ice fishing) and two campgrounds, Sky Harbor and South Shore, open through the winter. An entrance permit is required, and the pay stations take cards only."
  - place: berthoud-community-library
    for: "Small children, weekday mornings"
    why: "The library on Welch Avenue holds storytimes at 10:30 am on Tuesday for babies, Wednesday for pre-K and Friday for toddlers, and Musical Moments on Mondays at 4. It lends two state park pass backpacks for a week. Closed November 26 and 27 for Thanksgiving."
  - place: waggener-farm-park
    for: "Skaters and young kids"
    why: "More than 20 acres on Berthoud Parkway, shared with the recreation center: a concrete skate park, a miniature pump track, playgrounds for several ages, a basketball court, trails through native grass and farm field, and restrooms open in all four seasons."
  - place: berthoud-bike-park
    for: "Riders, on dry days"
    why: "The Town's 20-acre bike park on Meadowlark Drive: two jump and flow lines, a dual slalom track, a mile of cross-country trail, a bicycle playground and a 20,000-square-foot pump track. The Town closes it when it is wet, muddy, snowy or icy; its Facebook page posts the status."
  - place: berthoud-town-park
    for: "Summer Saturdays"
    why: "The Town's oldest park, on North 7th Street, rebuilt with a 12,000-square-foot inclusive playground, a stone water feature with 15 spouts that runs into early October, and a covered pavilion with a gas fire pit. The Berthoud Market fills it on summer Saturdays."
  - place: pioneer-park
    for: "Fishing and a picnic"
    why: "A neighborhood park at Indiana Avenue and 2nd Street with a fishing pond, a playground, a basketball court, a roller hockey rink, bocce and horseshoe pits (bring your own gear), three large pavilions and permanent restrooms."
