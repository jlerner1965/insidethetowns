The question lost in both counties. Larimer County, where most of the town votes, had 4,483 against and 3,061 for in its final posting of unofficial results, at 2:04 pm on November 13, which it says concludes its counting. Weld County had 65 against and 39 for in its final unofficial summary report of 12:40 pm that day. Together that is 4,548 to 3,100. Larimer County says its results remain unofficial until all election processes conclude, no later than November 25. This guide will give the certified count when it is published.

The certified question, as the Larimer County sample ballot prints it: "Shall the Town of Berthoud debt be increased $28,000,000, with a maximum repayment cost of up to $60,000,000, such debt to consist of sales and use tax revenue bonds to be payable from all or a portion of the Town's sales and use tax (without increasing taxes) and issued solely for parks and recreation purposes, including but not limited to the design, construction, and equipment of a community park site known as Mountain Vista Park and consisting of a combination of ballfields, aquatic recreation features, and related improvements…" The full text runs to the TABOR clause on the sample ballot.

The Board of Trustees voted 6–1 on August 11 to refer it, by the Berthoud Weekly Surveyor's account, with the Town administrator saying the debt could be serviced from the 1998 and 2019 one-percent sales taxes. The Town's Engage Berthoud page describes the park as a regional site with up to six ball fields, a playground and an aquatics center. This guide took no side.
