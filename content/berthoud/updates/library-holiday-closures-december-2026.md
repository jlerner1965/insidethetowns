The Berthoud Community Library's 2026 closures page lists its December and New Year's closings. The library closes at 6:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 23, and is closed Thursday and Friday, December 24 and 25, for Christmas Eve and Christmas. On Thursday, December 31, it closes at 2:00 p.m. The page also lists Friday, January 1, 2027, as New Year's Day. The library is at 236 Welch Avenue.
