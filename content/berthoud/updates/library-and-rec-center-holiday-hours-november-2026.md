The library's 2026 closures list has three November entries: Wednesday, November 11, Veterans Day; an early close at 6 pm on Wednesday, November 25, two hours before its usual Wednesday 8 pm; and Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27, for Thanksgiving. Perky Pre-K Storytime and the Digital Navigator drop-in skip November 11, the Thursday drop-in skips November 26 and Terrific Toddler Storytime skips November 27; the library's November newsletter also drops Bouncing Baby Storytime on Tuesday, November 24 and Perky Pre-K Storytime on Wednesday, November 25. The listings on this guide already say so. The [Berthoud Recreation Center](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/) runs its regular hours, 5 am to 9 pm, on Veterans Day and closes on Thanksgiving Day, November 26, by the Town's facility-schedules calendar. The Town's [Winter Activity Guide](https://www.berthoud.org/DocumentCenter/View/7956/Berthoud-Recreation-Winter-Activity-Guide-2026-27) gives the day after Thanksgiving, Friday, November 27, as open noon to 6 pm, with no child watch.
