The Town's August 3 release says it closed in July on 67 acres of Wilson Farm with open space funds and a Great Outdoors Colorado grant, following the 105-acre Schaal property bought in 2025. The Engage Berthoud project page for the corridor describes the Town as committed to conserving and stewarding the land along the river; the Jaskowski open space and the Newell Farm Park master plan are still in planning and open to comment there.
