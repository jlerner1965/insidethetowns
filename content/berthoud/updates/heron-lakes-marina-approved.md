Resolution 2026-25 passed on Trustee Anderson's motion, with Trustees Olbricht and Wing voting no, on the condition that the developer name the water provider with its preliminary plat application. The Board opened the hearing on September 22 and continued it to October 27 over questions about the metropolitan district that serves this phase of Heron Lakes. The land lies between Rabbit Run Lane and Heron Lakes Parkway and was annexed in 2018 and 2019. No building in the plan is over two stories, and the code counts the paired homes as single-family attached. A master plan is binding: later plats must follow it or apply to amend it. [What's being built in Berthoud](/articles/whats-being-built-in-berthoud/) has the rest of the Town's map.
