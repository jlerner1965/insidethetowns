The Town's Activity Guide page links the Winter Activity Guide 2026–27, in a full-screen reader and as an ADA-format PDF, under the line "Winter Activity Guide registration begins November 1, 2026"; registration is online through the Town's DaySmart portal, at the Recreation Center or by phone, 970-532-1600. The guide's youth sports pages and the Town's Memberships/Admission page give a $15 early-bird discount on select programs, with the higher price from November 15, so register by November 14 for the lower one. The Activity Guide page gives the pattern for the year: spring registration opens February 1, summer April 1, fall July 1 and winter November 1. Most of the winter [youth sports](/families/#program-youth-sports) programs take registrations until December 20, and [swim lessons](/families/#program-swim-lessons) open on the 1st of each month for classes two months ahead. The fall guide's last deadline, the [futsal clinic](/families/#program-youth-futsal-clinic) for ages 8 to 12, closed on November 1, and the winter guide lists no new futsal session.
