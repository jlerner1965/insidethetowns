The Town's Activity Guide page now links the Winter Activity Guide 2026–27, in a full-screen reader and as an ADA-format PDF, under the line "Winter Activity Guide registration begins November 1, 2026"; the guide was posted on November 1, as the page had promised, and winter early-bird registration runs through November. The page gives the pattern for the year: spring registration opens February 1 (early bird through February), summer April 1, fall July 1 and winter November 1, each with early-bird pricing through its first month. The fall guide's last deadline, the [futsal clinic](/families/#program-youth-futsal-clinic) for ages 8 to 12, closed on November 1, the day the winter guide opened; the [youth sports entry](/families/) on the family hub lists the winter leagues as the fall guide previewed them, and the Town's own winter guide controls.
