The Town's Activity Guide page gives the pattern for the year: spring registration opens February 1 (early bird through February), summer April 1, fall July 1 and winter November 1, each with early-bird pricing through its first month. Swim lessons can be registered up to two months ahead. The fall guide's youth basketball deadline of October 4 has passed, and its last open one is the [futsal clinic](/families/#program-youth-futsal-clinic) for ages 8 to 12, which closes registration on November 1, the day the winter guide opens; the [youth sports entry](/families/) on the family hub lists what the winter guide is expected to carry, and the Town's own guide controls.
