The Town's Activity Guide page links the Winter Activity Guide 2026–27, in a full-screen reader and as an ADA-format PDF, under the line "Winter Activity Guide registration begins November 1, 2026"; registration is online through the Town's DaySmart portal, at the Recreation Center or by phone, 970-532-1600. The guide's youth sports pages and the Town's Memberships/Admission page give the winter early-bird period as November 1 to 15, a $15 discount on select programs, with the increase starting November 15; the yearly calendar on the Activity Guide page says winter early bird runs through November. That calendar gives the pattern for the year: spring registration opens February 1, summer April 1, fall July 1 and winter November 1. Most of the winter [youth sports](/families/#program-youth-sports) programs take registrations until December 20, and [swim lessons](/families/#program-swim-lessons) open on the 1st of each month for classes two months ahead. The fall guide's last deadline, the [futsal clinic](/families/#program-youth-futsal-clinic) for ages 8 to 12, closed on November 1, and the winter guide lists no new futsal session.
