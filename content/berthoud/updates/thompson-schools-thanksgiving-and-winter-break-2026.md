The district's 2026–27 Calendar Highlights list Thanksgiving Break as November 23–27, Monday through Friday, and Winter Break as December 21 – January 4. A quarter ends on Friday, December 18, the last weekday before the break. On the other side, Tuesday, January 5 is a staff professional day with no students. Later in January the district is closed on Monday, January 18 for Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, and K–5 students have no school on Tuesday, January 19. The same page puts Spring Break at March 15–19. Berthoud's four public schools, Berthoud Elementary, Ivy Stockwell Elementary, Turner Middle School and Berthoud High School, are all Thompson schools.
