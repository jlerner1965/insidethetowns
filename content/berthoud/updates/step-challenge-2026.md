The Town's page puts it plainly: join from November 15 to December 15, track your daily steps with the free app, compete with friends and neighbors on the live leaderboard, and prizes go to the top finishers. It is free and open to everyone. Register online through the Town's recreation registration page, call 970-532-1600, or stop by the front desk at the [Berthoud Recreation Center](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/); once you are registered, the department sends the instructions for downloading the app and starting your count. The same page lists the center's fifth-anniversary celebration on Friday, November 20, 4 to 8 pm, five days into the challenge.
