The Little Thompson Observatory's Public Star Night is Friday, November 20: doors open at 7 pm, Jeff Bennett, Ph.D., gives a talk at 7:30 on the scale of the universe, and the telescopes are open for viewing from 7 to 10 pm, weather permitting. The observatory charges for none of its programs and takes donations.

It is on the east grounds of Berthoud High School, near the running track; use the school's address, 850 Spartan Avenue, and park in the high school's lot, since parking by the building is very limited. The telescope dome is kept at the outside temperature, so dress for the night, and bring a small flashlight for the path.
