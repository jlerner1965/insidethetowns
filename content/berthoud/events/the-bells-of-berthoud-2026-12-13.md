Berthoud's four churches with outdoor bells ring them together before Christmas, starting at 5:30 pm on Sunday, December 13, Sunday, December 20 and Christmas Eve. The Foursquare Church bell rings first, 21 times, then the Church of Christ, First United Methodist and First Presbyterian bells, each 21 times; then all four ring together for a minute, and the sequence repeats twice more. The Historical Society says the best place to hear all four, unless you live in Old Town, is Fickel Park. Former mayor Milan Karspeck began the tradition in 2004 and the Society carries it on.
