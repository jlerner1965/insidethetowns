The Little Thompson Observatory's December Public Star Night is Friday, December 18, 7 to 10 pm. John Ensworth gives the talk, Star of Wonder, and the telescopes are open for viewing, weather permitting. The observatory charges for none of its programs and takes donations.

It is on the east grounds of Berthoud High School, near the running track; use the school's address, 850 Spartan Avenue, and park in the high school's lot. The telescope dome is kept at the outside temperature, so dress for the night.
