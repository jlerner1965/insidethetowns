The Saturday after Thanksgiving, downtown merchants open with promotions, specials and events under Berthoud Main Street's "Buy it in Berthoud" banner. Two things are already on the day's calendar: City Star's Handmade Market, noon to 4 pm, and the Pioneer Museum, open 10 am to 4 pm.
