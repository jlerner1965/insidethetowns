Night two of Neon Nights at the Recreation Center: an adult dodgeball tournament under neon in the gym, on **Saturday, October 24**. Ages 16 and up; register a team through Berthoud Recreation.

The Town's printed fall activity guide had this on Friday the 23rd and the cosmic pickleball party on Saturday the 24th; the Town's Upcoming Events page and its home-page calendar, read on October 21, list them the other way round, and this listing follows those. The 6 pm start and the $60 team fee are from the printed guide; call Berthoud Recreation at 970-532-1600 if you want either settled before you go.
