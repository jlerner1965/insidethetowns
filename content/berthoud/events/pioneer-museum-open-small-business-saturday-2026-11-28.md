The Berthoud Historical Society opens the Little Thompson Valley Pioneer Museum on Mountain Avenue from 10 am to 4 pm on Small Business Saturday, outside its usual Wednesday-to-Saturday afternoon hours, so shoppers can warm up and walk through the collection between the downtown stores.
