An after-hours glow-in-the-dark pool party at the Recreation Center on Saturday, January 2, 6 to 8 pm: glowing lights, music, pool games and glow bracelets. For ages 2 and up; children under 6 and non-swimmers must have a paying adult with them in the water the whole time. $5 for a member, $10 for a non-member. Space is limited, and the guide asks families to register ahead through the Town's activity guide.
