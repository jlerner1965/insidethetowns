A sound bath, which the practitioner calls a sound journey, at the arts center on Massachusetts Avenue the week before Thanksgiving: monochord, singing bowls, tuning pipes, a 32-inch gong, chimes and seed rattles, with guided visualization. Friday, November 20, 6:30 to 7:45 pm; doors open at 6:20 and no one is admitted once the session has started. The floor is hardwood, so bring a thick mat or camping pad, a blanket and pillows, or a zero-gravity chair if you need to sit. Open to adults and mature youth 8 and older. The session needs at least five sign-ups three days before it runs; tickets through the organizer's Eventbrite page.
