The Berthoud Recreation Center at Waggener Farm Park opened in November 2021, and the Town marks five years on Friday, November 20, from 4 to 8 pm. The Town's events page lists bounce houses, face painting, aquatic games, sports challenges and fitness activities, giveaways every half hour, and a time capsule. Free, for all ages.
