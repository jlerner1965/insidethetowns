The Berthoud Recreation Center at Waggener Farm Park opened in November 2021, and the Town marks five years with a day of activities on Friday, November 20. The Town's calendar lists it as an all-day celebration and had not posted the schedule when this listing was checked; call the center at 970-532-1600 or watch berthoud.org for it.
