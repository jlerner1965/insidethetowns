The last day to enter the Berthoud Historical Society's Holiday Card Art Contest. This year's theme is Winter Wonderland; the winning artwork becomes Berthoud's holiday card. Enter in person at the Society with the submission form, or online through the contest page on the Society's site. The museum has an art station with forms and folders, so children can make their entry there. Entries are due by Saturday, November 7.
