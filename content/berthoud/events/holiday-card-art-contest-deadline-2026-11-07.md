The last day to enter the Berthoud Historical Society's Holiday Card Art Contest. This year's theme is Winter Wonderland; the winning artwork becomes Berthoud's holiday card. Bring the artwork and the submission form to the Society at 228 Mountain Avenue, or enter online through the contest page on the Society's site. The Winter Wonderland Art Station at the museum next door has forms and folders, so children can make their entry there. Entries are due by Saturday, November 14.
