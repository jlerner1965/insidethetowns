The second of the Town's two Holidaycation evenings: a drop-off evening of holiday games, arts and crafts for children ages 7 to 14 at the Recreation Center, 6 to 8 pm, run by Town recreation staff. $15 a night; register for one evening or both through the Town's online registration, where space is limited. The first is Saturday, December 12.
