The recreation center is Berthoud’s Larimer County vote center on Monday, November 2 (8 am to 5 pm) and Election Day, Tuesday, November 3 (7 am to 7 pm): vote in person, drop off a mail ballot, register, or get a replacement ballot. The county’s 24-hour drop box is at the Berthoud Community Library, 236 Welch Ave, walk-up and drive-up, until 7 pm on the 3rd. Ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm; anyone in line by 7 pm can vote. The county’s page lists every vote center and drop box.
