Two beers land the same afternoon: Raspberry Bandit, a brown ale fermented on Willamette Valley raspberries, and Cabaret, a sour raspberry brown ale aged in whiskey oak barrels. Acoustic blues players Johnny Johnston and Felonius Smith play slide guitar, mandolin and Dobro from 6 to 8:30 pm.
