A Very Merry Berthoud is an all-day holiday festival across Berthoud on Saturday, December 5, 2026, from the Winter Craft Fair at 9 am to the Parade of Lights at 5 pm and the tree lighting at 6 pm.

## Schedule

Berthoud Main Street's schedule for the day:

- **9 am to 3 pm:** Winter Craft Fair. The Town's calendar places it at the Berthoud Recreation Center at Waggener Farm Park, 1000 N. Berthoud Parkway, with more than 60 Colorado artisans; it is the fair's fifth year.
- **10 am to 3 pm:** Main Street Market.
- **10 am to 4 pm:** Berthoud Snowlympics.
- **11 am to 4 pm:** Meet Santa Claus.
- **5 pm:** [Parade of Lights](/events/parade-of-lights/) on Mountain Avenue, from 2nd Street to 8th Street.
- **6 pm:** tree lighting. The parade's own site lists carols and the lighting ceremony at 5:45, so stay downtown once the parade ends.

## Around downtown

The organizers also list craft and cookie making, letters-to-Santa mailboxes around downtown, blacksmithing demonstrations on Mountain Avenue, photos with Santa, carolers and a cowboy Christmas. Downtown shops are open through the day with gift wrapping and holiday deals, and shops and restaurants stay open after the tree lighting. The [Pioneer Museum is open for the day](/events/pioneer-museum-open-for-a-very-merry-berthoud-2026-12-05/) as well.

## Who runs it

The festival is presented by SouthState Bank and put on by Berthoud Main Street, the Town of Berthoud and the Berthoud Chamber, with the individual events run by local businesses and organizations. Main Street's page has the sign-ups for volunteers, vendors and parade floats.
