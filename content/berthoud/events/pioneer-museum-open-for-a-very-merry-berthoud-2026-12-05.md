The Berthoud Historical Society opens the Little Thompson Valley Pioneer Museum from 10 am to 4 pm on Saturday, December 5 for A Very Merry Berthoud, the town's all-day holiday festival that ends with the Parade of Lights and tree lighting downtown. The society's page does not mention an admission charge.
