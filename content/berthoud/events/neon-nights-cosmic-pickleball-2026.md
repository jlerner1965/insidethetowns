The first of two blacklight nights at the Recreation Center: a cosmic pickleball party under blacklight, glow gear encouraged, on **Friday, October 23**. Ages 16 and up; register a team through Berthoud Recreation.

The Town's printed fall activity guide put the pickleball party on Saturday the 24th and the dodgeball tournament on Friday the 23rd; the Town's Upcoming Events page and its home-page calendar, read on October 21, list them the other way round, pickleball on Friday and dodgeball on Saturday, and this listing follows those. The 6 to 9 pm time and the $45 team fee are from the printed guide; call Berthoud Recreation at 970-532-1600 if you want either settled before you go.
