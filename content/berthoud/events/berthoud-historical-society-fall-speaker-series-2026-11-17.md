November's talk is Historic Preservation in Berthoud: the Town's Historic Preservation Advisory Committee and Anne Best Johnson on the updates to the HPAC's historic preservation plan, in the community room at the recreation center, 7 to 9 pm, free. The Society brings in a speaker each month, September through November, on a topic tied to Berthoud and the Little Thompson Valley.
