November's talk is Historic Preservation in Berthoud: the Town's Historic Preservation Advisory Committee and Anne Best Johnson on the updates to the HPAC's historic preservation plan, at the Recreation Center, 7 to 9 pm, free. It is part of the Society's Fall Speaker Series, on the history of Berthoud and the Little Thompson Valley.
