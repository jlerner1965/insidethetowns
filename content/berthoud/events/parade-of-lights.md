The Berthoud Parade of Lights steps off at 5 pm on Saturday, December 5, 2026, and follows Mountain Avenue from 2nd Street to 8th Street, with carols and the tree lighting after it.

## Schedule

- **4 pm:** floats line up on 2nd Street.
- **5 pm:** the parade starts.
- **5:45 pm:** Christmas carols and the tree lighting ceremony, by the parade's own timeline. Berthoud Main Street's schedule for the day puts the tree lighting at 6 pm, so plan to stay until then.
- **6 pm:** downtown shops and restaurants are open with holiday specials.

## Route and where to watch

The parade begins on 2nd Street and follows Mountain Avenue through downtown Berthoud to 8th Street.

## Entering a float

Floats register through the form on the parade's site, [berthoudparadeoflights.com](https://berthoudparadeoflights.com/). Questions go to Kristina Portillo at 970-646-3119.

## Who runs it

The parade closes [A Very Merry Berthoud](/events/a-very-merry-berthoud/), the all-day festival presented by SouthState Bank and put on by Berthoud Main Street, the Town of Berthoud and the Berthoud Chamber. That page has the daytime schedule: the craft fair, the Main Street Market, Snowlympics and Santa.
