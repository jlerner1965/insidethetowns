A salon on the main block offering cutting, color, hair extensions and nails, with stylists who work with curly hair and a Curl Lab for specialist curly cuts. Anyone at any stage of chemotherapy gets a haircut free. Kids' cuts are booked by phone, since not every stylist does them; everything else books online. The salon asks for 24 hours' notice to change an appointment. Its site also carries [SIX Barbershop](/places/six-barbershop/), up the street at 428 Mountain Ave, on the same phone line. No opening hours are posted; call to book.
