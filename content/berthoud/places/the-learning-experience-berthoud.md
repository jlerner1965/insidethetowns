The Berthoud center of The Learning Experience, a national child care chain, takes children from six weeks to five years: infant care, toddler rooms, an early preschool class from about two and a half, and pre-K, preschool and kindergarten-readiness classes from three. It is open Monday to Friday, 6:30 am to 6 pm. For openings and tours, call or email berthoud@tlechildcare.com.
