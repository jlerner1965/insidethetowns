Old-school barbering on the main block, aimed at the men and boys of Berthoud: regular haircuts and beard and mustache maintenance, or the full service with a straight-razor shave, hot towel and waxing. The shop would rather you book online or in its app, or call. Walk-ins are taken when there's room; if nobody is free, take a buzzer and go get a coffee or a beer, and they buzz you when it's your turn. Cash and cards, Apple Pay included.

Open Tuesday to Thursday 10 to 8, Friday 10 to 6, Saturday 9 to 3 and Sunday 10 to 3; closed Monday.
