A downtown pub on the main block: burgers, sandwiches, wings and a full bar. For years it was the place most people meant when they said "meet you downtown". It closed in February 2026 ahead of the building being sold, which was confirmed by phone in October. The page stays so that anyone who looks for it finds the answer rather than a dead link.
