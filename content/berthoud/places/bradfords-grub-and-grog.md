A downtown pub on the main block: burgers, sandwiches, wings and a full bar. For years it was the place most people meant when they said "meet you downtown". A local radio station reported in February 2026 that it had closed ahead of the building being sold; the listing stays here until that is confirmed one way or the other, so that nobody arrives to a dark room.
