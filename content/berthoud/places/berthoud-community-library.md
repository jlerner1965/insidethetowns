The Berthoud Community Library District's building on Welch Avenue, a block off Mountain: storytimes on Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, a Monday writers group, a monthly book club, teen and kids' art and science programs, drop-in tech help, and the Berthoud Literary Festival each September. Open Monday to Saturday; late until 8 on Wednesday and Thursday.
