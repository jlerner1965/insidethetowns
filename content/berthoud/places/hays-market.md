The town's grocery store. Hays is a family business that started as Hays Mercantile in Johnstown in 1929 and still runs a store there. Weekly deals and digital coupons come through the Hays Market app, scanned at checkout. Open every day from 6 am to 9 pm; closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Easter.
