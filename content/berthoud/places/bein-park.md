Berthoud's soccer fields, at Spartan Avenue and 10th Street near Berthoud High School, with a walking and running trail around them and fitness stations along the way. The renovated playground is on the northwest side, the Bein baseball and softball complex is part of the park, and there are permanent restrooms. The Town lists Bein Park among the parks where dogs may be off leash for exercise. The Town's fenced dog park, opened in 2020 with separate sections for small and large dogs, water stations and a covered shelter (no restrooms), is also on 10th Street.
