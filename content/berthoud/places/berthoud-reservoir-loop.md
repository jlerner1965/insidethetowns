The Town opened Berthoud Reservoir to the public in 2020 with a walking trail, a restroom, paddle-craft access, fishing and a parking area, and lists the site as ADA accessible; more amenities are planned. Fishing is allowed only on the west portion of the reservoir, and the eastern cell is closed to the public. Anglers find rainbow trout, largemouth bass, black crappie and sunfish; a Colorado fishing license is required from age 16. The reservoir is not one of the Town's off-leash parks, so dogs stay on a leash.
