The Town opened Berthoud Reservoir to the public in 2020 with a walking trail around the water, a restroom, paddle-craft access, fishing and a parking area. The loop is 1.2 miles and nearly flat, with the foothills to the west. No fee or permit; dogs on leash. A master plan for more passive amenities is in progress.
