TPC Colorado is an 18-hole, par-72 championship course in Berthoud, laid out beside Lonetree, McNeil and Welch reservoirs and long enough, at over 7,991 yards from the back, for the Korn Ferry Tour's The Blue Championship; forward tees play from 4,157 yards. It is a members' club with a limited number of public tee times: non-members can book up to seven days ahead, Monday to Thursday after 11 am and Friday to Sunday after 1 pm, subject to availability, by calling (970) 663-5063.
