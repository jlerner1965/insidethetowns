Opened in November 2021 at Waggener Farm Park, the Town's first recreation center has a gymnasium, a fitness center, a climbing wall, and an aquatics area with a three-lane lap pool and a two-story water slide, plus a schedule of classes, lap swim and open gym that runs every day. Day passes and memberships; SilverSneakers and similar plans accepted. It closes for a maintenance week each September and hosts the Father Daughter Dance, Neon Nights and the December craft fair.

**Pool hours, school year 2026–27** (the Town's schedule for August 20, 2026 to May 28, 2027):

- **Lap swim:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday 5 am–4 pm (one lane only 10:45–11:45 on Wednesday and Friday); Tuesday and Thursday 5 am–4:15 pm and 7–8 pm; Saturday 7–11 am; none on Sunday.
- **Open swim**, every feature running, no lap lanes: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 4–8 pm; Saturday and Sunday 11 am–7 pm; none on Tuesday or Thursday.
- **Family swim**, the play pool and lazy river without the slide or climbing wall: Monday to Friday 9 am–4 pm (to 4:30 on Tuesday and Thursday).
- **Water walking** in the lazy river: weekdays 5–9 am, adults only; Tuesday and Thursday also 4:30–8 pm; Saturday 7–11 am.
- **Spa:** Monday to Friday 5 am–8 pm, Saturday 7 am–7 pm, Sunday 11 am–7 pm. Ages 6 and up; 6 to 13 with an adult.

The whole pool closes at 5 pm on the first Sunday of each month for lifeguard training, and the spa at 6 pm on the second and fourth Thursday. School-holiday hours differ; the Town posts them, with open gym and the day-by-day swim calendar, on [its facility schedules page](https://www.berthoud.org/1521/Facility-Schedules).
