The Berthoud branch of a Fort Collins practice, opened in November 2024 in the complex with UCHealth Berthoud, Starbucks, Bank of Colorado and Maverik. It takes new clients and always keeps same-day appointments open. Sick visits and urgent care sit alongside vaccinations, wellness checkups, puppy and kitten care, surgery, dental work, allergies and oncology, with X-rays, ultrasound and blood work done in house.

The phone line is shared with the Fort Collins clinic: press 2 for Berthoud. Appointments can also be booked online from the clinic's page. Open weekdays 8 to 6 and Saturdays 8 to 2.
