The small shaded park on Mountain Avenue between downtown and Town Hall, with sculptures by local artists (the bear is the landmark), picnic tables, a lawn for small gatherings, a tennis court with lights and portable restrooms. The sculptures include Harley and Second Chance, and some rotate; the Berthoud Arts & Humanities Alliance maintains the sculpture walk. The obvious picnic stop on a downtown walk.
