Built in 1916 by Dr. and Mrs. D.W. McCarty in the Denver Square style and later the Fickel family's home, the house on 7th Street was deeded to the Berthoud Historical Society in July 2008 and is kept with its furnishings and the doctor's 1930s office. Tours by appointment; $5, children 11 and under free.

The Historical Society says the home closes for the season on November 8, 2026, and reopens in spring 2027.
