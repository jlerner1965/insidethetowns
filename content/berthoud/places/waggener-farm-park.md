The town's largest active park, on Berthoud Parkway on the north side, sharing its site with the Recreation Center. A farm-themed playground with a tractor and playhouse, a wavy scooter path, a full-size basketball court, a skate park, multi-use fields, and the site of the Zombie Fun Run in October. The Town is planning the Berthoud Arboretum here: bids came in on April 7, 2026, the Board of Trustees asked for cost reductions, and the Town's projects page gives no construction dates yet.
