A park of more than 20 acres on Berthoud Parkway on the north side, sharing its site with the Recreation Center. Large turf fields, a concrete skate park, a pavilion, four-season restrooms, playgrounds for several ages, a basketball court, a miniature pump track, and trails that wind through native grass and a farm field. The Town is planning the Berthoud Arboretum here: bids came in on April 7, 2026, the Board of Trustees asked for cost reductions, and the Town's projects page gives no construction dates yet.
