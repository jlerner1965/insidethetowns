The family-owned coffee house on the main block: coffee, breakfast and lunch seven days a week, with seats outside in warmer weather. It is open 7:30 am to 2:30 pm Monday to Saturday and 8 am to 2:30 pm on Sunday. On Thursday evenings it serves an Italian dinner from 5 to 8. Call to order ahead for pickup.
