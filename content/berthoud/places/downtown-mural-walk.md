Historic downtown Berthoud is a walkable grid of brick storefronts and converted houses, and Berthoud Main Street, the Berthoud Arts & Humanities Alliance and their partners have filled it with large-scale murals, a rotating sculpture program, and the Bimson leaf motif cast into the sidewalks and worked into the lamp posts. Each year from about Memorial Day to about Veterans Day, the Town's Hometown Heroes banners honoring service members hang from the downtown streetlight poles. Start at the Pioneer Museum and walk west; Main Street's tour page lists the murals, it is free to explore, and a free Art in Public Places brochure is at Homestead Fine Art Gallery, City Star Brewing and The Rancher's Wife.
