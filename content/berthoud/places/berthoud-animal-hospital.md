A full-service veterinary clinic for dogs and cats, serving Berthoud and Loveland: wellness exams, vaccinations, parasite prevention, dental care, spay and neuter, surgery, internal medicine and emergency care consultations. It offers membership plans, and current clients can order medication and request refills online. Open weekdays 8 am to 5:30 pm; closed weekends.
