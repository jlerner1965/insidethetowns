The big water above Berthoud: a reservoir three miles long and about a mile wide in the foothills northwest of town, run by Larimer County Natural Resources with a marina, boat ramps, campgrounds, a swim beach from Memorial Day weekend to mid-September, year-round fishing, scuba and rock climbing, and about 1,000 acres of public land around it, with the three-mile Sundance Trail along the west side of the lake. An entrance permit is required: buy one at the entrance station, or a daily permit at the automated pay stations on South County Road 31 and West County Road 8E, which take cards only. Boats must be inspected for aquatic nuisance species before launching. In August 2026, Colorado Parks and Wildlife confirmed New Zealand mudsnails in Carter Lake: clean, drain and dry boats, paddleboards and fishing gear, and expect a boat leaving the lake to need inspection, and possibly decontamination, before it goes into other water. Flatiron and Pinewood reservoirs, also the County's, are to the north, off County Roads 31 and 18E.
