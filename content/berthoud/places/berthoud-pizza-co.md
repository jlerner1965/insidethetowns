Pizza, wings, stromboli and low-carb cauliflower pizza bowls from a small shop on North 4th Street, a few blocks north of downtown, for carry-out and delivery. The crust uses unbleached, unbromated flour, and the chorizo and Italian sausage are made in house. Orders are taken until 8 pm; breakfast sandwiches from 9 am. Closed Easter, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.
