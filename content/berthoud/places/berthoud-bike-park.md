Opened in late summer 2023, the 20-acre Town bike park on Meadowlark Drive has two long jump and flow lines, a quarter-mile dual slalom track, about a mile of cross-country singletrack, a bicycle playground for small riders, and a roughly 20,000-square-foot Velosolutions asphalt pump track, with parking and restrooms. The Town closes the park when it is wet, muddy, snowy or icy, for maintenance and for permitted events; its clay soil drains slowly, so trails can stay closed after they look dry from the road. Check the [Berthoud Bike Park Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BerthoudBikePark/) for conditions and closures before you go.
