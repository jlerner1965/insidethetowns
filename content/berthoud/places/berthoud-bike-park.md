Opened in late summer 2023, the 20-acre Town bike park on Meadowlark Drive has two long jump and flow lines, a quarter-mile dual slalom track, about a mile of cross-country singletrack, a bicycle playground for small riders, and a roughly 20,000-square-foot Velosolutions asphalt pump track. Open dawn to dusk, conditions permitting; no fee. Check the Town page for closures after rain.
