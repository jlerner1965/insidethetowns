The Derby Grille is a bar and grill open for lunch and dinner every day, Monday to Thursday 11 am to 11 pm and to 2 am Friday and Saturday, Sunday to midnight; it says hours may run later on weeknights, so call to confirm. Happy hour is Monday to Friday, 2 to 6 pm, with a dollar off domestic pints, well drinks and wine, and live bands play inside on Saturdays from April to September.
