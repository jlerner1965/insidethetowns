Berthoud Recreation's holiday get-together for its active adult (50 and over) program, and all are welcome. It is free; bring a favorite dish, side or dessert to share, with a copy of the recipe or ingredients for anyone with dietary needs, and refreshments are provided. The Town asks for registration through its winter activity guide.
