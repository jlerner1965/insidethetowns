The [Pioneer Museum](/places/pioneer-museum/) is open Wednesday to Saturday afternoons and children 11 and under are free. This fall's **Holiday Card Art Contest**, on a Winter Wonderland theme for kindergarten to 8th grade, [closed to entries on November 14](/events/holiday-card-art-contest-deadline-2026-11-14/); the winning artwork becomes the community's holiday card and joins the museum's collection. Members get a discount on summer day camp tuition, the Society says; its summer workshop page has no 2026 dates.
