The [Pioneer Museum](/places/pioneer-museum/) is open Wednesday to Saturday afternoons and children 11 and under are free. This fall's **Holiday Card Art Contest** has a Winter Wonderland theme, in two divisions, kindergarten to 5th grade and 6th to 8th: any two-dimensional medium on 8.5 by 11 inch paper, with a submission form a parent or guardian fills in. The winning artwork becomes the community's holiday card and joins the museum's collection, and the museum's Winter Wonderland Art Station has forms and folders so children can make an entry there; [artwork is due November 14](/events/holiday-card-art-contest-deadline-2026-11-14/). Members get a discount on summer day camp tuition, the Society says; its summer workshop page has no 2026 dates.
