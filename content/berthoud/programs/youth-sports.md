The Town runs its own recreational leagues, and the printed activity guide is the registration form: each program has a "more info / register" link into the Town's portal. **Fall** (registration from July 1): co-ed soccer 5 to 14 and Little Strikers at 4–5, boys' flag football 6 to 11 and a new girls' flag league 8 to 12, girls' softball 7 to 14, co-ed volleyball for 3rd to 8th grade, and CARA cross country 7 to 14. **Winter**: Little Dribblers at 4–5 and 1st/2nd-grade basketball (practices from October 19 and 28; both had an October 4 deadline), a new 7th–8th grade girls' basketball league with Friday-night games from January 17, and an eight-session **futsal clinic** for ages 8 to 12 (deadline November 1). Register based on the child's age on the first day of the season; the guide gives the date for each. Every league in the fall guide carried a $15 increase after its early-bird month, which the Town's Activity Guide page says applies to each season's first month.

The **winter activity guide** goes online November 1, and registration for its programs begins the same day. Youth **Power Hour**, a Wednesday 7 am strength and agility class for young athletes, runs year-round from the fitness side.
