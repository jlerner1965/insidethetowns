The library's October calendar lists a run of after-school sessions: **Tinker Tots** (a science hour, registration by phone), **Construct & Concoct** (a craft hour), **Budding Artists** (registration by phone), a family show, and **Donut Bug Me, I'm Reading!**, a weekly Wednesday club for middle-school readers with donuts provided, registration by phone. The library posts each month's dates on its [events and classes page](https://www.berthoudcommunitylibrary.org/events-classes); the ones on this guide's calendar are under [Family](/events/?category=family).
