Membership brings four newsletters a year, free admission to the [Pioneer Museum](/places/pioneer-museum/) and the McCarty-Fickel Home, early access to event tickets and a discount on summer day camp tuition, in the Society's words. The volunteer page lists nine areas and three ways to sign up. The public way in is the free speaker series at the [Berthoud Recreation Center](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/). The 2026 series, seven evening talks in winter, spring and fall, ended on November 17 with [historic preservation in Berthoud](/events/berthoud-historical-society-fall-speaker-series-2026-11-17/), and the Society says its 2027 lineup is coming soon.
