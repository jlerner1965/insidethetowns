Membership brings four newsletters a year, free admission to the [Pioneer Museum](/places/pioneer-museum/) and the McCarty-Fickel Home, early access to event tickets and a discount on the summer day camp. The volunteer page lists nine areas and three ways to sign up. The public way in is the free [fall speaker series](/events/berthoud-historical-society-fall-speaker-series-2026-11-17/): November 17 at 7 pm is historic preservation in Berthoud, with the Town's preservation committee.
