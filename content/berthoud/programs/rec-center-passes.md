The pass prices are the Town's memberships page's, checked November 8, 2026, and agree with its [winter 2026–27 activity guide](https://www.berthoud.org/DocumentCenter/View/7956/Berthoud-Recreation-Winter-Activity-Guide-2026-27); the two give different annual membership prices, so only the monthly ones are repeated here. The school-break hours are the winter guide's: over **Thanksgiving week** the pool runs open swim noon to 8 on November 23, 24 and 25, the whole center is closed on the 26th, and on the 27th it opens noon to 6 with open swim noon to 5. Winter break, New Year's and the spring days off are on the [Recreation Center's listing](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/). The regular school-year pool schedule, lap, open and family swim by day, is on the [Recreation Center's listing](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/) and the Town's [facility schedules page](https://www.berthoud.org/1521/Facility-Schedules). The whole pool closes at 5 pm on the first Sunday of each month for lifeguard training.
