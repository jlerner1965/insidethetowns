The pass prices and the school-break hours are from the Town's fall activity guide. On school days off the pool runs extended open swim: **November 2** noon to 8 pm (a Thompson staff day), and over **Thanksgiving week** noon to 8 on November 23 and 25, noon to 7 on the 24th, closed on the 26th, and noon to 5 on the 27th. The regular school-year pool schedule, lap, open and family swim by day, is on the [Recreation Center's listing](/places/berthoud-recreation-center/) and the Town's [facility schedules page](https://www.berthoud.org/1521/Facility-Schedules). The whole pool closes at 5 pm on the first Sunday of each month for lifeguard training.
