Berthoud's Learn to Swim program runs in nine levels from Parent/Baby–Tot (six months to three years, with a grown-up in the water) through preschool Dolphin and Stingray, the numbered levels for school-age swimmers, and an adult water introduction. The fall activity guide gives the blocks and prices; unsure of the level, the aquatics coordinator offers a free swim evaluation. The Recreation Center also runs a recreational **CARA swim team** for ages 6 to 18 on Tuesdays and Thursdays (session 2 is October 20 to December 17, $88 to $92 resident), **babysitter training** for ages 11 to 16 (next November 14, $85 resident), and a **lifeguard boot camp** for 11 to 16-year-olds (Fridays November 6 to 20, $50 resident).
