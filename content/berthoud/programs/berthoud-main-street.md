A 501(c)(3) dedicated to downtown, in its own words, working in partnership with local businesses, property owners and residents. Its calendar is most of the downtown year: the [scarecrow contest](/events/berthoud-scarecrow-contest-2026-10-17/) through October 31, Trick or Treat Street on October 24, [Small Business Saturday](/events/small-business-saturday-2026/) and [A Very Merry Berthoud](/events/a-very-merry-berthoud-2026/) on December 5. Its site does not say how to join or volunteer; it has a contact form and an email list.
