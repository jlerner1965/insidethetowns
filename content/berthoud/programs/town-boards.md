**Speak to the Board.** Any attendee may address the Board of Trustees for three minutes on a topic not on the agenda; sign the sheet in the meeting room. Public hearings take three minutes a person on the item. Meetings stream on the Town's YouTube channel, and agendas and minutes are on its [CivicClerk portal](https://berthoudco.portal.civicclerk.com/). The next is [October 27](/events/town-board-2026-10-27/).

**Serve on a committee.** The Parks, Open Space, Recreation and Trails committee, the Planning Commission, the Tree Advisory Committee, the Historic Preservation Advisory Committee and the [Youth Advisory Commission](https://berthoud.org/271/Youth-Advisory-Commission) are filled through the one [application form](https://www.berthoud.org/FormCenter/General-Forms-4/Advisory-Committee-Application-95); the Board interviews and appoints. The youth commission, formed in 2017, writes an annual report to the Board and meets monthly at Town Hall.
