**Speak to the Board.** Any attendee may address the Board of Trustees for three minutes on a topic not on the agenda; sign the sheet in the meeting room. Public hearings take three minutes a person on the item. Meetings stream on the Town's YouTube channel, and agendas and minutes are on its [CivicClerk portal](https://berthoudco.portal.civicclerk.com/). The next are [November 10](/events/town-board-2026-11-10/), [November 24](/events/town-board-2026-11-24/) and [December 8](/events/town-board-2026-12-08/).

**Serve on a committee.** The Historic Preservation Advisory Committee, the Local Licensing Authority, the Parks, Open Space, Recreation and Trails committee, the Planning Commission, the Tree Advisory Committee, the new Public Art Advisory Committee and the [Youth Advisory Commission](https://berthoud.org/271/Youth-Advisory-Commission) are all filled through the one [application form](https://www.berthoud.org/FormCenter/General-Forms-4/Advisory-Committee-Application-95), and the Board of Trustees appoints the members. The youth commission, formed in 2017, writes an annual report to the Board and meets monthly at Town Hall.
