The Berthoud Fire Protection District runs **Firefighter for a Day Kids Camp**, a day spent with the district's firefighters, for Berthoud resident children aged 7 to 12. The district's community outreach page says the camp returns on **Wednesday, August 4, 2027**, and that registration opens next summer, announced on the district's [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Berthoud-Fire-Protection-District-100064603483190/). The page does not give the hours, the place or a cost; the district is at 248 Welch Avenue, 970-532-2264.
