The Town's fall activity guide lists a **futsal clinic**, the fast indoor version of soccer, for co-ed players aged 8 to 12: ball control, movement on and off the ball and the rules of the game, in eight one-hour sessions over four weeks on Monday and Wednesday evenings, with practice times set by age at registration. It costs $60 for a resident and $70 for a non-resident, and the guide gives a registration deadline of **November 1**. The guide does not give the clinic's start date or its location; Berthoud Recreation, 970-532-1600, can say.

The fall guide is linked from the Town's [Activity Guide page](https://www.berthoud.org/1598/Activity-Guide), which says the winter guide goes online, and its registration opens, on November 1. Read October 26, 2026.
