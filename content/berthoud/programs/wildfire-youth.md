The nonprofit arts center on Massachusetts Avenue runs the [U-Create open studio](/events/u-create-open-studio-at-wildfire-arts-center/) on Saturday mornings, where it supplies the materials and an instructor suggests a project, and [Brush & Banter](/events/brush-and-banter-class-and-open-studio/) on Monday evenings for 14 and up. Its **Youth Street Art Program**, for ages 14 and up, designs and paints a collaborative community mural with the center at New Freedom Church; the center's page does not give a schedule or a price, so email or call to register. The fall activity guide also lists its beginner line dancing, watercolor and ukulele series for adults and 14-plus, each with a minimum class size. Memberships (individual $40, family $100) and a [volunteer application](https://www.wildfirearts.org/get-involved) are on its site.
