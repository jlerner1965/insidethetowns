The nonprofit arts center on Massachusetts Avenue runs the [U-Create open studio](/events/u-create-open-studio-at-wildfire-arts-center/) on Saturday mornings, where it supplies the materials and an instructor suggests a project; it breaks for November 28, December 26 and January 2 and is listed through May 15. **After-school art clubs** of five to ten children meet once a week by age: 5 to 7 on Tuesdays and 8 to 10 on Thursdays (4:15 to 5:15), 11 to 14 on Wednesdays (4:15 to 5:15) and 14 to 18 on Mondays (4 to 5:30); the fall clubs end the week of December 14 and the spring clubs start January 11, at $50 with materials. There is also a homeschool art class for 5 to 10 on Tuesday mornings or Friday afternoons ($165), and preschool art workshops for 3 to 5 ($10). [Brush & Banter](/events/brush-and-banter-class-and-open-studio/) is a $5 Monday-evening drop-in for 14 and up. The **Youth Street Art Program**, for ages 14 and up, designs and paints a collaborative community mural with the center at New Freedom Church; its page gives no schedule or price, so email or call to register. Scholarships for classes are available through programs@wildfirearts.org. Memberships (individual $40, family $100) and a [volunteer application](https://www.wildfirearts.org/get-involved) are on its site.
