The [library](/places/berthoud-community-library/) takes volunteers for its team or for a single event; fill in the adult or the youth (under 18) application and hand it in at the desk. The [Friends of the Berthoud Community Library](https://www.bclfriends.org/) is the nonprofit that funds programs the district's budget does not cover, mostly through book sales; its site gives an email and a Facebook page but no membership form or meeting date. The library's Board of Trustees meets at the library on the second Wednesday of the month at 7 pm, open to the public.
