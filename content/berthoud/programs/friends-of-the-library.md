The [library](/places/berthoud-community-library/) takes volunteers for its team or for a single event; fill in the adult (18 and over) or the youth (under 18) application and hand it in at the desk. The **Friends of the Berthoud Community Library** is the nonprofit that raises money for what property tax does not cover, such as the Summer Learning Program, the Fairy Gardens activity, bookshelves and signage; the library's page says it needs leadership candidates and volunteers, and the way in is an email to FOBCLD01@gmail.com, a word with library staff or its Facebook page. The library's Board of Trustees meets at the library on the second Wednesday of the month at 6:30 pm unless otherwise posted, open to the public; the board's page and its October agenda give 6:30, and the library's home page still says 7.
