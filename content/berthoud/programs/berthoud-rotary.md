The club's own page gives two meetings a month: a business meeting in the conference room at [Cornerstone Cafe](/places/cornerstone-cafe/) at noon on the second Wednesday, and a social in the barrel room at [City Star](/places/city-star-brewing/) at 5:30 on the fourth. Membership and dues go through the Rotary Club of Loveland. The page carries no date, so confirm the meeting before you go.
