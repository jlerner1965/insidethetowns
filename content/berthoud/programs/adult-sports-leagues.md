The Town runs four adult leagues this winter, each a six-week season that ends in a single-elimination tournament. Registration opened November 1.

- **Co-ed pickleball**: Monday matches between 6:30 and 9 pm from January 11, played indoors in the Recreation Center gym. A team can be any pair: two men, two women, or a man and a woman. $65 a team; register by January 3.
- **Men's basketball**: Tuesday games between 6:30 and 10:30 pm from January 12, all at the Recreation Center, six regular-season games before the tournament. Players must be 16 or older. $550 a team; register by December 20.
- **Co-ed volleyball**: Wednesday matches between 6:30 and 10:30 pm from January 13, for all skill levels. Each team needs at least three women. $335 a team; register by December 20.
- **Women's volleyball**: Thursday matches between 6:30 and 10:30 pm from January 14. $335 a team; register by December 20.

No team? The Town's [free agent sign-up](https://berthoud.org/1559/Free-Agent-Sign-Up), for players 16 and older, passes your name to a sports coordinator, who contacts you when a team needs players.
