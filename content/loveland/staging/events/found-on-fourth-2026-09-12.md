Found on Fourth highlights the ongoing revitalization of Fourth Street in downtown Loveland, offering viewers a rare glimpse into the transformation happening beneath their streets. The exhibition features photography by local artist Michael Simms, who has been documenting each stage of construction. His images provide an intimate look at the changing street, the people behind the work, and moments of beauty found in an active construction zone.

During excavation, crews uncovered a variety of small, everyday objects long buried beneath Fourth Street. Recovered from the soil and showcased in the exhibition, these items reveal traces of the community’s past and remind visitors that even ordinary things can hold surprising stories.
