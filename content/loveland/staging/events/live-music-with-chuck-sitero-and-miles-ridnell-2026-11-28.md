Chuck Sitero is a Lyons-based songwriter and the lead singer of High Lonesome, a nationally touring bluegrass band named "Top Ten Colorado Bluegrass Bands” (Denver Westword, 2024 & 2025). For more than three decades, Chuck has fronted acclaimed bluegrass bands, bringing his soulful vocals and heartfelt songwriting to stages across the country. In addition to his work with High Lonesome, Chuck loves playing duo shows, where he weaves together his own originals with timeless favorites from artists like Paul Simon, Jerry Garcia, John Hartford, Bob Dylan, Rodney Crowell, The Beatles, and more—showcasing the full range of his musical passions beyond bluegrass.

He'll be joined by Miles Ridnell on the upright bass who will also lend his vocals.

As always, Loveland Aleworks live music is free to attend.
