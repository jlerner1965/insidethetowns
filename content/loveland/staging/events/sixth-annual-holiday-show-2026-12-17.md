Sixth Annual Holiday Concert
A Loveland tradition filled with music, connection, and holiday cheer.

Step away from the holiday hustle and settle in for an evening that brings the season to life.

Join us December 17 from 6–9 PM for our Sixth Annual Holiday Concert, featuring Loveland’s beloved Dave Beegle, with special guests Valerie Vampola and Billy Scully. Enjoy holiday favorites and standout solo performances in an intimate setting where you can truly appreciate the talent of these incredible local musicians.

Now in its sixth year, this concert is a chance to gather as a community, celebrate local artistry, and make time for the people and traditions that make the holidays meaningful. Whether it’s your first time attending or a cherished part of your December, we invite you to make it part of your holiday season.

Your ticket includes a complimentary specialty beverage to start the evening. A cash bar will also offer beer, wine, and a festive holiday cocktail menu.

Bring someone special, gather your friends, or plan a holiday outing with family. Choose from theater-style seating or tables for two, four, or six to make the evening your own.

Seating is limited. Reserve your tickets and give yourself an evening to slow down, and enjoy the spirit of the holidays.
