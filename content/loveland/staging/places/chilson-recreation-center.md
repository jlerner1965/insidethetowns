The City of Loveland's recreation center on 4th Street, near downtown. Inside are cardio and strength equipment and a separate weight room, two gymnasiums with drop-in basketball, volleyball and pickleball, racquetball courts, an indoor walking and running track, and a lap pool beside a leisure pool with a kids' play area, a lazy river and two hot tubs. Group exercise classes run every day and come with a pass or the daily fee. Personal trainers, nutrition coaching and childcare are also offered.
