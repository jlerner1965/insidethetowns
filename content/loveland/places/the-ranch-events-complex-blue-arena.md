Blue Arena is home to the Colorado Eagles and books concerts, monster trucks and family shows, while the Pedersen Toyota Center and other buildings host trade shows, festivals and equine and livestock events; the Ranch also hosts the Larimer County Fair & PRCA Rodeo. Parking for Blue Arena events is sold through ParkWhiz, and the box office on the west side of the arena handles will call one hour before events. Leashed dogs are allowed outdoors only, not in the buildings unless they are with an event or are service animals.
