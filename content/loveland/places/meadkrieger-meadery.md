MeadKrieger lists Colorado Governor's Cup medals for several of its meads, including a 2025 gold for its orange blossom mead. It keeps a calendar of events, offers private rentals, and sells its meads online. Its hours page lists winter hours from Sunday, November 22, 2026: Wednesday and Thursday 4 to 8 pm, Friday 4 to 9 pm, Saturday noon to 9 pm and Sunday noon to 4 pm. Wednesday, November 18 ends at 6 pm for a ticketed talk.

Mead is honey wine. The lineup runs from traditional and oak-aged meads to fruit versions such as raspberry, blueberry and cherry, plus orange blossom and spiced; the menu on the meadery's site lists the current pours and food.
