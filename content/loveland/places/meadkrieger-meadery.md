MeadKrieger lists Colorado Governor's Cup medals for several of its meads, including a 2025 gold for its orange blossom mead. It keeps a calendar of events, offers private rentals, and sells its meads online. Its hours page notes that winter hours begin on Sunday, November 22, 2026, with closing one hour earlier, and that Wednesday, November 18 ends at 6 pm for a ticketed talk.

Mead is honey wine. The lineup runs from traditional and oak-aged meads to fruit versions such as raspberry, blueberry and cherry, plus orange blossom and spiced; the menu on the meadery's site lists the current pours and food.
