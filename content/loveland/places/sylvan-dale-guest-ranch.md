A historic working horse and cattle ranch in the foothills west of Loveland, run by the same family since 1946, an easy drive to Estes Park and Rocky Mountain National Park. It takes guests for dude-ranch vacations, bunk-and-breakfast stays and ranch lodging, with horseback riding and fishing, and hosts company retreats and weddings. It also runs holiday packages, such as a three-night Thanksgiving weekend with the feast and ranch meals.
