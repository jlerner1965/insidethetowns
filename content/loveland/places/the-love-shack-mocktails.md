The basement has coin-operated pool tables, electronic darts, giant Jenga and a themed Dungeon Gaming Room, and the semi-private Pink Cloud Room loft is set up for quieter conversation. The main event space can be rented for parties and celebrations, and event tickets are sold on the site. The Love Shack describes itself as LGBTQ+ owned.
