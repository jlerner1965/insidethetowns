Founder Mike Bernard named the restaurant after his mother, Justine; today long-time customers Amy Hummel and her son Jack run it, keeping the recipes and adding a refreshed full bar. It is open for dinner Tuesday to Sunday from 4 pm, and the site promotes Wine Wednesday and Give Back Tuesday.
