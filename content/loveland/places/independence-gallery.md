Independence Gallery shows paintings, sculpture, pottery, jewelry, photography and more by Northern Colorado artists, and new work rotates in regularly. Its framing shop handles art, photographs and keepsakes Tuesday through Friday, and the gallery recommends calling 970-231-5748 to book a framing appointment. Sunday opening is not guaranteed, so call ahead for a Sunday visit.
