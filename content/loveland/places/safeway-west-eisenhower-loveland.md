The Safeway at 2321 W. Eisenhower Boulevard, open every day from 6 am to 11 pm. The pharmacy keeps shorter hours: weekdays 9 to 8, Saturday 9 to 5 and Sunday 10 to 5, closed each day from 1:30 to 2. There is a bakery for custom cakes, and orders can be picked up through DriveUp & Go or delivered. SNAP EBT is accepted in the store and online.
