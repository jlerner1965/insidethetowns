The Recreation Trail is the City of Loveland's paved loop, in seven sections: the Big Thompson River Trail (Rossum Drive to US 287), the Civic Center Trail (US 287 to North Denver Avenue), the Boyd Lake Trail (North Denver Avenue to County Road 11C), the North Trail (County Road 11C to North Taft Avenue), the Westside Trail (North Taft Avenue to US 34), the Centerra Trail and the East Big Thompson Trail. It joins the Front Range Trail north from County Road 11C and the Long View Trail north from West 57th Street and North Taft Avenue to Fort Collins. Maps are at the Loveland Visitor Center, Boyd Lake State Park, the Chilson Recreation Center, bike shops and the Parks & Recreation office, which also hands out a Ticket to Trails passport for visiting all seven sections. The City counts more than 20 miles of soft-surface trails in its natural areas as well.
