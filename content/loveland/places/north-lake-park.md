The City of Loveland's park on the shore of Lake Loveland, at Taft Avenue and 29th Street beside Loveland High School. It has playgrounds, baseball, softball and athletic fields, tennis and basketball courts, horseshoe pits, drinking fountains with a jug fill, and the Norma & Lynn Hammond Amphitheater, a grass-seated stage that holds about 300. The Buckhorn Northern Railroad, a miniature train run by the Loveland Lions Club, gives rides in the summer season, Memorial Day to Labor Day.

Fishing is from the shore only, on the lake and the pond. Boating is not allowed: the surface rights belong to the Lake Loveland homeowners' association, and the water itself is the City of Greeley's.

Four shelters, all accessible, can be reserved through the City; each has tables, two grills, electrical outlets and a light, and holds up to 100 people. Courts and fields are booked by phone at least 24 hours ahead. In winter the flush restrooms by the tennis courts (900 Logan Drive) close, and accessible portable restrooms stand at North Lake Main, 2626 N. Taft Avenue.
