The City of Loveland's recreation center on 4th Street, near downtown. Inside are cardio and strength equipment and a separate weight room, two gymnasiums with drop-in basketball, volleyball and pickleball, racquetball courts, an indoor walking and running track, and a lap pool beside a leisure pool with a kids' play area, a lazy river and two hot tubs. Group exercise classes run every day and come with a pass or the daily fee. Personal trainers and nutrition coaching are also offered.

The center is open weekdays 5:30 am to 9 pm, Saturday 6 to 6 and Sunday 11 to 6. The pool area closes half an hour before the building, and from September to May the leisure pool closes on weekdays from 1 to 4. Childcare runs weekday mornings 8:45 to 1:30, Monday to Thursday 4 to 7, and Saturday 9 to noon from September to May. The Senior Center in the same building is open weekdays 8 to 5.

It closes on New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, keeps shorter hours on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and shuts for a week of maintenance early each fall. Most passes, classes and activities can be booked online at any hour or at the front desk; the City asks you to check the desk for the latest hours and prices. Call (970) 962-2FUN (2386).
