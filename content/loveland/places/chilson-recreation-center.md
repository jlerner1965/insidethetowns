The City of Loveland's recreation center on 4th Street, near downtown. Inside are cardio and strength equipment and a separate weight room, two gymnasiums with drop-in basketball, volleyball and pickleball, racquetball courts, an indoor walking and running track, a lap pool beside a leisure pool with a kids' play area, a lazy river and two hot tubs, a steam room, and lockers. Group exercise classes run every day and come with a pass or the daily fee. Personal trainers and nutrition coaching are also offered.

The center is open weekdays 5:30 am to 9 pm, Saturday 6 to 6 and Sunday 11 to 6. The pool area closes half an hour before the building, and from September to May the leisure pool closes on weekdays from 1 to 4. Childcare runs weekday mornings 8:45 to 1:30, Monday to Thursday 4 to 7, and Saturday 9 to noon from September to May. The Senior Center in the same building is open weekdays 8 to 5.

A day visit in 2026 is $7.50 for adults 19 to 61, $6.25 for 62 and over, $6 for ages 6 to 18 and $5 for ages 2 to 5; children 1 and under get in free with a paying adult. A 10-visit card is $67.50 for an adult ($56 for seniors, $54 for youth, $45 for toddlers). A one-year adult pass is $496 for Loveland city residents and $574 for everyone else, and a family pass (one or two adults and their children under 21 living at home) is $750 or $860; three-month, six-month and monthly-billing passes are sold too, and pass holders can add the Winona outdoor pool in summer or childcare. Childcare on its own is $3.75 a child for up to two hours. SilverSneakers, Renew Active and One Pass memberships are accepted.

It closes on New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, keeps shorter hours on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and shuts for a week of maintenance early each fall. Most passes, classes and activities can be booked online at any hour or at the front desk; the City asks you to check the desk for the latest hours and prices. Call (970) 962-2FUN (2386).
