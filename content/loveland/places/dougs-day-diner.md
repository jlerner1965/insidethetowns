Breakfast runs to omelets, breakfast burritos, hotcakes and the house Papas; lunch is burgers, sandwiches and salads. Sauces, soups, hash browns, fries, chips, syrup and dressings are made in-house. It is closed most holidays, and the site offers online ordering and a waitlist.
