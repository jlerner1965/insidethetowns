Opened in 2007, Chapungu displays 82 large stone sculptures by Zimbabwean artisans across natural and landscaped gardens, grouped around themes from Shona culture such as family, village life and the elders. Trails of concrete and crushed rock follow the Greeley and Loveland Irrigation Canal and cross bridges, and the whole park is handicap accessible. Admission is free; the Great Lawn hosts summer concerts and can be booked for events.
