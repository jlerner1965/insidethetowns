The Thompson Valley Art League runs the Lincoln Gallery, where updated work from its exhibiting members goes on display in rotation, and it offers art classes and workshops. The league also stages national, regional and local fine art shows each year and is home to Art in the Park, which it calls Loveland's longest-running art festival. The gallery invites visitors to opening receptions during downtown's Night on the Town.
