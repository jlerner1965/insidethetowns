The dinner menu centers on hand-selected steaks, fresh seafood and a considered wine list. Tables can be reserved online, and there is private dining. It is closed on the Fourth of July, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, and reservations are required on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
