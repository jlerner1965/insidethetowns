Beans are ethically traded and roasted in small batches, with seasonal origins from around the world. Everything is made fresh, so the shop invites customers to call or email orders ahead. The Foundry café also opens for downtown events in the Foundry Plaza, and a second location is at Centerra (1450 N Boyd Lake Ave).
