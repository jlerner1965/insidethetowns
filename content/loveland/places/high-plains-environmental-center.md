High Plains Environmental Center restores native ecosystems within the suburban landscape and manages 275 acres of open space and trails for recreation and wildlife habitat. Its demonstration gardens show what a native-plant garden can look like, and its offices, open by appointment, hold a small library, museum and classroom. The native plant nursery sells online for in-person pickup, with occasional on-site pop-up sales, and the center runs classes, events and volunteer days.
