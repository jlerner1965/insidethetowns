A non-smoking hotel on Peralta Drive off I-25, minutes from the Promenade Shops at Centerra. Free breakfast, WiFi and parking, a fitness center, guest laundry and event space, and a front desk staffed around the clock. Rooms have a mini-fridge, microwave and coffee maker. Dogs and cats stay for an extra fee.
