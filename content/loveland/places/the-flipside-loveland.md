Monday is unlimited-play night with one cover ($15 at the door, $10 after 9:30); Tuesday has pinball league play from 6:45 to 10 pm, Thursday brings free tastings and Sunday is happy hour all day. Walk-ins are welcome and there are no reservations; Wednesdays are set aside for private events, and the floor can be booked for one. Physical gift cards are sold at the bar.
