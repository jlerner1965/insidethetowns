Admission is $12 per person; walk-ins are accepted, but reservations are recommended on weekends and holidays, and visits may be limited to an hour when busy. Children must be at least 6 and closely supervised. The limited menu features coffee from Dark Heart Coffee Bar, teas, cold drinks and snacks.
