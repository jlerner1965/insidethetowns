Kids get an outdoor play area in the beer garden with a small slide and playhouse, plus an indoor play space in the taproom. Dogs are welcome in the taproom, on the patio and in the beer garden. Food trucks come Thursday through Sunday, there are snacks for sale, and you can bring in outside food at any time.
