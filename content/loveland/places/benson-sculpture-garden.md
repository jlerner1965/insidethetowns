Benson Sculpture Garden has showcased outdoor sculpture since 1985 and holds 190 permanent pieces, with a newer section across 29th Street added from 2015. The park has accessible sidewalks, a public restroom and benches (the arts council's FAQ says there are no picnic tables), and admission is free except on the show weekend in August, when part of the park also closes for setup. Groups of ten or more can book one of a limited number of tours through the arts council at 970-663-2940; pavilion reservations go through the City of Loveland.
