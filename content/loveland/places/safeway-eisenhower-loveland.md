The Safeway at 1451 W. Eisenhower Boulevard, open every day from 6 am to 11 pm. The pharmacy keeps shorter hours: weekdays 8 to 7, Saturday 9 to 6 and Sunday 10 to 6, closed each day from 1:30 to 2. Orders can be picked up through DriveUp & Go or delivered, and SNAP EBT is accepted in the store and online.
