A downtown bar and grill on 4th Street: tavern classics, rotating local taps, craft beer, premium scotch and cocktails. Happy hour runs Monday to Friday 11 to 6 and all day Sunday, with $1 off standard drafts and $5 wells, house wines, margaritas and bloody marys. Kids eat free all day Tuesday with an adult entrée. Live music is free every Friday and Saturday at 8:30 pm, with no cover. Open Sunday to Tuesday 11 to 10 and Wednesday to Saturday 11 to 11.
