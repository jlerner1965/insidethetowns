Loveland's accredited art and history museum, founded in 1937 by Harold Dunning, a local author, collector and mountain guide who had been gathering pioneer artifacts and stories since 1919. It keeps history exhibits alongside changing art exhibitions, and runs family events, classes for adults and children, lectures and poetry readings.

Open Tuesday and Wednesday 11 to 5, Thursday and Friday 11 to 7 and Saturday 10 to 4, and until 8 on second Fridays, the night of downtown's Night on the Town. Admission is $7 for Loveland residents, $10 for visitors and free for children 12 and under.
