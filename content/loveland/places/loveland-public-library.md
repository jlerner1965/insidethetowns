Loveland's public library, a City department, in the Civic Center on North Adams Avenue east of downtown. Open Tuesday to Thursday 10 to 6, Friday 11 to 5, and Monday and Saturday 11 to 3; closed Sunday. Free WiFi runs inside the building and out, on separate networks, with the passwords posted on the library's site. The phone is (970) 962-BOOK (2665).
