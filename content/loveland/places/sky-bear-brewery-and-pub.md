Sky Bear brews all of its beers in-house and adds craft cocktails, wine, spirits and non-alcoholic options. Events range from bingo and trivia to comedy, vinyl nights and dance parties, and there's a patio for drinks outside. The site says a new location is coming soon, so check the address before you go.
