The beers lean German and American, from Abners Moustache lager and Spinning Gold German pils to the King of Hops IPA, plus seasonals such as an Oktoberfest lager. There's a dog-friendly patio and a 5,000-square-foot event space you can book. Kitchen service ends an hour before the taproom closes.
