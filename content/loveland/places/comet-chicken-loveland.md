Tenders are all-white-meat Redbird chicken from Colorado, breaded in-house and fried or grilled, with a long list of scratch-made sauces; gluten-free buns are available. You can dine in with a local beer, order online for carryout or delivery, or order catering and family packs. Its other location is in Old Town Fort Collins.
