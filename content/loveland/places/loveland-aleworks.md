Nick Callaway and Kari Klapper opened Loveland Aleworks in 2012, and the taproom is open seven days a week, from noon to 11 pm Sunday to Thursday and noon to midnight on Friday and Saturday. The tap list mixes lagers, seasonal releases and experimental small batches with familiar favorites. The site lists weekly events including trivia, live music and food trucks.
