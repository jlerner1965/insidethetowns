Nick Callaway and Kari Klapper opened Loveland Aleworks in 2012, and the taproom is open seven days a week. Its website posts no opening hours, so call before you go. The tap list mixes lagers, seasonal releases and experimental small batches with familiar favorites. The site lists weekly events including trivia, live music and food trucks.
