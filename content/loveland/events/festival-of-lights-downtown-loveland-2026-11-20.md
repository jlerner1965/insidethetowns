Downtown Loveland's holiday lighting debut, Friday, November 20 from 5 to 8 pm along the newly rebuilt 4th Street between Garfield and Washington avenues. The night has live reindeer, the Sugarplum Artisan Market, a Jingle Jam Zone with live music, Loveland Aleworks beer and food trucks, photos with Santa, a stationary float parade, community performances with a Cheer-O-Meter, free hot chocolate and s'mores, and the Grinch at large. The tree lighting is at 7:30 pm in front of the Rialto Theater. The Downtown Partnership's page lists no admission charge.
