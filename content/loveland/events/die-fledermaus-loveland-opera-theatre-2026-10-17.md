Loveland Opera Theatre's new production of Johann Strauss II's operetta Die Fledermaus, sung in English and reset in 1895 Denver at the height of the gold boom, at the Rialto Theater. Six performances: Fridays October 9 and 16 at 7 pm, and weekend matinees at 2 pm on October 10, 11, 17 and 18.
