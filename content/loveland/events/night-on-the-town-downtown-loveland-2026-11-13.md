Downtown Loveland's monthly open house on the second Friday, November 13 this month: a self-guided art walk with opening receptions at Artworks Center for Contemporary Art, Independence Gallery, Lincoln Gallery, the Sidetrack Gallery and the Loveland Museum, shops open late with specials, and seasonal food, drink and beer releases at the bars, restaurants and breweries. The Downtown Partnership's page gives no set hours; each business posts its own.
