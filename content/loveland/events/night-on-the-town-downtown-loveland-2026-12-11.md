December's Night on the Town in downtown Loveland, on the second Friday, December 11: gallery openings at Artworks, Independence, Lincoln and Sidetrack galleries and the Loveland Museum, shops open late with holiday specials, and seasonal offers at the bars, restaurants and breweries. The Downtown Partnership's page gives no set hours; each business posts its own.
