Move & Meet NoCo, a Northern Colorado wellness social club, takes over The Love Shack on 4th Street for a Saturday morning of exercise and meeting people. SPENGA leads an optional 30-minute strength workout at 11:15; all fitness levels are welcome, and the workout is limited to 30 people. From 11:45 to 1 it is a social: a DJ playing 2000s throwbacks, local wellness vendors, B12 shots, protein bites, activewear and permanent jewelry.

Tickets are $25 for the social only (arrive at 11:45) or $30 with the workout. Every ticket includes one mocktail or coffee, and no workout is needed to come. Ticket sales close Friday, November 20.
