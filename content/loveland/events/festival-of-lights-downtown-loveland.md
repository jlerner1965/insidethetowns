Downtown Loveland's Festival of Lights is on Friday, November 20, 2026, from 5 to 8 pm on 4th Street between Garfield and Washington avenues, with the tree lighting at 7:30 pm in front of the Rialto Theater. The Downtown District's page lists no admission charge, and the carriage rides, art activities, hot chocolate and s'mores are free.

## What is on

- **Tree lighting, 7:30 pm:** on 4th Street in front of the Rialto Theater. It is the only item the page gives a time for.
- **Floats on Fourth:** a stationary parade, in its third year. Decorated floats park along the sides of 4th Street between Lincoln and Washington avenues, the middle of the street stays open to walk through, and marching bands and dancers move through the crowd. Judges award prizes.
- **Sugarplum Artisan Market:** more than 30 booths of local makers and vintage sellers, run by The Valentine Flea.
- **Jingle Jam Zone:** live music, Loveland Aleworks beer and food trucks.
- **For kids:** live reindeer, photos with Santa, free art activities, and community groups performing for a Cheer-O-Meter. Scrooge is the emcee and the Grinch wanders the street.
- **Carriage rides:** free, horse-drawn, around downtown.
- **Hot chocolate and s'mores:** free at the Meals on Wheels booth, with marshmallow roasting on site.

## New in 2026

The festival is on the rebuilt 4th Street. The Downtown District says the Heart Improvement Plan is complete and all five blocks are open for the night.

## Parking

The Foundry Parking Garage downtown is free and open to the public. The festival page gives no closure times for the streets around 4th Street and no weather plan.

## Who runs it

The Loveland Downtown Partnership, which says 8,000 to 10,000 people came in 2025. Questions go to event manager Nicole Fellure at nfellure@lovelandpartnership.org.
