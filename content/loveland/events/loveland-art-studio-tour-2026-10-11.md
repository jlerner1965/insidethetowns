The Loveland Creative District's 18th annual Art Studio Tour: painters, potters, jewelers, sculptors and other artists open their studios, pop-ups and galleries across Loveland for two October weekends, with live demonstrations and work for sale. Follow the self-guided map, available at the Loveland Museum. Free.
