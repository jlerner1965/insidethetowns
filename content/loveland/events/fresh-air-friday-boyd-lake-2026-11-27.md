Colorado Parks and Wildlife opens every state park free on Black Friday, November 27, Boyd Lake among them, so no daily vehicle pass or individual pass is needed that day. The park is open 5 am to 10 pm. Low water has closed the lake to boating for the rest of 2026; kayaks and paddleboards are still allowed. CPW calls the day Fresh Air Friday.
