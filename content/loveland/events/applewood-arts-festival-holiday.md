The holiday Applewood Arts Festival is at the Pedersen Toyota Center at The Ranch in Loveland from Friday, November 20 to Sunday, November 22, 2026: a ticketed VIP evening on Friday from 4:30 to 7:30 pm, then general admission on Saturday from 9:30 am to 4 pm and Sunday from 9:30 am to 3 pm.

## Hours and admission

- **Friday, November 20, 4:30 to 7:30 pm:** VIP evening, $20, with a glass of bubbly, a shopping tote and live music. Kids 12 and under get in free, without the tote or drink.
- **Saturday, November 21, 9:30 am to 4 pm, and Sunday, November 22, 9:30 am to 3 pm:** $8, and one ticket covers both days. Kids 12 and under are free. Veterans and active military are free on Saturday and Sunday, not on Friday.

Tickets are sold at the door. Buying ahead on the organizer's site costs $8.25 and $20.75, which includes the card fee.

## What it is

An indoor show of more than 135 local artisans: holiday decor and gifts, fine art, take-home foods, clothing and jewelry. The organizer, which counts 49 years of Colorado craft festivals, says that from 2026 at least 95 percent of booths sell handmade work only.

## Parking and strollers

Parking at The Ranch is free. Strollers are allowed after 1 pm on Saturday and all day Sunday. The Ranch is at 5280 Arena Circle.

## Who runs it

Applewood Arts Festivals of Fort Collins. Contact Jerry Robinson at 833-912-7753 or jrapplewoodfestivals@gmail.com.
