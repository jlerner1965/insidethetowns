picks:
  - place: benson-sculpture-garden
    for: "Sculpture, any time"
    why: "A free 10-acre park with about 190 sculptures on permanent display, a sculpture garden since 1985 and open all year at any time of day, with benches and a public restroom open 7 am to 9 pm. Admission is charged only on the show weekend in August."
  - place: chapungu-sculpture-park
    for: "An accessible walk"
    why: "A free 26-acre park just east of the Promenade Shops at Centerra, where 82 monumental stone sculptures by Zimbabwean artists line walking trails along the Greeley and Loveland Irrigation Canal. Opened in 2007, accessible throughout, and open all year, daily 6 am to 10 pm."
  - place: high-plains-environmental-center
    for: "Native gardens and trails"
    why: "The center's 275 acres of restored native open space and trails, and its demonstration gardens of native plants, are open dawn to dusk every day. The office is by appointment, and the center's events page lists its classes and volunteer days."
  - place: devils-backbone-open-space
    for: "A morning hike"
    why: "Larimer County's 3,007-acre open space off US 34 west of town, with 17.25 miles of trail that connect north to Rimrock and Horsetooth Mountain. Day use, sunrise to sunset all year; a $10-a-vehicle County entrance permit is required, and the County says to arrive before 10 am or after 4 pm for the best chance of a parking space."
  - place: boyd-lake-state-park
    for: "Birds on the lake"
    why: "A 2,082-acre Colorado Parks and Wildlife park that says it is best known for its birds, more than 200 species. Low water has closed the lake to boating for the rest of 2026 and the marina for the season; in winter, hunters and ice anglers use the park. Open 5 am to 10 pm; a day pass is $10 to $12 a vehicle with Colorado plates."
  - place: loveland-museum
    for: "A cold afternoon"
    why: "The art and history museum on North Lincoln Avenue, founded in 1937 by the local author, collector and mountain guide Harold Dunning. Open Tuesday and Wednesday 11 to 5, Thursday and Friday 11 to 7 and Saturday 10 to 4; $7 for Loveland residents, $10 for visitors, free for children 12 and under."
  - place: rialto-theater-center
    for: "A show downtown"
    why: "A 478-seat theater on East 4th Street that opened as a silent movie house in 1920 and is run by the City's Cultural Services Department, with ticketed shows through the year; the guide's calendar lists the next ones. The box office is open Tuesday and Thursday 12:30 to 5:30 and Friday 3 to 5:30, and an hour before each ticketed show."
  - place: the-ranch-events-complex-blue-arena
    for: "Hockey night"
    why: "Blue Arena, home ice of the American Hockey League's Colorado Eagles; the Ranch's other halls hold shows and festivals on dates of their own, on the guide's calendar. Box office in the arena, weekdays 10 to 5; arena parking is sold through ParkWhiz."

Nobody pays to be on the list. Each pick was checked against its own page, or the page of the agency that runs it, on 11 November 2026.

**In November.** Boyd Lake is closed to boats for the rest of 2026. Devil's Backbone closes at sunset, which comes early at this time of year, and the County's page has a webcam of its parking lot.

**Not on this list.** A place goes on it only when its own page confirms its hours that day, so some listings, including the City's recreation center and parks, wait for the next check.
