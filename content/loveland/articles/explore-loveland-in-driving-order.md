Loveland is a city of 76,378 along US 34, so its strengths are a drive apart: the sculpture garden on the north side, the downtown blocks of 4th Street, and the hogback trails west of town. This guide takes them in that order and parks twice; stops 02 to 04 are on or a block off East 4th Street, on foot.

## 01 · Benson Sculpture Garden

[Benson Sculpture Garden](/places/benson-sculpture-garden/) at 2908 Aspen Drive is a free 10-acre park with 188 permanently installed sculptures, home since 1985 to the Sculpture in the Park show each August, the one weekend when admission is charged and part of the park closes for setup. It is open year-round at any hour, with accessible sidewalks, a public restroom and benches but no picnic tables. The [sculpture walk guide](/articles/lovelands-sculpture-walk/) has the arts council's notes on parking, the 2027 show dates and the City's downtown pieces, so they are not repeated here.

*Getting there:* by car to Aspen Drive, on the north side of town. *Best for:* the first hour of the day, before downtown opens.

## 02 · Lunch at Henry's

[Henry's Pub](/places/henrys-pub/) at 234 East 4th Street serves classic and contemporary American food with weekly lunch specials, gluten-free and kids' menus, and a long list of local beer. Its site lists Sunday to Thursday 11 am–9 pm and Friday and Saturday 11 am–10 pm, closed on five holidays including Thanksgiving and Christmas; reservations are taken online.

*Getting there:* park once downtown. Door 222's site says the City is rebuilding five blocks of 4th Street in 2026 and that its alley entrance gives access to the 2nd Street parking garage, so expect work on the street. *Best for:* a lunch that fits any day of the week.

## 03 · Galleries and shops on 4th

[Lundeen Sculpture Studios](/places/lundeen-sculpture-studios/) at 338 East 4th Street is part gallery, part working bronze studio, open weekdays only, 9:30 am–3 pm. [Independence Gallery](/places/independence-gallery/) at 233 East 4th Street shows Northern Colorado artists Monday to Saturday from 11 am, with Sunday opening the gallery itself calls "sometimes, but not always". A block off 4th Street, the [Lincoln Gallery](/places/lincoln-gallery/) at 429 North Lincoln Avenue is open Tuesday to Saturday 12–6 pm, closed Sunday and Monday. [Mary's Mountain Cookies](/places/marys-mountain-cookies-loveland/) at 248 East 4th Street bakes until 9 or 10 pm.

*Getting there:* on foot from Henry's. *Best for:* a weekday, when Lundeen is open; a Saturday keeps Lincoln and Independence but not Lundeen.

## 04 · An evening: the Rialto or Door 222

The [Rialto Theater Center](/places/rialto-theater-center/) at 228 East 4th Street opened as a silent movie house in 1920 and is run by the City, with touring acts, local performing arts groups and films in a 478-seat theater. Buy tickets before you plan the evening: the fall box office opens Tuesday and Thursday 12:30–5:30 pm, Friday 3–5:30 pm, and 60 minutes before each ticketed event for that event only. [Door 222](/places/door-222/) at 222 East 4th Street, on the same block, opens Tuesday to Sunday from 4 pm until close for happy hour and dinner, with reservations through its site; it is closed on Monday. For a drink, [Verboten Brewing](/places/verboten-brewing/) at 127 East 5th Street pours from noon daily, with no kitchen.

*Getting there:* the same downtown parking. *Best for:* a Tuesday to Sunday evening with a ticket in hand.

## 05 · Devil's Backbone, on another half-day

[Devil's Backbone Open Space](/places/devils-backbone-open-space/) is Larimer County's 3,007 acres along the sandstone hogback west of town, with 17.25 miles of natural-surface trail shared by hikers, mountain bikers and horses. The trailhead is off US 34 just past mile marker 88, up Hidden Valley Drive, with restrooms and drinking water at the lot; it is day use only, sunrise to sunset, and a Larimer County entrance permit is required. Give it its own half-day: on weekends and holidays the lot fills, a full lot means the trails are at capacity, and the County says to arrive before 10 am or after 4 pm and to check its parking webcam before leaving home. Pair a morning there with lunch at Henry's, or a late-afternoon walk with dinner at Door 222; the listing has the County's rules in full. [Boyd Lake State Park](/places/boyd-lake-state-park/) is the other outdoor half-day, though in 2026 its marina is closed and low water has closed the lake to boating.

*Getting there:* by car, west on US 34. *Best for:* an early weekday morning; dogs on a leash, and left at home in hot weather.

## A simple first visit

1. Drive to Benson Sculpture Garden first and walk the paths.
2. Move the car downtown and have lunch at Henry's.
3. Walk 4th Street: Lundeen on a weekday, then the Independence and Lincoln galleries.
4. Dinner at Door 222 from 4 pm, Tuesday to Sunday, or a show you booked at the Rialto.
5. Save Devil's Backbone for another morning, before 10 am, with the County permit.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. US 34 crosses the city as Eisenhower Boulevard, out to I-25 one way and up the Big Thompson Canyon the other, and US 287 runs north to Fort Collins and south to Longmont. Denver is 46 miles south by I-25, about 55 minutes. Transfort, the City of Fort Collins' transit agency, runs the FLEX regional bus between Fort Collins, Loveland, Berthoud, Longmont and Boulder on weekday and Saturday timetables, posted on [its FLEX page](https://www.ridetransfort.com/routes/flex). The City of Loveland runs the COLT city buses, but its site, lovgov.org, refused every request made for this guide, so no COLT route or timetable is claimed here. Posted signs override anything written here.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The arts council describes Benson Sculpture Garden's sidewalks as accessible and lists a public restroom. Door 222 says its alley entrance is open during the 4th Street work and gives access to the 2nd Street parking garage, which is a route, not a statement about steps. The Rialto's site links the City's accessibility page, which refused the request made for this guide, so nothing about the theater's seating or entrances is confirmed here. At Devil's Backbone the County lists restrooms and drinking water at the trailhead and nothing about trail accessibility. Step-free entry at Henry's and the galleries, public restrooms downtown and designated accessible parking have not been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here.
