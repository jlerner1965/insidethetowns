Rustic Roots Salon & Spa is on West 4th Avenue. Its services page lists hair appointments, hand-tied hair extensions, lash lifts and extension sets, brow waxing and lamination, permanent makeup (brows, eyeliner and lip blushing), injectables such as Botox, chemical peels and fillers given by a licensed RN, and nails from an in-house nail technician; each service is booked through its own provider's link or number. Its About page names Sierra Tyner as the owner and one of its color and extension specialists.

Address and phone were read on the salon's home, contact and services pages on 10 October 2026; none of them gives opening hours, and nothing on them is dated. The Town of Severance's business directory, read the same day, lists the salon, and the Town's Hearts around Severance Scavenger Hunt page names it as one of its stops. The handoff packet's description adds nothing beyond what the site says.
