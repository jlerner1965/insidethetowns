A quiet park in the Timber Ridge subdivision with open lawn, shade trees, benches and picnic tables around a pond with a fountain. The pond is stocked with fish by the Department of Wildlife, as the page puts it, and fishing is allowed with a license, but swimming, playing in the pond and ice fishing are not. The facility page lists it as ADA accessible with parking. The Town's Kids Fishing Derby page says the 2026 derby for Severance residents was held here on May 16.
