Tecate Grill appears under Food and Drinks in the Town of Severance's business directory, linked to tecateseverance.com, which on 10 October 2026 redirected to tecateseveranceco.com. That site's title calls it a restaurant focused on the enjoyment of all ages, with tacos and drinks; nothing else could be read from it without a browser. The Town's Hearts around Severance Scavenger Hunt page, read the same day, names Tecate Grill as one of its stops.

The handoff packet of 10 October 2026, unconfirmed: a restaurant at 1018 Mahogany Way (the same street address as the Domino's store) with tacos al carbón, pastor tacos, burritos, enchiladas, quesabirria and burgers, and online ordering for pickup.
