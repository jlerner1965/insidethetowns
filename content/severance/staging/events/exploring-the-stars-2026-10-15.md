A stargazing evening at the Severance Library, run by the Clearview Library District with the Northern Colorado Astronomical Society. From 6 to 7 pm the society gives a talk in the Cottonwood room on sky-gazing basics and upcoming astronomical events; afterwards the group moves to the Severance Town Hall parking lot to look through telescopes, weather permitting. Open to ages 12 and up; registration is optional and only serves as a reminder. The library itself closes at 6 pm, so this is an after-hours program.
