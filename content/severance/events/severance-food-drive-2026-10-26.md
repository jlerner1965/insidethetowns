The Town's annual holiday food drive for neighbors in need. Leave nonperishable food in the bins at Town Hall from October 26 to November 6 (the Town says other drop-off sites are still to be announced), or drive through Town Hall on Saturday, November 7, from 10 am to noon with frozen turkeys or nonperishables. Most wanted: canned meals, fruit and vegetables, peanut butter, rice and pasta, canned tuna or chicken, soups, stuffing mix, canned pumpkin and shelf-stable milk; the Town also takes donations online.
