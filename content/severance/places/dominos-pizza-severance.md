The chain's Severance store, on Mahogany Way, does delivery and carryout: pizza, bread bites and cheesy bread, chicken, oven-baked pastas and sandwiches, salads and desserts. It opens at 10:30 am (10 am on Fridays and Saturdays) and stays open to 10 pm, or 11 pm at the end of the week. Orders go through the store's phone line or Domino's own site.
