The Town of Severance’s Parks & Trails map (Ayres, map 12, dated 13 April 2026) lists Tailholt Park as park 17, 11.9 acres, and places it east of Tailholt Avenue and north of Waterfowl Way, toward Franklin Lake (the map calls it Tailholt Park 4; a 0.2-acre Tailholt Pocket Park, number 18, sits at Tailholt and Central avenues). The Town’s Parks & Recreation page names the park and says its parks are spread through the subdivisions, each with its own amenities, but neither the page nor the map gives a street address or the amenities, so the address above is the map’s position. Parks & Recreation answers 970-686-1218.
