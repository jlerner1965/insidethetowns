The Town of Severance's offices stand at the corner of Weld County Road 74 and Timber Ridge Parkway and are open Monday to Friday, closing for lunch from noon to 1 pm; for after-hours drop-offs there is a box on the north side of the building. The Town's Community Gardens page sends would-be gardeners for the Tailholt Community Garden here, and the Great Western Trail Authority's About page says its board meets here on the first Thursday of each month at 5:30 pm. The Town's Trick or Treat Parade page puts this year's parade at Town Hall and the library on October 30, 3 to 5 pm, with trunk-or-treating and a haunted walk through both buildings; a nonperishable food item for the Weld Food Bank is the price of entry.
