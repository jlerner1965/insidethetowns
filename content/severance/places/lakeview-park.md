The Town's large park at the south end of the Lakeview subdivision is mostly open grass and trees, with covered picnic shelters, restrooms, barbecue pits, play equipment, a skateboard park and basketball courts. Drivers can come in from South 1st Street (Weld County Road 23) at Broadview Drive, where one of four parking areas sits; the others are at the south ends of Mallard Drive and Lakeview Drive and an asphalt lot at the park's south end.
