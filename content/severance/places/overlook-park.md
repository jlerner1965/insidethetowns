One of the Town of Severance's public parks, on Audubon Boulevard. The Town's Neighborhood Concert page says that with Severance Days not held in 2026, the Town brought people together here on October 3 for an afternoon and evening of live music, food trucks and vendors, closing Audubon Boulevard nearby for the weekend. The Town's Park Reservation Request page says a group of more than 50 always needs a reservation, filed at least 10 business days ahead; events with amplified sound, public admission or a road closure need a special event permit; and alcohol is not allowed in Town parks without a special event permit and a liquor license.
