Windsong Estate Event Center is a family-owned wedding and event venue on a private estate, which its About page places in "Severance/Fort Collins" and says has been in business since 2013. It hosts weddings, corporate events and private gatherings in indoor and outdoor spaces for up to 300 guests, with views of the Rocky Mountains, audio and visual equipment and adjustable lighting, and keeps a network of preferred caterers, dessert vendors and musicians. Its site posts pricing for weddings, special events and quinceañeras. It is a booked venue rather than a place to drop in: visits are by tour, Saturday and Sunday tours are by appointment only, and the site asks people to check its Instagram or Facebook for last-minute schedule changes or closures.

Address, phone and hours were read on the venue's home, About and Events pages on 10 October 2026. The site gives the address as Severance, CO 80524 in its contact block and as Fort Collins, CO 80524 in its footer and on its Events page; the events line, for calls or texts, is (970) 686-9690. The site carries no dated post, so the operating evidence is the Town of Severance's business directory, read the same day, which lists it under event centers.
