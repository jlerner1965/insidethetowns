The Town of Severance’s Parks & Trails map (Ayres, map 12, dated 13 April 2026) lists Severance Shores Park as park 15, 2.3 acres, and places it at the north end of Lake Vista Way in the Severance Shores subdivision, on the Windsor Reservoir shore. The Town’s Parks & Recreation page names the park and says its parks are spread through the subdivisions, each with its own amenities, but neither the page nor the map gives a street address or the amenities, so the address above is the map’s position. Parks & Recreation answers 970-686-1218.
