A Town of Severance park on Audubon Boulevard, the same street as Overlook Park. The Town's Parks & Recreation page lists it with its address but not its amenities. The park can be reserved through the Town's Park Reservation Request form, which that page says a group of more than 50 always needs, filed at least 10 business days ahead; alcohol is not allowed in Town parks without a special event permit and a liquor license.
