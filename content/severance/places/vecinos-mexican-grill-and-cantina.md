Vecinos Mexican Grill & Cantina is on West 4th Avenue. Its own site names burritos in a flour tortilla with beans, green chili, lettuce and cheese (beef, chicken, shredded beef, pork or steak, or as a bowl), deep-fried chimichangas, enchilada platters with red chili, green chili or both ("Xmas") and rice and beans, chili con queso with pico de gallo, and taquitos with guacamole and sour cream; gluten-free tortillas are offered on request. It keeps separate breakfast and drink menus, opens early on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, and takes orders for delivery or pickup online or by phone.
