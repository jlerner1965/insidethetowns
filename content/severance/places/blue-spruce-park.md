A quiet park in the Timber Ridge subdivision, reached from 4th Avenue at Timber Ridge Parkway and at Foxtail Way, with open lawn, shade trees, benches and picnic tables around a pond with a fountain. The pond is stocked with fish by the Department of Wildlife, as the Town's page puts it, and fishing is allowed with a license, but swimming, playing in the pond and ice fishing are not. The facility page lists it as ADA accessible, with parking. The Town's 2026 Kids Fishing Derby, for Severance residents, was held here on May 16.

The address, features and rules were read on the park's page in the Town's facilities directory on 10 October 2026. The Town's Parks & Recreation page, read the same day, lists the park at 50 S Timber Ridge Parkway instead; the facility page's own address is used here. The Town's Parks & Trails map, dated 13 April 2026, numbers it park 1, 3.2 acres, at the north end of Timber Ridge Parkway, and the Town's Kids Fishing Derby page dates this year's derby here, which is the evidence that it is a current Town park. Parks & Recreation answers 970-686-1218.
