A small park in the center of Old Town, at the Old Town Hall on 1st Street, which the Town also places at the northwest corner of East Harmony and Weld County Road 23. It is lawn and shade trees with picnic tables. It does not appear in the list on the Town's Parks & Recreation page, but has its own page in the Town's facilities directory.
