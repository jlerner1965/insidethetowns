## Editor's notes

Written 31 October 2026, the day the guide was completed for launch. Everything below was read that day.

**Town news worth a listing:** Heroes Hall, the veterans' building planned at the fairgrounds, gets its ceremonial groundbreaking on 7 November with a dedication of World Trade Center steel (County event page; the County's releases of 22 October 2024 and 23 October 2025 describe the building). The Downtown Alliance's holiday carriage rides sold their 75 in-person tickets on 29 October at the library; online sales open at noon on 18 November.

**Events found and not listed, with why:**
- Colorado Classic, a USAG gymnastics meet at the Events Center, 15 to 17 January 2027, $15 spectators; the Fairgrounds page gives no hours (https://www.douglasco.gov/fairground-events/colorado-classic-01152027/).
- Windish RV Show, Event Center and Indoor Arena, 8 and 9 January 2027: a dealer's sales event, outside the event standard (https://www.douglasco.gov/fairground-events/windish-rv-show-01082027/).
- St. Francis of Assisi Christmas Eve service at the Events Center, 24 December: still no time on the Fairgrounds page or the parish site; staged (https://www.douglasco.gov/fairground-events/st-francis-of-assisi-christmas-eve-service-12242026/, https://www.stfranciscr.org/mass-confession).
- Chamber Veterans Alliance talk with Dan Brite, 10 November, 4 to 6 pm, 420 Jerry St: the page does not say whether it is open to the public (https://business.castlerock.org/events/details/veterans-alliance-resilience-in-the-face-of-adversity-speaker-dan-brite-4-6-p-m-7060).
- Outlets Black Friday, 27 November, 7 am to 9 pm, free pancakes and entertainment: a sales day, not an event (https://www.outletsatcastlerock.com/event/45210-black-friday). Jingle Bucks coupon books, 1 to 25 November: a promotion.
- Library: Battle of the Books (ten dates, January to February) is a school-team competition; Epic Reads is a grades 4 to 6 book club; both out under the standard. Final Draft Book Club at Sudden Fiction (16 November, 21 December) likewise.
- The Edge's Winter Wonderland page gives weekend winter hours and no season dates (https://theedgezip.com/seasonal/winter-wonderland).
