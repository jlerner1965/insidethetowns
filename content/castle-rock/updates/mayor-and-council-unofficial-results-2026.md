Douglas County's November 3 ballot carried three Town of Castle Rock races, each a vote for one. In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 12:39 am on November 4: for mayor, Laura Cavey 17,358 votes, Amelia Thomas 9,971 and Stu Butler 7,048; for Councilmember, District 3, Matt Smith 2,339, Tiffany Coyle 2,185 and Chris Phelen 898; for Councilmember, District 5, Mitchell Sawin 2,874 and Patrick Reilly 1,718. Smith's lead over Coyle is 154 votes, in a count the county labels unofficial.
