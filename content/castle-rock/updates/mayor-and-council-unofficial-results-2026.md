Douglas County's November 3 ballot carried three Town of Castle Rock races, each a vote for one. In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 5:16 pm on November 5: for mayor, Laura Cavey 18,177 votes, Amelia Thomas 10,473 and Stu Butler 7,458; for Councilmember, District 3, Matt Smith 2,476, Tiffany Coyle 2,292 and Chris Phelen 956; for Councilmember, District 5, Mitchell Sawin 3,010 and Patrick Reilly 1,804. Smith's lead over Coyle is 184 votes, in a count the county labels unofficial.
