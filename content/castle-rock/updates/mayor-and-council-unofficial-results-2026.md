Douglas County's November 3 ballot carried three Town of Castle Rock races, each a vote for one. In the county's unofficial count, last updated at 5:07 pm on November 4: for mayor, Laura Cavey 17,758 votes, Amelia Thomas 10,145 and Stu Butler 7,202; for Councilmember, District 3, Matt Smith 2,411, Tiffany Coyle 2,220 and Chris Phelen 917; for Councilmember, District 5, Mitchell Sawin 2,940 and Patrick Reilly 1,747. Smith's lead over Coyle is 191 votes, in a count the county labels unofficial.
