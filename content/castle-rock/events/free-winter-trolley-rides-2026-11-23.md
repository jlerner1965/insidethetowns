The Downtown Alliance's free trolley loops past the holiday lights every Monday and Tuesday evening of the season. It runs a figure-eight from the Encore garage entrance on South Street, up Wilcox Street to a stop at Third Street, then along Perry Street to the school district's lot on 6th Street; a full loop takes about 12 minutes, and every stop takes wheelchairs and strollers.
