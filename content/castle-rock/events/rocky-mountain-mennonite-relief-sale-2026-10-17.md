A day of four auctions in the Events Center raising money for the relief and humanitarian work of Mennonite Central Committee: handmade and vintage goods, toys, and a 1 pm quilt auction of quilts, comforters and wall hangings. There is a kids' auction for ages 5 to 15. Admission is free; you pay only for what you win.
