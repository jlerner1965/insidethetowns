The Historical Society's October walking tour of downtown Castle Rock, told through its ghost stories and tragedies: the Rock, the Old Stone Church, the B&B Café and more. Tours start and end at the [Castle Rock Museum](/places/castle-rock-museum/) in the old depot; dress for the weather. Free, with donations encouraged, but places must be reserved with the museum at 303-814-3164 or museum@castlerockhistoricalsociety.org.
