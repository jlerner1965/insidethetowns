The Outlets at Castle Rock light their Christmas tree on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at a free evening from 4 to 7 pm with a concert, Santa's arrival and a holiday drone show. It is open to the public.

## Schedule

The Outlets give one clock time: the concert starts at 4 pm, and the listing ends at 7. The order of the evening is:

- **From 4 pm:** live music. The Eric Golden Band plays honky-tonk country, and Spinphony, a string quartet, plays classical pieces alongside rock and pop.
- **Santa's arrival:** in a Bentley Denver car, escorted by the Castle Rock Fire Department, the Douglas County and Castle View marching bands and the Castle View winter guard.
- **Tree lighting:** Santa lights what the Outlets call Colorado's tallest Christmas tree.
- **Drone show:** follows the lighting.

Jeff Schroeder and Jordan Lloyd host. The page gives no times for Santa, the lighting or the drone show, so come for the start if you want to see all three.

## Cost, drinks and the toy drive

Admission is free. Beer and wine are sold, with the proceeds going to the Humane Society of the South Platte Valley.

A toy drive runs alongside: bring a new, unwrapped toy for Cancer Fighters to the Rescue, Children's Hospital Colorado and the Help & Hope Center. Drop-offs at the Outlets are Customer Service, between Vuori and Perfumania, and Go! Calendars, Games and Toys, Suite 420 on 2nd Street.

## Who runs it

Outlets at Castle Rock, 5050 Factory Shops Boulevard. The event page says nothing about parking, pets or what happens in bad weather, and gives no contact for the event.
