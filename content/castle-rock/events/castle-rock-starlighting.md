Castle Rock Starlighting is on Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 2 to 6 pm in downtown Castle Rock: the ceremony starts at 5 pm at 4th and Wilcox, the star on the Rock is lit at about 5:30 pm, and fireworks follow, weather and conditions permitting. Admission is free.

## Schedule

- **2 to 4:15 pm:** Santa Claus in Festival Park.
- **2 to 4:45 pm:** the Biff Gore Band on the Main Stage at 4th and Wilcox.
- **5 pm:** the ceremony on the Main Stage, with Kirk Yuhnke of FOX31, Denise Plante of Great Day Colorado and Deborah Takahara of DCSO. It includes a reading of Helen Lowrie Marshall's poem "Castle Rock."
- **About 5:30 pm:** the star is lit, then fireworks straight after.

Through the afternoon: the firefighters' 90th annual chili supper at Fire Station 151, a kids' zone on 4th Street just west of Wilcox with face painting, a petting zoo and games, food trucks, hot chocolate, four Chamber drinks tents, and nonprofit and vendor booths. The Chamber takes cards only, no cash.

## Shuttles and parking

Free shuttles run from 1 to 8 pm from three lots:

- AdventHealth Castle Rock, Alexander lot, 2352 Meadows Boulevard
- Douglas County High School, south building, 2842 Front Street
- Douglas County Fairgrounds, Lot 5, 500 Fairgrounds Drive

The first two drop off at 5th and Wilcox; the fairgrounds shuttle drops between South and Phelps streets. Return shuttles leave from where you were dropped. Downtown, the Chamber points to the Encore garage at Perry and South streets and the Douglas County garage at 3rd and Jerry streets.

## Street closures

From 6 am to 8 pm: Perry Street from 5th to 2nd; North Wilcox Street from 5th to 3rd; 4th Street from Jerry to Perry; and 3rd and 2nd streets from Wilcox to Perry.

## Who runs it

The Castle Rock Chamber of Commerce, which started Starlighting in 1962; the star was first lit in 1936. Call 303-688-4597.
