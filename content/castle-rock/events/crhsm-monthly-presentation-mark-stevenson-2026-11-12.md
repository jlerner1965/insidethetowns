The Castle Rock Historical Society and Museum's November presentation is by retired Commander Mark Stevenson of the US Navy Medical Service Corps, who served more than 25 years in the Navy and Air Force and was at the Pentagon on 9/11. He speaks on Thursday, November 12, at 7 pm at the Philip & Jerry Miller Library, 100 S. Wilcox Street. The Society's post gives no price; questions go to museum@castlerockhistoricalsociety.org or 303-814-3164.
