A two-day buy, sell and trade show of firearms, knives, ammunition, hunting and outdoor gear, military surplus and collectibles, with appraisals on site and vendors from across the West. One $15 ticket covers both days, online or at the door; the organizer says there are no refunds, no pets, and strollers must be checked.
