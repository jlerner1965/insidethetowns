A daytime trick-or-treat in Festival Park for families with homeschooled children and little ones not yet in school, going booth to booth among local businesses and groups, with Halloween music, a face painter, a balloon artist and an ice cream truck. Parking is in the Encore garage, the library garage, the Douglas County garage and the garage at The View.
