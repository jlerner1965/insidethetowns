St. Francis of Assisi Catholic Church holds its Christmas Eve service in the Events Center at the fairgrounds, open to anyone who wants to gather for worship before Christmas. The County's page gives the date but no time; the parish puts its holy-day times in the bulletin, or call 303-688-3025.
