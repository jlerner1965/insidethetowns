Plum Creek Church's 22nd Thanksgiving-morning 5K, to run, walk or stroll, starting from the Events Center, with a free 1K Kids Dash, a health and wellness expo of more than 30 vendors, a DJ, food, face painting, a bounce house and a costume contest. Proceeds go to the Help & Hope Center, which helps Douglas and Elbert County families facing hardship or housing trouble. There are discounts for under-18s, over-60s, veterans, first responders and families.
