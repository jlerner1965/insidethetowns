Crush opened in November 2017. Nancie, a caterer for 15 years, writes the menu of shareable small plates, charcuterie and cheese boards and desserts; James, a sommelier for 20 years, builds the wine list around small family vineyards and importers. It is for guests 21 and over and takes reservations.
