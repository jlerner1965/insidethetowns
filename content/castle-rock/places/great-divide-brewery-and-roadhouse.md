Great Divide brews experimental small batches on a five-barrel pilot system here, available only at this location, and pours its Denver-brewed beers alongside. The kitchen serves comfort food and pub favorites, from burgers and entrée salads to pizzas, steaks and desserts, and takes to-go orders online.
