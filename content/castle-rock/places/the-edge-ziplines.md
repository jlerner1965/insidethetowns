An adventure park, established in 2014, inside Philip S. Miller Park. The guided zipline tour covers ten lines in two to three hours for ages 7 and up; the Sky Trek obstacle course takes children from age 4, and the adventure tower and ninja course are for ages 7 and up. Parking at the park is free, the activities run weather permitting, and packages combine two or three of them.
