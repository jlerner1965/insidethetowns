An adventure park, established in 2014, inside Philip S. Miller Park. The guided zipline tour covers ten lines in two to three hours with no minimum age and a weight range of 70 to 250 pounds; the Sky Trek obstacle course takes children from age 4, the adventure tower's own page says ages 4 and up, and the ninja course has no minimum age. Parking at the park is free, the activities run weather permitting, and packages combine two or three of them.
