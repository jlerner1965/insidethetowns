One of six Homegrown Tap & Dough restaurants in Colorado, serving hand-tossed pizzas, signature pastas, burgers and cannoli with craft cocktails. It does not take reservations but keeps an online waitlist, the patio is pet-free, and it does pickup and delivery orders and private events.
