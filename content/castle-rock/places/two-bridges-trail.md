A Douglas County meadow trail with loops from 2.7 miles up to the full 10.4, joining the Cherry Creek Trail. It borders the Colorado Horse Park, and the outer trail may close now and then for its equestrian events; the horse jumps are not for public use. The lot holds 30 cars and 12 to 15 horse trailers, motor vehicles are not allowed on the trail, and there is no water.
