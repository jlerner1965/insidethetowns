COFF33 brews coffee roasted by Lost Coffee, a local roaster: the Elusive Giraffe house blend and single origins from Guatemala, Colombia and Ethiopia. The room is built for laptops and meetings, with comfortable chairs, outlets and fast free wifi, and a meeting room and conference space can be booked.
