Wild Blue Yonder brews a rotating lineup on site and pours wine, cider, mules and margaritas alongside, with a kitchen serving pub fare from poutine to New Mexican pork green chili. Leashed dogs are allowed on the outdoor patios only, under county health rules. Happy hour runs weekdays from 3 to 6 pm and from 8 pm to close; groups under eight need no reservation, and an 1887 carriage house seats about 30 for private events.
