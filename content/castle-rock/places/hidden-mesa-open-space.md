Douglas County’s Hidden Mesa is cut by Cherry Creek and the paved Cherry Creek Trail. Its own trails run from the easy creek valley up to rockier ground on the mesa, by the Rocky Pass and Pleasant Pass connectors and the Mesa Run loop; up onto the mesa and back is close to 7 miles. Horses, bikes and hikers share them and dogs must be leashed. The water at the Grange is untreated and the Pleasant View trailhead has none.
