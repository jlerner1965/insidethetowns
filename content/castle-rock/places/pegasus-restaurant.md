Pegasus has served breakfast and lunch since 1984, with a menu that mixes Mexican-inspired dishes, Greek classics and American staples from the Torres brothers in the kitchen. The 5,000-square-foot restaurant runs through several rooms, with a private courtyard for warm days and a soapstone bar by the fireplace. The kitchen closes at 2 pm, though seated guests can stay on; after hours the space can be booked for events as Pegasus After Hours.
