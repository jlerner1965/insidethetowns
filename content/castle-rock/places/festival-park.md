The park in the middle of downtown, the Town's venue for its community events: First Fridays, the farmers market and the Western Heritage Welcome by the Town's own list, and on this guide's calendar Santa at Starlighting and the start of the holiday carriage rides by the big tree. It has a large pavilion, two fire pits, a splash pad, an open lawn, a creekside picnic area and Wi-Fi, and it is where the East Plum Creek Trail and the Sellars Gulch Trail meet, with access to the creek. Parks maintenance is on 720-733-2260.
