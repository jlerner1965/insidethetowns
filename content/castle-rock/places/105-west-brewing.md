105 West has brewed in Castle Rock since 2015 and has since opened a second taproom in Colorado Springs. It is a certified veteran-owned business, serves 105 W Pizza alongside its beers, and runs mug, case and couples’ clubs through its app. The taproom takes no tips; any cash guests leave goes to that month’s charity.
