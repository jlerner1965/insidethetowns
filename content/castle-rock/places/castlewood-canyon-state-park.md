A state park on Highway 83 near Franktown, east of Castle Rock, preserving 2,628 acres of the Black Forest region. Twelve miles of trails, rock climbing on walls up to 60 feet, picnicking and wildlife watching, with views across the Front Range to Pikes Peak. The park is open sunrise to sunset; the visitor center is open Wednesday to Sunday 9 to 5. A daily vehicle pass is $10 to $15, and an individual daily pass $4.
