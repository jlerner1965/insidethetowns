Colorado Parks and Wildlife’s Castlewood Canyon preserves 2,628 acres with about 12 miles of trails, rock climbing on walls up to 60 feet, more than 100 bird species and views of the Front Range and Pikes Peak. The main east entrance, five miles south of Franktown on Highway 83, has the visitor center, flush restrooms, a paved road and picnic areas with grills; the west entrance off Castlewood Canyon Road is less developed, with a gravel road and lots and vault toilets. Leashed dogs and horses are allowed.
