Renamed for Mark Weston, one of the founders of Douglas County’s open space program, the property climbs through ponderosa pine and Douglas fir to a mostly level loop around the top of the mesa. The round trip from the Spruce Mountain Road lot is 5.5 miles and moderately easy, though the steep, rutted east end and upper service road can be hard going. Horse trailers park at the Spruce Meadows trailhead on Noe Road, and the nearest water is the spigot at the Greenland trailhead.
