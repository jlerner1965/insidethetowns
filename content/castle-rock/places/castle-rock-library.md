Douglas County Libraries’ Castle Rock branch, named for its benefactors Philip and Jerry Miller, near downtown on South Wilcox Street. It houses the DCL Archives & Local History, where staff help with historical, archival and family-history research. Children have The Ranch, a western play space with a stable, a tractor and a small grain silo, and the building’s main-floor event halls and two outdoor plazas can be booked.
