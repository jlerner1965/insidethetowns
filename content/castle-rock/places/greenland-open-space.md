Greenland’s crusher-fine and natural-surface loop runs about 11 miles, easy to moderate with 500 feet of gain on the upper loop, and carries a stretch of the Colorado Front Range Trail five miles on to Palmer Lake. Horses, bikes and hikers share it, cattle graze along it in summer, and dogs must be leashed except in the enclosed off-leash area at the north trailhead. The north trailhead has untreated water, restrooms, a group picnic shelter and parking for 50 to 75 horse trailers; the south trailhead, without water, is at 751 W. Palmer Divide Avenue.
