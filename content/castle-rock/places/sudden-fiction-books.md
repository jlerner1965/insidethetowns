An independent bookseller in Ecclesia Market on Perry Street, owned by Gina, Christi and Joe, with a curated selection of new and used books across many genres. It runs a book club on the third Monday of the month in the shop, book bingo nights at Sinners & Saints across the market, and release parties. Hours are not posted on its site; call ahead.
