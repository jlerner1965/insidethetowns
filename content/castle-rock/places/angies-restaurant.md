Angie’s puts two kitchens under one roof: homemade spaghetti, meatballs and lasagna on the Italian side, burritos and Mexican green chile on the other, plus pizza, calzones and sandwiches. The bar pours beer, wine, mules and margaritas, including the house Italian margarita. Seating is by waitlist, with no reservations; there are weekday lunch specials, a take-home spaghetti dinner for four, and catering. The family runs a second Angie’s in Littleton.
