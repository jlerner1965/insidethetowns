From the trailhead the Lincoln Mountain Trail is 4.2 miles round trip, with a climb of almost 400 feet to the flat top, and the Palmer Divide Ranch Trail is 4.5 miles; joined, they make an 8.3-mile outing. Hikers, cyclists and riders share the natural-surface trails, and pets must be leashed. The trailhead has an information kiosk, a restroom and an unpaved lot for cars and 25 horse trailers.
