Provision serves a seasonal cocktail menu, a black book of classic cocktails, a whiskey list, wine and food. Many seatings are reservation-only, so it asks guests to book through OpenTable; the private Bookshelf Room takes parties of eight or fewer. It also holds tastings, spirit nights and cocktail trainings.
