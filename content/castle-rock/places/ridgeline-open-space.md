The Town's open space on the ridge above The Meadows, with 13.5 miles of trail counting the connections and feeder trails, paved and unpaved, rated moderate and, by the Town's own description, heavily used. The main trailhead is on Coachline Road. The Town names rattlesnakes and bluebirds as the wildlife to expect. The trails are adopted and maintained by the Ridgeline Wranglers, a volunteer group under the Town's POST Partners program; parks maintenance is on 720-733-2260, and the Town's trails planner is Rich Havel.
