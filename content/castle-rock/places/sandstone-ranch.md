The county approved buying Sandstone Ranch in December 2017 and calls it the crown jewel of its open space: red rock formations, sloping hay meadows, a working ranch and buildings from its first owners in the 1870s, with 3.5 miles of shared boundary with the Pike National Forest. Hikers, cyclists and riders use the 12-mile trail in the ranch’s eastern part, and a master plan sets how public access works alongside the ranching. The county’s page links directions and parking for the trailhead.
