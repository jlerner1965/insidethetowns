Rockyard has been brewing and serving food since 1999. Its medal winners include the Plum Creek Sour (Great American Beer Festival gold, 2017), Primadonna (World Beer Cup gold, 2018), the Warning Sign Imperial Bock and the Redhawk Scottish Ale. It opens at 11 am every day; the site gives no closing time.
