The Town's flagship park, 300 acres on West Plum Creek Parkway, holding the Millhouse events center, an outdoor amphitheater, the Miller Activity Complex, Challenge Hill, an adventure playground and a trail network, with The Edge's ziplines and adventure courses run inside it under a public-private partnership. The Town says it has been voted Best Place for Outdoor Fun every year since 2018.

The trails are 8 miles of native-surface single-track in four loops that connect, plus a mile of paved, accessible path around the park. Challenge Hill is the draw: 200 steps rising 178 feet, harder than it looks, with the view from the top as the reward.

The 2.5-acre Adventure Playground has a 40-foot slide and play built into the Gambel oak and ponderosa, beside a full-size synthetic-turf field with parking and lights. Next to the field, the Plaza has picnic pavilions, restrooms, a splash pad, an outdoor fireplace and more play. Restrooms and the fireplaces are open 7 am to 10 pm daily; the splash pad runs 10 am to 6 pm in season. Parks maintenance is on 720-733-2260 (parks@crgov.com).
