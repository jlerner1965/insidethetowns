Lunch and dinner on Wilcox Street in what its site calls the 1890s Castle Hotel and Bar, once a stop for quarry workers, ranchers and travelers. The menu leads with the pan-fried chicken and runs to chicken fried steak, meatloaf, brisket, soups, salads, sandwiches and pie, with takeout and delivery. No reservations; seating is first come, first served.
