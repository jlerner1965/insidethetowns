Z’Abbracci makes its pizzas, pastas, risotto, calzones, sandwiches and desserts from scratch, with 20 Colorado craft beers on tap and a full bar. Happy hour is 3 to 6 pm, brunch runs Saturday and Sunday from 9 am to 2 pm, and there is a heated patio as well as pickup, delivery and catering.
