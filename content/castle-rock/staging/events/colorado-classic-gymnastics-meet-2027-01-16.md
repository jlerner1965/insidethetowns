A USA Gymnastics-sanctioned women's meet at the fairgrounds from Friday 15 to Sunday 17 January, with developmental, Xcel and all-level divisions judged by two panels with electronic scoring, and age-group and team awards. Spectators pay $15. The county's page gives the dates but no session times; the meet director is on 303-703-8199.
