The Chamber's holiday opener since 1962: an afternoon of carolers, dance groups and live music on four stages around Wilcox Square, Santa and Mrs. Claus in Festival Park from 2 to 4:15 pm, a kids' zone and petting zoo, food trucks, beverage tents and the firefighters' 90th chili supper at Fire Station 151. The ceremony starts at 5 pm on the main stage at 4th and Wilcox, the star on the Rock is lit at about 5:30 pm and fireworks follow, weather permitting. Admission is free and the Chamber takes cards only; free shuttles run 1:30 to 8 pm from AdventHealth, Douglas County High School and the fairgrounds, and downtown streets close from 6 am.
