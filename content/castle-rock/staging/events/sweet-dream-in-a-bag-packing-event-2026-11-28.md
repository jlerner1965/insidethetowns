The Kiwanis Club of Castle Rock and Castle Rock Impact pack bedding for children in need with the Sweet Dream in a Bag charity on Saturday, November 28, from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm in Kirk Hall at the Douglas County Fairgrounds, and invite the community to come for as long as suits them. The goal this year is bedding for 200 children; the event counts toward high-school community service hours. The listing was read on the Chamber's Visit Castle Rock calendar and has not been confirmed with the organizers.
