Plum Creek Church's 22nd Thanksgiving-morning 5K fun run, walk or stroll through Castle Rock, starting from the Events Center, with a free 1K Kids Dash, a health and wellness expo of more than 30 vendors, a DJ, food, face painting, a bounce house, a dance-off and a costume contest. Proceeds go to the Help & Hope Center, which helps Douglas and Elbert County families in financial trouble. Discounts for under-18s, over-60s, veterans, first responders and families; the race office is on (303) 907-5410.
