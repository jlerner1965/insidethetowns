The Town of Castle Rock’s fireworks show will light up the sky this fall. Originally planned for July Fourth, the show has been rescheduled for Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, weather and fire restrictions permitting.

The 20- to 22-minute high-flying show will launch at 7 p.m. from Miller’s Landing, off Plum Creek Parkway just east of Philip S. Miller Park. A three-shot salute will signal the start of the display.

Where to watch

The remote-viewing show will be visible from many locations in Castle Rock. Recommended viewing locations include:

Downtown

Metzler Ranch Community Park

Philip S. Miller Park

Areas of the Red Hawk neighborhood

Rock Park

If you’re parking in neighborhoods or business parking lots to watch the show, please keep driveways clear and follow posted parking restrictions. Parking along major streets and roadways is not allowed.

Weather

The Town will work with safety personnel on the afternoon of the event to make weather-related decisions in the case of severe weather. If a severe weather event is expected near show time, please check the Town’s Facebook page for updates.
