The only in-person sale of tickets for the Downtown Alliance's holiday carriage rides, outside Event Hall A on the west side of the library. Staff hand numbers to the first 75 people in line at 3:30 pm (18 or over, with ID) and sales start at 4 pm; one ticket per person, each ticket booking a whole carriage for one ride at $59. The remaining tickets go on sale online at noon on Wednesday 18 November through the Alliance's page and social accounts.
