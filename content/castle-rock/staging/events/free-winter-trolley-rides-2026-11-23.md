First evening of the free trolley rides the Downtown Alliance runs under the holiday lights, every Monday and Tuesday evening from 23 November to 29 December. The trolley makes a figure-eight loop from the Encore garage entrance on South Street, up Wilcox to Third, then along Perry to the school-district lot on 6th; a full loop takes about twelve minutes and every stop is wheelchair and stroller accessible.
