A weekend expo of reptiles, amphibians, invertebrates, exotic animals, tropical and specialty plants and the supplies to keep them, with vendors from across the country, on Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October. There is face painting, giveaways and food trucks; VIP tickets add a Friday-evening preview and early Saturday entry. The county's page gives the dates but no hours; the organizer is on (720) 975-4040.
