First night of the Downtown Alliance's horse-drawn carriage rides through the holiday lights, picking up by the big tree in Festival Park. Rides run Wednesday to Friday 4:30 to 8:30 pm and Saturday and Sunday 5 to 8 pm until 3 January, skipping Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve; each lasts about ten minutes. A ticket books the whole carriage and must be bought ahead, in person on 29 October or online from 18 November; the Alliance says demand outstrips the 560 rides available.
