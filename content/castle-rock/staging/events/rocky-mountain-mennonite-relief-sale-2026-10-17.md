A day of four auctions in the Events Center raising money for Mennonite Central Committee relief work: a silent auction from 9 am, a general auction of handmade and vintage goods at 9:30, a kids' auction for ages 5 to 15 at 10 and the quilt auction, with quilts, comforters and wall hangings, at 1 pm. Admission is free; you pay only for what you bid on.
