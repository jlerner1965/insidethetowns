A daytime, booth-to-booth trick-or-treat in Festival Park put on by the Downtown Merchants Association, aimed at families with homeschooled and preschool-age children. Expect Halloween music, vendor booths, a face painter, a balloon artist and an ice-cream truck. The Alliance lists it among its free downtown events; parking is in the Encore garage, the library garage and the garage at The View.
