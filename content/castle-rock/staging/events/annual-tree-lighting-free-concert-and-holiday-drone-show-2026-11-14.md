Celebrate the start of the holiday season at Outlets at Castle Rock with our Annual Tree Lighting Celebration! Enjoy an unforgettable evening of live music, festive family fun, the lighting of Colorado’s tallest Christmas tree, and a dazzling holiday drone show illuminating the night sky. Gather your family and friends for one of Colorado’s favorite holiday traditions!

Event Highlights

Hosted by Jeff Schroeder and Jordan Lloyd, Big Brother 11contestants, social media personalities, podcast hosts, and TV co-host.
Live country music by local artist Eric Golden
An electrifying performance by Spinphony
Santa’s grand arrival in a Bentley Denver vehicle to light up Colorado’s tallest Christmas tree!
Santa’s arrival escorted by Castle Rock Fire Department, Douglas County and Castle View Marching Bands and Castle View Winter Guard.
A spectacular holiday drone show
Beer and wine available for purchase, with all proceeds benefiting Humane Society of the South Platte Valley (HSSPV)

The celebration kicks off at 4 p.m. with live music and holiday entertainment. Get ready for Santa’s special arrival, courtesy of Bentley Denver, as he makes his way to light up Colorado’s tallest Christmas tree! Gather with family and friends for this magical holiday moment before looking to the skies for a spectacular holiday drone show. Watch as illuminated drones create a dazzling nighttime display to make the evening even more unforgettable.
