Second and final day of the collectibles show at the fairgrounds, 10 am to 4 pm: trading cards, sports memorabilia, Pokemon, comics, toys, LEGO, Funko Pops, video-game and anime merchandise and hobby supplies across hundreds of vendor tables, with giveaways, food trucks and face painting. Tickets are sold online and at the door.
