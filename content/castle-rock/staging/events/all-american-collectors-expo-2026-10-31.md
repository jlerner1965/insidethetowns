Hundreds of tables of trading cards, sports memorabilia, Pokemon, comics, toys, LEGO, Funko Pops, video-game and anime collectibles and hobby supplies fill the fairgrounds for the weekend; Saturday is 10 am to 5 pm and the show continues Sunday 1 November. Giveaways, food trucks, face painting and special guests including voice actors are promised. VIP tickets add Friday-evening access and early entry on Saturday.
