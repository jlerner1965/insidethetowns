Sudden Fiction is an independent bookseller in the Ecclesia Market building on Perry Street, selling new releases and bestsellers and running a book club, private parties and events with local authors. The hours above are the store hours printed in the site footer and repeated on its Contact page; the body of its Hours & Directions page lists different, shorter hours (Mon 11 am–3 pm, Tue–Thu 11 am–5:30 pm, Fri–Sat 11 am–7:30 pm, Sun 10 am–4 pm), so call ahead if the time matters.
