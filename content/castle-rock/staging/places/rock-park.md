Visit Castle Rock describes a single-track loop up the Rock with about 370 feet of climbing, from 6,220 to 6,590 feet, on uneven ground, with views over downtown, the I-25 corridor, Pikes Peak and the Front Range. To be confirmed on the Town’s own page.
