Ecclesia gives an old Methodist Episcopal church a second life as a set of independent businesses on two levels: Sinners & Saints cocktail lounge and Wild + Blume flower bar upstairs; Arayes Grill, BAGL, Apres Coffee, The Compas and Sudden Fiction Books below. Sinners & Saints has live music on Fridays and DJ nights on Saturdays.
