The County’s fairgrounds host more than 800 events a year, from weddings and trade shows to concerts, festivals and rodeos, in indoor and outdoor spaces that can be rented. Open riding is offered in the indoor arena through the year, and the Douglas County Fair & Rodeo runs in late July and early August (July 24 to August 2 in 2026). The office is behind the flagpole in front of the Event Center.
