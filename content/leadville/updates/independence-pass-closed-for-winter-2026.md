CDOT's travel advisory of November 3: Colorado 82 over Independence Pass, between Aspen and Twin Lakes, is closed for the 2026–27 winter season. CDOT first closed the pass on Saturday, October 31, for a winter storm that brought several inches of snow and high winds, then made it the seasonal closure after crews found snow drifts blocking the road, with more storms forecast and on the advice of the Colorado Avalanche Information Center; plowing the switchbacks in winter is unsafe because of avalanche danger. The closure gates near Aspen and Twin Lakes stay shut until CDOT reopens the pass in spring, typically around the Memorial Day holiday weekend, weather permitting. Until after November 15, when the third hunting season ends, the closure is at the Lincoln Creek Gate; after that it moves to the usual gate at mile point 47.25. The pass closed on November 17 last year and on October 31 the year before.
