Leadville's historic center, outdoor access and mountain setting are part of the draw. Housing, services and daily travel depend on the address and the season.

## Begin with the city and county

The [City of Leadville](https://www.leadville-co.gov/) links community development and planning, the Housing Authority, permits and civic information. [Lake County](https://www.lakecountyco.gov/) supplies a separate set of county services and recreation information. Whether a property is inside the city decides which office handles each question.

[Community Development and Planning](https://www.leadville-co.gov/community-development-and-planning) answers questions about a specific address and its intended use. Utility providers and accounts vary by property, even among homes with a Leadville mailing address.

## Housing through the whole year

Ask whether a rental is a year-round home or short-term visitor lodging, and confirm the lease term, utilities, parking and who clears the snow. For a purchase, get the property records and inspections.

The City's Housing Authority runs its own programs; eligibility and availability come from the authority.

## Schools and community

[Lake County School District](https://www.lakecountyschools.net/) publishes school pages, district news and calendars. Enrollment, school assignment and transport depend on the address; the district answers those.

The library, recreation programs and public events on the calendar show the town beyond tourism, and a quiet shoulder-season visit shows routines that a race weekend does not.

## Winter and the route you depend on

Check the regular drives on [COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/), try the work and shopping trips in winter, and ask about snow clearing at home. The city's elevation is part of daily life.

The [Leadville out-of-season guide](/articles/leadville-out-of-season/) and the agencies' notices cover seasonal openings and fire closures, which can close trails while town businesses stay open.

## Make one ordinary-day visit

Check groceries, school and work travel, parking and the services you use regularly. For remote work, ask providers about the specific address and test the connection in the building. The [history](/articles/leadville-history/) and [family](/articles/leadville-with-kids/) guides cover the rest of town.
