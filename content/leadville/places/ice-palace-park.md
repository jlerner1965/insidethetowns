Ice Palace Park is one of the parks the Lake County Public Works Department maintains, at 1017 Harrison Avenue. The county's tourism panel describes it as a grass field with a pavilion and shaded picnic tables, on the Mineral Belt Trail and next to the Lake County Public Library and the National Mining Hall of Fame and Museum, with parking off West 10th Street or Harrison Avenue. Neither page gives hours, rules or restrooms.

The park's address and its upkeep by Public Works were read on Lake County's [Parks and Facilities page](https://lakecountyco.gov/382/Parks-and-Facilities), the description and parking on the county tourism panel's [Ice Palace Park page](https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/listing/ice-palace-park/), on 10 October 2026. Neither page carries a date; the listing rests on the county's current list of the parks it maintains.
