The Tabor Opera House is at 308 Harrison Avenue. Its site lists events and films, a Youth Collective of workshops and free films, and tours on its Visit page (https://taboroperahouse.org/visit); no regular opening hours or tour times were on the pages read. The calendar (https://taboroperahouse.org/events/calendar) on 10 October 2026 showed October Historic Tours running October 2–18, "Actually It Is Rocket Science" on October 10, The Addams Family Musical October 22–24, Beetlejuice on October 30 and The Nightmare Before Christmas on November 1; tickets are sold through its online box office.

The Visit page says public group tours, taking in the museum, the theater, the stage and the dressing rooms, run from late May to the middle of October, Friday through Sunday, with tickets online or at the door; private tours are $40 a person or $100 for a group of four, free for children 10 and under, booked by email or at 719-486-8409. Beginning October 11, it says, stage and dressing-room access may be limited or unavailable while The Addams Family is prepared. An accessible entrance on the south side reaches the auditorium and part of the tour, and the Harrison Avenue gift shop and first-floor storefront room are accessible; the upper floors are not.
