The Harrison Avenue Before & After is a morning coffee bar and an evening cocktail bar, open Sunday–Thursday 7 am–9 pm and Friday–Saturday 7 am–10 pm. Coffee food runs 7 am–2 pm: breakfast burritos in bacon, chorizo, vegetarian and vegan versions, plus bagel and croissant sandwiches. Bar food, 12:30–7:30 pm, is flatbreads and smaller snacks. Online ordering has its own availability, separate from the bar’s hours. The operator has other Leadville locations with their own hours.
