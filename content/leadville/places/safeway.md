Leadville's Safeway is on North Poplar Way near Mountain View Drive, open every day from 6 am to 11 pm. The pharmacy keeps shorter hours: weekdays 9 to 8, Saturday 9 to 5 and Sunday 10 to 5, closed each day from 1:30 to 2. The store has a deli, a bakery that takes custom cake orders, meat and seafood counters, and beer, wine and liquor. Orders can be picked up through DriveUp & Go or delivered, and SNAP EBT is accepted in the store and online.
