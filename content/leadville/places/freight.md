FREIGHT is an indoor, heated gathering hall in Leadville's old freight depot at 320 East 9th Street. It hosts weddings, concerts, dances, storytelling, meetings and private parties, holds up to 220 people, and runs a full bar under its own liquor license; it has no in-house caterer. Its 13 cabins, hotel-style rooms with kitchenettes, are on the property, and its footer also gives an address for the Slumberyard at FREIGHT, 321 East 11th Street. Its FAQ says the hall is ADA accessible but the historic floors are uneven, that its peak season is May through December, and that it is open year-round.

Its Boxcar Bar, in a restored boxcar, posts hours of Thursday to Sunday, 5 to 9 pm, which may shift for private events. The calendar on 30 October 2026 showed it open on those nights into January, Thanksgiving included, with Halloween at FREIGHT on October 31, trivia on November 6, a paint-and-sip night on November 8, and the annual Motherlode Holiday Market on December 11 (3–8 pm) and December 12–13 (noon–5 pm). Check the calendar for the night you want.

Address, phone, the Boxcar Bar's hours and the event dates were read on FREIGHT's [home page](https://freightleadville.com/) and its event calendar, capacity and house rules on its [FAQ page](https://freightleadville.com/freight-faqs/), on 30 October 2026; the dated November and December 2026 calendar is the evidence it is operating.
