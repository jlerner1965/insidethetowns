St. Vincent Health is the hospital of the St. Vincent General Hospital District, at 816 West 4th Street. Its emergency department is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, staffed by board-certified emergency providers in partnership with CarePoint Health. The emergency entrance, with its own patient parking, is around the back of the hospital. In an emergency, call 911.

The district runs its own ambulance service, with paramedics and EMTs answering 911 calls around the clock across Lake County's 400-square-mile service area. The hospital also lists imaging, laboratory, surgery and inpatient care. Primary care is next door, at the St. Vincent Family Health Center in the Legacy Building, the former hospital, at 822 West 4th Street. The main number for both is 719-486-0230.
