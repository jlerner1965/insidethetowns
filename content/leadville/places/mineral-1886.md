Mineral 1886 is the coffee cafe and bar on the ground floor of the Historic Delaware Hotel at 700 Harrison Avenue, open to hotel guests and everyone else; both rooms are first come, with no reservations. The coffee cafe is open every day from 7 am to noon, pouring Dazbog coffee, teas and boba, with breakfast burritos, English muffins baked in-house, muffins and house donuts. The bar is open every night from 5:30 to 10 pm, the kitchen until 9, with fourteen-inch pizzas from a House Pie to a Colorado Green Chile, smash burgers, wings, salads and shared plates, and cocktails, beer and wine.

Hours, phone and the menus were read on the [Mineral 1886 home page](https://mineral1886.com/) on 9 November 2026. Visit Leadville Twin Lakes' [listing](https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/listing/mineral-1886/) gives the same hours.
