Visit Leadville Twin Lakes, the tourism site for Leadville and Twin Lakes, runs the visitor center at 460 Harrison Avenue. Its home page now gives winter hours of 10 am to 4 pm, Thursday to Monday, without dates; its [Visitor Guide & Maps page](https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/visitor-guide-maps/) says the center is open daily in summer and "extended weekends in winter," and links the official visitor guide and Lake County, Turquoise Lake and Twin Lakes hiking maps and summer and winter biking maps. The site carries the area's community events calendar (https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/events/), a business directory, and a "Know Before You Go" page on current fire-related closures (https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/know-before-you-go/).

On 30 October 2026 the home page was offering an Octoberville gift card promotion: a $50 bonus card with a $100 gift card, while supplies last.

Address, phone and winter hours were read on the [Visit Leadville Twin Lakes home page](https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/), and the summer and winter pattern on its Visitor Guide & Maps page, on 30 October 2026.
