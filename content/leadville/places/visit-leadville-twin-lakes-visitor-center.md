Visit Leadville Twin Lakes, the tourism site for Leadville and Twin Lakes, runs the visitor center at 460 Harrison Avenue. Its home page gives summer hours of 10 am to 5 pm daily and does not list off-season hours. The site carries the area's community events calendar (https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/events/), a business directory, and a "Know Before You Go" page on current fire-related closures (https://www.leadvilletwinlakes.com/know-before-you-go/).

On 10 October 2026 the home page was promoting OctoberVille, a month-long celebration on Harrison Avenue with a rescheduled fireworks show and parade on October 17, and weekly Fall Watch 2026 color updates.
