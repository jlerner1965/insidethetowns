High Mountain Pies is at 115 West 4th Street and says it has served pizza in Leadville for more than twenty years. Its site lists pizza, wings, salads, ribs and garlic bread, with beer, wine and sodas, for dine-in, takeout or delivery; orders for takeout and delivery are taken by phone. The home page also says the patio is open.

Address, phone and hours (11 am to 10 pm every day) were read on the restaurant's [home page](https://www.hmpies.com/) on 10 October 2026. The site carries no dated post (its footer reads 2023), so the second source is the City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars), which lists it among the town's restaurants; that page carries no date of its own.
