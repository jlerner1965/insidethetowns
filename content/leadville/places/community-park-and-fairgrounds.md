Lake County’s park and fairgrounds on the west side of town, at West 6th Street and McWethy Drive. The County lists a rodeo arena, a children’s playground, indoor restrooms and a synthetic-turf sports field it calls the highest in the world, with the Mineral Belt Trail and the pool immediately adjacent.

The County publishes no hours for the park, and its page carries an availability calendar for the facility: check it before counting on the field, and check the pool’s own listing before counting on the pool.
