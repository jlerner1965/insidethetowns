Two Mile Brewing, at 101 Harrison Avenue, bills itself as America's highest brewery, the name a nod to Leadville's elevation. Its own site gives the address, the phone (719-293-2766) and the hours: Thursday 3 to 9 pm, Friday to Sunday 11 am to 9 pm, closed Monday to Wednesday. The site is an online-ordering page with menus and gift cards and shows nothing to a plain reader, so it was opened in a browser on 30 October 2026; it carries a 2026 copyright line and no dated notice.

Leadville Main Street's [Shops and Services page](https://www.leadvillemainstreet.com/shops-and-services/) and the City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars) list it too.
