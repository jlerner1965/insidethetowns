Inn the Clouds, five blocks east of Harrison Avenue on East 7th Street, has shared accommodation and private rooms with either shared or private bathrooms. Some rooms are in a second building about 15 feet from the main house, where the amenities and shared bathrooms are; some are on a lower level reached by stairs. Check-in is at 3 pm and check-out at 10 am. Ask the inn about a specific room if stairs or a private bathroom matter. The inn’s email is stay@inntheclouds.com.

Check-in times and room details were read on the inn’s [booking page](https://hotels.cloudbeds.com/en/reservation/1bnzrC), and the phone, email and directions on its [contact page](https://www.stayinntheclouds.com/contact-us), on 11 October 2026.
