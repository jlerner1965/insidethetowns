The Leadville Railroad runs from the depot at 326 East 7th Street. Its site says: "We run Daily trips from Memorial Day weekend through September. October–April we run weekly rides." Departure times are on its booking calendar. Its Schedule page lists 2026 spring, summer and fall fares of $60 for an adult and $40 for a child 4 to 12 in coach and $90 in the lounge car, for trips of 2.5 hours unless noted; engine and caboose seats cost extra in summer and fall. Oktoberfest trips (2.5 hours) run on October weekends, the Holiday Express (two hours, from $80) starts November 14, 2026, and the Winter Express is a two-hour round trip. On 30 October the home page still said summer 2027 tickets would be available in late October 2026.

The older address leadville-train.com redirects here.
