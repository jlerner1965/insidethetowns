Cycles of Life at 309 Harrison Avenue sells, services, ships and rents bikes. Its rental page lists Jamis Explorer 2 comfort bikes, 21-speed with a helmet included, at $40 a day for riding around town and the Mineral Belt Trail, with geared 24- and 26-inch kids' bikes, full-suspension, gravel and e-bikes; in winter it rents fat bikes and offers two-hour fat-biking lessons. Prices are from the rental page as read in October 2026.

In winter the service department adds ski and snowboard tuning (a wax from $20 for cross-country skis to $30 for a snowboard, edges and wax from $40) and rents classic and skate Nordic packages for $45 a day; it asks for reservations by phone at 719-486-5533.

Address, phone and hours were read on the shop's [home page](https://www.colbikes.com/), rentals and winter services on its [rentals page](https://www.colbikes.com/rentalsdemos) and [winter services page](https://www.colbikes.com/winter-ski-services), on 10 October 2026. The site carries no dated post, so the second source is the City of Leadville's [Retail Merchants page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/retail-merchants), which lists it; that page carries no date of its own.
