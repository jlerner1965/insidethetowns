The Leadville Ranger District of the Pike and San Isabel National Forests has its office at 810 Front Street. Its page gives the hours as 8 am to 4:30 pm, closed on federal holidays, and the phone as 719-486-0749. Its district page says the district covers 286,000 acres, including Mt. Elbert, Colorado's highest peak at 14,433 feet, seven other fourteeners, and parts of the Mt. Massive, Holy Cross, Collegiate Peaks and Buffalo Peaks wildernesses. The office page itself was last updated March 26, 2025. On 30 October 2026 the Forest's alerts page carried a Willow Fire area closure for the district, from south of Turquoise Lake to the Mount Massive trailhead and Halfmoon Creek, in effect October 1 to December 31, 2026. As a federal office it is closed for Veterans Day (November 11), Thanksgiving (November 26) and Christmas Day (December 25).

Current closures are on the Forest's alerts page (https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/psicc/alerts). Address, phone and hours from the [district office page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/psicc/offices/leadville-ranger-district), the district description from its [About the district page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/psicc/about-area/leadville-ranger-district), both read 30 October 2026.
