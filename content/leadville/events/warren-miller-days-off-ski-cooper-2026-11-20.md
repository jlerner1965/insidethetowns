A family-friendly screening of the 77th Warren Miller film, Days Off, hosted by Katie Burrell and presented by Follow the Footsteps, to kick off the ski season. Friday, November 20, 7 to 9:30 pm at the Cooper Lodge; concessions for sale. Tickets are $33, bought ahead on Ski Cooper's page, and the proceeds pay for Learn to Glide, the free ski day (lift ticket, rentals, lesson and lunch) for 4th and 5th graders in the Lake County School District.
