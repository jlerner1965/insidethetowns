Leadville Main Street's annual Parade of Lights comes down Harrison Avenue in downtown Leadville on Friday, December 4, 2026, at 6 pm. It is free to watch.

## Where to watch

The parade is on Harrison Avenue in the historic downtown. The listing gives no start or end point, so anywhere along the avenue downtown is the best information there is for now.

## What is not published yet

The Visit Leadville Twin Lakes listing says more details are to come. As of November 12 it gave no route, lineup, end time, road closure times or parking directions, and nothing on how to enter a float. Leadville Main Street's own site had no page for this year's parade.

## Who runs it

Leadville Main Street, the downtown program that also ran OctoberVille. Questions go to 719-398-1312 or leadvillemainstreet@gmail.com.
