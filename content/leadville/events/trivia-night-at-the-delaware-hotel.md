A new weekly trivia night in the lobby bar of the Delaware Hotel on Harrison Avenue, every Thursday at 7 pm, with happy hour from 5 to 7. Teams of up to six play on their phones, so no pen or paper is needed, and each week has a "Leadville Legends" round on the town's history. The winning team gets prizes and bites from the Mineral. The tourism calendar lists it through December 3, Thanksgiving included; no price is given.
