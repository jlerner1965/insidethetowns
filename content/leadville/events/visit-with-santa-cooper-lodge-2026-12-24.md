Santa stops at Ski Cooper on Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, from 11:30 am to 12:30 pm, in the main dining room on the second floor of the Cooper Lodge. Ski Cooper's page invites families to come chat with him before his long night; it gives no price and the visit is inside the lodge, so a lift ticket is not mentioned as required.
