The Alpine Orchestra, the community orchestra of the upper Arkansas Valley since 1991, opens its fall series in Leadville on Friday, November 13, at 7 pm at The Old Church on Harrison Avenue. The concert is free; donations are appreciated. The orchestra's own site gives the series dates, November 13 to 15, and names The Old Church as its Leadville hall, with the Salida and Buena Vista concerts on the Saturday and Sunday; the 7 pm time is from the Visit Leadville Twin Lakes calendar listing.
