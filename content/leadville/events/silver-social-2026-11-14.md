The museum's family-friendly year-end party, Saturday, November 14 from 5 to 8 pm, with food, drinks, a kid-friendly mining movie and prizes, celebrating mining's part in Leadville's heritage. Tickets are $30 for adults over 21, with two drink tickets, and $30 for ages 13 to 20, with two non-alcoholic drink tickets; children under 13 are $10, and members pay half. The museum's page moved the start from 6 to 5 pm between 10 October and 1 November; IDs and membership may be checked at the door.
