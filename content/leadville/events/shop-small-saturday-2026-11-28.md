Leadville Main Street's Small Business Saturday, the Saturday after Thanksgiving. Pick up a Shop Small passport at the Leadville Visitor Center on Harrison Avenue between noon and 4 pm on November 28, collect stamps at the participating local shops, and hand the stamped passport back by 4 pm for a chance at a raffle prize. Businesses have until Monday, November 23, to send their sales and promotions to Leadville Main Street so it can share them.

Questions go to Leadville Main Street at 719-398-1312 or leadvillemainstreet@gmail.com.
