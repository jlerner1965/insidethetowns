The Lake County Public Library is at 1115 Harrison Avenue. Its site gives the hours as Monday 10 am to 6 pm, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 10 am to 5 pm, Wednesday 10 am to 6 pm, Saturday noon to 4 pm, and closed Sunday. The Events & Programs page (https://www.lakecountypubliclibrary.org/events-programs) describes programs for kids (ages 0–11), teens (12–17) and adults, and calls Banned Books Week its annual flagship event; the dates are on the library's events calendar, which loads in the browser and could not be read for this guide.

The only dates on the pages read were a 2026 copyright and a notice of service disruptions on August 3 and 4 with no year, so check the calendar or call before relying on the hours.

Hours and phone from the library's [home page](https://www.lakecountypubliclibrary.org/), read 10 October 2026.
