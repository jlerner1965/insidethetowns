Before & After is the coffee and cocktail bar the owners of Treeline Kitchen opened in 2020 directly across the street from the restaurant, according to Treeline's home page. Its own site gives the Harrison Avenue location's hours as Sunday to Thursday 7 am to 9 pm and Friday and Saturday 7 am to 10 pm, takes online orders and gift cards, and lists two more coffee shops under the name: one at the CMC campus (Monday to Thursday 8 am to 7 pm, Friday to Sunday 8 am to 4 pm, "now open") and one at Mtn View.

Hours were read on [Before & After's home page](https://www.beforeandafterleadville.com/) on 10 October 2026; its /about, /contact and /menu pages answer 404. The address 612 Harrison Avenue is from Leadville Main Street's [Shops and Services page](https://www.leadvillemainstreet.com/shops-and-services/), and the City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars) lists it.
