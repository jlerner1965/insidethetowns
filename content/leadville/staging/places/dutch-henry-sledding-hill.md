Lake County’s sledding and tubing hill at 999 South Highway 24. The County says the hill is completely free if you bring your own sled, and that tube rental is available on Saturdays and Sundays during the winter. Its operating dates and hours for the 2026–27 season were not published when this was read, so the listing waits for them.
