Buchi Cafe Cubano lists two shops, one at 2651 West 38th Avenue in Denver and one at 401 Harrison Avenue in Leadville, phone 719-917-8188. The menu on its site is Cuban coffee (Cafe Cubano sweetened with raw sugar, cortadito, cafe con leche, and no decaf), breakfast sandwiches on pressed Cuban bread, lunch sandwiches such as the Cuban Mix and Pan con Lechon, empanadas, rice and beans, platanos maduros, key lime pie and tiramisu, with mojitos, margaritas and other cocktails. The site gives no prices and does not say which items the Leadville shop serves.

Leadville Main Street's [Shops and Services page](https://www.leadvillemainstreet.com/shops-and-services/), last modified in 2024, lists Buchi at 401 Harrison; the City's restaurant list does not. Address, phone and menu were read on [Buchi's home page](https://www.buchicafecubano.com/) on 10 October 2026, where the Leadville "Place Order" link is hidden and leads to a Square page that answers 404.
