City on a Hill Coffee & Espresso lists its Leadville shop at 508 Harrison Avenue with hours of 6 am to 3 pm Monday to Friday and 6 am to 4 pm Saturday and Sunday, and a second shop at 420 Main Street in Cañon City. The site sells its coffee online, with blends such as Mt. Massive Medium Roast and Father Dyer's Dark Roast. Its Leadville menu lists breakfast burritos ($9, limited each day, also as a gluten-free bowl), quiche on a potato crust ($4), baked goods, grab-and-go sandwiches, wraps and salads ($8), and from about October to June a soup of the day. No phone number appears on any page read.

Address and hours from the [shop's home page](https://cityonahillcoffee.com/), the menu from its [Leadville menu page](https://cityonahillcoffee.com/pages/city-on-a-hill-leadville-menu), both read 10 October 2026. The City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars) lists the shop.
