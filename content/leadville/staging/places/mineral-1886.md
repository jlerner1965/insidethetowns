Mineral 1886 is the bar and coffee cafe on the ground floor of the Historic Delaware Hotel at 700 Harrison Avenue; hotel guests and the public are both welcome, and seating in both rooms is first come. The cafe pours Dazbog coffee, teas and boba, with breakfast burritos, English muffins baked in-house, muffins and house donuts. In the evening the bar serves fourteen-inch pizzas from a House Pie to a Colorado Green Chile, smash burgers, wings, salads and shared plates, with cocktails, beer and wine. The site's banner says the full site is coming soon.

Its hours are not settled: the main text of the home page says the coffee cafe is open 7 am to noon every day and the bar 5:30 to 10 pm every night, with the kitchen taking last orders at 9 pm, while a separate hours block lower on the same page lists breakfast 7–11 am and lunch 11:30 am–2 pm daily and dinner Thursday to Sunday 5–9:30 pm. The Delaware Hotel itself is staged separately.

Address, phone and the menus were read on the [Mineral 1886 home page](https://mineral1886.com/) on 10 October 2026. Its only date is a 2026 copyright; the City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars) lists it, which would be the second source once the hours are settled.
