Treeline Kitchen at 615 Harrison Avenue is a family business opened in 2017 by Eric and Christine, who also opened Before & After, the coffee and cocktail bar across the street, in 2020, and added a coffee shop at the CMC campus in 2025 and the Mtn View Coffee Shop in 2026. Its rooftop, Above Treeline, which it calls America's highest-elevation rooftop bar, serves food and drink seasonally, May to September from noon to sunset, weather permitting. Reservations are taken through Resy up to 30 days ahead and guarantee the inside dining room; outdoor tables can be requested but not reserved, and parties of seven or more, events and catering are by phone. Happy hour is 3:30 to 5:30 pm.

The hours are the open question: the home page's banner reads "open wed-sun 3:30-9:00 pm," but the hours block on the home, reservations and contact pages lists every day of the week, 3:30 to 9 pm. The handoff packet gave the address as 615A Harrison Avenue; the restaurant's own pages say 615.

Address, phone, the rooftop season and the reservation rules were read on the restaurant's [home page](https://treelinekitchen.com/), [reservations page](https://treelinekitchen.com/reservations) and [contact page](https://treelinekitchen.com/contact) on 10 October 2026. The home page's mention of the coffee shop added in 2026 is dated evidence that it is operating, and the City of Leadville's [Restaurants & Bars page](https://www.leadville-co.gov/visitors/page/restaurants-bars) also lists it.
