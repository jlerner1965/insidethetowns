Elevation Goods is a shop at 324 Harrison Avenue that also sells online. Its About page says every item comes from a small business and that 90 percent of its makers are from Colorado; the online categories run from clothing, jewelry and accessories to home goods, food and drink, candles, wellness, paper goods and kids' things. The contact page gives summer shop hours of Sunday to Thursday 10 am to 5 pm and Friday and Saturday 10 am to 6 pm, says unusual closings are posted on Instagram, and gives no phone.
