An after-hours open house at the Healy House Museum on Friday, November 6, 3 to 5 pm, for 10th Mountain Division Day, with free admission to Winter Warriors: The 10th Mountain Division in World War II, the traveling exhibition that began its tour at Healy House, plus coffee and cookies. Parking is in front of the museum on East 10th Street, with overflow on West 10th across Harrison Avenue. The listing is on the Visit Leadville Twin Lakes calendar; History Colorado's own calendar could not be read.
