The Y teaches swimming in Boulder, Lafayette and Johnstown, organized by age, level and location, starting with self-rescue skills and water confidence and moving on to strokes. In **Swim Starters** a parent is in the water with a child of six months to three; **preschool** lessons take 3 to 5-year-olds; **school-age** lessons 6 to 12; **Swim Skills & Drills** builds technique and endurance for 6 to 17; there are adult lessons from 18 and private or semi-private lessons from six months up. The Y’s lesson flow chart, linked from the page, helps pick a level. The page does not say which levels each Y offers, or give session dates or prices. The Johnstown Y’s pools are a lap pool and a leisure pool with slides, diving boards and a splash pad.
