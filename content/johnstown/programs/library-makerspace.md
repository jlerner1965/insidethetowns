The library lists two Glowforge Pro laser cutters, two Cricut Maker die-cutters, two Ultimaker S3 3D printers and two button machines for its Makerspace, with resources for learning each one; the Milliken branch keeps its own Makerspace hours. The limits are set out on its page: one or two projects and one laser project a visit, 60 minutes on the computer, one 3D print a week of under six hours. The library also runs monthly Makerspace classes with supplies included, registered online, one class a month per person. The library’s teen and tween pages say children 10 and older may use the Makerspace without an adult.
