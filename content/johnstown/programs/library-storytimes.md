The library’s two weekly sessions for the youngest children. **Music & Movement Storytime** runs twice every Monday, at 10 and at 1, with stories, songs and movement on a theme that changes each week (pumpkins on October 26, numbers on November 2). **Babytime** on Thursdays at 11 is 15 minutes of songs, rhymes, lap bounces and a story, then 45 minutes of open play, designed for babies who are not yet walking and the adults with them. The library is closed November 11 and on Thanksgiving, November 26, so there is no Babytime that week. Both are on this guide’s calendar with the next date: [Music & Movement](/events/music-and-movement-storytime/) and [Babytime](/events/babytime-library/).
