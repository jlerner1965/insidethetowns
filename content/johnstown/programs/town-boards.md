The Town "invites residents to participate on Boards, Commissions, and Committees to act in an advisory capacity to the Town Council". The [application](https://johnstownco.gov/200/Boards-Commissions) is one online form; it asks which body you want, how long you have lived in Johnstown, and whether you can commit to evening meetings two to three times a month and to a full term.

**Planning and Zoning Commission.** Seven members appointed by the Town Council. It reviews annexations, zoning, subdivision plats and other development applications, and updates the Johnstown Comprehensive Plan. Meetings are in person on the second and fourth Wednesdays at 6 pm unless otherwise posted; the dates are on [this guide's calendar](/events/planning-zoning-2026-11-18/).

**Cemetery Committee.** Advises the Council on running and caring for the cemetery and reviews its rules when needed. The Town posts each meeting date.

**Housing Authority.** Its board oversees the Columbine Complex, 24 apartments for older residents at 202 North Greeley Avenue, and meets quarterly.

**Board of Appeals.** Hears appeals of the Building Official's orders and decisions on how the Town's codes are applied. It meets as posted.

**Public Art Committee.** Newly established to guide the Town's Public Art Plan. The first application period has closed, but the Town says it will "continue to welcome applications", keep them on file and consider them as openings come up. The [committee's page](https://johnstownco.gov/696/Public-Art-Committee) has the form; experience in the arts "is welcome but is not required".
