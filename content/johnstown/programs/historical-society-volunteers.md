The Johnstown Historical Society runs the [Historic Parish House and Museum](/places/parish-house-museum/) and says plainly, "We Need Your Help". Some jobs are one-time, such as helping at an event or fundraiser; some need training; others "only need a simple explanation or brief orientation".

- **Events.** Setting up, breaking down and cleaning up at the society's annual events: Johnstown's BBQ Day on the first Saturday of June, the Cemetery Crawl on the last Saturday of October, the Annual Meeting of the Membership each November and the Holiday Open House on the first Saturday of December.
- **Docents.** Trained docents open the museum, greet visitors and give the tour of the house.
- **Collections and oral history.** Recording and researching the objects the society acquires, and scheduling, filming and transcribing interviews.
- **Research.** Historic properties, past businesses and residents; the society says volunteers "can work on their own schedule and even from home".
- **Behind the scenes.** Data entry, general office work, grant research and writing, publicity and social media.
- **Committees.** Buildings and Grounds, Collections, Finance, Fundraising/Events, Memberships/Volunteers and Publicity/Marketing, each with a board member on it.

To start, fill in the interest form on the [Volunteer page](https://jhsco.org/get-involved/volunteer/), or phone or email; the society says it will match your skills and interests to what it needs. The same page has a printable list of the jobs.
