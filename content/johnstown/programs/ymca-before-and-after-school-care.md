The district’s own [before and after school care page](https://www.weldre5j.org/families/before-after-school-care) sends families to the Y, which runs the programs in the schools: STEAM, art and social-emotional activities from a Pop Culture Hero Coalition curriculum, plus homework help, sports and games, and a snack each day. Part-time after-school plans are back for 2026–27. Families who sign up for child care get a free family membership to the Y, activated online or at the front desk. Sibling discounts are 10% for a second child and 15% for each after; the Y takes CCAP and has its own financial assistance, which must be approved before registering online.
