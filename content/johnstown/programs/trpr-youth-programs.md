Thompson Rivers Parks and Recreation is the recreation partner Weld RE-5J’s [athletics page](https://www.weldre5j.org/families/athletics-activities) and the Town’s Neighbor Nights page both name. Its **Day Off Discovery Camps** run 9 am to noon for ages 5 to 8, $25, on days the district’s schools are closed, with drop-off and pick-up at the Milliken Athletic Complex; the next is December 4 (“Winter Wonderland”), with online registration open through December 2. On a Saturday each month it runs a **Park Discovery Day** for the same ages, 9 to noon, $25, visiting two parks around Northern Colorado: on December 5, Main Community Park and Boardwalk Park in Windsor. At the Johnstown Activity Center: **intermediate fencing** for 12 and up, November 2 to 25. At the Milliken Athletic Complex: **youth jiu-jitsu** for 4 to 11 on Tuesdays and Thursdays, November 3 to 19, and a monthly **Makers Club** for 6th to 8th graders (macramé gnome ornaments on November 12). Every listing is on its registration site with the openings left.
