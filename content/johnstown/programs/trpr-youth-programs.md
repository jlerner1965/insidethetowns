Thompson Rivers Parks and Recreation is the recreation partner Weld RE-5J’s [athletics page](https://www.weldre5j.org/families/athletics-activities) and the Town’s Neighbor Nights page both name. Its **Day Off Discovery Camps** run 9 am to noon for ages 5 to 8, $25, on days the district’s schools are closed, with drop-off and pick-up at the Milliken Athletic Complex: October 30 (“Halloween Spooktacular,” full), November 9 (“All Things Fall”) and December 4 (“Winter Wonderland”). On Saturdays it runs **Park Discovery Days** for the same ages, 9 to noon, visiting two parks around Northern Colorado (November 7 and December 5). At the Johnstown Activity Center: **Intro to Cheer** for 4 to 7 (Wednesdays at 4) and 8 to 11 (Wednesdays at 5) through December 9, and **fencing** for 12 and up: the beginners’ class on Mondays at 5 through November 30, the intermediate class Mondays and Wednesdays in November. At the Milliken Athletic Complex: **youth jiu-jitsu** for 4 to 11 on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 5 pm, November 3 to 19, and a monthly **Makers Club** for 6th to 8th graders (macramé gnome ornaments on November 12). Every listing is on its registration site with the openings left.
