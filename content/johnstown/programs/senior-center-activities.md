The Town's Senior Center runs a weekly round of low-cost activities. The Town asks people to sign up on the activity sheet, in person or by phone, and to cancel if they cannot come.

**No charge.** Diamond Dots (bring your own project) every Tuesday at 10 am; cards and pool every Wednesday at 12:30 pm; the book club on the third Friday of the month at 1:30 pm, with the month's book from the Senior Center Coordinator. Blood pressure checks are on the fourth Wednesday, 11 to 11:30 am, with no registration.

**Exercise.** A class for older adults every Tuesday and Friday at 9:15 am, $1 a class; Tai Chi Chih every Thursday at 10 am, $45 for 8 classes; a walking club on Mondays at 8:30 am from June to September, free.

**Friendly Fork lunch.** On Wednesdays, with the Weld County Area Agency on Aging. Reserve or cancel by 9:30 am on the Tuesday before at 970-587-5251. The suggested donation is $5 if you or your spouse or partner is 60 or older; the meal is $14 for anyone 59 and younger. The program has its own registration form at the center, and you do not need to be a Senior Center member.

**Membership.** A suggested $10 a person a year, expiring December 31, paid by cash, check or card on the Town's [membership form](https://johnstownco.gov/414/Membership) or at the center. Scholarships are available through the Coordinator. Members get the monthly newsletter and activity calendar by email before non-members. The center also runs trips; ask the center what is coming up.
