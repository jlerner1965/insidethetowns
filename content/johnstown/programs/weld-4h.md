Weld County 4-H is run from the CSU Extension office at 525 N. 15th Ave in Greeley. The county’s club list puts two clubs in Johnstown, under its Johnstown, Milliken and Berthoud area: **Calico ’N Jeans**, meeting second Mondays at Elwell Elementary, and **Pawsitive Directions**, meeting second Tuesdays at the YMCA in Johnstown; each has a leader to call and an email on the [area’s club page](https://www.weld4h.org/About-4-H/Clubs/Johnstown-Milliken-and-Berthoud-Area). Enrollment for the 2026–27 year opened in 4-H Online on October 1. Families enroll online and pay the club directly, $50 a member and $40 a Cloverbud; returning members who miss January 15 pay $60 and $50, and the last day for new members, and for Weld County Fair exhibitors, is March 1, 2027. Projects run from livestock and horses to robotics, photography, foods and nutrition, and woodworking; the county posts the 2026–27 project list on its home page.
