The school’s athletics pages list each team by season. **Fall**: football, volleyball, boys’ golf, boys’ soccer, gymnastics, cross country and softball. **Winter**: boys’ and girls’ basketball, boys’ and girls’ wrestling, and girls’ swim and dive. **Spring**: baseball, track and field, girls’ golf and girls’ soccer. **Cheer** and **poms** run year-round. To play, a student registers on ArbiterSports, using the school’s how-to guide, with a physical dated and signed within the last year; athletes may practice before the fee is paid but not compete. The school emails the clearance once the registration is processed. The pages do not give this year’s fee or the winter start dates. The games, records and scores are on [the Roughriders’ page here](/sports/).
