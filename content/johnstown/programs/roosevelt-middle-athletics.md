The school lists its teams by season: **fall** cross country (6th to 8th grade), football and volleyball (7th and 8th); **winter** girls’ basketball (7th and 8th, all skill levels, January 4 to February 28), boys’ basketball (7th and 8th, first practice October 12) and wrestling (6th to 8th, January 4 to March 7); **spring** track and field from March 9. Students who want fall soccer or flag football, and boys who want spring volleyball, can play for Knowledge Quest Academy’s Blazerstangs. Every athlete must be registered and approved before the first practice, and coaches send updates through the Remind app with a code for each team on the athletics page. The school’s vision statement says every interested student will have a chance to play regardless of ability or money.
