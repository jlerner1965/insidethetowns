The library’s Wednesday art session for its youngest patrons, one project a week chosen to make a mess: a stick scarecrow on November 18, a name turkey on November 25. There is none on November 11, when the library is closed. Each session is its own listing on this guide’s calendar, as it is on the library’s: [October 28](/events/messy-art-2026-10-28/), [November 4](/events/messy-art-2026-11-04/), [November 18](/events/messy-art-2026-11-18/) and [November 25](/events/messy-art-2026-11-25/).
