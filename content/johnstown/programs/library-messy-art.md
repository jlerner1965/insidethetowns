The library’s Wednesday art session for its youngest patrons, one project a week chosen to make a mess: stick scarecrows on November 18, name turkeys on November 25, paper snowflakes on December 2 and a melted snowman on December 9. Each session is its own listing on this guide’s calendar, as it is on the library’s: [November 18](/events/messy-art-2026-11-18/), [November 25](/events/messy-art-2026-11-25/), [December 2](/events/messy-art-2026-12-02/) and [December 9](/events/messy-art-2026-12-09/).
