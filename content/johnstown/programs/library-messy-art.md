The library’s Wednesday art session for its youngest patrons, one project a week chosen to make a mess: a stick scarecrow on November 18, a name turkey on November 25. There is none on November 11, when the library is closed. Each date is on this guide’s calendar, starting with [the next Messy Art](/events/messy-art/).
