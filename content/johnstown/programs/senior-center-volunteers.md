The Senior Center's volunteer page is a list of jobs, and the way in is a conversation with the Senior Center Coordinator, who also takes "other ideas". The list:

- **Friendly Fork**, the Wednesday lunch run with the Weld County Area Agency on Aging: set-up (sanitizing and setting tables, the coffee and tea bar), clean-up (clearing and washing dishes) and registration (checking people in and the program's paperwork).
- **Bingo**: calling the numbers.
- **Christmas Boutique Planning Committee**: planning runs all year for the event on the first Saturday of December.
- **Annual Holiday Lunches**, held in place of the Friendly Fork meals at the end of December, and the **Annual Clean Up Day**.
- **Donor relations**: contacting agencies, businesses and organizations about donations.
- **Event and activity planning**: ideas, and help finding events and restaurants.
- **Flower bed maintenance**, the center's **library**, and **upkeep**: recycling, organizing, windows and storage ("just pick one or do as many as you'd like").
- **Hay's Market receipts**: organizing, typing and submitting amounts; computer skills required.
- **Johnstown Senior Center Board**: the Town notes "commitment and application needed".
