The Y’s sports pages name Johnstown for **basketball** (learn-to-play for beginners, then leagues working on ball handling, shooting and footwork), **soccer** (learn-to-play, leagues, clinics and scrimmages) and **Sports of All Sorts** for ages 3 to 5. The Y also lists flag football, baseball and T-ball, and volleyball without naming a location. From its sports FAQ: team placement, the coach and practice time come about two weeks before games begin and the game schedule about a week before; practices are at the Ys and area school sites, depending on space; on a bad-weather day the weather hotline is updated two hours before the program. The pages do not give this winter’s dates or prices.
