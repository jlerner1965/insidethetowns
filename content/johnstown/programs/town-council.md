**Attend or comment.** The Council is the mayor and six members, elected from the Town at large. It meets on the first and third Mondays at 7 pm unless otherwise posted; when the Monday is a holiday, the Town says the meeting "will likely be pushed to the following Wednesday". In the Town's words, "Anyone is welcome to join the meeting in person, watch it on the website, or submit a remote public comment". Written comments go in on the [public comment form](https://johnstownco.gov/FormCenter/Town-Council-9/Online-Public-Comment-Submission-57) before 6 pm on the meeting night, and those not about a public hearing are read during the public comment period. Agendas are posted before each meeting and minutes after approval, on the Town's [CivicClerk portal](https://johnstownco.portal.civicclerk.com/). The next meeting is [December 7](/events/town-council-2026-12-07/).

**The vacant seat.** The Council took applications to fill a vacant seat, for a term that runs until the municipal election of April 4, 2028; the deadline has passed. An applicant must be a US citizen, a registered elector of the Town and a resident for at least 12 months, and cannot be a salaried Town employee while in office. Applications were due to the Town Clerk by 10 am on Monday, November 16, 2026. The Town said applicants may be interviewed on the evening of Monday, November 23.
