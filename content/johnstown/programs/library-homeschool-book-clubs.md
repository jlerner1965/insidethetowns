The library runs two homeschool book clubs, listed on its [tween](https://johnstownmillikenpubliclibraries.us/programs/tweens/) and [teen](https://johnstownmillikenpubliclibraries.us/programs/teens/) pages: one for 3rd to 5th graders and one for 7th to 12th graders, each reading award-winning and classic books. The pages do not give meeting days or a branch; ask when you contact the library. Teens who want community service hours can also volunteer at the library, helping with events, organizing materials or tutoring younger patrons; the application is linked from the teen page.
