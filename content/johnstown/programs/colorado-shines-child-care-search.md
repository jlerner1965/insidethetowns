Colorado Shines is the Colorado Department of Early Childhood’s search of licensed child care. Search by location and filter by type (child care center, preschool program, family child care) and by Universal Preschool. For help, contact the program or call the Colorado Shines Child Care Referral Line at 877-338-2273.
