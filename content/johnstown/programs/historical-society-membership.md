The Johnstown Historical Society is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, founded in 1988, that keeps the town's history and runs the [Historic Parish House and Museum](/places/parish-house-museum/) at 701 Charlotte Street. It says it relies on fundraisers, the Town of Johnstown, grants, volunteers and donations. Membership is by the year: $20 for an individual, $30 for a family, $30 for a church or club, $100 for a business, and $15 or $25 for an individual or family at 65 and over. Join on the [Become a Member](https://jhsco.org/get-involved/become-member/) form.

The society holds an Annual Meeting of the Membership each November and puts out a quarterly email newsletter, The Impact; sign up on its site. Its public events through the year are Johnstown's BBQ Day in June, the Cemetery Crawl in October and the [Holiday Open House](/events/holiday-open-house-parish-house-2026/) on Saturday, December 5, from 1 to 4 pm, which is free. The museum is free to visit and open Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Saturdays from 9 am to noon. To give time as well, see [volunteering with the society](/get-involved/#program-historical-society-volunteers).
