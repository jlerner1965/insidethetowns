The Y runs a full-day camp at the Johnstown Y on the days Weld RE-5J schools are closed, matched to the district’s calendar: October 30, November 9, November 23 to 25, December 4 and 18, December 21 to 23 and 28 to 30, January 4 and 18, February 1, 15 and 26, March 12, spring break March 15 to 19, March 22, April 2 and May 3. The Y closes all its programs on December 24 and 31, and its Thanksgiving camp runs Monday to Wednesday only. Its list also gives **January 5**, which the district’s calendar shows as the day school resumes; check with the Y before counting on it. Campers do sports, arts and crafts, group games, outdoor play and swimming. The camps page does not give the day rate. The Y’s [Weld RE-5J childcare page](https://www.ymcanoco.org/before-after-care/weld-re-5j) says sibling discounts (10% for a second child, 15% for each after) apply to school day off camp, and the Y takes CCAP and has its own financial assistance. More on the building at [the Johnstown YMCA](/places/johnstown-ymca/).
