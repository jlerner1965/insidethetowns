Weld RE-5J runs its early childhood program at two centers, the Elwell Early Learning Center and the Milliken Early Learning Center, with a curriculum for social, emotional, physical, cognitive and language skills. The district’s Early Childhood site says Universal Preschool registration is open for the 2026–27 school year and links its free preschool page. New students in the rest of the district register on [Weld RE-5J’s registration page](https://www.weldre5j.org/registration).
