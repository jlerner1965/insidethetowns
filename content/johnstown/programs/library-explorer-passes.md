The library lends day passes to 17 attractions, among them the American Museum of Western Art, the Butterfly Pavilion, the Colorado History Center, Colorado State Parks, the Denver Zoo, the Denver Firefighters Museum, the Forney Museum of Transportation, the Fort Collins Museum of Discovery, The Gardens at Spring Creek and the Cheyenne Children’s Museum. Passes cannot be reserved: they go first come, first served, one per family per week, to cardholders 18 and older, picked up in person. Each admits up to four; some let more in at no charge and others charge for each extra person.
