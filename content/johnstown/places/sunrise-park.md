A park on North 6th Street at the edge of the fields, with a scenic walking path for a stroll or a jog, a shaded picnic structure and two shelters that can be reserved for parties and reunions. The Town replaced the playground on the park's north side in 2026, under a contract the Town Council awarded in June: a climbing dome, a slide tower with a canopy and a climbing wall on a poured rubber surface.
