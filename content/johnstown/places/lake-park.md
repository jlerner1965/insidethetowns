A quiet park around the Johnstown Reservoir, reached from Highway 60 by High Plains Boulevard and Rocksbury Lane, with street parking in the neighborhood around it. It has a walking path, picnic shelters with electricity, a playground and restrooms. Fishing is allowed with a Town permit (sold at Town Hall on weekdays) for largemouth and smallmouth bass, rainbow trout, channel catfish, bluegill, crappie and carp; no swimming, boating or ice fishing.
