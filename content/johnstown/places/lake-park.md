A quiet park around the Town reservoir at the edge of Old Town, with a walking path, picnic shelters with electricity, a playground and restrooms. Fishing is allowed with a Town permit (sold at Town Hall on weekdays) for largemouth and smallmouth bass, rainbow trout, channel catfish, bluegill, crappie and carp; no swimming, boating or ice fishing.
