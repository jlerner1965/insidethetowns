The sports bar on the Parish Avenue block: a full bar, burgers, wings and bar plates, screens for every game, and late hours on Friday and Saturday.

The web address the restaurant used to publish no longer belongs to it; it posts on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/cassidyssportsgrill/), which carries no hours. The hours above are as they stood on September 18, 2026, and the owner of this guide confirmed the restaurant is open on October 20, 2026.
