The Wingate carries Loveland's name but sits in Johnstown, at 5360 Ronald Reagan Blvd by the I-25 and US 34 interchange. Its own page lists a fitness center, an indoor pool and jetted hot tub, a 24-hour business center, free daily hot breakfast, free WiFi and free parking, non-smoking rooms with larger suites for extended stays, and one meeting room for up to 20. The hotel takes pets.
