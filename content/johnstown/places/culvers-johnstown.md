The Wisconsin chain's restaurant at I-25 and US 34, owned and run by Dillon Brewster: ButterBurgers, cheese curds and frozen custard, with a Flavor of the Day posted on the restaurant's page. The dining room is open 10 am to 10 pm every day; the drive-thru stays open to 10:30, and to 11 on Friday and Saturday.
