The town's grocery store, run by the family whose first store, Hays Mercantile Co., opened in Johnstown in 1929; it also runs Berthoud's. Bakery, deli, meat, produce, floral and sushi, and wine and beer. Weekly deals and digital coupons come through the Hays Market app. Open every day from 6 am to 9 pm; closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Easter.

The pharmacy in the store is [Good Day Pharmacy](https://www.gooddaypharmacy.com/pharmacy-in-johnstown-co), 970-587-1128: Monday to Friday 9 am to 7 pm, Saturday 9 am to 1 pm, closed Sunday.
