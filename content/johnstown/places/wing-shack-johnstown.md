The Johnstown branch of Wing Shack, a regional chain with shops from Greeley to Laramie, on North Parish Avenue in Old Town. Wings in the chain's own sauces, fries (from crinkle cut to sweet potato tots and loaded asado fries), onion rings, cheese sticks and handhelds; order ahead online or call. The location page gives its hours as 11 am to 10 pm without naming the days, so call before a Sunday or a holiday.
