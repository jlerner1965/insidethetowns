Johnstown Jingle is Sunday, December 6, 2026, from 2 to 5 pm in downtown Johnstown, with the community tree lighting at 5:30 pm.

## Schedule

- **2 to 5 pm:** holiday activities, entertainment, vendors, food trucks, and visits with Santa and his reindeer.
- **5:30 pm:** the community tree lighting.

## What is there

The Johnstown Downtown Development Authority describes an afternoon of holiday activities and seasonal entertainment around visits with Santa and his reindeer. Its sponsorship sheet names three of the attractions: Santa's sleigh with Clydesdales, a trackless train billed as the Polar Express, and a photo booth.

Vendor space is limited and chosen by the DDA. Vendors set up inside the Senior Center because of the chance of weather, and those who ask can be placed outside.

## Weather

The event is held snow or shine.

## The toy drive

The Jingle grew out of a community toy drive, and visitors can still give to it at the event. The DDA calls it a giveback event: $100 of every vendor fee goes to Christmas for Johnstown children in need.

## Who runs it

The Johnstown Downtown Development Authority. Questions go to kmonroe@johnstowndda.org. Vendor, food truck and sponsor sign-ups are on the DDA's Johnstown Jingle page.
