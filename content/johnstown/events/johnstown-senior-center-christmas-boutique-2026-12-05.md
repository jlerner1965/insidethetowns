The Town's Senior Center holds its Christmas Boutique on Saturday, December 5, from 9 am to 3 pm at the center on Charlotte Street. The Town's calendar gives the name, date and hours and nothing more; the Senior Center Coordinator is on 970-587-5251. The Historical Society's holiday open house at the Parish House, 701 Charlotte Street, is the same afternoon, 1 to 4.
