The 14th annual Thanksgiving-morning race in Johnstown, from 21 South Parish Avenue: a 1K for children 12 and under at 7:45 am, then a 5K at 8 that you can walk, jog or run. Every registered runner may nominate a family in Johnstown or Milliken to receive a grocery gift card for a holiday meal. Registrations through November 13 include a T-shirt; after that the fee is the same and there is no shirt.
