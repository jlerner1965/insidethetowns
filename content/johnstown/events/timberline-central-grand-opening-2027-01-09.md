Timberline Gymnastics opens its new Johnstown gym, Timberline Central, with a free afternoon for families. Learn about the gym's programs, climb around the open play area and stay for an exhibition by the gym's elite athletes.

The 32,000-square-foot building is next to Scheels. It replaces the gym's Windsor location, whose students move to Central in January.
