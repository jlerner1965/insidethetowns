The Senior Center is Weld County’s vote center in Johnstown: open Monday, November 2, 8 am to 5 pm, and Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 7 am to 7 pm. Vote in person, drop off a mail ballot, register, or get a replacement ballot. Neither county lists a 24-hour drop box in Johnstown; Weld’s nearest is at Milliken Town Hall, 1101 Broad St, and Larimer’s are in Loveland (the county’s Loveland campus, 200 Peridot Ave). A voter registered in the Larimer County part of town uses a Larimer vote center, the nearest in Loveland, 7 am to 7 pm on the 3rd. Ballots must be received, not postmarked, by 7 pm; anyone in line by 7 pm can vote.
