Stories, songs and movement for young children every Monday from 10 to 11 am at the Johnstown library, through December 14. Each week has its own theme: drums on November 16, giving thanks on November 23, feelings on November 30, imagination on December 7 and cookies on December 14.

The library runs the same program again at 1 pm: [Music & Movement Storytime (afternoon)](/events/music-and-movement-storytime-afternoon/).
