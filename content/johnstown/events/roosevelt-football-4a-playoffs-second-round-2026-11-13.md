The Roughriders (9-1) are the No. 1 seed in CHSAA’s 4A state bracket, which gave them a bye through the first round (November 5 to 7). Their first playoff game is the second round, at home on Friday, November 13, at 7 pm, against No. 17 Montbello, which beat No. 16 George Washington 24–17 on November 5. The quarterfinals follow on November 20 and 21.
