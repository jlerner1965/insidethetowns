The Roughriders (9-1) are the No. 1 seed in CHSAA’s 4A bracket and open their playoffs at home after a first-round bye: Friday, November 13, at 7 pm against No. 17 Montbello (8-3), which beat George Washington 24–17 on November 5. CHSAA lists Roosevelt as the home team, and the school’s calendar puts the game at Randall Hess Stadium at the high school. The winner plays in the quarterfinals on November 20 or 21.
