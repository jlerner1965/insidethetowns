The library's calendar carries two Library Closed entries: both the Johnstown and Milliken locations are closed on Wednesday, November 11, and on Thursday, November 26. [Babytime](/events/babytime-library/), the Thursday morning session, skips November 26. On its other days the Johnstown library keeps its posted hours: Monday and Friday 9 am to 5 pm, Tuesday to Thursday 9 am to 8 pm, Saturday 9 am to 1 pm, closed Sunday. Trash pickup does not move. The Town's Garbage and Recycling page says a holiday week with a one-day delay moves Monday's pickup to Tuesday and Wednesday's to Thursday, and the Town's calendar lists these two weeks on the usual days: trash and recycling for homes east of Colorado Boulevard on Wednesday, November 11, then trash and recycling for homes west of it on Monday, November 23, and east of it on Wednesday, November 25. Carts go to the curb by 7 am. The same calendar marks the Planning and Zoning Commission's November 11 meeting canceled; the Commission [meets on November 18](/events/planning-zoning-2026-11-18/) instead.
