Ernest Health’s long-term acute care hospital on Union Street ceased operating as one on October 1, 2026, by its own site. Its notice says the 20 non-ICU beds will be added as inpatient rehabilitation beds at Northern Colorado Rehabilitation Hospital, the sister hospital at 4401 Union St, taking it from 40 beds to 60, on a date it has yet to set. Former patients who want copies of their medical records are told to call the rehabilitation hospital at 970-619-3400. This guide first carried the closing in its weekly notes of October 26; it is here so it stays findable after that week.
