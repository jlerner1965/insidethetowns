Ernest Health’s long-term acute care hospital on Union Street ceased operating as one on October 1, 2026, by its own site. The company has said the 20 non-ICU beds move to Northern Colorado Rehabilitation Hospital, the sister hospital at 4401 Union St, with the date to be announced. This guide first carried the closing in its weekly notes of October 26; it is here so it stays findable after that week.
