The Town's Cone Zone update for the week of October 26 lists a full closure of Weld County Road 46 from Galloway Drive through Telep Avenue (Weld County Road 15), starting Wednesday, October 28, 2026, for utility and roadway work associated with the Granary development. The Town's anticipated completion is the end of May 2027, and it marks every date on the page as tentative and weather-dependent. The same update closed Telep Avenue itself between County Roads 42 and 46 for resurfacing and guardrail installation, with completion expected at the end of October 27. Cone Zone is the Town's weekly list of closures and lane work; it posts the same update on its social media pages.
