The Town's vacancy page says the Town Council is accepting applications to fill a Councilmember vacancy, which the Johnstown Home Rule Charter requires it to fill within thirty days of the seat becoming vacant. The appointment lasts until the Johnstown municipal election of April 4, 2028. Under Section 2.3 of the charter an applicant must be a citizen of the United States, a registered elector of the Town and a resident of the Town for at least twelve months, and cannot be a salaried employee of the Town while in office. Applications are due to the Town Clerk, Meghan Martinez, no later than 10 am on Monday, November 16, 2026: through the online form on the page, on a downloaded application returned to Town Hall at 450 S Parish, or by email to mmartinez@johnstownco.gov. Attachments are limited to two single-sided pages. Applicants may be interviewed on the evening of Monday, November 23, and the Town asks them to keep it free. The form notes that the Council's regular meetings are on the first and third Mondays of the month, with work sessions historically on the second and fourth Mondays. Questions go to the Town Clerk at 970-587-4664.
