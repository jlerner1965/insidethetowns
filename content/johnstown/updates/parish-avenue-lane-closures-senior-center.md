The Town's Cone Zone page for the week of November 16 says Parish Avenue has daytime lane closures for utility work associated with the Johnstown Senior Center, starting November 16, between Weld County Road 46.5 and Weld County Road 46. The Town gives November 20, 2026 as the anticipated completion date and says all information is weather pending and all completion dates are tentative. The same week's page keeps Weld County Road 46 closed from Galloway Drive to Telep Avenue and the Larimer County Roads 3 and 18 intersection closed for widening.
