The Town of Johnstown announced the death of Councilmember Damien Berg in a statement dated October 31, 2026 and posted on November 2. Berg, a United States Army veteran and a Colorado State University graduate, settled in Johnstown with his family in 2007, joined the Town Council in August 2011 and served until August 2015, returned to the Council in January 2019, was elected in April 2020, served briefly as Mayor Pro Tem in 2023 and was re-elected in April 2024. The Town credits him with an instrumental role in establishing the Johnstown Veterans Memorial and the Johnstown Honors Program, which recognizes past and present residents who have served in the armed forces, and, as a former Commander of VFW Post 2585, with helping to organize the town's Memorial Day and Veterans Day ceremonies. "The impact Damien had on Johnstown is immeasurable," Town Manager Matt LeCerf said in the statement.
