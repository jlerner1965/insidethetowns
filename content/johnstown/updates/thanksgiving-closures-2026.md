[UCHealth Urgent Care – Johnstown](https://www.uchealth.org/locations/uchealth-urgent-care-johnstown/), 4846 Larimer Pkwy, lists itself closed on Thursday, November 26; its usual hours are 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 8 am to 6 pm at weekends. The [YMCA of Northern Colorado's holiday hours](https://www.ymcanoco.org/membership/facility-hours) have every branch, the Johnstown Y on Settler Way among them, open until noon on Wednesday, November 25, Thanksgiving Eve, and closed on Thanksgiving Day. Weld County's [motor vehicle offices](https://www.weld.gov/Government/Departments/Clerk-and-Recorder/Motor-Vehicle-Department/Contact-Us) and its [Treasurer's office](https://www.weld.gov/Government/Departments/Treasurer) say they are closed November 26 and 27 for Thanksgiving. The Town's [calendar](https://johnstownco.gov/Calendar.aspx) keeps trash and recycling pickup on the usual days: Monday, November 23, for homes west of Colorado Boulevard and Wednesday, November 25, for homes east of it, carts at the curb by 7 am.
