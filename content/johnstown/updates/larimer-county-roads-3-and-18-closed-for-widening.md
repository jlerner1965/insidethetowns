The Town's Cone Zone page for the week of November 16 says the intersection of Larimer County Roads 3 and 18 is closed for intersection widening tied to the Thompson River Ranch development, starting November 12. The Town gives December 4, 2026 as the anticipated completion date and says all information is weather pending and all completion dates are tentative. The Larimer County road-closure list shows a full closure of County Road 3 for about a quarter mile from County Road 18, from November 12 to December 4, last updated November 9.
