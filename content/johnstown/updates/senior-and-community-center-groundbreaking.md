The Town's Town Council page, in its entry on the mayor, lists groundbreaking set for a new senior and community center in November 2026, and names services and support for Johnstown's senior population, including a new senior and community center, among the mayor's priorities. The Town has not posted a day for the groundbreaking. Its Cone Zone page for the week of November 16 has [daytime lane closures on Parish Avenue](/updates/parish-avenue-lane-closures-senior-center/) between Weld County Road 46.5 and Weld County Road 46 for utility work associated with the Johnstown Senior Center.
