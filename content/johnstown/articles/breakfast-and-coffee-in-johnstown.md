Johnstown's breakfast is in two places: Old Town on Parish Avenue, and out at the 2534 interchange by Johnstown Plaza, with the old Johnson's Corner diner on the I-25 frontage road in between. Five places, earliest first within each. Hours are as each business posts them, read on November 3, 2026.

## Old Town

[Santiago's](/places/santiagos/) on Parish Avenue opens at 6, Monday to Saturday, for breakfast burritos and green chile, and the kitchen carries on until 8. It is closed on Sundays. You can eat in or take it with you; [the to-go menu](https://eatatsantiagos.com/userfiles/2168/Full_ToGo_Menu.pdf) is a PDF.

On the same block, [Black Sheep Coffee & Crepes](/places/black-sheep-coffee-and-crepes/) is the coffee house: espresso drinks and sweet and savory crepes, 7 to 2 on weekdays and 8 to 2 on Saturday and Sunday. It posts special hours for its events on its own site. [The menu](https://www.blacksheepjohnstown.com/eats-drinks) is online.

## Out at 2534

[Duck Donuts](/places/duck-donuts/) off Larimer Parkway makes its donuts to order, with coffee and egg-and-cheese sandwiches. It opens at 6 every day but closes at noon on weekdays, staying open to 6 pm on Saturday and Sunday. [The menu](https://www.duckdonuts.com/menu/4842-larimer-parkway-johnstown-co-80534) is on its page.

[Urban Egg](/places/urban-egg/) at Johnstown Plaza is the sit-down breakfast and brunch room, 7 to 2 on weekdays and to 2:30 at the weekend. It takes reservations and runs a waitlist, both linked from its location page. It is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. [The menu](https://www.urbanegg.com/menu/) is the chain's.

## The I-25 diner

The [Black Bear Diner at Johnson's Corner](/places/johnsons-corner/) on the I-25 frontage road took over the landmark truck stop's restaurant in 2025. It serves breakfast all day, every day from 6 am to 10 pm, and takes orders online; call (970) 667-2069. [The menu](https://blackbeardiner.com/menus/?store=black-bear-diner-199) is on its site.
