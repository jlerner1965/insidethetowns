Johnstown is one town with two of most things. It sits in Weld and Larimer counties — about 73 per cent of residents on the Weld side at the 2020 census, 27 per cent on the Larimer side — because the town has annexed west and north-west across the line over time.

Underneath that are three more boundaries, and none of them is the county line exactly. [The moving guide](/moving-here/) covers utilities, commuting and housing. This is the part that decides which of them apply to a particular address.

## Two fire districts

Most people do not expect this one. The town's own [fire districts page](https://johnstownco.gov/408/Fire-Districts) names two:

- **[Front Range Fire Rescue](https://frfr.colorado.gov/)** covers the Weld County side. It was formed from the former Johnstown and Milliken fire protection districts, which combined in 2015 and became a single district in 2018, with stations in Johnstown, Hill n Park and Milliken.
- **Loveland Fire Rescue Authority**, through the Loveland Rural Fire Protection District, covers the Larimer County side — including developments such as Johnstown Plaza and Iron Horse.

The [Johnson's Corner](/places/johnsons-corner/) area is treated separately again.

In an emergency none of this matters: call 911 and the right engine is dispatched. It matters for inspections, plan reviews and burn permits, for the fire-district line on a tax bill, and for knowing whose number to ring about anything that is not an emergency. Check the address against the town's page rather than inferring it from the county.

## Two school districts

Most of Johnstown is [Weld County School District RE-5J](https://www.weldre5j.org/) — Elwell, Letford and Pioneer Ridge elementaries, Roosevelt Middle and Roosevelt High, all in town. The north-west corner, on the Larimer side, is [Thompson School District R2-J](https://www.thompsonschools.org/), which is Loveland's district. The town [lists both](https://johnstownco.gov/409/Schools).

"Johnstown schools" is therefore not one answer. Confirm an address with the district itself before you commit.

## Three electricity companies

The town's [utility service providers page](https://johnstownco.gov/510/Utility-Service-Providers) gives each one a patch of the town:

| Provider | Service area, as the town states it |
|---|---|
| Xcel Energy | "Central Downtown Johnstown and 2534 Area" |
| United Power | "Pioneer Ridge area and south" |
| Poudre Valley REA | "Johnstown North of CR 48 1/2 and West of I25" |

Natural gas is Xcel throughout. Rates and terms differ between a member-owned co-operative and an investor-owned utility, so which one serves an address is a real question, not a formality.

## What the county decides

Which side you are on sets the assessor who values your house and hears an appeal, where you record a deed, which county court hears a case, and which county contests appear on your ballot beside the town's own.

## Before you make an offer

1. Which county is the address in? — the town, or either county assessor.
2. Which fire district? — the town's [fire districts page](https://johnstownco.gov/408/Fire-Districts).
3. Which school district? — [Weld RE-5J](https://www.weldre5j.org/) or [Thompson R2-J](https://www.thompsonschools.org/), confirmed by the district.
4. Which power company, and is the parcel in a metropolitan district? — the [utility providers page](https://johnstownco.gov/510/Utility-Service-Providers) and the [state special district map](https://gis.dola.colorado.gov/CO_SpecialDistrict).

Checked against each body's own site on September 20, 2026. Boundaries move and districts merge — this town has already seen two fire districts become one. Where it matters, confirm with the body itself. [Tell us if something here is out of date](/contact/).
