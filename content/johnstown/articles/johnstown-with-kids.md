Johnstown has two halves for a family: Old Town, where the parks, the library and the museum are, and the interchange at 2534, where the big attractions are. A good day uses both.

## No school today?

Weld RE-5J's days off, with what is open on each, are on [Johnstown Families' no-school days list](/families/#breaks). The YMCA runs a School Day Off Camp at the [Johnstown Y](/places/johnstown-ymca/) on the district's days off, 7 am to 6 pm, and registration for each day closes at midnight on the Wednesday before (the Y's camps page, read October 26).

## The library

The [Glenn A. Jones, M.D. Memorial Library](/places/glenn-a-jones-library/) on South Parish Avenue, next to Town Hall, runs a Music & Movement storytime and a Messy Art session weekly, Babytime on Thursdays, and a run of seasonal crafts through October. It has a makerspace and a library of things. Open until 8 Tuesday to Thursday, until 5 Monday and Friday, Saturday to 1; closed Sunday. The [events page](/events/?category=family) has the week's sessions.

## Old Town's parks

[Parish Park](/places/parish-park/), at Charlotte and Raymond beside the Parish House Museum, is the town's original park: a playground, restrooms, sand volleyball and walking paths under mature trees, with two reservable shelters. [Lake Park](/places/lake-park/), off Highway 60 at 149 Alabaster Way, has a walking path around the Town reservoir, a playground and fishing for bass, trout, catfish and crappie with a Town permit sold at Town Hall on weekdays. [Eddie Aragon Park](/places/eddie-aragon-park/) in Johnstown Center has the skate park, a lighted outdoor hockey rink, a basketball court and a playground. All the Town parks are open 5 am to 10 pm.

[Pioneer Ridge Park](/places/pioneer-ridge-park/), beside the elementary school on the east side, has a free nine-hole disc golf course that opened in June 2021, an easy first course for children.

## The museum, free

The [Historic Parish House & Museum](/places/parish-house-museum/) is town founder Harvey Parish's 1914 Craftsman bungalow on Charlotte Street, kept by the Historical Society with the family's furnishings and a piece of the 1924 Johnstown meteorite, which is the thing to promise a child. Free, on Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday mornings, 9 to 12.

## Crepes in Old Town

[Black Sheep Coffee & Crepes](/places/black-sheep-coffee-and-crepes/) on Parish Avenue is the family-friendly creperie and coffee shop: sweet and savoury crepes, weekdays 7 to 2 and weekends from 8.

## The pool: the YMCA

The [Johnstown Community YMCA](/places/johnstown-ymca/) on Settler Way, north of Old Town, is the town's recreation center: a lap pool and a leisure pool with slides and diving boards, a gym, an indoor track and fitness classes. Weekdays from 5 am, Saturday 7 to 7, Sunday 8 to 7.

## The Ferris wheel: Scheels

[Scheels](/places/scheels/) at the 2534 interchange is a 250,000-square-foot sporting-goods store that is as much an attraction as a shop: a 65-foot indoor Ferris wheel, a 16,000-gallon saltwater aquarium, a wildlife mountain, an arcade, a candy factory and Ginna's Café. Weekdays 9:30 to 9, Saturday from 9, Sunday 10 to 6. It costs nothing to walk around; the wheel and the arcade charge.

Two doors along, [Duck Donuts](/places/duck-donuts/) fries cake donuts to order and dresses them while you wait, weekdays 6 to noon and weekends 6 to 6, and [Urban Egg](/places/urban-egg/) does pancakes and skillets from 7 with gluten-free and dairy-free options marked.
