A lunchtime book discussion in Juniper Library’s fireplace area, noon to 1:30 pm; bring your lunch. The November 12 book is *The Book Woman of Troublesome Creek* by Kim Michele Richardson, and the December 10 book is *The Borrowed Life of Frederick Fife* by Anna Johnston. Copies are at the circulation desk, and there is an option to attend online.
