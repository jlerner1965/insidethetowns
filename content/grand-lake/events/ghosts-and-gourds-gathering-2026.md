The Town of Grand Lake’s free Halloween afternoon in Town Square Park and along the downtown boardwalk, Saturday, October 24, noon to 4 pm: a pumpkin patch, pumpkin painting, hot cider, face painting and park games. The costume contest is 1–2 pm at the gazebo, and boardwalk trick-or-treating is 2–4 pm. Author Stan Yan draws caricatures noon–2 pm and 3–4 pm, with a reading at Juniper Library from 2 to 3 pm, which is listed separately.
