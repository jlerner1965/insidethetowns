Rocky Mountain Folk School is a 501(c)(3) non-profit founded in 2021 that teaches traditional arts, crafts and practical skills in year-round workshops; it says it has offered more than 400 workshops to nearly 1,000 students, with a growing focus on leatherworking, silversmithing and woodworking. It works from two buildings: the Pitkin Building on the town square, at 315 Pitkin, and the Grand Spirit Makerspace at 1128 Park Ave, Unit A. Workshops need a signed waiver, and the school asks students to arrive 15 to 20 minutes early. Its mailing address is PO Box 545, Grand Lake, CO 80447.
