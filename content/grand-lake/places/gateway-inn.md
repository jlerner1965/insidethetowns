Gateway Inn’s rooms have log furnishings and individual layouts, the penthouse included, so the room type on the booking page matters. The inn has a sauna, a hot tub, Wi-Fi and space for boats and snowmobiles, and Wild Bear Tavern is inside.

The front desk is staffed **8 am to 8 pm**. Guests arriving later should tell the inn in advance; after-hours calls are for emergencies. Online booking is open at any hour.

The inn warns that Highway 34 over Trail Ridge is closed in the winter months; check the [National Park Service’s road conditions](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/road_status.htm) before a winter drive from Estes Park.

Address, phone and desk hours were read on the inn’s [contact page](https://gatewayinn.com/contact/), and rooms and amenities on its [About](https://gatewayinn.com/about/) and [Amenities](https://gatewayinn.com/about/grand-lake-co-amenities/) pages, on 11 October 2026.
