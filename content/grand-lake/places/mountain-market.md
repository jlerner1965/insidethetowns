Mountain Market is a locally owned specialty grocery and butcher that says it has served Grand Lake and Rocky Mountain National Park since 1953. It stocks produce, cut meat and seafood, groceries, craft beer, seltzers and wine, and names its local suppliers, among them Sisu Farms eggs from Granby, Mystic Mountain Mushrooms and Grand Lake Candle Co. from Grand Lake, Tabernash Honey Co., Haystack Mountain Creamery cheese and Steamboat Meat & Seafood.

Address, phone and hours were read on the market's home page on 10 October 2026, where the daily 9 am to 8 pm hours are headed "fall hours"; summer hours may differ. The page carries no dated post; the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/mountain-market/18/) lists the market at the same address and phone, which is the second source that it is operating.
