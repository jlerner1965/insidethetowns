Rapids Lodge has stood beside the Tonahutu River since 1915 and has a dining room, a bar, a riverside patio and a small number of suites. It is open Thursday to Saturday, with happy hour 3–5 pm and dinner 5–8 pm. Reservations are for dinner in the dining room only, made online through Resy and held with a credit card; bar and riverside seating is first come, first served, and patio seating depends on the weather. Its dining page lists happy hour, evening dining, and a winter prix fixe and family-style supper. There is no dress code; the lodge asks for mountain casual rather than athletic wear. It offers many gluten-free dishes but says it cannot serve guests with celiac disease who need a kitchen free of cross-contact. Children are welcome; pets are not, though service animals are.

Address and phone were read on the lodge's [home page](https://rapidslodge.com/), hours and policies on its [FAQ page](https://rapidslodge.com/faqs/) and the menus on its [dining page](https://rapidslodge.com/dining/), all on 18 November 2026. The site shows no dated post; the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/the-historic-rapids-restaurant/109/) lists the restaurant at the same address and phone, which is the second source that it is operating.
