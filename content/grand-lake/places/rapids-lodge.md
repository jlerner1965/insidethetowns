Rapids Lodge has stood beside the Tonahutu River since 1915 and has a dining room, a bar, a riverside patio and a small number of suites. Lunch is 11 am–2 pm and dinner 5–9 pm, last seating 8:45 pm, Tuesday to Sunday; it is closed Monday. Reservations are for dinner in the dining room only, made online through Resy and held with a credit card; bar and riverside seating is first come, first served, and patio seating depends on the weather. There is a midday menu, an evening menu and a to-go menu called Lodge Provisions. There is no dress code; the lodge asks for mountain casual rather than athletic wear. It offers many gluten-free dishes but says it cannot serve guests with celiac disease who need a kitchen free of cross-contact. Children are welcome; pets are not, though service animals are.

Address and phone were read on the lodge's [home page](https://rapidslodge.com/), hours and policies on its [FAQ page](https://rapidslodge.com/faqs/) and the menus on its [dining page](https://rapidslodge.com/dining/), all on 10 October 2026. The site shows no dated post; the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/the-historic-rapids-restaurant/109/) lists the restaurant at the same address and phone, which is the second source that it is operating.
