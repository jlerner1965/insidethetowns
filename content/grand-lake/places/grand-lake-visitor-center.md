The Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce runs the visitor center at 14700 US Hwy 34 (mailing address PO Box 99, Grand Lake, CO 80447). Its page gives only "Summer Hours": daily, 9 am to 4 pm, with no dates for when summer starts or ends and no winter hours, so call before driving out in the off-season. The chamber mails a free visitor guide on request and its site carries the lodging, dining and events listings for the town.
