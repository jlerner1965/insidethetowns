The Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce runs the visitor center at 14700 US Hwy 34 (mailing address PO Box 99, Grand Lake, CO 80447). Its page gives "Winter Hours": open 7 days a week, 10 am to 3 pm, with no dates for when winter starts or ends. The chamber mails a free visitor guide on request and its site carries the lodging, dining and events listings for the town.
