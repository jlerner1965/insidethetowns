The Kawuneeche Visitor Center is outside the town, on US Highway 34 north of Grand Lake, inside Rocky Mountain National Park. The National Park Service says it is open year-round, with rangers to answer questions about the park, exhibits, and a Rocky Mountain Conservancy Nature Store; food, drinks and pets are not allowed inside. The park lists it open daily from 9 am to 5 pm and closed on Thanksgiving Day (26 November 2026) and Christmas Day. The phone number the park gives is its main information line, 970-586-1206; the Town of Grand Lake's [Rocky Mountain National Park page](https://www.townofgrandlake.com/community/page/rocky-mountain-national-park) lists 970-627-3471 for the center itself.

The park's [visitor centers page](https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm) builds each center's hours, address and phone from the park's own data file ([romo_linked_data.json](https://www.nps.gov/romo/romo_linked_data.json)), which was read on 10 October 2026 and carries the 2026 Thanksgiving and Christmas closures.
