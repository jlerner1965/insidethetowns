A professional theatre at 800 Grand Avenue. The box office number is 970-627-3421 and the office is 970-627-5087 (mailing address PO Box 1682, Grand Lake, CO 80447). In summer the box office opens Monday to Saturday from noon to 5 pm or until showtime, closed Sunday; in fall it opens Tuesday to Sunday, closed Monday. In winter the box office is closed and the administrative office across the street, open Monday to Thursday 9 am to 4:30 pm and Friday 10 am to 2 pm, takes orders; will call opens an hour early for Home for the Holidays and the New Year's Eve show. Wheelchair seating and assisted-listening devices are available; order by phone to arrange accessible seating.

The theatre's [current season page](https://www.rockymountainrep.com/show-tickets/current-season/) lists the fall and holiday shows (a Boulder Ballet program, a radio-hour show, Home for the Holidays and a New Year's Eve special) and Colorado Gives Day on December 8, 2026. Hours and address from the [box office page](https://www.rockymountainrep.com/show-tickets/box-office-info/), read 10 October 2026; the site is dated 2026.
