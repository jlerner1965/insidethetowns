Town Square Park, often called Town Park, sits in the middle of the downtown boardwalk at 1025 Grand Avenue. The Town says it was dedicated for public use in the original platting of the town in 1905 and is the gathering place for parades, arts and crafts fairs and concerts; in winter its trees carry holiday lights. Around the lawn are the [Juniper Library](/places/juniper-library/), the Town Hall offices, the Community House, Heckert Pavilion, a playground, a basketball court and public restrooms, with a gazebo, flower gardens and a row of ornamental aspens. Alcohol is not allowed in any Town park. The Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's parks page says the playground has adaptive equipment for children of all abilities.

The Town's pages list its 24 October 2026 community event, and the chamber's [parks page](https://gograndlake.com/things-to-do/parks-playgrounds/) describes the park as well.
