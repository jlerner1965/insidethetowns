The lodge sits on US 34 at 15500 US-34, with rooms, cabins and a pool; it is run by Highway West Vacations. Check-in starts at 3 pm and check-out is 11 am. Up to two dogs per room are welcome at $50 each, registered at the front desk, everywhere except the tavern, the pool and the Jupe units; no other pets. Its restaurant, Huntington House Tavern (970-800-4164), serves brunch, lunch and dinner with a menu that changes by season; on 10 October 2026 its page said "Currently closed for the season". The lodge's site does not give the dates of its operating season, so call before planning an off-season stay.
