Miyauchi's makes small-batch ice cream on site, with flavors that rotate, and sells shakes and sundaes alongside a grill menu. Its hours page gives monthly hours for May to September only. In June, July and August the grill is open Monday to Thursday 11 am–7 pm and Friday to Sunday 11 am–8 pm, and the ice cream counter daily 11 am–9 pm. In May and September the grill is open Monday to Thursday 11 am–6 pm and Friday to Sunday 11 am–7 pm, and ice cream Monday to Thursday 11 am–8 pm and Friday to Sunday 11 am–8:30 pm. It posts no hours for October to April.

The snack bar's own site gives two street numbers: 1025 Lake Avenue in its page header and structured data, and 1029 Lake Avenue in its footer; the chamber's listing uses 1029. Call before relying on either for a map pin. Phone and hours were read on the [hours page](https://www.miyauchis.com/hours) and home page on 10 October 2026. The site shows no dated post; the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/miyauchi-snack-bar-%26-homemade-ice-cream/163/) lists it with the same phone, which is the second source that it is operating.
