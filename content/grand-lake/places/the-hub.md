The Hub makes coffee and serves breakfast burritos (bacon, sausage, egg and potato, and chicken chorizo), soup, locally sourced bagels and pastries, and beer and wine. It is open daily from 7 am to 3 pm, and the kitchen closes at 2:30 pm. It also sells its own mugs, sweatshirts and T-shirts, and gift cards.

Address, phone and hours were read on The Hub's home page on 10 October 2026. The page carries no dated post; the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/the-hub/33/) lists The Hub at the same address and phone, which is the second source that it is operating.
