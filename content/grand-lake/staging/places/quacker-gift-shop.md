Quacker Gift Shop sells rubber ducks, plush and fudge from 1034 Grand Avenue, across from Town Park. Its About page says it began with "a little rubber duckie in soap to keep the kids entertained" and that, fifteen years and three stores later, it has a long-time home in Grand Lake. The phone number is the same one The Mountain Gal boutique lists.

Address and phone were read on the shop's home, About and Contact pages on 10 October 2026. The home page's newest posts are dated June 3, 2023 and carry a "Fall 2023" heading; no hours were shown. The Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/quacker-gift-shop/133/) lists the shop at the same address and phone.
