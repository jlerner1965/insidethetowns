The Mountain Gal calls itself Grand Lake's "Mountain Chic Boutique". Its online shop carries women's clothing from brands including Umgee, POL, Judy Blue, Zenana and Adora, with denim, accessories and jewelry. The phone in its structured data, 970-798-8014, is the same number Quacker Gift Shop lists.

Read on the boutique's home and contact pages on 10 October 2026; neither gives a street address or hours. The packet placed it at 1034 Grand Avenue (unconfirmed); the Grand Lake Area Chamber of Commerce's [member page](https://gograndlake.com/listing/the-mountain-gal-boutique/11/) gives 1030 Grand Ave. and the same phone.
