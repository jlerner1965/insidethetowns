The Grand Lake Metropolitan Recreation District runs the Nordic Center over its golf course in winter: about 35 km of groomed trails for classic and skate skiing, a dog-friendly ski and snowshoe loop, ski and snowshoe rentals, lessons, a retail shop and a free tubing hill (no sharp metal sled edges; donations welcome). Trail passes were $25 for skiing and $15 for snowshoeing when read; Grand County kids ski free on Fridays except holidays. The page was headed "Closed for the 2026 Season" and asked people not to walk on the ski trails. In summer the same ground is the district's 18-hole golf course, with the Sly Fox Restaurant.

The Nordic Center page was re-read on 10 October 2026 through a web fetch (the robots-aware reader received compressed pages it could not decode; robots.txt allows reading) and still showed the 2026 closure, the $25 ski and $15 snowshoe passes and the tubing hill as closed. The Town of Grand Lake's [district page](https://www.townofgrandlake.com/community/page/grand-lake-metropolitan-recreation-district) gives the address, 1415 GCR 48, and phones: district office 970-627-8872, golf pro shop and Nordic center 970-627-8008. The packet described groomed classic and skate trails, snowshoe routes, rentals, lessons and a dog-friendly loop, which match the district's page.
