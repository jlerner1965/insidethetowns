Grand Pizza says it has served Grand Lake since 1995 from the boardwalk on Grand Avenue, with 32 pizza toppings, calzones, grilled panini, salads made with produce prepared daily, and a selection of Colorado beers, plus soda and children's drinks. It offers dine-in and takeout, takes no reservations, and cannot seat parties larger than eight. The full menu is available for takeout, ordered by phone only; there is no delivery. Email GrandPizza@gmail.com; mailing address PO Box 1905.
