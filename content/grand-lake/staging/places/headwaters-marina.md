Headwaters Marina is owned and operated by the Town of Grand Lake, across from Lakefront Park and steps from the boardwalk. Boat rentals are first come, first served, with no reservations: pedal boats $35 an hour for one or two people and $50 for three or four (no dogs); 16.5-foot, 60-horsepower Crestliner sport boats for up to four, $120 an hour (pets welcome); and pontoons for 6, 8 or 13 people at $160, $180 and $200 an hour (pets welcome). Monday to Thursday, a fourth hour is free after three. One-hour scenic lake tours leave at 11 am and 2 pm Sunday to Thursday and also 5 pm Friday and Saturday, by reservation only, at $35 for adults and $15 for children 12 and under, no dogs; sunset cruises are by request for private groups. The Town also keeps free day-use boat slips beside the marina (no docking 2 am to 5 am) and seasonal kayak slips (Town Hall, 970-627-3435); the public boat ramp is at the East Inlet.
