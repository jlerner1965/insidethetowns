The Kauffman House was built in 1892 as a hotel and is, the society says, the only log hotel built in Grand Lake before 1900 that still stands; it is on the National Register of Historic Places and the Grand Lake Area Historical Society bought and restored it in 1973. Inside are a parlor and an upstairs bedroom papered from a c. 1915 photograph, a preserved section of wall covered in newspapers from December 1893 and January 1894, a strip of the original wallpaper, and a gallery added in 1990 whose exhibits change. There are geocaches on the grounds and a virtual tour online.

Open Monday to Saturday 11 am to 5 pm, closed Sunday, from Memorial Day through August, then weekends in September and on special occasions through the year. Adults $5, children 12 and under free. Email historygrandlake@gmail.com.
