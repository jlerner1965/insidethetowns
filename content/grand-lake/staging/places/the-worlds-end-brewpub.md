The World's End Brewpub at 813 Grand Avenue serves build-your-own and set flatbread pizzas (meat lovers, Mediterranean, Gut Buster, vegetarian), burnt-ends street tacos, pub-style fish and chips, chicken tenders, wild game bratwurst, wings and snacks such as a Bavarian pretzel, burrata and cheese curds, with a kids' menu, growler fills and non-alcoholic beers. It says it is releasing one keg of house beer each Thursday after repairing its brewing system. Because the venue is small and staff limited, it takes no reservations, and takeout is restricted at peak times.
