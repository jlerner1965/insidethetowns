The Grand Lake Metropolitan Recreation District calls the course one of Colorado's original mountain courses, designed by Dick Phelps with rolling fairways and subtle greens. It has a driving range, putting green, lessons and a pro shop, sells season passes and takes tee times online. Dogs are not allowed on the course. The Town of Grand Lake's page on the district says it has an 18-hole course, and lists the district office at 970-627-8872, the golf pro shop and Nordic center at 970-627-8008, recreation at 970-627-8328 and the Sly Fox Restaurant at 970-627-3922, at 1415 GCR 48. In winter the same ground is the [Nordic center](https://www.grandlakerecreation.com/nordiccenter/).
