Grand Lake Marina & Boater's Choice says it has rented boats on Grand Lake since 1972, from its dock at 1132 Lake Avenue. Its motor-boat page lists three Sylvan Mirage pontoons for 8, 10 and 12 passengers (2026 model year), 13- and 15-foot Boston Whaler fishing boats and a 17-foot Lund (2025 model year), each with life jackets and fuel included and pets allowed. Its paddle-sports page lists canoes, single and double kayaks and inflatable stand-up paddleboards, with no pets and a minimum age of 3. Bookings run through FareHarbor. The Wake Coffee Shop is on the same dock.
