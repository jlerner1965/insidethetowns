Busey Brews is a locally owned smokehouse and brewery on East 1st Street. It smokes meats and tofu, pours house-brewed beer from 22 taps, and also has house-made kombucha and ginger ale, wine and cocktails, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-friendly options. Dine-in and takeout.

Happy hour is Monday to Friday 3–5 pm, holidays excluded, and live trivia runs Thursdays 6:30–8 pm. The site gives its number as 855-NED-BREW (855-633-2739).
