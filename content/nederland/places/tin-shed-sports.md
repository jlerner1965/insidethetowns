Tin Shed Sports is a locally owned, full-service bike and ski shop on East 2nd Street, next door to Ned General. It rents and demos mountain and gravel bikes and alpine and backcountry skis, services bikes, sells parts, apparel and gear, and takes used bikes and skis on consignment.

The shop says riders can head straight from its door onto local trails and gravel roads.
