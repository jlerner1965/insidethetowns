Bristlecone Bookshop is an independent bookstore in downtown Nederland carrying a curated selection of new books, bookish goods and items made by local artisans. It hosts events and sells memberships.

Its site lists hours for Friday, Saturday and Sunday only. It also sells online through Bookshop.org and audiobooks through Libro.fm.
