The Caribou Room is a music venue just north of downtown Nederland, on the corner of Highway 72 and County Road 128. It has a professional kitchen and two bars; the venue's dining page (https://www.thecaribouroom.com/dining) says the Caribou Kitchen is open 7–10 pm on show nights, with table service ending at 9:30.

Shows are listed on its calendar (https://www.thecaribouroom.com/calendar), where doors and dinner are typically at 7 pm and shows at 8. Its venue policies (https://www.thecaribouroom.com/about-us) say guests under 18 at all-ages shows must be supervised by a parent or guardian, some shows are 16+, outside food and drink are not allowed, and backpacks and larger bags go to coat check or stay in the car.
