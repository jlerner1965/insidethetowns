Mountain Man is an outdoor store on the Peak to Peak Highway on the south side of town, across from the Train Cars and the Carousel of Happiness. Founded by John Thompson, it stocks camping and backpacking gear, ski and snow gear, boots and snowshoes, workwear, socks and gloves, Indian Peaks trail maps and guides, trail food and local gifts, plus some used gear.

It has been in Nederland since 2012.
