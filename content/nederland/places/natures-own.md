Nature's Own is a rock shop with stores in Nederland and Fort Collins. The Nederland shop on East 1st Street carries mineral specimens and geodes, fossils, meteorites, carved stone homewares, Himalayan salt lamps and gemstone and silver jewelry.

It runs a monthly birthstone sale, and the shops take inventory questions by phone.
