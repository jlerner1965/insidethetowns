The Town of Nederland lists Barker Meadows Reservoir on its Trails & Open Spaces page, at East Street and Boulder Canyon Drive, with public parking, picnic tables, fishing and hiking trails.

Guercio Memorial Park, the Town's baseball field, is on East Street beside the reservoir and also has public parking. The Town's page gives no hours for either.
