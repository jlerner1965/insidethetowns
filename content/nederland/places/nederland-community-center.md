The Town of Nederland’s community center, on Highway 72 North, is where its fitness and dance classes, concerts and performances, Art at the Center and community groups meet, and where the Town board and the municipal court sit. Rooms can be rented for gatherings.

The office and the fitness center keep different hours, posted on the Town’s page; this listing carries neither, since one schedule would misstate the other. Call 303-258-9721 for the day’s hours, fees and drop-in access. Recurring classes are the center’s own calendar’s, not listed here as events.
