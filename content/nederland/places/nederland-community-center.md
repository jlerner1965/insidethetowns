The Town of Nederland’s community center, on Highway 72 North, is where its fitness and dance classes, concerts and performances, Art at the Center and community groups meet, and where the Town board and the municipal court sit. Rooms can be rented for gatherings.

The Town’s Fitness Place page posts fitness hours of Monday to Thursday 8 am–8 pm, Friday 8 am–7 pm and Saturday 9 am–3 pm, closed Sunday; the Community Center page prints the same times, split between the office and the fitness center, and posts no separate weekday office hours, so this listing carries none. Call 303-258-9721 for the day’s hours, fees and drop-in access. Recurring classes are the center’s own calendar’s, not listed here as events.

Address, phone and hours were read on the Town’s Community Center and Fitness Place pages on 30 October 2026.
