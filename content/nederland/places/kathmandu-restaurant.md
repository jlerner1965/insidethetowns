Kathmandu Nederland serves Nepali and Indian food and has been open since 1999. It is between the visitors center and the RTD parking lot, close to the traffic circle. There is a sister restaurant in Boulder.

A lunch buffet runs daily 11 am to 4 pm, and a dinner buffet Fridays 4 to 9 pm. Catering is available.
