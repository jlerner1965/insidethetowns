NedRink is the Town of Nederland's ice rink and tennis court site on Indian Peaks Drive. The Town lists an Olympic-sized ice rink during the winter months, with skate rentals, and full-sized tennis courts during the warmer months, plus public parking.

The Town's NedRINK page calls it Colorado's largest outdoor ice rink and says it is open December to February, for hockey, recreational skating, Learn to Skate lessons, curling, stick and puck and ice rentals. On 10 October 2026 it said the rink was closed for the season. The rink's number is 303-258-3167, extension 6100. Neither page gives session hours.

Address and the tennis courts are from the Town's Trails & Open Spaces page; the season, activities and phone from the Town's NedRINK page (https://nederlandco.org/1353/NedRINK). Both were read on 10 October 2026.
