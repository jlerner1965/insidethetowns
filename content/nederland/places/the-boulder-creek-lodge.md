The Boulder Creek Lodge is a hotel on Lakeview Drive, a walk from the shops and restaurants in town. Its creekside rooms look out on Boulder Creek, and it also has standard king, double queen, king suite, double king suite and family-style rooms. The suites are on the second floor and there is no elevator.

Check-in is 4 to 10 pm and check-out 11 am. Parking and wifi are free, and there is a guest laundromat, complimentary coffee and wheelchair access. Pets are welcome. A multi-purpose room takes events and groups.
