Eldora Mountain Resort is a ski area 21 miles west of Boulder, with a base at 9,200 feet and a top at 10,600, 680 acres of terrain and 10 lifts. It has eight Woodward terrain parks, a Nordic center with 40 kilometres of classic, skate and snowshoe trails, and is an Ikon Pass partner. Most of the base area and many runs are on private land, and the resort is closed to all public access once the ski season ends. Opening day for 2026–27 is scheduled for Friday, November 13. The resort's hours page lists lifts running 9 am to 4 pm on weekdays and from 8:30 am on weekends, while its contact page says 9 am to 4 pm every day, so check the morning snow report before you go. Single-occupancy vehicles pay to park on weekends, holidays and weekday powder days of 10 inches or more.
