Chipeta Park is one of the parks on the Town of Nederland's Trails & Open Spaces list. Parks and trails inside town limits are maintained by the Town's Public Works department, following the plan set by the Parks, Recreation and Open Space Advisory Board.

The Town lists picnic tables, a shelter, play equipment, swings and a children's pond here. Its Veterans Memorial Park, with benches and a memorial, sits on the Tungsten Trail just east of Chipeta Park.
