Mountain People's Co-op is a grocery on East First Street, member-owned and run since 1979. It carries organic and locally sourced produce, natural products, specialty and local goods, bulk herbs, teas, spices, grains, flours, coffee and legumes, vitamins and personal care. There are two hot lunches a day with vegan and gluten-free choices, pre-made soups and sandwiches, breakfast burritos and fresh-baked cookies, cakes and bars.
