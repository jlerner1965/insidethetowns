Ned General is a cafe and bar with some goods for sale, on East 2nd Street next door to Tin Shed Sports. Its menu (https://nedgeneral.com/portfolio-item/menu/) serves breakfast from 7 to 11:30 am, then salads, shareables, burgers and sandwiches until close.

Food is made to order, and the menu asks for about 10 minutes. Sandwiches can be made gluten-free on request.
