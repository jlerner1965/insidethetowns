A Poe-themed murder mystery after hours at the Nederland Community Library: each guest is given a character, then everyone gathers clues and questions suspects, with catered small bites, themed cocktails and a silent auction for the library's youth and accessibility programs. For ages 18 and up; tickets are limited, non-refundable but transferable, and include two drink tickets. Gothic or cocktail attire is suggested.
