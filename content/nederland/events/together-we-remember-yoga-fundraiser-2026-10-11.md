Eldora marks a year since the fire with a donation yoga class upstairs at its Indian Peaks Lodge, the net proceeds going to the Nederland Fire Protection District. Diana and Michelle of Tadasana Mountain Yoga co-teach, and all levels are welcome. Bring a mat and warm layers, pre-register to be sure of a spot, and allow time to park and walk to the lodge.
