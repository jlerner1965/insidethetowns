Eldora has not opened for the 2026–27 season and has not named its opening day: its hours page said it was closed on November 18, 2026. Once it opens there are two ways up from Nederland. Take RTD's NB bus from the free Nederland Park-n-Ride, 300 Jackson Street, which drops you at the Alpenglow chairlift, or drive with two or more people in the car, since a car with only its driver pays Eldora's fee on weekends, holidays and powder days. Eldora takes [no parking reservations](https://www.eldora.com/the-mountain/questions/hours-faq/).

This is for anyone skiing Eldora from Nederland or Boulder. The resort's own shuttle from Boulder has ended, so RTD's bus is the only one.

## Bus or car

| | The NB bus | Your car |
|---|---|---|
| Where you start | Nederland Park-n-Ride, 300 Jackson Street: 75 free spaces, for people riding transit | Eldora's lots; no reservations |
| What it costs | $2.75 for a 3-hour pass, $1.35 discounted; 19 and under ride free | Free with two or more people; a fee for a car with one person on fee days |
| On a weekend | A bus every hour | Busiest, most of all in December, January and February |
| Where it leaves you | In front of the Alpenglow chairlift | The lots; the Elk Lot is for cars with three or more |

## The bus

RTD's [NB route](https://app.rtd-denver.com/route/NB/schedule?serviceType=1&direction=Westbound&branch=NB) runs from Downtown Boulder Station up Boulder Canyon to the Nederland Park-n-Ride, Nederland High School and Eldora. It goes to Eldora only while the resort is open. Pay by tapping a contactless card or phone on the validator, or with RTD's [fares](https://www.rtd-denver.com/fares-passes/fares); skis board at the Nederland stops.

| Day | From the Nederland Park-n-Ride | Last bus back from Eldora |
|---|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 6:06, 6:57, 8:13 and 9:13 am; 10:43 am; 12:13, 12:56, 2:21, 3:23, 3:58 and 4:43 pm | 5:11 pm |
| Saturday | Every hour, 6:43 am to 5:42 pm | 5:12 pm |
| Sunday and holidays | Every hour, 7:40 am to 5:42 pm | 6:12 pm |

The bus takes about 16 to 19 minutes from the Park-n-Ride to Eldora. After the last bus from Eldora, later buses leave from Nederland High School. RTD runs its Sunday schedule on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day.

The [Park-n-Ride](https://www.rtd-denver.com/how-to-ride/parking) is free for 24 hours, first come, first served, with six bike racks and six lockers. RTD says parking there without riding transit is not allowed and the car can be towed.

## Driving

- **The fee:** Eldora charges [single-occupancy vehicles](https://www.eldora.com/the-mountain/questions/getting-to-eldora/) on Saturdays, Sundays, some holiday periods and weekdays with 10 inches or more of new snow, and may add days without notice. The 2026–27 fee days and price are not yet posted. Season pass holders pay it too.
- **Free:** any car with two or more people on a fee day. Cars with three or more may use the Elk Lot, the west end of the lot, seven days a week, first come.
- **No overnight parking** or camping.
- **Dogs** stay in the lots, leashed or in the car, and never on the snow.
- **The road:** Colorado's [traction law](https://www.codot.gov/travel/winter-driving/tractionlaw) can be put in force on any state highway, Colorado 119 included: tires rated all-weather or for snow with 3/16-inch tread, or chains. Ignoring it costs $100 and a $33 surcharge.
- **In town:** when it snows, parking on the Town's right-of-way [is not allowed](https://www.nederlandco.gov/1325/Snow-Plowing); plows start at 4 am.

Eldora shows live parking and road status in a drawer on every page and on its [snow report](https://www.eldora.com/the-mountain/current-conditions/snow-report-weather/). It says non-holiday weekdays are the easiest days to park.

## If the lots are full

Eldora publishes only what happens when its HOV zone fills: those cars park anywhere else in the lots. If a weekend looks full, leave the car at the Nederland Park-n-Ride and ride the hourly bus up, or stay in town:

- **Carousel of Happiness,** 20 Lakeview Drive: [open](https://carouselofhappiness.org/visit-the-carousel/) Monday and Thursday 11 am–5 pm, Friday to Sunday 10 am–5 pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday; last ride 4:45 pm. A ride is $3, and babies under 9 months ride free. No pets in the building.
- **Nederland Community Library,** 200 Highway 72 North: [open](https://nederland.colibraries.org/location-and-hours/) Monday to Thursday 10 am–7 pm, Friday and Saturday 10 am–5 pm, Sunday 1–5 pm; closed Thanksgiving Day and the day after.
- **Mud Lake Open Space,** [Boulder County's 231 acres](https://bouldercounty.gov/open-space/parks-and-trails/mud-lake/): five easy trails of 0.7 to 1.3 miles, pets on a leash, ice skating allowed, open sunrise to sunset.
- **The visitors center,** 45 West 1st Street: [open](https://www.nederlandco.gov/1434/Nederland-Visitors-Center) Wednesday to Monday, 11 am–5 pm, with public restrooms behind the building [from 9 am to 3 pm](https://www.nederlandco.gov/1436/Directions).

## The Nordic Center is a separate ticket

An Eldora alpine pass or ticket [covers the alpine area only](https://www.eldora.com/the-mountain/questions/policies-terms/); the Nordic Center's trails need a Nordic ticket or pass, and 2026–27 day tickets are [not yet on sale](https://www.eldora.com/nordic-center/trail-tickets-passes/). Anyone crossing Eldora's land on the Forest Service's Jenny Creek Trail needs a [free day-use pass](https://www.eldora.com/nordic-center/jenny-creek-arestua-hut/) from the Nordic Center; no dogs on Jenny Creek.
