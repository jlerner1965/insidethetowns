Nederland sits at the top of Boulder Canyon at 8,235 feet, eighteen miles up Colorado 119 from Boulder. Downtown is 1st Street and the blocks beside it, and the Town runs its [visitors center](/places/nederland-visitors-center/) in the 1st Street parking lot in the middle of it, with maps, camping and parking information, and public restrooms along the back of the building open 9 am–3 pm. Park there for the downtown stops. Mud Lake is a drive, and so is the carousel, which is why this is a driving order rather than a walk.

## 01 · Decent Bagel

[Decent Bagel](/places/decent-bagel/) at 35 East 1st Street is open daily 7:30 am–5:30 pm, but bagel sales stop at 2 pm; after that the mercantile side stays open until 5:30. That cutoff is why it is the first stop. Online ordering for the Nederland shop is on its site. If you are earlier still, [Ned General](/places/ned-general/) on East 2nd Street serves breakfast from 7 to 11:30 am every day.

*Getting there:* on foot from the 1st Street lot. *Best for:* breakfast or an early lunch, before the 2 pm cutoff.

## 02 · Mud Lake Open Space

[Mud Lake](/places/mud-lake-open-space/) at 2034 County Road 126 is Boulder County open space, 231 acres between 8,250 and 8,600 feet. The county lists five easy trails from 0.7 to 1.3 miles: the Caribou Ranch Link, the Kinnickinnick Loop, the Tungsten Loop, the Chickadee Trail and the Sherwood Forest Trail. The trailhead has 31 car spaces, 2 ADA spaces, a restroom, picnic tables and a 50-person group shelter that can be reserved. The rules: day use only, sunrise to sunset; pets on a leash; bikes on designated trails only; no swimming, fires or camping. Fishing is allowed but the lake is not stocked. The county's trail-closures page says when a trail is shut.

Hessie, the Indian Peaks trailhead beyond Eldora, is a different kind of day, with almost no parking and a weekend shuttle: [before you drive to Hessie](/articles/before-you-drive-to-hessie/) has the 2026 arrangements, and Mud Lake is one of the county's own suggestions when Hessie is full.

*Getting there:* by car, to the trailhead at 2034 County Road 126. *Best for:* an hour's walk at altitude, with the dog on a leash and children along.

## 03 · Carousel of Happiness

The [Carousel of Happiness](/places/carousel-of-happiness/) is a restored 1910 Looff carousel whose animals were hand-carved by Scott Harrison over 26 years, turning to a 1913 Wurlitzer band organ, in the Caribou Village Shopping Center at 20 Lakeview Drive, just off Colorado 119 between the Train Cars and the Wild Bear Nature Center. Rides are $3, infants under 9 months ride free, and the last ride is at 4:45 pm. Its posted hours are Monday and Thursday 11 am–5 pm and Friday to Sunday 10 am–5 pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday; its visit page says it is generally open every day from Memorial Day to Labor Day and at weekends the rest of the year, so call 303-258-3457 before an off-season visit. Upstairs is a puppet theater with a window onto the gears. Pets are not allowed in the building. In 2026 it says it will open late on Halloween.

*Getting there:* by car; from the traffic circle, cross the bridge, turn left on Lakeview Drive and right into the shopping-center lot. *Best for:* twenty minutes with or without children, on a day it is open.

## 04 · Nederland Mining Museum

The [Nederland Mining Museum](/places/nederland-mining-museum/) at 200 North Bridge Street is Boulder County's museum of hard-rock mining, with trams, ore carts, engines, historic photographs and claim maps indoors and out. Admission is free. It is open Fridays to Sundays 11 am–5 pm from June 1 through October 31, so in 2026 its last days are Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October.

*Getting there:* on foot from the 1st Street lot. *Best for:* half an hour on a weekend before the end of October.

## 05 · Crosscut or Kathmandu

Two kitchens close to the lot. [Crosscut Pizzeria & Taphouse](/places/crosscut-pizzeria-and-taphouse/) at 4 East 1st Street, on the corner with Colorado 119, is wood-fired pizza with gluten-free crust available; it takes no reservations, leashed dogs are welcome on the patio, and the hours are Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 3–9 pm, Friday and Saturday 11:30 am–9 pm, Sunday 11:30 am–8 pm, closed Tuesday. [Kathmandu Restaurant](/places/kathmandu-restaurant/) at 110 North Jefferson Street, between the visitors center and the RTD lot near the traffic circle, has served Nepali and Indian food since 1999: Sunday to Thursday 11 am–9:30 pm, Friday and Saturday to 10 pm, with a lunch buffet daily 11 am–4 pm.

*Getting there:* on foot from the 1st Street lot. *Best for:* lunch on a weekday, when Crosscut is not yet open and Kathmandu's buffet is; dinner on any night but Tuesday for the pizza.

## A simple first visit

1. Park in the 1st Street lot; the restrooms behind the visitors center are open 9 am–3 pm.
2. A bagel before 2 pm.
3. Drive to Mud Lake and walk one loop.
4. Back in town, the carousel on any day but Tuesday or Wednesday, and the mining museum on a weekend through October.
5. Pizza at Crosscut (closed Tuesday) or the buffet at Kathmandu.

## Getting here

Drive times are estimates for normal conditions and vary with traffic, weather and construction; check a live routing service before you set out. From Boulder it is eighteen miles up Boulder Canyon on Colorado 119, about forty minutes; from Denver about an hour. [COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) carries the live state of the canyon and the Peak to Peak.

Boulder County's Hessie page says RTD's NB route runs hourly from Boulder to Nederland High School, with free weekend parking at Downtown Boulder Station and the Boulder County Justice Center. Check [RTD](https://www.rtd-denver.com/) for the current timetable and fare.

## Accessibility: what we can confirm

The Carousel of Happiness says it has a wheelchair ramp, step stools, stationary benches and easy-to-ride seats, that the building is walker-friendly, that staff can lend headphones or turn the music off, and that its restrooms are accessible and have diaper-changing stations. The county lists 2 ADA parking spaces, one van-accessible, and a restroom near the Mud Lake trailhead. The Town's public restrooms behind the visitors center are open 9 am–3 pm.

Step-free entrances at Decent Bagel, Crosscut, Kathmandu and the mining museum, and the state of the sidewalks and curb ramps on 1st Street, have not been confirmed and are not claimed here; contact a business directly before relying on it.
