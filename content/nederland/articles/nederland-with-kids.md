Nederland had 1,471 people at the [2020 Census](https://storage.googleapis.com/co-publicdata/historical-census.csv), so the list is short and close together: a carousel, a library, a few Town parks, two county open spaces up the road, and Eldora for the winter. None of the Town's park or Community Center pages lists a swimming pool or a splash pad. Here is what there is, with the rules, ages and prices each operator publishes as of October 2026.

## The Carousel of Happiness

The [Carousel of Happiness](/places/carousel-of-happiness/) is just off Colorado 119 in the Caribou Village Shopping Center at 20 Lakeview Drive. Rides are $3, babies under 9 months ride free, and a 10-ride punch card is $25. Anyone can come in to see the carousel and hear its story free of charge ([visit page](https://carouselofhappiness.org/visit-the-carousel/)).

- **Upstairs** is a puppet theater stocked with gently used animal puppets, and a window for watching the carousel's old gears turn.
- **For sensitive ears** staff can lend headphones or turn the music off. There are stationary benches, step stools and a wheelchair ramp, and the restrooms have diaper-changing stations.
- **No pets** go inside. Service animals may come into the gift shop but stay off the ride.
- **Hours.** The site's hours box says 11 to 5 on Monday and Thursday and 10 to 5 Friday to Sunday, closed Tuesday and Wednesday, with the last ride at 4:45. The visit page says it is generally open every day from Memorial Day to Labor Day and on weekends through fall, winter and early spring. In the off-season, call 303-258-3457 first.

## The library

The [Nederland Community Library](/places/nederland-community-library/) at 200 Highway 72 North is open 10 to 7 Monday to Thursday, 10 to 5 Friday and Saturday, and 1 to 5 on Sunday ([library](https://nederland.colibraries.org/)).

- **Storytime** is on Fridays at 10:15 and on Tuesdays with Wild Bear Nature Center; the first Friday of the month is Yoga Storytime ([storytime](https://nederland.colibraries.org/storytime-2/)). The library gives Tuesday's time as 10:15 and [Wild Bear](https://wildbear.org/children) as 10:30, and Wild Bear calls it free for "littles and their grownups".
- **Costume Storytime** is on Friday 30 October at 10:15 ([page](https://nederland.colibraries.org/costume-storytime/)).
- **The After-School Makers Club** for grades 1 to 5 runs on Thursdays from 3:40 to 5 pm, from 24 September to 19 November. A school bus brings children from Nederland Elementary, there is a snack, and pick-up is at the library at 5. Sharp tools and glue guns are used; sign up on the [club page](https://nederland.colibraries.org/kids-after-school-makers-club/).
- **The toy library**, puzzles and STEAM kits are listed on the [Kids + Families page](https://nederland.colibraries.org/kids-families/).
- **Experience passes** cover days down the hill ([passes](https://nederland.colibraries.org/experience-passes/)). The Children's Museum of Denver pass is up to four tickets, and the Denver Zoo pass admits up to four for a set date, with children 2 and under free. A Colorado State Parks backpack goes out for a week to adult cardholders.
- **A card** is free to residents of the Nederland mountain area. Sign up in person with proof of mailing address and a photo ID; a parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18 ([card page](https://nederland.colibraries.org/get-a-library-card/)).

## Parks, the skatepark and the rink

The Town's [parks list](https://nederlandco.org/1446/Trails-Open-Spaces) is the place to start.

- **[Chipeta Park](/places/chipeta-park/)** at 254 Lakeview Drive has play equipment, swings, a children's pond, a shelter and picnic tables.
- **The [Nathan Lazarus Skatepark](/places/nathan-lazarus-skatepark/)** at 155 East 1st Street is for skateboards and roller skates or blades only. It has a pool, a flow bowl, a cradle, a hoodie and a wall ride.
- **[NedRINK](/places/nedrink/)** at 500 Indian Peaks Drive is an outdoor rink, open December to February, with skate rentals, Learn to Skate lessons, hockey, curling and stick and puck ([NedRINK](https://nederlandco.org/1353/NedRINK)). Sessions depend on the weather, and the [schedule](https://nederlandco.org/1359/Schedule) says to check for closures before you leave home. On the [price list](https://nederlandco.org/1357/Prices), an under-18 day pass is $8 for Nederland and Gilpin residents and $10 for visitors. Youth skate rental is $5, Learn to Skate is $15 a session, and children 3 and under skate free. A family season pass is $220 for residents and $240 for visitors.
- **The Community Center gym** offers drop-in basketball, pickleball and kids' kung fu, and the studio hosts children's dance classes ([Fitness Place](https://nederlandco.org/1417/Fitness-Place)). A drop-in visit is $3 for children 12 and under and $5 over 12. A family membership, two adults and their children through high school, is $75 a month ([fees](https://nederlandco.org/1418/Fitness-Place-Fees)).

## The Mining Museum

The [Nederland Mining Museum](/places/nederland-mining-museum/) at 200 North Bridge Street is free. It has trams, ore carts, engines and a mineral display indoors and out, and it is open Friday to Sunday from 11 to 5, June 1 through October 31 ([Boulder County](https://bouldercounty.gov/open-space/education/museums/nederland-mining-museum/)). That makes Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October the last days of this season. On Sunday 18 October the county runs [gold panning](/events/gold-panning-2026-10-18/) there for children 5 and older and adults, in three half-hour sessions at noon, 12:45 and 1:30; [register](https://bouldercounty.gov/event/gold-panning-oct-18/) for one.

## Easy trails

**[Mud Lake Open Space](/places/mud-lake-open-space/)**, which the Town lists as "Excellent for Children and Families", has five easy trails of 0.7 to 1.3 miles, a restroom, picnic tables and a 50-person shelter you can reserve ([county page](https://bouldercounty.gov/open-space/parks-and-trails/mud-lake/)). Dogs must be leashed, bikes stay on designated trails, ice skating is allowed and swimming is not. The county publishes a Nature Detectives Mystery Guide, in English and Spanish, and lists an audio hiking app made by the Boulder Watershed Collective.

**Caribou Ranch**, next door, has the 1.2-mile DeLonde Trail (easy), the 1.8-mile Blue Bird Loop (moderate) and the buildings of the old Blue Bird Mine. No pets or bikes are allowed. It is closed every year from April 1 through June 30 for spring migratory birds and elk calving ([county page](https://bouldercounty.gov/open-space/parks-and-trails/caribou-ranch/)).

**[Brainard Lake](/places/brainard-lake-recreation-area/)**, west of Ward, closes its summer gate in about mid-October. In winter you park at the Gateway Trailhead for the ski and snowshoe trails ([Forest Service](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/brainard-lake-recreation-area)).

## Wild Bear: days off and night hikes

[Wild Bear Nature Center](https://wildbear.org/children) runs **PEAK Days Off** on most Boulder Valley school days off, from 8 am to 4 pm, outdoors at Mud Lake and around town, for $90 a day. Scholarships are available, and Wild Bear accepts state childcare assistance (CCCAP). The [details](https://wildbear.org/peak) give the ages as 6 to 12, though the page's heading says 6 to 13; five-year-olds can join from January 2027. Pre-registration is required. Coming dates are 12 and 13 October, 11 November, 23 to 25 November and 21 to 23 December.

**Owl Moon** is a naturalist-led night hike at Mud Lake on Saturday 24 October from 6:30 to 8 pm, with registration online ([event page](https://wildbear.org/events/mpn47pgiv23q1bd4tsfbmnkmrlj7sh-xbbg9-fayxl-5978j-lxmds-m85pl-ty2yl-ck54d-8bbyc-m9bds-2sx4a)). Another is listed for 21 November.

## Eldora in winter

Eldora's private and group ski and snowboard lessons, and its 90-minute Nordic group lessons, are for ages 4 and up. A helmet is required in every ski or snowboard lesson ([lessons](https://www.eldora.com/lessons-rentals/single-day-lessons/)). Everything is sold online, with no sales at the mountain, and day tickets usually go on sale in October. The resort says its [multi-week kids' programs](https://www.eldora.com/lessons-rentals/ski-ride-school/) are back this season. A [family bundle](https://www.eldora.com/tickets-passes/family-bundle/) takes up to 15 percent off season passes for three to six family members.

## Fishing

The Town lists fishing at **[Barker Meadows Reservoir](/places/barker-meadows-reservoir/)**, off East Street and Boulder Canyon Drive, which also has parking and picnic tables. Mud Lake allows fishing but is not stocked, so the county says a catch is unlikely. In Colorado, anyone 15 or under fishes free ([Colorado Parks and Wildlife](https://cpw.state.co.us/activities/fishing/fishing-licenses-and-dates)).

## The calendar

- **October:** a [Winnie the Pooh birthday party](/events/winnie-the-pooh-birthday-party-2026-10-16/) at the library on the 16th, gold panning on the 18th and [Día de los Muertos](/events/dia-de-los-muertos-2026-10-21/) at the library on the 21st. Owl Moon is on the 24th. Very Nice Brewing's [pumpkin-carving party](/events/halloween-eve-pumpkin-carving-party-2026-10-30/) on the 30th welcomes families; bring a pumpkin. [Halloween at The Vault](/events/halloween-at-the-vault-2026-10-31/) on the 31st has trick-or-treating at the shops through the day, and the Carousel says it will be open late that night.
- **Winter:** NedRINK opens in December, weather permitting, and Wild Bear runs Thanksgiving and winter-break days. The Town's [community events](https://nederlandco.org/1443/Community-Events) include the Holiday Mountain Market, a craft fair that closes out the year, with Mr. and Mrs. Claus.
- **Summer:** the same list has the Farmer's Market every other Sunday from May to October and the Carousel's summer circus at Chipeta Park.
