Train Cars Coffee & Kava is a cafe in three historic railroad cars that have been in Nederland since the late 1970s: a 1906 Colorado & Southern Pullman coach, a Sells-Floto circus advertising car and a 1910 caboose. It serves locally roasted coffee, kava and sandwiches and hosts music, art and performance events.
