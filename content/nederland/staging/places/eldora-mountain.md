Eldora Mountain Resort is a ski area 21 miles west of Boulder, with a base at 9,200 feet and a top at 10,600, 680 acres of terrain and 10 lifts. It has eight Woodward terrain parks, a Nordic center with 40 kilometres of classic, skate and snowshoe trails, and is an Ikon Pass partner. Most of the base area and many runs are on private land, and the resort is closed to all public access once the ski season ends.
