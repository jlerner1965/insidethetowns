The Covered Wagon is a restaurant and saloon on East 1st Street, owned by Jen Davis. Leashed, well-behaved dogs are allowed on the back patio; inside are dining rooms, a game room with a pool table and a large TV for sports, with music events posted on its Facebook page. The building is full of antiques, including a covered wagon collection, mining tools and a piano from about 1880.
