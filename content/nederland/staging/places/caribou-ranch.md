Boulder County Parks & Open Space runs Caribou Ranch, 2,151 acres between 8,300 and 10,000 feet in the shadow of the Continental Divide. Its trails are the Blue Bird Loop (1.8 miles, moderate) and the DeLonde Trail (1.2 miles, easy), passing the Blue Bird Mine complex and the DeLonde Homestead. There is a restroom and picnic tables near the trailhead; horse-trailer parking is at Mud Lake, which connects by the Caribou Ranch Link trail.

Rules: day use only, no pets, no bikes, no fires, hunting or camping.
