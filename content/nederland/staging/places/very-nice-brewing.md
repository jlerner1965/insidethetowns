Very Nice Brewing opened in Nederland on October 25, 2012, and also runs a taproom in Black Hawk. The Nederland taproom is upstairs; dogs on a leash are welcome but must enter at the front and go straight upstairs. Many of its beers are brewed gluten-reduced, and its Nederland events page lists live music.
