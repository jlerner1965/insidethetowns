New Moon bakes its pastries from scratch, including gluten-free and vegan items, and serves breakfast sandwiches and oven-baked artisan sandwiches all day. Its house roast and decaf come from Firestation Roasters in Nederland. There is indoor seating and an outdoor patio, and it shows work by local artists.
