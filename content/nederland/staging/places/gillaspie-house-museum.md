The house was built in 1905–06 for Dr. Carbon Gillaspie, Nederland's doctor and for a time its mayor, and his wife Grace. Their son and his wife gave it to the Town of Nederland in May 1975 on condition that it be kept open as a museum. The Nederland Area Historical Society runs it with volunteers; it has no annual budget and asks for donations.
