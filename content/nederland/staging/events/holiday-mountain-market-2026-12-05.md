Nederland's Holiday Mountain Market, the Town's craft fair to close out the year, with local vendors, live music and a visit from Mr. and Mrs. Claus at the Community Center. The Nederland Food Pantry, which has a booth, lists it for Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 10 am to 5 pm each day; the Town's page had not posted the dates on November 1.
