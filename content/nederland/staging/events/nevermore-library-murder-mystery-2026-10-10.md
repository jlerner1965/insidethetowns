A Poe-themed murder mystery fundraiser held after hours in the Nederland Community Library, with a silent auction supporting its youth and accessibility programmes. Each ticket comes with an assigned character; guests gather clues and question suspects while catered bites and themed cocktails are served. Adults only, limited tickets, gothic or cocktail attire encouraged.
