Halloween at The Vault, the building that holds Very Nice Brewing's Nederland taproom. Children can trick-or-treat at the shops in The Vault and at neighbouring Mountain Man Outdoor Store during the day; in the evening there are treats for adults and live music from Chris Myth and Scott Spanbauer on pedal steel in the taproom.
