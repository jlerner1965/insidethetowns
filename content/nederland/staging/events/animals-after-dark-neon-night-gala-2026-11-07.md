The Carousel of Happiness's annual fundraising gala, this year themed Neon Night, raising money for the nonprofit's programming and a fire suppression system for the carousel. The evening runs from a cocktail hour and dinner with music by Ben Melius through door prizes, a paddle raise, carousel rides and a late dance party with DJs. A free charter shuttle from Boulder is offered; tickets are $100, or $150 for the limited VIP tier.
