A talk in the Nederland Mining Museum's Evening at the Museum series, on the Cash Mine in the Gold Hill district: its history from the 1872 claim and the mineralogy of its gold-silver telluride ores, including third-level mining, milling and ore microscopy. Run by Boulder County Parks & Open Space; space is limited and registration is required.
