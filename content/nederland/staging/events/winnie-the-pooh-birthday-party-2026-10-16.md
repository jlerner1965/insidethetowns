A drop-in Winnie-the-Pooh birthday party at the Nederland Community Library. Children can bring a stuffed animal, write a birthday note to Pooh and work through self-serve party stations; a short storytime at 4 pm is followed by honey treats, games, crafts and a scavenger hunt.
