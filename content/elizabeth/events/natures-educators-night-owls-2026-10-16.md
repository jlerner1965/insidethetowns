Nature's Educators bring live animal ambassadors to the library on the Friday of fall break, an owl among them, for a look at the creatures that come out after dark and the features that let them thrive at night. Spooky science for the week before Halloween. Two identical sessions, at 1:30 and 3:30; pick one.
