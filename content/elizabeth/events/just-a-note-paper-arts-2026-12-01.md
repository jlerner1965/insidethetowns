The library's paper-arts circle meets Tuesday, December 1, 10 am to 1 pm: card making, scrapbooking, art journaling, artist trading cards, planners, doodling and coloring. Bring your own supplies and projects; the point is to share ideas, techniques and resources. Newcomers and seasoned crafters both welcome. Food and drink are allowed and there are outlets.
