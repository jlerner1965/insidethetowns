The Town of Elizabeth’s tree lighting on Main Street, Saturday, December 5, 3 to 6 pm, with the tree lit at 5 pm. The program includes Santa and Mrs. Claus, elf crafts, sweet treats and food trucks along Main Street. Parking is at Town Hall, the Depot lot and on side streets, with accessible parking at the north end of the Gesin lot. Expect street closures and follow the posted traffic signs.
