The evening half of the library's book club, for readers who cannot make the Monday lunchtime meeting: the same month's title, discussed on the third Tuesday at 5:30. October's book is Ender's Game by Orson Scott Card; in November the group picks its 2027 list. Copies are held at the desk. Free.
