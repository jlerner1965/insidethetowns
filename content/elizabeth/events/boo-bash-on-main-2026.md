The Elizabeth Area Chamber of Commerce takes over South Main Street on the Saturday before Halloween: a harvest festival among the brick storefronts and a Trick or Treat Street where downtown businesses hand out candy. Costumes expected. The Chamber posts the vendor list and the block closures in the fortnight before.
