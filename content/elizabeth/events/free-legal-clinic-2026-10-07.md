A free virtual legal clinic for people handling a civil (non-criminal) matter without a lawyer, run from the library on the first Wednesday of the month, 2 to 3:40. Volunteer attorneys answer questions, help with forms and explain court procedure; the library provides the computer and the technical help. It is information only, not representation. Spots are limited, so call the branch on 303-646-3416 to sign up.
