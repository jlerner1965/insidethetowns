<p>Discover your roots with the <em>Elizabeth Heritage Crew</em>, genealogy group at the Elizabeth Library! This relaxed, drop-in gathering meets on the <strong>first Saturday of every month from 10:00 AM to noon</strong> in the library meeting room. No RSVP or ongoing commitment is required—just come when you can.</p><p>Whether you're new to family history or an experienced researcher, you’ll have the chance to learn, explore, and collaborate with others who share a passion for genealogy. Participants can work together on a variety of topics, including census and vital records, land and probate records, DNA research, and tips for using Ancestry and FamilySearch.</p><p>All are welcome. Bring your questions, your curiosity, and any projects you’re working on as we trace lineage and uncover stories from the past together.</p>
