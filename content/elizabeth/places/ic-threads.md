A custom embroidery business on Main Street, stitching logos, names and designs onto shirts, caps and bags to order rather than selling clothing off the rack. Its site lists corporate and small-company logos, team uniforms, ranch and farm logos and brands, club logos, and military, law enforcement and fire work. It will digitize a customer's own artwork or logo, stitch on its own stock or on an item brought in, and make a sample to approve, in person or by email or text, before the final piece is stitched.
