A kitchen and bar on Elizabeth Street with a traditional American menu: lunch and dinner from 11 on weekdays, brunch from 10 at weekends, and the bar open to 1 am on Friday and Saturday. The kitchen closes before the bar does: at 9 pm Monday to Thursday, 10 pm Friday and Saturday, and 8 pm Sunday, so order food before then.
