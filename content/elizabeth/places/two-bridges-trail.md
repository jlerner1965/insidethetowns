The nearest real hiking is west of town on Bayou Gulch Road, where Douglas County's Bayou Gulch Open Space and Two Bridges Trail run through gulches and ponderosa. Douglas County counts 10.4 miles of trail across the property, so a short loop and a long afternoon are both there; the county’s map shows the junctions. Hikers, horses and mountain bikes, open year round, with connections toward the Cherry Creek Trail.
