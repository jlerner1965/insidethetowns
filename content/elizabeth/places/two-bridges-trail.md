The nearest real hiking is west of town on Bayou Gulch Road, where Douglas County’s Bayou Gulch Open Space and Two Bridges Trail wander over gently rolling prairie: upland grassland, creekside stretches and a meadow along a wheat field, with views of Pikes Peak. The county counts 10.4 miles of trail in loops from 2.7 to 10.4 miles; its map shows the junctions. Hikers, horses and mountain bikes, snowshoes and cross-country skis when there is snow, dogs on leash, and a connection to the Cherry Creek Trail. The open space is leased to the Colorado Horse Park, so access is limited during its scheduled events, and the horse jumps are not for public use. The trailhead lot, on the south side of Bayou Gulch Road two miles east of Parker Road, holds 30 cars and 12 to 15 horse trailers.
