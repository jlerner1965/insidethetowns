A mini-donut counter in the front lobby of the original Elizabeth Hotel on Main Street, frying to order in classic cinnamon-sugar, a list of signature flavors and a donut of the month. The Lamb family now runs it.

Party platters need at least 24 hours' notice: a box holds 36 mini donuts in up to six flavors, and the shop asks for the event details with the order. Larger orders, weddings included, are arranged directly by phone or email.

Address, phone, hours and the party-platter terms were read on the shop's own site on 30 October 2026, and the new owners on its Our Story page. The site also carries an older set of hours, hidden on the page, that disagrees; call ahead if the times matter.
