Eighteen holes through the pines at the north end of Evans Park, with enough trees to make you think about the line. The first tee is just off the parking area for the pond. The Park & Recreation District charges $5 a player toward upkeep, payable by Venmo at the start of the course; check its page for private-event closures.

In late 2017 the district's board had staff build the course; local players Cameron Burrow and Jeremy Priest helped design the first nine holes, and after months of play it was redesigned and expanded to eighteen. A rental set of three discs (driver, midrange and putter) is $5 from the park office, open Monday to Friday 9 am–5 pm, with no rentals outside those hours. Dogs stay on leash, and the district asks players to use a spotter on blind throws, watch for the prickly pear cactus and not smoke on the course.

The layout, fees, rental hours and rules were read on the district's disc golf page on 10 October 2026 (prices as of that date), which carries a notice of a private-event closure on 18 July 2026.
