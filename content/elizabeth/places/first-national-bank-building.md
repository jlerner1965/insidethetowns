Built in 1907 and run as the First National Bank of Elizabeth (from 1914 the Elizabeth State Bank) until 1933, when the Elizabeth State Bank was taken over by the Elbert County Bank in Elbert, the building at 188 South Main is the anchor of the historic block. It was placed on the National Register of Historic Places on January 31, 2023. It now holds a business rather than a bank; look at it from the street.
