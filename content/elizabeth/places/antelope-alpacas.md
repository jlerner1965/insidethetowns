A fibre arts center on Main Street selling alpaca yarn, roving and finished goods. Beyond yarn, the shop stocks batting, books and tools for projects, and clothing, blankets and socks for gifts. It is open Saturdays, 10 am–5 pm.

The shop no longer teaches. Its classes page says the fibre arts classes stopped in the pandemic and a 2022 restart was cancelled for low sign-ups, and it lists no classes now.

Address, phone, hours and stock were read on the shop's home and Find Us pages on 30 October 2026, and the class history on its Classes page the same day; the site carries a 2025 copyright line and no dated post.
