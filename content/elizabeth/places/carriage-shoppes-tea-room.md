A tea room in the Carriage Shoppes building at the corner of Poplar and Main, across from the police station: three dining rooms in a historic livery stable, premium teas served by the pot, homemade lunches and afternoon high tea, with the antique and gift stalls next door. The tea room prefers email for questions and arrangements.

The tea room's site puts the neighbouring antique store at about 5,000 square feet with around 25 local artists; the store's own site says over 14 local artisans and vendors.

Name, address, phone and hours were read on the tea room's home, Location and Contact pages on 30 October 2026; none carries a dated post.
