The Park & Rec District's other park, and the one most families use: a splash pad, a skate park of metal ramps and rails with a wooden half-pipe on about 3,000 square feet of concrete, open any daylight hour to boards, bikes, scooters and skates; playgrounds for ages two to five and six to twelve; two pickleball courts west of the splash pad; two lighted ball fields and two multi-use fields; a seasonal snack shack; and a fishing pond stocked with trout by Colorado Parks and Wildlife, with a third-mile trail loop around Dragonfly Pond. Leashed dogs anywhere except the fields and the splash pad.
