The Elizabeth Stampede is the town's biggest weekend: a Professional Rodeo Cowboys Association rodeo over three days at the Casey Jones Park arena, with a concert the night before and a Saturday-morning parade down Main Street, hosted since 2026 by the Elizabeth Chamber of Commerce. In 2026 the concert was on June 4 and the rodeo ran June 5 to 7. The 2027 Stampede is June 3 to 6; its schedule and tickets are not out yet.

The 2027 dates, the 2026 schedule and the parade details were read on the Stampede's own site on 30 October 2026.
