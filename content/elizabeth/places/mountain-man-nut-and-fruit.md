The Colorado snack company's shop in the Elizabeth Mercantile building on Main Street: nuts, dried fruit, trail mixes, chocolates and candy by weight, with gift tins at Christmas. The company's store page says each of its shops sets its own hours and stock, and to call the store for gift trays and holiday orders.

Address, phone and hours were read on Mountain Man Nut & Fruit's own store page on 10 October 2026, which lists the Elizabeth shop among the company's stores. The Elizabeth shop's separate site, mtnmaneliz.com, which the guide had cited, is now a password page reading "New Website Coming Soon!" with holiday hours for an unstated year, so it is not used. The Elizabeth Main Street Program's directory, read the same day, also lists the store at 207 S Main Street.
