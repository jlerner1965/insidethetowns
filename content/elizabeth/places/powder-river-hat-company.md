A hat maker's shop on Main Street where custom cowboy hats are built start to finish by hand, from beaver-felt hat bodies, shaped and fitted in the room. Worth the look even if you are not buying.

Address, phone and hours were read on the shop's own site on 30 October 2026, which carries no date. The Elizabeth Main Street Program's directory, read the same day, lists the shop at 232 S Main St instead, the address it also gives Randy's Antiques and Art; the guide follows the shop's own site.
