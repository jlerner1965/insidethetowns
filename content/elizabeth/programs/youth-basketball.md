The Park and Recreation District’s basketball league, open to ages 6 to 12 in three co-ed divisions (6–8, 8–10 and 10–12). A child whose age falls in two divisions plays in whichever the parents choose, by size and ability. Teams start practicing January 4 and play eight Saturday games from January 9; the 8–10 and 10–12 divisions end with a tournament on February 27. The district has no gym, so practices are in the school gyms after school activities finish, usually at 5:30, 6:30 or 7:30.

The district is also hiring **basketball officials** for the Saturday games: 14 and up, $20 an hour to start, with a training session in January 2027 at Legacy Academy, 12 to 1:15 pm, date to be set. Sign-up for the training is in the same Rec1 catalog.
