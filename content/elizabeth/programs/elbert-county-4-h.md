4-H in Elbert County is run by Colorado State University Extension from its office at the Kiowa fairgrounds, through community clubs that meet monthly and take on projects of the members’ choosing. Four meet in Elizabeth: **Running Creek**, first Mondays at 6:30 pm at Elizabeth Middle School; **High Plains**, second Tuesdays at 6 pm, and **Gold Creek**, third Mondays at 6:30 pm, both at Harvest Bible Church, 826 S Elbert St; and **Sun Country**, third Wednesdays at 6:15 pm at Son Country Community Church, 7114 Sun Country Drive. Each club’s leader and phone number are on [the Extension’s club list](https://extension.colostate.edu/elbert/offering/elbert-4-h/4-h-clubs/).

The Extension says to talk to a club’s leader first, then enroll on 4-HOnline (the email that follows says “pending”) and pay the $35 fee to the office. Fall enrollment runs October 1 to December 31. Members go to the monthly meetings; animal and shooting sports projects add required trainings and their own fees, and families download the project manuals from the state 4-H site.
