The Park and Recreation District’s Kids Club runs a day camp on each school break, timed to the Elizabeth School District calendar. The Thanksgiving camp is at **Singing Hills Elementary**, for ages 5 to 12; registration opened October 21 and closes November 18, and space is limited. The Kids Club page gives its break camps as 7 am to 6 pm at $55 a day, more on a field-trip day, and every child needs the Kids Club’s 2026–27 registration packet on file first; one packet covers every Kids Club program for the school year. The camp page does not say which days of the break, November 23 to 30, it runs.
