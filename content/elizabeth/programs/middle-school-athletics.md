From this school year the middle school’s old league, the NPAC, is part of one larger league, the Pikes Peak Athletic Conference–Middle School: 34 schools in sub-leagues of five. The 2026–27 seasons: football, August 8 to October 3; cross country and girls’ volleyball, August 10 to September 26; girls’ softball, September 28 to October 31; wrestling, September 28 to November 14; boys’ basketball, November 16 to January 16; girls’ basketball, January 18 to March 3; and track and field, April 5 to May 15.

Before a first practice, a student needs a sports physical, the participation form and an emergency contact form at the front office. Eligibility is checked every Friday: one F or two D’s in any class makes a student ineligible for that week’s games, and a student ineligible for three weeks is removed from the team. The athletic handbook and the forms are linked on the school’s athletics page.
