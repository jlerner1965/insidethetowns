The Park and Recreation District runs the before- and after-school program at both elementary schools. **Before school**, care starts at 6:30 am and runs until the school day begins, $13 a day. **After school**, it runs from dismissal to 6 pm, $15 a day, with activities led by the Kids Club staff; there is none on early-dismissal days. It is a state-licensed childcare program, so a family fills out the 2026–27 registration packet once, valid August to August, and then books and pays for each day ahead in the district’s online catalog, by school and semester. There are no drop-ins, and a child not on the day’s roster is sent home.

If a child booked for the day will not come, call the park office, 303-646-3599, or email info@elizabethpr.com that day for a credit. On an unplanned school closing the district credits every booked account. The same staff run day camps on the school breaks, 7 am to 6 pm at $55 a day; the next is the [Thanksgiving camp](/families/#program-thanksgiving-break-day-camp).
