The Park and Recreation District’s volleyball league for 6-to-10-year-olds, in a 6–8 and an 8–10 division. Practices are in the evening once or twice a week, and games are Saturday afternoons at EMS or Frontier, as the district puts it, from the week of March 6 to April 17. Registration runs December 1 to February 16; the fee is $85, and the district asks for volunteer coaches. The rules and last season’s schedules are on the district’s page.
