The Park and Recreation District’s co-ed soccer league, in four divisions from 4–6 to 10–12; a player must reach the division’s minimum age before or during the season. Teams practice once a week and play one Saturday game, eight weeks a season, with the emphasis, the district says, on fundamentals and fun. The fall 2026 season ran August 22 to October 10 at $100. The district lists coaching materials tied to MLS, better jerseys and tickets to a Colorado Rapids game among this year’s changes.

A **player clinic** is on Saturday, October 24: ages 8 to 10 from 9 to 10:30 am and ages 6 to 8 from 10:45 am to 12:15 pm, registered in the Rec1 catalog. The page does not give the clinic’s place or price.
