The school’s athletics pages list the Cardinals’ teams by season: **fall** cross country, girls’ flag football, football, boys’ golf, gymnastics, boys’ soccer, softball, volleyball and spirit (poms); **winter** boys’ and girls’ basketball, boys’ wrestling, girls’ wrestling (which competes at Kiowa High School) and girls’ swimming (at Ponderosa); **spring** baseball, esports, girls’ golf, girls’ soccer, boys’ swimming (at Ponderosa), track and boys’ volleyball.

To play, a parent registers the student on GoBound and uploads the most recent physical; the school says not to wait for the official start date, because a student has to be registered and cleared, by email, before the first tryout. Preseason workouts need neither. A student with two or more F’s on the Monday eligibility report sits out that week, and one F brings an academic warning by email. The game calendar is on [GoBound](https://www.gobound.com/co/schools/elizabethhs); the records and scores are on [the Cardinals’ page here](/sports/).
