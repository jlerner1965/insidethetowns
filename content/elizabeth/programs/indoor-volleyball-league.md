Six-on-six volleyball on Friday nights in the middle-school gym, for players 16 and older, in upper and lower divisions. Teams register together, up to 12 on a roster, for $400; games go to 25, at 6, 7 and 8 pm. A team plays an even split of men and women, or more women than men. The season is six weeks with a tournament at the end; registration opens November 1, and the district has not posted the first night.
