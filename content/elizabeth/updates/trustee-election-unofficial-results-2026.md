The Town's four open Board of Trustees seats were on Elbert County's November 3 ballot, six candidates for four places and up to four votes a ballot. The county's updated count, posted as unofficial at 4:56 pm on November 4: Tracy J. Hutchins 621 votes, Ryan McKenna 591, Paul Schwarzkopf 525, Ramon Anthony Atencio 515, Paula B. Ray 509 and Deborah Kula 457, from 1,230 ballots cast in the race. In the election-night count of 7:36 pm on November 3, Ray was third and Atencio fifth. The top four take the seats, and six votes now separate fourth place from fifth in a count the county labels unofficial.
