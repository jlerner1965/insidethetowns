The Town's four open Board of Trustees seats were on Elbert County's November 3 ballot, six candidates for four places and up to four votes a ballot. The county's election-night count, posted as unofficial at 7:36 pm on November 3: Tracy J. Hutchins 517 votes, Ryan McKenna 488, Paula B. Ray 428, Paul Schwarzkopf 425, Ramon Anthony Atencio 421 and Deborah Kula 384, from 970 ballots cast in the race. The top four take the seats, and four votes separate fourth place from fifth in a count the county labels unofficial.
