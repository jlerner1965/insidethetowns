Highway 5, the road up Mount Blue Sky, is closed to cars for the rest of 2026: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/timed-entry/10087438) says it closed to vehicles on October 5. When it reopens (on May 22 in 2026), anyone driving up needs a timed ticket from Recreation.gov, bought before leaving Idaho Springs because there is no cell service on the mountain. In 2026 it cost $20 a car and $15 a motorcycle, and federal passes such as America the Beautiful are no longer accepted. The 2027 dates and prices are not yet published.

This is for anyone planning the drive from Idaho Springs, America's highest paved road. Since 2026 Denver Mountain Parks has [run the recreation area](https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Parks-Recreation/News/2026/Denver-Mountain-Parks-to-take-helm-on-Mt.-Blue-Sky-this-Summer), collecting the fees, managing the reservations and setting the hours.

## What you need when the road is open

| | The rule in 2026 |
|---|---|
| Ticket | A timed ticket from Recreation.gov to park at Mount Goliath, Summit Lake or the summit between 7 am and 6 pm |
| Cost | $20 a car, $15 a motorcycle; bikes and hikers from below the welcome station free |
| Free passes | From Denver Public Library and the Clear Creek County Library District with a library card; enrolled tribal members enter free |
| When to book | 75 percent of tickets 30 days ahead and 25 percent two days ahead, both at 8 am Mountain Time |
| Your window | Enter within your two-hour slot, then stay all day; one reservation a day, holder in the car with photo ID |
| At the gate | No tickets sold at the welcome station; show it printed or saved on your phone |
| Cancelling | Up to 24 hours ahead, refunded less $2; nothing for no-shows or weather |
| Vehicles | 30 feet long at most; motorhomes not recommended |

The road climbs over 7,000 feet in its 28 miles from Idaho Springs, the [Forest Service says](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/mount-blue-sky-recreation-area-scenic-byway), to 14,130 feet, with no guardrails and many cyclists. Dogs are welcome on a hand-held leash.

## What is up there

- **Mount Goliath and the Dos Chappell Nature Center,** 3 miles up Highway 5: the quarter-mile Alpine Gardens Loop, and a [nature center](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/mt-goliath-natural-area-dos-chappell-nature-center) open daily, 10 am–5 pm; vault toilets.
- **Summit Lake Park:** Denver's 160 acres, the highest city park in North America, with restrooms and an accessible path to the Chicago Lakes overlook; [no drinking water](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/summit-lake-park).
- **The summit,** 14,264 feet: vault toilets, no water, the ruins of the Crest House and a short trail to the top.
- **Echo Lake Lodge,** at the bottom of Highway 5, is [closed](https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Parks-Recreation/Mountain-Parks/Echo-Lake-Lodge-Park) while Denver redesigns and rebuilds it.

Recreation.gov warns that the weather changes fast: intense sun, cold, snow, hail, high wind and thin air, and the road can close any summer day for winter conditions. Bring warm clothes whatever it is like in town.

## When the road is closed: below the gate

Echo Lake Park, at the foot of Highway 5 on Colorado 103, is Denver's 24-acre lake at 10,600 feet, open from an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset with [dogs on a leash](https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Parks-Recreation/Urban-Parks-Trails/Park-Rules-Policies). From the [Echo Lake Trailhead](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/echo-lake-trailhead), with vault toilets, the [Chicago Lakes Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/trails/chicago-lakes-trail) runs 6 miles one way into the wilderness; it needs a free self-issued permit from the trailhead, dogs stay on a hand-held leash, and the Forest Service lists it for skiing and snowshoeing. Walkers and cyclists from below the welcome station pay nothing.

On Colorado 103 between I-70 and West Chicago Creek Road, a [Forest Service order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/alerts/colo-hwy-103-closure) bans camping, and being there after sundown or before sunrise, within a quarter mile of the highway.

## When the road is closed: in town

The [Argo Mill & Tunnel](https://mightyargo.com/winter-hours), 2350 Riverside Drive, runs hour-long mill tours with gold or gemstone panning every day from 10 am to 4 pm, the last tour at 3 pm, through January 3, 2027, except Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Book ahead: walk-ins fill same-day tours. The cable car keeps its own winter dates, closed on set days each week.

## Before you go

- **Tickets and the 2027 season:** [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/timed-entry/10087438), where Denver will post next year's dates.
- **The roads:** [COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) for Colorado 103 and I-70; the traction law applies on I-70 between Morrison and Dotsero from September 1 to May 31.
- **Questions:** the recreation area's line, 970-295-6600.
