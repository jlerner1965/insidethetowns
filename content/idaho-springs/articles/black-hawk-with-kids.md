Black Hawk's casinos are an adult itinerary. A family day here is built from a public space, one activity suited to the children's ages and a meal.

## Keep the first stop small

[Gregory Street Plaza](/places/gregory-street-plaza/) is the town stop. Its streets, sculptures and older buildings make a short outing on their own, and the City's calendar adds dated family events such as October's Halloween Street program. The [history guide](/articles/black-hawk-history/) tells the gold-camp story behind the buildings.

Some casino restaurants appear in the food directory. Ask the property whether children are admitted and how a family reaches the dining room.

## Have an indoor alternative

[Gilpin County Public Library](/places/gilpin-county-public-library/) is on Highway 119 north of the casino district, apart from the downtown walk, so plan transport between the two. Check its program calendar for age ranges and closure notices before driving out.

## Choose one outdoor scale

[Golden Gate Canyon State Park](/places/golden-gate-canyon-state-park/) is run by Colorado Parks and Wildlife, whose visitor pages carry the trail map, entry details and conditions. Read them, and the forecast, before choosing between a short walk and a full hike.

[Maryland Mountain and Quartz Valley](/places/maryland-mountain-quartz-valley-open-space-park/) is the City's open space above town: a trail system shared with other users, not a fenced playground. Head back to town if snow, mud or time rules out the longer route.

[Explore Black Hawk](/articles/explore-black-hawk-in-driving-order/) lays out the wider area. A short event or plaza visit, lunch and one modest outdoor stop make a full family day.
