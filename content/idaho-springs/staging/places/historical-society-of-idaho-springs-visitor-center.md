The Historical Society of Idaho Springs keeps a visitor center and museum at 2060 Miner Street, where visitors can shop for crafts and souvenirs and tour the museum. The society's stated mission is "to initiate, promote and nurture a historic preservation ethic in the greater Idaho Springs community", and its site has pages on the town's historic sites, local lore and history. It describes the Mount Blue Sky road, "the highest paved road in North America", as starting at the visitor center.

The society's page gives hours by season only, with no days of the week: summer 9:30 am to 5 pm, winter 10 am to 4 pm. Address, phone and hours were read on the society's home page on 10 October 2026; the page carries no dated post or notice, so call before making a special trip.
