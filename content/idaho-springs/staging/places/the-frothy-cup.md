The Frothy Cup roasts its coffee in small batches at the shop, buying Fair Trade Organic, Rainforest Alliance and single-source green beans, and sells light, medium and dark roasts online and wholesale. Its history page says the 1885 Zeller building at 1510 Miner Street has housed coffee roasters for over 30 years, and that the shop took the name The Frothy Cup in 2014. The menu lists drip, cold brew and espresso drinks, frozen drinks, organic fruit smoothies and teas; giant Cowboy Cookies and Frothydoodles; giant muffins made primarily with organic, non-GMO ingredients; and hot food including smothered green chili breakfast burritos, quiche and tamales.

Address and phone were read on the café's Contact page, and the rest on its home, menu and Our Story pages, on 10 October 2026. No page gives the opening hours. The City of Idaho Springs' visitor site, read the same day, lists The Frothy Cup at 1510 Miner St with the same number.
