The Phoenix Gold Mine is outside Idaho Springs, on Trail Creek Road. The family that runs it says it has given tours since 1988. Its underground tour, open all year with gold panning included, follows wide, flat, well-lit passages with no steps; the site lists it at $28 for adults, $22 for children 3 to 16 and free for babies. The Centurion train ride and dark mine tour, seasonal and reached by a five-to-ten-minute uphill walk, is $32 and $28, and panning in Trail Creek itself is $18 and $16; call ahead about creek conditions in spring and fall. Pets are welcome under its policy, and group rates start at 15 people. Activities depend on the weather.

Hours, rates, address and phone were read on the mine's home and Hours & Rates pages on 10 October 2026; its blog feed's newest post is from August 2024. An XpoPress feature dated 23 July 2026 describes the mine's tours in the present tense (and dates the first tours to 1984), but it is a trade publication, not a local report.
