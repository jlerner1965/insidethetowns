Indian Hot Springs' own site could not be read: it serves only a JavaScript shell with the title "Indian Hot Springs" and the tagline "Proudly preserved since 1865". No detail here has been checked against the resort's pages.

Read elsewhere on 10 October 2026: the City of Idaho Springs' visitor site says Indian Hot Springs has welcomed visitors for over 150 years, that it was approved for a Colorado historical grant under which a team of experts began a restoration plan in 2025, and that improvements planned for 2026 and beyond include new water systems, outdoor pools and kid-friendly areas, upgraded electrical, water and mechanical systems and better accommodations. The City's Virginia Canyon parking page points visitors to free "Soda Creek parking, north of the Indian Hot Springs". The Historical Society of Idaho Springs' history page traces the springs to Dr. Cummings' bath house of 1863 and the Ocean Bath House of 1871, and says the springs are still a popular attraction.

The handoff packet (not confirmed): 302 Soda Creek Road; a mineral-water pool inside a plant-filled dome, with other soaking options on the property; weekend pool reservations required; no lifeguards.
