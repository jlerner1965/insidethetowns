St. Mary's Glacier is outside Idaho Springs, a short drive up Fall River Road. The Clear Creek County Tourism Bureau's page, read on 10 October 2026, puts it in the Arapaho National Forest and describes an easy-to-moderate trail of 1.5 miles from the parking area to the base of the glacier, starting at 10,428 feet and reaching 10,848 at the base, with the top of the snowfield at 11,236 feet; it puts the round trip at about an hour and 45 minutes. Dogs are allowed on a leash. Trailhead parking is $20 a vehicle a day, cash or check, with restrooms in the paid lot. The bureau notes that the "glacier" has been reclassified as a semi-permanent snowfield because it does not move.

Clear Creek County's Current Conditions page, read the same day, lists the Forest Service trails including St. Mary's Glacier as open, without a date. None of these figures has been read on a land manager's own page.
