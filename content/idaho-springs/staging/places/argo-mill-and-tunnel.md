The Argo Mill & Tunnel on Riverside Drive is the mill, over 110 feet tall, that the operator says processed over $100 million in gold ore; the Argo Tunnel was once 4.2 miles long and flooded in 1943, and about 150 feet of the tunnel, up to the bulkhead door, is open to visitors. The mill was placed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1978. A guided tour covers the site's history, the tunnel entrance, the mill and its original equipment, and ends with a 20-minute gold-panning lesson; the operator says to allow 1.5 to 2 hours in all.

Tours start promptly at the top of the hour, and the last tour of the day varies by season. Admission is $30 for adults, $20 for children 5–16 and $4 for ages 1–4, with no charge for infants; gold panning ($21) and gemstone panning ($14) are sold separately without a reservation. Online reservations close two hours before a tour, walk-ins are first come, first served, and groups of 25 or more must reserve. The Mercantile gift shop is open seven days a week, 10 am to 6 pm, with winter hours of 10 am to 5 pm. Children under 40 inches must be carried or hand-held through the tunnel.
