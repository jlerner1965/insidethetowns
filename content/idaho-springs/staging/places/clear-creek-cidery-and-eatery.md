Clear Creek Cidery & Eatery serves cider, beer, cocktails, wine and mug drinks with a full kitchen, and says it has the largest selection of hard ciders in the Colorado mountains; its own cider is listed as "coming soon". The menu runs to chili and soup, burgers and sandwiches, entrees, appetizers and salads, and a children's menu and desserts, with gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan choices; gluten-free dishes are prepared in the same kitchen as gluten-containing food. Large groups can call to reserve.

Address, phone and kitchen hours were read on the cidery's home and Contact pages on 10 October 2026. Both pages carry the undated banner "Chimichangas are BACK! Closed Tuesday & Wednesday this week", which the daily hours do not reflect. The City of Idaho Springs' visitor site, read the same day, lists the cidery with the same number at 1438 Miner Street.
