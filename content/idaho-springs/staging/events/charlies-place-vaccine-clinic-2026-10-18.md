Low-cost pet vaccination and microchip clinic at the county's animal shelter in Dumont, west of Idaho Springs, starting at noon. First come, first served, and everyone in line will be served; bring prior vaccination and veterinary records if you have them. Vaccines are $20 each and microchips $20 with free lifetime updates. Contact Sue LaBarron, slebarron@clearcreekcounty.us. Read on the county calendar on 10 October 2026.
