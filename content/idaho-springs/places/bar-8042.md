Bar 8042 sits in the center of Ameristar’s casino, with fireplaces, a video wall and alcoholic-beverage service **24 hours a day**. DJs play Friday and Saturday nights from 9 pm to 2 am: the October schedule names DJ Amen on October 16–17, Erin Stereo on October 23–24 and DJ Dizzy D on October 30–31.
