Clear Creek Rafting Company runs beginner, intermediate and advanced trips on Clear Creek from its base on Whitewater Road. Its site says trips go daily from mid-May through mid-August, depending on water conditions, and include free digital photos and a wetsuit and splash jacket. Arkansas River trips are run by its sister company, Whitewater Adventure Outfitters. Prices and hours are not on the home page; booking runs through its online reservation system.
