The Microtel at Georgetown Lake has an indoor pool and hot tub, laundry facilities, and complimentary breakfast, Wi-Fi and parking. Standard rooms come with a mini-refrigerator, microwave and coffee/tea maker, and the hotel advertises **24/7 service**.

Up to two dogs are allowed in rooms for an additional fee; the dog option is not available for suites. The hotel’s page gives no check-in or check-out times, so ask when booking.

The hotel is at the lake end of Georgetown, away from the downtown museums and shops.
