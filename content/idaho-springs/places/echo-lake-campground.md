Echo Lake Campground sits at 10,600 feet near Echo Lake, at the base of Mount Blue Sky on Highway 103, on the Clear Creek Ranger District. From I-70 westbound take the Idaho Springs exit 240 onto Highway 103, go south 14 miles to the junction with Highway 5, turn right on Highway 5 and left into the campground. It has 17 sites, 10 for RVs and 7 for tents, each with a picnic table and fire ring, up to eight people a site, with parking spurs of 20 to 55 feet; tents go on the pads and vehicles on the spur. Vault toilets; no electric, water or sewer hookups, though the page's water line says potable water is available, so confirm before relying on it. A single site is $26 a night, reserved on Recreation.gov. Fishing in the lake and hiking nearby, and the drive up to the summit of Mount Blue Sky. Dogs on a hand-held leash.

These notes were read on the Forest Service's campground page on 10 October 2026, last updated 14 September 2026, which marked the site closed without a reason or reopening date.
