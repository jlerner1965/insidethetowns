Tommyknocker Brewery & Pub has brewed on Miner Street since 1994. Its site lists a Mushroom Swiss Burger, the Mountain Melt with maple pepper bacon, a giant pretzel with TKB mustard, raspberry chipotle wings, pimento mac and cheese, a fried cod taco, a pulled pork sandwich and a brisket cheesesteak, and names Peach Dream and Mountain West among its beers. It caters parties and corporate events; inquire by phone. Online ordering and gift cards run through Toast.

Hours, phone and address were read on the brewery's home page on 10 October 2026. The site carries no dated post; the Clear Creek Metropolitan Recreation District's 2026 Downtown Ghost Tours start at the corner of Miner Street and 14th Avenue, outside Tommyknocker, and the district's page says the brewery gives ticket holders a discount, which is the dated confirmation that it is operating this season.
