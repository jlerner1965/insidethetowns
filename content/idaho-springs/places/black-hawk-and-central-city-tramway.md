The Black Hawk & Central City Tramway is the City of Black Hawk's free bus, which the City says is made possible by the casinos. It runs seven days a week: Monday to Thursday every 30 minutes from 10 am to 2:30 am; Friday to Sunday every 30 minutes from 10 am to noon, every 20 minutes from noon to 2 am and every 30 minutes from 2 to 3:30 am. The City calls the intervals approximate. MV Transportation operates it, and the City refers schedule questions to MV at 303-761-3145. The route brochure the City links lists 19 stops, among them Gregory Plaza, Lower Gregory, Main Street in Central City and the casinos.

Drivers stop only at designated stops. Service animals are allowed; open drinks and smoking are not. The buses are wheelchair accessible, and any rider within three-quarters of a mile of the route can ask for an off-route pickup by calling MV dispatch at 303-761-3145, extension 1, at least two hours ahead, where the road is paved and wide enough for the bus to turn.

Hours, frequency and phone were read on the City's Shuttle Service page on 17 October 2026. The City Council's agenda of April 8, 2026 carried the Colorado Department of Transportation operating grant for the "Black Hawk and Central City Tramway for 2026", the dated confirmation that it runs this year.
