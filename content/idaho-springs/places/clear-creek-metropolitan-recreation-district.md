The Clear Creek Metropolitan Recreation District (CCMRD) runs the recreation center and pool at 98 12th Avenue. The rec center is open weekdays 6:30 am to 8:30 pm and weekends 10 am to 4 pm. Pool hours on weekdays are adult swim 7 to 10 am (to 9 am on Fridays) and open swim 10 am to 7:30 pm, with the lap pool closed Wednesdays from 5 to 7:30 pm; weekends are open swim 10 am to 4 pm. Holiday hours on the district's page: closed New Year's Day, Easter, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day; 10 am to 4 pm on July 5 and Halloween; 6:30 am to 5 pm on Thanksgiving Eve and New Year's Eve; 9 am to 8:30 pm the Friday after Thanksgiving.

The district also runs the autumn Downtown and Cemetery Ghost Tours, the Halloween Zombie Fun Run and the November Pumpkin SMASH, listed on this guide's events page. Hours were read on the district's Hours page and the address, phone and email (csa@ccmrd.com) on its Contact page on 10 October 2026; its events page carried October and November 2026 dates.
