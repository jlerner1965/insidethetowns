Uplift Inn uses keyless self check-in: the inn sends an access code before arrival, and the lobby is being updated. Check-in is **after 4 pm** and check-out **by 10 am**; early arrival or late departure must be arranged in advance.

In-room Wi-Fi is free, and on-site parking is free, first come, first served, for one car per room; extra cars may need paid street parking on Colorado Boulevard. Bed arrangements and access vary by room type, set out on the rooms page.

Up to two dogs per room are allowed, for a nightly pet fee; cats are not. Dogs stay leashed outside and cannot be left alone in the room.

Address and phone were read on the inn’s [contact page](https://www.upliftinn.com/contact), and arrival, parking and pet rules on its [policies page](https://www.upliftinn.com/policies), on 11 October 2026.
