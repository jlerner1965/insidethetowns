Mountain Gems is a family-owned jeweler that says it has one of Colorado's largest collections of gemstone and mineral jewelry. It sells its own designs, which it describes as drawn from the Colorado landscape, along with pendants, rings, earrings and rare finds, and takes custom design commissions and full jewelry repair. The shop is in unit B at 1447 Miner Street.

Address, phone and hours were read on the shop's home page on 10 October 2026; the page notes that hours may vary, and shows nothing dated beyond a copyright line.
