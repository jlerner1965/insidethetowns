The Majestic Art Gallery is a cooperative of member artists, about 35 of them on its artist pages and in its own count, with new work coming in weekly. The City of Idaho Springs' visitor site places it in the historic Faivre Building and lists glass, pottery, jewelry, sculpture, photography and original oil and watercolor paintings among its work. Its events page for summer 2026 listed the gallery's part in the Burro Races on 24 May, a Fur-ling Fest on 6 June at which it gave 15 percent of art sales to the event's animal rescues, and the Fourth of July.

Address, phone and hours were read on the gallery's home page on 10 October 2026; its events page, read the same day, carries the summer 2026 dates, the evidence that it is operating. The City's visitor site lists the gallery at 1636 Miner with a different number, 303-567-9303.
