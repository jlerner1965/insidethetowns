Two Brothers Deli serves breakfast and lunch, sandwiches, breakfast wraps and soup, with coffee, espresso, beer and wine, and takes orders online or by phone. It says it uses locally sourced, organic produce where it can, paper straws, birch-wood cutlery and other compostable to-go packaging, that 80 percent of its in-house waste is recycled or composted, and that it is powered by 30 rooftop solar panels.

Address, phone and hours were read on the deli's home page on 10 October 2026; the page shows nothing dated beyond a 2019 copyright line. The City of Idaho Springs' visitor site, read the same day, lists Two Brothers Deli at 1424 Miner with the same number, the second, independent source that it is operating.
