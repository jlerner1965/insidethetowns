Idaho Springs is a small downtown on a major interstate corridor, surrounded by mountains. Looking at Georgetown, up I-70 in the same school district, or Black Hawk, over Virginia Canyon in Gilpin County? Each has its own guide: [Moving to Georgetown](/articles/moving-to-georgetown/) and [Moving to Black Hawk](/articles/moving-to-black-hawk/). A weekend on Miner Street will not answer the utility, school, parking or commute questions for a particular home; the places below can.

## City services and the property

The [City of Idaho Springs](https://www.idahospringsco.com/) site links utility billing, water and wastewater, planning, police and transportation, and its [Utility Billing page](https://www.idahospringsco.com/Utilities) is the starting point for municipal accounts. A postal address does not show whether a property is inside the city, so confirm that and which providers serve it.

For building work or a rental plan, take the address and the proposed use to the City's planning staff, and check the actual approvals and property records before relying on a listing description.

## Schools

[Clear Creek School District RE-1](https://ccsdre1.org/) runs the area's public schools on a four-day school week, with optional Enrichment Fridays. The district is at 320 Highway 103 in Idaho Springs, 303-567-3850, and its enrollment page has the current process. Ask about the school assignment, enrollment documents and transport for the address, and plan childcare or work around the school calendar.

## Getting around

The City's [Transportation Options page](https://www.idahospringsco.com/transportation-options) links the current transport services. Check the route and timetable for the workday trip you would make; where it depends on a connection, check both services.

Check [COtrip](https://www.cotrip.org/) before a mountain or interstate drive, and try a prospective commute at the time of day you would make it, in more than one season; distance alone does not give a drive time.

## Everyday life

The [Idaho Springs Public Library](/places/idaho-springs-public-library/), the recreation district, the parks and the downtown businesses are places to start meeting the community, and the calendar lists public events, library programs included.

Check where you will buy groceries, get the services you need and park at home. For remote work, get an address-level answer from the internet provider and test the connection on site. For a rental, get utilities, snow removal, parking and the full lease term in writing from the landlord.

## A useful first visit

Do the ordinary errands before the sightseeing: visit the block where you might live, drive the school or work route and check the services at that address. Then the [walking-order guide](/articles/explore-idaho-springs-in-walking-order/) and [family guide](/articles/idaho-springs-with-kids/) show how town life fits around those routines. Mountain recreation is an advantage of the setting, with its own access and seasons to plan for.
