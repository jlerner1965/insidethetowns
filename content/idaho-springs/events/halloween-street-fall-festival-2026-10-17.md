The City’s free Halloween Street & Fall Festival fills historic Gregory Street with Halloween sculptures, photo stations, a pumpkin passport and a mine maze. Saturday, October 17, 11 am to 3 pm; the festival returns Saturday, October 24, and Halloween Night on October 31 runs to its own hours.
