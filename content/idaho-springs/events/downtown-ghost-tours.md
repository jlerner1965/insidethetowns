A 1.3-mile walking tour of downtown "filled with history, mystery, and haunts", every Wednesday at 7 pm from September 23 through October 28. It starts at the corner of Miner Street and 14th Avenue by Tommyknocker Brewery, loops up to the Argo Mill and ends at the Idaho Springs Public Library. Tickets are $26.44 with fee, and $36.73 for the last tour on October 28, when the organizer says a guest from a paranormal communications group joins the walk. On 10 October 2026 the October 14 tour was marked sold out. Tommyknocker Brewery and Smokin' Yard's BBQ give ticket holders a discount, per the recreation district's home page.
