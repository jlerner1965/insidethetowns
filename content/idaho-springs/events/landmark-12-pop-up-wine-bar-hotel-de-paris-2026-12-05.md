The Hotel de Paris Museum opens Landmark 12, its pop-up wine bar inside the 1875 hotel on 6th Street, on the two Georgetown Christmas Market weekends: December 5, 6, 12 and 13 from 4 to 8 pm. The museum describes it as a quiet place to sip among the original 19th-century rooms, not a bar and not a museum. The home page also announces Holiday Splendor of the Gilded Age, the museum decorated for a Victorian Christmas, without dates. No prices are posted.
