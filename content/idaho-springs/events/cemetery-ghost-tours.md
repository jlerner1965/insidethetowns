A walk around the hillside of the Idaho Springs Cemetery with stories of its famous, infamous and forgotten residents, every Thursday at 7 pm from September 24 through October 29. Meet the guide at the top of the hill near the cemetery gate; the organizer says to look for a yellow Jeep. Tickets are $26.44 with fee, and $36.73 for the last tour on October 29, which was marked sold out on 10 October 2026 along with October 8; October 15 and 22 were still on sale. Tommyknocker Brewery and Smokin' Yard's BBQ give ticket holders a discount, per the recreation district's home page. Read on the ticket page linked from the district's site on 10 October 2026.
